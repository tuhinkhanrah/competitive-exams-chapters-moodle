1. Syllabus Overview & Learning Objectives / पाठ्यक्रम अवलोकन एवं उद्देश्य

English

Rectilinear kinematics is foundational to mechanics, yielding 1 to 2 direct questions annually in NEET UG. Key target concepts include:

  • Point Object: Approximating objects as particles when size ≪ distance moved.
  • Calculus Kinematics: Instantaneous velocity \(v = \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}\) and acceleration \(a = \frac{\mathrm{d}v}{\mathrm{d}t} = v\frac{\mathrm{d}v}{\mathrm{d}x}\).
  • Motion Graphs: Tangent slopes (\(\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}, \frac{\mathrm{d}v}{\mathrm{d}t}\)) and areas under curves (\(\int v\,\mathrm{d}t, \int a\,\mathrm{d}t\)).
  • Uniformly Accelerated Motion: Derivation and sign conventions for the kinematic equations.
  • Free Fall & Gravity: Vertical symmetry, Galileo's law of odd numbers, stopping distance, and reaction time.
  • Relative Velocity in 1D: Collinear motion equations for overtaking and approaching bodies.

  • अवकलन गतिकी: तात्क्षणिक वेग \(v = \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}\) तथा त्वरण \(a = \frac{\mathrm{d}v}{\mathrm{d}t} = v\frac{\mathrm{d}v}{\mathrm{d}x}\) का निर्धारण।
  • ग्राफ विश्लेषण: ढाल (स्पर्श रेखा) तथा वक्र के अंतर्गत क्षेत्रफल का भौतिक अर्थ।
  • एकसमान त्वरित गति: शुद्धगतिक समीकरणों का निगमन व उचित चिन्ह परिपाटी।

2. Rectilinear Motion & Point Object / सरल रेखीय गति एवं बिन्दु वस्तु

English

Rectilinear motion is motion along a single straight line. To analyze motion without internal degrees of freedom, we treat the moving body as a point object:

Point Object Criterion: An object can be modeled as a point object if its linear dimensions are negligibly small compared to the distance it traverses in the given time interval.

Examples: A train traveling several hundred kilometers between cities acts as a point object; however, while crossing a 150 m bridge, its size cannot be neglected.

हिन्दी

किसी सरल रेखा के अनुदिश होने वाली गति को ऋजुरेखीय या सरल रेखीय गति कहते हैं। विश्लेषण को सरल बनाने हेतु वस्तु को बिन्दु वस्तु माना जाता है:

बिन्दु वस्तु की कसौटी: यदि किसी वस्तु का आकार उसके द्वारा तय की गई दूरी की तुलना में नगण्य हो, तो उसे बिन्दु वस्तु माना जाता है।

उदाहरण: दो स्टेशनों के बीच सैकड़ों किलोमीटर दौड़ती रेलगाड़ी बिन्दु वस्तु है, किन्तु 150 मीटर लंबे पुल को पार करते समय उसकी लंबाई नगण्य नहीं मानी जा सकती।

3. Instantaneous Velocity & Speed / तात्क्षणिक वेग एवं चाल

English

Average velocity \(\bar{v} = \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}\) gives the overall rate of displacement over a finite interval \(\Delta t\). Instantaneous velocity \(v\) gives the rate of change of position at a specific instant \(t\):

\[ v = \lim_{\Delta t \to 0} \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t} = \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} \] Graphical Meaning: Instantaneous velocity equals the slope of the tangent to the position-time (\(x\text{--}t\)) curve at that instant.

Instantaneous Speed: The instantaneous speed is the absolute magnitude of instantaneous velocity: \(\text{Speed} = |v(t)| = \left|\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}\right|\). Over a finite interval, average speed \(\ge |\text{average velocity}|\), but at any infinitesimal instant, instantaneous speed \(\equiv |\text{instantaneous velocity}|\).

औसत वेग \(\bar{v} = \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}\) किसी समयांतराल में विस्थापन की दर है, जबकि तात्क्षणिक वेग \(v\) किसी विशिष्ट क्षण पर स्थिति परिवर्तन की दर है:

\[ v = \lim_{\Delta t \to 0} \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t} = \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} \] ग्राफीय अर्थ: किसी क्षण पर तात्क्षणिक वेग, उस क्षण पर स्थिति-समय ग्राफ पर खींची गई स्पर्श रेखा की ढाल (प्रवणता) के बराबर होता है।

तात्क्षणिक चाल: तात्क्षणिक चाल वेग का निरपेक्ष मान है: \(|v| = |\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}|\)। परिमित समयांतराल में औसत चाल \(\ge |\text{औसत वेग}|\), परन्तु किसी क्षण पर तात्क्षणिक चाल \(\equiv |\text{तात्क्षणिक वेग}|\) होती है।

4. Acceleration & Motion Graphs / त्वरण एवं गति के ग्राफ

English

Acceleration describes the rate of change of velocity with respect to time:

Average Acceleration: \(\bar{a} = \frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t} = \frac{v_2 - v_1}{t_2 - t_1}\)
Instantaneous Acceleration: \(a = \lim_{\Delta t \to 0}\frac{\Delta v}{\Delta t} = \frac{\mathrm{d}v}{\mathrm{d}t} = \frac{\mathrm{d}^2x}{\mathrm{d}t^2} = v\frac{\mathrm{d}v}{\mathrm{d}x}\)

Curvature of \(x\text{--}t\) Graph:

  • \(a > 0\): Concave upward (parabolic curve opening upward, \(\frac{\mathrm{d}^2x}{\mathrm{d}t^2} > 0\)).
  • \(a < 0\): Concave downward (parabolic curve opening downward, \(\frac{\mathrm{d}^2x}{\mathrm{d}t^2} < 0\)).
  • \(a = 0\): Straight line (uniform velocity, zero acceleration).

5. Kinematic Equations for Constant Acceleration / एकसमान त्वरित गति के समीकरण

English

When acceleration \(a\) is strictly constant, integration yields the canonical kinematic equations:

  1. \(v = v_0 + at\)   (from \(\int_{v_0}^v \mathrm{d}v = a\int_0^t \mathrm{d}t\))
  2. \(x = x_0 + v_0 t + \frac{1}{2}at^2\)   (from \(\int_{x_0}^x \mathrm{d}x = \int_0^t (v_0 + at)\,\mathrm{d}t\))
  3. \(v^2 = v_0^2 + 2a(x - x_0)\)   (from \(\int_{v_0}^v v\,\mathrm{d}v = a\int_{x_0}^x \mathrm{d}x\))
  4. \(x - x_0 = \bar{v}t = \left(\frac{v_0 + v}{2}\right)t\)
  5. \(s_n = v_0 + \frac{a}{2}(2n - 1)\)   (Displacement in the \(n\)-th second)

Important Physical Applications

  • Free Fall under Gravity (\(a = -g = -9.8\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\)): For a body dropped from rest: \(v = -gt\), \(y = -\frac{1}{2}gt^2\), \(v^2 = -2gy\).
    For upward projection with speed \(u\): Max height \(H = \frac{u^2}{2g}\), Flight time \(T = \frac{2u}{g}\).
  • Galileo's Law of Odd Numbers: Distances traversed in successive equal time intervals \(\tau\) by a body falling from rest are in the ratio: \[ y_1 : y_2 : y_3 : y_4 : \dots = 1 : 3 : 5 : 7 : \dots \]
  • Stopping Distance of Vehicles: With initial velocity \(v_0\) and braking deceleration \(-a\): \[ d_s = \frac{v_0^2}{2a} \implies d_s \propto v_0^2 \] Doubling initial speed quadruples the required stopping distance.
  • Driver Reaction Time: Time \(t_r\) between perception and braking action. Using a dropped ruler falling distance \(d\): \[ t_r = \sqrt{\frac{2d}{g}} \]

  • गैलीलियो का विषम अंक नियम: विराम से गिरते पिण्ड द्वारा समान समयांतरालों \(\tau\) में तय दूरियों का अनुपात \(1 : 3 : 5 : 7 : \dots\) होता है।
  • विराम दूरी: ब्रेक लगाने पर रुकने से पूर्व तय दूरी \(d_s = \frac{v_0^2}{2a} \implies d_s \propto v_0^2\)। चाल दोगुनी करने पर विराम दूरी 4 गुना हो जाती है।

6. Relative Velocity in One Dimension / एक विमीय सापेक्ष वेग

English

When two bodies \(A\) and \(B\) move along the same straight line with ground velocities \(v_A\) and \(v_B\):

\[ v_{AB} = v_A - v_B \quad (\text{Velocity of } A \text{ relative to } B) \] \[ v_{BA} = v_B - v_A = -v_{AB} \]
  • Same Direction: Relative speed is the difference of magnitudes: \(|v_A - v_B|\).
  • Opposite Directions (Approaching / Receding): Relative speed is the sum of magnitudes: \(v_A - (-v_B) = v_A + v_B\).
  • Relative Acceleration under Gravity: Since both freely falling bodies experience \(a = -g\), their relative acceleration is \(a_{\text{rel}} = (-g) - (-g) = 0\). Thus, in free fall, one body moves with constant relative velocity with respect to another.
हिन्दी

सरल रेखा पर गतिमान दो पिण्डों \(A\) व \(B\) के लिए धरातल के सापेक्ष वेग \(v_A\) तथा \(v_B\) होने पर:

\[ v_{AB} = v_A - v_B \quad (B \text{ के सापेक्ष } A \text{ का वेग}) \]
  • समान दिशा: सापेक्ष चाल परिमाणों का अंतर होती है (\(|v_A - v_B|\))।
  • विपरीत दिशा: सापेक्ष चाल परिमाणों का योग होती है (\(v_A + v_B\))।
  • मुक्त पतन में सापेक्ष त्वरण: दोनों का त्वरण समान होने से \(a_{\text{rel}} = (-g) - (-g) = 0\) होता है। अतः एक मुक्त गिरते पिण्ड के सापेक्ष दूसरा पिण्ड एकसमान वेग से गति करता है।

8. Step-by-Step Worked Examples / चरणबद्ध हल किए गए उदाहरण

English

Worked Example 1: Calculus Differentiation of Position Function

Problem: An object's position along the \(x\)-axis is \(x(t) = a + bt^2\), where \(a = 8.5\,\mathrm{m}\), \(b = 2.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\), and \(t\) is in seconds. Find (a) velocity at \(t = 0\,\mathrm{s}\) and \(t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}\), and (b) average velocity between \(t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}\) and \(t = 4.0\,\mathrm{s}\).

Solution:
(a) \(v(t) = \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} = 2bt = 2(2.5)t = 5.0\,t\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\).
- At \(t = 0\,\mathrm{s}\): \(v(0) = \mathbf{0\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}}\).
- At \(t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}\): \(v(2) = 5.0(2.0) = \mathbf{10.0\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}}\).
(b) \(\bar{v} = \frac{x(4) - x(2)}{4 - 2} = \frac{[a + b(16)] - [a + b(4)]}{2} = 6b = 6(2.5) = \mathbf{15.0\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}}\).
हिन्दी

हल किया गया उदाहरण 1: स्थिति फलन का अवकलन

प्रश्न: \(x\)-अक्ष पर गतिमान वस्तु की स्थिति \(x(t) = a + bt^2\) है (\(a = 8.5\,\mathrm{m}\), \(b = 2.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\))। (a) \(t = 0\,\mathrm{s}\) व \(t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}\) पर वेग, तथा (b) \(t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}\) से \(4.0\,\mathrm{s}\) के मध्य औसत वेग ज्ञात कीजिए।

हल:
(a) \(v = \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} = 2bt = 5.0\,t\)। \(t = 0\) पर \(v = \mathbf{0\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}}\); \(t = 2\,\mathrm{s}\) पर \(v = \mathbf{10.0\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}}\)।
(b) \(\bar{v} = \frac{x(4) - x(2)}{4 - 2} = \frac{12b}{2} = 6(2.5) = \mathbf{15.0\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}}\)।
English

Worked Example 2: Vertical Launch from a 25 m Building

Problem: A ball is thrown vertically upwards with \(v_0 = 20\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) from the top of a \(25.0\,\mathrm{m}\) tall tower. Taking \(g = 10\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\) and ground as \(y = 0\): (a) How high will it rise? (b) After how long does it hit the ground?

Solution:
(a) Upward direction positive. At peak, \(v = 0\):
\(v^2 = v_0^2 + 2(-g)(y - y_0) \implies 0 = 20^2 - 20(y - 25) \implies y - 25 = \mathbf{20.0\,\mathrm{m}}\).
Maximum height above ground is \(y_{\text{max}} = 25 + 20 = \mathbf{45.0\,\mathrm{m}}\).
(b) Setting final ground coordinate \(y = 0\):
\(y = y_0 + v_0 t - \frac{1}{2}gt^2 \implies 0 = 25 + 20t - 5t^2 \implies t^2 - 4t - 5 = 0\).
Factoring: \((t - 5)(t + 1) = 0 \implies t = \mathbf{5.0\,\mathrm{s}}\) (discarding unphysical \(t = -1\,\mathrm{s}\)).
हिन्दी

हल किया गया उदाहरण 2: 25 मीटर मीनार की छत से प्रक्षेपण

प्रश्न: \(25.0\,\mathrm{m}\) ऊँची मीनार से गेंद \(20\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) वेग से ऊपर फेंकी जाती है (\(g = 10\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\))। (a) अधिकतम ऊँचाई, तथा (b) धरातल तक पहुँचने में कुल समय ज्ञात कीजिए।

हल:
(a) उच्चतम बिन्दु पर \(v = 0 \implies 0 = 20^2 - 2(10)(y - 25) \implies y - 25 = \mathbf{20.0\,\mathrm{m}}\)। धरातल से ऊँचाई \(= \mathbf{45.0\,\mathrm{m}}\)।
(b) धरातल पर \(y = 0 \implies 0 = 25 + 20t - 5t^2 \implies t^2 - 4t - 5 = 0 \implies t = \mathbf{5.0\,\mathrm{s}}\)।

Worked Example 3: Relative Velocity of a Bullet Fired at a Fleeing Car

Problem: A police van moving at \(30\,\mathrm{km\,h^{-1}}\) fires a bullet with muzzle speed \(150\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) at a thief's car fleeing in the same direction at \(192\,\mathrm{km\,h^{-1}}\). With what speed does the bullet hit the thief's car?

Solution:
Convert speeds: \(v_P = 30 \times \frac{5}{18} = \frac{25}{3}\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\), \(v_T = 192 \times \frac{5}{18} = \frac{160}{3}\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\).
Bullet ground speed: \(v_{BG} = v_{\text{muzzle}} + v_P = 150 + \frac{25}{3} = \frac{475}{3}\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\).

(C) \(+12\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\)

10. Common Pitfalls & Checklist / सामान्य त्रुटियाँ एवं पुनरीक्षण सूची

English
1. Sign of Acceleration vs. Speeding Up: Positive acceleration does NOT always mean speeding up! A particle speeds up if \(v\) and \(a\) share the same sign (\(v > 0, a > 0\) OR \(v < 0, a < 0\)). If signs differ, it slows down.
2. Zero Velocity with Non-Zero Acceleration: At the highest point of vertical flight, \(v = 0\), but \(a = -g \ne 0\). Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity, not the instantaneous velocity itself.
3. Constant vs. Variable Acceleration: Formulas \(v = v_0 + at\) and \(x = v_0 t + \frac{1}{2}at^2\) apply ONLY when \(a\) is constant. If \(a = f(t)\) or \(f(x)\), calculus integration must be used.
4. Distance vs. Displacement: Total distance \(s \ge |\Delta x|\). In a round trip, displacement is zero, but total distance is positive.
हिन्दी
1. त्वरण के चिन्ह का भ्रम: धनात्मक त्वरण का अर्थ सदैव चाल बढ़ना नहीं होता। यदि \(v\) व \(a\) दोनों के चिन्ह समान हों तो चाल बढ़ती है; विपरीत चिन्ह होने पर चाल घटती है।
2. शून्य वेग पर अशून्य त्वरण: ऊर्ध्वाधर गति के शीर्ष पर \(v = 0\) होता है, किन्तु त्वरण \(a = -g\) निरंतर कार्य करता रहता है।
3. गति के समीकरणों की सीमा: \(v = v_0 + at\) केवल नियत त्वरण पर मान्य है। परिवर्ती त्वरण में समाकलन आवश्यक है।
4. दूरी बनाम विस्थापन: किसी पूर्ण यात्रा (लौटने) में विस्थापन शून्य हो सकता है, किन्तु दूरी सदैव धनात्मक होती है।

2. Rectilinear Motion & Point Object / सरल रेखीय गति एवं बिन्दु वस्तु

English

Rectilinear motion is motion along a single straight line. To analyze motion without internal degrees of freedom, we treat the moving body as a point object:

Point Object Criterion: An object can be modeled as a point object if its linear dimensions are negligibly small compared to the distance it traverses in the given time interval.

Examples: A train traveling several hundred kilometers between cities acts as a point object; however, while crossing a 150 m bridge, its size cannot be neglected.

हिन्दी

3. Instantaneous Velocity & Speed / तात्क्षणिक वेग एवं चाल

English

Average velocity \(\bar{v} = \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t}\) gives the overall rate of displacement over a finite interval \(\Delta t\). Instantaneous velocity \(v\) gives the rate of change of position at a specific instant \(t\):

\[ v = \lim_{\Delta t \to 0} \frac{\Delta x}{\Delta t} = \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} \] Graphical Meaning: Instantaneous velocity equals the slope of the tangent to the position-time (\(x\text{--}t\)) curve at that instant.

Instantaneous Speed: The instantaneous speed is the absolute magnitude of instantaneous velocity: \(\text{Speed} = |v(t)| = \left|\frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}\right|\). Over a finite interval, average speed \(\ge |\text{average velocity}|\), but at any infinitesimal instant, instantaneous speed \(\equiv |\text{instantaneous velocity}|\).

5. Kinematic Equations for Constant Acceleration / एकसमान त्वरित गति के समीकरण

English

When acceleration \(a\) is strictly constant, integration yields the canonical kinematic equations:

  1. \(v = v_0 + at\)   (from \(\int_{v_0}^v \mathrm{d}v = a\int_0^t \mathrm{d}t\))
  2. \(x = x_0 + v_0 t + \frac{1}{2}at^2\)   (from \(\int_{x_0}^x \mathrm{d}x = \int_0^t (v_0 + at)\,\mathrm{d}t\))
  3. \(v^2 = v_0^2 + 2a(x - x_0)\)   (from \(\int_{v_0}^v v\,\mathrm{d}v = a\int_{x_0}^x \mathrm{d}x\))
  4. \(x - x_0 = \bar{v}t = \left(\frac{v_0 + v}{2}\right)t\)
  5. \(s_n = v_0 + \frac{a}{2}(2n - 1)\)   (Displacement in the \(n\)-th second)

Important Physical Applications

  • Free Fall under Gravity (\(a = -g = -9.8\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\)): For a body dropped from rest: \(v = -gt\), \(y = -\frac{1}{2}gt^2\), \(v^2 = -2gy\).
    For upward projection with speed \(u\): Max height \(H = \frac{u^2}{2g}\), Flight time \(T = \frac{2u}{g}\).
  • Galileo's Law of Odd Numbers: Distances traversed in successive equal time intervals \(\tau\) by a body falling from rest are in the ratio: \[ y_1 : y_2 : y_3 : y_4 : \dots = 1 : 3 : 5 : 7 : \dots \]
  • Stopping Distance of Vehicles: With initial velocity \(v_0\) and braking deceleration \(-a\): \[ d_s = \frac{v_0^2}{2a} \implies d_s \propto v_0^2 \] Doubling initial speed quadruples the required stopping distance.
  • Driver Reaction Time: Time \(t_r\) between perception and braking action. Using a dropped ruler falling distance \(d\): \[ t_r = \sqrt{\frac{2d}{g}} \]

6. Relative Velocity in One Dimension / एक विमीय सापेक्ष वेग

English

When two bodies \(A\) and \(B\) move along the same straight line with ground velocities \(v_A\) and \(v_B\):

\[ v_{AB} = v_A - v_B \quad (\text{Velocity of } A \text{ relative to } B) \] \[ v_{BA} = v_B - v_A = -v_{AB} \]
  • Same Direction: Relative speed is the difference of magnitudes: \(|v_A - v_B|\).
  • Opposite Directions (Approaching / Receding): Relative speed is the sum of magnitudes: \(v_A - (-v_B) = v_A + v_B\).
  • Relative Acceleration under Gravity: Since both freely falling bodies experience \(a = -g\), their relative acceleration is \(a_{\text{rel}} = (-g) - (-g) = 0\). Thus, in free fall, one body moves with constant relative velocity with respect to another.
हिन्दी

सरल रेखा पर गतिमान दो पिण्डों \(A\) व \(B\) के लिए धरातल के सापेक्ष वेग \(v_A\) तथा \(v_B\) होने पर:

\[ v_{AB} = v_A - v_B \quad (B \text{ के सापेक्ष } A \text{ का वेग}) \]
  • समान दिशा: सापेक्ष चाल परिमाणों का अंतर होती है (\(|v_A - v_B|\))।
  • विपरीत दिशा: सापेक्ष चाल परिमाणों का योग होती है (\(v_A + v_B\))।
  • मुक्त पतन में सापेक्ष त्वरण: दोनों का त्वरण समान होने से \(a_{\text{rel}} = (-g) - (-g) = 0\) होता है। अतः एक मुक्त गिरते पिण्ड के सापेक्ष दूसरा पिण्ड एकसमान वेग से गति करता है।

Worked Example 1: Calculus Differentiation of Position Function

Problem: An object's position along the \(x\)-axis is \(x(t) = a + bt^2\), where \(a = 8.5\,\mathrm{m}\), \(b = 2.5\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\), and \(t\) is in seconds. Find (a) velocity at \(t = 0\,\mathrm{s}\) and \(t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}\), and (b) average velocity between \(t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}\) and \(t = 4.0\,\mathrm{s}\).

Solution:
(a) \(v(t) = \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} = 2bt = 2(2.5)t = 5.0\,t\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\).
- At \(t = 0\,\mathrm{s}\): \(v(0) = \mathbf{0\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}}\).
- At \(t = 2.0\,\mathrm{s}\): \(v(2) = 5.0(2.0) = \mathbf{10.0\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}}\).
(b) \(\bar{v} = \frac{x(4) - x(2)}{4 - 2} = \frac{[a + b(16)] - [a + b(4)]}{2} = 6b = 6(2.5) = \mathbf{15.0\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}}\).

Worked Example 2: Vertical Launch from a 25 m Building

Problem: A ball is thrown vertically upwards with \(v_0 = 20\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) from the top of a \(25.0\,\mathrm{m}\) tall tower. Taking \(g = 10\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-2}}\) and ground as \(y = 0\): (a) How high will it rise? (b) After how long does it hit the ground?

Solution:
(a) Upward direction positive. At peak, \(v = 0\):
\(v^2 = v_0^2 + 2(-g)(y - y_0) \implies 0 = 20^2 - 20(y - 25) \implies y - 25 = \mathbf{20.0\,\mathrm{m}}\).
Maximum height above ground is \(y_{\text{max}} = 25 + 20 = \mathbf{45.0\,\mathrm{m}}\).
(b) Setting final ground coordinate \(y = 0\):
\(y = y_0 + v_0 t - \frac{1}{2}gt^2 \implies 0 = 25 + 20t - 5t^2 \implies t^2 - 4t - 5 = 0\).
Factoring: \((t - 5)(t + 1) = 0 \implies t = \mathbf{5.0\,\mathrm{s}}\) (discarding unphysical \(t = -1\,\mathrm{s}\)).

Worked Example 3: Relative Velocity of a Bullet Fired at a Fleeing Car

(B) Displacement = \(32.5\,\mathrm{m}\), Distance = \(47.5\,\mathrm{m}\)

Practice Question 3 (Galileo's Odd Number Law): A stone falls freely from rest under gravity. If it traverses distances \(h_1, h_2, h_3\) during the 1st second, 2nd second, and 3rd second respectively, what is the ratio \(h_1 : h_2 : h_3\)?
(A) \(1 : 2 : 3\)
(B) \(1 : 4 : 9\)
(C) \(1 : 3 : 5\)
(D) \(1 : \sqrt{2} : \sqrt{3}\)
Correct Answer: (C)
Solution:
Displacement in \(n\)-th second from rest is \(s_n = \frac{g}{2}(2n - 1)\).

Practice Question 6 (Relative Velocity - Opposite Directions): Train A travels North at \(54\,\mathrm{km\,h^{-1}}\), and Train B travels South at \(90\,\mathrm{km\,h^{-1}}\) on parallel tracks. What is the velocity of Train B relative to Train A?

10. Common Pitfalls & Checklist / सामान्य त्रुटियाँ एवं पुनरीक्षण सूची

English
1. Sign of Acceleration vs. Speeding Up: Positive acceleration does NOT always mean speeding up! A particle speeds up if \(v\) and \(a\) share the same sign (\(v > 0, a > 0\) OR \(v < 0, a < 0\)). If signs differ, it slows down.
2. Zero Velocity with Non-Zero Acceleration: At the highest point of vertical flight, \(v = 0\), but \(a = -g \ne 0\). Acceleration is the rate of change of velocity, not the instantaneous velocity itself.
3. Constant vs. Variable Acceleration: Formulas \(v = v_0 + at\) and \(x = v_0 t + \frac{1}{2}at^2\) apply ONLY when \(a\) is constant. If \(a = f(t)\) or \(f(x)\), calculus integration must be used.
4. Distance vs. Displacement: Total distance \(s \ge |\Delta x|\). In a round trip, displacement is zero, but total distance is positive.
हिन्दी
1. त्वरण के चिन्ह का भ्रम: धनात्मक त्वरण का अर्थ सदैव चाल बढ़ना नहीं होता। यदि \(v\) व \(a\) दोनों के चिन्ह समान हों तो चाल बढ़ती है; विपरीत चिन्ह होने पर चाल घटती है।
2. शून्य वेग पर अशून्य त्वरण: ऊर्ध्वाधर गति के शीर्ष पर \(v = 0\) होता है, किन्तु त्वरण \(a = -g\) निरंतर कार्य करता रहता है।
3. गति के समीकरणों की सीमा: \(v = v_0 + at\) केवल नियत त्वरण पर मान्य है। परिवर्ती त्वरण में समाकलन आवश्यक है।
4. दूरी बनाम विस्थापन: किसी पूर्ण यात्रा (लौटने) में विस्थापन शून्य हो सकता है, किन्तु दूरी सदैव धनात्मक होती है।

11. Final Quick Recap / अंतिम त्वरित सारांश

English

Core Formula Reference Sheet:

  • Differential: \(v = \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t}\), \quad \(a = \frac{\mathrm{d}v}{\mathrm{d}t} = v\frac{\mathrm{d}v}{\mathrm{d}x}\)
  • Integrals: \(\Delta x = \int v\,\mathrm{d}t\) (Area under \(v\text{--}t\)), \quad \(\Delta v = \int a\,\mathrm{d}t\) (Area under \(a\text{--}t\))
  • Kinematic Equations (Constant \(a\)): \[ v = v_0 + at, \quad x = x_0 + v_0 t + \frac{1}{2}at^2, \quad v^2 = v_0^2 + 2a(x - x_0) \]
  • \(n\)-th Second: \(s_n = v_0 + \frac{a}{2}(2n - 1)\)
  • Galileo Odd Ratios: \(1 : 3 : 5 : 7 : 9 \dots\)
  • Free Fall: Max height \(H = \frac{u^2}{2g}\), \quad Flight Time \(T = \frac{2u}{g}\)
  • Stopping Distance: \(d_s = \frac{v_0^2}{2a} \implies d_s \propto v_0^2\)
  • Reaction Time: \(t_r = \sqrt{\frac{2d}{g}}\)
  • Relative Velocity: \(v_{AB} = v_A - v_B\)
