2. Molecular Nature of Matter & Behaviour of Real vs. Ideal Gases | পদার্থের আণবিক প্রকৃতি এবং বাস্তব বনাম আদর্শ গ্যাস

English

Atomic Nature and Length Scales: All matter is composed of atoms and molecules in constant motion. The characteristic diameter of an atom or molecule is of the order of \(1\,\text{Å} = 10^{-10}\,\text{m}\). In solids and liquids, molecules are closely packed with average intermolecular separations of approximately \(2\,\text{Å}\) to \(3\,\text{Å}\). In gases at standard temperature and pressure (STP), the average intermolecular separation is roughly \(30\,\text{Å}\) to \(40\,\text{Å}\) (an order of magnitude larger than molecular dimensions), while the mean free path is on the order of \(1000\,\text{Å}\) or more.

Equation of State of an Ideal Gas: An ideal gas is a theoretical substance whose particles have zero volume and experience no intermolecular forces. It strictly obeys the ideal gas equation under all conditions:

Ideal Gas Equation of State:
\[ PV = \mu RT = N k_{\mathrm{B}} T = n k_{\mathrm{B}} T V \] \[ P = \frac{\rho R T}{M_0} = \frac{1}{3} \rho v_{\mathrm{rms}}^2 \]

Where \(\mu = \frac{M}{M_0} = \frac{N}{N_{\mathrm{A}}}\) is the number of moles, \(R = 8.314\,\mathrm{J\cdot mol^{-1}\cdot K^{-1}}\) is the universal gas constant, \(k_{\mathrm{B}} = \frac{R}{N_{\mathrm{A}}} = 1.38 \times 10^{-23}\,\mathrm{J\cdot K^{-1}}\) is the Boltzmann constant, \(N_{\mathrm{A}} = 6.02 \times 10^{23}\,\mathrm{mol^{-1}}\) is Avogadro's number, \(\rho\) is mass density, and \(n = N/V\) is the molecular number density.

Real Gases and Conditions for Ideality: Real gases behave like an ideal gas only at low pressures and high temperatures. At low pressure, the gas is rarefied, making intermolecular distances large and intermolecular potential energy negligible. At high temperature, the average molecular kinetic energy far exceeds any potential attraction between molecules.

3. Microscopic Model & Derivation of Gas Pressure | গতিতত্ত্বের স্বীকার্য ও চাপের রাশিমালা প্রতিপাদন

English

Fundamental Postulates of Kinetic Theory of Gases:

  1. A gas consists of a very large number of identical, tiny, hard, spherical particles called molecules.
  2. The actual volume occupied by the gas molecules themselves is completely negligible compared to the total volume of the container.
  3. Molecules are in a state of incessant, chaotic, random motion in all possible directions with all possible speeds.
  4. There are no attractive or repulsive intermolecular forces except during collisions. Between collisions, molecules travel in straight lines at constant speed.
  5. Collisions of gas molecules with each other and with the container walls are perfectly elastic; total kinetic energy and linear momentum are conserved.
  6. The duration of a collision is infinitesimal compared to the time interval between successive collisions.
  7. Newtonian classical mechanics applies to molecular motions, and gravitational effects on molecules are negligible due to their small masses and high thermal velocities.

Derivation of Pressure Exerted by an Ideal Gas:

Consider a cubical container of side \(L\) (volume \(V = L^3\)) containing \(N\) identical molecules, each of mass \(m\). Let the faces of the cube be perpendicular to the Cartesian axes \(x, y, z\).

Consider a molecule moving with velocity \(\vec{v} = (v_x, v_y, v_z)\). When it collides elastically with a wall perpendicular to the \(x\)-axis:

  • Its \(y\) and \(z\) velocity components remain unchanged: \(v_y' = v_y, v_z' = v_z\).
  • Its \(x\)-velocity component is reversed: \(v_x' = -v_x\).
  • The change in momentum of the molecule is: \(\Delta p_x = -m v_x - (m v_x) = -2 m v_x\).
  • By conservation of momentum, the momentum transferred to the wall per collision is: \(\Delta p_{\mathrm{wall}} = +2 m v_x\).

In a small time interval \(\Delta t\), the molecules having velocity component \(v_x\) that can strike an area \(A\) of the wall must lie within a cylinder of base \(A\) and length \(v_x \Delta t\). The volume of this cylinder is \(A v_x \Delta t\). Since molecular motion is isotropic, on average half (\(1/2\)) of the molecules in this volume are moving towards the wall, and the other half are moving away.

Number of collisions with wall area \(A\) in time \(\Delta t\):

\[ \Delta N = \frac{1}{2} n A v_x \Delta t \]

Total momentum transferred to the wall in time \(\Delta t\):

\[ Q = \Delta N \times (2 m v_x) = \left(\frac{1}{2} n A v_x \Delta t\right)(2 m v_x) = n m A v_x^2 \Delta t \]

The force exerted on the wall is the rate of momentum transfer:

\[ F = \frac{Q}{\Delta t} = n m A v_x^2 \]

Pressure is force per unit area. Taking into account the distribution of velocities of all molecules:

\[ P = \frac{F}{A} = n m \overline{v_x^2} \]

Since molecular motion in equilibrium is isotropic (no preferred direction):

\[ \overline{v_x^2} = \overline{v_y^2} = \overline{v_z^2} = \frac{1}{3}\left(\overline{v_x^2} + \overline{v_y^2} + \overline{v_z^2}\right) = \frac{1}{3}\overline{v^2} \]

Substituting this isotropy condition yields the celebrated formula:

Fundamental Kinetic Pressure Formula:
\[ P = \frac{1}{3} n m \overline{v^2} = \frac{1}{3} \frac{M_{\mathrm{total}}}{V} \overline{v^2} = \frac{1}{3} \rho v_{\mathrm{rms}}^2 \]

Where \(\rho = n m\) is the mass density of the gas and \(v_{\mathrm{rms}} = \sqrt{\overline{v^2}}\) is the root-mean-square speed.

আদর্শ গ্যাসের চাপের রাশিমালা প্রতিপাদন:

\(L\) বাহুবিশিষ্ট একটি ঘনকাকৃতি পাত্রের আয়তন \(V = L^3\)। এতে \(m\) ভরবিশিষ্ট \(N\) সংখ্যক সদৃশ অণু রয়েছে। একটি অণুর বেগ \(\vec{v} = (v_x, v_y, v_z)\)। যখন অণুটি \(x\)-অক্ষের লম্ব দেয়ালে স্থিতিস্থাপকভাবে ধাক্কা খায়:

  • \(y\) ও \(z\) উপাংশ অপরিবর্তিত থাকে এবং \(x\) উপাংশ বিপরীতমুখী হয় (\(-v_x\))।
  • অণুটির ভরবেগের পরিবর্তন: \(\Delta p_x = -m v_x - (m v_x) = -2mv_x\)।
  • ভরবেগ সংরক্ষণ নীতি অনুযায়ী দেয়ালে অর্পিত ভরবেগ: \(+2mv_x\)।

\(\Delta t\) সময়ে দেয়ালের \(A\) ক্ষেত্রফলে আঘাতকারী অণুগুলি \(A v_x \Delta t\) আয়তনের চোঙের মধ্যে থাকে। সমদিকধর্মী গতির কারণে এই অণুগুলির অর্ধেক (\(1/2\)) দেয়ালের দিকে ধাবিত হয়।

\(\Delta t\) সময়ে মোট সংঘর্ষের সংখ্যা: \(\Delta N = \frac{1}{2} n A v_x \Delta t\)।

দেয়ালে প্রযুক্ত মোট ভরবেগ: \(Q = (2mv_x) \times \left(\frac{1}{2} n A v_x \Delta t\right) = n m A v_x^2 \Delta t\)।

অতএব দেয়ালের ওপর বল \(F = \frac{Q}{\Delta t} = n m A v_x^2\), এবং চাপ \(P = \frac{F}{A} = n m \overline{v_x^2}\)।

যেহেতু অণুগুলির গতির কোনো নির্দিষ্ট পছন্দসই দিক নেই (সমদিকধর্মিতা):

\[ \overline{v_x^2} = \overline{v_y^2} = \overline{v_z^2} = \frac{1}{3}\overline{v^2} \]

সুতরাং গ্যাসের চাপের চূড়ান্ত সূত্রটি দাঁড়ায়:

আদর্শ গ্যাসের চাপের চূড়ান্ত সূত্র:
\[ P = \frac{1}{3} n m \overline{v^2} = \frac{1}{3} \rho v_{\mathrm{rms}}^2 \]

যেখানে \(\rho = n m\) হলো গ্যাসের ঘনত্ব এবং \(v_{\mathrm{rms}} = \sqrt{\overline{v^2}}\) হলো মূল গড় বর্গ বেগ।

4. Kinetic Interpretation of Temperature & Molecular Speeds | পরম উষ্ণতার গতীয় ব্যাখ্যা এবং আণবিক গতিবেগ

English

Relation Between Internal Energy, Pressure, and Temperature:

Multiplying the pressure formula by volume \(V\):

\[ PV = \frac{1}{3} n V m \overline{v^2} = \frac{2}{3} N \left(\frac{1}{2} m \overline{v^2}\right) \]

The total translational kinetic energy \(E\) of \(N\) molecules is \(E = N \left(\frac{1}{2} m \overline{v^2}\right)\). Therefore:

Pressure and Kinetic Energy Density Relation:
\[ PV = \frac{2}{3} E \implies P = \frac{2}{3} \frac{E}{V} = \frac{2}{3} E_V \]

Pressure is equal to two-thirds of the translational kinetic energy per unit volume (\(E_V\)).

Equating \(PV = \frac{2}{3}E\) with the ideal gas law \(PV = N k_{\mathrm{B}} T = \mu R T\):

Kinetic Energy per Molecule and per Mole:
\[ E = \frac{3}{2} N k_{\mathrm{B}} T = \frac{3}{2} \mu R T \] \[ \overline{\varepsilon}_{\mathrm{trans}} = \frac{E}{N} = \frac{1}{2} m \overline{v^2} = \frac{3}{2} k_{\mathrm{B}} T \]

Key Takeaway: The average translational kinetic energy of a molecule depends solely on absolute temperature \(T\). It is completely independent of pressure, volume, and the nature or mass of the molecule.

Characteristic Molecular Speeds:

At absolute temperature \(T\), the Maxwell-Boltzmann distribution defines three characteristic speeds:

Summary of Molecular Speeds:
  1. Root-Mean-Square Speed (\(v_{\mathrm{rms}}\)): \[ v_{\mathrm{rms}} = \sqrt{\overline{v^2}} = \sqrt{\frac{3 k_{\mathrm{B}} T}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{3 R T}{M_0}} = \sqrt{\frac{3 P}{\rho}} \approx 1.732 \sqrt{\frac{RT}{M_0}} \]
  2. Average (Mean) Speed (\(v_{\mathrm{avg}}\)): \[ v_{\mathrm{avg}} = \overline{v} = \sqrt{\frac{8 k_{\mathrm{B}} T}{\pi m}} = \sqrt{\frac{8 R T}{\pi M_0}} \approx 1.596 \sqrt{\frac{RT}{M_0}} \]
  3. Most Probable Speed (\(v_{\mathrm{mp}}\)): \[ v_{\mathrm{mp}} = \sqrt{\frac{2 k_{\mathrm{B}} T}{m}} = \sqrt{\frac{2 R T}{M_0}} \approx 1.414 \sqrt{\frac{RT}{M_0}} \]

Ratio of Speeds: \(v_{\mathrm{rms}} : v_{\mathrm{avg}} : v_{\mathrm{mp}} = \sqrt{3} : \sqrt{\frac{8}{\pi}} : \sqrt{2} \approx 1.732 : 1.596 : 1.414\)

Hence: \(v_{\mathrm{rms}} > v_{\mathrm{avg}} > v_{\mathrm{mp}}\)

Comparison with Speed of Sound: The speed of sound in an ideal gas is \(v_{\mathrm{sound}} = \sqrt{\frac{\gamma R T}{M_0}}\). Because \(\gamma < 3\) for all gases (e.g., \(\gamma = 5/3 \approx 1.67\) for monatomic gas), the speed of sound is always slightly less than the rms speed: \(v_{\mathrm{sound}} < v_{\mathrm{rms}}\).

5. Law of Equipartition of Energy & Specific Heat Capacities | শক্তির সমবণ্টন নীতি এবং আপেক্ষিক তাপগ্রাহিতা

English

Degrees of Freedom (\(f\)): The degree of freedom of a dynamical system is defined as the total number of independent coordinates or independent quadratic energy terms required to completely specify the position and configuration of the system.

  • Translational degrees of freedom: Any molecule free to move in 3D space possesses 3 translational degrees of freedom (\(\frac{1}{2}mv_x^2 + \frac{1}{2}mv_y^2 + \frac{1}{2}mv_z^2\)).
  • Rotational degrees of freedom:
    • Monatomic gas (He, Ne, Ar): Spherically symmetric point mass; moment of inertia about any axis passing through the nucleus is negligibly small. Rotational degrees of freedom = 0. Total \(f = 3\).
    • Diatomic gas (rigid dumbbell, \(\mathrm{O_2, N_2, CO}\)): Rotates about two mutually perpendicular axes orthogonal to the internuclear axis (Figure below). Rotation about the internuclear bond axis has negligible moment of inertia. Rotational degrees of freedom = 2. Total \(f = 3 + 2 = 5\).
    • Non-linear polyatomic gas (\(\mathrm{H_2O, NH_3, CH_4}\)): Rotates about 3 independent axes. Rotational degrees of freedom = 3. Total \(f = 3 + 3 = 6\) (rigid).
  • Vibrational modes: At higher temperatures, atoms oscillate along their bond axis. Each vibrational mode contributes two quadratic terms: kinetic energy (\(\frac{1}{2} m \dot{y}^2\)) and potential energy (\(\frac{1}{2} k y^2\)). Thus, each vibrational mode contributes \(2 \times \frac{1}{2} k_{\mathrm{B}} T = k_{\mathrm{B}} T\) to average thermal energy.

Law of Equipartition of Energy: In thermal equilibrium at absolute temperature \(T\), the total energy of a dynamical system is partitioned equally among all its degrees of freedom, each quadratic energy term contributing an average energy of \(\frac{1}{2} k_{\mathrm{B}} T\).

Internal Energy and Molar Heat Capacities:

For 1 mole of an ideal gas with \(f\) active degrees of freedom:

\[ U = N_{\mathrm{A}} \left(f \times \frac{1}{2} k_{\mathrm{B}} T\right) = \frac{f}{2} R T \] \[ C_v = \left(\frac{\mathrm{d}U}{\mathrm{d}T}\right)_v = \frac{f}{2} R \] \[ C_p = C_v + R = \left(\frac{f}{2} + 1\right) R \] \[ \gamma = \frac{C_p}{C_v} = 1 + \frac{2}{f} \]

6. Mean Free Path & Collision Dynamics | গড় মুক্ত পথ এবং সংঘর্ষের গতিবিদ্যা

English

Physical Meaning of Mean Free Path: Although gas molecules move at speeds comparable to the speed of sound (\(\sim 500\,\mathrm{m/s}\)), diffusion of gases and odour propagation is relatively slow. This occurs because gas molecules undergo incessant collisions, causing them to follow a zigzag random walk. The average distance travelled by a molecule between two successive collisions is called the mean free path (\(\lambda\) or \(l\)).

Derivation of Mean Free Path:

Let molecules be rigid spheres of diameter \(d\). Consider one molecule moving with average speed \(\langle v \rangle\) while momentarily regarding all other molecules as stationary. It will collide with any molecule whose center lies within a distance \(d\) from its center. In time \(\Delta t\), this molecule sweeps out an effective collision cylinder of cross-sectional area \(\pi d^2\) and length \(\langle v \rangle \Delta t\).

Collision cylinder volume: \(V_{\mathrm{cyl}} = \pi d^2 \langle v \rangle \Delta t\).

If \(n\) is the number of molecules per unit volume (number density), the number of collisions suffered in time \(\Delta t\) is:

\[ N_{\mathrm{coll}} = n \pi d^2 \langle v \rangle \Delta t \]

Collision rate per unit time: \(\nu_{\mathrm{coll}} = \frac{N_{\mathrm{coll}}}{\Delta t} = n \pi d^2 \langle v \rangle\).

The average time between successive collisions (mean collision time \(\tau\)) is:

\[ \tau = \frac{1}{\nu_{\mathrm{coll}}} = \frac{1}{n \pi d^2 \langle v \rangle} \]

Mean free path (elementary formula):

\[ \lambda_{\mathrm{simple}} = \langle v \rangle \tau = \frac{1}{n \pi d^2} \]

In reality, all molecules are moving simultaneously. Taking into account the relative velocity of colliding molecules (\(v_{\mathrm{rel}} = \sqrt{2}\langle v \rangle\)), the exact formula derived by Clausius and Maxwell is:

Exact Mean Free Path Formula:
\[ \lambda = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2} n \pi d^2} \]

Using the ideal gas relation \(P = n k_{\mathrm{B}} T \implies n = \frac{P}{k_{\mathrm{B}} T}\), the mean free path can also be expressed in terms of macroscopic variables:

\[ \lambda = \frac{k_{\mathrm{B}} T}{\sqrt{2} \pi d^2 P} \]

Parametric Dependencies for NEET:

  • \(\lambda \propto \frac{1}{d^2}\) (inversely proportional to the square of molecular diameter).
  • \(\lambda \propto \frac{1}{n}\) (inversely proportional to number density).
  • At constant temperature (\(T = \text{const}\)): \(\lambda \propto \frac{1}{P}\) (inversely proportional to pressure).
  • At constant volume (\(V = \text{const}\), meaning \(n = N/V = \text{const}\)): \(\lambda\) is independent of temperature.
  • Collision frequency: \(\nu_c = \frac{\langle v \rangle}{\lambda} = \sqrt{2} \pi n d^2 \langle v \rangle \propto \frac{P}{\sqrt{T}}\).

গড় মুক্ত পথের সমীকরণ প্রতিপাদন:

ধরা যাক অণুগুলি \(d\) ব্যাসের গোলক। যদি একটি নির্দিষ্ট অণু \(\langle v \rangle\) বেগে চলে এবং বাকি অণুগুলি সাময়িকভাবে স্থির থাকে, তবে কেন্দ্র থেকে \(d\) দূরত্বের মধ্যে অবস্থিত যেকোনো অণুর সাথে সংঘর্ষ ঘটবে। \(\Delta t\) সময়ে অণুটি \(\pi d^2\) প্রস্থচ্ছেদের ক্ষেত্রফল এবং \(\langle v \rangle \Delta t\) দৈর্ঘ্যের একটি চোঙাকার স্থান অতিক্রম করে।

সংঘর্ষ চোঙের আয়তন \(V_{\mathrm{cyl}} = \pi d^2 \langle v \rangle \Delta t\)। একক আয়তনে অণুর সংখ্যা (সংখ্যা ঘনত্ব) \(n\) হলে, \(\Delta t\) সময়ে সংঘর্ষ সংখ্যা:

\[ N_{\mathrm{coll}} = n \pi d^2 \langle v \rangle \Delta t \]

প্রকৃতপক্ষে সমস্ত অণুই চলমান থাকায় আপেক্ষিক বেগ \(\sqrt{2}\langle v \rangle\) বিবেচনা করতে হয়। ফলে ম্যাক্সওয়েলের সংশোধিত নিখুঁত সূত্রটি দাঁড়ায়:

গড় মুক্ত পথের চূড়ান্ত সূত্র:
\[ \lambda = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2} n \pi d^2} \]

যেহেতু \(P = n k_{\mathrm{B}} T\), সুতরাং \(n = \frac{P}{k_{\mathrm{B}} T}\) বসিয়ে ম্যাক্রোস্কোপিক রূপটি পাওয়া যায়:

\[ \lambda = \frac{k_{\mathrm{B}} T}{\sqrt{2} \pi d^2 P} \]

NEET পরীক্ষার জন্য গুরুত্বপূর্ণ নির্ভরতা:

  • \(\lambda \propto \frac{1}{d^2}\) (অণুর ব্যাসের বর্গের ব্যস্তানুপাতিক)।
  • স্থির তাপমাত্রায়: \(\lambda \propto \frac{1}{P}\) (চাপের ব্যস্তানুপাতিক)।
  • স্থির আয়তনে (\(V = \text{ধ্রুবক}\), অর্থাৎ \(n = N/V = \text{ধ্রুবক}\)): গড় মুক্ত পথ \(\lambda\) তাপমাত্রার ওপর নির্ভর করে না।
  • সংঘর্ষ কম্পাঙ্ক: \(\nu_c = \frac{\langle v \rangle}{\lambda} \propto \frac{P}{\sqrt{T}}\)।

Worked Example 1: Molecular Volume vs. Actual Volume of Water Vapour

Problem: The density of liquid water is \(1000\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^{-3}}\) and that of water vapour at \(100\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\) and \(1\,\mathrm{atm}\) is \(0.6\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^{-3}}\). (i) Estimate the ratio of molecular volume to total volume occupied by water vapour. (ii) Estimate the radius of a water molecule assuming it to be spherical (Molar mass of \(\mathrm{H_2O} = 18\,\mathrm{g\cdot mol^{-1}}\), \(N_{\mathrm{A}} = 6.02 \times 10^{23}\,\mathrm{mol^{-1}}\)).

Solution:

(i) For a given mass \(M\), volume is inversely proportional to density: \(V = M/\rho\).
In the liquid state, molecules are tightly packed, so the total volume is virtually equal to the molecular volume: \(V_{\mathrm{mol}} = M / \rho_{\mathrm{liquid}}\).
In the vapour state, the actual volume occupied is \(V_{\mathrm{vap}} = M / \rho_{\mathrm{vap}}\).
Fractional volume occupied by molecules in the vapour state:

\[ \frac{V_{\mathrm{mol}}}{V_{\mathrm{vap}}} = \frac{\rho_{\mathrm{vap}}}{\rho_{\mathrm{liquid}}} = \frac{0.6}{1000} = 6 \times 10^{-4} \]

(ii) Mass of a single water molecule:

\[ m = \frac{M_0}{N_{\mathrm{A}}} = \frac{0.018\,\mathrm{kg}}{6.02 \times 10^{23}} \approx 3 \times 10^{-26}\,\mathrm{kg} \]

Volume of a single molecule (taking density equal to bulk liquid \(\rho = 1000\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^{-3}}\)):

\[ V_1 = \frac{m}{\rho_{\mathrm{liquid}}} = \frac{3 \times 10^{-26}\,\mathrm{kg}}{1000\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^{-3}}} = 3 \times 10^{-29}\,\mathrm{m^3} \] \[ \frac{4}{3} \pi r^3 = 3 \times 10^{-29} \implies r^3 \approx 7.16 \times 10^{-30}\,\mathrm{m^3} \implies r \approx 1.93 \times 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{m} \approx 2\,\text{Å} \]

সমস্যা: তরল জলের ঘনত্ব \(1000\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^{-3}}\) এবং \(100\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\) ও \(1\,\mathrm{atm}\) চাপে জলীয় বাষ্পের ঘনত্ব \(0.6\,\mathrm{kg\cdot m^{-3}}\)। (i) বাষ্পের মোট আয়তনের তুলনায় আণবিক আয়তনের ভগ্নাংশ নির্ণয় করো। (ii) জলের অণুকে গোলকাকার ধরে তার ব্যাসার্ধ অনুমান করো (\(\mathrm{H_2O}\)-এর মোলার ভর \(= 18\,\mathrm{g\cdot mol^{-1}}\))।

সমাধান:

(i) তরলে অণুগুলি প্রায় গায়ে গায়ে লেগে থাকে, তাই তরল অবস্থার আয়তনই হলো প্রকৃত আণবিক আয়তন।
বাষ্পে আণবিক আয়তনের ভগ্নাংশ:

\[ \frac{V_{\mathrm{mol}}}{V_{\mathrm{vap}}} = \frac{\rho_{\mathrm{vap}}}{\rho_{\mathrm{liquid}}} = \frac{0.6}{1000} = 6 \times 10^{-4} \]

(ii) একটি জল অণুর ভর \(m = \frac{0.018}{6.02 \times 10^{23}} \approx 3 \times 10^{-26}\,\mathrm{kg}\)।
একটি অণুর আয়তন \(V_1 = \frac{m}{\rho} = \frac{3 \times 10^{-26}}{1000} = 3 \times 10^{-29}\,\mathrm{m^3}\)।
\(\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3 = 3 \times 10^{-29} \implies r \approx 2 \times 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{m} = 2\,\text{Å}\)।

Worked Example 2: Gas Mixture and rms Speeds

Problem: A closed vessel contains a mixture of Helium (\(\mathrm{He}\), monatomic, atomic mass \(4\,\mathrm{u}\)) and Oxygen (\(\mathrm{O_2}\), diatomic, molecular mass \(32\,\mathrm{u}\)) at temperature \(T = 300\,\mathrm{K}\). Find: (i) The ratio of the average translational kinetic energy per molecule of \(\mathrm{He}\) to that of \(\mathrm{O_2}\). (ii) The ratio of their rms speeds \(v_{\mathrm{rms}}(\mathrm{He}) / v_{\mathrm{rms}}(\mathrm{O_2})\).

Solution:

(i) The average translational kinetic energy per molecule of any ideal gas depends only on absolute temperature: \(\overline{\varepsilon} = \frac{3}{2} k_{\mathrm{B}} T\). Since both gases are in thermal equilibrium at the same temperature \(T\):

\[ \frac{\overline{\varepsilon}_{\mathrm{He}}}{\overline{\varepsilon}_{\mathrm{O_2}}} = \frac{\frac{3}{2} k_{\mathrm{B}} T}{\frac{3}{2} k_{\mathrm{B}} T} = 1:1 \]

(ii) The rms speed is given by \(v_{\mathrm{rms}} = \sqrt{\frac{3 R T}{M_0}}\). Thus, \(v_{\mathrm{rms}} \propto \frac{1}{\sqrt{M_0}}\):

\[ \frac{v_{\mathrm{rms}}(\mathrm{He})}{v_{\mathrm{rms}}(\mathrm{O_2})} = \sqrt{\frac{M_0(\mathrm{O_2})}{M_0(\mathrm{He})}} = \sqrt{\frac{32}{4}} = \sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{2} \approx 2.83 \]

সমস্যা: একটি আবদ্ধ পাত্রে \(300\,\mathrm{K}\) তাপমাত্রায় হিলিয়াম (\(\mathrm{He}\), পারমাণবিক ভর \(4\,\mathrm{u}\)) ও অক্সিজেন (\(\mathrm{O_2}\), আণবিক ভর \(32\,\mathrm{u}\)) গ্যাসের মিশ্রণ রয়েছে। নির্ণয় করো: (i) \(\mathrm{He}\) এবং \(\mathrm{O_2}\)-এর অণুপ্রতি গড় চলন গতিশক্তির অনুপাত। (ii) এদের মূল গড় বর্গ বেগের অনুপাত \(v_{\mathrm{rms}}(\mathrm{He}) / v_{\mathrm{rms}}(\mathrm{O_2})\)।

সমাধান:

(i) যেকোনো আদর্শ গ্যাসের অণুপ্রতি গড় চলন গতিশক্তি কেবল তাপমাত্রার ওপর নির্ভর করে: \(\overline{\varepsilon} = \frac{3}{2} k_{\mathrm{B}} T\)। উভয় গ্যাস একই তাপমাত্রায় থাকায় গতিশক্তির অনুপাত \(1:1\)।
(ii) মূল গড় বর্গ বেগ \(v_{\mathrm{rms}} = \sqrt{\frac{3RT}{M_0}}\) হওয়ায়:

\[ \frac{v_{\mathrm{rms}}(\mathrm{He})}{v_{\mathrm{rms}}(\mathrm{O_2})} = \sqrt{\frac{32}{4}} = \sqrt{8} = 2\sqrt{2} \approx 2.83 \]
Worked Example 3: Mean Free Path and Collision Time Calculation

Problem: Estimate the mean free path and collision time for nitrogen molecules (\(\mathrm{N_2}\)) at temperature \(27\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\) and pressure \(1.0\,\mathrm{atm} \approx 1.01 \times 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}\). Assume the molecular diameter is \(d = 2.0\,\text{Å} = 2.0 \times 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{m}\) and the average speed is \(\langle v \rangle \approx 475\,\mathrm{m/s}\). (\(k_{\mathrm{B}} = 1.38 \times 10^{-23}\,\mathrm{J/K}\)).

Solution:

First, find the number density \(n\):

\[ n = \frac{P}{k_{\mathrm{B}} T} = \frac{1.01 \times 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}}{(1.38 \times 10^{-23}\,\mathrm{J/K})(300\,\mathrm{K})} = \frac{1.01 \times 10^5}{4.14 \times 10^{-21}} \approx 2.44 \times 10^{25}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}} \]

The mean free path \(\lambda\) is:

\[ \lambda = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2} n \pi d^2} = \frac{1}{1.414 \times (2.44 \times 10^{25}) \times \pi \times (2.0 \times 10^{-10})^2} \] \[ \lambda = \frac{1}{1.414 \times 2.44 \times 10^{25} \times 3.1416 \times 4.0 \times 10^{-20}} = \frac{1}{4.336 \times 10^6} \approx 2.3 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m} = 2300\,\text{Å} \]

The average time between successive collisions:

\[ \tau = \frac{\lambda}{\langle v \rangle} = \frac{2.3 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}}{475\,\mathrm{m/s}} \approx 4.84 \times 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{s} \]

Collision frequency \(\nu_c = 1/\tau \approx 2.07 \times 10^9\,\mathrm{collisions/s}\).

সমস্যা: \(27\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\) তাপমাত্রা ও \(1.0\,\mathrm{atm}\) (\(1.01 \times 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}\)) চাপে নাইট্রোজেন অণুর গড় মুক্ত পথ এবং দুটি সংঘর্ষের মধ্যবর্তী সময় নির্ণয় করো। অণুর ব্যাস \(d = 2.0\,\text{Å}\) এবং গড় বেগ \(\langle v \rangle \approx 475\,\mathrm{m/s}\)।

সমাধান:

সংখ্যা ঘনত্ব \(n = \frac{P}{k_{\mathrm{B}} T} = \frac{1.01 \times 10^5}{(1.38 \times 10^{-23})(300)} \approx 2.44 \times 10^{25}\,\mathrm{m^{-3}}\)।
গড় মুক্ত পথ:

\[ \lambda = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2} n \pi d^2} \approx 2.3 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m} = 2300\,\text{Å} \]

সংঘর্ষের মধ্যবর্তী গড় সময়:

\[ \tau = \frac{\lambda}{\langle v \rangle} = \frac{2.3 \times 10^{-7}}{475} \approx 4.84 \times 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{s} \]

সংঘর্ষ কম্পাঙ্ক \(\nu_c = 1/\tau \approx 2.07 \times 10^9\,\mathrm{s^{-1}}\)।

Practice Question 2

English: At what absolute temperature will the root-mean-square speed of an Oxygen molecule (\(\mathrm{O_2}\), molar mass \(32\,\mathrm{g\cdot mol^{-1}}\)) be equal to the root-mean-square speed of a Hydrogen molecule (\(\mathrm{H_2}\), molar mass \(2\,\mathrm{g\cdot mol^{-1}}\)) at \(-73\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\)?

  • (B) \(1600\,\mathrm{K}\)
  • (C) \(3200\,\mathrm{K}\)
  • (D) \(6400\,\mathrm{K}\)
Correct Answer: (C)

Worked Solution (English): The root-mean-square speed of a gas molecule is \(v_{\mathrm{rms}} = \sqrt{\frac{3 R T}{M_0}}\).
For Hydrogen (\(\mathrm{H_2}\)): \(T_1 = -73\,^\circ\mathrm{C} = 273 - 73 = 200\,\mathrm{K}\), and \(M_1 = 2\,\mathrm{g\cdot mol^{-1}}\).
For Oxygen (\(\mathrm{O_2}\)): \(M_2 = 32\,\mathrm{g\cdot mol^{-1}}\).

\[ \sqrt{\frac{3 R T_{\mathrm{O_2}}}{M_{\mathrm{O_2}}}} = \sqrt{\frac{3 R T_{\mathrm{H_2}}}{M_{\mathrm{H_2}}}} \implies \frac{T_{\mathrm{O_2}}}{32} = \frac{200}{2} \] \[ T_{\mathrm{O_2}} = 32 \times 100 = 3200\,\mathrm{K} \]

  • (C) \(3200\,\mathrm{K}\)

হাইড্রোজেনের ক্ষেত্রে তাপমাত্রা \(T_1 = 273 - 73 = 200\,\mathrm{K}\) এবং \(M_1 = 2\,\mathrm{g\cdot mol^{-1}}\)।

Practice Question 3

English: An ideal gas enclosed in a container of volume \(V\) exerts a pressure \(P\). If the kinetic energy per unit volume of the gas is denoted by \(E_V\), which of the following relations is correct?

  • (A) \(P = E_V\)
  • (B) \(P = \frac{1}{2} E_V\)
  • (C) \(P = \frac{2}{3} E_V\)
  • (D) \(P = \frac{3}{2} E_V\)
Correct Answer: (C)

Worked Solution (English): By kinetic theory, pressure is \(P = \frac{1}{3} \rho \overline{v^2} = \frac{1}{3} \frac{M}{V} \overline{v^2}\).
The total translational kinetic energy is \(E = \frac{1}{2} M \overline{v^2}\), so the kinetic energy per unit volume is \(E_V = \frac{E}{V} = \frac{1}{2} \rho \overline{v^2}\).
Expressing \(P\) in terms of \(E_V\):

\[ P = \frac{2}{3} \left(\frac{1}{2} \rho \overline{v^2}\right) = \frac{2}{3} E_V \]

Hence, the pressure is two-thirds of the translational kinetic energy density.

বাংলা (Bengali): \(V\) আয়তনের পাত্রে আবদ্ধ একটি আদর্শ গ্যাসের চাপ \(P\)। যদি গ্যাসের প্রতি একক আয়তনের গতিশক্তি \(E_V\) হয়, তবে নিচের কোন সম্পর্কটি সঠিক?

  • (A) \(P = E_V\)
  • (B) \(P = \frac{1}{2} E_V\)
  • (C) \(P = \frac{2}{3} E_V\)
  • (D) \(P = \frac{3}{2} E_V\)

বিশ্লেষণ ও সমাধান (বাংলা): গতিতত্ত্ব অনুযায়ী চাপ \(P = \frac{1}{3} \rho \overline{v^2}\)।
প্রতি একক আয়তনের চলন গতিশক্তি \(E_V = \frac{1}{2} \rho \overline{v^2}\)।

9. Common Pitfalls & High-Yield Revision Checklist | সাধারণ ভুলত্রুটি ও রিভিশন চেকলিস্ট

English

Critical Pitfalls in NEET UG:
  • Confusing Average Speed with RMS Speed: Remember that \(\overline{v^2} \neq (\overline{v})^2\). Thus, \(v_{\mathrm{rms}} > v_{\mathrm{avg}} > v_{\mathrm{mp}}\). In numerical problems, make sure you square the speeds first before taking the average when computing \(v_{\mathrm{rms}}\).
  • Temperature Units: Always convert Celsius to Kelvin (\(T = \theta + 273.15\)). Never substitute Celsius values into \(v_{\mathrm{rms}} = \sqrt{3RT/M}\) or \(E = \frac{3}{2}k_{\mathrm{B}}T\).
  • Mean Free Path with Constant Volume: If temperature increases at constant volume, the mean free path does not change, because number density \(n = N/V\) is constant. Only collision frequency increases!
  • Vibrational Modes: Remember that each vibrational mode contributes 2 degrees of freedom (\(k_{\mathrm{B}}T\) per mole, \(R\) to \(C_v\)), not 1.

High-Yield Checklist for NEET:

  • \(P = \frac{1}{3} \rho v_{\mathrm{rms}}^2 = \frac{2}{3} E_V\)
  • \(\overline{\varepsilon}_{\mathrm{trans}} = \frac{3}{2} k_{\mathrm{B}} T\) (per molecule, independent of mass)
  • \(v_{\mathrm{rms}} = \sqrt{\frac{3 R T}{M_0}} = \sqrt{\frac{3 P}{\rho}}\)
  • \(C_v = \frac{f}{2}R, \quad C_p = \left(\frac{f}{2} + 1\right)R, \quad \gamma = 1 + \frac{2}{f}\)
  • \(\gamma = 1.67\) (Monatomic), \(1.40\) (Rigid Diatomic), \(1.33\) (Non-linear Triatomic)
  • \(\lambda = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2} n \pi d^2} = \frac{k_{\mathrm{B}} T}{\sqrt{2} \pi d^2 P}\)
  • Dulong-Petit law for solids: \(C = 3R \approx 25\,\mathrm{J\cdot mol^{-1}\cdot K^{-1}}\)

10. Concise Final Recap | সংক্ষেপিত চূড়ান্ত সারসংক্ষেপ

English

The kinetic theory of gases provides an indispensable microscopic foundation for thermodynamics. Pressure originates from the rate of momentum transferred during elastic collisions of rapidly moving molecules with container walls. Absolute temperature represents the mean translational kinetic energy of molecules. The equipartition theorem elegantly distributes thermal energy equally (\(\frac{1}{2}k_{\mathrm{B}}T\) per quadratic mode) among translational, rotational, and vibrational degrees of freedom, precisely predicting the molar specific heat capacities of gases and solids. Finally, the mean free path explains transport properties, showing how finite molecular dimensions lead to microscopic scattering and macroscopic diffusion.
