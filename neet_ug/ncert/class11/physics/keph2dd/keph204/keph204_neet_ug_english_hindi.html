1. Introduction & Macroscopic Perspective | प्रस्तावना एवं स्थूल दृष्टिकोण

English

Thermodynamics is the macroscopic branch of physics dealing with heat, temperature, and the inter-conversion of thermal energy and mechanical work. Unlike mechanics—which tracks individual particles, external forces, torques, and center-of-mass motion—thermodynamics focuses on bulk systems in terms of measurable macroscopic state variables such as pressure \(P\), volume \(V\), temperature \(T\), mass \(m\), and composition.

Historical Development: In early physics, heat was erroneously considered an invisible fluid called caloric that flowed from hot to cold bodies. In 1798, Benjamin Thomson (Count Rumford) disproved the caloric theory while boring brass cannons; he observed that enormous heat was generated continuously by drill friction irrespective of drill sharpness, proving that heat is not a conserved fluid substance but a form of energy converted from mechanical work.

Macroscopic vs. Microscopic: While the kinetic theory of gases describes molecular velocities and degrees of freedom, pure thermodynamics describes bulk systems without requiring detailed assumptions about microscopic molecular constitution. When a speeding bullet penetrates a wooden target, its macroscopic mechanical kinetic energy converts into disordered internal kinetic energy, elevating the temperature of both the bullet and the wood.

हिन्दी

ऊष्मागतिकी (Thermodynamics) भौतिक विज्ञान की वह स्थूल (मैक्रोस्कोपिक) शाखा है जो ऊष्मा, ताप तथा ऊष्मीय ऊर्जा और यांत्रिक कार्य के अंतःरूपांतरण का अध्ययन करती है। यांत्रिकी के विपरीत—जहाँ बाह्य बलों, बल-आघूर्णों और द्रव्यमान केंद्र की गति पर विचार किया जाता है—ऊष्मागतिकी स्थूल चरों जैसे दाब \(P\), आयतन \(V\), ताप \(T\), द्रव्यमान \(m\) और संगठन के संदर्भ में केवल निकाय की आंतरिक अवस्था से संबंधित होती है।

ऐतिहासिक विकास: प्रारंभिक काल में ऊष्मा को कैलोरिक (caloric) नामक भारहीन द्रव माना जाता था जो गर्म वस्तु से ठंडी वस्तु की ओर बहता था। 1798 में बेंजामिन थॉमसन (काउंट रमफोर्ड) ने पीतल की तोपों को बोर करते समय देखा कि निरंतर घर्षण से असीमित ऊष्मा उत्पन्न होती है, चाहे ड्रिल की धार तीक्ष्ण हो या भोथरी। इससे कैलोरिक सिद्धांत अमान्य हो गया और यह सिद्ध हुआ कि ऊष्मा कार्य से उत्पन्न ऊर्जा का ही एक रूप है।

स्थूल बनाम सूक्ष्म दृष्टिकोण: जहाँ अणुगति सिद्धांत अणुओं के वेग वितरण और आणविक संरचना की विवेचना करता है, वहीं विशुद्ध ऊष्मागतिकी में आणविक विवरण की आवश्यकता नहीं होती। जब एक गतिशील गोली लकड़ी के गुटके में धँसकर रुक जाती है, तो उसकी बाह्य यांत्रिक गतिज ऊर्जा गोली और लकड़ी के अणुओं की अव्यवस्थित आंतरिक ऊर्जा में बदल जाती है, जिससे उनका ताप बढ़ जाता है।

2. Thermal Equilibrium & Zeroth Law | तापीय साम्यावस्था एवं ऊष्मागतिकी का शून्यवाँ नियम

English

Thermodynamic Equilibrium: A state is in thermodynamic equilibrium when its macroscopic variables (\(P, V, T, m\)) remain constant over time throughout the system. This requires simultaneous mechanical equilibrium (no unbalanced forces/pressure gradients), chemical equilibrium (no compositional change or spontaneous reactions), and thermal equilibrium (uniform and unchanging temperature).

Boundary Walls:

  • Adiabatic Wall: An insulating boundary that prevents heat/energy transfer between systems (\(\Delta Q = 0\)). Systems separated by an adiabatic wall remain at independent temperatures.
  • Diathermic Wall: A thermally conducting boundary that permits heat flow until the contacting bodies attain identical temperatures.
Zeroth Law of Thermodynamics: Two systems separately in thermal equilibrium with a third system are in thermal equilibrium with each other.

Zeroth Law of Thermodynamics (Formulated by R.H. Fowler, 1931):

"If two thermodynamic systems \(A\) and \(B\) are each in thermal equilibrium with a third system \(C\), then \(A\) and \(B\) are also in thermal equilibrium with each other." \[ T_A = T_C \quad \text{and} \quad T_B = T_C \implies T_A = T_B \]

Significance: The Zeroth Law formally defines and introduces temperature as a universal scalar state variable characterizing thermal equilibrium. Bodies in mutual thermal equilibrium possess identical values of temperature.

हिन्दी

तापीय साम्यावस्था (Thermal Equilibrium): कोई ऊष्मागतिक निकाय तब साम्यावस्था में होता है जब उसके सभी स्थूल चर (\(P, V, T, m\)) समय के साथ नियत रहते हैं। इसके अंतर्गत यांत्रिक साम्यावस्था (असंतुलित बलों का अभाव), रासायनिक साम्यावस्था (अभिक्रियाओं का अभाव), तथा तापीय साम्यावस्था (समस्त निकाय में समान व स्थिर ताप) सम्मिलित हैं।

सीमांत दीवारें (Boundary Walls):

  • रुद्धोष्म दीवार (Adiabatic Wall): ऊष्मारोधी दीवार जो निकाय और परिवेश के मध्य ऊष्मा का आदान-प्रदान पूर्णतः रोकती है (\(\Delta Q = 0\))।
  • ऊष्मापारगम्य दीवार (Diathermic Wall): ऊष्मा चालक दीवार जो ऊष्मा प्रवाह की अनुमति देती है, जिससे संपर्क में आने वाले निकाय समान ताप प्राप्त करते हैं।

ऊष्मागतिकी का शून्यवाँ नियम (R.H. Fowler, 1931):

"यदि दो निकाय \(A\) तथा \(B\) किसी तीसरे निकाय \(C\) के साथ पृथक-पृथक तापीय साम्यावस्था में हों, तो वे परस्पर भी तापीय साम्यावस्था में होते हैं।" \[ T_A = T_C \quad \text{तथा} \quad T_B = T_C \implies T_A = T_B \]

भौतिक महत्त्व: शून्यवाँ नियम ताप (Temperature) की अवधारणा को आधार प्रदान करता है। ताप वह भौतिक अदिश राशि है जिसका मान परस्पर तापीय साम्य में स्थित निकायों के लिए एकसमान होता है।

3. Heat, Work & Internal Energy | ऊष्मा, कार्य एवं आंतरिक ऊर्जा

English

Internal Energy (\(U\)): The microscopic energy stored within a thermodynamic system. It is the sum total of random translational, rotational, and vibrational kinetic energies of all molecules plus the intermolecular potential energy, measured in a reference frame where the system's center of mass is at rest. Bulk kinetic energy of motion is strictly excluded.

  • \(U\) is a state variable (state function): its value depends entirely on the current equilibrium state \((P, V, T)\), not on the path taken. For an ideal gas (where intermolecular forces are negligible), \(U\) depends exclusively on temperature: \(U = U(T)\).

Heat (\(Q\)) and Work (\(W\)): Modes of energy transfer across system boundaries.

  • Heat (\(Q\)): Energy transferred across a boundary purely due to a temperature difference between system and surroundings.
  • Work (\(W\)): Energy transferred by macroscopic mechanical means (e.g., piston movement) that do not depend on a temperature difference. For quasi-static boundary expansion against pressure \(P\): \[ \mathrm{d}W = P\,\mathrm{d}V \implies W = \int_{V_1}^{V_2} P\,\mathrm{d}V \]
  • Crucial Conceptual Rule: Heat and work are energy in transit (path functions). A system possesses internal energy, but it never possesses a specific "amount of heat" or "amount of work".
Two distinct pathways to change internal energy: thermal energy exchange (heat) and boundary displacement (work).
हिन्दी

आंतरिक ऊर्जा (\(U\)): किसी निकाय में आणविक स्तर पर संचित कुल ऊर्जा है। यह द्रव्यमान केंद्र के विरामावस्था तंत्र में अणुओं की यादृच्छिक गतिज ऊर्जाओं (स्थानांतरीय, घूर्णी, कंपन) तथा अंतर-आणविक स्थितिज ऊर्जाओं का योग होती है। निकाय की समग्र बाह्य गतिज ऊर्जा इसमें सम्मिलित नहीं होती।

  • \(U\) एक अवस्था चर (State Function) है: इसका मान केवल वर्तमान संतुलन अवस्था \((P, V, T)\) पर निर्भर करता है, पथ पर नहीं। आदर्श गैस हेतु \(U\) केवल ताप का फलन है: \(U = U(T)\)।

ऊष्मा (\(Q\)) एवं कार्य (\(W\)): ऊर्जा स्थानांतरण के दो रूप हैं।

  • ऊष्मा (\(Q\)): तापांतर के कारण निकाय और परिवेश की सीमा के आर-पार प्रवाहित होने वाली ऊर्जा।
  • कार्य (\(W\)): यांत्रिक साधनों (जैसे पिस्टन विस्थापन) द्वारा तापांतर के बिना स्थानांतरित ऊर्जा। परिसीमा कार्य: \[ \mathrm{d}W = P\,\mathrm{d}V \implies W = \int_{V_1}^{V_2} P\,\mathrm{d}V \]
  • महत्त्वपूर्ण नियम: ऊष्मा और कार्य संक्रमणकालीन ऊर्जा (Path Functions) हैं। निकाय में आंतरिक ऊर्जा विद्यमान होती है, परन्तु यह कहना वैज्ञानिक रूप से अर्थहीन है कि निकाय में "ऊष्मा की अमुक मात्रा" या "कार्य की मात्रा" संचित है।

4. First Law of Thermodynamics | ऊष्मागतिकी का प्रथम नियम

English

The First Law of Thermodynamics is the macroscopic statement of the universal principle of conservation of energy applied to thermal systems.

\[ \Delta Q = \Delta U + \Delta W \quad \text{or} \quad \Delta Q = \Delta U + P\,\Delta V \]
  • \(\Delta Q > 0\) when heat is added to the system; \(\Delta Q < 0\) when heat leaves the system.
  • \(\Delta W > 0\) when work is done by the system (expansion); \(\Delta W < 0\) when work is done on the system (compression).
  • \(\Delta U = U_f - U_i\) represents the change in internal energy. Even though \(\Delta Q\) and \(\Delta W\) are path-dependent, the difference \(\Delta Q - \Delta W = \Delta U\) is strictly path-independent.

Application to Phase Change (Boiling of Water): When \(1\,\mathrm{g}\) of liquid water at \(100^\circ\mathrm{C}\) evaporates into steam at \(1\,\mathrm{atm}\) (\(1.013 \times 10^5\,\mathrm{N/m^2}\)), its volume expands from \(V_l = 1\,\mathrm{cm^3}\) to \(V_g = 1671\,\mathrm{cm^3}\). Latent heat of vaporization \(L_v = 2256\,\mathrm{J/g}\).

\[ \Delta Q = 2256\,\mathrm{J} \] \[ \Delta W = P(V_g - V_l) = 1.013 \times 10^5 \times (1671 - 1) \times 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{m^3} \approx 169.2\,\mathrm{J} \] \[ \Delta U = \Delta Q - \Delta W = 2256\,\mathrm{J} - 169.2\,\mathrm{J} = 2086.8\,\mathrm{J} \] Takeaway for NEET: Over \(92.5\%\) of supplied latent heat is utilized in breaking intermolecular bonds (\(\Delta U\)), while only \(\approx 7.5\%\) is spent in performing external work (\(P\Delta V\)).
हिन्दी

ऊष्मागतिकी का प्रथम नियम ऊष्मीय निकायों पर लागू ऊर्जा संरक्षण का व्यापक नियम है।

\[ \Delta Q = \Delta U + \Delta W \quad \text{अथवा} \quad \Delta Q = \Delta U + P\,\Delta V \]
  • \(\Delta Q > 0\) जब निकाय को ऊष्मा दी जाती है; \(\Delta Q < 0\) जब निकाय से ऊष्मा निष्कासित होती है।
  • \(\Delta W > 0\) जब निकाय द्वारा कार्य किया जाता है (प्रसार); \(\Delta W < 0\) जब निकाय पर कार्य किया जाता है (संपीड़न)।
  • \(\Delta U = U_f - U_i\) आंतरिक ऊर्जा परिवर्तन है। यद्यपि \(\Delta Q\) और \(\Delta W\) पथ पर निर्भर करते हैं, परन्तु उनका अंतर \(\Delta Q - \Delta W = \Delta U\) पूर्णतः पथ-निरपेक्ष (State Function) होता है।

अवस्था परिवर्तन (जल का वाष्पन) में अनुप्रयोग: \(1\,\mathrm{atm}\) दाब (\(1.013 \times 10^5\,\mathrm{N/m^2}\)) पर \(100^\circ\mathrm{C}\) के \(1\,\mathrm{g}\) जल का आयतन \(V_l = 1\,\mathrm{cm^3}\) से वाष्प आयतन \(V_g = 1671\,\mathrm{cm^3}\) हो जाता है। वाष्पन की गुप्त ऊष्मा \(L_v = 2256\,\mathrm{J/g}\) है।

\[ \Delta Q = 2256\,\mathrm{J} \] \[ \Delta W = P(V_g - V_l) = 1.013 \times 10^5 \times 1670 \times 10^{-6} \approx 169.2\,\mathrm{J} \] \[ \Delta U = \Delta Q - \Delta W = 2256 - 169.2 = 2086.8\,\mathrm{J} \] NEET हेतु निष्कर्ष: प्रदत्त गुप्त ऊष्मा का \(92.5\%\) से अधिक भाग अंतराण्विक आकर्षण बलों को तोड़ने (\(\Delta U\)) में व्यय होता है, जबकि केवल \(\approx 7.5\%\) भाग बाह्य परिसीमा कार्य (\(P\Delta V\)) में प्रयुक्त होता है।

5. Specific Heat Capacity & Mayer's Relation | विशिष्ट ऊष्मा धारिता एवं मेयर का संबंध

English

Definitions:

  • Heat Capacity (\(S\)): Heat needed to raise system temperature by \(\Delta T\): \(S = \dfrac{\Delta Q}{\Delta T}\).
  • Specific Heat Capacity (\(s\)): Heat capacity per unit mass: \(s = \dfrac{1}{m}\dfrac{\Delta Q}{\Delta T}\quad (\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}K^{-1}})\).
  • Molar Specific Heat Capacity (\(C\)): Heat capacity per mole: \(C = \dfrac{1}{\mu}\dfrac{\Delta Q}{\Delta T}\quad (\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}})\).

Specific Heat of Water & Calorie Definition: The calorie was historically defined as the heat required to raise \(1\,\mathrm{g}\) of water by \(1^\circ\mathrm{C}\). Because specific heat of water varies slightly with temperature (minimum near \(37^\circ\mathrm{C}\) to \(40^\circ\mathrm{C}\)), the standard calorie is precisely fixed as the heat required to raise \(1\,\mathrm{g}\) of water from \(14.5^\circ\mathrm{C}\) to \(15.5^\circ\mathrm{C}\) at standard atmospheric pressure: \[ 1\,\mathrm{cal} = 4.186\,\mathrm{J} \implies s_{\text{water}} = 4186\,\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}K^{-1}} \]

Variation of specific heat capacity of water with temperature (at 1 atm).

Molar Heat Capacity of Solids (Dulong-Petit Law): In a solid lattice of \(N_A\) atoms vibrating in 3 dimensions, each atom has average energy \(3 k_B T\). Total molar internal energy \(U = 3 N_A k_B T = 3RT\). Neglecting thermal expansion (\(\Delta V \approx 0\)): \[ C = \frac{\Delta U}{\Delta T} = 3R \approx 3 \times 8.314 \approx 24.9\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}} \] This agrees with experimental values for metals (Al, Cu, Pb, Ag) at room temperature; light elements like carbon (diamond) form an exception due to quantum effects at room temperature.

Molar Heat Capacities of Ideal Gases & Mayer's Relation:

  • At constant volume: \(W = 0 \implies C_v = \left(\dfrac{\Delta Q}{\Delta T}\right)_v = \dfrac{\mathrm{d}U}{\mathrm{d}T}\).
  • At constant pressure: \(C_p = \left(\dfrac{\Delta Q}{\Delta T}\right)_p = \dfrac{\mathrm{d}U}{\mathrm{d}T} + P\left(\dfrac{\Delta V}{\Delta T}\right)_p\).
  • From \(PV = RT\) for \(1\,\mathrm{mol}\): \(P\left(\dfrac{\Delta V}{\Delta T}\right)_p = R\). Hence:
    \[ C_p - C_v = R \quad \text{(Mayer's Relation)} \] \[ \gamma = \frac{C_p}{C_v} > 1, \quad C_v = \frac{R}{\gamma - 1}, \quad C_p = \frac{\gamma R}{\gamma - 1} \]
हिन्दी

परिभाषाएँ:

  • ऊष्मा धारिता (\(S\)): निकाय का ताप \(\Delta T\) बढ़ाने हेतु आवश्यक ऊष्मा: \(S = \dfrac{\Delta Q}{\Delta T}\)।
  • विशिष्ट ऊष्मा धारिता (\(s\)): प्रति एकांक द्रव्यमान ऊष्मा धारिता: \(s = \dfrac{1}{m}\dfrac{\Delta Q}{\Delta T}\quad (\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}K^{-1}})\)।
  • मोलर विशिष्ट ऊष्मा धारिता (\(C\)): प्रति मोल ऊष्मा धारिता: \(C = \dfrac{1}{\mu}\dfrac{\Delta Q}{\Delta T}\quad (\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}})\)।

जल की विशिष्ट ऊष्मा एवं कैलोरी की मानक परिभाषा: ऐतिहासिक रूप से \(1\,\mathrm{g}\) जल का ताप \(1^\circ\mathrm{C}\) बढ़ाने हेतु आवश्यक ऊष्मा को \(1\,\mathrm{cal}\) कहा गया। चूँकि जल की विशिष्ट ऊष्मा ताप के साथ सूक्ष्म रूप से परिवर्तित होती है (\(37^\circ\mathrm{C}\) पर न्यूनतम), अतः मानक कैलोरी को \(1\,\mathrm{g}\) जल का ताप \(14.5^\circ\mathrm{C}\) से \(15.5^\circ\mathrm{C}\) तक बढ़ाने हेतु आवश्यक ऊष्मा के रूप में परिभाषित किया गया है: \[ 1\,\mathrm{cal} = 4.186\,\mathrm{J} \implies s_{\text{जल}} = 4186\,\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}K^{-1}} \]

ठोसों की मोलर ऊष्मा धारिता (डूलांग-पेटिट नियम): ठोस के प्रत्येक परमाणु की 3 विमाओं में दोलन हेतु औसत ऊर्जा \(3k_B T\) होती है। एक मोल हेतु \(U = 3 N_A k_B T = 3RT\)। ठोसों में तापीय प्रसार नगण्य होने के कारण (\(\Delta V \approx 0\)): \[ C = \frac{\Delta U}{\Delta T} = 3R \approx 24.9\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}} \] सामान्य ताप पर अधिकांश धातुएँ (Al, Cu, Ag, Pb) इसका पालन करती हैं, जबकि कार्बन (हीरा) अपवाद है।

गैसों की विशिष्ट ऊष्माएँ एवं मेयर का संबंध:

  • स्थिर आयतन पर: \(W = 0 \implies C_v = \dfrac{\mathrm{d}U}{\mathrm{d}T}\)।
  • स्थिर दाब पर: \(C_p = \dfrac{\mathrm{d}U}{\mathrm{d}T} + P\left(\dfrac{\Delta V}{\Delta T}\right)_p\)। चूँकि \(PV = RT \implies P\left(\dfrac{\Delta V}{\Delta T}\right)_p = R\)।
    \[ C_p - C_v = R \quad \text{(मेयर का सूत्र)} \] \[ \gamma = \frac{C_p}{C_v} > 1, \quad C_v = \frac{R}{\gamma - 1}, \quad C_p = \frac{\gamma R}{\gamma - 1} \]

6. State Variables, Intensive/Extensive & Quasi-Static Processes | अवस्था चर एवं स्थैतिक-कल्प प्रक्रम

English

State Variables: Macroscopic properties characterizing a system in thermodynamic equilibrium. They do not depend on system history.

  • Extensive Variables: Depend linearly on the size/mass of the system. If a system in equilibrium is halved by an imaginary divider, extensive variables are halved. Examples: Volume \(V\), Internal Energy \(U\), Mass \(M\), Enthalpy \(H\), Entropy \(S\).
  • Intensive Variables: Independent of the size/mass of the system; remain unchanged when the system is partitioned. Examples: Pressure \(P\), Temperature \(T\), Density \(\rho\), Specific heat \(s\).

Quasi-Static Process: A strictly idealized, infinitely slow thermodynamic transformation where the system remains infinitesimally close to thermal and mechanical equilibrium with its surroundings at every single stage. Real processes that proceed sufficiently slowly without turbulence, rapid expansion, or large thermal gradients approximate quasi-static conditions.

हिन्दी

अवस्था चर (State Variables): साम्यावस्था में स्थित निकाय के अभिलक्षणिक स्थूल गुण। ये निकाय के इतिहास या पथ पर निर्भर नहीं करते।

8. Second Law of Thermodynamics & Carnot Engine | द्वितीय नियम एवं कार्नो इंजन

English

While the First Law guarantees energy conservation, it places no constraint on the directional flow of heat. The Second Law of Thermodynamics dictates the natural arrow of time and prohibits the 100% conversion of heat into work in a cyclic engine.

  • Kelvin-Planck Statement: It is impossible to construct a heat engine operating in a cycle that absorbs heat from a single thermal reservoir and converts the entire amount of heat into work without rejecting any heat to a colder sink (\(\eta < 1\)).
  • Clausius Statement: It is impossible to construct a cyclic refrigerator whose sole result is the transfer of heat from a cooler body to a warmer body without the expenditure of external work (\(\mathrm{COP} \ne \infty\)).
  • These two statements are logically equivalent: violation of one implies violation of the other.

The Carnot Cycle: Sadi Carnot (1824) devised an idealized, fully reversible cyclic engine working between a hot reservoir at temperature \(T_1\) and a cold reservoir at temperature \(T_2\). It consists of 4 successive reversible steps:

  1. Step \(1 \to 2\) (Isothermal Expansion): System absorbs heat \(Q_1\) at \(T_1\). Work \(W_1 = Q_1 = \mu R T_1 \ln(V_2/V_1)\).
  2. Step \(2 \to 3\) (Adiabatic Expansion): Temperature falls from \(T_1\) to \(T_2\). Work \(W_2 = \dfrac{\mu R(T_1 - T_2)}{\gamma - 1}\).
  3. Step \(3 \to 4\) (Isothermal Compression): System rejects heat \(Q_2\) to sink at \(T_2\). Work done on gas: \(W_3 = Q_2 = \mu R T_2 \ln(V_3/V_4)\).
  4. Step \(4 \to 1\) (Adiabatic Compression): Temperature rises from \(T_2\) back to \(T_1\). Work done on gas: \(W_4 = \dfrac{\mu R(T_1 - T_2)}{\gamma - 1}\).
The 4-step reversible Carnot cycle represented on the P-V plane.

Using adiabatic relations \(T_1 V_2^{\gamma-1} = T_2 V_3^{\gamma-1}\) and \(T_1 V_1^{\gamma-1} = T_2 V_4^{\gamma-1}\), we obtain \(\dfrac{V_2}{V_1} = \dfrac{V_3}{V_4}\). Therefore:

\[ \frac{Q_1}{Q_2} = \frac{T_1}{T_2} \] \[ \eta = \frac{W}{Q_1} = 1 - \frac{Q_2}{Q_1} = 1 - \frac{T_2}{T_1} \]

Carnot's Theorem:

  • No heat engine operating between two given thermal reservoirs at temperatures \(T_1\) and \(T_2\) can have higher efficiency than a reversible Carnot engine operating between the same reservoirs (\(\eta_{\text{irreversible}} < \eta_{\text{Carnot}}\)).
  • The efficiency of a Carnot engine depends solely on the temperatures of the source and sink, and is strictly independent of the nature or construction of the working substance.

  1. चरण \(1 \to 2\) (समतापी प्रसार): \(T_1\) पर ऊष्मा \(Q_1\) का अवशोषण। कार्य \(W_1 = Q_1 = \mu R T_1 \ln(V_2/V_1)\)।

  4. चरण \(4 \to 1\) (रुद्धोष्म संपीड़न): ताप पुनः \(T_2\) से बढ़कर \(T_1\)। गैस पर कार्य \(W_4 = \dfrac{\mu R(T_1 - T_2)}{\gamma - 1}\)।
\[ \frac{Q_1}{Q_2} = \frac{T_1}{T_2} \] \[ \eta = \frac{W}{Q_1} = 1 - \frac{Q_2}{Q_1} = 1 - \frac{T_2}{T_1} \]

कार्नो प्रमेय (Carnot's Theorem):

  • किन्हीं दो दिए गए स्थिर तापों \(T_1\) व \(T_2\) के मध्य कार्य करने वाले किसी भी अउत्क्रमणीय इंजन की दक्षता उसी ताप परिसर में कार्य करने वाले उत्क्रमणीय कार्नो इंजन से अधिक नहीं हो सकती (\(\eta_{\text{irr}} < \eta_{\text{rev}}\))।
  • कार्नो इंजन की दक्षता केवल स्रोत और सिंक के परम तापों पर निर्भर करती है, कार्यकारी पदार्थ की प्रकृति पर नहीं।

Practice Question 1 (MCQ - Single Correct)

An ideal gas is taken through an isothermal expansion at temperature \(T\) from volume \(V\) to \(2V\), absorbing heat \(Q_1\). Another sample of identical mass of the same gas is taken through an adiabatic expansion from the same initial state \((P, V)\) to volume \(2V\), during which work done is \(W_2\). Which of the following statements is mathematically and physically correct?

(A) \(Q_1 < W_2\) and the final temperature in adiabatic process is higher than \(T\)
(B) \(Q_1 > W_2\) and the final temperature in adiabatic process is lower than \(T\)
(C) \(Q_1 = W_2\) because the volume change \(\Delta V = V\) is identical in both processes
(D) \(Q_1 > W_2\) and the final pressure in the adiabatic process is greater than in the isothermal process
Correct Answer: (B)
Explanation: In an isothermal process, \(\Delta U = 0\), so work done \(W_1 = Q_1 = \mu RT \ln(2V/V) = \mu RT \ln 2\). On the \(P\)-\(V\) diagram, the isothermal curve lies strictly above the adiabatic curve during expansion because the magnitude of adiabatic slope \(\left|\frac{\mathrm{d}P}{\mathrm{d}V}\right| = \gamma \frac{P}{V}\) is \(\gamma\) times steeper than isothermal slope \(\frac{P}{V}\). The area under the isothermal curve from \(V\) to \(2V\) is therefore greater than the area under the adiabatic curve: \(W_1 > W_2 \implies Q_1 > W_2\). Furthermore, in an adiabatic expansion, \(\Delta Q = 0 \implies \Delta U = -W_2 < 0\), causing the temperature to drop: \(T_f = T(V/2V)^{\gamma-1} < T\).

Practice Question 2 (Carnot Engine Efficiency)

A reversible Carnot engine operates between two temperatures \(T_1\) (source) and \(T_2\) (sink), having an efficiency of \(40\%\). When the temperature of the sink is decreased by \(50\,\mathrm{K}\), its efficiency increases to \(50\%\). The temperatures \(T_1\) and \(T_2\) of the source and sink are respectively:

(A) \(T_1 = 500\,\mathrm{K}, \; T_2 = 300\,\mathrm{K}\)
(B) \(T_1 = 600\,\mathrm{K}, \; T_2 = 360\,\mathrm{K}\)
(C) \(T_1 = 400\,\mathrm{K}, \; T_2 = 240\,\mathrm{K}\)
(D) \(T_1 = 650\,\mathrm{K}, \; T_2 = 390\,\mathrm{K}\)
Correct Answer: (A)
Explanation:
Efficiency of a Carnot engine: \(\eta = 1 - \dfrac{T_2}{T_1}\).
Case 1: \(0.40 = 1 - \dfrac{T_2}{T_1} \implies \dfrac{T_2}{T_1} = 0.60 \implies T_2 = 0.60\,T_1\).
Case 2: When sink temperature becomes \(T_2 - 50\): \[ 0.50 = 1 - \frac{T_2 - 50}{T_1} \implies \frac{T_2 - 50}{T_1} = 0.50 \implies T_2 - 50 = 0.50\,T_1 \] Subtracting Case 2 from Case 1: \[ (0.60\,T_1) - (0.50\,T_1) = 50 \implies 0.10\,T_1 = 50 \implies T_1 = \frac{50}{0.10} = 500\,\mathrm{K} \] Then, \(T_2 = 0.60 \times 500 = 300\,\mathrm{K}\).

Practice Question 3 (Assertion-Reason Type)

Given below are two statements: one is labelled as Assertion (A) and the other is labelled as Reason (R).

Assertion (A): When an ideal gas undergoes free expansion into an evacuated insulated container, its temperature remains unchanged, yet the process is completely irreversible.

Reason (R): In free expansion, the gas does zero boundary work against vacuum (\(W = 0\)), absorbs no heat (\(Q = 0\)), and its intermediate states are non-equilibrium states that cannot be plotted on a \(P\)-\(V\) diagram.

(A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A)
(B) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A)
(C) (A) is true but (R) is false
(D) (A) is false but (R) is true
Correct Answer: (A)
Explanation: In free expansion, the gas expands against zero external resisting pressure (\(P_{\text{ext}} = 0\)), so work done \(\Delta W = 0\). Since the container is insulated, \(\Delta Q = 0\). According to the First Law, \(\Delta U = \Delta Q - \Delta W = 0\). For an ideal gas, \(U = U(T)\), which implies \(\Delta T = 0\) (temperature remains constant). However, the expansion is uncontrolled, turbulent, and spontaneous, driving the gas through non-equilibrium states lacking well-defined \(P\) and \(T\). The gas will not spontaneously re-compress back into the initial chamber without external work; hence it is strictly irreversible. Thus, Reason (R) correctly explains both the constant temperature and the irreversible nature.

Practice Question 4 (First Law & Rate of Energy Transfer)

An electric heater supplies thermal energy to an enclosed gas at a constant rate of \(100\,\mathrm{W}\). Simultaneously, the gas expands against a movable piston performing mechanical work on the surroundings at a rate of \(75\,\mathrm{J/s}\). At what rate is the internal energy of the gas increasing?

(A) \(175\,\mathrm{W}\)
(B) \(75\,\mathrm{W}\)
(C) \(25\,\mathrm{W}\)
(D) \(-25\,\mathrm{W}\)
Correct Answer: (C)
Explanation:
From the First Law in rate form: \[ \frac{\mathrm{d}Q}{\mathrm{d}t} = \frac{\mathrm{d}U}{\mathrm{d}t} + \frac{\mathrm{d}W}{\mathrm{d}t} \] Given: \(\dfrac{\mathrm{d}Q}{\mathrm{d}t} = +100\,\mathrm{W}\) (heat added) and \(\dfrac{\mathrm{d}W}{\mathrm{d}t} = +75\,\mathrm{W}\) (work done by the system).
Therefore: \[ \frac{\mathrm{d}U}{\mathrm{d}t} = \frac{\mathrm{d}Q}{\mathrm{d}t} - \frac{\mathrm{d}W}{\mathrm{d}t} = 100 - 75 = 25\,\mathrm{W} \] The internal energy is increasing at a rate of \(25\,\mathrm{J/s}\) (or \(25\,\mathrm{W}\)).

11. Common Pitfalls & Revision Checklist | सामान्य त्रुटियाँ एवं पुनरावलोकन सूची

English

High-Yield Pitfalls for NEET Candidates:

  • Confusing Heat with Internal Energy: Never say "the gas has \(500\,\mathrm{J}\) of heat". A body possesses internal energy; heat is strictly energy in transit across boundaries driven by a temperature difference.
  • Sign Conventions in First Law: In Physics (NCERT/NEET convention), \(\Delta Q = \Delta U + \Delta W\). Here, \(W > 0\) for work done by the gas (expansion, \(\Delta V > 0\)), and \(W < 0\) for work done on the gas (compression, \(\Delta V < 0\)). Notice that Chemistry uses the IUPAC convention \(\Delta U = q + w\), where work done on the system is positive. Do not mix them!
  • Slope of Adiabatic vs. Isothermal: \(\left|\frac{\mathrm{d}P}{\mathrm{d}V}\right|_{\text{adiabatic}} = \gamma \left|\frac{\mathrm{d}P}{\mathrm{d}V}\right|_{\text{isothermal}}\). Because \(\gamma > 1\), the adiabatic curve drops faster during expansion and rises steeper during compression.
  • Free Expansion (\(P_{\text{ext}} = 0\)): In an insulated container, free expansion yields \(W = 0\), \(Q = 0\), \(\Delta U = 0\), and \(\Delta T = 0\) (for an ideal gas). But it is not quasi-static and not reversible!
  • Carnot Engine Temperature Units: Always convert temperatures to Kelvin (\(\mathrm{K}\)) when computing Carnot efficiency: \(\eta = 1 - \frac{T_2}{T_1}\). Never use Celsius!

Quick Revision Checklist:

  • Zeroth Law \(\implies\) Temperature exists and governs thermal equilibrium.
  • First Law \(\implies \Delta Q = \Delta U + W\); \(\Delta U\) is a state function; \(Q\) and \(W\) are path functions.
  • For 1 mole of ideal gas: \(C_p - C_v = R\); \(C_v = \frac{R}{\gamma - 1}\); \(C_p = \frac{\gamma R}{\gamma - 1}\).
  • Dulong-Petit law for solids at room temperature: \(C \approx 3R \approx 24.9\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}K^{-1}}\).
  • Work in isothermal process: \(W = \mu RT \ln(V_2/V_1)\).
  • Work in adiabatic process: \(W = \frac{\mu R(T_1 - T_2)}{\gamma - 1} = \frac{P_1V_1 - P_2V_2}{\gamma - 1}\).
  • Work in cyclic process = Area enclosed on \(P\)-\(V\) diagram (positive if clockwise, negative if counter-clockwise).
  • Carnot efficiency: \(\eta = 1 - \frac{T_2}{T_1} = \frac{W}{Q_1} = 1 - \frac{Q_2}{Q_1}\).

12. Final Recap | अंतिम संक्षेप

English

Thermodynamics links microscopic disorder with macroscopic variables. The Zeroth Law establishes temperature as the marker of thermal equilibrium. The First Law states energy conservation, balancing heat input, internal energy variation, and boundary work. State variables depend only on the current equilibrium state, classified into size-dependent extensive variables (\(V, U, M\)) and size-independent intensive variables (\(P, T, \rho\)). The Second Law imposes fundamental limits on energy conversion: heat cannot spontaneously flow from cold to hot without external work (Clausius), and no cyclic heat engine can convert heat entirely into work (Kelvin-Planck). The reversible Carnot engine defines the theoretical peak efficiency \(\eta = 1 - T_2/T_1\) for all thermal cycles.

ऊष्मागतिकी स्थूल अवस्था चरों के माध्यम से ऊर्जा रूपांतरण का अध्ययन करती है। शून्यवाँ नियम ताप की अवधारणा स्थापित करता है। प्रथम नियम ऊर्जा संरक्षण का नियम है, जो अवशोषित ऊष्मा, आंतरिक ऊर्जा परिवर्तन तथा बाह्य कार्य को संतुलित करता है। अवस्था चरों को विस्तीर्ण (\(V, U, M\)) तथा गहन (\(P, T, \rho\)) चरों में वर्गीकृत किया जाता है। द्वितीय नियम ऊर्जा प्रवाह की दिशा और रूपांतरण की सीमा निर्धारित करता है—ऊष्मा का स्वतः ठंडी से गर्म वस्तु की ओर प्रवाह असंभव है (क्लॉसियस), और किसी चक्रीय इंजन की दक्षता \(100\%\) नहीं हो सकती (केल्विन-प्लांक)। कार्नो इंजन किन्हीं दो तापों के मध्य कार्य करने वाले सभी इंजनों के लिए अधिकतम दक्षता \(\eta = 1 - T_2/T_1\) की सीमा निर्धारित करता है।
