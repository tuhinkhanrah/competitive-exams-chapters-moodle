Hydrocarbons: NEET UG Master Tutorial

हाइड्रोकार्बन: नीट (NEET UG) परीक्षा हेतु सम्पूर्ण एवं प्रामाणिक ट्यूटोरियल

1. Classification of Hydrocarbons / हाइड्रोकार्बन का वर्गीकरण

Hydrocarbons are binary organic compounds containing exclusively carbon and hydrogen atoms. They are classified into:

  • Acyclic (Aliphatic):
    • Saturated: Alkanes (\(C_n H_{2n+2}\)) possessing only single \(C-C\) and \(C-H\) \(\sigma\)-bonds.
    • Unsaturated: Alkenes (\(C_n H_{2n}\), containing \(C=C\)) and Alkynes (\(C_n H_{2n-2}\), containing \(C\equiv C\)).
  • Cyclic:
    • Alicyclic: Cycloalkanes and cycloalkenes possessing aliphatic chemical properties.
    • Aromatic (Arenes): Highly stable, planar, conjugated cyclic rings adhering to Hückel's \((4n+2)\pi\) electron rule (e.g., benzene, toluene).
हिंदी सारांश: हाइड्रोकार्बन केवल कार्बन तथा हाइड्रोजन से निर्मित यौगिक हैं। ये अचक्रीय (संतृप्त ऐल्केन तथा असंतृप्त ऐल्कीन व ऐल्काइन) और चक्रीय (ऐलीसाइक्लिक तथा हकल नियम का पालन करने वाले ऐरोमैटिक एरीन) में वर्गीकृत होते हैं।

2. Alkanes (Paraffins) / ऐल्केन (पैराफिन)

Alkanes (\(C_n H_{2n+2}\)) are saturated hydrocarbons characterised by inertness toward polar reagents under ambient conditions (hence named paraffins, meaning little affinity).

Structure, Bonding & Conformations

All carbon atoms are \(sp^3\) hybridised with tetrahedral geometry (bond angle \(109.5^\circ\)). The \(C-C\) single bond length is \(154\,\mathrm{pm}\) (\(348\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)) and \(C-H\) is \(112\,\mathrm{pm}\) (\(414\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)).

Conformations of Ethane: Rotation about the \(C-C\) \(\sigma\)-bond is hindered by an energy barrier of \(12.55\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\) (\(3.0\,\mathrm{kcal\,mol^{-1}}\)) termed torsional strain, caused by repulsive interactions between adjacent \(C-H\) bond electron clouds.

  • Eclipsed Conformation: Hydrogens align directly opposite each other (\(\theta = 0^\circ\)). Maximum torsional strain, least stable.
  • Staggered Conformation: Hydrogens are positioned maximally far apart (\(\theta = 60^\circ\)). Minimum repulsion, most stable (by \(12.55\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)).
  • Stability Order: \(\text{Staggered} > \text{Skew} > \text{Eclipsed}\). Thermal energy at room temperature rapidly interconverts conformers.
Structure Overview: Newman projections of ethane. Staggered is more stable by \(12.55\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\).
संरचना एवं संरूपण: एथेन में एकल बंध के चारों ओर घूर्णन मरोड़ी प्रतिबल (\(12.55\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)) के कारण बाधित होता है। सांतरित (Staggered, कोण \(60^\circ\)) रूप ग्रस्त (Eclipsed, कोण \(0^\circ\)) से अधिक स्थायी होता है।

Physical Properties

Boiling Point: Increases with molecular mass (stronger dispersion forces). As branching increases, the molecular shape becomes spherical and surface area decreases, leading to lower boiling points: \(n\text{-pentane} > \text{isopentane} > \text{neopentane}\).

Melting Point: Neopentane has an unusually high melting point (\(256\,\mathrm{K}\)) compared to isopentane (\(113\,\mathrm{K}\)) due to close packing of spherical molecules in the crystal lattice.

Preparation Methods

  1. Catalytic Hydrogenation: \(R-CH=CH_2 + H_2 \xrightarrow{Pt/Pd\text{ or }Ni,\,\Delta} R-CH_2-CH_3\)
  2. Reduction of Alkyl Halides: \(R-X + Zn + H^+ \longrightarrow R-H + Zn^{2+} + X^-\)
  3. Wurtz Reaction: \(2R-X + 2Na \xrightarrow{\text{dry ether}} R-R + 2NaX\) (Best for symmetrical alkanes with even carbon count; two different halides yield a 3-alkane mixture; methane cannot be made).
  4. Decarboxylation (Dumas): \(R-COONa + NaOH \xrightarrow{CaO,\,\Delta} R-H + Na_2CO_3\) (Alkane has one less carbon).
  5. Kolbe's Electrolysis: \(2RCOO^- Na^+ + 2H_2O \xrightarrow{\text{electrolysis}} R-R + 2CO_2\text{ (Anode)} + H_2\text{ (Cathode)} + 2NaOH\) (Solution becomes basic, \(pH\) rises).

Chemical Properties

  • Free Radical Chlorination Mechanism:
    1. Initiation: \(Cl_2 \xrightarrow{h\nu} 2\,Cl^\bullet\) (Homolytic bond fission).
    2. Propagation: \(CH_4 + Cl^\bullet \longrightarrow CH_3^\bullet + HCl\) (Rate-determining step); \(CH_3^\bullet + Cl_2 \longrightarrow CH_3Cl + Cl^\bullet\).
    3. Termination: Coupling of radicals (\(Cl^\bullet + Cl^\bullet \to Cl_2\); \(CH_3^\bullet + Cl^\bullet \to CH_3Cl\); \(CH_3^\bullet + CH_3^\bullet \to C_2H_6\)). Formation of trace ethane confirms methyl free radicals.
  • Controlled Oxidation:
    • \(2CH_4 + O_2 \xrightarrow{Cu\,/\,523\,\mathrm{K},\,100\,\mathrm{atm}} 2CH_3OH\)
    • \(CH_4 + O_2 \xrightarrow{Mo_2O_3,\,\Delta} HCHO + H_2O\)
    • \((CH_3)_3CH \xrightarrow{[O],\,KMnO_4} (CH_3)_3C-OH\) (Selective oxidation of tertiary hydrogen).
  • Aromatisation: \(CH_3(CH_2)_4CH_3 \xrightarrow[773\,\mathrm{K},\,10-20\,\mathrm{atm}]{Cr_2O_3\,/\,Al_2O_3} C_6H_6\text{ (Benzene)} + 4H_2\)

3. Alkenes (Olefins) / ऐल्कीन

Alkenes (\(C_n H_{2n}\)) possess a carbon-carbon double bond (\(C=C\)) consisting of one strong \(\sigma\)-bond (\(397\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)) and one lateral \(\pi\)-bond (\(284\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)). The \(C=C\) bond length is \(134\,\mathrm{pm}\). The exposed, loosely held \(\pi\)-electrons act as a nucleophile, directing alkenes predominantly toward electrophilic addition reactions.

Geometrical Isomerism (cis-trans)

Restricted rotation around the \(C=C\) \(\pi\)-bond produces stereoisomers in alkenes of the form \(RCH=CHR'\):

  • cis-isomer: Identical groups on the same side. Vector dipoles reinforce (\(\mu > 0\)), giving higher polarity and a higher boiling point.
  • trans-isomer: Identical groups on opposite sides. Vector dipoles oppose (\(\mu \approx 0\)). Symmetrical packing in the crystal lattice gives it a higher melting point and lower solubility than the cis-isomer.
Stereochemical Comparison: Geometrical isomers of but-2-ene and dipole moments.

Preparation of Alkenes

  • From Alkynes:
    • cis-alkene: Hydrogenation over Lindlar's catalyst (\(H_2, Pd/BaSO_4 + \text{quinoline}\)).
    • trans-alkene: Birch reduction with \(Na\) in liquid \(NH_3\) at \(195\,\mathrm{K}\).
  • Dehydrohalogenation of Alkyl Halides (\(\beta\)-Elimination): Heating with alc. \(KOH\). Follows Saytzeff's Rule: The more substituted (hyperconjugatively stabilised) alkene is the major product.
  • Dehalogenation of Vicinal Dihalides: \(BrCH_2-CH_2Br + Zn \xrightarrow{\Delta} CH_2=CH_2 + ZnBr_2\).

Electrophilic Addition Reactions

1. Markovnikov's Rule

In the addition of an unsymmetrical reagent (\(HX\)) to an unsymmetrical alkene, the negative part attaches to the doubly bonded carbon carrying fewer hydrogens. Electrophilic addition begins with \(H^+\) addition to yield the more stable carbocation intermediate (\(3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ\)):

\[CH_3-CH=CH_2 + H^+ \longrightarrow CH_3-\overset{+}{C}H-CH_3\text{ (stable }2^\circ\text{ carbocation)} \xrightarrow{Br^-} CH_3-CH(Br)-CH_3\]

2. Kharasch Peroxide Effect (Anti-Markovnikov Addition)

In the presence of organic peroxides (e.g., benzoyl peroxide), \(HBr\) only adds contrary to Markovnikov's rule, generating 1-bromopropane via a free-radical chain mechanism.

Why only HBr? (Thermodynamic Explanation)

Both propagation steps (addition of \(X^\bullet\) to the alkene and abstraction of \(H\) from \(H-X\)) must be exothermic (\(\Delta H < 0\)). For \(HCl\), the \(H-Cl\) bond is too strong (\(430.5\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)), making the second step endothermic. For \(HI\), the \(H-I\) bond is weak, but \(I^\bullet\) radicals rapidly couple into \(I_2\) (\(2I^\bullet \to I_2\)), making the first step endothermic. Only for \(HBr\) are both steps exothermic. Hence, \(HCl\) and \(HI\) do not exhibit the peroxide effect.

3. Baeyer's Test & Ozonolysis

  • Baeyer's Test: Decolourisation of cold, dilute neutral/alkaline \(KMnO_4\) (pink to brown \(MnO_2\)) with the formation of vicinal glycols tests for unsaturation: \[CH_2=CH_2 + H_2O + [O] \xrightarrow{\text{cold dil. }KMnO_4} CH_2(OH)-CH_2(OH)\]
  • Ozonolysis: Cleavage with \(O_3\) followed by \(Zn / H_2O\) identifies double bond position: \[R_2C=CHR' \xrightarrow{O_3} \text{Ozonide} \xrightarrow{Zn\,/\,H_2O} R_2C=O + R'CHO + ZnO\]

4. Alkynes / ऐल्काइन

Alkynes (\(C_n H_{2n-2}\)) contain a \(C\equiv C\) triple bond comprising one coaxial \(\sigma\)-bond and two mutually perpendicular \(\pi\)-bonds, with linear geometry (\(180^\circ\)). The \(C\equiv C\) bond length is \(120\,\mathrm{pm}\) (\(823\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)).

Orbital Representation: Cylindrical \(\pi\)-electron sheath and linear geometry of ethyne.

Preparation Methods

  1. From Calcium Carbide: \(CaC_2 + 2H_2O \longrightarrow Ca(OH)_2 + HC\equiv CH \uparrow\)
  2. From Vicinal Dihalides: \[CH_2Br-CH_2Br \xrightarrow{\text{alc. }KOH} CH_2=CHBr \xrightarrow{NaNH_2} HC\equiv CH\] Note: Sodamide (\(NaNH_2\)) is required in the second step to eliminate \(HBr\) from the unreactive vinyl halide.

Acidic Character of Terminal Alkynes

The \(sp\) hybridised carbon in alkynes has \(50\%\) \(s\)-character, rendering it significantly more electronegative than \(sp^2\) (\(33.3\%\)) or \(sp^3\) (\(25\%\)) carbon. Consequently, the \(C-H\) bond in terminal alkynes is strongly polarised, and hydrogen can be abstracted by strong bases like \(Na\) metal or \(NaNH_2\):

\[HC\equiv CH + Na \longrightarrow HC\equiv C^- Na^+ + \frac{1}{2}H_2 \uparrow\]
Relative Acidity Order: \(HC\equiv CH > CH_3-C\equiv CH \gg CH_2=CH_2 > CH_3-CH_3\)
\(H_2O > ROH > HC\equiv CH > NH_3 > CH_4\)

Key Reactions of Alkynes

  • Hydration (Kucherov Reaction): Alkynes add water in the presence of \(dil. H_2SO_4 + HgSO_4\) at \(333\,\mathrm{K}\). Ethyne yields ethanal; all higher terminal and internal alkynes yield ketones: \[HC\equiv CH + H_2O \xrightarrow[333\,\mathrm{K}]{HgSO_4\,/\,H_2SO_4} [CH_2=CH-OH] \xrightarrow{\text{tautomerises}} CH_3-CHO\] \[CH_3-C\equiv CH + H_2O \xrightarrow{Hg^{2+}\,/\,H^+} [CH_3-C(OH)=CH_2] \xrightarrow{\text{tautomerises}} CH_3-CO-CH_3\]
  • Cyclic Trimerisation: Passing ethyne through a red-hot iron tube at \(873\,\mathrm{K}\) synthesises benzene: \[3\,HC\equiv CH \xrightarrow{\text{Red hot Fe tube},\,873\,\mathrm{K}} C_6H_6\text{ (Benzene)}\]

5. Aromatic Hydrocarbons (Arenes) / ऐरोमैटिक हाइड्रोकार्बन

Benzene (\(C_6H_6\)) is a planar regular hexagonal molecule where all six \(C-C\) bonds are equal (\(139\,\mathrm{pm}\)), intermediate between single (\(154\,\mathrm{pm}\)) and double (\(134\,\mathrm{pm}\)) bonds. The resonance stabilisation energy of benzene is \(150.6\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\) (\(36\,\mathrm{kcal\,mol^{-1}}\)).

Aromaticity (Hückel's Rule)

A planar, cyclic, conjugated ring system possessing \((4n+2)\pi\) electrons (\(n = 0, 1, 2, 3, \dots\)) is aromatic. Benzene has \(6\pi\) electrons (\(n=1\)); cyclooctatetraene has \(8\pi\) electrons (\(4n\)) and avoids antiaromaticity by adopting a non-planar tub-shaped conformation, making it non-aromatic.

Reaction Pathway: Three-step \(S_E\mathrm{Ar}\) mechanism of benzene via the arenium ion (\(\sigma\)-complex).

Electrophilic Aromatic Substitution (\(S_E\mathrm{Ar}\)) Mechanism

  1. Generation of Electrophile (\(E^+\)):
    • Nitration: \(HNO_3 + 2H_2SO_4 \rightleftharpoons NO_2^+ + H_3O^+ + 2HSO_4^-\) (Electrophile: \(NO_2^+\)).
    • Halogenation: \(Cl_2 + AlCl_3 \rightleftharpoons Cl^+ + [AlCl_4]^-\) (Electrophile: \(Cl^+\)).
    • Sulphonation: \(2H_2SO_4 \rightleftharpoons SO_3 + H_3O^+ + HSO_4^-\) (Neutral electrophile: \(SO_3\)).
  2. Formation of Arenium Ion (\(\sigma\)-Complex / Wheland Intermediate): Electrophile attacks the ring, disrupting aromaticity and forming an \(sp^3\) hybridised carbon. The carbocation is resonance-stabilised across ortho and para carbons. This is the slow, rate-determining step.
  3. Loss of Proton: Fast abstraction of the proton from the \(sp^3\) carbon by a base restores the aromatic sextet.

Friedel-Crafts Reactions & Directive Influence

Alkylation: \(C_6H_6 + CH_3Cl \xrightarrow{\text{anhyd. }AlCl_3} C_6H_5-CH_3 + HCl\). When 1-chloropropane is used, the \(1^\circ\) propyl carbocation rearranges via a 1,2-hydride shift to the \(2^\circ\) isopropyl carbocation; the major product is cumene (isopropylbenzene).

Acylation: \(C_6H_6 + CH_3COCl \xrightarrow{\text{anhyd. }AlCl_3} C_6H_5-COCH_3 + HCl\). The acylium ion (\(CH_3-\overset{+}{C}=O\)) is resonance-stabilised; hence, no rearrangement occurs.

Carcinogenicity and Toxicity

Polynuclear aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) with more than two fused benzene rings (e.g., 1,2-benzanthracene, 3-methylcholanthrene, 1,2-benzpyrene) are formed by incomplete combustion of coal, petroleum, and tobacco smoke. In vivo, they are metabolised into reactive diol-epoxides that covalently bind to DNA guanine bases, inducing mutations and oncogenesis.

6. High-Yield Worked Examples / हल किए गए उदाहरण

Example 1: Alkene Ozonolysis Deduction / ओजोनीकरण द्वारा संरचना निर्धारण

Problem: An alkene 'A' on reductive ozonolysis (\(O_3\), then \(Zn/H_2O\)) produces propanone and ethanal in 1:1 molar ratio. Determine 'A' and state if it exhibits geometrical isomerism.

Solution:

Align the two carbonyl products: \((CH_3)_2C=O\) and \(O=CH-CH_3\). Remove oxygens and connect carbons: \((CH_3)_2C=CH-CH_3\) (2-Methylbut-2-ene). Carbon-2 holds two identical methyl groups; therefore, it cannot exhibit geometrical isomerism.

Example 2: Friedel-Crafts Carbocation Rearrangement / फ्रीडेल-क्राफ्ट्स पुनर्विन्यास

Problem: Predict the major product when benzene is treated with 1-chloropropane in the presence of anhydrous \(AlCl_3\).

Solution:

1-Chloropropane reacts with \(AlCl_3\) to form a \(1^\circ\) carbocation (\(CH_3CH_2\overset{+}{C}H_2\)), which spontaneously undergoes a 1,2-hydride shift to generate the more stable \(2^\circ\) isopropyl carbocation (\(CH_3-\overset{+}{C}H-CH_3\)). Electrophilic attack on benzene yields cumene (isopropylbenzene) as the major product.

Example 3: Aromaticity Verification / ऐरोमैटिकता का निर्धारण

Problem: Classify (i) Cyclopentadienyl anion and (ii) Cyclooctatetraene as aromatic, antiaromatic, or non-aromatic.

Solution:

(i) Cyclopentadienyl anion: Cyclic, planar, conjugated with two \(\pi\)-bonds and one negative-charge lone pair \(= 6\pi\) electrons. Matches \(4n+2\) (\(n=1\)) \(\implies\) Aromatic.
(ii) Cyclooctatetraene: Contains \(8\pi\) electrons (\(4n\)). To avoid antiaromatic destabilisation, it puckers into a non-planar tub conformation \(\implies\) Non-aromatic.

7. NEET UG Practice Questions / नीट (NEET UG) अभ्यास प्रश्न

Practice Question 1 (NEET Single-Correct MCQ)
Which of the following alkanes cannot be synthesised in good yield by the Wurtz reaction?
(A) n-Hexane
(B) 2,3-Dimethylbutane
(C) n-Heptane
(D) n-Butane
Correct Answer: (C) n-Heptane

Explanation: The Wurtz reaction gives high yields for symmetrical alkanes with an even number of carbons. Synthesising an odd-carbon alkane like n-heptane requires two different alkyl halides (\(C_3\) and \(C_4\)), which produces an inseparable mixture of three alkanes (\(C_6H_{14}\), \(C_7H_{16}\), \(C_8H_{18}\)).

अभ्यास प्रश्न 1 (हिंदी रूपांतरण)
निम्नलिखित में से किस ऐल्केन को वुर्ट्ज़ अभिक्रिया द्वारा उत्तम लब्धि में नहीं बनाया जा सकता है?
(A) n-हेक्सेन
(B) 2,3-डाइमेथिलब्यूटेन
(C) n-हेप्टेन
(D) n-ब्यूटेन
सही उत्तर: (C) n-हेप्टेन

विस्तृत हल: विषम कार्बन संख्या वाले ऐल्केन (n-हेप्टेन) बनाने के लिए दो भिन्न ऐल्किल हैलाइड लेने पड़ते हैं, जिससे तीन ऐल्केनों का जटिल मिश्रण प्राप्त होता है जिसे पृथक करना कठिन होता है।

Practice Question 2 (NEET Assertion-Reason)
Assertion (A): Chlorobenzene is less reactive than benzene toward electrophilic aromatic substitution, but directs incoming electrophiles to ortho and para positions.
Reason (R): The strong \(-I\) effect of chlorine deactivates the ring overall, but its \(+R\) resonance effect selectively stabilises the arenium ion formed at ortho and para positions.
(A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A).
(B) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A).
(C) (A) is true but (R) is false.
(D) (A) is false but (R) is true.
Correct Answer: (A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A).

Explanation: In halobenzenes, inductive withdrawal (\(-I\)) outweighs resonance donation (\(+R\)), decreasing net ring electron density (deactivation). However, during ortho/para attack, chlorine's lone pair directly participates in resonance, lowering activation energy for those pathways.

अभिकथन (A): क्लोरोबेंजीन, इलेक्ट्रॉनरागी प्रतिस्थापन के प्रति बेंजीन से कम क्रियाशील है परन्तु यह ऑर्थो-पैरा निर्देशक है।

विस्तृत हल: क्लोरीन में \(-I > +R\) होने से वलय निष्क्रिय होता है, परन्तु ऑर्थो तथा पैरा आक्रमण में अनुनादी स्थायित्व मिलने के कारण यह ऑर्थो-पैरा निर्देशक होता है।

Practice Question 3 (NEET Statement-Based MCQ)
Statement I: Addition of \(HBr\) to propene in the presence of benzoyl peroxide yields 1-bromopropane.
Statement II: Under identical conditions, \(HCl\) and \(HI\) also give anti-Markovnikov addition products.
(A) Both Statement I and Statement II are correct.
(B) Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect.
(C) Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect.
(D) Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct.
Correct Answer: (C) Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect.

Explanation: The peroxide effect is observed exclusively with \(HBr\). For \(HCl\), the \(H-Cl\) bond is too strong for radical propagation. For \(HI\), iodine radicals recombine into \(I_2\) rather than adding to the alkene. Hence, \(HCl\) and \(HI\) always add via the Markovnikov rule.

कथन II: समान परिस्थितियों में \(HCl\) तथा \(HI\) भी ऐन्टी-मार्कोनीकॉफ योगज उत्पाद देते हैं।
(A) कथन I और II दोनों सही हैं।
(B) कथन I और II दोनों गलत हैं।
(C) कथन I सही है परन्तु कथन II गलत है।
(D) कथन I गलत है परन्तु कथन II सही है।
सही उत्तर: (C) कथन I सही है परन्तु कथन II गलत है।

विस्तृत हल: परॉक्साइड प्रभाव केवल \(HBr\) द्वारा दर्शाया जाता है। उष्मागतिकीय कारणों से \(HCl\) तथा \(HI\) मार्कोनीकॉफ नियम का ही पालन करते हैं।

(D) a \(\to\) i, b \(\to\) ii, c \(\to\) iii, d \(\to\) iv
Correct Answer: (A) a \(\to\) iii, b \(\to\) iv, c \(\to\) i, d \(\to\) ii

Explanation: Lindlar's catalyst gives cis-alkene (iii); Birch reduction gives trans-alkene (iv); Kucherov hydration of propyne gives propanone (i); Aromatisation of n-hexane uses \(Cr_2O_3/Al_2O_3\) (ii).

अभ्यास प्रश्न 4 (हिंदी रूपांतरण)
स्तंभ I का स्तंभ II से सही मिलान कीजिए।
(A) a \(\to\) iii, b \(\to\) iv, c \(\to\) i, d \(\to\) ii
(B) a \(\to\) iv, b \(\to\) iii, c \(\to\) i, d \(\to\) ii
(C) a \(\to\) iii, b \(\to\) iv, c \(\to\) ii, d \(\to\) i
(D) a \(\to\) i, b \(\to\) ii, c \(\to\) iii, d \(\to\) iv
सही उत्तर: (A) a \(\to\) iii, b \(\to\) iv, c \(\to\) i, d \(\to\) ii

विस्तृत हल: लिंडलर उत्प्रेरक सिस-उत्पाद देता है; बर्च अपचयन ट्रांस-उत्पाद देता है; कुचेरोव अभिक्रिया कीटोन बनाती है; तथा क्रोमिया उत्प्रेरक ऐरोमैटिकरण करता है।

8. Common Pitfalls & Revision Checklist / सावधानियाँ एवं चेकलिस्ट

Frequent NEET Traps:
  • Kolbe's Gas Distribution: Anode evolves Alkane + \(CO_2\); Cathode evolves \(H_2\); Solution pH becomes alkaline.
  • Terminal Alkyne Acidity: Only terminal alkynes (\(R-C\equiv CH\)) react with \(Na\) or \(NaNH_2\). Non-terminal alkynes (like but-2-yne) do not react.
  • Peroxide Selectivity: Operates strictly for \(HBr\). With \(HCl\) or \(HI\), always apply Markovnikov's rule.
  • Halobenzene Directive Effect: Halogens are deactivating yet ortho/para-directing.
Rapid Revision Points:
  • ☑ Staggered ethane is more stable than eclipsed by \(12.55\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\) (torsional barrier).
  • ☑ Lindlar's catalyst (\(Pd/BaSO_4 + \text{quinoline}\)) \(\to\) cis-alkene; Birch reduction (\(Na/liq. NH_3\)) \(\to\) trans-alkene.
  • ☑ Hydration of ethyne yields ethanal; all other alkynes yield ketones.
  • ☑ Hückel's Rule requires a planar, cyclic, conjugated ring with \((4n+2)\pi\) electrons.

9. Concise Final Recap / अंतिम सारांश

Hydrocarbons form the bedrock of organic chemistry for NEET UG:

  • Alkanes: Saturated \(sp^3\) systems undergoing free radical substitution; boiling points decrease with branching; conformations governed by torsional strain.
  • Alkenes: Planar \(sp^2\) olefins exhibiting cis-trans isomerism; undergo electrophilic addition (Markovnikov) and peroxide-assisted radical addition (\(HBr\) only); cleaved by ozonolysis.
  • Alkynes: Linear \(sp\) systems; terminal alkynes are acidic; undergo Kucherov hydration to carbonyls and cyclic trimerisation to benzene.
  • Arenes: Aromatic \((4n+2)\pi\) rings undergoing electrophilic substitution via the arenium ion (\(\sigma\)-complex); halogens are deactivating yet ortho/para-directing.
  • Carcinogenicity: Polynuclear aromatic hydrocarbons (PAHs) from incomplete combustion form covalent DNA adducts inducing cancer.
अंतिम सारांश: यह ट्यूटोरियल ऐल्केन, ऐल्कीन, ऐल्काइन तथा ऐरोमैटिक यौगिकों की संरचना, विरचन, अभिक्रियाओं, इलेक्ट्रॉनिक क्रियाविधियों तथा कैंसरजन्यता का संपूर्ण NEET UG पाठ्यक्रम आच्छादित करता है।
