1. Learning Objectives & Big-Picture Overview

English

Chemical transformations that involve a simultaneous change in the oxidation states of reacting species are called redox reactions. From cellular respiration and fuel combustion to metal extraction, batteries, corrosion, and environmental phenomena such as hydrogen fuel systems and stratospheric ozone depletion, redox chemistry underpins both biological and industrial processes.

By completing this tutorial, you will be able to:

  • Identify oxidation, reduction, oxidants (oxidising agents), and reductants (reducing agents) using classical, electronic, and oxidation-number models.
  • Assign oxidation numbers accurately, including tricky cases such as peroxides, superoxides, metal hydrides, and species with fractional average oxidation states.
  • Classify redox reactions into combination, decomposition, displacement, and disproportionation types.
  • Balance complex ionic redox equations in acidic and basic media via both the oxidation-number and half-reaction methods.
  • Connect redox reactions to titrimetric indicators and electrochemical cells using standard electrode potentials (\(E^\circ\)).
हिन्दी

वे रासायनिक रूपांतरण जिनमें अभिकारक प्रजातियों की ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाओं में एक साथ परिवर्तन होता है, रेडॉक्स (अपचयोपचय) अभिक्रियाएँ कहलाती हैं। कोशिकीय श्वसन और ईंधन के दहन से लेकर धातु निष्कर्षण, बैटरियों, संक्षारण तथा हाइड्रोजन ईंधन प्रणालियों और ओज़ोन क्षरण जैसी पर्यावरणीय घटनाओं तक, रेडॉक्स रसायन जैविक और औद्योगिक दोनों प्रक्रियाओं का आधार है।

इस ट्यूटोरियल को पूरा करने के बाद, आप सक्षम होंगे:

  • चिरसम्मत, इलेक्ट्रॉनिक और ऑक्सीकरण-संख्या मॉडलों का उपयोग करके ऑक्सीकरण, अपचयन, ऑक्सीकारक और अपचायक की पहचान करने में।
  • परॉक्साइड, सुपरऑक्साइड, धातु हाइड्राइड और भिन्नात्मक औसत ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाओं वाले जटिल यौगिकों सहित ऑक्सीकरण संख्या की सटीक गणना करने में।
  • रेडॉक्स अभिक्रियाओं को संयोजन, अपघटन, विस्थापन और असमानुपातन (disproportionation) प्रकारों में वर्गीकृत करने में।
  • ऑक्सीकरण-संख्या विधि और अर्ध-अभिक्रिया विधि दोनों द्वारा अम्लीय एवं क्षारीय माध्यम में जटिल आयनिक रेडॉक्स समीकरणों को संतुलित करने में।
  • मानक इलेक्ट्रोड विभव (\(E^\circ\)) के माध्यम से रेडॉक्स अभिक्रियाओं को अनुमापन सूचकों और विद्युत-रासायनिक सेलों से जोड़ने में।

2. Three Conceptual Models of Oxidation & Reduction

2.1 Classical & Electronic Perspectives

English

Historically, chemists defined redox changes through observable elemental additions and removals, and later unified them through electron transfer:

  • Classical Oxidation: Addition of oxygen or any more electronegative element to a substance, or removal of hydrogen or any electropositive element from a substance. For example, burning sulphur in dioxygen (\(\mathrm{S} + \mathrm{O_2} \rightarrow \mathrm{SO_2}\)) or oxidising potassium ferrocyanide \(\mathrm{K_4[Fe(CN)_6]}\) to \(\mathrm{K_3[Fe(CN)_6]}\) by removing electropositive potassium.
  • Classical Reduction: Removal of oxygen or an electronegative element, or addition of hydrogen or an electropositive element. For example, thermal decomposition of mercuric oxide (\(2\mathrm{HgO} \rightarrow 2\mathrm{Hg} + \mathrm{O_2}\)) or hydrogenation of ethene.
  • Electronic Concept: Oxidation is the loss of electrons (de-electronation), whereas reduction is the gain of electrons (electronation). Because free electrons cannot accumulate in bulk matter, oxidation and reduction always occur simultaneously.
  • Oxidant (Oxidising Agent): The species that accepts electrons and gets reduced itself while oxidising another species.
  • Reductant (Reducing Agent): The species that donates electrons and gets oxidised itself while reducing another species.
हिन्दी

ऐतिहासिक रूप से रसायनज्ञों ने रेडॉक्स परिवर्तनों को तत्वों के योग या निष्कासन के आधार पर परिभाषित किया और बाद में उन्हें इलेक्ट्रॉन स्थानांतरण के सिद्धांत द्वारा एकीकृत किया:

  • चिरसम्मत ऑक्सीकरण: किसी पदार्थ में ऑक्सीजन या किसी अधिक विद्युत-ऋणात्मक तत्व का जुड़ना, अथवा उससे हाइड्रोजन या किसी विद्युत-धनात्मक तत्व का निकलना। उदाहरण के लिए, डाइऑक्सीजन में सल्फर का दहन (\(\mathrm{S} + \mathrm{O_2} \rightarrow \mathrm{SO_2}\)) या पोटैशियम फेरोसायनाइड \(\mathrm{K_4[Fe(CN)_6]}\) से विद्युत-धनात्मक पोटैशियम के निष्कासन द्वारा \(\mathrm{K_3[Fe(CN)_6]}\) में ऑक्सीकरण।
  • चिरसम्मत अपचयन: ऑक्सीजन या विद्युत-ऋणात्मक तत्व का निकलना, अथवा हाइड्रोजन या विद्युत-धनात्मक तत्व का जुड़ना। उदाहरण के लिए, मर्क्यूरिक ऑक्साइड का तापीय अपघटन (\(2\mathrm{HgO} \rightarrow 2\mathrm{Hg} + \mathrm{O_2}\)) या एथीन का हाइड्रोजनीकरण।
  • इलेक्ट्रॉनिक अवधारणा: ऑक्सीकरण इलेक्ट्रॉनों का त्याग है, जबकि अपचयन इलेक्ट्रॉनों का ग्रहण है। चूँकि मुक्त इलेक्ट्रॉन द्रव्य में संचित नहीं हो सकते, इसलिए ऑक्सीकरण और अपचयन सदैव साथ-साथ होते हैं।
  • ऑक्सीकारक (Oxidant): वह प्रजाति जो इलेक्ट्रॉन ग्रहण करती है और स्वयं अपचयित होकर दूसरे पदार्थ को ऑक्सीकृत करती है।
  • अपचायक (Reductant): वह प्रजाति जो इलेक्ट्रॉन दान करती है और स्वयं ऑक्सीकृत होकर दूसरे पदार्थ को अपचयित करती है।

2.2 Oxidation Number, Rules & Stock Notation

English

For covalent reactions (such as \(\mathrm{CH_4} + 4\mathrm{Cl_2} \rightarrow \mathrm{CCl_4} + 4\mathrm{HCl}\)), electrons are shared rather than completely transferred. To track electron shifts systematically, chemists use oxidation number (often used interchangeably with oxidation state): the hypothetical charge an atom would bear if every shared electron pair in a covalent bond were assigned entirely to the more electronegative atom.

In terms of oxidation number: Oxidation is an increase in oxidation number; Reduction is a decrease in oxidation number.

Rule / Species Category Assigned Oxidation Number Key Exceptions & NEET Traps
Free / uncombined elements (\(\mathrm{O_3, P_4, S_8, Al}\)) \(0\) All atoms in homonuclear molecules have \(0\).
Monoatomic ions (\(\mathrm{Na^+, Mg^{2+}, Cl^-}\)) Equal to ion charge Alkali metals are always \(+1\); alkaline earth metals are always \(+2\); \(\mathrm{Al}\) is \(+3\) in compounds.
Hydrogen (\(\mathrm{H}\)) \(+1\) \(-1\) in binary metal hydrides (\(\mathrm{LiH, NaH, CaH_2}\)).
Oxygen (\(\mathrm{O}\)) \(-2\) Peroxides (\(\mathrm{H_2O_2, Na_2O_2}\)): \(-1\); Superoxides (\(\mathrm{KO_2, RbO_2}\)): \(-1/2\); with fluorine (\(\mathrm{OF_2}\): \(+2\); \(\mathrm{O_2F_2}\): \(+1\)).
Fluorine (\(\mathrm{F}\)) & other halogens \(-1\) (for \(\mathrm{F}\) always) \(\mathrm{Cl, Br, I}\) are \(-1\) as halides, but show positive states (\(+1\) to \(+7\)) in oxoacids/oxoanions.
Sum rule \(0\) (neutral) or ion charge Algebraic sum of all atoms' oxidation numbers equals total charge on the molecule or polyatomic ion.

Stock Notation: Proposed by Alfred Stock, this convention writes the oxidation state of a metal as a Roman numeral in parentheses immediately after the metal symbol—for instance, \(\mathrm{Fe(II)O}\), \(\mathrm{Fe_2(III)O_3}\), \(\mathrm{Sn(II)Cl_2}\), \(\mathrm{Sn(IV)Cl_4}\), and \(\mathrm{HAu(III)Cl_4}\).

2.3 The Paradox of Fractional Oxidation Numbers

English

Formula-based calculations occasionally yield fractional oxidation states—for example, \(+4/3\) for carbon in \(\mathrm{C_3O_2}\), \(+16/3\) for bromine in \(\mathrm{Br_3O_8}\), and \(+2.5\) for sulphur in \(\mathrm{S_4O_6^{2-}}\) (tetrathionate ion). Because electrons are never transferred in fractions, a fractional value simply represents an average oxidation state across chemically non-equivalent atoms of the same element in the structure:

  • Carbon suboxide (\(\mathrm{O=C=C^*=C=O}\)): Two terminal carbons are each in \(+2\), while the central carbon (\(\mathrm{C^*}\)) bonded only to two identical carbons is in \(0\). Average: \((2 + 0 + 2)/3 = +4/3\).
  • Tetrathionate ion (\(\mathrm{S_4O_6^{2-}}\)): Two terminal sulphur atoms bonded to oxygen are each in \(+5\), while the two central \(\mathrm{S-S}\) bonded sulphurs are each in \(0\). Average: \((5 + 0 + 0 + 5)/4 = +2.5\).
  • Tribromooctaoxide (\(\mathrm{Br_3O_8}\)): Two terminal bromines are in \(+6\), and the middle bromine is in \(+4\). Average: \((6 + 4 + 6)/3 = +16/3\).
  • Mixed oxides: \(\mathrm{Fe_3O_4}\) (\(\mathrm{FeO \cdot Fe_2O_3}\), average \(+8/3\)) and \(\mathrm{Pb_3O_4}\) (\(2\mathrm{PbO \cdot PbO_2}\), average \(+8/3\)) contain metal ions in two distinct whole-number oxidation states. (True fractional molecular-orbital states exist only in species like \(\mathrm{O_2^+}\) [\(+1/2\)] and \(\mathrm{O_2^-}\) [\(-1/2\)].)
हिन्दी

सूत्र-आधारित गणनाओं से कभी-कभी भिन्नात्मक ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाएँ प्राप्त होती हैं—जैसे \(\mathrm{C_3O_2}\) में कार्बन की \(+4/3\), \(\mathrm{Br_3O_8}\) में ब्रोमीन की \(+16/3\), तथा \(\mathrm{S_4O_6^{2-}}\) (टेट्राथायोनेट आयन) में सल्फर की \(+2.5\)। चूँकि इलेक्ट्रॉनों का स्थानांतरण कभी अंशों में नहीं होता, इसलिए भिन्नात्मक मान केवल संरचना में एक ही तत्व के रासायनिक रूप से असमान परमाणुओं की औसत ऑक्सीकरण अवस्था को दर्शाता है:

  • कार्बन सबऑक्साइड (\(\mathrm{O=C=C^*=C=O}\)): दो सिरों वाले कार्बन परमाणु \(+2\) अवस्था में हैं, जबकि दो समान कार्बनों से जुड़ा मध्य कार्बन (\(\mathrm{C^*}\)) \(0\) अवस्था में है। औसत: \((2 + 0 + 2)/3 = +4/3\)।
  • टेट्राथायोनेट आयन (\(\mathrm{S_4O_6^{2-}}\)): ऑक्सीजन से जुड़े दो बाह्य सल्फर परमाणु \(+5\) में हैं, जबकि आपस में जुड़े दो मध्य सल्फर परमाणु \(0\) अवस्था में हैं। औसत: \((5 + 0 + 0 + 5)/4 = +2.5\)।
  • ट्राइब्रोमोऑक्टाऑक्साइड (\(\mathrm{Br_3O_8}\)): दो बाह्य ब्रोमीन \(+6\) में तथा मध्य ब्रोमीन \(+4\) अवस्था में है। औसत: \((6 + 4 + 6)/3 = +16/3\)।
  • मिश्रित ऑक्साइड: \(\mathrm{Fe_3O_4}\) (\(\mathrm{FeO \cdot Fe_2O_3}\), औसत \(+8/3\)) और \(\mathrm{Pb_3O_4}\) (\(2\mathrm{PbO \cdot PbO_2}\), औसत \(+8/3\)) में धातु आयन दो भिन्न पूर्णांक ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाओं में उपस्थित होते हैं। (वास्तविक भिन्नात्मक अवस्था केवल \(\mathrm{O_2^+}\) [\(+1/2\)] तथा \(\mathrm{O_2^-}\) [\(-1/2\)] जैसी प्रजातियों में पाई जाती है।)

3. Classification of Redox Reactions & Disproportionation

English
Reaction Type Core Criterion Representative Equation & Note
Combination \(\mathrm{A + B \rightarrow C}\); at least one reactant must be in the elemental form. \(3\mathrm{Mg(s)} + \mathrm{N_2(g)} \xrightarrow{\Delta} \mathrm{Mg_3N_2(s)}\)
Decomposition Breakdown of a compound into two or more components, with at least one product in the elemental state. \(2\mathrm{KClO_3(s)} \xrightarrow{\Delta} 2\mathrm{KCl(s)} + 3\mathrm{O_2(g)}\)
Non-redox contrast: \(\mathrm{CaCO_3(s)} \xrightarrow{\Delta} \mathrm{CaO(s)} + \mathrm{CO_2(g)}\) (no change in oxidation numbers).
Metal Displacement A more reactive metal displaces a less reactive metal ion from its compound. \(\mathrm{Zn(s)} + \mathrm{CuSO_4(aq)} \rightarrow \mathrm{ZnSO_4(aq)} + \mathrm{Cu(s)}\)
(Used in metallurgical extraction like \(\mathrm{TiCl_4} + 2\mathrm{Mg}\)).
Non-Metal Displacement Displacement of \(\mathrm{H_2}\) (from cold water, steam, or acids) or \(\mathrm{O_2}\) / halogens. \(\mathrm{Cl_2(g)} + 2\mathrm{Br^-(aq)} \rightarrow 2\mathrm{Cl^-(aq)} + \mathrm{Br_2(l)}\)
(Basis of the Layer Test; oxidising power: \(\mathrm{F_2 > Cl_2 > Br_2 > I_2}\)).
Disproportionation A single element in an intermediate oxidation state is simultaneously oxidised and reduced. \(2\mathrm{H_2O_2(aq)} \rightarrow 2\mathrm{H_2O(l)} + \mathrm{O_2(g)}\) (\(\mathrm{O}: -1 \rightarrow -2\) & \(0\))
\(\mathrm{Cl_2} + 2\mathrm{OH^-} \rightarrow \mathrm{ClO^-} + \mathrm{Cl^-} + \mathrm{H_2O}\)
Crucial NEET Exception: Fluorine (\(\mathrm{F_2}\)) never undergoes disproportionation because, as the most electronegative element, it cannot exhibit any positive oxidation state. With alkali, \(\mathrm{F_2}\) forms \(\mathrm{F^-}\) and \(\mathrm{OF_2}\) (and \(\mathrm{O_2}\) with water). Similarly, an ion where the element is already at its maximum oxidation state (such as \(\mathrm{ClO_4^-}\), where \(\mathrm{Cl} = +7\)) or minimum oxidation state (such as \(\mathrm{Cl^-}\), where \(\mathrm{Cl} = -1\)) cannot disproportionate.

4. Balancing Redox Equations & Redox Titrations

4.1 Two Standard Balancing Methods

English

Method A — Oxidation Number Method:

  1. Write the skeletal ionic equation and assign oxidation numbers to identify atoms undergoing change.
  2. Balance the atoms undergoing oxidation-state change, then calculate total increase and total decrease in oxidation number. Multiply by suitable integers so that total increase equals total decrease.
  3. Balance total ionic charge by adding \(\mathrm{H^+}\) (in acidic medium) or \(\mathrm{OH^-}\) (in basic medium) to the appropriate side.
  4. Balance hydrogen atoms by adding \(\mathrm{H_2O}\) molecules; verify that oxygen atoms and total charge match on both sides.

Method B — Half-Reaction (Ion-Electron) Method:

  1. Split the skeletal equation into separate oxidation and reduction half-reactions.
  2. In each half-reaction, balance all atoms except \(\mathrm{O}\) and \(\mathrm{H}\).
  3. Balance \(\mathrm{O}\) by adding \(\mathrm{H_2O}\), and balance \(\mathrm{H}\) by adding \(\mathrm{H^+}\). (For basic medium, add an equal number of \(\mathrm{OH^-}\) ions to both sides for every \(\mathrm{H^+}\) present, combining \(\mathrm{H^+ + OH^- \rightarrow H_2O}\)).
  4. Balance charge in each half-reaction by adding electrons (\(\mathrm{e^-}\)).
  5. Equalise electron count across both half-reactions, add them together, and cancel common terms.

5. Electrode Processes, Galvanic Cells & Standard Electrode Potentials

English

When zinc metal is dipped directly into aqueous \(\mathrm{CuSO_4}\), electrons transfer directly and heat is evolved. If we separate the two half-reactions into two beakers—\(\mathrm{Zn}\) in \(\mathrm{ZnSO_4(aq)}\) and \(\mathrm{Cu}\) in \(\mathrm{CuSO_4(aq)}\)—we obtain two redox couples written as \(\mathrm{Zn^{2+}/Zn}\) and \(\mathrm{Cu^{2+}/Cu}\) (oxidised form before reduced form).

Connecting the electrodes externally via a metallic wire and internally via a salt bridge (a U-tube filled with \(\mathrm{KCl}\) or \(\mathrm{NH_4NO_3}\) gelled in agar-agar) forms a Daniell cell:

  • Anode (Negative electrode in galvanic cell): Oxidation occurs (\(\mathrm{Zn(s) \rightarrow Zn^{2+}(aq) + 2e^-}\)). Electrons flow from anode to cathode through the external wire.
  • Cathode (Positive electrode in galvanic cell): Reduction occurs (\(\mathrm{Cu^{2+}(aq) + 2e^- \rightarrow Cu(s)}\)). Conventional current flows opposite to electron flow (from cathode to anode).
  • Salt Bridge Role: Completes the internal electrical circuit by ion migration and maintains electrical neutrality without mixing the two solutions.
हिन्दी

English

Standard Electrode Potential (\(E^\circ\)): Defined at \(298\,\mathrm{K}\), unit concentration (\(1\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}}\)) for aqueous ions, and \(1\,\mathrm{atm}\) pressure for gases, measured relative to the standard hydrogen electrode (\(\mathrm{H^+/H_2}\), \(E^\circ = 0.00\,\mathrm{V}\)). By IUPAC convention, all tabulated values are standard reduction potentials.

  • More negative \(E^\circ\): The reduced form is a stronger reducing agent than \(\mathrm{H_2}\) (e.g., \(\mathrm{Li^+ / Li}: -3.05\,\mathrm{V}\), \(\mathrm{K^+ / K}: -2.93\,\mathrm{V}\), \(\mathrm{Zn^{2+} / Zn}: -0.76\,\mathrm{V}\)).
  • More positive \(E^\circ\): The oxidised form is a stronger oxidising agent (e.g., \(\mathrm{F_2 / F^-}: +2.87\,\mathrm{V}\), \(\mathrm{MnO_4^- / Mn^{2+}}: +1.51\,\mathrm{V}\), \(\mathrm{Cl_2 / Cl^-}: +1.36\,\mathrm{V}\), \(\mathrm{Ag^+ / Ag}: +0.80\,\mathrm{V}\), \(\mathrm{Cu^{2+} / Cu}: +0.34\,\mathrm{V}\)).
  • Spontaneity / Feasibility Rule: A redox reaction is feasible if \(E^\circ_{\text{cell}} = E^\circ_{\text{cathode (reduction)}} - E^\circ_{\text{anode (oxidation)}} > 0\). Equivalently, a species with a higher \(E^\circ\) can oxidise the reduced form of a couple with a lower \(E^\circ\).

  • स्वतःप्रवर्तिता / सुगमता नियम: कोई रेडॉक्स अभिक्रिया तब संभव होती है जब \(E^\circ_{\text{cell}} = E^\circ_{\text{कैथोड (अपचयन)}} - E^\circ_{\text{ऐनोड (ऑक्सीकरण)}} > 0\) हो। अर्थात् उच्च \(E^\circ\) वाली प्रजाति निम्न \(E^\circ\) वाले युग्म के अपचयित रूप को ऑक्सीकृत कर सकती है।

6. Worked Examples

English

Worked Example 1: Oxidation Number with Peroxide Linkage & Stock Notation

Problem: Determine the oxidation number of sulphur in peroxomonosulphuric acid (\(\mathrm{H_2SO_5}\)) and explain why a naive formula calculation gives an impossible value. Also write the Stock notation for \(\mathrm{Cr_2O_3}\) and \(\mathrm{SnCl_4}\).

Solution:

  1. A naive calculation assuming all five oxygen atoms are \(-2\) gives \(2(+1) + x + 5(-2) = 0 \implies x = +8\). However, sulphur (Group 16) has only 6 valence electrons and cannot exceed \(+6\).
  2. Structural correction: \(\mathrm{H_2SO_5}\) contains one peroxide linkage (\(\mathrm{-O-O-}\)) in which two oxygen atoms have oxidation number \(-1\) each, while the remaining three oxygen atoms are normal oxides (\(-2\) each): \[2(+1) + x + 3(-2) + 2(-1) = 0 \implies 2 + x - 6 - 2 = 0 \implies x = +6\] Thus, the true oxidation state of sulphur in \(\mathrm{H_2SO_5}\) is \(+6\).
  3. Stock Notation: In \(\mathrm{Cr_2O_3}\), \(2y + 3(-2) = 0 \implies y = +3\), so it is written \(\mathrm{Cr_2(III)O_3}\). In \(\mathrm{SnCl_4}\), \(z + 4(-1) = 0 \implies z = +4\), so it is written \(\mathrm{Sn(IV)Cl_4}\).
हिन्दी

प्रश्न: परॉक्सोमोनोसल्फ्यूरिक अम्ल (\(\mathrm{H_2SO_5}\)) में सल्फर की ऑक्सीकरण संख्या ज्ञात कीजिए तथा समझाइए कि सामान्य सूत्र-गणना असंभव मान क्यों देती है। साथ ही \(\mathrm{Cr_2O_3}\) और \(\mathrm{SnCl_4}\) के लिए स्टॉक संकेतन लिखिए।

Worked Example 2: Balancing a Redox Equation in Basic Medium

Problem: Balance the following ionic reaction in basic medium using the half-reaction method: \[\mathrm{ClO^-(aq) + Cr(OH)_3(s) \rightarrow Cl^-(aq) + CrO_4^{2-}(aq)}\]

Solution:

  1. Reduction half-reaction: \(\mathrm{ClO^- \rightarrow Cl^-}\). Balance \(\mathrm{O}\) with \(\mathrm{H_2O}\) and \(\mathrm{H}\) with \(\mathrm{H^+}\): \[\mathrm{ClO^- + 2H^+ + 2e^- \rightarrow Cl^- + H_2O}\] Convert to basic medium by adding \(2\mathrm{OH^-}\) to both sides: \[\mathrm{ClO^- + H_2O + 2e^- \rightarrow Cl^- + 2OH^-}\]
  2. Oxidation half-reaction: \(\mathrm{Cr(OH)_3 \rightarrow CrO_4^{2-}}\). Balance \(\mathrm{O}\) with \(1\,\mathrm{H_2O}\) on left, then \(\mathrm{H}\) with \(5\,\mathrm{H^+}\) on right, and \(3\,\mathrm{e^-}\) on right: \[\mathrm{Cr(OH)_3 + H_2O \rightarrow CrO_4^{2-} + 5H^+ + 3e^-}\] Add \(5\mathrm{OH^-}\) to both sides: \[\mathrm{Cr(OH)_3 + 5OH^- \rightarrow CrO_4^{2-} + 4H_2O + 3e^-}\]
  3. Equalise electrons: Multiply reduction half by 3 and oxidation half by 2 (6 electrons each), then add and cancel common terms (\(3\mathrm{H_2O}\) and \(6\mathrm{OH^-}\)): \[3\mathrm{ClO^-(aq) + 2Cr(OH)_3(s) + 4OH^-(aq) \rightarrow 3Cl^-(aq) + 2CrO_4^{2-}(aq) + 5H_2O(l)}\]

Worked Example 3: Predicting Feasibility & Stoichiometry from \(E^\circ\) Values

Problem: Given standard reduction potentials at \(298\,\mathrm{K}\): \(E^\circ(\mathrm{Fe^{3+}/Fe^{2+}}) = +0.77\,\mathrm{V}\) and \(E^\circ(\mathrm{I_2/I^-}) = +0.54\,\mathrm{V}\). (a) Is the reaction between \(\mathrm{Fe^{3+}(aq)}\) and \(\mathrm{I^-(aq)}\) feasible? (b) Suppose \(25\,\mathrm{mL}\) of a hypothetical \(0.20\,\mathrm{M}\) \(\mathrm{Fe^{3+}}\) solution reacts completely with excess \(\mathrm{I^-}\); calculate the moles of \(\mathrm{I_2}\) liberated.

Solution:

  1. Since \(E^\circ(\mathrm{Fe^{3+}/Fe^{2+}}) = +0.77\,\mathrm{V} > E^\circ(\mathrm{I_2/I^-}) = +0.54\,\mathrm{V}\), \(\mathrm{Fe^{3+}}\) is reduced at the cathode and \(\mathrm{I^-}\) is oxidised at the anode: \[E^\circ_{\text{cell}} = 0.77 - 0.54 = +0.23\,\mathrm{V} > 0\] Because \(E^\circ_{\text{cell}} > 0\), the reaction \(2\mathrm{Fe^{3+}(aq)} + 2\mathrm{I^-(aq)} \rightarrow 2\mathrm{Fe^{2+}(aq)} + \mathrm{I_2(s)}\) is feasible.
  2. Stoichiometry (assumed values): Moles of \(\mathrm{Fe^{3+}} = 0.20\,\mathrm{mol\,L^{-1}} \times 0.025\,\mathrm{L} = 5.0 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{mol}\). From the balanced equation, \(2\,\mathrm{mol}\) \(\mathrm{Fe^{3+}}\) liberate \(1\,\mathrm{mol}\) \(\mathrm{I_2}\), so: \[n(\mathrm{I_2}) = \frac{5.0 \times 10^{-3}}{2} = 2.5 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{mol}\]

Practice Question 6 (Assertion–Reason MCQ):

Assertion (A): Fluorine does not exhibit a disproportionation reaction when treated with aqueous alkali.

Reason (R): Fluorine is the most electronegative element and cannot show any positive oxidation state.

  • (A) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A.
  • (B) Both A and R are true, but R is not the correct explanation of A.
  • (C) A is true, but R is false.
  • (D) A is false, but R is true.

Answer: (A) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A.

Solution: Disproportionation of a halogen (\(\mathrm{X_2}\)) from \(0\) state requires simultaneous reduction to \(-1\) and oxidation to a positive state (e.g., \(+1\) in \(\mathrm{XO^-}\)). Practice Question 8 (Numerical-Application MCQ): In an iodometric titration (assuming standard conditions), \(0.04\,\mathrm{mol}\) of liberated \(\mathrm{I_2}\) reacts completely with sodium thiosulphate (\(\mathrm{Na_2S_2O_3}\)) in the presence of starch indicator. How many moles of \(\mathrm{S_2O_3^{2-}}\) are consumed, and what is the average oxidation state of sulphur in the product \(\mathrm{S_4O_6^{2-}}\)?

  • (A) \(0.04\,\mathrm{mol}\) and \(+2.0\)
  • (B) \(0.08\,\mathrm{mol}\) and \(+2.5\)
  • (C) \(0.02\,\mathrm{mol}\) and \(+2.5\)
  • (D) \(0.08\,\mathrm{mol}\) and \(+5.0\)

Answer: (B) \(0.08\,\mathrm{mol}\) and \(+2.5\)

Solution: The balanced titration reaction is \(\mathrm{I_2(aq)} + 2\mathrm{S_2O_3^{2-}(aq)} \rightarrow 2\mathrm{I^-(aq)} + \mathrm{S_4O_6^{2-}(aq)}\). Therefore, \(0.04\,\mathrm{mol}\) of \(\mathrm{I_2}\) consumes \(2 \times 0.04 = 0.08\,\mathrm{mol}\) of \(\mathrm{S_2O_3^{2-}}\). 8. Common NEET Pitfalls & Revision Checklist

English
  • Trap 1 — Exceeding Group Maximum Oxidation State: If a formula calculation gives an oxidation number higher than the element's valence electrons (e.g., \(+8\) for \(\mathrm{S}\) in \(\mathrm{H_2SO_5}\)), check immediately for a peroxide (\(\mathrm{-O-O-}\)) linkage.
  • Trap 2 — Confusing Average with Actual Oxidation State: Fractional oxidation numbers (\(+4/3\) in \(\mathrm{C_3O_2}\), \(+2.5\) in \(\mathrm{S_4O_6^{2-}}\), \(+8/3\) in \(\mathrm{Fe_3O_4}\)) are averages of distinct whole-number oxidation states.
  • Trap 3 — Non-Redox Decomposition: Not every decomposition is redox; reactions like \(\mathrm{CaCO_3 \rightarrow CaO + CO_2}\) involve no electron transfer.
  • Trap 4 — Sign of \(E^\circ\) and Reducing Strength: More negative \(E^\circ\) means stronger reducing agent (like \(\mathrm{Li, K}\)); more positive \(E^\circ\) means stronger oxidising agent (like \(\mathrm{F_2, MnO_4^-}\)).
हिन्दी
  • सावधानी 1 — समूह की अधिकतम ऑक्सीकरण अवस्था से अधिक मान: यदि सूत्र-गणना से किसी तत्व के संयोजी इलेक्ट्रॉनों से अधिक मान प्राप्त हो (जैसे \(\mathrm{H_2SO_5}\) में \(\mathrm{S}\) के लिए \(+8\)), तो तुरंत परॉक्साइड (\(\mathrm{-O-O-}\)) बंध की जाँच करें।
  • सावधानी 2 — औसत और वास्तविक अवस्था में भ्रम: भिन्नात्मक ऑक्सीकरण संख्याएँ (\(\mathrm{C_3O_2}\) में \(+4/3\), \(\mathrm{S_4O_6^{2-}}\) में \(+2.5\), \(\mathrm{Fe_3O_4}\) में \(+8/3\)) भिन्न पूर्णांक ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाओं का औसत मात्र हैं।
  • सावधानी 3 — गैर-रेडॉक्स अपघटन: प्रत्येक अपघटन अभिक्रिया रेडॉक्स नहीं होती; जैसे \(\mathrm{CaCO_3 \rightarrow CaO + CO_2}\) में कोई इलेक्ट्रॉन स्थानांतरण नहीं होता।
  • सावधानी 4 — \(E^\circ\) का चिह्न एवं अपचायक शक्ति: अधिक ऋणात्मक \(E^\circ\) का अर्थ है प्रबलतम अपचायक (जैसे \(\mathrm{Li, K}\)); अधिक धनात्मक \(E^\circ\) का अर्थ है प्रबलतम ऑक्सीकारक (जैसे \(\mathrm{F_2, MnO_4^-}\))।

9. Concise Final Recap

English

Redox reactions couple simultaneous oxidation (electron loss / oxidation number increase) and reduction (electron gain / oxidation number decrease). Mastery for NEET UG rests on four pillars: (1) applying oxidation-number rules and structural exceptions accurately, (2) distinguishing disproportionation from other redox classes, (3) balancing ionic equations cleanly in acidic and basic media, and (4) using standard reduction potentials (\(E^\circ\)) to rank oxidants/reductants and predict reaction feasibility.

हिन्दी

रेडॉक्स अभिक्रियाएँ एक साथ होने वाले ऑक्सीकरण (इलेक्ट्रॉन त्याग / ऑक्सीकरण संख्या में वृद्धि) और अपचयन (इलेक्ट्रॉन ग्रहण / ऑक्सीकरण संख्या में कमी) को जोड़ती हैं। NEET UG में दक्षता चार स्तंभों पर टिकी है: (1) ऑक्सीकरण-संख्या नियमों और संरचनात्मक अपवादों का सटीक प्रयोग, (2) असमानुपातन को अन्य रेडॉक्स वर्गों से अलग पहचानना, (3) अम्लीय व क्षारीय माध्यम में आयनिक समीकरणों का सही संतुलन, तथा (4) मानक अपचयन विभव (\(E^\circ\)) द्वारा ऑक्सीकारकों/अपचायकों की तुलना एवं अभिक्रिया की सुगमता का निर्धारण।
