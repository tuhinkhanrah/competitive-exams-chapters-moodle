1. Learning Objectives & Overview / শিখন উদ্দেশ্য ও সারসংক্ষেপ

English

Photosynthesis is the fundamental physico-chemical process by which green plants (autotrophs) capture solar energy and convert inorganic substrates—carbon dioxide and water—into energy-rich organic carbohydrates while releasing molecular oxygen. Every heterotrophic organism on Earth depends directly or indirectly on this process for food and atmospheric oxygen.

Key Learning Objectives for NEET UG

  • Trace the historical experiments that established the roles of air, light, chlorophyll, and water in oxygenic photosynthesis.
  • Relate chloroplast ultrastructure (thylakoid membranes vs. stroma) to the spatial division of light-dependent and carbon-fixation reactions.
  • Distinguish between Photosystem I (P700) and Photosystem II (P680), non-cyclic (Z-scheme) vs. cyclic electron flow, and chemiosmotic ATP synthesis.
  • Master the stoichiometry and enzymatic steps of the Calvin (C3) cycle, the C4 (Hatch–Slack) pathway with Kranz anatomy, and photorespiration (C2 cycle).
  • Apply Blackman's Law of Limiting Factors to interpret how light intensity, CO2 concentration, temperature, and water regulate photosynthetic yield.

2. Foundational Experiments & Overall Equation / প্রাথমিক পরীক্ষাসমূহ ও সামগ্রিক সমীকরণ

English

Simple leaf-starch tests demonstrate two basic requirements: testing a variegated leaf or a leaf partially masked with black paper confirms that starch forms only in illuminated green regions (proving necessity of chlorophyll and light), whereas enclosing half a leaf in a vessel containing KOH-soaked cotton (which absorbs CO2) yields a negative starch test in that half (proving necessity of CO2).

  • Joseph Priestley (1770): Using a sealed bell jar with a burning candle and a mouse, he showed that respiration and combustion foul the air, whereas introducing a mint plant restores the air so the candle keeps burning and the mouse survives. (Priestley discovered oxygen in 1774.)
  • Jan Ingenhousz (1730–1799): Repeated Priestley's setup in sunlight and darkness, proving that sunlight is essential for air purification and that only green parts of aquatic plants release oxygen bubbles in bright light.
  • Julius von Sachs (1854): Showed that green parts synthesize glucose, which is stored as starch, and located chlorophyll within specialized organelles later named chloroplasts.
  • T.W. Engelmann (1843–1909): Split white light with a prism onto the green alga Cladophora suspended with aerobic bacteria. Bacteria clustered in the blue and red wavelengths—producing the first action spectrum of photosynthesis.
  • Cornelius van Niel (1897–1985): Studying purple and green sulphur bacteria, he demonstrated that photosynthesis is a light-dependent redox process where hydrogen from an oxidizable donor (\(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{A}\)) reduces \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) to carbohydrate. Because sulphur bacteria use \(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{S}\) and release sulphur/sulphate rather than \(\mathrm{O}_2\), he deduced that \(\mathrm{O}_2\) evolved by green plants comes from \(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}\), not \(\mathrm{CO}_2\)—a conclusion later confirmed using heavy oxygen (\({}^{18}\mathrm{O}\)) radioisotopic/isotopic tracing.

Van Niel's Generalized Equation:

\[2\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{A} + \mathrm{CO}_2 \xrightarrow{\text{Light}} 2\mathrm{A} + \mathrm{CH}_2\mathrm{O} + \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}\]

Overall Balanced Equation for Oxygenic Photosynthesis:

\[6\mathrm{CO}_2 + 12\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O} \xrightarrow{\text{Light}} \mathrm{C}_6\mathrm{H}_{12}\mathrm{O}_6 + 6\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O} + 6\mathrm{O}_2\]

Why 12 molecules of water as substrate? Because 12 \(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}\) molecules are split to supply the 24 electrons and 24 protons needed to reduce 6 \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) (yielding 6 \(\mathrm{O}_2\) entirely from water and 6 newly formed \(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}\) molecules).

3. Chloroplast Division of Labour & Pigments / ক্লোরোপ্লাস্টের শ্রমবিভাগ ও রঞ্জক পদার্থ

English

Mesophyll cells of green leaves contain abundant chloroplasts that align along cell walls to receive optimal incident light (parallel to walls under moderate/low light; perpendicular under intense light to avoid photodamage). Within each chloroplast, there is a strict division of labour:

  • Membrane System (Grana & Stroma Lamellae): Traps light energy and synthesizes ATP and NADPH. These directly light-driven steps constitute the Light Reactions (photochemical phase).
  • Stroma (Matrix): Contains enzymes that utilize ATP and NADPH to reduce \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) into sugars (stored as starch). These are the Dark Reactions (biosynthetic/carbon reactions)—not because they occur in darkness, but because they are not directly light-driven.

Photosynthetic Pigments

Paper chromatography separates leaf pigments into four distinct bands:

  • Chlorophyll a (bright or blue-green): The chief photosynthetic pigment and reaction centre molecule; absorbs maximally in blue (~430 nm) and red (~660–680 nm) regions.
  • Chlorophyll b (yellow-green), Xanthophylls (yellow), and Carotenoids (yellow to yellow-orange): Act as accessory pigments. They broaden the wavelength range of absorbed light, transfer excitation energy to chlorophyll a, and shield chlorophyll a from photo-oxidation.

Because accessory pigments contribute energy across intermediate wavelengths, the action spectrum (rate of photosynthesis vs. wavelength) is broader than the absorption spectrum of chlorophyll a alone, though both peak in the blue and red regions.

4. Light Reactions, Z-Scheme & Chemiosmosis / আলোক দশা, Z-স্কিম ও কেমিওসমোসিস

English

Pigments are organized into two discrete photochemical Light Harvesting Complexes (LHC) within Photosystem I (PS I) and Photosystem II (PS II) (named in order of discovery, not function). Each photosystem consists of an antenna (hundreds of protein-bound pigment molecules) funneling energy to a single chlorophyll a reaction centre: P700 (700 nm peak) in PS I, and P680 (680 nm peak) in PS II.

Non-Cyclic Photophosphorylation (The Z-Scheme) & Water Splitting

  1. Excitation at PS II: P680 absorbs 680 nm red light, exciting electrons to a primary electron acceptor.
  2. Splitting of Water (Photolysis): Lost electrons from PS II are continuously replenished by the water-splitting complex located on the inner (lumen) side of the thylakoid membrane: \[2\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O} \longrightarrow 4\mathrm{H}^+ + \mathrm{O}_2 + 4\mathrm{e}^-\]
  3. Downhill Electron Transport: Electrons move downhill on a redox potential scale through cytochromes (plastoquinone, cytochrome \(b_6f\), plastocyanin) to PS I, driving proton pumping.
  4. Excitation at PS I & NADP+ Reduction: P700 absorbs 700 nm light, passing excited electrons to an acceptor and then downhill via ferredoxin and NADP reductase (on the stroma side) to reduce \(\mathrm{NADP}^+\) to \(\mathrm{NADPH} + \mathrm{H}^+\).

Because both PS II and PS I operate in series, this produces both ATP and NADPH + H+ (plus \(\mathrm{O}_2\)).

Cyclic Photophosphorylation

When only PS I is functional—such as in stroma lamellae (which lack both PS II and NADP reductase) or when only light of wavelength beyond 680 nm is available—excited electrons from P700 cycle back to PS I via the electron transport system. This yields only ATP (no NADPH, no \(\mathrm{O}_2\) evolution).

Chemiosmotic Hypothesis of ATP Synthesis

ATP synthesis is driven by a proton gradient across the thylakoid membrane, with high \(\mathrm{H}^+\) concentration (low pH) in the thylakoid lumen and low \(\mathrm{H}^+\) in the stroma. Three mechanisms build this gradient:

  • Water splitting occurs on the lumen side, releasing \(\mathrm{H}^+\) directly into the lumen.
  • The primary electron acceptor transfers electrons to an H-carrier (plastoquinone) that picks up \(\mathrm{H}^+\) from the stroma and releases it into the lumen upon passing electrons to the inner carrier.
  • NADP reductase on the stroma side consumes stromal \(\mathrm{H}^+\) to form \(\mathrm{NADPH} + \mathrm{H}^+\).

Protons diffuse back down their electrochemical gradient into the stroma through the transmembrane channel CF0 of ATP synthase. This energy induces a conformational change in the stromal catalytic head CF1, synthesizing ATP from ADP and \(\mathrm{P}_i\).

5. The Biosynthetic Phase: Calvin Cycle (C3 Pathway) / কেলভিন চক্র (C3 পথ)

English

Using radioactive \({}^{14}\mathrm{C}\) tracing in algae, Melvin Calvin discovered that the first stable product of \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) fixation is a 3-carbon organic acid, 3-phosphoglyceric acid (3-PGA), and that the primary \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) acceptor is a 5-carbon ketose sugar, ribulose-1,5-bisphosphate (RuBP). Crucially, the Calvin cycle operates in all photosynthetic plants (both C3 and C4).

The cycle proceeds in three stages:

  1. Carboxylation: \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) is fixed onto RuBP (5C) to form two molecules of 3-PGA (3C), catalysed by RuBP carboxylase-oxygenase (RuBisCO)—the most abundant enzyme on Earth.
  2. Reduction: Two molecules of 3-PGA are phosphorylated and reduced to triose phosphates using 2 ATP and 2 NADPH per \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) fixed.
  3. Regeneration: To keep the cycle running, RuBP is regenerated from triose phosphates at the cost of 1 ATP per \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) fixed.

Cost per \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) fixed: \(3\,\mathrm{ATP} + 2\,\mathrm{NADPH}\)

Cost per Glucose (\(\mathrm{C}_6\mathrm{H}_{12}\mathrm{O}_6\), 6 turns): \(6\,\mathrm{CO}_2 + 18\,\mathrm{ATP} + 12\,\mathrm{NADPH} \longrightarrow \mathrm{C}_6\mathrm{H}_{12}\mathrm{O}_6 + 18\,\mathrm{ADP} + 18\,\mathrm{P}_i + 12\,\mathrm{NADP}^+\)

NEET Insight: Non-cyclic photophosphorylation produces ATP and NADPH in a 1:1 (or nearly equal) ratio, whereas the Calvin cycle needs a 3:2 ratio. Cyclic photophosphorylation supplies the extra ATP needed to bridge this gap.

6. The C4 (Hatch–Slack) Pathway & Photorespiration / C4 পথ ও আলোকশ্বসন

English

Tropical plants such as maize and sorghum possess a specialized leaf anatomy called Kranz anatomy ("wreath"), where vascular bundles are surrounded by concentric layers of large bundle sheath cells containing abundant chloroplasts, thick walls impervious to gas exchange, and no intercellular spaces.

Mechanism of the C4 Cycle

  • In Mesophyll Cells: Atmospheric \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) (as \(\mathrm{HCO}_3^-\)) is fixed onto a 3C molecule, phosphoenolpyruvate (PEP), by PEP carboxylase (PEPcase) to form the 4C acid oxaloacetic acid (OAA). Mesophyll cells lack RuBisCO.
  • Transport & Decarboxylation: OAA is converted to malic or aspartic acid and transported via plasmodesmata into bundle sheath cells, where it is decarboxylated to release \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) and a 3C acid.
  • Calvin Cycle & Regeneration: The released \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) enters the Calvin cycle inside bundle sheath cells (which are rich in RuBisCO but lack PEPcase). The 3C acid returns to the mesophyll to regenerate PEP (using 2 additional ATP equivalents per \(\mathrm{CO}_2\), making total cost 5 ATP + 2 NADPH per \(\mathrm{CO}_2\), or 30 ATP + 12 NADPH per glucose).

Photorespiration (C2 Cycle) — Why C4 Plants Outperform C3 Plants

RuBisCO's active site binds competitively to both \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) and \(\mathrm{O}_2\) depending on their relative concentrations. In C3 plants under high temperature, high light, or low \(\mathrm{CO}_2\)/\(\mathrm{O}_2\) ratio, \(\mathrm{O}_2\) binds to RuBisCO, converting RuBP into one molecule of 3-PGA (3C) and one molecule of phosphoglycolate (2C). This wasteful process—photorespiration—synthesizes no sugar, no ATP, and no NADPH, while releasing \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) and consuming ATP.

C4 plants avoid photorespiration because decarboxylation of C4 acids concentrates \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) around RuBisCO in the gas-tight bundle sheath cells, ensuring RuBisCO acts exclusively as a carboxylase. Consequently, C4 plants exhibit higher biomass productivity and tolerate higher temperatures.

7. Factors Affecting Photosynthesis & Law of Limiting Factors / সালোকসংশ্লেষের নিয়ন্ত্রক শর্তাবলি

English

Photosynthesis is regulated by internal (plant) factors (leaf number, size, age, orientation, mesophyll/chloroplast count, internal \(\mathrm{CO}_2\), chlorophyll content) and external factors (light, \(\mathrm{CO}_2\), temperature, water).

Blackman's Law of Limiting Factors (1905): If a chemical process is affected by more than one factor, its rate will be determined by the factor which is nearest to its minimal value—the factor that directly alters the rate if its quantity is changed.
  • Light Intensity: At low intensities, rate increases linearly with light. At higher intensities, the curve plateaus as other factors become limiting. Light saturation occurs at just 10% of full sunlight (so light is rarely limiting except in dense shade). Excessive light breaks down chlorophyll.
  • CO2 Concentration: The major limiting factor in nature (atmosphere contains only 0.03–0.04% \(\mathrm{CO}_2\)). Increasing \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) up to 0.05% boosts fixation; higher levels can be damaging long-term. Under high light, C4 plants saturate around 360 µL L−1, whereas C3 plants respond beyond 450 µL L−1 (used commercially in \(\mathrm{CO}_2\)-enriched greenhouses for tomatoes and bell peppers).
  • Temperature: Dark reactions are enzymatic and strongly temperature-sensitive. C4 plants have a higher temperature optimum (30–40 °C) than C3 plants (20–25 °C).
  • Water: Acts mainly indirectly—water stress causes stomatal closure (restricting \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) entry) and leaf wilting (reducing surface area and metabolic activity).

সালোকসংশ্লেষ অভ্যন্তরীণ শর্ত (পাতার সংখ্যা, আকার, বয়স, বিন্যাস, ক্লোরোপ্লাস্ট সংখ্যা, ক্লোরোফিলের পরিমাণ) এবং বাহ্যিক শর্ত (আলো, \(\mathrm{CO}_2\), উষ্ণতা, জল) দ্বারা প্রভাবিত হয়।

ব্ল্যাকম্যানের সীমাবদ্ধকারী শর্তের সূত্র (১৯০৫): কোনো জৈব-রাসায়নিক প্রক্রিয়া একাধিক শর্ত দ্বারা প্রভাবিত হলে, যে শর্তটি তার ন্যূনতম মানের সবচেয়ে কাছাকাছি থাকে, সেই শর্তটিই প্রক্রিয়াটির হার নির্ধারণ করে।
  • আলোর তীব্রতা: কম আলোয় হার রৈখিকভাবে বাড়ে, কিন্তু পূর্ণ সূর্যালোকের মাত্র ১০% তীব্রতায় আলোক-সম্পৃক্তি (light saturation) ঘটে এবং অন্য শর্ত সীমাবদ্ধকারী হয়ে ওঠে। অতি তীব্র আলো ক্লোরোফিল ভেঙে দেয়।
  • CO2-এর ঘনত্ব: প্রকৃতিতে এটি প্রধান সীমাবদ্ধকারী শর্ত (বায়ুমণ্ডলে ০.০৩–০.০৪%)। ০.০৫% পর্যন্ত বৃদ্ধিতে হার বাড়ে। উচ্চ আলোয় C4 উদ্ভিদ ৩৬০ µL L−1 মাত্রায় সম্পৃক্ত হয়, কিন্তু C3 উদ্ভিদ ৪৫০ µL L−1-এর বেশি ঘনত্বেও সাড়া দেয় (যা গ্রিনহাউসে টমেটো ও ক্যাপসিকাম চাষে ব্যবহৃত হয়)।
  • উষ্ণতা: অন্ধকার দশার উৎসেচকীয় বিক্রিয়াগুলো উষ্ণতা-নিয়ন্ত্রিত। C4 উদ্ভিদ উচ্চ উষ্ণতায় (৩০–৪০ °C) এবং C3 উদ্ভিদ অপেক্ষাকৃত কম উষ্ণতায় (২০–২৫ °C) সর্বাধিক হার দেখায়।
  • জল: জলস্বল্পতা পরোক্ষভাবে পত্ররন্ধ্র বন্ধ করে (CO2 প্রবেশ কমায়) এবং পাতা নেতিয়ে দিয়ে সালোকসংশ্লেষের হার হ্রাস করে।

8. Worked Examples / আদর্শ সমাধানসহ উদাহরণ

English • Worked Example 1

Problem (Assumed Scenario): Suppose a scientist supplies radioactively labelled \({}^{14}\mathrm{CO}_2\) to a C3 leaf and a C4 leaf (both assumed to be photosynthesizing actively) to synthesize 3 moles of glucose via their respective pathways. Calculate the total moles of ATP and NADPH consumed in each plant, and identify the first labelled compound detected after 5 seconds.

Solution:

  • C3 Plant: Synthesizing 1 mole of glucose requires 6 turns of the Calvin cycle, consuming 18 ATP and 12 NADPH. For 3 moles of glucose: \[\text{ATP} = 3 \times 18 = 54\text{ moles}, \quad \text{NADPH} = 3 \times 12 = 36\text{ moles}\] The first \({}^{14}\mathrm{C}\)-labelled product is 3-PGA.
  • C4 Plant: Each \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) fixed requires 2 extra ATP to regenerate PEP from the 3C acid in the mesophyll, plus 3 ATP and 2 NADPH in the bundle sheath Calvin cycle (total 5 ATP + 2 NADPH per \(\mathrm{CO}_2\), or 30 ATP + 12 NADPH per glucose). For 3 moles of glucose: \[\text{ATP} = 3 \times 30 = 90\text{ moles}, \quad \text{NADPH} = 3 \times 12 = 36\text{ moles}\] The first \({}^{14}\mathrm{C}\)-labelled product is OAA (oxaloacetic acid).
বাংলা • সমাধানসহ উদাহরণ ১

সমস্যা (কাল্পনিক পরিস্থিতি): ধরো, একজন বিজ্ঞানী সক্রিয়ভাবে সালোকসংশ্লেষকারী একটি C3 ও একটি C4 পাতায় তেজস্ক্রিয় \({}^{14}\mathrm{CO}_2\) সরবরাহ করে ৩ মোল গ্লুকোজ সংশ্লেষ পর্যবেক্ষণ করছেন। উভয় উদ্ভিদে মোট কত মোল ATP ও NADPH ব্যয় হবে তা নির্ণয় করো এবং প্রথম তেজস্ক্রিয়-চিহ্নিত যৌগটির নাম লেখো।

সমাধান:

  • C3 উদ্ভিদ: ১ মোল গ্লুকোজ তৈরিতে ১৮ ATP ও ১২ NADPH লাগে। সুতরাং ৩ মোল গ্লুকোজের জন্য: \[\text{ATP} = 3 \times 18 = 54\text{ mol}, \quad \text{NADPH} = 3 \times 12 = 36\text{ mol}\] প্রথম \({}^{14}\mathrm{C}\)-চিহ্নিত যৌগটি হলো 3-PGA।
  • C4 উদ্ভিদ: মেসোফিলে PEP পুনর্গঠনে প্রতি \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) পিছু অতিরিক্ত ২ ATP এবং কেলভিন চক্রে ৩ ATP + ২ NADPH লাগে (অর্থাৎ প্রতি গ্লুকোজে ৩০ ATP ও ১২ NADPH)। ৩ মোল গ্লুকোজের জন্য: \[\text{ATP} = 3 \times 30 = 90\text{ mol}, \quad \text{NADPH} = 3 \times 12 = 36\text{ mol}\] প্রথম \({}^{14}\mathrm{C}\)-চিহ্নিত যৌগটি হলো OAA (অক্সালোঅ্যাসিটিক অ্যাসিড)।
English • Worked Example 2

Problem (Assumed Scenario): An experimental chloroplast suspension is illuminated exclusively with far-red monochromatic light of wavelength 700 nm (assumed sufficient intensity), while ADP, \(\mathrm{P}_i\), and \(\mathrm{NADP}^+\) are supplied in excess. Will \(\mathrm{O}_2\), ATP, and NADPH be produced?

Solution:

  • Because the wavelength is 700 nm (beyond 680 nm), PS II (P680) is not excited, while PS I (P700) is excited.
  • Without PS II activity, photolysis of water does not occur, so no \(\mathrm{O}_2\) is released and no electrons reach \(\mathrm{NADP}^+\) (hence no NADPH is formed).
  • Electrons excited from P700 undergo cyclic photophosphorylation through the electron transport chain back to PS I, generating a proton gradient that drives ATP synthesis only.
বাংলা • সমাধানসহ উদাহরণ ২

সমস্যা (কাল্পনিক পরিস্থিতি): একটি পরীক্ষামূলক ক্লোরোপ্লাস্ট দ্রবণে পর্যাপ্ত ADP, \(\mathrm{P}_i\) ও \(\mathrm{NADP}^+\) সরবরাহ করে কেবল ৭০০ nm তরঙ্গদৈর্ঘ্যের আলো ফেলা হলো। এতে কি \(\mathrm{O}_2\), ATP এবং NADPH উৎপন্ন হবে?

সমাধান:

  • আলোর তরঙ্গদৈর্ঘ্য ৭০০ nm (৬৮০ nm-এর বেশি) হওয়ায় PS II (P680) উদ্দীপিত হবে না, কেবল PS I (P700) সক্রিয় হবে।
  • PS II নিষ্ক্রিয় থাকায় জলের আলোক-বিশ্লেষণ ঘটবে না—ফলে কোনো \(\mathrm{O}_2\) নির্গত হবে না এবং কোনো NADPH তৈরি হবে না।
  • P700 থেকে নির্গত ইলেকট্রন চক্রীয় ফটোফসফোরাইলেশনের মাধ্যমে পুনরায় PS I-এ ফিরে আসবে এবং কেবলমাত্র ATP সংশ্লেষিত হবে।

Practice Question 4 (Single-Correct MCQ)

Which combination of ATP and NADPH is required exclusively during the regeneration stage of the Calvin cycle to synthesize one molecule of sucrose (12 carbons)?

  • (A) 6 ATP and 0 NADPH
  • (B) 12 ATP and 0 NADPH
  • (C) 24 ATP and 24 NADPH
  • (D) 36 ATP and 24 NADPH

Answer: (B)

Explanation: One sucrose molecule (12C) requires fixing 12 \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) molecules (12 turns of the Calvin cycle). Each turn uses 1 ATP (and 0 NADPH) in the regeneration step to convert triose phosphate back into RuBP. Therefore, \(12 \times 1 = 12\text{ ATP}\) and 0 NADPH are used in regeneration.

10. Common Pitfalls & Revision Checklist / সাধারণ ভুল ও পুনরালোচনা তালিকা

English
  • Pitfall 1 — "Dark reactions occur at night": Incorrect. They are called dark reactions only because they do not directly require photons, but they stop shortly after light ceases because they depend on ATP and NADPH from the light reactions.
  • Pitfall 2 — "PS I acts before PS II": Incorrect. In non-cyclic electron flow, PS II (P680) acts first, followed by PS I (P700).
  • Pitfall 3 — "C4 plants lack the Calvin cycle": Incorrect. C4 plants use the Calvin cycle in their bundle sheath cells to synthesize glucose; the Hatch–Slack pathway is an upstream \(\mathrm{CO}_2\)-concentrating pump.
  • Pitfall 4 — "Phosphoglycolate is a 3C compound": Incorrect. In photorespiration, oxygenation of RuBP (5C) forms one 3-PGA (3C) and one phosphoglycolate (2C).
বাংলা (Bengali)
  • সাধারণ ভুল ১ — "অন্ধকার দশা কেবল রাতে ঘটে": এটি ভুল। সরাসরি ফোটনের প্রয়োজন হয় না বলেই একে অন্ধকার দশা বলা হয়, তবে আলোক দশায় তৈরি ATP ও NADPH-এর ওপর নির্ভরশীল হওয়ায় আলো নিভে যাওয়ার কিছুক্ষণ পর এটি বন্ধ হয়ে যায়।
  • সাধারণ ভুল ২ — "PS I আগে কাজ করে": ভুল। অচক্রীয় প্রবাহে PS II (P680) প্রথমে এবং PS I (P700) পরে কাজ করে।
  • সাধারণ ভুল ৩ — "C4 উদ্ভিদে কেলভিন চক্র ঘটে না": ভুল। C4 উদ্ভিদের বান্ডিল শীথ কোষে শর্করা তৈরির জন্য কেলভিন চক্র অবশ্যই ঘটে।
  • সাধারণ ভুল ৪ — "ফসফোগ্লাইকোলেট একটি ৩-কার্বন যৌগ": ভুল। আলোকশ্বসনে RuBP (5C) জারিত হয়ে ১টি 3-PGA (3C) এবং ১টি ফসফোগ্লাইকোলেট (2C) গঠন করে।

11. Concise Final Recap / সংক্ষিপ্ত চূড়ান্ত সারসংক্ষেপ

English
  • Light Reactions (Thylakoids): PS II (P680) splits water (\(\mathrm{O}_2\) evolved from \(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}\)); Z-scheme makes ATP + NADPH; Cyclic PS I makes only ATP; Chemiosmosis accumulates \(\mathrm{H}^+\) in lumen (low pH) and forms ATP via \(\mathrm{CF}_0\text{–}\mathrm{CF}_1\).
  • Calvin Cycle (Stroma): Universal in all photosynthetic plants; RuBisCO fixes \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) onto RuBP (5C) → 2 × 3-PGA (3C); 1 Glucose = 6 turns = 18 ATP + 12 NADPH.
  • C4 & Photorespiration: Kranz anatomy + PEPcase (mesophyll, OAA 4C) + RuBisCO (bundle sheath) concentrates \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) and eliminates photorespiration.
  • Limiting Factors: Light saturates at 10% sunlight; C4 saturates at ~360 µL L−1 \(\mathrm{CO}_2\), C3 beyond 450 µL L−1.
বাংলা (Bengali)
  • আলোক দশা (থাইলাকয়েড): PS II (P680) জল বিশ্লেষণ করে (\(\mathrm{O}_2\) আসে \(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}\) থেকে); Z-স্কিমে ATP + NADPH তৈরি হয়; চক্রীয় PS I-এ কেবল ATP; কেমিওসমোসিসে লুমেনে \(\mathrm{H}^+\) জমা হয়ে \(\mathrm{CF}_0\text{–}\mathrm{CF}_1\)-এর মাধ্যমে ATP তৈরি হয়।
  • কেলভিন চক্র (স্ট্রোমা): সর্বজনীন পথ; RuBisCO-এর সাহায্যে RuBP (5C) + \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) → ২ × 3-PGA (3C); ১ গ্লুকোজ = ৬ আবর্তন = ১৮ ATP + ১২ NADPH।
  • C4 পথ ও আলোকশ্বসন: ক্র্যাঞ্জ শারীরস্থান ও PEPcase (মেসোফিল, OAA 4C) বান্ডিল শীথে \(\mathrm{CO}_2\) বাড়িয়ে আলোকশ্বসন প্রতিরোধ করে।
  • সীমাবদ্ধকারী শর্ত: ১০% সূর্যালোকে আলোক-সম্পৃক্তি; C4 ৩৬০ µL L−1 ও C3 ৪৫০ µL L−1-এর বেশি \(\mathrm{CO}_2\)-এ সম্পৃক্ত হয়।
