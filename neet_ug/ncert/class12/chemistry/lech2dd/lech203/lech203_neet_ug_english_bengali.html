1. Learning Objectives & Chapter Overview / শিক্ষণ উদ্দেশ্য ও অধ্যায় পরিচিতি

English

Carbonyl compounds—molecules containing the carbon–oxygen double bond (\(>\mathrm{C=O}\))—form the backbone of synthetic organic chemistry and biological metabolism. Depending on the groups bonded to the carbonyl carbon, this family branches into aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids, and acid derivatives.

After completing this tutorial, you will be able to:

  • Assign IUPAC and common names to aliphatic, cyclic, and aromatic aldehydes, ketones, and carboxylic acids.
  • Relate the \(sp^2\)-hybridised trigonal planar geometry and bond polarity of the carbonyl and carboxyl groups to their boiling points, solubility, and chemical reactivity.
  • Select appropriate reagents for preparing aldehydes, ketones, and carboxylic acids without over-oxidation or over-reduction.
  • Explain nucleophilic addition mechanisms, \(\alpha\)-hydrogen reactivity (aldol and cross-aldol condensations), and disproportionation (Cannizzaro reaction).
  • Compare acid strengths using \(\mathrm{p}K_a\) values, resonance stabilisation, and inductive effects of electron-withdrawing (EWG) and electron-donating (EDG) substituents.
  • Distinguish functional groups rapidly using Tollens', Fehling's, iodoform, 2,4-DNP, and sodium hydrogencarbonate tests.

2. Carbonyl Taxonomy, Structure & Nomenclature / কার্বনিল শ্রেণিবিভাগ, গঠন ও নামকরণ

English

2.1 Functional Group Classification

  • Aldehydes (\(\mathrm{R-CHO}\)): The carbonyl carbon is bonded to at least one hydrogen atom (and one alkyl/aryl group, or another \(\mathrm{H}\) in methanal).
  • Ketones (\(\mathrm{R-CO-R'}\)): The carbonyl carbon is bonded to two carbon-containing groups (alkyl or aryl).
  • Carboxylic Acids (\(\mathrm{R-COOH}\)): The carbonyl group is bonded to a hydroxyl (\(-\mathrm{OH}\)) group, forming the carboxyl group.
  • Carboxylic Acid Derivatives: Replacing \(-\mathrm{OH}\) with \(-\mathrm{X}\) gives acyl halides (\(\mathrm{RCOX}\)), with \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\) gives amides (\(\mathrm{RCONH_2}\)), with \(-\mathrm{OR'}\) gives esters (\(\mathrm{RCOOR'}\)), and with \(-\mathrm{OCOR'}\) gives acid anhydrides (\(\mathrm{RCOOCOR'}\)).

2.2 Orbital Structure and Polarity of \(>\mathrm{C=O}\)

The carbonyl carbon is \(sp^2\)-hybridised, forming three coplanar \(\sigma\) bonds separated by approximately \(120^\circ\). Its unhybridised \(p\)-orbital overlaps sideways with a \(p\)-orbital of oxygen to form a \(\pi\) bond, placing the \(\pi\)-electron cloud above and below the molecular plane. Oxygen retains two non-bonding lone pairs.

Because oxygen is significantly more electronegative than carbon, the \(\mathrm{C=O}\) bond is strongly polarised. Resonance gives a dipolar contributor (\(\mathrm{C^+-O^-}\)), making the carbonyl carbon an electrophilic (Lewis acid) centre and the carbonyl oxygen a nucleophilic (Lewis base) centre. Consequently, carbonyl compounds have larger dipole moments than ethers.

2.3 Key Nomenclature Rules

Class Common Naming IUPAC Naming
Aldehydes Replace -ic acid of corresponding acid with -aldehyde (e.g., Acetaldehyde, Isobutyraldehyde). Substituents use \(\alpha, \beta, \gamma\) starting from carbon next to \(-\mathrm{CHO}\). Replace alkane -e with -al; \(-\mathrm{CHO}\) carbon is C-1. On a ring, use suffix carbaldehyde (e.g., Cyclohexanecarbaldehyde). If 3+ \(-\mathrm{CHO}\) groups are on a chain, use tricarbaldehyde (e.g., Propane-1,2,3-tricarbaldehyde).
Ketones Name the two alkyl/aryl groups + ketone (e.g., Methyl n-propyl ketone). Alkyl phenyl ketones use acyl prefix + phenone (Acetophenone). Substituents use \(\alpha, \alpha', \beta, \beta'\). Replace alkane -e with -one; number from the end nearer to \(>\mathrm{C=O}\). In cyclic ketones, carbonyl carbon is C-1 (e.g., 2-Methylcyclohexanone).
Carboxylic Acids Derived from Latin/Greek natural sources ending in -ic acid (Formic, Acetic, Butyric, Oxalic, Malonic, Succinic, Glutaric, Adipic acids). Replace alkane -e with -oic acid; \(-\mathrm{COOH}\) carbon is C-1. For polycarboxylic acids, retain alkane name + dicarboxylic acid, etc. (e.g., Butanedioic acid, Propane-1,2,3-tricarboxylic acid).

২.২ \(>\mathrm{C=O}\) মূলকের কক্ষক গঠন ও মেরুতা

কার্বনিল কার্বনটি \(sp^2\)-সংকরায়িত এবং প্রায় \(120^\circ\) কোণে একই সমতলে তিনটি \(\sigma\) বন্ধন গঠন করে। কার্বনের অসংকরায়িত \(p\)-কক্ষক অক্সিজেনের \(p\)-কক্ষকের সাথে পাশাপাশি অধিক্রমণ করে একটি \(\pi\) বন্ধন তৈরি করে, যার ফলে আণবিক তলের উপরে ও নিচে \(\pi\)-ইলেকট্রন মেঘ অবস্থান করে। অক্সিজেনের দুটি নিঃসঙ্গ ইলেকট্রন জোড় থাকে।

কার্বনের তুলনায় অক্সিজেনের তড়িৎ-ঋণাত্মকতা বেশি হওয়ায় \(\mathrm{C=O}\) বন্ধনটি অত্যন্ত মেরুবর্তী হয়। রেজোন্যান্সের কারণে একটি দ্বিমেরু গঠন (\(\mathrm{C^+-O^-}\)) সৃষ্টি হয়, ফলে কার্বনিল কার্বন ইলেকট্রন-আকর্ষী (লুইস অ্যাসিড) এবং অক্সিজেন কেন্দ্রাকর্ষী (লুইস ক্ষার) হিসেবে আচরণ করে। এজন্য কার্বনিল যৌগের দ্বিমেরু ভ্রামক ইথারের চেয়ে বেশি হয়।

3. Preparation of Aldehydes and Ketones / অ্যালডিহাইড ও কিটোন প্রস্তুতি

English

3.1 Common Methods for Both

  • Controlled Oxidation of Alcohols: Primary alcohols give aldehydes (using mild reagents like PCC to avoid over-oxidation to carboxylic acids), while secondary alcohols give ketones (using anhydrous \(\mathrm{CrO_3}\) or PCC).
  • Catalytic Dehydrogenation: Passing vapours of volatile primary or secondary alcohols over heated heavy metal catalysts (\(\mathrm{Cu}\) or \(\mathrm{Ag}\) at \(573\,\mathrm{K}\)) yields aldehydes or ketones, respectively.
  • Reductive Ozonolysis of Alkenes: Treating an alkene with \(\mathrm{O_3}\) followed by \(\mathrm{Zn/H_2O}\) cleaves the \(\mathrm{C=C}\) bond to form aldehydes, ketones, or a mixture depending on alkene substitution.
  • Hydration of Alkynes: Addition of water in the presence of \(\mathrm{HgSO_4}\) and \(\mathrm{H_2SO_4}\) converts ethyne exclusively to ethanal (acetaldehyde), whereas all higher/substituted alkynes yield ketones via Markovnikov hydration and tautomerisation.

3.3 Exclusive Methods for Ketones

  • From Acyl Chlorides & Dialkylcadmium: Grignard reagents react with \(\mathrm{CdCl_2}\) to form \(\mathrm{R_2Cd}\), which is less reactive than \(\mathrm{RMgX}\) and reacts with \(\mathrm{R'COCl}\) to stop cleanly at the ketone (\(\mathrm{R'-CO-R}\)).
  • From Nitriles: Treating \(\mathrm{R-C\equiv N}\) with \(\mathrm{R'MgX}\) in dry ether followed by acidic hydrolysis (\(\mathrm{H_3O^+}\)) gives ketone \(\mathrm{R-CO-R'}\).
  • Friedel–Crafts Acylation: Benzene or an activated arene reacts with \(\mathrm{RCOCl}\) or \(\mathrm{ArCOCl}\) in the presence of anhydrous \(\mathrm{AlCl_3}\) to yield aromatic ketones.

4. Physical Properties & Nucleophilic Addition / ভৌত ধর্ম ও কেন্দ্রাকর্ষী সংযোজন

English

4.1 Boiling Points and Solubility

  • Boiling Point Trend: For comparable molecular masses, boiling points follow:
    \[\text{Hydrocarbons (van der Waals)} < \text{Ethers (weak dipole)} < \text{Aldehydes / Ketones (strong dipole–dipole)} < \text{Alcohols (intermolecular H-bonding)}\]
  • Water Solubility: Lower aldehydes and ketones (up to 4 carbons, such as methanal, ethanal, and propanone) are miscible with water because their carbonyl oxygen forms hydrogen bonds with water molecules. Solubility drops rapidly as the hydrophobic alkyl chain lengthens.

4.2 Nucleophilic Addition Mechanism & Reactivity

Unlike alkenes (which undergo electrophilic addition), polar carbonyls undergo nucleophilic addition. A nucleophile (\(\mathrm{Nu^-}\)) attacks the electrophilic carbonyl carbon approximately perpendicular to the \(sp^2\) plane, changing carbon hybridisation from \(sp^2\) to \(sp^3\) and forming a tetrahedral alkoxide intermediate. Rapid protonation yields the neutral addition product.

Tutorial Diagram 2: Perpendicular nucleophilic attack on a planar carbonyl carbon to form a tetrahedral adduct.
Reactivity Rule (High-Yield for NEET): Aldehydes are more reactive than ketones towards nucleophilic addition for two reasons:
  1. Steric Factor: Two alkyl/aryl groups in ketones hinder nucleophile approach more than one group in aldehydes.
  2. Electronic Factor: Two \(+I\) alkyl groups in ketones reduce the \(\delta^+\) charge (electrophilicity) on the carbonyl carbon more strongly than one alkyl group in aldehydes.
  3. Aromatic Carbonyls: Benzaldehyde is less reactive than aliphatic aldehydes (like propanal) because resonance with the benzene ring reduces the positive charge on the carbonyl carbon.

4.3 Important Nucleophilic Additions & Addition–Elimination Reactions

  • \(\mathrm{HCN}\) (Base-catalysed): Generates \(\mathrm{CN^-}\) nucleophile to form cyanohydrins (\(\mathrm{RCH(OH)CN}\)).
  • \(\mathrm{NaHSO_3}\): Forms crystalline, water-soluble bisulphite adducts (equilibrium lies right for aldehydes and unhindered methyl ketones). Used to separate and purify aldehydes, as dilute acid or alkali regenerates the original carbonyl.
  • Alcohols / Diols (Dry \(\mathrm{HCl}\)): Aldehydes + 1 eq. \(\mathrm{R'OH} \rightleftharpoons\) Hemiacetal \(\xrightarrow{\mathrm{R'OH}}\) Acetal (gem-dialkoxy). Ketones + ethylene glycol \(\rightleftharpoons\) cyclic ethylene glycol ketal. Acetals/ketals are stable to base but hydrolyse in aqueous acid.
  • Ammonia Derivatives (\(\mathrm{H_2N-Z}\), weakly acidic pH): Reversible addition followed by dehydration gives \(>\mathrm{C=N-Z}\):
    • \(\mathrm{Z = -H}\) (\(\mathrm{NH_3}\)) \(\rightarrow\) Imine; \(\mathrm{Z = -R}\) (amine) \(\rightarrow\) Schiff's base.
    • \(\mathrm{Z = -OH}\) (hydroxylamine) \(\rightarrow\) Oxime; \(\mathrm{Z = -NH_2}\) (hydrazine) \(\rightarrow\) Hydrazone.
    • \(\mathrm{Z = -NHC_6H_5}\) \(\rightarrow\) Phenylhydrazone; \(\mathrm{Z = -NH-C_6H_3(NO_2)_2}\) (2,4-DNP) \(\rightarrow\) 2,4-Dinitrophenylhydrazone (yellow/orange/red solid used to identify carbonyls).
    • \(\mathrm{Z = -NHCONH_2}\) (semicarbazide) \(\rightarrow\) Semicarbazone. Note: Only the \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\) away from \(\mathrm{C=O}\) attacks because the other \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\) lone pair is delocalised into \(\mathrm{C=O}\) by resonance.

5. Reduction, Oxidation, \(\alpha\)-Hydrogen & Ring Reactions / বিজারণ, জারণ ও \(\alpha\)-হাইড্রোজেনের বিক্রিয়া

English

5.1 Reduction

  • To Alcohols: \(\mathrm{NaBH_4}\), \(\mathrm{LiAlH_4}\), or \(\mathrm{H_2/Ni}\) reduces aldehydes to \(1^\circ\) alcohols and ketones to \(2^\circ\) alcohols.
  • To Hydrocarbons (\(>\mathrm{C=O} \rightarrow >\mathrm{CH_2}\)):
    • Clemmensen Reduction: \(\mathrm{Zn\text{-}Hg} / \text{conc. }\mathrm{HCl}\) (acidic medium; avoid if acid-sensitive groups like \(-\mathrm{OH}\) or alkenes are present).
    • Wolff–Kishner Reduction: \(\mathrm{NH_2NH_2}\) followed by \(\mathrm{KOH} / \text{ethylene glycol}, \Delta\) (basic medium; avoid if base-sensitive groups like esters or halogens are present).

5.2 Oxidation & Distinguishing Tests

  • Tollens' Test (Ammoniacal \(\mathrm{AgNO_3}\)): Warm with \([\mathrm{Ag(NH_3)_2}]^+\) in alkaline medium. Both aliphatic and aromatic aldehydes give a bright silver mirror (\(\mathrm{Ag}\)) and carboxylate ion. Ketones do not respond.
  • Fehling's Test: Mix Fehling A (aq. \(\mathrm{CuSO_4}\)) + Fehling B (alkaline Rochelle salt). Heat with aliphatic aldehydes to obtain a reddish-brown precipitate of \(\mathrm{Cu_2O}\). Crucial Exception: Aromatic aldehydes (like benzaldehyde) and ketones do NOT give Fehling's test.
  • Haloform / Iodoform Test (\(\mathrm{I_2 + NaOH}\) or \(\mathrm{NaOI}\)): Given by compounds having \(\mathrm{CH_3-CO-}\) (methyl ketones and ethanal) or \(\mathrm{CH_3-CH(OH)-}\) (which oxidises in situ to \(\mathrm{CH_3-CO-}\)). Forms a yellow precipitate of \(\mathrm{CHI_3}\) and a carboxylate salt with one carbon less. Does not cleave \(\mathrm{C=C}\) double bonds.

5.3 Reactions Due to \(\alpha\)-Hydrogen & Disproportionation

  • Acidity of \(\alpha\)-H: The electron-withdrawing \(>\mathrm{C=O}\) group and resonance stabilisation of the enolate conjugate base make \(\alpha\)-hydrogens acidic.
  • Aldol Condensation: Aldehydes or ketones with at least one \(\alpha\)-hydrogen react in dilute alkali (\(\mathrm{NaOH}\) or \(\mathrm{Ba(OH)_2}\)) to give \(\beta\)-hydroxy aldehydes (aldols) or \(\beta\)-hydroxy ketones (ketols), which dehydrate on heating to \(\alpha,\beta\)-unsaturated carbonyls.
  • Cross-Aldol Condensation: Between two different carbonyls. If both have \(\alpha\)-H (e.g., ethanal + propanal), a mixture of four products (two self-aldol + two cross-aldol) forms. If one lacks \(\alpha\)-H (e.g., benzaldehyde + acetophenone), one major cross-aldol product (benzalacetophenone) predominates.
  • Cannizzaro Reaction: Aldehydes without any \(\alpha\)-hydrogen (e.g., \(\mathrm{HCHO}\), \(\mathrm{C_6H_5CHO}\), \(\mathrm{(CH_3)_3CCHO}\)) undergo self-oxidation and reduction (disproportionation) with concentrated alkali (\(\mathrm{NaOH}/\mathrm{KOH}\)) to give one molecule of alcohol and one molecule of carboxylic acid salt.
  • Electrophilic Ring Substitution: In aromatic aldehydes and ketones, \(-\mathrm{CHO}\) and \(-\mathrm{COR}\) are strongly deactivating and meta-directing (e.g., nitration of benzaldehyde with \(\mathrm{HNO_3/H_2SO_4}\) at \(273\text{–}283\,\mathrm{K}\) gives m-nitrobenzaldehyde).

৫.৩ \(\alpha\)-হাইড্রোজেনের বিক্রিয়া ও অসামঞ্জস্যতা

  • অ্যালডল ঘনীভবন: অন্তত একটি \(\alpha\)-হাইড্রোজেন যুক্ত অ্যালডিহাইড বা কিটোন লঘু ক্ষারের উপস্থিতিতে \(\beta\)-হাইড্রক্সি অ্যালডিহাইড (অ্যালডল) বা কিটোল গঠন করে, যা উত্তপ্ত করলে নিরুদিত হয়ে \(\alpha,\beta\)-অসম্পৃক্ত কার্বনিল যৌগ দেয়।
  • ক্রস-অ্যালডল ঘনীভবন: দুটি ভিন্ন কার্বনিল যৌগের মধ্যে বিক্রিয়া। উভয়েরই \(\alpha\)-H থাকলে ৪টি উৎপাদের মিশ্রণ তৈরি হয়। একটিতে \(\alpha\)-H না থাকলে (যেমন বেঞ্জ্যালডিহাইড + অ্যাসিটোফেনোন) একটি প্রধান উৎপাদ তৈরি হয়।
  • ক্যানিজারো বিক্রিয়া: \(\alpha\)-হাইড্রোজেন বিহীন অ্যালডিহাইড (যেমন \(\mathrm{HCHO}\), \(\mathrm{C_6H_5CHO}\)) গাঢ় ক্ষার (\(\mathrm{NaOH}/\mathrm{KOH}\)) সহ উত্তপ্ত করলে স্বতঃ-জারণ ও বিজারণের মাধ্যমে এক অণু অ্যালকোহল ও এক অণু কার্বক্সিলিক অ্যাসিডের লবণ উৎপন্ন করে।
  • ইলেকট্রন-আকর্ষী প্রতিস্থাপন: অ্যারোমেটিক অ্যালডিহাইড ও কিটোনে \(-\mathrm{CHO}\) এবং \(-\mathrm{COR}\) মূলক বলয় নিষ্ক্রিয়কারী এবং মেটা-নির্দেশক।

6. Carboxylic Acids: Preparation, Physical Properties & Acidity / কার্বক্সিলিক অ্যাসিড: প্রস্তুতি, ভৌত ধর্ম ও অম্লত্ব

English

6.1 Preparation Methods

  • Oxidation of \(1^\circ\) Alcohols & Aldehydes: Using alkaline/acidic \(\mathrm{KMnO_4}\), \(\mathrm{K_2Cr_2O_7/H_2SO_4}\), or Jones reagent (\(\mathrm{CrO_3\cdot H_2SO_4}\)).
  • Side-Chain Oxidation of Alkylbenzenes: Vigorous oxidation with \(\mathrm{KMnO_4\text{-}KOH}\) followed by \(\mathrm{H_3O^+}\) converts any \(1^\circ\) or \(2^\circ\) alkyl side chain (regardless of length) into \(-\mathrm{COOH}\). Exception: Tertiary alkyl groups (no benzylic \(\mathrm{H}\)) are not oxidised.
  • Hydrolysis of Nitriles & Amides: \(\mathrm{R-CN} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{H^+/\mathrm{OH^-}, H_2O}} \mathrm{R-CONH_2} \xrightarrow{\Delta} \mathrm{R-COOH}\).
  • Grignard Reagent + Dry Ice (\(\mathrm{CO_2}\)): \(\mathrm{RMgX + CO_2} \xrightarrow{\text{dry ether}} \mathrm{RCOOMgX} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{H_3O^+}} \mathrm{RCOOH}\) (increases chain length by 1 carbon).
  • Hydrolysis of Acid Derivatives: Acyl chlorides, anhydrides, and esters hydrolyse in aqueous acid or base (followed by acidification) to yield carboxylic acids.

6.2 Physical Properties & Dimerisation

Carboxylic acids have higher boiling points than aldehydes, ketones, and even alcohols of comparable molecular mass because they form stronger, more extensive intermolecular hydrogen bonds. Even in the vapour phase or aprotic solvents, most carboxylic acids exist as cyclic dimers.

6.3 Acidity Trends (\(\mathrm{p}K_a = -\log K_a\))

Smaller \(\mathrm{p}K_a\) means a stronger acid. Carboxylic acids (\(\mathrm{p}K_a \approx 4\text{–}5\)) are stronger acids than phenols (\(\mathrm{p}K_a \approx 10\)) and alcohols (\(\mathrm{p}K_a \approx 16\)) because the conjugate base carboxylate ion (\(\mathrm{RCOO^-}\)) is stabilised by two equivalent resonance structures where the negative charge is delocalised over two electronegative oxygen atoms. In phenoxide ion, resonance structures are non-equivalent and place negative charge on less electronegative ring carbons.

  • Electron-Withdrawing Groups (EWG, \(-I\) / \(-R\)): Stabilise \(\mathrm{RCOO^-}\) and increase acidity in the order: \(\mathrm{Ph < I < Br < Cl < F < CN < NO_2 < CF_3}\). Inductive effect weakens rapidly with distance from \(-\mathrm{COOH}\).
  • Electron-Donating Groups (EDG, \(+I\) / \(+R\)): Destabilise \(\mathrm{RCOO^-}\) and decrease acidity (e.g., \(\mathrm{HCOOH > CH_3COOH > CH_3CH_2COOH}\)).
  • Aromatic Acids: Direct attachment of phenyl or vinyl to \(-\mathrm{COOH}\) increases acidity relative to acetic acid because of the higher electronegativity of the \(sp^2\)-hybridised carbon. On a benzene ring, p-nitrobenzoic acid (\(\mathrm{p}K_a = 3.41\)) > benzoic acid (\(\mathrm{p}K_a = 4.19\)) > p-methoxybenzoic acid (\(\mathrm{p}K_a = 4.46\)).

৬.২ ভৌত ধর্ম ও ডাইমার গঠন

সমতুল্য আণবিক ভরের অ্যালডিহাইড, কিটোন ও অ্যালকোহলের চেয়ে কার্বক্সিলিক অ্যাসিডের স্ফুটনাঙ্ক বেশি হয়, কারণ এরা শক্তিশালী আন্তঃআণবিক হাইড্রোজেন বন্ধন দ্বারা আবদ্ধ থাকে। এমনকি বাষ্পীয় দশায় বা অ্যাপ্রোটিক দ্রাবকেও এরা চাক্রিক ডাইমার রূপে অবস্থান করে।

৬.৩ অম্লত্বের ক্রম (\(\mathrm{p}K_a = -\log K_a\))

\(\mathrm{p}K_a\) মান যত কম, অ্যাসিড তত তীব্র। কার্বক্সিলিক অ্যাসিড (\(\mathrm{p}K_a \approx 4\text{–}5\)) ফেনল (\(\mathrm{p}K_a \approx 10\)) ও অ্যালকোহলের (\(\mathrm{p}K_a \approx 16\)) চেয়ে বেশি আম্লিক, কারণ এর অনুবন্ধী ক্ষার কার্বক্সিলেট আয়ন (\(\mathrm{RCOO^-}\)) দুটি সমতুল্য রেজোন্যান্স গঠন দ্বারা স্থায়িত্ব লাভ করে যেখানে ঋণাত্মক আধান দুটি তড়িৎ-ঋণাত্মক অক্সিজেন পরমাণুর ওপর বিস্তৃত থাকে।

  • ইলেকট্রন-আকর্ষী মূলক (EWG): \(\mathrm{RCOO^-}\) আয়নকে স্থিতিশীল করে অম্লত্ব বাড়ায়: \(\mathrm{Ph < I < Br < Cl < F < CN < NO_2 < CF_3}\)।
  • ইলেকট্রন-দাতা মূলক (EDG): \(\mathrm{RCOO^-}\) আয়নের স্থায়িত্ব কমিয়ে অম্লত্ব হ্রাস করে (যেমন: \(\mathrm{HCOOH > CH_3COOH > CH_3CH_2COOH}\))।
  • অ্যারোমেটিক অ্যাসিড: \(sp^2\)-সংকরায়িত কার্বনের অধিক তড়িৎ-ঋণাত্মকতার কারণে বেঞ্জোয়িক অ্যাসিড অ্যাসিটিক অ্যাসিডের চেয়ে তীব্র। ক্রম: p-নাইট্রোবেঞ্জোয়িক অ্যাসিড (\(\mathrm{p}K_a = 3.41\)) > বেঞ্জোয়িক অ্যাসিড (\(\mathrm{p}K_a = 4.19\)) > p-মিথক্সিবেঞ্জোয়িক অ্যাসিড (\(\mathrm{p}K_a = 4.46\))।

Worked Example 2 (Acidity Ranking): Arrange the following four acids in increasing order of their acid strength: (i) 3-chlorobutanoic acid, (ii) 2-chlorobutanoic acid, (iii) butanoic acid, (iv) 2-fluorobutanoic acid.

Solution:

  • Butanoic acid has no electron-withdrawing halogen (\(+I\) alkyl chain only), so it is the weakest acid.
  • Between 3-chlorobutanoic acid and 2-chlorobutanoic acid, the \(-I\) effect decreases rapidly with distance; hence, the \(\alpha\)-chloro isomer (2-chlorobutanoic acid) is stronger than the \(\beta\)-chloro isomer (3-chlorobutanoic acid).
  • Between 2-fluorobutanoic acid and 2-chlorobutanoic acid, fluorine has a stronger \(-I\) effect than chlorine, stabilising the carboxylate anion more effectively.
  • Increasing Order: Butanoic acid < 3-chlorobutanoic acid < 2-chlorobutanoic acid < 2-fluorobutanoic acid.

English • Practice Question 1

Which of the following aldehydes will undergo a Cannizzaro reaction when heated with concentrated aqueous \(\mathrm{KOH}\), and NOT an aldol condensation?

  1. 2-Methylpropanal
  2. 2,2-Dimethylpropanal
  3. Phenylacetaldehyde
  4. Butanal

Correct Answer: B (2,2-Dimethylpropanal)

Worked Solution: Cannizzaro reaction occurs exclusively in aldehydes that possess no \(\alpha\)-hydrogen. In 2,2-dimethylpropanal (\(\mathrm{(CH_3)_3C-CHO}\)), the \(\alpha\)-carbon is bonded to three methyl groups and one \(-\mathrm{CHO}\) group, leaving zero \(\alpha\)-hydrogens. All other options (2-methylpropanal, phenylacetaldehyde, and butanal) have at least one \(\alpha\)-hydrogen and therefore undergo aldol condensation.

10. Common Pitfalls & Revision Checklist / সাধারণ ভুল ও পুনরালোচনা তালিকা

English

Common NEET UG Pitfalls to Avoid

  • Fehling's Test Trap: Benzaldehyde gives a positive Tollens' test, not a positive Fehling's test.
  • Semicarbazide Attack Site: Only the terminal \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\) (not adjacent to \(\mathrm{C=O}\)) acts as the nucleophile.
  • Reduction Selectivity: \(\mathrm{NaBH_4}\) reduces aldehydes/ketones, never carboxylic acids or esters.
  • Alkylbenzene Oxidation: \(\mathrm{KMnO_4/KOH}\) oxidises \(1^\circ\) and \(2^\circ\) side chains completely to \(-\mathrm{COOH}\), but tert-butylbenzene (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5C(CH_3)_3}\)) resists oxidation because it has no benzylic hydrogen.
  • Phenols vs Carboxylic Acids: Both react with \(\mathrm{NaOH}\) and \(\mathrm{Na}\), but only carboxylic acids liberate \(\mathrm{CO_2}\) with \(\mathrm{NaHCO_3}\).
বাংলা (Bengali)

NEET UG পরীক্ষায় এড়িয়ে চলার মতো সাধারণ ভুলসমূহ

  • ফেহলিং পরীক্ষার ফাঁদ: বেঞ্জ্যালডিহাইড টলেন্স পরীক্ষায় সাড়া দিলেও ফেহলিং পরীক্ষায় সাড়া দেয় না।

11. Concise Final Recap / সংক্ষিপ্ত চূড়ান্ত সারসংক্ষেপ

English

The \(sp^2\)-hybridised \(>\mathrm{C=O}\) group governs the chemistry of aldehydes and ketones through nucleophilic addition (aldehydes > ketones; aliphatic > aromatic) and \(\alpha\)-hydrogen acidity (aldol condensation with \(\alpha\)-H; Cannizzaro disproportionation without \(\alpha\)-H). Carboxylic acids exhibit elevated boiling points via cyclic H-bonded dimers, superior acidity due to equivalent carboxylate resonance (enhanced by EWG, lowered by EDG), and characteristic reactions such as esterification, \(\mathrm{SOCl_2}\) acyl chloride synthesis, HVZ \(\alpha\)-halogenation, and sodalime decarboxylation.

বাংলা (Bengali)

\(sp^2\)-সংকরায়িত \(>\mathrm{C=O}\) মূলক অ্যালডিহাইড ও কিটোনের কেন্দ্রাকর্ষী সংযোজন (অ্যালডিহাইড > কিটোন; অ্যালিফ্যাটিক > অ্যারোমেটিক) এবং \(\alpha\)-হাইড্রোজেনের বিক্রিয়া (\(\alpha\)-H থাকলে অ্যালডল ঘনীভবন; \(\alpha\)-H না থাকলে ক্যানিজারো বিক্রিয়া) নিয়ন্ত্রণ করে। অন্যদিকে, কার্বক্সিলিক অ্যাসিড চাক্রিক ডাইমার গঠনের জন্য উচ্চ স্ফুটনাঙ্ক, সমতুল্য কার্বক্সিলেট রেজোন্যান্সের জন্য উচ্চ অম্লত্ব (EWG দ্বারা বৃদ্ধি, EDG দ্বারা হ্রাস) এবং এস্টারিফিকেশন, HVZ \(\alpha\)-হ্যালোজিনেশন ও সোডালাইম ডিকার্বক্সিলেশনের মতো গুরুত্বপূর্ণ বিক্রিয়া প্রদর্শন করে।
