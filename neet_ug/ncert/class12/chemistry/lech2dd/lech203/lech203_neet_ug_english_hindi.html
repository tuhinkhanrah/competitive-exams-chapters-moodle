English

Carbonyl compounds contain the carbon–oxygen double bond (\(>\mathrm{C=O}\)) and form the backbone of many biomolecules, flavours, solvents, and synthetic intermediates. For NEET UG, this unit is high-yield because questions frequently test reagent selectivity, nucleophilic addition reactivity order, oxidation distinguishing tests, \(\alpha\)-hydrogen condensations, and substituent effects on carboxylic acid \(\mathrm{p}K_a\) values.

  • Apply IUPAC and common nomenclature rules to aldehydes, ketones, and carboxylic acids.
  • Relate \(sp^2\)-hybridised carbonyl geometry and bond polarity to nucleophilic addition reactivity.
  • Master key synthetic methods (Rosenmund, Stephen, DIBAL-H, Etard, Gattermann–Koch, Friedel–Crafts acylation, Grignard + \(\mathrm{CO_2}\)).
  • Distinguish aldehydes from ketones using Tollens', Fehling's, and haloform tests.
  • Predict products of aldol, cross-aldol, Cannizzaro, Clemmensen, Wolff–Kishner, and Hell–Volhard–Zelinsky reactions.
  • Compare acid strengths of aliphatic and aromatic carboxylic acids using inductive and resonance effects.

2. Structure, Polarity & Nomenclature | संरचना, ध्रुवता एवं नामकरण

English

2.1 Carbonyl Group Geometry & Electronic Nature

In aldehydes (\(\mathrm{R-CHO}\)), ketones (\(\mathrm{R-CO-R'}\)), and carboxylic acids (\(\mathrm{R-COOH}\)), the carbonyl carbon is \(sp^2\)-hybridised, forming three coplanar \(\sigma\)-bonds separated by approximately \(120^\circ\). The remaining unhybridised \(p\)-orbital overlaps laterally with a \(p\)-orbital of oxygen to create a \(\pi\)-bond, placing the \(\pi\)-electron cloud above and below the molecular plane.

Because oxygen is more electronegative than carbon, the \(\mathrm{C=O}\) bond is strongly polarised. Resonance between the neutral structure (\(>\mathrm{C=O}\)) and the dipolar contributor (\(>\mathrm{C^+-O^-}\)) gives carbonyl compounds substantial dipole moments (higher than ethers). Consequently, the carbonyl carbon acts as an electrophile (Lewis acid), while the carbonyl oxygen acts as a nucleophile (Lewis base).

In carboxylic acids, lone-pair donation from the hydroxyl oxygen (\(\mathrm{-OH}\)) into the carbonyl carbon creates an additional resonance contributor, reducing the positive charge density on the carboxyl carbon and making it less electrophilic than aldehyde or ketone carbonyl carbons.

2.1 कार्बोनिल समूह की ज्यामिति एवं इलेक्ट्रॉनिक प्रकृति

ऐल्डिहाइड (\(\mathrm{R-CHO}\)), कीटोन (\(\mathrm{R-CO-R'}\)) और कार्बोक्सिलिक अम्ल (\(\mathrm{R-COOH}\)) में कार्बोनिल कार्बन \(sp^2\)-संकरित होता है तथा लगभग \(120^\circ\) के कोण पर तीन समतलीय \(\sigma\)-आबंध बनाता है। कार्बन का शेष असंकरित \(p\)-कक्षक ऑक्सीजन के \(p\)-कक्षक से पार्श्व अतिव्यापन कर \(\pi\)-आबंध बनाता है, जिससे \(\pi\)-इलेक्ट्रॉन अभ्र तल के ऊपर और नीचे स्थित रहता है।

ऑक्सीजन की उच्च विद्युतऋणात्मकता के कारण \(\mathrm{C=O}\) आबंध अत्यधिक ध्रुवित होता है। उदासीन संरचना (\(>\mathrm{C=O}\)) और द्विध्रुवीय संरचना (\(>\mathrm{C^+-O^-}\)) के मध्य अनुनाद के कारण कार्बोनिल यौगिकों का द्विध्रुव आघूर्ण ईथरों से अधिक होता है। अतः कार्बोनिल कार्बन एक इलेक्ट्रॉनरागी (लुईस अम्ल) तथा कार्बोनिल ऑक्सीजन एक नाभिकरागी (लुईस क्षार) केंद्र के रूप में कार्य करता है।

कार्बोक्सिलिक अम्लों में हाइड्रॉक्सिल ऑक्सीजन (\(\mathrm{-OH}\)) के एकाकी इलेक्ट्रॉन युग्म का कार्बोनिल कार्बन की ओर अनुनाद संचरण होने से कार्बोक्सिल कार्बन पर धनावेश घनत्व घट जाता है, जिससे यह ऐल्डिहाइड व कीटोन की तुलना में कम इलेक्ट्रॉनरागी होता है।

2.2 Essential IUPAC & Common Nomenclature Rules

  • Aldehydes: Replace the terminal -e of the alkane with -al. The \(\mathrm{-CHO}\) carbon is always C-1. When \(\mathrm{-CHO}\) is attached to a ring, use the suffix -carbaldehyde (e.g., cyclohexanecarbaldehyde; benzenecarbaldehyde / benzaldehyde). If three \(\mathrm{-CHO}\) groups are attached to an open chain symmetrically (e.g., \(\mathrm{OHC-CH_2-CH(CHO)-CH_2-CHO}\)), none is included in the parent chain to treat all three equally: propane-1,2,3-tricarbaldehyde.
  • Ketones: Replace -e with -one, numbering from the end closer to \(\mathrm{C=O}\). When a higher-priority group (such as \(\mathrm{-CHO}\) or \(\mathrm{-COOH}\)) is present, the ketonic oxygen is named as the prefix oxo- (e.g., 3-oxopentanal). Alkyl phenyl ketones carry the suffix -phenone (acetophenone, propiophenone).
  • Carboxylic Acids: Replace -e with -oic acid (C-1 is the \(\mathrm{-COOH}\) carbon). Common names use Greek letters (\(\alpha, \beta, \gamma\)) starting from the carbon adjacent to the functional group. Dicarboxylic acids such as \(\mathrm{HOOC-(CH_2)_n-COOH}\) follow the classic series: \(n=0\) Oxalic, \(n=1\) Malonic, \(n=2\) Succinic, \(n=3\) Glutaric, \(n=4\) Adipic acid.

3. Synthetic Routes to Aldehydes & Ketones | ऐल्डिहाइड एवं कीटोन के विरचन

English

Selective oxidation, controlled reduction, and organometallic coupling allow chemists to stop cleanly at the aldehyde or ketone stage without over-oxidation to carboxylic acids or over-reduction to alcohols.

  • Alcohol Oxidation & Dehydrogenation: Primary alcohols give aldehydes with mild/anhydrous oxidants (e.g., PCC) or catalytic dehydrogenation over \(\mathrm{Cu}\) or \(\mathrm{Ag}\) at \(573\,\mathrm{K}\); secondary alcohols yield ketones.
  • Hydrocarbons: Reductive ozonolysis (\(\mathrm{O_3}\), then \(\mathrm{Zn/H_2O}\)) cleaves \(\mathrm{C=C}\) into aldehydes and/or ketones. Acid-catalysed hydration (\(\mathrm{HgSO_4/H_2SO_4}\)) of ethyne gives ethanal, whereas all higher alkynes yield ketones.
  • Acyl Chlorides → Aldehydes (Rosenmund Reduction): \(\mathrm{RCOCl + H_2 \xrightarrow{Pd-BaSO_4} RCHO + HCl}\). Barium sulphate poisons the Pd catalyst to prevent further reduction to alcohol.
  • Nitriles & Esters → Aldehydes:
    • Stephen Reaction: \(\mathrm{R-CN \xrightarrow{SnCl_2/HCl} R-CH=NH \xrightarrow{H_3O^+} R-CHO}\).
    • DIBAL-H: \(\mathrm{AlH(i\text{-}Bu)_2}\) followed by \(\mathrm{H_2O}\) selectively reduces nitriles and esters to aldehydes without touching \(\mathrm{C=C}\) double bonds.
  • Aromatic Aldehydes from Arenes:
    • Etard Reaction: Toluene + \(\mathrm{CrO_2Cl_2}\) in \(\mathrm{CS_2}\) forms a brown chromium complex, hydrolysed to benzaldehyde.
    • Chromic Oxide + Acetic Anhydride: Toluene + \(\mathrm{CrO_3/(CH_3CO)_2O}\) at \(273\text{–}283\,\mathrm{K}\) traps the aldehyde as benzylidene diacetate, preventing over-oxidation; acid hydrolysis gives benzaldehyde.
    • Side-chain Chlorination: Toluene \(\xrightarrow{\mathrm{Cl_2}/h\nu}\) benzal chloride (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5CHCl_2}\)) \(\xrightarrow{\mathrm{H_2O},\,373\,\mathrm{K}}\) benzaldehyde.
    • Gattermann–Koch Reaction: Benzene + \(\mathrm{CO + HCl \xrightarrow{Anhyd.\,AlCl_3/CuCl}}\) benzaldehyde.
  • Ketone-Specific Routes:
    • Dialkylcadmium: \(2\mathrm{R'COCl + R_2Cd \rightarrow 2R'COR + CdCl_2}\) (where \(\mathrm{R_2Cd}\) is prepared from \(2\mathrm{RMgX + CdCl_2}\)). Unlike \(\mathrm{RMgX}\), \(\mathrm{R_2Cd}\) stops at the ketone.
    • Nitriles + Grignard Reagent: \(\mathrm{R-CN + R'MgX \xrightarrow{ether} \text{imine salt} \xrightarrow{H_3O^+} R-CO-R'}\).
    • Friedel–Crafts Acylation: \(\mathrm{ArH + RCOCl \xrightarrow{Anhyd.\,AlCl_3} Ar-CO-R + HCl}\).

  • नाइट्राइल व एस्टर से ऐल्डिहाइड:
    • स्टीफन अभिक्रिया: \(\mathrm{R-CN \xrightarrow{SnCl_2/HCl} R-CH=NH \xrightarrow{H_3O^+} R-CHO}\)।
    • DIBAL-H: \(\mathrm{AlH(i\text{-}Bu)_2}\) और तत्पश्चात \(\mathrm{H_2O}\) नाइट्राइल तथा एस्टर को \(\mathrm{C=C}\) द्वि-आबंध को प्रभावित किए बिना चयनात्मक रूप से ऐल्डिहाइड में अपचयित करता है।
  • ऐरीन से ऐरोमैटिक ऐल्डिहाइड:
    • ईटार्ड अभिक्रिया: टॉलूईन + \(\mathrm{CrO_2Cl_2}\) (\(\mathrm{CS_2}\) में) क्रोमियम संकुल बनाता है, जो जल-अपघटन पर बेन्ज़ैल्डिहाइड देता है।
    • क्रोमिक ऑक्साइड + ऐसीटिक ऐनहाइड्राइड: \(273\text{–}283\,\mathrm{K}\) पर \(\mathrm{CrO_3/(CH_3CO)_2O}\) बेन्ज़िलिडीन डाइऐसीटेट बनाता है, जो अम्लीय जल-अपघटन से बेन्ज़ैल्डिहाइड देता है।
    • पार्श्व-शृंखला क्लोरीनीकरण: टॉलूईन \(\xrightarrow{\mathrm{Cl_2}/h\nu}\) बेन्ज़ल क्लोराइड (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5CHCl_2}\)) \(\xrightarrow{\mathrm{H_2O},\,373\,\mathrm{K}}\) बेन्ज़ैल्डिहाइड।
    • गाटरमान–कोख अभिक्रिया: बेन्ज़ीन + \(\mathrm{CO + HCl \xrightarrow{\text{निर्जल } AlCl_3/CuCl}}\) बेन्ज़ैल्डिहाइड।
  • कीटोन-विशिष्ट विधियाँ:
    • डाइऐल्किलकैडमियम: \(2\mathrm{R'COCl + R_2Cd \rightarrow 2R'COR + CdCl_2}\) (जहाँ \(\mathrm{R_2Cd}\), \(2\mathrm{RMgX + CdCl_2}\) से बनता है)।
    • नाइट्राइल + ग्रिग्नार्ड अभिकर्मक: \(\mathrm{R-CN + R'MgX \xrightarrow{\text{ईथर}} \text{इमीन लवण} \xrightarrow{H_3O^+} R-CO-R'}\)।
    • फ्रीडेल–क्राफ्ट्स ऐसिलन: \(\mathrm{ArH + RCOCl \xrightarrow{\text{निर्जल } AlCl_3} Ar-CO-R + HCl}\)।

4. Physical Properties & Nucleophilic Addition | भौतिक गुणधर्म एवं नाभिकरागी योगज

English

4.1 Boiling Point and Solubility Trends

For comparable molecular masses, boiling points follow the order:

\[\text{Alkane (van der Waals)} < \text{Ether (weakly polar)} < \text{Aldehyde / Ketone (dipole–dipole)} < \text{Alcohol (H-bonding)} < \text{Carboxylic Acid (dimeric H-bonding)}\]

Lower aldehydes and ketones (methanal, ethanal, propanone) are completely miscible with water because their carbonyl oxygen forms hydrogen bonds with water molecules. As the hydrophobic alkyl chain lengthens, aqueous solubility drops sharply.

4.2 Mechanism & Reactivity Order of Nucleophilic Addition

A nucleophile (\(\mathrm{Nu^-}\)) attacks the electrophilic \(sp^2\) carbonyl carbon perpendicular to the trigonal plane, converting it into a tetrahedral \(sp^3\) alkoxide intermediate, which then accepts \(\mathrm{H^+}\) to yield the neutral addition product.

  • Aldehydes vs Ketones: Aldehydes are consistently more reactive than ketones due to: (1) Steric factor — ketones have two bulky \(\mathrm{R}\) groups hindering \(\mathrm{Nu^-}\) approach; (2) Electronic factor — two \(+I\) alkyl groups in ketones reduce the \(\delta^+\) charge on carbonyl carbon more strongly.
  • Aliphatic vs Aromatic: Benzaldehyde is less reactive than propanal because \(\pi\)-conjugation from the benzene ring delocalises electrons onto the carbonyl group, reducing its electrophilicity.
  • Substituted Benzaldehydes: Electron-withdrawing groups (\(\mathrm{-NO_2}\)) increase carbonyl \(\delta^+\) and enhance reactivity, whereas electron-donating groups (\(\mathrm{-CH_3}\), \(\mathrm{-OCH_3}\)) decrease reactivity. Thus: \(p\text{-Tolualdehyde} < \text{Benzaldehyde} < p\text{-Nitrobenzaldehyde}\).

नाभिकरागी (\(\mathrm{Nu^-}\)) समतलीय \(sp^2\) कार्बोनिल कार्बन पर लंबवत दिशा से आक्रमण कर चतुष्फलकीय \(sp^3\) ऐल्कॉक्साइड मध्यवर्ती बनाता है, जो माध्यम से \(\mathrm{H^+}\) ग्रहण कर उदासीन योगज उत्पाद देता है।

  • ऐल्डिहाइड बनाम कीटोन: ऐल्डिहाइड कीटोन की अपेक्षा अधिक अभिक्रियाशील होते हैं: (1) त्रिविम कारक — कीटोन में दो बड़े \(\mathrm{R}\) समूह \(\mathrm{Nu^-}\) के आक्रमण में बाधा डालते हैं; (2) इलेक्ट्रॉनिक कारक — कीटोन में दो \(+I\) ऐल्किल समूह कार्बोनिल कार्बन के \(\delta^+\) आवेश को अधिक कम कर देते हैं।
  • ऐलिफैटिक बनाम ऐरोमैटिक: बेन्ज़ैल्डिहाइड, प्रोपेनैल से कम अभिक्रियाशील होता है क्योंकि बेन्ज़ीन वलय का अनुनाद कार्बोनिल कार्बन की इलेक्ट्रॉनरागी प्रकृति को घटा देता है।
  • प्रतिस्थापित बेन्ज़ैल्डिहाइड: इलेक्ट्रॉन-अपकर्षी समूह (\(\mathrm{-NO_2}\)) अभिक्रियाशीलता बढ़ाते हैं, जबकि इलेक्ट्रॉन-दाता समूह (\(\mathrm{-CH_3}\), \(\mathrm{-OCH_3}\)) अभिक्रियाशीलता घटाते हैं। अतः: \(p\text{-टॉलूऐल्डिहाइड} < \text{बेन्ज़ैल्डिहाइड} < p\text{-नाइट्रोबेन्ज़ैल्डिहाइड}\)।

5. Key Reactions of Aldehydes & Ketones | ऐल्डिहाइड एवं कीटोन की प्रमुख अभिक्रियाएँ

English

5.1 Addition & Addition–Elimination Reactions

  • HCN (Base-catalysed): Forms cyanohydrins via \(\mathrm{CN^-}\) attack.
  • \(\mathrm{NaHSO_3}\): Forms crystalline water-soluble bisulphite adducts (equilibrium favours products for aldehydes and unhindered methyl ketones; used for purification and regenerated by dilute acid/alkali).
  • Alcohols / Dry \(\mathrm{HCl}\): Aldehydes + 1 eq. \(\mathrm{ROH}\) \(\rightleftharpoons\) hemiacetal \(\rightleftharpoons\) acetal (gem-dialkoxy). Ketones react with ethylene glycol (\(\mathrm{HOCH_2CH_2OH}\)) to form cyclic ethylene glycol ketals. Both are stable to base and hydrolysed back by aqueous acid.
  • Ammonia Derivatives (\(\mathrm{H_2N-Z}\)): Weakly acidic medium (\(\mathrm{pH}\approx 3.5\)) promotes dehydration to \(>\mathrm{C=N-Z}\):
    • \(\mathrm{Z = -OH}\) (Hydroxylamine) → Oxime
    • \(\mathrm{Z = -NH_2}\) (Hydrazine) → Hydrazone
    • \(\mathrm{Z = -NHC_6H_3(NO_2)_2}\) (2,4-DNP) → 2,4-Dinitrophenylhydrazone (yellow/orange/red ppt, diagnostic test for carbonyls)
    • \(\mathrm{Z = -NHCONH_2}\) (Semicarbazide) → Semicarbazone (Note: only the \(\mathrm{-NH_2}\) away from \(\mathrm{C=O}\) attacks, because the \(\mathrm{-NH_2}\) attached to \(\mathrm{C=O}\) is resonance-delocalised and non-nucleophilic).

5.2 Reductions

  • To Alcohols: \(\mathrm{NaBH_4}\), \(\mathrm{LiAlH_4}\), or \(\mathrm{H_2/Ni}\) reduces aldehydes to \(1^\circ\) alcohols and ketones to \(2^\circ\) alcohols.
  • To Hydrocarbons (\(\mathrm{C=O \rightarrow CH_2}\)):
    • Clemmensen Reduction: \(\mathrm{Zn\text{-}Hg / conc.\,HCl}\) (avoid if molecule has acid-sensitive groups like \(\mathrm{-OH}\) or \(\mathrm{C=C}\)).
    • Wolff–Kishner Reduction: \(\mathrm{NH_2NH_2}\), then \(\mathrm{KOH}\) / ethylene glycol, heat (avoid if molecule has base-sensitive groups like \(\mathrm{-X}\) or esters).

5.3 Oxidation & Distinguishing Tests

  • Tollens' Test: Ammoniacal \(\mathrm{AgNO_3}\) (\([\mathrm{Ag(NH_3)_2]^+}\), \(\mathrm{OH^-}\)) oxidises both aliphatic and aromatic aldehydes to \(\mathrm{RCOO^-}\) and deposits a bright silver mirror (\(\mathrm{Ag}\)). Ketones do not react.
  • Fehling's Test: Alkaline \(\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}\) tartrate complex oxidises aliphatic aldehydes to \(\mathrm{RCOO^-}\), giving a red-brown precipitate of \(\mathrm{Cu_2O}\). Aromatic aldehydes (like benzaldehyde) and ketones do NOT respond to Fehling's test.
  • Haloform (Iodoform) Reaction: Compounds with \(\mathrm{CH_3-CO-}\) or \(\mathrm{CH_3-CH(OH)-}\) react with \(\mathrm{I_2/NaOH}\) (\(\mathrm{NaOI}\)) to form a yellow precipitate of \(\mathrm{CHI_3}\) and a carboxylate salt with one fewer carbon. \(\mathrm{C=C}\) double bonds remain unaffected.

5.4 \(\alpha\)-Hydrogen Reactions & Disproportionation

  • Aldol Condensation: Aldehydes or ketones with at least one \(\alpha\)-hydrogen react in dilute alkali (\(\mathrm{NaOH}\) or \(\mathrm{Ba(OH)_2}\)) to give \(\beta\)-hydroxy aldehydes (aldol) or \(\beta\)-hydroxy ketones (ketol), which dehydrate on heating to \(\alpha,\beta\)-unsaturated carbonyls. Cross-aldol between two different \(\alpha\)-H aldehydes gives a mixture of 4 products.
  • Cannizzaro Reaction: Aldehydes with no \(\alpha\)-hydrogen (e.g., \(\mathrm{HCHO}\), \(\mathrm{C_6H_5CHO}\), \(\mathrm{(CH_3)_3C-CHO}\)) undergo disproportionation in concentrated alkali (\(\Delta\)) to yield one molecule of alcohol and one molecule of carboxylic acid salt.

6. Carboxylic Acids: Preparation, Acidity & Reactions | कार्बोक्सिलिक अम्ल: विरचन, अम्लता एवं अभिक्रियाएँ

English

6.1 Key Preparations

  • Oxidation: \(1^\circ\) alcohols or aldehydes with \(\mathrm{KMnO_4/OH^-}\), \(\mathrm{K_2Cr_2O_7/H^+}\), or Jones reagent (\(\mathrm{CrO_3\cdot H_2SO_4}\)).
  • Alkylbenzenes: Vigorous oxidation with \(\mathrm{KMnO_4/KOH,\Delta}\) followed by \(\mathrm{H_3O^+}\) converts any \(1^\circ\) or \(2^\circ\) alkyl side chain (having at least one benzylic H) into \(\mathrm{-COOH}\). Tertiary alkyl groups (e.g., tert-butyl) are not oxidised.
  • Nitriles & Grignard Reagents (Ascending the series): \(\mathrm{R-CN \xrightarrow{H^+\text{ or }OH^-,\,H_2O,\,\Delta} R-COOH}\); \(\mathrm{R-MgX + CO_2 \xrightarrow{\text{dry ether}} \xrightarrow{H_3O^+} R-COOH}\).
  • Hydrolysis of Derivatives: Acyl chlorides, anhydrides, esters, and amides hydrolyse to carboxylic acids.

6.2 Acidity Trends & Substituent Effects (High-Yield NEET Topic)

Carboxylic acids react with \(\mathrm{NaHCO_3}\) with brisk effervescence of \(\mathrm{CO_2}\) (a diagnostic test distinguishing them from phenols). Carboxylic acids (\(\mathrm{p}K_a \approx 4\text{–}5\)) are much stronger acids than phenols (\(\mathrm{p}K_a \approx 10\)) and alcohols (\(\mathrm{p}K_a \approx 16\)) because the conjugate base carboxylate ion (\(\mathrm{RCOO^-}\)) is stabilised by two equivalent resonance structures with negative charge delocalised equally over two electronegative oxygen atoms, whereas phenoxide has non-equivalent structures placing negative charge on less electronegative carbons.

  • Inductive Effect (\(-I\) vs \(+I\)): Electron-withdrawing groups (EWG) stabilise \(\mathrm{RCOO^-}\) and increase acidity (lower \(\mathrm{p}K_a\)): \(\mathrm{CF_3 > NO_2 > CN > F > Cl > Br > I > Ph}\). Electron-donating alkyl groups (\(+I\)) destabilise \(\mathrm{RCOO^-}\) and decrease acidity. Thus: \(\mathrm{HCOOH > CH_3COOH > CH_3CH_2COOH}\).
  • \(sp^2\) Carbon Attachment: Direct attachment of phenyl or vinyl to \(\mathrm{-COOH}\) increases acidity relative to saturated analogues due to higher electronegativity of \(sp^2\) carbon (e.g., benzoic acid, \(\mathrm{p}K_a = 4.19\), is stronger than acetic acid, \(\mathrm{p}K_a = 4.76\)).
  • Aromatic Carboxylic Acids: \(4\text{-Nitrobenzoic acid}\ (\mathrm{p}K_a = 3.41) > \text{Benzoic acid}\ (\mathrm{p}K_a = 4.19) > 4\text{-Methoxybenzoic acid}\ (\mathrm{p}K_a = 4.46)\).

6.3 Characteristic Reactions

  • Cleavage of \(\mathrm{C-OH}\): Heating with \(\mathrm{P_2O_5}\) or \(\mathrm{H_2SO_4}\) gives anhydrides; \(\mathrm{R'OH/H^+}\) gives esters (nucleophilic acyl substitution); \(\mathrm{SOCl_2}\) (preferred, gaseous byproducts \(\mathrm{SO_2 + HCl}\)), \(\mathrm{PCl_5}\), or \(\mathrm{PCl_3}\) gives \(\mathrm{RCOCl}\); \(\mathrm{NH_3/\Delta}\) gives amides (phthalic acid + \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) on strong heating gives phthalimide).
  • Reduction: \(\mathrm{LiAlH_4/ether}\) or \(\mathrm{B_2H_6}\) (selective, leaves ester/nitro/halo intact) reduces \(\mathrm{-COOH}\) to \(\mathrm{-CH_2OH}\). \(\mathrm{NaBH_4}\) does not reduce carboxylic acids.
  • Decarboxylation: \(\mathrm{RCOONa \xrightarrow{NaOH + CaO (3:1),\,\Delta} R-H + Na_2CO_3}\). Kolbe electrolysis of \(\mathrm{RCOONa(aq)}\) gives \(\mathrm{R-R}\).
  • Hell–Volhard–Zelinsky (HVZ) Reaction: Carboxylic acids with \(\alpha\)-H react with \(\mathrm{Cl_2}\) or \(\mathrm{Br_2}\) in presence of red phosphorus followed by \(\mathrm{H_2O}\) to give \(\alpha\)-halocarboxylic acids.
  • Ring Substitution: \(\mathrm{-COOH}\) is deactivating and meta-directing. Benzoic acid does not undergo Friedel–Crafts reaction because \(\mathrm{AlCl_3}\) coordinates with the \(\mathrm{-COOH}\) group.

  • प्रेरणिक प्रभाव (\(-I\) बनाम \(+I\)): इलेक्ट्रॉन-अपकर्षी समूह (EWG) \(\mathrm{RCOO^-}\) को स्थायी कर अम्लता बढ़ाते हैं: \(\mathrm{CF_3 > NO_2 > CN > F > Cl > Br > I > Ph}\)। इलेक्ट्रॉन-दाता ऐल्किल समूह (\(+I\)) अम्लता घटाते हैं: \(\mathrm{HCOOH > CH_3COOH > CH_3CH_2COOH}\)।
  • \(sp^2\) कार्बन से जुड़ाव: फेनिल या वाइनिल समूह सीधे \(\mathrm{-COOH}\) से जुड़ने पर \(sp^2\) कार्बन की उच्च विद्युतऋणात्मकता के कारण अम्लता बढ़ जाती है (बेन्ज़ोइक अम्ल \(\mathrm{p}K_a = 4.19\) > ऐसीटिक अम्ल \(\mathrm{p}K_a = 4.76\))।

Worked Example 2: Structure Elucidation via Chemical Tests

Worked Example 3: Ranking Acid Strength

Problem: Arrange the following in increasing order of acid strength: \(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2COOH}\), \(\mathrm{ClCH_2CH_2COOH}\), \(\mathrm{CH_3CH(Cl)COOH}\), \(\mathrm{FCH_2COOH}\).

Increasing order: \(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2COOH < ClCH_2CH_2COOH < CH_3CH(Cl)COOH < FCH_2COOH}\).

9. Common Pitfalls, Revision Checklist & Final Recap | सामान्य त्रुटियाँ एवं त्वरित पुनरावृत्ति

English • Common Pitfalls
  • Fehling's vs Tollens': Never assume benzaldehyde gives Fehling's test—it gives a silver mirror with Tollens' reagent but fails Fehling's test.
  • Semicarbazide Attack Site: Only the terminal \(\mathrm{-NH_2}\) (hydrazinic nitrogen) reacts with carbonyls; the amide \(\mathrm{-NH_2}\) adjacent to \(\mathrm{C=O}\) is deactivated by resonance.
  • \(\mathrm{NaBH_4}\) Limitation: \(\mathrm{NaBH_4}\) reduces aldehydes/ketones but cannot reduce \(\mathrm{-COOH}\) or esters; use \(\mathrm{B_2H_6}\) or \(\mathrm{LiAlH_4}\) for carboxylic acids.
  • Tertiary Alkylbenzenes: \(\mathrm{KMnO_4/KOH}\) oxidises \(1^\circ\) and \(2^\circ\) alkyl side chains on benzene to \(\mathrm{-COOH}\), but tertiary alkyl groups (no benzylic H) remain untouched.
हिन्दी • सामान्य त्रुटियाँ
  • फेहलिंग बनाम टॉलेन: बेन्ज़ैल्डिहाइड टॉलेन अभिकर्मक के साथ रजत दर्पण देता है, परन्तु फेहलिंग परीक्षण नहीं देता।
  • सेमीकार्बेज़ाइड आक्रमण स्थल: केवल \(\mathrm{C=O}\) से दूर स्थित \(\mathrm{-NH_2}\) समूह ही कार्बोनिल से जुड़ता है; निकटवर्ती \(\mathrm{-NH_2}\) अनुनाद के कारण कम नाभिकरागी होता है।
  • \(\mathrm{NaBH_4}\) की सीमा: \(\mathrm{NaBH_4}\) ऐल्डिहाइड/कीटोन को अपचयित करता है, परन्तु \(\mathrm{-COOH}\) या एस्टर को अपचयित नहीं करता; इसके लिए \(\mathrm{B_2H_6}\) या \(\mathrm{LiAlH_4}\) प्रयोग करें।
  • तृतीयक ऐल्किलबेन्ज़ीन: \(\mathrm{KMnO_4/KOH}\) द्वारा केवल \(1^\circ\) व \(2^\circ\) ऐल्किल शृंखलाएँ ऑक्सीकृत होती हैं; तृतीयक ऐल्किल समूह (बेन्ज़िलिक H रहित) ऑक्सीकृत नहीं होते।
English • Concise Final Recap

Core Takeaway: Carbonyl chemistry revolves around the polarised \(sp^2\) \(\mathrm{C=O}\) bond (nucleophilic addition: aldehydes > ketones; EWG accelerates) and \(\alpha\)-C–H acidity (aldol when \(\alpha\)-H is present; Cannizzaro when absent). Carboxylic acids owe their high acidity (\(\mathrm{NaHCO_3}\) effervescence) and elevated boiling points (H-bonded dimers) to the carboxyl group and its equivalent-resonance carboxylate anion, with \(-I\)/\(-R\) substituents further strengthening acidity.

हिन्दी • संक्षिप्त अंतिम सारांश

मुख्य निष्कर्ष: कार्बोनिल रसायन ध्रुवित \(sp^2\) \(\mathrm{C=O}\) आबंध (नाभिकरागी योगज: ऐल्डिहाइड > कीटोन; EWG अभिक्रियाशीलता बढ़ाते हैं) तथा \(\alpha\)-C–H अम्लता (\(\alpha\)-H होने पर ऐल्डोल; न होने पर कैनिज़ारो) पर आधारित है। कार्बोक्सिलिक अम्लों की उच्च अम्लता (\(\mathrm{NaHCO_3}\) के साथ \(\mathrm{CO_2}\)) और उच्च क्वथनांक (H-आबंधित द्वितय) कार्बोक्सिल समूह तथा समतुल्य अनुनाद वाले कार्बोक्सिलेट आयन के कारण होते हैं।
