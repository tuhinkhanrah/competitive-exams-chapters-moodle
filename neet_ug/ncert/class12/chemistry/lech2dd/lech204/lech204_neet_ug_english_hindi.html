1. Objectives & Overview | उद्देश्य एवं परिदृश्य

English

Amines are organic nitrogenous derivatives of ammonia (\(\mathrm{NH}_3\)) formed by replacing one, two, or all three hydrogen atoms with alkyl or aryl groups. They play a vital commercial role as intermediates in the synthesis of pharmaceuticals, agrochemicals, polymers, and synthetic dyes.

Crucial Biomolecules and Drugs for NEET:

  • Adrenaline & Ephedrine: Both contain a secondary amino group (\(2^\circ\)) and physiologically function to elevate arterial blood pressure.
  • Novocain (Procaine): Synthetic amino compound widely employed as a local anaesthetic in dentistry.
  • Benadryl (Diphenhydramine): Classic antihistaminic drug bearing a tertiary amino group (\(3^\circ\)).
  • Quaternary Ammonium Salts: Widely formulated as cationic surfactants (fabric softeners, detergents).
  • Diazonium Salts (\(\mathrm{ArN_2^+X^-}\)): Unsurpassed synthetic gateways for preparing substituted aromatic hydrocarbons, haloarenes, cyanides, phenols, and azo dyes.
हिन्दी

ऐमीन, अमोनिया (\(\mathrm{NH}_3\)) के कार्बनिक व्युत्पन्न हैं, जो अमोनिया के एक, दो या तीनों हाइड्रोजन परमाणुओं को ऐल्किल अथवा ऐरिल समूहों द्वारा प्रतिस्थापित करने पर बनते हैं। दवाओं (औषधियों), रेशों (पॉलिमरों) और रंजकों के संश्लेषण में ऐमीन प्रमुख मध्यवर्तियों के रूप में प्रयुक्त होते हैं।

NEET परीक्षा हेतु महत्वपूर्ण औषधियाँ व जैव अणु:

  • ऐड्रीनेलिन एवं इफेड्रिन: दोनों में द्वितीयक ऐमीनो समूह (\(2^\circ\)) उपस्थित होता है; ये रक्तदाब (Blood Pressure) बढ़ाने के कार्य आते हैं।
  • नोवोकेन: एक संश्लेषित ऐमीनो यौगिक है जो दंत चिकित्सा में निश्चेतक (Local Anaesthetic) के रूप में प्रयुक्त होता है।
  • बेनाड्रिल: एक प्रसिद्ध प्रति-हिस्टैमिनिक (Antihistaminic) औषधि है, जिसमें तृतीयक ऐमीनो समूह (\(3^\circ\)) होता है।
  • चतुष्क अमोनियम लवण: इनका उपयोग धनायनिक पृष्ठ-सक्रियकों (Surfactants) के रूप में होता है।
  • डाइऐज़ोनियम लवण (\(\mathrm{ArN_2^+X^-}\)): ऐरोमैटिक यौगिकों जैसे हैलोऐरीन, सायनो, फीनॉल तथा ऐज़ो रंजकों के संश्लेषण में केंद्रीय मध्यवर्ती हैं।

2. Structure and Geometry of Amines | ऐमीन की संरचना एवं ज्यामिति

English

Like ammonia, the central nitrogen atom in amines is trivalent and possesses an unshared lone pair of electrons. The nitrogen atom undergoes \(sp^3\) hybridisation. Its electron-pair geometry is tetrahedral, while its molecular molecular shape is pyramidal.

Three of the \(sp^3\) hybrid orbitals overlap with orbitals of hydrogen or carbon atoms (forming \(\sigma\) bonds). The fourth \(sp^3\) hybrid orbital accommodates the unshared lone pair. Because lone pair–bond pair repulsion exceeds bond pair–bond pair repulsion, the bond angle \(\mathrm{C-N-E}\) (where \(\mathrm{E} = \mathrm{C}\) or \(\mathrm{H}\)) is compressed to less than the tetrahedral angle of \(109.5^\circ\). In trimethylamine, \((\mathrm{CH}_3)_3\mathrm{N}\), the \(\mathrm{C-N-C}\) bond angle is experimentally found to be \(108^\circ\).

हिन्दी

अमोनिया की भाँति ऐमीन में भी केंद्रीय नाइट्रोजन परमाणु त्रिसंयोजी (trivalent) होता है और उस पर एक असहभाजित इलेक्ट्रॉन युग्म (lone pair) उपस्थित रहता है। यहाँ नाइट्रोजन परमाणु \(sp^3\) संकरित होता है। इसकी इलेक्ट्रॉन युग्म ज्यामिति चतुष्फलकीय तथा अणु का आकार पिरैमिडी (Pyramidal) होता है।

नाइट्रोजन के तीन \(sp^3\) संकरित कक्षक हाइड्रोजन या कार्बन के कक्षकों के साथ अतिव्यापन कर सिग्मा (\(\sigma\)) आबंध बनाते हैं, जबकि चौथे कक्षक में एकाकी युग्म रहता है। एकाकी युग्म-आबंधी युग्म (lp-bp) प्रतिकर्षण के कारण \(\mathrm{C-N-E}\) आबंध कोण (\(\mathrm{E} = \mathrm{C}\) या \(\mathrm{H}\)) सामान्य चतुष्फलकीय कोण (\(109.5^\circ\)) से कम हो जाता है। उदाहरणार्थ, ट्राइमेथिलऐमीन में यह आबंध कोण \(108^\circ\) पाया जाता है।

3. Classification and Nomenclature | वर्गीकरण एवं नामकरण

English

Amines are classified according to the number of alkyl or aryl radicals attached directly to the nitrogen atom:

  • Primary (\(1^\circ\)) Amine: Exactly one hydrogen replaced: \(\mathrm{R-NH_2}\) or \(\mathrm{Ar-NH_2}\). Possesses the functional group \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\).
  • Secondary (\(2^\circ\)) Amine: Two hydrogens replaced: \(\mathrm{R-NH-R'}\) or \(\mathrm{Ar-NH-R}\). Possesses the imino group \(>\mathrm{NH}\).
  • Tertiary (\(3^\circ\)) Amine: All three hydrogens replaced: \(\mathrm{R_3N}\) or \(\mathrm{Ar-NR_2}\). Possesses tertiary nitrogen \(>\mathrm{N}-\).

Simple vs. Mixed Amines: If all alkyl/aryl groups are identical, the amine is 'simple' (e.g., \(\mathrm{CH_3-NH-CH_3}\)); if distinct, it is 'mixed' (e.g., \(\mathrm{CH_3-NH-C_2H_5}\)).

IUPAC Rules: Primary aliphatic amines are named as alkanamines (replacing terminal '-e' of alkane with '-amine'). In secondary and tertiary amines, the largest alkyl group forms the parent alkanamine chain, and substituents attached to nitrogen are assigned the locant prefix N- (e.g., N-methylethanamine).

हिन्दी

नाइट्रोजन परमाणु से जुड़े ऐल्किल या ऐरिल समूहों की संख्या के आधार पर ऐमीनों को वर्गीकृत किया जाता है:

  • प्राथमिक (\(1^\circ\)) ऐमीन: अमोनिया का केवल एक हाइड्रोजन प्रतिस्थापित होता है: \(\mathrm{R-NH_2}\) या \(\mathrm{Ar-NH_2}\) (ऐमीनो समूह)।
  • द्वितीयक (\(2^\circ\)) ऐमीन: दो हाइड्रोजन परमाणु प्रतिस्थापित होते हैं: \(\mathrm{R-NH-R'}\) (इमीनो समूह)।
  • तृतीयक (\(3^\circ\)) ऐमीन: तीनों हाइड्रोजन परमाणु प्रतिस्थापित होते हैं: \(\mathrm{R_3N}\)।

यदि सभी ऐल्किल अथवा ऐरिल समूह समान हों तो उन्हें 'सरल ऐमीन' तथा भिन्न होने पर 'मिश्रित ऐमीन' कहते हैं।

IUPAC नामकरण पद्धति: प्राथमिक ऐमीनों को संगत ऐल्केन के अंत से 'e' हटाकर 'amine' जोड़कर ऐल्केनामीन लिखा जाता है। द्वितीयक व तृतीयक ऐमीन में नाइट्रोजन पर उपस्थित प्रतिस्थापियों को निर्दिष्ट करने हेतु लोकेंट N- का प्रयोग किया जाता है (जैसे: N-मेथिलएथेनामीन)।

4. Methods of Preparation of Amines | ऐमीन विरचन की विधियाँ

English

1. Reduction of Nitro Compounds

Nitroalkanes or nitroarenes undergo smooth reduction to primary amines by catalytic hydrogenation (\(\mathrm{H_2}\) in the presence of finely divided \(\mathrm{Ni}\), \(\mathrm{Pt}\), or \(\mathrm{Pd}\)) or chemical reduction with active metals in mineral acid (\(\mathrm{Sn/HCl}\) or \(\mathrm{Fe/HCl}\)):

\[\mathrm{Ar-NO_2} + 3\mathrm{H_2} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Pd / Ethanol}} \mathrm{Ar-NH_2} + 2\mathrm{H_2O}\] \[\mathrm{Ar-NO_2} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Fe + HCl}} \mathrm{Ar-NH_2} + 2\mathrm{H_2O}\]
High-Yield NEET Fact: Reduction with iron scrap (\(\mathrm{Fe}\)) and hydrochloric acid (\(\mathrm{HCl}\)) is commercially and industrially preferred over \(\mathrm{Sn/HCl}\) because \(\mathrm{FeCl_2}\) produced during the reduction undergoes in situ aqueous hydrolysis to regenerate \(\mathrm{HCl}\). Consequently, only a catalytic triggering amount of hydrochloric acid is required.

2. Hofmann Ammonolysis of Alkyl Halides

Nucleophilic substitution in which ethanolic ammonia cleaves the \(\mathrm{C-X}\) bond of an alkyl or benzyl halide at \(373\,\mathrm{K}\) in a sealed tube:

\[\mathrm{NH_3} + \mathrm{R-X} \longrightarrow \mathrm{R-NH_3^+ X^-} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{NaOH}} \mathrm{R-NH_2} + \mathrm{H_2O} + \mathrm{NaX}\]

Because the primary amine formed is itself an active nucleophile, it continues reacting with remaining \(\mathrm{R-X}\) to yield a mixture of \(2^\circ\) amine, \(3^\circ\) amine, and finally quaternary ammonium salt \([\mathrm{R_4N^+X^-}]\):

\[\mathrm{RNH_2} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{RX}} \mathrm{R_2NH} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{RX}} \mathrm{R_3N} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{RX}} \mathrm{R_4N^+X^-}\]

Control of Product: To obtain the primary amine as the major product, a large excess of ammonia must be used. Reactivity order of halides: \(\mathrm{R-I} > \mathrm{R-Br} > \mathrm{R-Cl}\).

3. Reduction of Nitriles (Ascent of Series)

Nitriles (\(\mathrm{R-C\equiv N}\)) are reduced with lithium aluminium hydride (\(\mathrm{LiAlH_4}\)) or sodium amalgam in ethanol (\(\mathrm{Na(Hg)/C_2H_5OH}\)) or catalytic hydrogenation (\(\mathrm{H_2/Ni}\)) to primary amines containing one more carbon atom than the precursor alkyl halide:

\[\mathrm{R-C\equiv N} \xrightarrow[\text{or } \mathrm{LiAlH_4}]{\mathrm{H_2 / Ni}} \mathrm{R-CH_2-NH_2}\]

4. Reduction of Amides

Carboxamides on reduction with \(\mathrm{LiAlH_4}\) followed by aqueous workup yield primary amines with the same number of carbon atoms:

\[\mathrm{R-CONH_2} \xrightarrow[(2)\,\mathrm{H_2O}]{(1)\,\mathrm{LiAlH_4}} \mathrm{R-CH_2-NH_2}\]

5. Gabriel Phthalimide Synthesis

Exclusive method for the synthesis of pure aliphatic primary amines (\(1^\circ\)). Phthalimide reacts with ethanolic \(\mathrm{KOH}\) to generate potassium phthalimide, which undergoes nucleophilic substitution (\(\mathrm{S_N2}\)) with an alkyl halide to produce N-alkylphthalimide. Subsequent alkaline hydrolysis liberates pure primary aliphatic amine:

\[\text{Phthalimide} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{alc.\,KOH}} \text{Potassium Phthalimide} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{R-X}} \text{\textit{N}-Alkylphthalimide} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{NaOH(aq)}} \text{Sodium Phthalate} + \mathrm{R-NH_2}\]
Key NEET Limitation: Aromatic primary amines (e.g., aniline) cannot be prepared by Gabriel synthesis because aryl halides do not undergo nucleophilic substitution with the bulky phthalimide anion under normal conditions due to partial double-bond character of the \(\mathrm{C_{Ar}-X}\) bond.

6. Hofmann Bromamide Degradation Reaction (Descent of Series)

Primary acid amides are converted to primary amines containing one carbon less than the starting amide by heating with bromine in aqueous or ethanolic \(\mathrm{NaOH}\) (or \(\mathrm{KOH}\)):

\[\mathrm{R-CO-NH_2} + \mathrm{Br_2} + 4\mathrm{NaOH} \xrightarrow{\Delta} \mathrm{R-NH_2} + \mathrm{Na_2CO_3} + 2\mathrm{NaBr} + 2\mathrm{H_2O}\]

The reaction involves an intramolecular rearrangement where the migrating group (\(\mathrm{R}\) or \(\mathrm{Ar}\)) moves from the carbonyl carbon to the nitrogen atom with retention of configuration.

हिन्दी

1. नाइट्रो यौगिकों का अपचयन

नाइट्रो यौगिकों का सूक्ष्म विभाजित \(\mathrm{Ni}\), \(\mathrm{Pt}\) या \(\mathrm{Pd}\) की उपस्थिति में उत्प्रेरकीय हाइड्रोजनीकरण द्वारा अथवा अम्लीय माध्यम में धातुओं (\(\mathrm{Sn/HCl}\) या \(\mathrm{Fe/HCl}\)) द्वारा अपचयन कराने पर प्राथमिक ऐमीन प्राप्त होते हैं:

\[\mathrm{Ar-NO_2} + 3\mathrm{H_2} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Pd / Ethanol}} \mathrm{Ar-NH_2} + 2\mathrm{H_2O}\] \[\mathrm{Ar-NO_2} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Fe + HCl}} \mathrm{Ar-NH_2} + 2\mathrm{H_2O}\]
NEET परीक्षा हेतु महत्वपूर्ण तथ्य: अपचयन हेतु लोहे की छीलन (\(\mathrm{Fe}\)) तथा हाइड्रोक्लोरिक अम्ल (\(\mathrm{HCl}\)) को प्राथमिकता दी जाती है क्योंकि अभिक्रिया में बना \(\mathrm{FeCl_2}\) जल-अपघटित होकर पुनः \(\mathrm{HCl}\) मुक्त करता है। अतः अभिक्रिया प्रारंभ करने हेतु अत्यंत अल्प मात्रा में \(\mathrm{HCl}\) की आवश्यकता होती है।

2. ऐल्किल हैलाइडों का हॉफमान अमोनीअपघटन

ऐल्किल अथवा बेन्जिल हैलाइडों की अमोनिया के ऐथेनॉली विलयन के साथ \(373\,\mathrm{K}\) पर सीलबंद नली में अभिक्रिया कराने पर \(\mathrm{C-X}\) आबंध का विदलन होता है:

\[\mathrm{NH_3} + \mathrm{R-X} \longrightarrow \mathrm{R-NH_3^+ X^-} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{NaOH}} \mathrm{R-NH_2} + \mathrm{H_2O} + \mathrm{NaX}\]

बनी हुई प्राथमिक ऐमीन पुनः नाभिकरागी के रूप में क्रिया करती है जिससे द्वितीयक, तृतीयक ऐमीन तथा अंततः चतुष्क अमोनियम लवण (\(\mathrm{R_4N^+X^-}\)) का मिश्रण बनता है। यदि प्राथमिक ऐमीन को मुख्य उत्पाद के रूप में प्राप्त करना हो तो अमोनिया का अत्यधिक आधिक्य (large excess) लिया जाता है। हैलाइडों की क्रियाशीलता क्रम: \(\mathrm{RI > RBr > RCl}\)।

3. नाइट्राइलों का अपचयन (कार्बन श्रृंखला की वृद्धि)

नाइट्राइल (\(\mathrm{R-C\equiv N}\)) का \(\mathrm{LiAlH_4}\) अथवा \(\mathrm{Na(Hg)/C_2H_5OH}\) या \(\mathrm{H_2/Ni}\) द्वारा अपचयन कराने पर जनक हैलाइड की तुलना में एक अधिक कार्बन परमाणु वाली प्राथमिक ऐमीन प्राप्त होती है:

\[\mathrm{R-C\equiv N} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{H_2 / Ni}} \mathrm{R-CH_2-NH_2}\]

4. ऐमाइडों का अपचयन

ऐमाइडों का \(\mathrm{LiAlH_4}\) द्वारा अपचयन कराने पर समान कार्बन संख्या वाली प्राथमिक ऐमीन प्राप्त होती है:

\[\mathrm{R-CONH_2} \xrightarrow[(2)\,\mathrm{H_2O}]{(1)\,\mathrm{LiAlH_4}} \mathrm{R-CH_2-NH_2}\]

5. गैब्रिएल थैलिमाइड संश्लेषण

यह केवल शुद्ध ऐलिफैटिक प्राथमिक ऐमीन के विरचन की उत्तम विधि है। थैलिमाइड की क्रिया ऐल्कोहॉली \(\mathrm{KOH}\) से कराने पर पोटैशियम थैलिमाइड बनता है, जो ऐल्किल हैलाइड से क्रिया कर N-ऐल्किलथैलिमाइड देता है। इसके क्षारीय जल-अपघटन से प्राथमिक ऐमीन मुक्त होती है:

\[\text{थैलिमाइड} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{alc.\,KOH}} \text{पोटैशियम थैलिमाइड} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{R-X}} \text{\textit{N}-ऐल्किलथैलिमाइड} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{NaOH(aq)}} \text{सोडियम थैलेट} + \mathrm{R-NH_2}\]
प्रतिबंध: ऐरोमैटिक प्राथमिक ऐमीन (जैसे ऐनिलीन) इस विधि द्वारा नहीं बनाई जा सकतीं क्योंकि ऐरिल हैलाइड थैलिमाइड ऋणायन के साथ नाभिकरागी प्रतिस्थापन प्रदर्शित नहीं करते।

6. हॉफमान ब्रोमैमाइड निम्नीकरण अभिक्रिया

प्राथमिक ऐमाइड को जलीय या ऐथेनॉलिक सोडियम हाइड्रॉक्साइड विलयन में ब्रोमीन (\(\mathrm{Br_2}\)) के साथ गर्म करने पर एक कम कार्बन परमाणु वाली प्राथमिक ऐमीन प्राप्त होती है:

\[\mathrm{R-CO-NH_2} + \mathrm{Br_2} + 4\mathrm{NaOH} \xrightarrow{\Delta} \mathrm{R-NH_2} + \mathrm{Na_2CO_3} + 2\mathrm{NaBr} + 2\mathrm{H_2O}\]

इस अभिक्रिया में ऐल्किल अथवा ऐरिल समूह का कार्बोनिल कार्बन से नाइट्रोजन परमाणु पर अंतःअणुक पुनर्विन्यास (migration) होता है।

5. Physical Properties of Amines | ऐमीन के भौतिक गुणधर्म

English
  • Physical State and Odour: Lower aliphatic amines are gases with an unpleasant fishy smell. Primary aliphatic amines with three or more carbon atoms are liquids, whereas higher members are solids. Aniline and other arylamines are pure colourless liquids/solids but rapidly darken upon storage owing to aerial oxidation.
  • Solubility: Lower aliphatic amines dissolve readily in water by forming intermolecular hydrogen bonds with water molecules. Solubility sharply decreases with increasing molar mass because the bulky non-polar hydrophobic alkyl group impedes hydration. Higher amines are virtually insoluble in water. Comparing butan-1-ol and butan-1-amine: butan-1-ol is more soluble because oxygen is more electronegative (\(3.5\)) than nitrogen (\(3.0\)), enabling stronger intermolecular \(\mathrm{H}\)-bonding with water.
  • Boiling Points: Amines possess polar \(\mathrm{N-H}\) bonds and form intermolecular hydrogen bonds. The order of boiling points for isomeric amines is: \[\mathbf{1^\circ \text{ Amine} > 2^\circ \text{ Amine} > 3^\circ \text{ Amine}}\] Primary amines have two acidic hydrogens per molecule, creating extensive H-bonding networks. 6. Basicity of Amines: Gas vs Aqueous Phase | ऐमीन की क्षारकीय प्रकृति

English

Amines act as Lewis bases owing to the unshared lone pair on the trivalent nitrogen atom. Basicity is expressed quantitatively through dissociation constant \(K_b\) or \(\mathrm{p}K_b = -\log K_b\):

\[\mathrm{R-NH_2} + \mathrm{H_2O} \rightleftharpoons \mathrm{R-NH_3^+} + \mathrm{OH^-}, \quad K_b = \frac{[\mathrm{R-NH_3^+}][\mathrm{OH^-}]}{[\mathrm{R-NH_2}]}\]
Core Principle: Smaller \(\mathrm{p}K_b\) (or higher \(K_b\)) \(\implies\) Stronger Base!

1. Gas Phase Order (Pure Inductive Effect)

In the non-solvating gas phase, electron-releasing \(+\mathrm{I}\) alkyl groups disperse the positive charge of the conjugate ammonium ion, directly enhancing stability:

\[\mathbf{3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ > \mathrm{NH_3}}\]

2. Aqueous Phase Order (Interplay of Three Factors)

In aqueous solution, three competing influences dictate stability of the substituted ammonium cation:

  1. Inductive Effect (\(+\mathrm{I}\)): Favours \(3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ\).
  2. Solvation Effect (H-bonding with water): Smaller cations form more hydrogen bonds with water molecules. Greater hydration disperses charge and stabilises the ion: \[\mathrm{RNH_3^+} \; (3 \text{ H-bonds}) > \mathrm{R_2NH_2^+} \; (2 \text{ H-bonds}) > \mathrm{R_3NH^+} \; (1 \text{ H-bond}) \implies 1^\circ > 2^\circ > 3^\circ\]
  3. Steric Hindrance: Crowding by alkyl groups impedes hydrogen bonding by solvent water molecules.

  • For Ethyl substituted amines in water: \[\mathbf{(C_2H_5)_2NH > (C_2H_5)_3N > C_2H_5NH_2 > NH_3} \quad (2^\circ > 3^\circ > 1^\circ > \mathrm{NH_3})\]
  • For Methyl substituted amines in water: \[\mathbf{(CH_3)_2NH > CH_3NH_2 > (CH_3)_3N > NH_3} \quad (2^\circ > 1^\circ > 3^\circ > \mathrm{NH_3})\]
In both ethyl and methyl systems, the secondary amine (\(2^\circ\)) is always the most basic in aqueous solution!

3. Arylamines versus Ammonia

Aniline is substantially weaker as a base than ammonia (\(\mathrm{p}K_b \text{ of aniline} = 9.38\) vs \(\mathrm{p}K_b \text{ of } \mathrm{NH_3} = 4.75\)):

  • In aniline, the lone pair on nitrogen is delocalized over the benzene ring across 5 resonance structures, making it far less available for protonation.
  • The protonated anilinium ion (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5NH_3^+}\)) has only 2 Kekulé resonance structures. Therefore, aniline is far more resonance-stabilized than the anilinium ion.
  • Substituent Effects on Aniline: Electron-releasing groups (\(-\mathrm{OCH_3}\), \(-\mathrm{CH_3}\)) enhance basic strength; electron-withdrawing groups (\(-\mathrm{NO_2}\), \(-\mathrm{SO_3H}\), \(-\mathrm{COOH}\), \(-\mathrm{X}\)) decrease basic strength.

7. Distinctive Reactions of Amines | ऐमीन की विशिष्ट रासायनिक अभिक्रियाएँ

English

1. Acylation and Benzoylation

Primary and secondary amines react with acid chlorides, acid anhydrides, or esters via nucleophilic acyl substitution to yield amides. The reaction is run in the presence of a stronger tertiary base like pyridine to neutralize the liberated \(\mathrm{HCl}\) and drive the equilibrium forward:

\[\mathrm{C_2H_5NH_2} + \mathrm{CH_3COCl} \xrightarrow{\text{Pyridine}} \mathrm{C_2H_5NHCOCH_3} + \mathrm{HCl}\]

Reaction with benzoyl chloride (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5COCl}\)) is termed benzoylation (Schotten-Baumann reaction):

\[\mathrm{CH_3NH_2} + \mathrm{C_6H_5COCl} \longrightarrow \mathrm{CH_3NHCOC_6H_5} + \mathrm{HCl}\]

2. Carbylamine Reaction (Isocyanide Test)

Exclusive diagnostic test for primary amines (\(1^\circ\), both aliphatic and aromatic). On heating with chloroform and ethanolic \(\mathrm{KOH}\), primary amines form extremely foul-smelling isocyanides (carbylamines):

\[\mathrm{R-NH_2} + \mathrm{CHCl_3} + 3\mathrm{KOH} \xrightarrow{\Delta} \mathrm{R-NC} + 3\mathrm{KCl} + 3\mathrm{H_2O}\]

Secondary and tertiary amines do NOT give this test.

3. Reaction with Nitrous Acid (\(\mathrm{HNO_2}\))

Nitrous acid is generated in situ from \(\mathrm{NaNO_2} + \mathrm{HCl}\):

  • Aliphatic \(1^\circ\) Amines: Form highly unstable alkyldiazonium salts that rapidly decompose to release nitrogen gas quantitatively along with a mixture of alcohols, alkenes, and alkyl halides. Quantitative evolution of \(\mathrm{N_2}\) is utilized to estimate amino acids and proteins: \[\mathrm{R-NH_2} + \mathrm{HNO_2} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{NaNO_2 + HCl}} [\mathrm{R-N_2^+Cl^-}] \xrightarrow{\mathrm{H_2O}} \mathrm{ROH} + \mathrm{N_2}\uparrow + \mathrm{HCl}\]
  • Aromatic \(1^\circ\) Amines (Diazotisation): React at ice-cold temperatures (\(273\text{--}278\,\mathrm{K}\) or \(0\text{--}5\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\)) to form stable arenediazonium salts: \[\mathrm{C_6H_5NH_2} + \mathrm{NaNO_2} + 2\mathrm{HCl} \xrightarrow{273\text{--}278\,\mathrm{K}} \mathrm{C_6H_5N_2^+Cl^-} + \mathrm{NaCl} + 2\mathrm{H_2O}\]

4. Hinsberg's Test (Distinction of \(1^\circ, 2^\circ, 3^\circ\) Amines)

Reagent: Benzenesulphonyl chloride (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5SO_2Cl}\)) [or modernly p-toluenesulphonyl chloride].

  • Primary (\(1^\circ\)) Amine: Yields an N-alkylbenzenesulphonamide possessing an acidic \(\mathrm{N-H}\) proton (strongly electron-withdrawing \(\mathrm{-SO_2-}\) group). Hence, it is soluble in aqueous alkali (\(\mathrm{NaOH}\)).
  • Secondary (\(2^\circ\)) Amine: Yields an N,N-dialkylbenzenesulphonamide lacking any acidic proton on nitrogen. Hence, it is insoluble in alkali.
  • Tertiary (\(3^\circ\)) Amine: Does not contain any replaceable hydrogen on nitrogen; hence it does not react with Hinsberg's reagent.

5. Electrophilic Aromatic Substitution of Aniline

The \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\) group is a powerful activating group and strongly ortho/para-directing:

  • Bromination with Bromine Water: Aniline undergoes instantaneous polyhalogenation without any catalyst to yield a white precipitate of 2,4,6-tribromoaniline: \[\mathrm{C_6H_5NH_2} + 3\mathrm{Br_2(aq)} \longrightarrow \text{2,4,6-tribromoaniline} \downarrow \text{(white ppt)} + 3\mathrm{HBr}\] Controlled Monobromination: Protect \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\) by acetylation with acetic anhydride to form acetanilide (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5NHCOCH_3}\)). In acetanilide, resonance between nitrogen lone pair and carbonyl oxygen decreases electron donation into the ring. Bromination with \(\mathrm{Br_2/CH_3COOH}\) followed by acidic/alkaline hydrolysis gives 4-bromoaniline as the major product.
  • Nitration: Direct nitration with concentrated \(\mathrm{HNO_3 + H_2SO_4}\) at \(288\,\mathrm{K}\) yields a mixture of:
    • 51% para-nitroaniline
    • 47% meta-nitroaniline (unusually high!)
    • 2% ortho-nitroaniline
    NEET Reason: In strongly acidic medium, aniline undergoes protonation to form the anilinium ion (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5NH_3^+}\)), which is strongly deactivating and meta-directing. To obtain p-nitroaniline exclusively, the amino group must first be protected by acetylation.
  • Sulphonation: Heating aniline with conc. \(\mathrm{H_2SO_4}\) at \(453\text{--}473\,\mathrm{K}\) yields sulphanilic acid (p-aminobenzenesulphonic acid), which exists predominantly as an internal dipolar zwitterion: \[\mathrm{H_3N^+-C_6H_4-SO_3^-}\]
  • Friedel-Crafts Reaction Failure: Aniline does NOT undergo Friedel-Crafts alkylation or acylation because the Lewis acid catalyst (\(\mathrm{AlCl_3}\)) forms an adduct salt with the basic \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\) group. The resulting positively charged nitrogen deactivates the benzene ring towards electrophilic attack.

The diazonium group is an outstanding leaving group, readily expelled as inert \(\mathrm{N_2}\) gas:

  • Sandmeyer Reaction: Introduction of \(-\mathrm{Cl}\), \(-\mathrm{Br}\), or \(-\mathrm{CN}\) using cuprous salts dissolved in corresponding halogen acids: \[\mathrm{ArN_2^+X^-} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Cu_2Cl_2 / HCl}} \mathrm{ArCl} + \mathrm{N_2}\] \[\mathrm{ArN_2^+X^-} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Cu_2Br_2 / HBr}} \mathrm{ArBr} + \mathrm{N_2}\] \[\mathrm{ArN_2^+X^-} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{CuCN / KCN}} \mathrm{ArCN} + \mathrm{N_2}\]
  • Gattermann Reaction: Halogen introduction using copper powder and halogen acid: \[\mathrm{ArN_2^+X^-} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Cu / HCl}} \mathrm{ArCl} + \mathrm{N_2} + \mathrm{CuX}\] Sandmeyer yields are superior to Gattermann yields.
  • Replacement by Iodide (\(-\mathrm{I}\)): Simply warm with aqueous potassium iodide (\(\mathrm{KI}\)): \[\mathrm{ArN_2^+Cl^-} + \mathrm{KI} \longrightarrow \mathrm{ArI} + \mathrm{KCl} + \mathrm{N_2}\]
  • Replacement by Fluoride (\(-\mathrm{F}\), Balz-Schiemann Reaction): Precipitate fluoroborate with \(\mathrm{HBF_4}\), followed by dry thermal decomposition: \[\mathrm{ArN_2^+Cl^-} + \mathrm{HBF_4} \longrightarrow \mathrm{ArN_2^+BF_4^-} \xrightarrow{\Delta} \mathrm{ArF} + \mathrm{BF_3} + \mathrm{N_2}\]
  • Reduction to Arene (\(-\mathrm{H}\)): By hypophosphorous acid (\(\mathrm{H_3PO_2}\), phosphinic acid) or ethanol: \[\mathrm{ArN_2^+Cl^-} + \mathrm{H_3PO_2} + \mathrm{H_2O} \longrightarrow \mathrm{ArH} + \mathrm{N_2} + \mathrm{H_3PO_3} + \mathrm{HCl}\] \[\mathrm{ArN_2^+Cl^-} + \mathrm{CH_3CH_2OH} \longrightarrow \mathrm{ArH} + \mathrm{N_2} + \mathrm{CH_3CHO} + \mathrm{HCl}\]
  • Replacement by Hydroxyl (\(-\mathrm{OH}\)): Warm aqueous diazonium solution to \(283\,\mathrm{K}\): \[\mathrm{ArN_2^+Cl^-} + \mathrm{H_2O} \xrightarrow{283\,\mathrm{K}} \mathrm{ArOH} + \mathrm{N_2} + \mathrm{HCl}\]
  • Replacement by Nitro Group (\(-\mathrm{NO_2}\)): Heat fluoroborate salt with aqueous \(\mathrm{NaNO_2}\) in presence of copper: \[\mathrm{ArN_2^+BF_4^-} \xrightarrow[\Delta]{\mathrm{NaNO_2 / Cu}} \mathrm{ArNO_2} + \mathrm{N_2} + \mathrm{NaBF_4}\]

B. Coupling Reactions (Retention of the Diazo Group)

Arenediazonium cations act as weak electrophiles and undergo electrophilic aromatic substitution with electron-rich aromatic partners (phenols and arylamines) at the para position, forming brightly coloured azo dyes with extended \(\pi\)-conjugation (\(-\mathrm{N=N}-\)):

  • Coupling with Phenol: In mildly alkaline medium (\(\mathrm{pH} = 9\text{--}10\)) yields p-hydroxyazobenzene (orange dye): \[\mathrm{C_6H_5N_2^+Cl^-} + \mathrm{C_6H_5OH} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{OH^-}} p\text{-}\mathrm{HO-C_6H_4-N=N-C_6H_5} + \mathrm{Cl^-} + \mathrm{H_2O}\]
  • Coupling with Aniline: In mildly acidic medium (\(\mathrm{pH} = 4\text{--}5\)) yields p-aminoazobenzene (yellow dye): \[\mathrm{C_6H_5N_2^+Cl^-} + \mathrm{C_6H_5NH_2} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{H^+}} p\text{-}\mathrm{H_2N-C_6H_4-N=N-C_6H_5} + \mathrm{Cl^-} + \mathrm{H_2O}\]

English

Conversion Problem 1: Convert 4-Nitrotoluene into 2-Bromobenzoic acid

  1. Bromination: 4-Nitrotoluene treated with \(\mathrm{Br_2 / Fe}\) directs bromine to the position ortho to the activating methyl group (and meta to the deactivating nitro group), yielding 2-bromo-4-nitrotoluene.
  2. Selective Reduction: Reduce the nitro group to an amino group using \(\mathrm{Sn / HCl}\) (or \(\mathrm{Fe / HCl}\)) to give 3-bromo-4-methylaniline.
  3. Diazotisation: Treat with \(\mathrm{NaNO_2 + HCl}\) at \(273\text{--}278\,\mathrm{K}\) to form the diazonium chloride salt.
  4. Reductive Deamination: React with hypophosphorous acid (\(\mathrm{H_3PO_2 + H_2O}\)) to displace the diazonium group with hydrogen, producing 2-bromotoluene (o-bromotoluene).
  5. Side-chain Oxidation: Oxidize the methyl group to carboxylic acid with alkaline potassium permanganate (\(\mathrm{KMnO_4 / OH^-}\), heat) followed by acidification, yielding 2-bromobenzoic acid.

Conversion Problem 2: Convert Aniline into 1,3,5-Tribromobenzene

  1. Bromination: Aniline \(+ 3\mathrm{Br_2(aq)} \longrightarrow\) 2,4,6-tribromoaniline (white precipitate).
  2. Diazotisation: 2,4,6-tribromoaniline \(+ \mathrm{NaNO_2} + \mathrm{HCl}\) at \(273\text{--}278\,\mathrm{K} \longrightarrow\) 2,4,6-tribromobenzenediazonium chloride.
  3. Deamination: Treat with \(\mathrm{H_3PO_2 + H_2O}\) (or \(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2OH}\)) to remove the diazo group, yielding pure 1,3,5-tribromobenzene.

Question 1 (Single Correct MCQ)

Which one of the following amines is the most basic in aqueous solution?

  • (A) \(\mathrm{CH_3NH_2}\)
  • (B) \((\mathrm{CH_3})_2\mathrm{NH}\)
  • (C) \((\mathrm{CH_3})_3\mathrm{N}\)
  • (D) \(\mathrm{NH_3}\)
Correct Answer: (B) \((\mathrm{CH_3})_2\mathrm{NH}\)

Explanation: In aqueous solution, the basic strength of methyl-substituted aliphatic amines depends on the combined effect of \(+\mathrm{I}\) inductive effect, solvation (hydration) energy of the substituted ammonium cation, and steric hindrance. The experimental order of basicity for methyl-substituted amines in aqueous medium is:

\[(\mathrm{CH_3})_2\mathrm{NH} > \mathrm{CH_3NH_2} > (\mathrm{CH_3})_3\mathrm{N} > \mathrm{NH_3} \quad (2^\circ > 1^\circ > 3^\circ > \mathrm{NH_3})\]

Thus, dimethylamine is the strongest base among the given options.

Question 3 (Assertion - Reason)

Assertion (A): Direct nitration of aniline with a nitrating mixture (\(\mathrm{HNO_3 + H_2SO_4}\)) produces a substantial amount (47%) of meta-nitroaniline along with para-nitroaniline.
Reason (R): In strongly acidic medium, aniline is protonated to form anilinium ion which is meta-directing.

  • (A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A).
  • (B) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is NOT the correct explanation of (A).

  • (D) (A) is false but (R) is true.
Correct Answer: (A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A).

Explanation: Under strongly acidic nitrating conditions, aniline acts as a base and accepts a proton from \(\mathrm{H_2SO_4}\) to form the anilinium ion (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5NH_3^+}\)). This accounts for the significant 47% yield of m-nitroaniline.

Question 4 (Reagent & Test MCQ)

An organic compound with molecular formula \(\mathrm{C_4H_{11}N}\) reacts with benzenesulphonyl chloride to yield a solid compound which is insoluble in alkali. The starting compound is:

  • (A) Butan-1-amine
  • (B) 2-Methylpropan-2-amine
  • (C) N-Ethylethanamine
  • (D) N,N-Dimethylethanamine
Correct Answer: (C) N-Ethylethanamine

Explanation: Under Hinsberg's test:
1. Primary amines give sulphonamides that have an acidic hydrogen on nitrogen and are soluble in alkali.
2. Secondary amines react with benzenesulphonyl chloride to form N,N-dialkylsulphonamides which lack acidic hydrogen on nitrogen and are therefore insoluble in alkali.
3. Tertiary amines do not react.
Among the options, N-ethylethanamine (diethylamine, \((\mathrm{C_2H_5})_2\mathrm{NH}\)) is a secondary amine (\(2^\circ\)), matching all criteria.

  • (A) ब्यूटेन-1-ऐमीन

  • (C) N-ऐथिलएथेनामीन
  • (D) N,N-डाइमेथिलएथेनामीन
सही उत्तर: (C) N-ऐथिलएथेनामीन

हल एवं व्याख्या: हिन्सबर्ग परीक्षण के नियमानुसार:
1. प्राथमिक ऐमीन से बना सल्फोनेमाइड क्षार में विलेय होता है।

दिए गए विकल्पों में N-ऐथिलएथेनामीन (डाइएथिलऐमीन) एक द्वितीयक ऐमीन (\(2^\circ\)) है।

Question 5 (Multi-Step Reaction Scheme)

Consider the reaction sequence given below:

\[\mathrm{CH_3CH_2Br} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{KCN}} [A] \xrightarrow{\mathrm{LiAlH_4}} [B] \xrightarrow[273\text{--}278\,\mathrm{K}]{\mathrm{HNO_2}} [C]\]

The final organic product \([C]\) is:

  • (A) \(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2COOH}\)
  • (B) \(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2OH}\)
  • (C) \(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2NH_2}\)
  • (D) \(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2Cl}\)
Correct Answer: (B) \(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2OH}\)

Explanation:
Step 1: Nucleophilic substitution of bromoethane by cyanide ion yields propanenitrile \([A] = \mathrm{CH_3CH_2CN}\).
Step 2: Reduction of the nitrile with \(\mathrm{LiAlH_4}\) yields propan-1-amine \([B] = \mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2NH_2}\).
Step 3: Primary aliphatic amines react with nitrous acid (\(\mathrm{HNO_2}\)) to form an unstable diazonium salt, which decomposes quantitatively with the evolution of \(\mathrm{N_2}\) gas to give propan-1-ol \([C] = \mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2OH}\).

निम्नलिखित अभिक्रिया अनुक्रम पर विचार कीजिए:

\[\mathrm{CH_3CH_2Br} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{KCN}} [A] \xrightarrow{\mathrm{LiAlH_4}} [B] \xrightarrow[273\text{--}278\,\mathrm{K}]{\mathrm{HNO_2}} [C]\]

अंतिम कार्बनिक उत्पाद \([C]\) क्या है?

  • (A) \(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2COOH}\)
  • (B) \(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2OH}\)
  • (C) \(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2NH_2}\)
  • (D) \(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2Cl}\)
सही उत्तर: (B) \(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2OH}\)

हल एवं व्याख्या:
पद 1: ब्रोमोएथेन की \(\mathrm{KCN}\) से क्रिया द्वारा प्रोपेननाइट्राइल \([A] = \mathrm{CH_3CH_2CN}\) बनता है।
पद 2: \(\mathrm{LiAlH_4}\) द्वारा नाइट्राइल के अपचयन से प्रोपेन-1-ऐमीन \([B] = \mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2NH_2}\) प्राप्त होती है।
पद 3: प्राथमिक ऐलिफैटिक ऐमीन नाइट्रस अम्ल के साथ अस्थायी डाइऐज़ोनियम लवण बनाती है जो जल-अपघटित होकर \(\mathrm{N_2}\) गैस मुक्त करती है तथा मुख्य उत्पाद के रूप में प्रोपेन-1-ऑल \([C] = \mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2OH}\) देती है।

11. Common Pitfalls & High-Yield Revision Checklist | सामान्य त्रुटियाँ एवं त्वरित दोहराव

English
  • Aqueous vs. Gas Phase Basicity: Do not use gas-phase order (\(3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ\)) for aqueous solutions. Remember the mnemonic: Ethyl = 231 \((2^\circ > 3^\circ > 1^\circ)\) and Methyl = 213 \((2^\circ > 1^\circ > 3^\circ)\).
  • Carbon Count in Preparations:
    • Nitrile reduction (\(\mathrm{R-CN} \to \mathrm{R-CH_2NH_2}\)): +1 Carbon (Ascent of series).
    • Hofmann Bromamide (\(\mathrm{R-CONH_2} \to \mathrm{R-NH_2}\)): -1 Carbon (Descent of series).
  • Nitration Yields of Aniline: NEET specifically asks about the 47% meta product. Remember that it arises because anilinium ion formed in acid is meta-directing!
  • Friedel-Crafts Failure: Aniline does NOT undergo alkylation or acylation due to adduct salt formation with the Lewis acid catalyst \(\mathrm{AlCl_3}\).
  • Aromatic Gabriel Synthesis: Gabriel phthalimide synthesis cannot prepare aniline or other arylamines because aryl halides do not undergo nucleophilic substitution.
  • Hinsberg's Test Summary: \(1^\circ \to\) Soluble in alkali; \(2^\circ \to\) Insoluble in alkali; \(3^\circ \to\) No reaction.
हिन्दी
  • जलीय बनाम गैसीय क्षारकता: जलीय माध्यम हेतु याद रखें: ऐथिल समूह = 231 \((2^\circ > 3^\circ > 1^\circ)\) तथा मेथिल समूह = 213 \((2^\circ > 1^\circ > 3^\circ)\)।
  • कार्बन संख्या में परिवर्तन:
    • नाइट्राइल अपचयन (\(\mathrm{R-CN} \to \mathrm{R-CH_2NH_2}\)): +1 कार्बन की वृद्धि।
    • हॉफमान ब्रोमैमाइड अभिक्रिया: 1 कार्बन की कमी।
  • ऐनिलीन का नाइट्रीकरण: अम्लीय माध्यम में ऐनिलीनियम आयन बनने के कारण 47% मेटा उत्पाद बनता है।
  • फ्रीडेल-क्राफ्ट्स अभिक्रिया: ऐनिलीन \(\mathrm{AlCl_3}\) के साथ संकुल लवण बना लेती है, अतः यह अभिक्रिया नहीं होती।
  • गैब्रिएल थैलिमाइड सीमा: इससे ऐरोमैटिक प्राथमिक ऐमीन नहीं बनाई जा सकतीं।
  • हिन्सबर्ग परीक्षण: \(1^\circ \to\) क्षार में विलेय; \(2^\circ \to\) क्षार में अविलेय; \(3^\circ \to\) कोई अभिक्रिया नहीं।

12. Final Chapter Summary | अध्याय सारांश

English

Amines are organic pyramidal derivatives of ammonia with \(sp^3\) hybridised nitrogen and bond angles slightly less than \(109.5^\circ\). They are classified as \(1^\circ, 2^\circ\), or \(3^\circ\) based on nitrogen substitution. While gas-phase basicity follows pure inductive order (\(3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ\)), aqueous basicity reflects a balance of inductive, hydration, and steric effects (\(2^\circ > 3^\circ > 1^\circ\) for ethyl, \(2^\circ > 1^\circ > 3^\circ\) for methyl). Aniline is weaker than ammonia due to resonance delocalization. Primary amines uniquely give the carbylamine test and can be identified via the Hinsberg reagent. Diazonium salts (\(\mathrm{ArN_2^+X^-}\)) serve as vital synthetic linchpins for producing haloarenes, phenols, cyanides, and azo coupling dyes.

हिन्दी

ऐमीन अमोनिया के पिरैमिडी ज्यामिति वाले कार्बनिक व्युत्पन्न हैं जिनमें नाइट्रोजन \(sp^3\) संकरित होता है। इन्हें \(1^\circ, 2^\circ, 3^\circ\) में वर्गीकृत किया जाता है। जलीय माध्यम में क्षारकता प्रेरणिक, विलायकन और त्रिविम प्रभावों का सम्मिश्रण है (ऐथिल: \(2^\circ > 3^\circ > 1^\circ\); मेथिल: \(2^\circ > 1^\circ > 3^\circ\))। ऐनिलीन अनुनादी विस्थानिकरण के कारण अमोनिया से दुर्बल क्षारक है। प्राथमिक ऐमीन विशिष्ट रूप से कार्बिलऐमीन परीक्षण देती हैं। हिन्सबर्ग अभिकर्मक द्वारा प्राथमिक, द्वितीयक व तृतीयक ऐमीन में विभेद किया जाता है। डाइऐज़ोनियम लवण (\(\mathrm{ArN_2^+X^-}\)) हैलोऐरीन, फीनॉल, नाइट्राइल तथा ऐज़ो रंजकों के विरचन हेतु अत्यंत महत्वपूर्ण मध्यवर्ती हैं।
