1. Learning Objectives & Syllabus Overview | শিখন উদ্দেশ্য ও পাঠ্যসূচির রূপরেখা

English

Amines are organic derivatives of ammonia (\(\mathrm{NH_3}\)) in which one, two, or all three hydrogen atoms are replaced by alkyl or aryl groups. They form the structural backbone of biological molecules such as proteins, vitamins, alkaloids, and hormones (for example, adrenaline and ephedrine), as well as synthetic pharmaceuticals (such as novocain and benadryl), polymers, and surfactants. Aromatic diazonium salts serve as versatile synthetic intermediates for introducing functional groups onto benzene rings and manufacturing azo dyes.

After completing this tutorial, you will be able to:

  • Describe the \(sp^3\) hybridized pyramidal geometry of amines and classify them into primary (\(1^\circ\)), secondary (\(2^\circ\)), and tertiary (\(3^\circ\)) amines.
  • Assign systematic IUPAC and common names to aliphatic and aromatic amines, including \(N\)-substituted derivatives.
  • Select appropriate synthetic routes for preparing \(1^\circ\), \(2^\circ\), and \(3^\circ\) amines, and identify key limitations such as those in ammonolysis and Gabriel phthalimide synthesis.
  • Explain trends in boiling point, aqueous solubility, and basicity (gas phase versus aqueous phase) using inductive, solvation, steric, and resonance effects.
  • Distinguish \(1^\circ\), \(2^\circ\), and \(3^\circ\) amines chemically using Hinsberg's reagent, the carbylamine test, and nitrous acid.
  • Predict electrophilic aromatic substitution products of aniline and use acetylation to control reactivity.
  • Apply diazotisation and diazonium salt reactions (Sandmeyer, Gattermann, Balz-Schiemann, reduction, hydrolysis, and azo coupling) to multi-step aromatic conversions.

English

2.1 Orbital Structure & Geometry

In amines, the trivalent nitrogen atom forms three \(\sigma\)-bonds with carbon or hydrogen atoms and retains one unshared pair of electrons (lone pair). Consequently, nitrogen is \(sp^3\) hybridized with a pyramidal geometry. Because lone-pair–bond-pair repulsion exceeds bond-pair–bond-pair repulsion, the \(\mathrm{C-N-E}\) bond angle (where \(\mathrm{E = C}\) or \(\mathrm{H}\)) is compressed below the ideal tetrahedral angle of \(109.5^\circ\). For instance, in trimethylamine, \(\mathrm{(CH_3)_3N}\), the \(\mathrm{C-N-C}\) bond angle is \(108^\circ\).

English

2.2 Classification & Nomenclature Rules

Amines are classified according to the number of carbon substituents attached directly to nitrogen:

  • Primary (\(1^\circ\)) amine: One \(\mathrm{H}\) of \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) replaced (\(\mathrm{R-NH_2}\) or \(\mathrm{Ar-NH_2}\)). Note that \(\mathrm{(CH_3)_3C-NH_2}\) is a \(1^\circ\) amine even though the carbon attached to \(\mathrm{N}\) is tertiary, because nitrogen itself is bonded to only one carbon.
  • Secondary (\(2^\circ\)) amine: Two \(\mathrm{H}\) atoms replaced (\(\mathrm{R-NH-R'}\)).
  • Tertiary (\(3^\circ\)) amine: All three \(\mathrm{H}\) atoms replaced (\(\mathrm{R-N(R')R''}\)).
  • Simple vs. Mixed amines: If all alkyl/aryl groups on nitrogen are identical, the amine is simple; if at least two differ, it is mixed.

IUPAC Nomenclature: Replace the terminal -e of the parent alkane with -amine (alkanamine). For \(2^\circ\) and \(3^\circ\) amines, select the longest carbon chain as the parent alkanamine and designate other alkyl groups on nitrogen using the locant N-. When two or more \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\) groups are present, retain the -e of the alkane (e.g., ethane-1,2-diamine). For arylamines, \(\mathrm{C_6H_5NH_2}\) is named benzenamine (or accepted IUPAC name aniline).

3. Methods of Preparation of Amines | অ্যামিন প্রস্তুতির পদ্ধতিসমূহ

English

Selecting the right synthetic method in NEET UG problems depends on whether the carbon chain length stays the same, increases by one carbon (ascent), or decreases by one carbon (descent), and whether the target amine is aliphatic or aromatic.

  1. Reduction of Nitro Compounds: Nitroarenes and nitroalkanes are reduced to \(1^\circ\) amines using \(\mathrm{H_2/Pd}\) (in ethanol) or active metals in acid (\(\mathrm{Sn/HCl}\) or \(\mathrm{Fe/HCl}\)).
    \[\mathrm{Ar-NO_2} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{Fe/HCl} \text{ or } \mathrm{Sn/HCl}} \mathrm{Ar-NH_2}\]
    Key Exam Fact: Scrap \(\mathrm{Fe}\) with \(\mathrm{HCl}\) is industrially preferred because \(\mathrm{FeCl_2}\) formed during reaction hydrolyses to regenerate \(\mathrm{HCl}\); thus, only a catalytic amount of \(\mathrm{HCl}\) is needed to initiate reduction.
  2. Ammonolysis of Alkyl Halides: Heating an alkyl or benzyl halide with ethanolic \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) in a sealed tube at \(373\,\mathrm{K}\) yields a substituted ammonium salt, which releases the free amine upon treatment with \(\mathrm{NaOH}\). Because the \(1^\circ\) amine formed acts as a stronger nucleophile, it reacts further with \(\mathrm{R-X}\) to give a mixture of \(1^\circ\), \(2^\circ\), \(3^\circ\) amines and a quaternary ammonium salt (\(\mathrm{R_4N^+X^-}\)). Using a large excess of \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) makes the \(1^\circ\) amine the major product. Reactivity order of halides: \(\mathrm{R-I} > \mathrm{R-Br} > \mathrm{R-Cl}\).
  3. Reduction of Nitriles (Ascent of Series): Reducing \(\mathrm{R-C\equiv N}\) with \(\mathrm{LiAlH_4}\), \(\mathrm{H_2/Ni}\), or \(\mathrm{Na(Hg)/C_2H_5OH}\) gives \(\mathrm{R-CH_2-NH_2}\). Starting from \(\mathrm{R-X}\) via \(\mathrm{NaCN}\) followed by reduction increases the carbon chain by one \(\mathrm{CH_2}\) unit.
  4. Reduction of Amides (Same Carbon Count): Amides (\(\mathrm{R-CONH_2}\)) treated with \(\mathrm{LiAlH_4}\) followed by \(\mathrm{H_2O}\) yield \(\mathrm{R-CH_2-NH_2}\).
  5. Gabriel Phthalimide Synthesis (Pure \(1^\circ\) Aliphatic Amines Only): Phthalimide reacts with ethanolic \(\mathrm{KOH}\) to form potassium phthalimide, which undergoes \(\mathrm{S_N2}\) reaction with an alkyl halide (\(\mathrm{R-X}\)) to give \(N\)-alkylphthalimide. Subsequent alkaline hydrolysis (\(\mathrm{NaOH(aq)}\)) liberates pure \(1^\circ\) aliphatic amine without \(2^\circ/3^\circ\) contamination.
    Critical Limitation: Aromatic \(1^\circ\) amines (like aniline) cannot be prepared by Gabriel synthesis because aryl halides do not undergo nucleophilic substitution with the phthalimide anion.
  6. Hoffmann Bromamide Degradation (Descent of Series): Treating an amide with \(\mathrm{Br_2}\) in aqueous or ethanolic \(\mathrm{NaOH}\) produces a \(1^\circ\) amine containing one carbon atom less than the parent amide, via migration of the \(\mathrm{R}\) or \(\mathrm{Ar}\) group from carbonyl carbon to nitrogen.
    \[\mathrm{R-CONH_2 + Br_2 + 4NaOH \longrightarrow R-NH_2 + Na_2CO_3 + 2NaBr + 2H_2O}\]

  2. অ্যালকিল হ্যালাইডের অ্যামোনোলিসিস: বদ্ধ নলে \(373\,\mathrm{K}\) তাপমাত্রায় অ্যালকিল বা বেনজাইল হ্যালাইডকে ইথানলীয় \(\mathrm{NH_3}\)-সহ উত্তপ্ত করলে প্রতিস্থাপিত অ্যামোনিয়াম লবণ গঠিত হয়, যা \(\mathrm{NaOH}\)-এর সাথে বিক্রিয়ায় মুক্ত অ্যামিন দেয়। উৎপন্ন \(1^\circ\) অ্যামিন পুনরায় \(\mathrm{R-X}\)-এর সাথে বিক্রিয়া করে \(1^\circ\), \(2^\circ\), \(3^\circ\) অ্যামিন ও কোয়াটারনারি অ্যামোনিয়াম লবণের (\(\mathrm{R_4N^+X^-}\)) মিশ্রণ তৈরি করে। অতিরিক্ত পরিমাণ \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) ব্যবহার করলে \(1^\circ\) অ্যামিন প্রধান উৎপাদ হিসেবে পাওয়া যায়। হ্যালাইডের সক্রিয়তার ক্রম: \(\mathrm{R-I} > \mathrm{R-Br} > \mathrm{R-Cl}\)।

  4. অ্যামাইডের বিজারণ (সমান কার্বন সংখ্যা): \(\mathrm{R-CONH_2}\)-কে \(\mathrm{LiAlH_4}\) ও পরে \(\mathrm{H_2O}\) দ্বারা বিজারিত করলে \(\mathrm{R-CH_2-NH_2}\) উৎপন্ন হয়।
  5. গ্যাব্রিয়েল থ্যালিমাইড সংশ্লেষণ (কেবল বিশুদ্ধ \(1^\circ\) অ্যালিফ্যাটিক অ্যামিন): থ্যালিমাইড ইথানলীয় \(\mathrm{KOH}\)-এর সাথে বিক্রিয়ায় পটাশিয়াম থ্যালিমাইড গঠন করে, যা অ্যালকিল হ্যালাইডের সাথে \(\mathrm{S_N2}\) বিক্রিয়ায় \(N\)-অ্যালকিলথ্যালিমাইড দেয়। এর ক্ষারীয় আর্দ্রবিশ্লেষণে (\(\mathrm{NaOH(aq)}\)) \(2^\circ/3^\circ\) অ্যামিনের মিশ্রণ ছাড়াই বিশুদ্ধ \(1^\circ\) অ্যালিফ্যাটিক অ্যামিন পাওয়া যায়।
    গুরুত্বপূর্ণ সীমাবদ্ধতা: এই পদ্ধতিতে অ্যারোমেটিক \(1^\circ\) অ্যামিন (যেমন অ্যানিলিন) প্রস্তুত করা যায় না, কারণ অ্যারাইল হ্যালাইড থ্যালিমাইড অ্যানায়নের সাথে নিউক্লিওফিলিক প্রতিস্থাপন বিক্রিয়ায় অংশ নেয় না।
  6. হফম্যান ব্রোমামাইড অবনমন (কার্বন সংখ্যা হ্রাস): জলীয় বা ইথানলীয় \(\mathrm{NaOH}\) দ্রবণে \(\mathrm{Br_2}\)-সহ অ্যামাইডকে উত্তপ্ত করলে মূল অ্যামাইডের চেয়ে একটি কম কার্বনবিশিষ্ট \(1^\circ\) অ্যামিন উৎপন্ন হয়; এক্ষেত্রে কার্বনিল কার্বন থেকে \(\mathrm{R}\) বা \(\mathrm{Ar}\) মূলক নাইট্রোজেনে স্থানান্তরিত হয়।
    \[\mathrm{R-CONH_2 + Br_2 + 4NaOH \longrightarrow R-NH_2 + Na_2CO_3 + 2NaBr + 2H_2O}\]

4. Physical Properties: Boiling Point & Solubility | ভৌত ধর্ম: স্ফুটনাঙ্ক ও দ্রাব্যতা

English
  • Physical State & Odour: Lower aliphatic amines are gases with a fishy odour. Primary amines with three or more carbons are liquids, and higher homologues are solids. Pure arylamines like aniline are colourless, but darken upon storage due to atmospheric oxidation.
  • Aqueous Solubility: Lower aliphatic amines dissolve readily in water by forming intermolecular hydrogen bonds with \(\mathrm{H_2O}\). As molar mass increases, the hydrophobic alkyl chain grows larger, decreasing solubility. Because oxygen (electronegativity \(3.5\)) is more electronegative than nitrogen (\(3.0\)), alcohols form stronger \(\mathrm{H}\)-bonds and are more soluble in water and higher boiling than amines of comparable molar mass.
  • Boiling Point Order of Isomeric Amines: Intermolecular \(\mathrm{H}\)-bonding depends on the number of \(\mathrm{N-H}\) bonds available. Primary amines (\(-\mathrm{NH_2}\)) have two \(\mathrm{H}\) atoms for \(\mathrm{H}\)-bonding, secondary amines (\(>\mathrm{NH}\)) have one, and tertiary amines (\(\mathrm{R_3N}\)) have none. Therefore, for isomeric amines of identical molar mass:
    \[\text{Boiling Point: } 1^\circ\text{ Amine} > 2^\circ\text{ Amine} > 3^\circ\text{ Amine} > \text{Alkane (similar mass)}\]

5. Basic Character & Structure–Basicity Relationships | অ্যামিনের ক্ষারকীয় ধর্ম ও গঠন-ভিত্তিক তুলনা

English

Due to the lone pair on nitrogen, amines act as Lewis bases and proton acceptors (Brønsted–Lowry bases). In water:

\[\mathrm{R-NH_2 + H_2O \rightleftharpoons R-NH_3^+ + OH^-}, \quad K_b = \frac{[\mathrm{R-NH_3^+}][\mathrm{OH^-}]}{[\mathrm{R-NH_2}]}, \quad \mathrm{p}K_b = -\log K_b\]

A larger \(K_b\) or smaller \(\mathrm{p}K_b\) indicates a stronger base. Basic strength depends on the stability of the conjugate ammonium cation relative to the free amine.

5.1 Aliphatic Amines: Gas Phase vs. Aqueous Phase

  • Gas Phase: Only the electron-releasing inductive effect (\(+I\)) of alkyl groups operates, stabilizing the positive charge on the ammonium ion. Thus:
    \[3^\circ\text{ Amine} > 2^\circ\text{ Amine} > 1^\circ\text{ Amine} > \mathrm{NH_3}\]
  • Aqueous Phase: Three competing factors govern basicity: (i) \(+I\) effect (favours \(3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ\)), (ii) solvation via \(\mathrm{H}\)-bonding of the conjugate acid with \(\mathrm{H_2O}\) (favours \(1^\circ > 2^\circ > 3^\circ\) because \(\mathrm{RNH_3^+}\) forms three \(\mathrm{H}\)-bonds while \(\mathrm{R_3NH^+}\) forms only one), and (iii) steric hindrance to solvation by bulky alkyl groups. Their net interplay produces two classic NEET orders:
    \[\text{Ethyl series (aq): } \mathrm{(C_2H_5)_2NH} > \mathrm{(C_2H_5)_3N} > \mathrm{C_2H_5NH_2} > \mathrm{NH_3}\] \[\text{Methyl series (aq): } \mathrm{(CH_3)_2NH} > \mathrm{CH_3NH_2} > \mathrm{(CH_3)_3N} > \mathrm{NH_3}\]

5.2 Aromatic Amines vs. Ammonia & Substituent Effects

Aniline (\(\mathrm{p}K_b = 9.38\)) is a much weaker base than ammonia (\(\mathrm{p}K_b = 4.75\)) and aliphatic amines (\(\mathrm{p}K_b \approx 3.0\text{–}4.2\)). In aniline, the nitrogen lone pair is delocalized into the benzene ring across five resonating structures, reducing its availability for protonation. Upon protonation, the resulting anilinium ion (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5NH_3^+}\)) has only two Kekulé structures and loses this extra resonance stabilization.

  • Electron-releasing groups (\(-\mathrm{OCH_3}\), \(-\mathrm{CH_3}\)) increase electron density on nitrogen and stabilize the anilinium ion, increasing basicity.
  • Electron-withdrawing groups (\(-\mathrm{NO_2}\), \(-\mathrm{SO_3H}\), \(-\mathrm{COOH}\), \(-\mathrm{X}\)) pull electron density away and destabilize the anilinium ion, decreasing basicity.
  • Benzylamine (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2NH_2}\), \(\mathrm{p}K_b = 4.70\)) is far more basic than aniline because the \(-\mathrm{CH_2}-\) spacer prevents resonance between the lone pair and the benzene ring.

6. Chemical Reactions & Distinction Tests | রাসায়নিক বিক্রিয়া ও শনাক্তকরণ পরীক্ষা

English

6.1 Acylation & Benzoylation

Primary and secondary amines react with acid chlorides (\(\mathrm{RCOCl}\)), anhydrides (\(\mathrm{(RCO)_2O}\)), or esters in the presence of a stronger base such as pyridine (which scavenges \(\mathrm{HCl}\) and drives the equilibrium forward) to form amides. Tertiary amines lack an \(\mathrm{N-H}\) bond and do not undergo acylation. Reaction with benzoyl chloride (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5COCl}\)) is called benzoylation. With carboxylic acids at room temperature, amines simply form ammonium salts.

6.3 Electrophilic Aromatic Substitution in Aniline

The \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\) group is a powerful activating, ortho/para-directing group:

  • Bromination: Treating aniline with \(\mathrm{Br_2/H_2O}\) at room temperature immediately gives a white precipitate of 2,4,6-tribromoaniline. To obtain monobromoaniline (\(p\)-bromoaniline as major product), protect \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\) by acetylation with \(\mathrm{(CH_3CO)_2O}\)/pyridine to form acetanilide (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5NHCOCH_3}\)), brominate with \(\mathrm{Br_2/CH_3COOH}\), and hydrolyse (\(\mathrm{H^+}\) or \(\mathrm{OH^-}\)). Resonance of the nitrogen lone pair with the carbonyl oxygen in acetanilide moderates ring activation.
  • Nitration: Direct nitration of aniline with \(\mathrm{HNO_3/H_2SO_4}\) at \(288\,\mathrm{K}\) causes tarry oxidation and gives 47% m-nitroaniline alongside 51% p- and 2% o-isomers, because strong acid protonates aniline into the meta-directing anilinium ion (\(-\mathrm{NH_3^+}\)). Protection as acetanilide prior to nitration yields p-nitroaniline cleanly after hydrolysis.
  • Sulphonation: Heating aniline with conc. \(\mathrm{H_2SO_4}\) at \(453\text{–}473\,\mathrm{K}\) gives sulphanilic acid (p-aminobenzenesulphonic acid), which exists as a dipolar zwitterion (\(\mathrm{^+H_3N-C_6H_4-SO_3^-}\)).
  • Friedel–Crafts Reaction: Aniline does not undergo Friedel–Crafts alkylation or acylation because it forms an acid–base salt with the Lewis acid catalyst \(\mathrm{AlCl_3}\), placing a positive charge on nitrogen that strongly deactivates the ring.

Common NEET UG Pitfalls to Avoid

  • Classification trap: \(\mathrm{(CH_3)_3CNH_2}\) (2-methylpropan-2-amine) is a \(1^\circ\) amine, not \(3^\circ\); always count carbons attached directly to nitrogen, not the \(\alpha\)-carbon.
  • Aqueous vs. Gas-phase basicity: Do not apply \(3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ\) to aqueous solutions! Remember \(\mathrm{(C_2H_5)_2NH > (C_2H_5)_3N > C_2H_5NH_2}\) and \(\mathrm{(CH_3)_2NH > CH_3NH_2 > (CH_3)_3N}\).
  • Hoffmann vs. Nitrile ascent: Hoffmann bromamide removes one carbon (\(\mathrm{RCONH_2 \rightarrow RNH_2}\)); nitrile reduction via \(\mathrm{NaCN}\) adds one carbon (\(\mathrm{RX \rightarrow RCN \rightarrow RCH_2NH_2}\)).
  • Hinsberg solubility: \(1^\circ\) amine product is soluble in alkali; \(2^\circ\) amine product is insoluble in alkali; \(3^\circ\) amine does not react.
Final 30-Second Recap: Amines are \(sp^3\) pyramidal Lewis bases. Isomeric boiling points follow \(1^\circ > 2^\circ > 3^\circ\). Aniline is much less basic than aliphatic amines due to resonance, undergoes polybromination and 47% meta-nitration unless protected by acetylation, and fails Friedel–Crafts reactions. Diazotisation at \(273\text{–}278\,\mathrm{K}\) converts \(\mathrm{ArNH_2}\) into \(\mathrm{ArN_2^+Cl^-}\), unlocking halides, nitriles, phenols, arenes, nitroarenes, and azo dyes.

NEET UG পরীক্ষায় এড়িয়ে চলার মতো সাধারণ ভুল

  • শ্রেণিবিভাগের ফাঁদ: \(\mathrm{(CH_3)_3CNH_2}\) একটি \(1^\circ\) অ্যামিন, \(3^\circ\) নয়; সর্বদা নাইট্রোজেনের সাথে সরাসরি যুক্ত কার্বন সংখ্যা গণনা করুন।
  • জলীয় বনাম গ্যাসীয় ক্ষারকীয়তা: জলীয় দ্রবণে \(3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ\) ক্রম প্রয়োগ করবেন না! মনে রাখুন \(\mathrm{(C_2H_5)_2NH > (C_2H_5)_3N > C_2H_5NH_2}\) এবং \(\mathrm{(CH_3)_2NH > CH_3NH_2 > (CH_3)_3N}\)।
  • হফম্যান বনাম নাইট্রাইল পথ: হফম্যান ব্রোমামাইডে একটি কার্বন কমে; \(\mathrm{NaCN}\) হয়ে নাইট্রাইল বিজারণে একটি কার্বন বাড়ে।
  • হিন্সবার্গ দ্রাব্যতা: \(1^\circ\) অ্যামিনের উৎপাদ ক্ষারে দ্রবণীয়; \(2^\circ\) অ্যামিনের উৎপাদ ক্ষারে অদ্রবণীয়; \(3^\circ\) অ্যামিন বিক্রিয়া করে না।
চূড়ান্ত সারসংক্ষেপ: অ্যামিন হলো \(sp^3\) পিরামিডীয় লুইস ক্ষারক। সমাবয়বী স্ফুটনাঙ্কের ক্রম \(1^\circ > 2^\circ > 3^\circ\)। রেজোন্যান্সের কারণে অ্যানিলিন অ্যালিফ্যাটিক অ্যামিনের চেয়ে দুর্বল ক্ষারক এবং অ্যাসিটাইলেশন ছাড়া সরাসরি নাইট্রেশনে 47% m-নাইট্রোঅ্যানিলিন দেয় ও ফ্রিডেল–ক্রাফটস বিক্রিয়া দেয় না। \(273\text{–}278\,\mathrm{K}\)-এ ডায়াজোটাইজেশনের মাধ্যমে উৎপন্ন \(\mathrm{ArN_2^+Cl^-}\) থেকে হ্যালাইড, নাইট্রাইল, ফেনল, অ্যারিন এবং অ্যাজো রঞ্জক সহজেই প্রস্তুত করা যায়।
