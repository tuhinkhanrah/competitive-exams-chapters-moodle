2. Structure, Geometry & Classification / অ্যামিনের গঠন, জ্যামিতি ও শ্রেণিবিভাগ

EnglishMolecular Structure & Geometry: Amines are organic derivatives of ammonia (\(\mathrm{NH_3}\)) in which one, two, or three hydrogen atoms are replaced by alkyl or aryl groups. The central nitrogen atom is trivalent and carries one non-bonding unshared electron pair (lone pair). The nitrogen orbitals undergo \(sp^3\) hybridisation. Three of the four \(sp^3\) hybrid orbitals overlap with either carbon \(sp^3\) or hydrogen \(1s\) orbitals to form three \(\sigma\)-bonds, while the fourth orbital accommodates the lone pair. Consequently, the molecular geometry is pyramidal (derived from an underlying tetrahedral electron-pair geometry).

Due to strong lone pair–bond pair repulsions (exceeding bond pair–bond pair repulsions in accordance with VSEPR theory), the \(\mathrm{C-N-E}\) bond angle (where \(\mathrm{E}\) is \(\mathrm{C}\) or \(\mathrm{H}\)) is compressed to less than the ideal tetrahedral angle of \(109.5^\circ\). For example, in trimethylamine \(\left(\mathrm{CH_3}\right)_3\mathrm{N}\), the \(\mathrm{C-N-C}\) bond angle is contracted to \(108^\circ\).

Classification: Amines are categorised based on the degree of substitution at the nitrogen atom:

  • Primary (\(1^\circ\)) Amines: One hydrogen atom of \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) is replaced by an alkyl/aryl group (\(\mathrm{R-NH_2}\) or \(\mathrm{Ar-NH_2}\)).
  • Secondary (\(2^\circ\)) Amines: Two hydrogen atoms are replaced (\(\mathrm{R-NH-R'}\)). These are classified as simple (symmetrical, e.g., \(\mathrm{CH_3-NH-CH_3}\)) or mixed (unsymmetrical, e.g., \(\mathrm{CH_3-NH-C_2H_5}\)).
  • Tertiary (\(3^\circ\)) Amines: All three hydrogen atoms are replaced (\(\mathrm{R_3N}\)). Likewise, they may be simple or mixed.
  • Quaternary Ammonium Salts: Four organic groups covalently bonded to nitrogen, creating a permanently charged cation: \(\left[\mathrm{R_4N}\right]^+\mathrm{X^-}\).
NEET High-Yield Focus: Naturally Occurring & Pharmacological Amines
  • Adrenaline & Ephedrine: Both contain a secondary amino group (\(2^\circ\)); used clinically to constrict blood vessels and increase blood pressure.
  • Novocain (Procaine): Synthetic amino ester used as a local anaesthetic in dentistry.
  • Benadryl (Diphenhydramine): Contains a tertiary amino group (\(3^\circ\)); widely used as an antihistaminic medication.
  • Quaternary Ammonium Salts: Possess long hydrophobic chains and polar ammonium heads; commercially utilised as cationic surfactants and fabric softeners.

বাংলাআণবিক গঠন ও জ্যামিতি: অ্যামিন হলো অ্যামোনিয়ার (\(\mathrm{NH_3}\)) জৈব জাতক, যেখানে এক বা একাধিক হাইড্রোজেন পরমাণু অ্যালকাইল বা অ্যারাইল মূলক দ্বারা প্রতিস্থাপিত থাকে। অ্যামিনের কেন্দ্রীয় নাইট্রোজেন পরমাণু ত্রিযোজী এবং এতে একটি নিঃসঙ্গ ইলেকট্রন জোড় (lone pair) বিদ্যমান। কেন্দ্রীয় নাইট্রোজেন পরমাণু \(sp^3\) সংকরায়িত। চারটি সংকর কক্ষকের মধ্যে তিনটি সংকর কক্ষক কার্বন বা হাইড্রোজেনের সাথে অভিলেপন করে তিনটি \(\sigma\)-বন্ধন তৈরি করে এবং চতুর্থটিতে নিঃসঙ্গ ইলেকট্রন জোড় অবস্থান করে। ফলে অ্যামিনের জ্যামিতিক গঠন হয় পিরামিডীয় (pyramidal)।

VSEPR তত্ত্ব অনুসারে নিঃসঙ্গ জোড়–বন্ধন জোড় বিকর্ষণের মান বন্ধন জোড়–বন্ধন জোড় বিকর্ষণের চেয়ে বেশি হওয়ায় \(\mathrm{C-N-E}\) বন্ধন কোণ সুষম চতুস্তলকীয় কোণের (\(109.5^\circ\)) চেয়ে কিছুটা সংকুচিত হয়। উদাহরণস্বরূপ, ট্রাইমিথাইলঅ্যামিনে \(\mathrm{C-N-C}\) বন্ধন কোণের মান প্রায় \(108^\circ\)।

শ্রেণিবিভাগ: নাইট্রোজেন পরমাণুতে প্রতিস্থাপিত অ্যালকাইল বা অ্যারাইল মূলকের সংখ্যার ওপর ভিত্তি করে অ্যামিনকে শ্রেণিবদ্ধ করা হয়:

  • প্রাইমারি (\(1^\circ\)) অ্যামিন: অ্যামোনিয়ার একটি হাইড্রোজেন প্রতিস্থাপিত হলে গঠিত হয় (\(\mathrm{R-NH_2}\) বা \(\mathrm{Ar-NH_2}\))।
  • সেকেন্ডারি (\(2^\circ\)) অ্যামিন: দুটি হাইড্রোজেন প্রতিস্থাপিত হলে গঠিত হয় (\(\mathrm{R-NH-R'}\))। দুটি মূলক একই হলে সরল (simple) এবং ভিন্ন হলে মিশ্র (mixed) অ্যামিন বলে।
  • টারশিয়ারি (\(3^\circ\)) অ্যামিন: তিনটি হাইড্রোজেনই প্রতিস্থাপিত হলে গঠিত হয় (\(\mathrm{R_3N}\))।
  • কোয়াটারনারি অ্যামোনিয়াম লবণ: নাইট্রোজেনের সাথে চারটি মূলক যুক্ত হয়ে ধনাত্মক আধানযুক্ত ক্যাটায়ন গঠন করে: \(\left[\mathrm{R_4N}\right]^+\mathrm{X^-}\)।

জৈবিক ও চিকিৎসাগত গুরুত্ব (NEET স্পেশাল):

  • অ্যাড্রেনালিন ও এফেড্রিন: উভয়ের মধ্যেই সেকেন্ডারি অ্যামিনো মূলক (\(2^\circ\)) উপস্থিত; রক্তচাপ বৃদ্ধি করতে ব্যবহৃত হয়।
  • নভোকেইন: ডেন্টিস্ট্রিতে ব্যবহৃত কৃত্রিম চেতনানাশক।
  • বেনাড্রিল: টারশিয়ারি অ্যামিনো মূলকযুক্ত অ্যান্টিহিস্টামিনিক ড্রাগ।
  • কোয়াটারনারি অ্যামোনিয়াম লবণ: পৃষ্ঠতল সক্রিয় পদার্থ বা সারফ্যাক্ট্যান্ট (surfactant) হিসেবে ব্যবহৃত হয়।

3. Nomenclature & Isomerism / নামকরণ ও সমাবয়বতা

EnglishIUPAC and Common Nomenclature Rules:

  • Primary Amines: In IUPAC nomenclature, the suffix 'e' of the corresponding alkane is replaced by 'amine' to form alkanamine (e.g., \(\mathrm{CH_3NH_2}\) is methanamine). In common nomenclature, the alkyl group is named followed by the word amine as one single word (e.g., methylamine).
  • Polyamines: When multiple amino groups are located on the main carbon chain, their positions are designated by numerical locants. The terminal 'e' of the alkane name is retained, followed by di, tri, etc. (e.g., \(\mathrm{H_2N-CH_2-CH_2-NH_2}\) is ethane-1,2-diamine; \(\mathrm{H_2N-(CH_2)_6-NH_2}\) is hexane-1,6-diamine).
  • Secondary & Tertiary Amines: The largest alkyl chain is selected as the parent alkanamine. Other alkyl groups attached directly to nitrogen are specified as substituents using the italicised locant prefix N- (e.g., \(\mathrm{CH_3-NH-CH_2CH_3}\) is N-methylethanamine; \(\left(\mathrm{C_2H_5}\right)_3\mathrm{N}\) is N,N-diethylethanamine).
  • Arylamines: The simplest aryl amine is \(\mathrm{C_6H_5NH_2}\), systematically named benzenamine (aniline is an universally accepted IUPAC name). Substituted arylamines include 2-methylbenzenamine (o-toluidine) and 4-bromobenzenamine (p-bromoaniline).

Isomerism in Amines: Amines with the same molecular formula exhibit multiple types of structural isomerism:

  • Chain Isomerism: Differing carbon chain skeletons (e.g., butan-1-amine vs. 2-methylpropan-1-amine).
  • Position Isomerism: Different location of the amino group on the same parent chain (e.g., propan-1-amine vs. propan-2-amine).
  • Metamerism: Different alkyl chains distributed around the secondary or tertiary nitrogen atom (e.g., diethylamine \(\mathrm{C_2H_5-NH-C_2H_5}\) vs. methylpropylamine \(\mathrm{CH_3-NH-C_3H_7}\)).
  • Functional Isomerism: Primary, secondary, and tertiary amines with the same molecular formula are functional isomers of one another because their characteristic functional groups (\(-\mathrm{NH_2}\), \(-\mathrm{NH}-\), and \(-\mathrm{N}<\)) differ in chemical properties.

Example: For the molecular formula \(\mathrm{C_4H_{11}N}\), there are 8 structural isomers: 4 primary (\(1^\circ\)), 3 secondary (\(2^\circ\)), and 1 tertiary (\(3^\circ\)). For \(\mathrm{C_3H_9N}\), there are 4 structural isomers: 2 primary (propan-1-amine, propan-2-amine), 1 secondary (N-methylethanamine), and 1 tertiary (N,N-dimethylmethanamine).

বাংলাIUPAC ও প্রচলিত নামকরণের নিয়মাবলি:

  • প্রাইমারি অ্যামিন: সংশ্লিষ্ট অ্যালকেনের নামের শেষের 'e' বর্ণটি বাদ দিয়ে 'amine' যুক্ত করে অ্যালকানঅ্যামিন (alkanamine) নামকরণ করা হয় (যেমন, \(\mathrm{CH_3NH_2}\) হলো মিথানঅ্যামিন)। সাধারণ পদ্ধতিতে অ্যালকাইল মূলকের নামের শেষে এক শব্দে 'amine' যুক্ত করা হয় (যেমন, মিথাইলঅ্যামিন)।
  • পলিঅ্যামিন: একাধিক অ্যামিনো মূলক উপস্থিত থাকলে অ্যালকেনের নামের শেষের 'e' বর্ণটি অপরিবর্তিত থাকে এবং সংখ্যাসূচক অবস্থানসহ di, tri ইত্যাদি যোগ করা হয় (যেমন, \(\mathrm{H_2N-CH_2-CH_2-NH_2}\) হলো ইথেন-1,2-ডাইঅ্যামিন)।
  • সেকেন্ডারি ও টারশিয়ারি অ্যামিন: দীর্ঘতম অ্যালকাইল শৃঙ্খলটিকে মূল অ্যালকানঅ্যামিন ধরা হয় এবং নাইট্রোজেনের সাথে যুক্ত অন্য মূলকগুলিকে N- লোক্যান্ট দ্বারা নির্দেশ করা হয় (যেমন, \(\mathrm{CH_3-NH-CH_2CH_3}\) হলো N-মিথাইলইথানঅ্যামিন)।
  • অ্যারোমেটিক অ্যামিন: বেনজিনের সাথে সরাসরি \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\) যুক্ত যৌগ \(\mathrm{C_6H_5NH_2}\)-কে বেনজিনঅ্যামিন বা অ্যানিলিন বলা হয়।

অ্যামিনের সমাবয়বতা (Isomerism): অ্যামিন যৌগগুলি শৃঙ্খল সমাবয়বতা (chain isomerism), অবস্থান সমাবয়বতা (position isomerism), মেটামারিজম (metamerism) এবং কার্যকরী মূলক সমাবয়বতা (functional isomerism) প্রদর্শন করে। প্রাইমারি, সেকেন্ডারি ও টারশিয়ারি অ্যামিন পরস্পরের কার্যকরী মূলক সমাবয়বী। \(\mathrm{C_4H_{11}N}\) আণবিক সংকেতের মোট ৮টি সমাবয়বী রয়েছে (৪টি \(1^\circ\), ৩টি \(2^\circ\), ১টি \(3^\circ\)) এবং \(\mathrm{C_3H_9N}\)-এর ৪টি সমাবয়বী রয়েছে (২টি \(1^\circ\), ১টি \(2^\circ\), ১টি \(3^\circ\))।

4. Methods of Preparation of Amines / অ্যামিন প্রস্তুতির পদ্ধতিসমূহ

EnglishAmines are prepared industrially and in the laboratory via six primary pathways:

1. Reduction of Nitro Compounds

Nitro compounds are reduced to primary amines by catalytic hydrogenation using \(\mathrm{H_2}\) gas in the presence of finely divided Raney \(\mathrm{Ni}\), \(\mathrm{Pd}\), or \(\mathrm{Pt}\) in ethanol, or by chemical reduction with active metals in acidic medium (\(\mathrm{Sn / HCl}\) or \(\mathrm{Fe / HCl}\)):

\[\mathrm{Ar-NO_2 + 3H_2 \xrightarrow{Pd / C_2H_5OH} Ar-NH_2 + 2H_2O}\] \[\mathrm{R-NO_2 \xrightarrow{Fe + HCl} R-NH_2 + 2H_2O}\]
NEET High-Yield Rationale: Why is Fe + HCl preferred over Sn + HCl?

Reduction using scrap iron (\(\mathrm{Fe}\)) and hydrochloric acid is strongly preferred commercially because the \(\mathrm{FeCl_2}\) formed as an initial byproduct undergoes in situ hydrolysis to regenerate hydrochloric acid (\(\mathrm{FeCl_2 + 2H_2O \rightarrow Fe(OH)_2 + 2HCl}\)). Consequently, only a very small, catalytic amount of hydrochloric acid is needed to initiate and sustain the complete reduction, making the process highly economical and environmentally efficient.

2. Ammonolysis of Alkyl Halides (Hoffmann's Ammonolysis)

An alkyl or benzyl halide heated with an ethanolic solution of ammonia at \(373\,\mathrm{K}\) in a sealed tube undergoes nucleophilic substitution (\(\mathrm{S_N2}\)), wherein the halogen atom is displaced by an amino group:

\[\mathrm{NH_3 + R-X \longrightarrow R-\overset{+}{N}H_3 \bar{X} \xrightarrow{NaOH} R-NH_2 + H_2O + NaX}\]

The primary amine product possesses an unshared pair of electrons and acts as a nucleophile, reacting competitively with remaining alkyl halide to yield secondary amines, tertiary amines, and ultimately a quaternary ammonium salt:

\[\mathrm{R-NH_2 \xrightarrow{RX} R_2NH \xrightarrow{RX} R_3N \xrightarrow{RX} [R_4N]^+X^-}\]

Order of reactivity of halides: \(\mathrm{R-I > R-Br > R-Cl}\) (governed by \(\mathrm{C-X}\) bond dissociation enthalpy).
Synthetic Control: To maximise the yield of primary amine, a large excess of ammonia is employed. Conversely, using excess alkyl halide forces the reaction to the quaternary ammonium stage.

3. Reduction of Nitriles

Nitriles on reduction with lithium aluminium hydride (\(\mathrm{LiAlH_4}\)) or catalytic hydrogenation (\(\mathrm{H_2 / Ni}\) or \(\mathrm{Na(Hg) / C_2H_5OH}\)) yield primary amines:

\[\mathrm{R-C\equiv N \xrightarrow{H_2 / Ni \text{ or } LiAlH_4} R-CH_2-NH_2}\]

Ascent of Series: This reaction serves as an essential method for extending the carbon chain by one carbon atom (\(\mathrm{R-X \xrightarrow{NaCN} R-CN \xrightarrow{reduction} R-CH_2NH_2}\)).

4. Reduction of Amides

Amides undergo reduction with lithium aluminium hydride followed by aqueous workup to yield primary amines with the same number of carbon atoms:

\[\mathrm{R-CONH_2 \xrightarrow{(i)\,LiAlH_4,\;(ii)\,H_2O} R-CH_2-NH_2}\]

5. Gabriel Phthalimide Synthesis

Gabriel synthesis is exclusively used for the preparation of pure aliphatic primary amines without contamination from secondary or tertiary amines:

  1. Phthalimide is deprotonated by ethanolic \(\mathrm{KOH}\) to form potassium phthalimide.
  2. Potassium phthalimide undergoes \(\mathrm{S_N2}\) substitution on an alkyl halide (\(\mathrm{R-X}\)) to produce \(N\)-alkylphthalimide.
  3. Alkaline hydrolysis of \(N\)-alkylphthalimide with aqueous \(\mathrm{NaOH}\) releases the primary amine and water-soluble sodium phthalate.
NEET Rule: Why can Aniline NOT be prepared by Gabriel Synthesis?

Aromatic primary amines (e.g., aniline) cannot be prepared by Gabriel phthalimide synthesis because aryl halides do not undergo nucleophilic substitution (\(\mathrm{S_N2}\)) with the planar phthalimide anion. The carbon-halogen bond in aryl halides has partial double bond character due to resonance with the benzene ring, and nucleophilic backside attack is sterically and electronically blocked by the aromatic \(\pi\)-electron cloud.

6. Hoffmann Bromamide Degradation Reaction

When a primary carboxamide is treated with bromine in aqueous or ethanolic \(\mathrm{NaOH}\) (or \(\mathrm{KOH}\)), an intramolecular rearrangement occurs wherein the alkyl or aryl group migrates from the carbonyl carbon to the nitrogen atom:

\[\mathrm{R-CONH_2 + Br_2 + 4NaOH \longrightarrow R-NH_2 + Na_2CO_3 + 2NaBr + 2H_2O}\]

Descent of Series: The resulting amine contains one carbon atom less than the starting amide. Both aliphatic and aromatic primary amines can be prepared by this method (e.g., benzamide yields aniline).

5. Physical Properties & Intermolecular Forces / ভৌত ধর্মাবলি ও আন্তঃআণবিক বল

English1. Physical State & Odour: Lower aliphatic amines (methanamine, ethanamine, dimethylamine) are gases at room temperature with a pronounced fishy, ammoniacal odour. Primary amines with three to eight carbon atoms are volatile liquids, whereas higher amines (\(\mathrm{C_8}\) and above) are solids. Pure aniline and other arylamines are virtually colourless, but undergo facile aerial oxidation on storage to develop a dark reddish-brown colouration.

2. Intermolecular Hydrogen Bonding & Boiling Points: Nitrogen has an electronegativity of 3.0, generating a polar \(\mathrm{N-H}\) bond that establishes intermolecular hydrogen bonding (\(\mathrm{N-H\cdots N}\)).

  • Primary amines have two acidic hydrogens on nitrogen and establish an extensive 3D intermolecular hydrogen-bonded network.
  • Secondary amines possess only one hydrogen atom on nitrogen and form weaker, linear hydrogen-bonded associations.
  • Tertiary amines have no hydrogen atom bonded to nitrogen and cannot form intermolecular hydrogen bonds with themselves (they interact solely via weaker dipole-dipole forces).

Therefore, for isomeric amines of identical molar mass, the boiling point sequence is strictly: Primary (\(1^\circ\)) > Secondary (\(2^\circ\)) > Tertiary (\(3^\circ\)).

As the hydrophobic alkyl chain grows larger (\(\mathrm{C_6}\) and above), the hydrophobic shielding outweighs hydrogen bonding, and water solubility diminishes drastically.

NEET Question: Why is butan-1-ol more soluble in water than butan-1-amine?

Due to the higher electronegativity of oxygen (3.5) compared to nitrogen (3.0), the \(\mathrm{-OH}\) group is more polar than the \(\mathrm{-NH_2}\) group. Consequently, butan-1-ol forms stronger and more thermodynamically favourable hydrogen bonds with water molecules than butan-1-amine, imparting greater water solubility.

  • ভৌত অবস্থা ও গন্ধ: নিম্নতর অ্যালিফ্যাটিক অ্যামিনগুলি আঁশটে (fishy) অ্যামোনিয়ার মতো গন্ধযুক্ত গ্যাস। \(C_3\) থেকে তরল এবং উচ্চতরগুলি কঠিন। বিশুদ্ধ অ্যানিলিন বর্ণহীন হলেও বায়বীয় জারণের কারণে বাদামি বর্ণ ধারণ করে।
  • স্ফুটনাঙ্কের তুলনা: নাইট্রোজেনের সাথে যুক্ত হাইড্রোজেনের উপস্থিতির কারণে অ্যামিন আন্তঃআণবিক হাইড্রোজেন বন্ধন গঠন করে।
    • \(1^\circ\) অ্যামিনে দুটি \(\mathrm{H}\) থাকায় হাইড্রোজেন বন্ধন সবচেয়ে শক্তিশালী।
    • \(2^\circ\) অ্যামিনে একটি \(\mathrm{H}\) থাকায় হাইড্রোজেন বন্ধন তুলনামূলক দুর্বল।
    • \(3^\circ\) অ্যামিনে নাইট্রোজেনের সাথে কোনো \(\mathrm{H}\) না থাকায় নিজেদের মধ্যে কোনো হাইড্রোজেন বন্ধন তৈরি হয় না।

  • অ্যালকোহল বনাম অ্যামিন: অক্সিজেনের তড়িৎঋণাত্মকতা (\(3.5\)) নাইট্রোজেনের (\(3.0\)) চেয়ে বেশি হওয়ায় অ্যালকোহলের \(\mathrm{O-H}\) বন্ধন অধিক পোলার এবং শক্তিশালী হাইড্রোজেন বন্ধন গঠন করে। ফলে সমআণবিক ভরের ক্ষেত্রে স্ফুটনাঙ্ক ও জলের দ্রাব্যতা উভয়ই অ্যামিনের চেয়ে অ্যালকোহলে বেশি হয় (যেমন, বিউটান-1-অল > বিউটান-1-অ্যামিন)।

6. Chemical Reactions of Amines / অ্যামিনের রাসায়নিক বিক্রিয়াসমূহ

EnglishThe reactivity of amines is dictated by the difference in electronegativity between nitrogen and hydrogen, the presence of an unshared electron pair on nitrogen, and the number of active hydrogen atoms attached to nitrogen.

1. Basic Character of Amines

Being Lewis bases due to the unshared lone pair on nitrogen, amines react readily with mineral acids to form ionic substituted ammonium salts:

\[\mathrm{R-NH_2 + HX \rightleftharpoons R-\overset{+}{N}H_3 \bar{X}}\] \[\mathrm{C_6H_5NH_2 + HCl \rightleftharpoons C_6H_5\overset{+}{N}H_3 \bar{C}l \quad \text{(Anilinium chloride)}}\]

These salts are soluble in water but insoluble in non-polar organic solvents (e.g., diethyl ether). Treatment of the salt with a strong mineral base (\(\mathrm{NaOH}\)) regenerates the uncharged parent amine:

\[\mathrm{R-\overset{+}{N}H_3 \bar{X} + \bar{O}H \longrightarrow R-NH_2 + H_2O + \bar{X}}\]

This reversible acid-base precipitation reaction provides the fundamental analytical protocol for separating amines from neutral or acidic water-insoluble organic compounds.

Quantitative Basicity (\(K_b\) and \(\mathrm{p}K_b\)):
In aqueous solution, the basicity equilibrium is expressed as:

\[\mathrm{R-NH_2 + H_2O \rightleftharpoons R-\overset{+}{N}H_3 + \bar{O}H}\] \[K_b = \frac{[\mathrm{R-\overset{+}{N}H_3}][\mathrm{\bar{O}H}]}{[\mathrm{R-NH_2}]}, \quad \mathrm{p}K_b = -\log K_b\]

A higher \(K_b\) (or smaller \(\mathrm{p}K_b\)) corresponds to a stronger base. For ammonia, \(\mathrm{p}K_b = 4.75\). Aliphatic amines have \(\mathrm{p}K_b\) values between \(3.00\) and \(4.22\), making them substantially stronger bases than ammonia. Arylamines (e.g., aniline, \(\mathrm{p}K_b = 9.38\)) are drastically weaker bases than ammonia.

Structure–Basicity Relationships

(A) Gas Phase Basicity:
In the absence of a solvent, basicity depends exclusively on the electron-releasing inductive effect (\(+I\)) of alkyl groups, which disperses the positive charge of the substituted ammonium cation. Basicity increases systematically with the number of alkyl substituents: \[\text{Gas Phase Basicity: } \mathbf{3^\circ \text{ Amine} > 2^\circ \text{ Amine} > 1^\circ \text{ Amine} > NH_3}\] \[\mathrm{(C_2H_5)_3N > (C_2H_5)_2NH > C_2H_5NH_2 > NH_3}\]

(B) Aqueous Phase Basicity (Interplay of 3 Effects):
In water, basicity is governed by an intricate combination of three simultaneous effects:

  1. Inductive Effect (\(+I\)): Alkyl groups push electrons toward nitrogen, increasing lone-pair availability and dispersing the positive charge in the conjugate acid: \(3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ\).
  2. Solvation / Hydration Effect: The conjugate ammonium cation is stabilised via hydrogen bonding with water molecules. Greater number of hydrogens on nitrogen allows more extensive H-bonding: \[\mathrm{R\overset{+}{N}H_3 \text{ (3 H-bonds)} > R_2\overset{+}{N}H_2 \text{ (2 H-bonds)} > R_3\overset{+}{N}H \text{ (1 H-bond)}}\] Thus, solvation stabilises the \(1^\circ\) cation most, favouring the basicity order: \(1^\circ > 2^\circ > 3^\circ\).
  3. Steric Hindrance: Bulkier alkyl groups crowd the nitrogen atom, physically shielding the cation and hindering hydrogen bonding with surrounding water molecules.
NEET Master Memory Rules: Aqueous Basicity Sequences

Due to the subtle balance of inductive, hydration, and steric factors:

  • Methyl-substituted amines (Rule: 2, 1, 3):
    \[\mathbf{(CH_3)_2NH > CH_3NH_2 > (CH_3)_3N > NH_3} \quad (2^\circ > 1^\circ > 3^\circ > \mathrm{NH_3})\] (\(\mathrm{p}K_b\): \(3.27 < 3.38 < 4.22 < 4.75\))
  • Ethyl-substituted amines (Rule: 2, 3, 1):
    \[\mathbf{(C_2H_5)_2NH > (C_2H_5)_3N > C_2H_5NH_2 > NH_3} \quad (2^\circ > 3^\circ > 1^\circ > \mathrm{NH_3})\] (\(\mathrm{p}K_b\): \(3.00 < 3.25 < 3.29 < 4.75\))

(C) Arylamines vs. Ammonia (Resonance Delocalisation):
Aniline (\(\mathrm{p}K_b = 9.38\)) is a much weaker base than ammonia (\(\mathrm{p}K_b = 4.75\)) because the unshared electron pair on nitrogen is conjugated with the \(\pi\)-electron system of the benzene ring. Aniline is a resonance hybrid of five contributing structures, in three of which the nitrogen atom acquires a partial positive charge while the ortho and para ring positions acquire negative charge density.

In contrast, when aniline accepts a proton, the resulting anilinium cation (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5\overset{+}{N}H_3}\)) has only two Kekulé resonance structures. Because aniline (5 structures) is resonance-stabilised to a greater extent than the anilinium ion (2 structures), protonation is thermodynamically unfavourable, rendering aniline a weak base.

Substituent Effects on Arylamine Basicity:

  • Electron-Donating Groups (EDG) (\(\mathrm{-OCH_3, -CH_3}\)) increase electron density on nitrogen and stabilise the conjugate acid via \(+I / +M\) effects, thereby increasing basic strength (e.g., \(p\)-toluidine > aniline).
  • Electron-Withdrawing Groups (EWG) (\(\mathrm{-NO_2, -COOH, -SO_3H, -X}\)) withdraw electron density via \(-I / -M\) effects and destabilise the cation, decreasing basic strength (e.g., aniline > \(p\)-nitroaniline).

2. Alkylation

Amines react with alkyl halides via nucleophilic substitution (\(\mathrm{S_N2}\)), undergoing successive alkylation from primary to secondary, tertiary, and finally quaternary ammonium salts.

3. Acylation

Primary and secondary amines react with acid chlorides, acid anhydrides, and esters via nucleophilic acyl substitution to form \(N\)-substituted amides. The reaction is performed in the presence of a base stronger than the amine (e.g., pyridine) to remove the \(\mathrm{HCl}\) byproduct and shift the equilibrium to completion:

\[\mathrm{C_2H_5NH_2 + CH_3COCl \xrightarrow{\text{Pyridine}} C_2H_5NHCOCH_3 + HCl}\] \[\mathrm{C_6H_5NH_2 + (CH_3CO)_2O \xrightarrow{\text{Pyridine}} C_6H_5NHCOCH_3 \quad \text{(Acetanilide)} + CH_3COOH}\]

Benzoylation (Schotten-Baumann Reaction): Amines react with benzoyl chloride (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5COCl}\)) to produce benzamides:

\[\mathrm{CH_3NH_2 + C_6H_5COCl \longrightarrow CH_3NHCOC_6H_5 + HCl}\]

Note: Tertiary amines do not undergo acylation because they lack an exchangeable hydrogen on nitrogen.

4. Carbylamine Reaction (Isocyanide Test)

Both aliphatic and aromatic primary amines on heating with chloroform (\(\mathrm{CHCl_3}\)) and ethanolic potassium hydroxide (\(\mathrm{KOH}\)) form intensely foul-smelling isocyanides (carbylamines):

\[\mathrm{R-NH_2 + CHCl_3 + 3KOH \xrightarrow{\Delta} R-NC + 3KCl + 3H_2O}\] \[\mathrm{C_6H_5NH_2 + CHCl_3 + 3KOH \xrightarrow{\Delta} C_6H_5NC + 3KCl + 3H_2O}\]

Secondary and tertiary amines do not show this reaction. It serves as a definitive diagnostic test for primary amino groups.

5. Reaction with Nitrous Acid (\(\mathrm{HNO_2}\))

Nitrous acid is unstable and prepared in situ from \(\mathrm{NaNO_2}\) and dilute mineral acid (\(\mathrm{HCl}\)):

  • Primary Aliphatic Amines: React with nitrous acid to yield highly unstable alkyldiazonium salts, which rapidly decompose with the quantitative liberation of nitrogen gas and formation of alcohols: \[\mathrm{R-NH_2 + HNO_2 \xrightarrow{NaNO_2 + HCl} [R-N_2^+Cl^-] \xrightarrow{H_2O} ROH + N_2\uparrow + HCl}\] (The quantitative measurement of liberated \(\mathrm{N_2}\) is utilised clinically to estimate amino acids and proteins.)
  • Primary Aromatic Amines: React with nitrous acid at ice-cold temperatures (\(273 - 278\,\mathrm{K}\)) to yield water-soluble arenediazonium salts: \[\mathrm{C_6H_5NH_2 + NaNO_2 + 2HCl \xrightarrow{273-278\,K} C_6H_5N_2^+Cl^- + NaCl + 2H_2O}\]

6. Reaction with Arylsulphonyl Chloride (Hinsberg's Test)

Benzenesulphonyl chloride (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5SO_2Cl}\)), known as Hinsberg's reagent (modern alternative: \(p\)-toluenesulphonyl chloride), distinguishes and separates primary, secondary, and tertiary amines:

  • Primary (\(1^\circ\)) Amine: Forms \(N\)-ethylbenzenesulphonamide. The hydrogen attached to nitrogen is strongly acidic due to the powerful electron-withdrawing nature of the adjacent sulphonyl (\(\mathrm{-SO_2-}\)) group. Hence, it dissolves readily in aqueous alkali (\(\mathrm{NaOH}\)). Acidification precipitates the sulphonamide.
  • Secondary (\(2^\circ\)) Amine: Forms \(N,N\)-diethylbenzenesulphonamide. Because there is no acidic hydrogen bonded to nitrogen, it is neutral and completely insoluble in aqueous alkali.
  • Tertiary (\(3^\circ\)) Amine: Has no hydrogen on nitrogen and does not react with Hinsberg's reagent. It remains insoluble in aqueous alkali but dissolves in dilute acid.

7. Electrophilic Aromatic Substitution of Aniline

The amino group (\(\mathrm{-NH_2}\)) is an exceptionally powerful activating and ortho, para-directing group due to resonance donation of the nitrogen lone pair.

(A) Bromination:
Aniline reacts spontaneously with bromine water (\(\mathrm{Br_2 / H_2O}\)) at room temperature to produce a white precipitate of 2,4,6-tribromoaniline:

\[\mathrm{C_6H_5NH_2 + 3Br_2 \xrightarrow{H_2O} 2,4,6-Tribromoaniline\downarrow + 3HBr}\]

Controlled Monobromination via Protecting Strategy: To prepare the monosubstituted 4-bromoaniline, the activating effect of the amino group is tamed by acetylation with acetic anhydride in pyridine. The resulting acetanilide is brominated with \(\mathrm{Br_2}\) in glacial \(\mathrm{CH_3COOH}\) to yield \(p\)-bromoacetanilide, followed by alkaline or acidic hydrolysis to unmask 4-bromoaniline.

Mechanism of Protection: Why does Acetanilide activate the ring less than Aniline?

In acetanilide (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5-NH-COCH_3}\)), the nitrogen lone pair participates in resonance with both the benzene ring and the carbonyl oxygen (\(\mathrm{C=O}\)):
\[\mathrm{-NH-\overset{O}{\overset{||}{C}}-CH_3 \longleftrightarrow -\overset{+}{N}H=\overset{\bar{O}}{\overset{|}{C}}-CH_3}\]
Because oxygen is highly electronegative, electron density is preferentially pulled toward oxygen, making the nitrogen lone pair less available for donation into the benzene ring. Hence, the activating effect of the acetamido group is markedly less than that of the free amino group.

(B) Nitration:
Direct nitration of aniline with a nitrating mixture (\(\text{conc. }\mathrm{HNO_3} + \text{conc. }\mathrm{H_2SO_4}\)) at \(288\,\mathrm{K}\) yields tarry oxidation products alongside an anomalous product distribution:

  • \(p\)-Nitroaniline: 51%
  • \(m\)-Nitroaniline: 47%
  • \(o\)-Nitroaniline: 2%
NEET High-Yield: Why does Nitration of Aniline give 47% meta product?

In the strongly acidic nitrating medium, basic aniline is protonated to form the anilinium ion (\(\mathrm{-NH_3^+}\)). The positively charged ammonium group acts as a powerful electron-withdrawing, strongly deactivating group that directs the incoming nitronium ion (\(\mathrm{NO_2^+}\)) to the meta position. The equilibrium between free aniline (yielding \(p\)- and \(o\)-products) and anilinium ion (yielding \(m\)-product) leads to the significant (47%) formation of \(m\)-nitroaniline.

Controlled Nitration: Protecting the \(\mathrm{-NH_2}\) group as acetanilide, followed by nitration at \(288\,\mathrm{K}\) and subsequent hydrolysis, gives \(p\)-nitroaniline exclusively as the major product.

(C) Sulphonation:
Aniline reacts with concentrated \(\mathrm{H_2SO_4}\) to form anilinium hydrogensulphate, which upon prolonged heating at \(453 - 473\,\mathrm{K}\) rearranges to form sulphanilic acid (\(p\)-aminobenzenesulphonic acid). Sulphanilic acid exists predominantly as a dipolar internal salt known as a Zwitterion:

\[\mathrm{H_2N-C_6H_4-SO_3H \rightleftharpoons \overset{+}{H_3}N-C_6H_4-SO_3^- \quad \text{(Zwitterion)}}\]

(D) Failure in Friedel-Crafts Reactions:
Aniline does not undergo Friedel-Crafts alkylation or acylation reactions. Anhydrous aluminium chloride (\(\mathrm{AlCl_3}\)), used as a Lewis acid catalyst, forms a coordinate acid-base complex with the unshared lone pair on the amino nitrogen atom (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5-NH_2^+-\bar{A}lCl_3}\)). The resulting positive formal charge on nitrogen exerts a massive \(-I\) effect, powerfully deactivating the benzene ring toward electrophilic attack.

7. Diazonium Salts (\(\mathrm{ArN_2^+X^-}\)) / ডায়াজোনিয়াম লবণ

EnglishArenediazonium salts have the general structural formula \(\mathrm{Ar-\overset{+}{N}\equiv N\;X^-}\), where \(\mathrm{Ar}\) is an aromatic aryl ring and \(\mathrm{X^-}\) is an anion such as \(\mathrm{Cl^-}\), \(\mathrm{Br^-}\), \(\mathrm{HSO_4^-}\), or \(\mathrm{BF_4^-}\).

Resonance Stability of Arenediazonium Ion:
Primary aliphatic diazonium salts are notoriously unstable and fragment explosively even below \(0^\circ\mathrm{C}\) to liberate \(\mathrm{N_2}\). In sharp contrast, arenediazonium salts are stable in cold aqueous solution (\(273 - 278\,\mathrm{K}\)). This stability arises from resonance delocalisation of the positive charge on the diazo nitrogen onto the ortho and para carbons of the benzene ring through conjugation with the aromatic \(\pi\)-system.

Preparation (Diazotisation):
Benzenediazonium chloride is prepared by treating ice-cold aniline dissolved in excess hydrochloric acid with an aqueous solution of sodium nitrite at \(273 - 278\,\mathrm{K}\) (\(0 - 5^\circ\mathrm{C}\)):

\[\mathrm{C_6H_5NH_2 + NaNO_2 + 2HCl \xrightarrow{273 - 278\,K} C_6H_5N_2^+Cl^- + NaCl + 2H_2O}\]

Because arenediazonium chloride decomposes upon warming and detonates when dried, it is never isolated in crystalline form; it is maintained in ice-cold aqueous solution and utilised immediately.

Physical Properties:
Benzenediazonium chloride is a colourless, water-soluble crystalline solid that is stable in cold solution but hydrolyses rapidly above \(283\,\mathrm{K}\). Conversely, benzenediazonium fluoroborate (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5N_2^+BF_4^-}\)) is water-insoluble and exceptionally stable at room temperature.

Reactions of Diazonium Salts

The chemical reactions of diazonium salts are classified into two broad categories:

Category A: Reactions Involving Displacement of Nitrogen

The diazonium group (\(\mathrm{-N_2^+}\)) is among the best leaving groups known in organic chemistry because the departing molecule is dinitrogen gas (\(\mathrm{N_2}\)), whose exceptional thermodynamic stability (\(N\equiv N\) bond energy \(= 945\,\mathrm{kJ\cdot mol^{-1}}\)) provides an overwhelming entropic driving force.

  1. Sandmeyer and Gattermann Reactions (Replacement by \(\mathrm{-Cl, -Br, -CN}\)):
    • Sandmeyer Reaction: Introduction of chloro, bromo, or cyano groups by treating the diazonium salt solution with \(\mathrm{Cu(I)}\) salts: \[\mathrm{ArN_2^+Cl^- \xrightarrow{Cu_2Cl_2 / HCl} Ar-Cl + N_2\uparrow}\] \[\mathrm{ArN_2^+Cl^- \xrightarrow{Cu_2Br_2 / HBr} Ar-Br + N_2\uparrow}\] \[\mathrm{ArN_2^+Cl^- \xrightarrow{CuCN / KCN} Ar-CN + N_2\uparrow}\]
    • Gattermann Reaction: Chlorine or bromine is introduced using finely divided copper powder in the presence of the corresponding halogen acid: \[\mathrm{ArN_2^+Cl^- \xrightarrow{Cu / HCl} Ar-Cl + N_2 + CuCl}\] \[\mathrm{ArN_2^+Cl^- \xrightarrow{Cu / HBr} Ar-Br + N_2 + CuCl}\]
    • NEET Comparison: The Sandmeyer reaction provides significantly higher yields and cleaner product profiles than the Gattermann reaction.
  2. Replacement by Iodide Ion (\(\mathrm{-I}\)):

    Aryl iodides cannot be synthesised by direct iodination. However, simply warming the diazonium salt solution with aqueous potassium iodide produces iodobenzene quantitatively:

    \[\mathrm{ArN_2^+Cl^- + KI \longrightarrow Ar-I + KCl + N_2\uparrow}\]
  3. Replacement by Fluoride Ion (\(\mathrm{-F}\)) — Balz-Schiemann Reaction:

    Aryl fluorides cannot be prepared by direct fluorination. Treatment of arenediazonium chloride with fluoroboric acid precipitates arenediazonium fluoroborate, which upon gentle dry heating decomposes cleanly to yield aryl fluoride:

    \[\mathrm{ArN_2^+Cl^- + HBF_4 \longrightarrow Ar-N_2^+BF_4^-\downarrow \xrightarrow{\Delta} Ar-F + BF_3 + N_2\uparrow}\]
  4. Replacement by Hydrogen (\(\mathrm{-H}\)) — Reductive Deamination:

    Mild reducing agents reduce diazonium salts to parent arenes while themselves being oxidised:

    \[\mathrm{ArN_2^+Cl^- + H_3PO_2 + H_2O \longrightarrow Ar-H + N_2\uparrow + H_3PO_3 + HCl}\] \[\mathrm{ArN_2^+Cl^- + CH_3CH_2OH \longrightarrow Ar-H + N_2\uparrow + CH_3CHO + HCl}\]

    (Hypophosphorous acid \(\mathrm{H_3PO_2}\) is oxidised to phosphorous acid \(\mathrm{H_3PO_3}\); ethanol is oxidised to ethanal.)

  5. Replacement by Hydroxyl Group (\(\mathrm{-OH}\)):

    If the diazonium solution is warmed to \(283\,\mathrm{K}\) (\(10^\circ\mathrm{C}\)) or above, it undergoes hydrolytic substitution to produce phenol:

    \[\mathrm{ArN_2^+Cl^- + H_2O \xrightarrow{\Delta} Ar-OH + N_2\uparrow + HCl}\]
  6. Replacement by Nitro Group (\(\mathrm{-NO_2}\)):

    When diazonium fluoroborate is heated with aqueous sodium nitrite in the presence of catalytic copper powder, nitroarene is produced:

    \[\mathrm{Ar-N_2^+BF_4^- + NaNO_2 \xrightarrow{Cu,\,\Delta} Ar-NO_2 + N_2\uparrow + NaBF_4}\]

Category B: Reactions Involving Retention of Diazo Group (Azo Coupling Reactions)

The arenediazonium cation is a weak electrophile. It attacks highly activated aromatic rings (such as phenols and arylamines) predominantly at the less sterically hindered para position (or ortho if the para position is occupied). The resulting azo compounds contain the extended conjugated \(\mathrm{-N=N-}\) chromophore bridging two aromatic rings, imparting vibrant colours:

  • Coupling with Phenol: Carried out in mildly alkaline medium (\(\mathrm{pH} = 9 - 10\)). The alkaline condition converts phenol into the phenoxide ion (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5O^-}\)), which is far more nucleophilic and strongly activated than phenol. The reaction produces \(p\)-hydroxyazobenzene (a brilliant orange dye): \[\mathrm{C_6H_5N_2^+Cl^- + C_6H_5OH \xrightarrow{\bar{O}H,\,pH\,9-10} C_6H_5-N=N-C_6H_4-OH\,(p) + Cl^- + H_2O}\]
  • Coupling with Aniline: Carried out in mildly acidic medium (\(\mathrm{pH} = 4 - 5\)). The weakly acidic pH prevents protonation of the amino group (which would deactivate the ring to anilinium) while keeping diazonium stable. 8. High-Yield Conversions & Synthetic Roadmaps / উচ্চ-ফলনশীল রূপান্তর ও রোডম্যাপ

EnglishMastery of aromatic multi-step synthesis mediated by diazonium intermediates is frequently tested in NEET UG. Here are the classic benchmark synthetic sequences:

Conversion 1: 4-Nitrotoluene to 2-Bromobenzoic Acid

Direct bromination of toluene or benzoic acid cannot yield 2-bromobenzoic acid due to directing group conflicts. The nitro group in 4-nitrotoluene is used as a directing guide and later removed via diazonium deamination:

  1. Bromination: 4-Nitrotoluene treated with \(\mathrm{Br_2 / Fe}\) directs bromine to the position ortho to methyl (meta to nitro), yielding 2-bromo-4-nitrotoluene.
  2. Reduction: \(\mathrm{Sn / HCl}\) selectively reduces the nitro group to an amino group, forming 2-bromo-4-methylaniline.
  3. Diazotisation: \(\mathrm{NaNO_2 + HCl}\) at \(273 - 278\,\mathrm{K}\) converts the amino group to the diazonium salt.
  4. Reductive Deamination: Hypophosphorous acid (\(\mathrm{H_3PO_2 + H_2O}\)) removes the diazo group, yielding 2-bromotoluene.
  5. Side-chain Oxidation: Vigorous oxidation with alkaline potassium permanganate (\(\mathrm{KMnO_4 / \bar{O}H}\)) oxidises the methyl group to a carboxylic acid, producing pure 2-bromobenzoic acid.

Conversion 2: Aniline to 1,3,5-Tribromobenzene

Direct bromination of benzene cannot introduce three bromine atoms strictly meta to each other. The activating power of the amino group is exploited and subsequently eliminated:

  1. Tribromination: Aniline treated with bromine water (\(\mathrm{Br_2 / H_2O}\)) yields 2,4,6-tribromoaniline quantitatively.
  2. Diazotisation: \(\mathrm{NaNO_2 + HCl}\) at \(273 - 278\,\mathrm{K}\) yields 2,4,6-tribromobenzenediazonium chloride.
  3. Deamination: Treatment with \(\mathrm{H_3PO_2 + H_2O}\) or ethanol (\(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2OH}\)) removes the diazonium group cleanly, delivering symmetrical 1,3,5-tribromobenzene.

Practice Question 2EnglishWhich of the following amines cannot be prepared by Gabriel phthalimide synthesis?

Detailed Solution: The nitration reaction is performed under strongly acidic conditions (\(\mathrm{HNO_3 + H_2SO_4}\)). The weakly basic amino group of aniline accepts a proton to form the anilinium cation: \(\mathrm{C_6H_5NH_2 + H^+ \rightleftharpoons C_6H_5\overset{+}{N}H_3}\). 10. Common Pitfalls & High-Yield NEET Revision Checklist / সাধারণ ভুলভ্রান্তি ও রিভিশন চেকলিস্ট

EnglishAvoid these frequent traps and verify key takeaways before entering the examination:

  • Classification Trap: Do not confuse carbon classification with amine classification. In tert-butylamine \(\left(\mathrm{CH_3}\right)_3\mathrm{C-NH_2}\), the nitrogen is bonded to only ONE carbon; hence it is a primary (\(1^\circ\)) amine, even though the carbon skeleton is tertiary!
  • Gabriel Synthesis Trap: Remember that Gabriel synthesis is strictly limited to primary aliphatic amines. Neither aniline nor secondary/tertiary amines can be prepared by this method.
  • Hoffmann Bromamide Carbon Count: Always deduct exactly ONE carbon from the amide. Butanamide (\(4\,\mathrm{C}\)) yields propan-1-amine (\(3\,\mathrm{C}\)). The lost carbon is expelled as \(\mathrm{Na_2CO_3}\).
  • Aqueous Basicity Trap: Memorise the numeric rules: for methyl amines, order is 2, 1, 3; for ethyl amines, order is 2, 3, 1. Do not use gas-phase order (\(3 > 2 > 1\)) unless the question explicitly specifies "in gas phase" or "in non-polar solvent".
  • Nitration of Aniline Trap: Aniline does not give exclusively ortho/para products on direct nitration; \(47\%\) meta-nitroaniline is formed due to in situ anilinium ion generation.
  • Friedel-Crafts Trap: Aniline does not undergo alkylation or acylation due to complexation with \(\mathrm{AlCl_3}\).
  • Azo Dye Coupling pH Trap: Coupling with phenol requires alkaline medium (\(\mathrm{pH} = 9 - 10\)) to produce an orange dye; coupling with aniline requires mildly acidic medium (\(\mathrm{pH} = 4 - 5\)) to produce a yellow dye.

বাংলাNEET পরীক্ষার জন্য সতর্কতামূলক রিভিশন চেকলিস্ট:

  • শ্রেণিবিভাগের বিভ্রান্তি: কার্বনের ডিগ্রি আর অ্যামিনের ডিগ্রি এক নয়। টার্ট-বিউটাইলঅ্যামিনে \(\left(\mathrm{CH_3}\right)_3\mathrm{C-NH_2}\) নাইট্রোজেনের সাথে মাত্র একটি কার্বন যুক্ত থাকায় এটি একটি প্রাইমারি (\(1^\circ\)) অ্যামিন।
  • গ্যাব্রিয়েল সংশ্লেষণের সীমাবদ্ধতা: এটি কেবল অ্যালিফ্যাটিক \(1^\circ\) অ্যামিন তৈরি করতে পারে; অ্যানিলিন বা \(2^\circ / 3^\circ\) অ্যামিন তৈরি করা যায় না।
  • হফম্যান ব্রোম্যামাইডে কার্বন সংখ্যা: উৎপাদে সর্বদা অ্যামাইডের চেয়ে ১টি কার্বন কম থাকে (কার্বনটি \(\mathrm{Na_2CO_3}\) হিসেবে অপসারিত হয়)।
  • জলীয় ক্ষারকত্ব ক্রম: মিথাইল জাতকের জন্য ২, ১, ৩ এবং ইথাইল জাতকের জন্য ২, ৩, ১। প্রশ্নে "গ্যাসীয় দশা" উল্লেখ না থাকলে সর্বদা জলীয় ক্রম প্রয়োগ করবে।
  • অ্যানিলিনের সরাসরি নাইট্রেশন: গাঢ় অ্যাসিডে অ্যানিলিনিয়াম আয়ন গঠিত হওয়ায় ৪৭% মেটা-নাইট্রোঅ্যানিলিন উৎপন্ন হয়।
  • ফ্রিডেল-ক্রাফটস বিক্রিয়া: \(\mathrm{AlCl_3}\) প্রভাবকের সাথে লবণ গঠনের কারণে অ্যানিলিন ফ্রিডেল-ক্রাফটস বিক্রিয়া দেয় না।

11. Final Recap & Exam Readiness Summary / চূড়ান্ত সারসংক্ষেপ ও পরীক্ষার প্রস্তুতি

EnglishAmines are pyramidal derivatives of ammonia with \(sp^3\) hybridised nitrogen carrying one lone pair (\(\mathrm{C-N-C}\) angle \(\approx 108^\circ\) in trimethylamine). Intermolecular hydrogen bonding dictates boiling points (\(1^\circ > 2^\circ > 3^\circ\)), with alcohols boiling higher due to stronger \(\mathrm{O-H\cdots O}\) bonds. Basicity in aqueous solution reflects an intricate interplay of inductive (\(+I\)), hydration, and steric effects (\(\mathrm{Et_2NH > Et_3N > EtNH_2 > NH_3}\) vs. \(\mathrm{Me_2NH > MeNH_2 > Me_3N > NH_3}\)). Aniline is weakly basic due to lone pair delocalisation over 5 resonance structures. Distinctive identification tests include Carbylamine (\(1^\circ\) only, foul isocyanide) and Hinsberg's reagent (\(1^\circ\) soluble in alkali, \(2^\circ\) insoluble in alkali, \(3^\circ\) no reaction). Benzenediazonium chloride, prepared via diazotisation at \(273 - 278\,\mathrm{K}\), serves as a versatile synthetic portal for introducing \(\mathrm{-Cl, -Br, -I, -F, -CN, -OH, -NO_2}\), or \(\mathrm{-H}\) via Sandmeyer, Gattermann, Balz-Schiemann, and reductive deamination, and produces azo dyes via electrophilic coupling.

বাংলাঅ্যামিন হলো অ্যামোনিয়ার পিরামিডীয় জাতক যেখানে নাইট্রোজেন \(sp^3\) সংকরায়িত এবং একটি নিঃসঙ্গ জোড় বহন করে (বন্ধন কোণ প্রায় \(108^\circ\))। আন্তঃআণবিক হাইড্রোজেন বন্ধনের তীব্রতার ক্রম: \(1^\circ > 2^\circ > 3^\circ\)। জলীয় দ্রবণে ক্ষারকত্বের ক্রম আবেশীয়, হাইড্রেশন ও স্টেরিক বাধার ভারসাম্য দ্বারা নিয়ন্ত্রিত হয় (ইথাইলের ক্ষেত্রে \(2^\circ > 3^\circ > 1^\circ > \mathrm{NH_3}\) এবং মিথাইলের ক্ষেত্রে \(2^\circ > 1^\circ > 3^\circ > \mathrm{NH_3}\))। অ্যানিলিনে রেজোন্যান্সের কারণে নিঃসঙ্গ জোড় বলয়ে বিস্তৃত থাকায় ক্ষারকত্ব খুব কম। কার্বিলঅ্যামিন পরীক্ষা কেবল \(1^\circ\) অ্যামিন দেয় এবং হিন্সবার্গ পরীক্ষায় \(1^\circ\) অ্যামিন ক্ষারে দ্রবণীয় ও \(2^\circ\) ক্ষারে অদ্রবণীয় সালফোনামাইড গঠন করে। ডায়াজোটাইজেশন দ্বারা প্রস্তুত বেনজিনডায়াজোনিয়াম ক্লোরাইড বিভিন্ন নিউক্লিওফিলিক প্রতিস্থাপনের মাধ্যমে অ্যারোমেটিক রূপান্তরের প্রধান চাবিকাঠি এবং অ্যাজো রঞ্জক তৈরিতে ব্যবহৃত হয়।
