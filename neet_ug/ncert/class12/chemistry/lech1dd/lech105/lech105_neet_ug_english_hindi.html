1. Learning Objectives & Exam Overview / अधिगम उद्देश्य एवं परीक्षा अवलोकन

English Core Overview

Coordination compounds form the bedrock of inorganic chemistry, transition metal chemistry, and bio-inorganic mechanisms. In the NEET UG medical entrance examination, this chapter regularly contributes 2 to 3 direct questions (8 to 12 marks) spanning Werner's formulation, IUPAC naming, geometrical/optical isomerism, spin-only magnetic moments (\(\mu_s = \sqrt{n(n+2)}\,\mathrm{BM}\)), and crystal field splitting energy (\(\Delta_\mathrm{o}\) and \(\Delta_\mathrm{t}\)).

NEET UG Core Competencies
  • Apply Werner's postulates to deduce primary (ionisable) vs. secondary (non-ionisable) valencies from precipitation stoichiometry with \(\mathrm{AgNO_3}\).
  • Accurately formulate and systematically name mononuclear neutral, cationic, and anionic coordination complexes according to latest IUPAC conventions.
  • Classify ligands by denticity and ambidentate nature, identifying structural isomers (ionisation, linkage, coordination, solvate) and stereoisomers (cis/trans, fac/mer, and chiral enantiomers).
  • Predict orbital hybridisation, coordination geometry, and paramagnetism/diamagnetism using Valence Bond Theory (VBT).
  • Apply Crystal Field Theory (CFT) to evaluate \(d\)-orbital splitting in octahedral and tetrahedral fields, calculate CFSE, explain electronic transitions (colours), and predict high-spin vs. low-spin electronic configurations.
  • Understand synergic bonding (\(\sigma\)-donation and \(\pi\)-backdonation) in metal carbonyls and their biological/analytical applications (haemoglobin, chlorophyll, cis-platin, EDTA).

2. Werner's Theory of Coordination Compounds / वर्नर का उपसहसंयोजन सिद्धांत

English Core Theory

Alfred Werner (1893) formulated the first systematic theoretical basis for the structures and properties of coordination compounds by synthesizing and chemically characterizing cobalt(III) chloride ammines. His core postulates are:

Postulates of Werner's Theory
  1. Dual Valency: Metals in coordination complexes exhibit two distinct types of valency:
    • Primary Valency (Principal Valency): Ionisable, non-directional, corresponds strictly to the oxidation state of the central metal ion, and is satisfied only by negative ions (anions).
    • Secondary Valency (Auxiliary Valency): Non-ionisable, directional in three-dimensional space, corresponds strictly to the coordination number of the metal ion, and is satisfied by neutral molecules or negative ions.
  2. Fixed Secondary Valency: Every central metal ion has a fixed secondary valency (coordination number) in a particular oxidation state (e.g., \(\mathrm{Co^{3+}}\) and \(\mathrm{Pt^{4+}}\) have a secondary valency of 6; \(\mathrm{Pt^{2+}}\) and \(\mathrm{Pd^{2+}}\) have 4).
  3. Spatial Directionality: Secondary valencies are directed toward definite positions in three-dimensional space, determining the characteristic geometric shape (coordination polyhedron) of the complex (e.g., octahedral, tetrahedral, or square planar).

Experimental Validation: Cobalt(III) Chloride Ammines

Double Salt vs. Coordination Complex (NEET High-Yield Trap)
  • Double Salt (द्विक लवण): Formed by the crystallisation of two stable salts in equimolar proportions (e.g., Mohr's salt: \(\mathrm{FeSO_4 \cdot (NH_4)_2SO_4 \cdot 6H_2O}\); Potash Alum: \(\mathrm{KAl(SO_4)_2 \cdot 12H_2O}\); Carnallite: \(\mathrm{KCl \cdot MgCl_2 \cdot 6H_2O}\)). They completely dissociate into individual simple ions in aqueous solution and give positive confirmatory tests for all constituent ions (e.g., \(\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}\), \(\mathrm{NH_4^+}\), \(\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}\)).
  • Coordination Complex (उपसहसंयोजन संकुल): Retains its structural identity in the solid state as well as in aqueous solution (e.g., Potassium ferrocyanide: \(\mathrm{K_4[Fe(CN)_6]}\)). It ionises into \(4\mathrm{K^+}\) and \(\mathrm{[Fe(CN)_6]^{4-}}\), but does not dissociate into free \(\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}\) and \(\mathrm{CN^-}\) ions, failing tests for individual iron or cyanide ions.

वर्नर के सिद्धांत की प्रमुख अभिधारणाएँ:

  1. दोहरी संयोजकता: उपसहसंयोजन यौगिकों में धातु दो प्रकार की संयोजकता दर्शाते हैं:
    • प्राथमिक संयोजकता (Primary Valency): आयननीय (ionisable), अदिशिक (non-directional) होती है तथा धातु की ऑक्सीकरण अवस्था के बराबर होती है। यह केवल ऋणायनों द्वारा संतुष्ट होती है।
    • द्वितीयक संयोजकता (Secondary Valency): अनायननीय (non-ionisable), दैशिक (directional) होती है तथा धातु की उपसहसंयोजन संख्या (Coordination Number) के बराबर होती है। यह उदासीन अणुओं या ऋणायनों द्वारा संतुष्ट होती है।
  2. प्रत्येक धातु आयन की द्वितीयक संयोजकता निश्चित होती है (जैसे \(\mathrm{Co^{3+}}\) की 6 होती है)।
  3. द्वितीयक संयोजकताएँ त्रिविम में निश्चित दिशाओं में दिष्ट होती हैं, जिससे संकुल की एक विशिष्ट ज्यामिति (अष्टफलकीय, चतुष्फलकीय अथवा वर्ग समतलीय) निर्धारित होती है।

द्विक लवण एवं उपसहसंयोजन संकुल में अंतर: कार्नेलाइट (\(\mathrm{KCl \cdot MgCl_2 \cdot 6H_2O}\)) और मोहर लवण (\(\mathrm{FeSO_4 \cdot (NH_4)_2SO_4 \cdot 6H_2O}\)) जल में पूर्णतः अपने संघटक आयनों में वियोजित हो जाते हैं। इसके विपरीत, \(\mathrm{K_4[Fe(CN)_6]}\) जल में \(\mathrm{K^+}\) तथा \(\mathrm{[Fe(CN)_6]^{4-}}\) देता है, परन्तु यह स्वतंत्र \(\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}\) या \(\mathrm{CN^-}\) आयन नहीं देता।

3. Fundamental Terminology & Coordination Polyhedra / मूलभूत शब्दावली एवं उपसहसंयोजन बहुफलक

English Core Terminology
Core Definitions

1. Coordination Entity: A central metal atom or ion bonded to a fixed number of ions or neutral molecules. For example, in \(\mathrm{[CoCl_3(NH_3)_3]}\), the entire bracketed unit is the coordination entity.

2. Central Atom / Ion: The transition metal atom or ion to which a fixed number of ligands are bound in a definite spatial arrangement. Because it accepts electron pairs into its vacant orbitals, it acts as a Lewis acid.

3. Ligands: Ions or neutral molecules bound to the central metal atom/ion via coordinate covalent (dative) bonds, donating electron pairs. Ligands act as Lewis bases.

4. Coordination Number (CN): The total number of ligand donor atoms directly attached to the central metal ion by \(\sigma\)-bonds. Crucial Note: \(\pi\)-bonds between ligand and metal are not counted towards the coordination number.

5. Coordination Sphere: The central metal atom and the directly bonded ligands enclosed inside square brackets \([\;]\). The ionisable species outside the square brackets are called counter ions.

6. Coordination Polyhedron: The spatial arrangement of the ligand donor atoms directly attached to the central atom. Common polyhedra are octahedral, tetrahedral, square planar, trigonal bipyramidal, and square pyramidal.

7. Oxidation State: The residual charge the central atom would carry if all the ligands were removed along with the electron pairs shared with the central atom.

8. Homoleptic vs. Heteroleptic Complexes:
• Homoleptic: Metal is bound to only one kind of donor group (e.g., \(\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_6]^{3+}}\), \(\mathrm{[Fe(CN)_6]^{4-}}\)).
• Heteroleptic: Metal is bound to more than one kind of donor group (e.g., \(\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_4Cl_2]^+}\), \(\mathrm{[PtCl_2(NH_3)_2]}\)).

Detailed Classification of Ligands by Denticity

  • Unidentate (Monodentate): Binds through a single donor atom. Examples: \(\mathrm{H_2O}\) (aqua), \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) (ammine), \(\mathrm{Cl^-}\) (chlorido), \(\mathrm{OH^-}\) (hydroxido), \(\mathrm{CN^-}\) (cyanido), \(\mathrm{NO_2^-}\) (nitrito-N), \(\mathrm{CO}\) (carbonyl).
  • Didentate (Bidentate): Binds through two donor atoms simultaneously.
    • Neutral: Ethane-1,2-diamine (\(\mathrm{H_2N-CH_2-CH_2-NH_2}\), abbreviated as 'en').
    • Anionic: Oxalate ion (\(\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}}\) or \(\mathrm{ox}\)), Glycinate ion (\(\mathrm{NH_2-CH_2-COO^-}\) or \(\mathrm{gly}\)).
  • Polydentate (Hexadentate): Binds through multiple donor atoms.
    • Ethylenediaminetetraacetate ion (\(\mathrm{EDTA^{4-}}\)): Possesses two nitrogen donor atoms and four oxygen donor atoms (hexadentate).
  • Chelate Ligand and Chelate Effect: When a di- or polydentate ligand binds to a single metal ion forming a 5- or 6-membered cyclic ring, the ligand is termed a chelating ligand. Complexes containing chelate rings are significantly more thermodynamically stable than analogous complexes with unidentate ligands. This thermodynamic enhancement is known as the chelate effect (primarily driven by a favourable increase in entropy, \(\Delta S > 0\)).
  • Ambidentate Ligand: A unidentate ligand containing two different donor atoms, either of which can coordinate to the metal, producing linkage isomerism:
    • Nitrito ligand: coordinated via nitrogen (\(\mathrm{-NO_2}\), nitrito-N) or via oxygen (\(\mathrm{-ONO}\), nitrito-O).
    • Thiocyanate ligand: coordinated via sulfur (\(\mathrm{-SCN}\), thiocyanato-S) or via nitrogen (\(\mathrm{-NCS}\), thiocyanato-N / isothiocyanato).

उपसहसंयोजन सत्ता एवं लिगैंड: केंद्रीय धातु परमाणु/आयन इलेक्ट्रॉन युग्म ग्रहण करता है, अतः यह लुईस अम्ल है। लिगैंड इलेक्ट्रॉन युग्म प्रदान करते हैं, अतः ये लुईस क्षारक कहलाते हैं।

दंतुरता (Denticity) के आधार पर लिगैंड:
1. एकदंतुर (Unidentate): केवल एक दाता परमाणु से जुड़ते हैं (उदा. \(\mathrm{H_2O}\), \(\mathrm{NH_3}\), \(\mathrm{Cl^-}\), \(\mathrm{CN^-}\))।
2. द्विदंतुर (Didentate): दो दाता परमाणुओं से जुड़ते हैं (उदा. एथेन-1,2-डाइऐमीन 'en', ऑक्सैलेटो \(\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}}\))।
3. षट्दंतुर (Hexadentate): \(\mathrm{EDTA^{4-}}\) (दो N तथा चार O दाता परमाणु)।
4. कीलेट लिगैंड एवं कीलेट प्रभाव: जब कोई द्वि- या बहुदंतुर लिगैंड धातु आयन के साथ 5- या 6-सदस्यीय वलय बनाता है, तो संकुल का स्थायित्व अत्यधिक बढ़ जाता है। इसे कीलेट प्रभाव कहते हैं।
5. उभयदंती लिगैंड (Ambidentate Ligand): वे एकदंतुर लिगैंड जो दो भिन्न परमाणुओं द्वारा संकलित हो सकते हैं (जैसे \(\mathrm{-NO_2}\) में N या O; \(\mathrm{-SCN}\) में S या N)।

4. IUPAC Rules for Formulas and Systematic Nomenclature / नामकरण एवं सूत्र लेखन के IUPAC नियम

English IUPAC Framework
Rules for Writing Formulas of Mononuclear Coordination Entities
  1. The central metal atom is placed first within the square brackets.
  2. Ligands are listed in alphabetical order according to their chemical symbols (or abbreviations), irrespective of their electrical charge.
  3. Polydentate ligands and abbreviations (e.g., en, ox, py) are enclosed in parentheses \(( \ldots )\).
  4. There is no space between the ligands and the metal atom within the coordination sphere.
  5. The entire coordination entity is enclosed in square brackets \([\;]\). Counter ions are written outside the brackets. The charge on the coordination sphere is balanced by the counter ions.
Rules for Naming Coordination Compounds
  1. Order of Naming: The cation is named first, whether it is a simple ion or complex ion, followed by the anion.
  2. Naming the Coordination Entity:
    • Ligands are named first in alphabetical order, followed by the name of the central metal atom/ion.
    • Names of anionic ligands end in -o (e.g., \(\mathrm{Cl^-}\) = chlorido, \(\mathrm{F^-}\) = fluorido, \(\mathrm{CN^-}\) = cyanido, \(\mathrm{OH^-}\) = hydroxido, \(\mathrm{NO_2^-}\) = nitrito-N, \(\mathrm{ONO^-}\) = nitrito-O, \(\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}\) = sulphato, \(\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}}\) = oxalato).
    • Names of neutral ligands are unchanged, with specific exceptions: \(\mathrm{H_2O}\) = aqua, \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) = ammine (note the double 'm'), \(\mathrm{CO}\) = carbonyl, \(\mathrm{NO}\) = nitrosyl.
  3. Numerical Prefixes: Prefixes di-, tri-, tetra-, penta-, hexa- designate the number of individual ligands. If the ligand name already contains a numerical prefix (e.g., ethane-1,2-diamine), the prefixes bis-, tris-, tetrakis- are used, and the ligand name is placed in parentheses.
  4. Oxidation State: The oxidation state of the central metal is indicated by a Roman numeral in parentheses immediately following the metal's name (without space).
  5. Ending of the Metal Name:
    • In a cationic or neutral complex, the metal is given its normal English name (e.g., cobalt, platinum, chromium, iron, copper).
    • In an anionic complex entity, the metal name ends with the suffix -ate, often using its Latin stem:
      Iron (\(\mathrm{Fe}\)) → ferrate; Copper (\(\mathrm{Cu}\)) → cuprate; Silver (\(\mathrm{Ag}\)) → argentate; Gold (\(\mathrm{Au}\)) → aurate; Lead (\(\mathrm{Pb}\)) → plumbate; Tin (\(\mathrm{Sn}\)) → stannate; Cobalt (\(\mathrm{Co}\)) → cobaltate; Platinum (\(\mathrm{Pt}\)) → platinate.

IUPAC नामकरण के प्रमुख नियम:

  • धनायन का नाम पहले तथा ऋणायन का नाम बाद में लिखा जाता है।
  • संकुल सत्ता में लिगैंडों के नाम वर्णमाला क्रम (alphabetical order) में लिखे जाते हैं।
  • ऋणायनी लिगैंडों के अंत में -ओ (-o) अनुलग्न आता है (जैसे क्लोरिडो, सायनिडो, ऑक्सैलेटो)। उदासीन लिगैंडों के सामान्य नाम रहते हैं, सिवाय: \(\mathrm{H_2O}\) = ऐक्वा, \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) = ऐम्मीन, \(\mathrm{CO}\) = कार्बोनिल।
  • यदि संकुल सत्ता ऋणायनी है, तो केंद्रीय धातु के नाम के अंत में -एट (-ate) जोड़ा जाता है (जैसे \(\mathrm{Fe}\) → फेरेट, \(\mathrm{Ag}\) → अर्जेन्टेट, \(\mathrm{Al}\) → ऐल्युमिनेट)।
  • धातु की ऑक्सीकरण अवस्था को नाम के तुरंत बाद कोष्ठक में रोमन अंकों में दर्शाया जाता है।

5. Isomerism in Coordination Compounds / उपसहसंयोजन यौगिकों में समावयवता

English Core Theory

Isomers are two or more compounds that possess identical chemical formulas but differ in their structural arrangements or spatial orientations. Coordination compounds exhibit two primary classes of isomerism:

A. Structural Isomerism (संरचनात्मक समावयवता)

  • 1. Linkage Isomerism: Arises in complexes containing ambidentate ligands that can coordinate via different donor atoms.
    Example: \(\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_5(NO_2)]Cl_2}\) (yellow-red, Co-N bond, nitrito-N) and \(\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_5(ONO)]Cl_2}\) (red, Co-O bond, nitrito-O).
  • 2. Coordination Isomerism: Occurs when both the cation and anion are complex entities, arising from the interchange of ligands between different metal centers.
    Example: \(\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_6][Cr(CN)_6]}\) and \(\mathrm{[Cr(NH_3)_6][Co(CN)_6]}\).
  • 3. Ionisation Isomerism: Arises when a counter ion displaces a ligand from the coordination sphere, producing different ions in aqueous solution.
    Example: \(\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_5(SO_4)]Br}\) (red, gives precipitate with \(\mathrm{AgNO_3}\)) and \(\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_5Br]SO_4}\) (red-violet, gives precipitate with \(\mathrm{BaCl_2}\)).
  • 4. Solvate (Hydrate) Isomerism: Differs by whether a solvent molecule (typically \(\mathrm{H_2O}\)) is directly bonded as a ligand inside the coordination sphere or present as free water of crystallisation in the crystal lattice.
    Example: \(\mathrm{[Cr(H_2O)_6]Cl_3}\) (violet, 3 ionisable \(\mathrm{Cl^-}\)), \(\mathrm{[CrCl(H_2O)_5]Cl_2 \cdot H_2O}\) (grey-green, 2 ionisable \(\mathrm{Cl^-}\)), and \(\mathrm{[CrCl_2(H_2O)_4]Cl \cdot 2H_2O}\) (dark green, 1 ionisable \(\mathrm{Cl^-}\)).

B. Stereoisomerism (त्रिविम समावयवता)

Stereoisomers have the same chemical bonds but differ in the spatial arrangement of ligands.

1. Geometrical Isomerism (ज्यामितीय समावयवता)

  • Square Planar Complexes (\(\mathrm{[MA_2B_2]}\)): Adjacent positions (\(90^\circ\)) give the cis-isomer; opposite positions (\(180^\circ\)) give the trans-isomer.
    Example: \(\mathrm{[PtCl_2(NH_3)_2]}\) exists as cis-platin (active antitumor agent) and trans-platin (biologically inactive).
    Note: Square planar complexes of type \(\mathrm{[MABCD]}\) exhibit 3 geometrical isomers (two cis and one trans).
    NEET Rule: Tetrahedral complexes (\(\mathrm{[MA_4]}\), \(\mathrm{[MA_2B_2]}\)) never exhibit geometrical isomerism because all four positions are adjacent and chemically equivalent (\(109.5^\circ\)).
  • Octahedral Complexes (\(\mathrm{[MA_4B_2]}\)):
    Example: \(\mathrm{[CoCl_2(NH_3)_4]^+}\) exists as violet cis (chlorides at \(90^\circ\)) and green trans (chlorides at \(180^\circ\)).
  • Facial (fac) and Meridional (mer) Isomerism (\(\mathrm{[MA_3B_3]}\)):
    • Facial (fac): Three identical donor atoms occupy the corners of a single triangular face of the octahedron.
    • Meridional (mer): Three identical donor atoms occupy positions around the meridian (equatorial perimeter) of the octahedron.
    Example: \(\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_3(NO_2)_3]}\).

2. Optical Isomerism (प्रकाशिक समावयवता)

Optical isomers (enantiomers) are non-superimposable mirror images of each other that rotate plane-polarized light in opposite directions (dextrorotatory, \(d\) or \(+\), and laevorotatory, \(l\) or \(-\)). A molecule must lack an improper rotation axis (most commonly a center of inversion \(i\) or plane of symmetry \(\sigma\)) to exhibit chirality.

High-Yield NEET Rules for Optical Activity
  • Type \(\mathrm{[M(AA)_3]^{n\pm}}\) (e.g., \(\mathrm{[Co(en)_3]^{3+}}\), \(\mathrm{[Fe(ox)_3]^{3-}}\)): Always chiral, lacks symmetry planes, exists as a pair of optically active \(d\)- and \(l\)-enantiomers.
  • Type \(\mathrm{[M(AA)_2B_2]^{n\pm}}\) (e.g., \(\mathrm{[CoCl_2(en)_2]^+}\)):
    • cis-isomer: Lacks a plane of symmetry, is chiral, and exists as optically active enantiomers.
    • trans-isomer: Possesses an inversion center and planes of symmetry; optically inactive (achiral).

उपसहसंयोजन यौगिकों में समावयवता के प्रकार:

  1. बंधन समावयवता (Linkage): उभयदंती लिगैंडों (जैसे \(\mathrm{-NO_2}\) व \(\mathrm{-ONO}\)) के भिन्न दाता परमाणुओं द्वारा जुड़ने पर।
  2. उपसहसंयोजन समावयवता (Coordination): जब धनायन और ऋणायन दोनों संकुल हों तथा धातुओं के मध्य लिगैंडों का विनिमय हो (उदा. \(\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_6][Cr(CN)_6]}\))।
  3. आयनीकरण समावयवता (Ionisation): उपसहसंयोजन मंडल के अंदर व बाहर के आयनों के परस्पर विनिमय से (उदा. \(\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_5(SO_4)]Br}\) तथा \(\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_5Br]SO_4}\))।
  4. विलायकयोजन समावयवता (Solvate/Hydrate): विलायक (जल) अणु के लिगैंड अथवा क्रिस्टलन-जल के रूप में भिन्न स्थिति में होने से।
  5. ज्यामितीय समावयवता (Geometrical): cis/trans तथा fac/mer। चतुष्फलकीय संकुल सममिति के कारण ज्यामितीय समावयवता प्रदर्शित नहीं करते।
  6. प्रकाशिक समावयवता (Optical): वे त्रिविम समावयवी जो एक-दूसरे के अन-अध्यारोपणीय (non-superimposable) दर्पण प्रतिबिंब होते हैं। सममिति तल की अनुपस्थिति अनिवार्य है (जैसे \(\mathrm{[Co(en)_3]^{3+}}\) तथा cis-\(\mathrm{[CoCl_2(en)_2]^+}\))। trans-\(\mathrm{[CoCl_2(en)_2]^+}\) सममित होने के कारण प्रकाशिक अक्रिय होता है।

6. Bonding in Coordination Compounds: VBT and CFT / उपसहसंयोजन यौगिकों में बंधन

English Core Theory: Valence Bond Theory

Valence Bond Theory (VBT), developed by Linus Pauling, posits that the central metal ion provides an appropriate number of vacant orbitals matching its coordination number. These hybridise to form a set of equivalent hybrid orbitals that overlap with ligand lone-pair orbitals to form coordinate covalent \(\sigma\)-bonds.

Magnetic Moment Formula (Spin-Only)

The spin-only magnetic moment (\(\mu_s\)) is determined by the number of unpaired electrons (\(n\)):

\[ \mu_s = \sqrt{n(n+2)} \quad \mathrm{Bohr\;Magnetons\;(BM)} \]

Typical values: \(n=1 \implies \mu_s = 1.73\,\mathrm{BM}\); \(n=2 \implies 2.83\,\mathrm{BM}\); \(n=3 \implies 3.87\,\mathrm{BM}\); \(n=4 \implies 4.90\,\mathrm{BM}\); \(n=5 \implies 5.92\,\mathrm{BM}\).

Classic VBT Case Studies (High-Yield NEET Comparison)

  • \(\mathrm{[NiCl_4]^{2-}}\) vs. \(\mathrm{[Ni(CN)_4]^{2-}}\):
    • In \(\mathrm{[NiCl_4]^{2-}}\), \(\mathrm{Ni^{2+}}\) is \(3d^8\). Chloride (\(\mathrm{Cl^-}\)) is a weak field ligand and cannot pair the two unpaired electrons in \(3d\). Thus, one \(4s\) and three \(4p\) orbitals hybridise to form \(sp^3\) (tetrahedral). It has 2 unpaired electrons, so it is paramagnetic (\(\mu_s = 2.83\,\mathrm{BM}\)).
    • In \(\mathrm{[Ni(CN)_4]^{2-}}\), cyanide (\(\mathrm{CN^-}\)) is a strong field ligand, forcing the two \(3d\) unpaired electrons to pair up. This vacates one \(3d\) orbital, leading to \(dsp^2\) (square planar) hybridisation. It has 0 unpaired electrons, making it diamagnetic (\(\mu_s = 0\,\mathrm{BM}\)).
  • \(\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_6]^{3+}}\) vs. \(\mathrm{[CoF_6]^{3-}}\):
    • In \(\mathrm{[Co(NH_3)_6]^{3+}}\), \(\mathrm{Co^{3+}}\) is \(3d^6\). Ammonia causes pairing of all six \(3d\) electrons into three \(d\)-orbitals (\(t_{2g}^6\)), leaving two vacant \(3d\) orbitals for \(d^2sp^3\) hybridisation. It is an inner orbital, low-spin, diamagnetic complex.
    • In \(\mathrm{[CoF_6]^{3-}}\), fluoride (\(\mathrm{F^-}\)) is a weak field ligand and cannot force pairing. The complex utilises the outer \(4d\) orbitals, giving \(sp^3d^2\) hybridisation. It has 4 unpaired electrons and is an outer orbital, high-spin, strongly paramagnetic complex (\(\mu_s = 4.90\,\mathrm{BM}\)).
Critical Limitations of Valence Bond Theory
  • VBT provides no quantitative explanation for the electronic absorption spectra (colours) of coordination compounds.
  • It cannot distinguish between weak field and strong field ligands on a fundamental physical basis (it cannot explain why \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) forces pairing in \(\mathrm{Co^{3+}}\) but not in \(\mathrm{Ni^{2+}}\)).
  • It makes no exact quantitative thermodynamic or kinetic stability predictions.
  • It fails to distinguish reliably between 4-coordinate tetrahedral and square planar structures without prior magnetic data.

Crystal Field Theory (CFT) / क्रिस्टल क्षेत्र सिद्धांत

Crystal Field Theory treats the metal-ligand bond as purely ionic, resulting from electrostatic interactions between the central metal cation and the surrounding ligands (modelled as point negative charges or dipoles). In an isolated gaseous metal atom or ion, all five \(d\)-orbitals (\(d_{xy}, d_{yz}, d_{zx}, d_{x^2-y^2}, d_{z^2}\)) are degenerate (have equal energy).

1. Octahedral Complexes (\(O_h\))

In an octahedral field, six ligands approach along the Cartesian axes (\(\pm x, \pm y, \pm z\)). Orbitals pointing directly along the axes (\(d_{x^2-y^2}\) and \(d_{z^2}\), termed the \(e_g\) set) experience stronger electrostatic repulsion and are raised in energy by \(+\frac{3}{5}\Delta_\mathrm{o}\) (\(+0.6\Delta_\mathrm{o}\)). Orbitals pointing between the axes (\(d_{xy}, d_{yz}, d_{zx}\), termed the \(t_{2g}\) set) experience less repulsion and are stabilized by \(-\frac{2}{5}\Delta_\mathrm{o}\) (\(-0.4\Delta_\mathrm{o}\)).

Figure 3: Crystal Field Splitting of d-orbitals in an Octahedral Field (\(O_h\))
The Spectrochemical Series (NEET High-Yield Memory Sequence)

Ligands are arranged in order of increasing crystal field splitting energy (\(\Delta_\mathrm{o}\)):

\[ \mathrm{I^- < Br^- < S^{2-} < SCN^- < Cl^- < N_3^- < F^- < OH^- < C_2O_4^{2-} \approx H_2O < NCS^- < edta^{4-} < NH_3 < en < NO_2^- < CN^- < CO} \]

Rule of Thumb: Halides and Oxygen-donors are generally weak field ligands (\(\Delta_\mathrm{o} < P\)); Nitrogen- and Carbon-donors (\(\mathrm{en, NO_2^-, CN^-, CO}\)) are strong field ligands (\(\Delta_\mathrm{o} > P\)).

Electronic Configurations in Octahedral Complexes (\(d^4\) to \(d^7\))

For \(d^1, d^2, d^3\) systems, electrons occupy the lower \(t_{2g}\) orbitals singly according to Hund's rule (\(t_{2g}^1, t_{2g}^2, t_{2g}^3\)), irrespective of ligand field strength. For \(d^4\) to \(d^7\), competition arises between \(\Delta_\mathrm{o}\) and the electron pairing energy (\(P\)):

  • Weak Field, High-Spin Case (\(\Delta_\mathrm{o} < P\)): Pairing energy is larger than splitting energy. The 4th electron enters the higher energy \(e_g\) orbital:
    • \(d^4: t_{2g}^3 e_g^1\) (\(n=4, \mu_s = 4.90\,\mathrm{BM}\))
    • \(d^5: t_{2g}^3 e_g^2\) (\(n=5, \mu_s = 5.92\,\mathrm{BM}\))
    • \(d^6: t_{2g}^4 e_g^2\) (\(n=4, \mu_s = 4.90\,\mathrm{BM}\))
    • \(d^7: t_{2g}^5 e_g^2\) (\(n=3, \mu_s = 3.87\,\mathrm{BM}\))
  • Strong Field, Low-Spin Case (\(\Delta_\mathrm{o} > P\)): Splitting energy is larger than pairing energy. Electrons pair up in the lower \(t_{2g}\) orbitals before populating \(e_g\):
    • \(d^4: t_{2g}^4 e_g^0\) (\(n=2, \mu_s = 2.83\,\mathrm{BM}\))
    • \(d^5: t_{2g}^5 e_g^0\) (\(n=1, \mu_s = 1.73\,\mathrm{BM}\))
    • \(d^6: t_{2g}^6 e_g^0\) (\(n=0, \text{diamagnetic}\))
    • \(d^7: t_{2g}^6 e_g^1\) (\(n=1, \mu_s = 1.73\,\mathrm{BM}\))

2. Tetrahedral Complexes (\(T_d\))

In a tetrahedral field, four ligands approach from the alternate corners of a cube. No ligand points directly at any metal \(d\)-orbital, but the orbitals pointing between Cartesian axes (\(d_{xy}, d_{yz}, d_{zx}\), designated the \(t_2\) set) are closer to the incoming ligand directions than the axial orbitals (\(d_{x^2-y^2}, d_{z^2}\), designated the \(e\) set).

  • The splitting pattern is inverted relative to octahedral: \(e\) orbitals are stabilized by \(-\frac{3}{5}\Delta_\mathrm{t}\), and \(t_2\) orbitals are destabilized by \(+\frac{2}{5}\Delta_\mathrm{t}\). Note: The subscript 'g' is omitted because tetrahedral complexes lack an inversion centre.
  • The magnitude of tetrahedral splitting is substantially smaller: \[ \Delta_\mathrm{t} = \frac{4}{9}\Delta_\mathrm{o} \]
  • Because \(\Delta_\mathrm{t}\) is always smaller than the pairing energy (\(P\)), tetrahedral complexes are virtually always high-spin. Pairing of electrons never occurs before all five orbitals are half-filled.

Colour in Coordination Compounds (d-d Transitions)

The characteristic colours of coordination compounds originate from electronic transitions between split \(d\)-orbitals (\(d\text{–}d\) transitions). When white light passes through a complex solution, the complex absorbs photons of energy corresponding to the crystal field splitting (\(h\nu = \Delta_\mathrm{o}\)). The colour observed by the human eye is the complementary colour to the absorbed wavelength.

Classic NCERT / NEET Colour Case Studies
  • \(\mathrm{[Ti(H_2O)_6]^{3+}}\): Titanium is \(3d^1\). The single electron occupies the \(t_{2g}\) level (\(t_{2g}^1 e_g^0\)). It absorbs yellow-green light (\(\lambda \approx 498\,\mathrm{nm}\)) and is promoted to the \(e_g\) level (\(t_{2g}^0 e_g^1\)). It transmits purple/violet light. On heating, the coordinated water molecules are driven off, the crystal field disappears, and anhydrous \(\mathrm{TiCl_3}\) becomes completely colourless.
  • Anhydrous \(\mathrm{CuSO_4}\) vs. Hydrated \(\mathrm{CuSO_4 \cdot 5H_2O}\): Anhydrous \(\mathrm{CuSO_4}\) is white (no ligand field to split \(d\)-orbitals), whereas hydrated \(\mathrm{[Cu(H_2O)_4]SO_4 \cdot H_2O}\) is intensely blue due to \(d\text{–}d\) absorption in the red-orange region.
  • Ruby and Emerald:
    • Ruby: \(\mathrm{Al_2O_3}\) containing trace \(\mathrm{Cr^{3+}}\) in octahedral sites. \(\mathrm{Cr^{3+}}\) \(d\text{–}d\) transitions absorb yellow-green light, transmitting deep red.
    • Emerald: Beryl (\(\mathrm{Be_3Al_2Si_6O_{18}}\)) containing \(\mathrm{Cr^{3+}}\). Due to different silicate/beryllate ligand fields, \(\Delta_\mathrm{o}\) shifts to absorb red and blue, transmitting green.
  • Absence of Colour: Complexes with \(d^0\) (e.g., \(\mathrm{Sc^{3+}}\), \(\mathrm{Ti^{4+}}\)) or completely filled \(d^{10}\) configurations (e.g., \(\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}\), \(\mathrm{Cd^{2+}}\)) cannot undergo \(d\text{–}d\) transitions and are colourless.

संयोजकता आबंध सिद्धांत (VBT) के मुख्य बिंदु: धातु आयन अपनी उपसहसंयोजन संख्या के बराबर रिक्त कक्षक प्रदान करता है जो संकरित होकर लिगैंडों के एकाकी युग्मों के साथ उपसहसंयोजक बंध बनाते हैं।

क्रिस्टल क्षेत्र सिद्धांत (CFT):

  • अष्टफलकीय क्षेत्र में 5 समभ्रंश (degenerate) \(d\)-कक्षक दो समूहों में विपाटित हो जाते हैं:
    • उच्च ऊर्जा वाले दो कक्षक: \(e_g\) (\(d_{x^2-y^2}, d_{z^2}\)), ऊर्जा वृद्धि: \(+0.6\Delta_\mathrm{o}\)
    • निम्न ऊर्जा वाले तीन कक्षक: \(t_{2g}\) (\(d_{xy}, d_{yz}, d_{zx}\)), ऊर्जा कमी: \(-0.4\Delta_\mathrm{o}\)
  • स्पेक्ट्रमी-रासायनिक श्रेणी (Spectrochemical Series): लिगैंडों को उनके क्रिस्टल क्षेत्र विपाटन ऊर्जा (\(\Delta_\mathrm{o}\)) के बढ़ते क्रम में व्यवस्थित करने पर प्राप्त श्रेणी। दुर्बल क्षेत्र लिगैंड (\(\Delta_\mathrm{o} < P\)) उच्च-चक्रण (high-spin) संकुल बनाते हैं; प्रबल क्षेत्र लिगैंड (\(\Delta_\mathrm{o} > P\)) निम्न-चक्रण (low-spin) संकुल बनाते हैं।
  • चतुष्फलकीय संकुल: विपाटन क्रम उल्टा होता है (\(e\) नीचे, \(t_2\) ऊपर) तथा \(\Delta_\mathrm{t} = \frac{4}{9}\Delta_\mathrm{o}\)। \(\Delta_\mathrm{t}\) का मान युग्मन ऊर्जा (\(P\)) से सदैव कम होने के कारण चतुष्फलकीय संकुल सदैव उच्च चक्रण (high spin) होते हैं।
  • रंग का कारण: \(d\text{–}d\) संक्रमण। जब संकुल दृश्य प्रकाश से एक निश्चित तरंगदैर्घ्य अवशोषित करता है, तो उसका पूरक रंग (complementary colour) दिखाई देता है। \(d^0\) तथा \(d^{10}\) विन्यास वाले संकुल रंगहीन होते हैं।

7. Bonding in Metal Carbonyls / धातु कार्बोनिलों में बंधन

English Core Theory

Homoleptic metal carbonyls (compounds containing only carbon monoxide ligands coordinated to transition metals in zero oxidation state) possess distinctive physical and chemical structures. Common examples include \(\mathrm{Ni(CO)_4}\) (tetrahedral), \(\mathrm{Fe(CO)_5}\) (trigonal bipyramidal), \(\mathrm{Cr(CO)_6}\) (octahedral), \(\mathrm{Mn_2(CO)_{10}}\) (two square pyramidal units joined by a Mn-Mn bond), and \(\mathrm{Co_2(CO)_8}\) (two bridging CO ligands and a Co-Co bond).

The Synergic Effect (Synergic Bonding Mechanism)

The metal-carbon bond in metal carbonyls possesses both \(\sigma\) and \(\pi\) character, giving rise to unique thermodynamic stability via a self-reinforcing cooperative mechanism termed synergic bonding:

The Two Components of Synergic Bonding
  1. \(\mathrm{M \leftarrow C}\) \(\sigma\)-Bond Formation: The carbon monoxide ligand donates its non-bonding lone pair of electrons from its filled \(\sigma\)-molecular orbital (carbon \(sp\) hybrid) into a suitable vacant \(d\)-orbital of the metal atom.
  2. \(\mathrm{M \rightarrow C}\) \(\pi\)-Backdonation: Concurrently, a filled non-bonding \(d\)-orbital of the metal overlaps sideways with the empty antibonding \(\pi^*\) (\(\pi\)-star) molecular orbitals of the carbon monoxide molecule, transferring excess negative electron density back to the ligand.
NEET High-Yield Inferences on Bond Strength and Bond Length
  • As electron density on the central metal increases (e.g., negative charge on the complex), \(\mathrm{M \rightarrow C}\) backbonding increases.
  • Greater \(\pi\)-backdonation strengthens the \(\mathrm{M-C}\) bond (increases bond order, shortens \(\mathrm{M-C}\) bond length).
  • Simultaneously, populating the \(\pi^*\) antibonding orbital of \(\mathrm{CO}\) weakens the \(\mathrm{C-O}\) bond (decreases \(\mathrm{C-O}\) bond order, lengthens \(\mathrm{C-O}\) bond length, and lowers \(\mathrm{C-O}\) stretching frequency in infrared spectroscopy).
  • Example Sequence: \(\mathrm{C-O}\) bond length follows the order: \(\mathrm{[V(CO)_6]^- > [Cr(CO)_6] > [Mn(CO)_6]^+}\).

धातु कार्बोनिलों में पश्च-बंधन एवं सहक्रियात्मक प्रभाव (Synergic Effect):

  • \(\mathrm{M \leftarrow C}\) \(\sigma\)-आबंध: कार्बन मोनोऑक्साइड का कार्बन परमाणु अपने भरे हुए कक्षक से इलेक्ट्रॉन युग्म धातु के रिक्त \(d\)-कक्षक को दान करता है।
  • \(\mathrm{M \rightarrow C}\) \(\pi\)-पश्च-दान (Back-donation): धातु के भरे हुए \(d\)-कक्षक से इलेक्ट्रॉन घनत्व \(\mathrm{CO}\) के रिक्त प्रति-आबंधन \(\pi^*\) आण्विक कक्षक में स्थानांतरित होता है।
  • यह सहक्रियात्मक आबंध \(\mathrm{M-C}\) आबंध को मजबूत तथा \(\mathrm{C-O}\) आबंध को दुर्बल बनाता है, जिससे \(\mathrm{C-O}\) आबंध लंबाई बढ़ जाती है तथा आबंध कोटि घट जाती है।

8. Stability & High-Yield Applications / संकुलों का स्थायित्व एवं प्रमुख अनुप्रयोग

English Core Applications

The stability of a complex in solution refers to the degree of association between metal and ligands at equilibrium. For the stepwise formation:

\[ \mathrm{M + 4L \rightleftharpoons ML_4} \quad \implies \quad \beta_4 = K_1 \cdot K_2 \cdot K_3 \cdot K_4 \]

Stability increases with higher positive charge density of the central metal ion (\(\text{Charge} / \text{Ionic radius}\)), higher basic strength of the ligand, and the presence of chelate rings.

High-Yield Medical, Biological, and Industrial Applications (NEET Focus)

  • 1. Biological Complexes:
    • Chlorophyll: Coordination compound of Magnesium (\(\mathrm{Mg^{2+}}\)), essential for plant photosynthesis.
    • Haemoglobin: Coordination compound of Iron (\(\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}\)) in a porphyrin ring, functions as the respiratory oxygen carrier in mammals.
    • Vitamin B₁₂ (Cyanocobalamin): Coordination compound of Cobalt (\(\mathrm{Co^{3+}}\)), essential for red blood cell maturation and pernicious anaemia prevention.
    • Carboxypeptidase-A & Carbonic Anhydrase: Metalloenzymes containing coordinated Zinc (\(\mathrm{Zn^{2+}}\)).
  • 2. Medicinal Chemistry:
    • cis-Platin (\(\mathrm{cis\text{-}[PtCl_2(NH_3)_2]}\)): Widely utilized chemotherapeutic drug that binds cellular DNA and effectively inhibits tumor growth.
    • EDTA Therapy: Calcium disodium EDTA (\(\mathrm{CaNa_2[EDTA]}\)) is administered in the treatment of systemic lead (\(\mathrm{Pb}\)) poisoning. Lead displaces calcium to form a much more stable, water-soluble, excretion-safe lead-EDTA chelate.
    • D-Penicillamine & Desferrioxamine: Chelate excess Copper (\(\mathrm{Cu}\)) in Wilson's disease and excess Iron (\(\mathrm{Fe}\)) in thalassaemia, respectively.
  • 3. Analytical & Industrial Metallurgy:
    • Gold & Silver Extraction (MacArthur-Forrest Cyanide Process): Crushed ores are leached with dilute \(\mathrm{NaCN}\) in the presence of atmospheric \(\mathrm{O_2}\) to form soluble dicyanido complexes: \[ 4\mathrm{Au} + 8\mathrm{CN^-} + 2\mathrm{H_2O} + \mathrm{O_2} \rightarrow 4\mathrm{[Au(CN)_2]^-} + 4\mathrm{OH^-} \] The noble metal is subsequently precipitated by displacement with zinc powder: \[ 2\mathrm{[Au(CN)_2]^-} + \mathrm{Zn} \rightarrow \mathrm{[Zn(CN)_4]^{2-}} + 2\mathrm{Au}\downarrow \]
    • Purification of Nickel (Mond's Process): Impure nickel is volatilized at \(330\text{–}350\,\mathrm{K}\) into volatile tetracarbonylnickel(0) and then thermally decomposed to pure metal at \(450\text{–}470\,\mathrm{K}\): \[ \mathrm{Ni} + 4\mathrm{CO} \xrightarrow{330\text{–}350\,\mathrm{K}} \mathrm{[Ni(CO)_4]} \xrightarrow{450\text{–}470\,\mathrm{K}} \mathrm{Ni} + 4\mathrm{CO} \]
    • Qualitative Analysis: Detection of \(\mathrm{Ni^{2+}}\) by dimethylglyoxime (DMG) in ammoniacal medium, forming an insoluble bright rosy-red chelate precipitate \(\mathrm{[Ni(dmg)_2]}\) stabilized by intramolecular hydrogen bonds.

Worked Example 1: Deducing Secondary Valence and Formulation from Precipitation Stoichiometry

Problem: When \(0.01\,\mathrm{mol}\) of a coordination complex with the empirical formula \(\mathrm{CrCl_3 \cdot 5H_2O}\) is treated with excess aqueous \(\mathrm{AgNO_3}\), \(2.87\,\mathrm{g}\) of white precipitate of \(\mathrm{AgCl}\) is obtained. Deduce: (i) the secondary valency of chromium, (ii) the modern structural formula of the complex, and (iii) the number of ions released per formula unit in water. (Molar mass of \(\mathrm{AgCl} = 143.5\,\mathrm{g/mol}\)).

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. Calculate moles of \(\mathrm{AgCl}\) precipitated: \[ \text{Moles of } \mathrm{AgCl} = \frac{\text{Mass}}{\text{Molar mass}} = \frac{2.87\,\mathrm{g}}{143.5\,\mathrm{g/mol}} = 0.02\,\mathrm{mol} \]
  2. Determine ionisable chloride ions per mole of complex: \[ \frac{\text{Moles of } \mathrm{AgCl}}{\text{Moles of complex}} = \frac{0.02\,\mathrm{mol}}{0.01\,\mathrm{mol}} = 2 \] Thus, 2 chloride ions reside outside the coordination sphere as ionisable counter ions.
  3. Deduce coordination sphere and formula: The remaining 1 chloride ion and all 5 water molecules are inside the coordination sphere. Modern formulation: \(\mathbf{[CrCl(H_2O)_5]Cl_2}\).
  4. Secondary valency & total ions: The ligands directly bonded to \(\mathrm{Cr}\) are five \(\mathrm{H_2O}\) and one \(\mathrm{Cl^-}\), giving a secondary valency (coordination number) of 6. Ionisation in water: \(\mathrm{[CrCl(H_2O)_5]Cl_2 \rightarrow [CrCl(H_2O)_5]^{2+} + 2Cl^-}\) (Total = 3 ions; 1:2 electrolyte).

Worked Example 2: Determining Oxidation State and Coordination Number

Problem: Find the coordination number and oxidation state of the central metal in \(\mathrm{K_3[Fe(C_2O_4)_3]}\) and \(\mathrm{[Co(en)_2(H_2O)Cl]SO_4}\).

Step-by-Step Solution:

  • For \(\mathrm{K_3[Fe(C_2O_4)_3]}\):
    • Ligand: Oxalate (\(\mathrm{C_2O_4^{2-}}\)) is a didentate ligand with a charge of \(-2\).
    • Three didentate ligands provide \(3 \times 2 = 6\) donor bonds. Thus, Coordination Number = 6.
    • Oxidation state: Let the oxidation state of Fe be \(x\). \[ 3(+1) + x + 3(-2) = 0 \implies 3 + x - 6 = 0 \implies x = +3 \] Thus, the oxidation state is +3. IUPAC: Potassium trioxalatoferrate(III).
  • For \(\mathrm{[Co(en)_2(H_2O)Cl]SO_4}\):
    • Ligands: Two didentate en (\(2 \times 2 = 4\) donors), one unidentate aqua (1 donor), and one unidentate chlorido (1 donor).
    • Total donor atoms = \(4 + 1 + 1 = 6\). Thus, Coordination Number = 6.
    • Oxidation state: en is 0, \(\mathrm{H_2O}\) is 0, \(\mathrm{Cl}\) is \(-1\), and \(\mathrm{SO_4}\) is \(-2\). \[ x + 2(0) + 1(0) + (-1) + (-2) = 0 \implies x - 3 = 0 \implies x = +3 \] Thus, the oxidation state is +3.

Worked Example 3: VBT Prediction of Geometry and Magnetic Moment

Problem: Using Valence Bond Theory, explain the electronic structure, hybridisation, geometry, and spin-only magnetic moment of \(\mathrm{[Mn(CN)_6]^{3-}}\) (Atomic number of \(\mathrm{Mn} = 25\)).

Step-by-Step Solution:

  1. Neutral \(\mathrm{Mn}\) ground state: \([\mathrm{Ar}]\,3d^5 4s^2\).
  2. Oxidation state in \(\mathrm{[Mn(CN)_6]^{3-}}\): \(x + 6(-1) = -3 \implies x = +3\).
    Electron configuration of \(\mathrm{Mn^{3+}}\): \([\mathrm{Ar}]\,3d^4\).
  3. Cyanide (\(\mathrm{CN^-}\)) is a strong field ligand. It causes pairing of two \(3d\) electrons, leaving 2 unpaired electrons and clearing two vacant \(3d\) orbitals: \[ 3d \text{ configuration after pairing}: (\upharpoonleft\downharpoonright)(\upharpoonleft)(\upharpoonleft)(\quad)(\quad) \]
  4. The two empty \(3d\), one \(4s\), and three \(4p\) orbitals hybridise to form six equivalent \(d^2sp^3\) hybrid orbitals directed towards the corners of an octahedron (inner orbital, low-spin complex).
  5. Number of unpaired electrons (\(n\)) = 2. \[ \mu_s = \sqrt{n(n+2)} = \sqrt{2(2+2)} = \sqrt{8} \approx 2.83\,\mathrm{BM} \] The complex is paramagnetic.

Worked Example 4: Crystal Field Splitting and Electron Allocation

Problem: An octahedral complex \(\mathrm{[Fe(H_2O)_6]^{3+}}\) has a magnetic moment of \(5.92\,\mathrm{BM}\), whereas \(\mathrm{[Fe(CN)_6]^{3-}}\) has a magnetic moment of \(1.73\,\mathrm{BM}\). Account for these observations using Crystal Field Theory.

Step-by-Step Solution:

  • For both complexes, the central ion is \(\mathrm{Fe^{3+}}\) (\(3d^5\) system).
  • In \(\mathrm{[Fe(H_2O)_6]^{3+}}\), \(\mathrm{H_2O}\) is a weak field ligand: \(\Delta_\mathrm{o} < P\).
    Electrons do not pair in \(t_{2g}\) and occupy all five orbitals singly: \(t_{2g}^3 e_g^2\).
    Number of unpaired electrons \(n = 5\). \[ \mu_s = \sqrt{5(5+2)} = \sqrt{35} \approx 5.92\,\mathrm{BM} \quad (\text{High-spin complex}) \]
  • In \(\mathrm{[Fe(CN)_6]^{3-}}\), \(\mathrm{CN^-}\) is a strong field ligand: \(\Delta_\mathrm{o} > P\).
    All five electrons are forced into the lower-energy \(t_{2g}\) orbitals: \(t_{2g}^5 e_g^0\).
    Number of unpaired electrons \(n = 1\). \[ \mu_s = \sqrt{1(1+2)} = \sqrt{3} \approx 1.73\,\mathrm{BM} \quad (\text{Low-spin complex}) \]

उदाहरण 2 का सारांश: \(\mathrm{K_3[Fe(C_2O_4)_3]}\) में ऑक्सैलेट द्विदंतुर है, अतः \(3 \times 2 = 6\) दाता परमाणुओं के कारण उपसहसंयोजन संख्या 6 तथा Fe की ऑक्सीकरण अवस्था +3 है।

Practice Question 4 NEET Single-Correct MCQ · Optical Activity

Which of the following coordination entities is optically active (chiral and resolves into enantiomers)?

(A) trans-\(\mathrm{[CoCl_2(en)_2]^+}\)
(B) cis-\(\mathrm{[CoCl_2(en)_2]^+}\)
(C) trans-\(\mathrm{[PtCl_2(NH_3)_2]}\)
(D) \(\mathrm{[NiCl_4]^{2-}}\)
Correct Answer: (B) Worked Solution:

Detailed Symmetry Analysis:
• Condition for Optical Activity: A coordination entity is optically active if and only if it is chiral, which requires the absence of a plane of symmetry (\(\sigma\)) and a centre of inversion (\(i\)).
• cis-\(\mathrm{[CoCl_2(en)_2]^+}\): In the cis-isomer, the two chloride ligands are adjacent (\(90^\circ\)). The two bidentate ethane-1,2-diamine rings lie in mutually perpendicular planes. This geometry lacks both a plane of symmetry and an inversion centre. Therefore, it is chiral and exists as a pair of non-superimposable mirror images (\(d\)- and \(l\)-enantiomers).
• trans-\(\mathrm{[CoCl_2(en)_2]^+}\): The two chloride ligands lie on opposite vertices (\(180^\circ\)), with the two 'en' ligands lying in the equatorial plane. It contains a centre of inversion (\(i\)) and multiple planes of symmetry. Thus, it is achiral and optically inactive.
• trans-\(\mathrm{[PtCl_2(NH_3)_2]}\): Square planar complex possessing a molecular plane of symmetry; strictly achiral.
• \(\mathrm{[NiCl_4]^{2-}}\): Symmetric tetrahedral geometry; achiral.

Practice Question 6 NEET Single-Correct MCQ · Crystal Field Theory

For a \(d^4\) central metal ion in an octahedral crystal field, the pairing energy \(P\) is \(24{,}000\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}\) and the crystal field splitting energy \(\Delta_\mathrm{o}\) is \(18{,}000\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}\). The ground-state electronic configuration of the complex and the spin-only magnetic moment are:

(A) \(t_{2g}^4 e_g^0\) and \(2.83\,\mathrm{BM}\)
(B) \(t_{2g}^3 e_g^1\) and \(4.90\,\mathrm{BM}\)
(C) \(t_{2g}^2 e_g^2\) and \(4.90\,\mathrm{BM}\)
(D) \(t_{2g}^4 e_g^0\) and \(0\,\mathrm{BM}\)
Correct Answer: (B) Worked Solution:

Step-by-Step CFT Calculation:
1. Compare \(\Delta_\mathrm{o}\) with \(P\): \[ \Delta_\mathrm{o} = 18{,}000\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}, \quad P = 24{,}000\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}} \implies \Delta_\mathrm{o} < P \]
2. Determine Electron Distribution:
Since the splitting energy is less than the pairing energy (\(\Delta_\mathrm{o} < P\)), the energy penalty to pair two electrons in a \(t_{2g}\) orbital is higher than the energy required to promote an electron into the higher \(e_g\) level.
Therefore, Hund's rule is obeyed, leading to a weak field, high-spin configuration.
The first three electrons singly occupy the \(t_{2g}\) orbitals: \(t_{2g}^3\).
The fourth electron enters the higher-energy \(e_g\) orbital: \(e_g^1\).
Configuration: \(t_{2g}^3 e_g^1\).
3. Calculate Spin-Only Magnetic Moment:
Total unpaired electrons \(n = 3 (\text{in } t_{2g}) + 1 (\text{in } e_g) = 4\). \[ \mu_s = \sqrt{n(n+2)} = \sqrt{4(4+2)} = \sqrt{24} \approx 4.90\,\mathrm{BM} \]

(A) \(t_{2g}^4 e_g^0\) तथा \(2.83\,\mathrm{BM}\)
(B) \(t_{2g}^3 e_g^1\) तथा \(4.90\,\mathrm{BM}\)
(C) \(t_{2g}^2 e_g^2\) तथा \(4.90\,\mathrm{BM}\)
(D) \(t_{2g}^4 e_g^0\) तथा \(0\,\mathrm{BM}\)
सही उत्तर: (B) विस्तृत हल:

चरणबद्ध हल:
1. यहाँ \(\Delta_\mathrm{o} < P\) है (\(18{,}000 < 24{,}000\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}\))।
2. चूँकि विपाटन ऊर्जा का मान युग्मन ऊर्जा से कम है, अतः इलेक्ट्रॉन युग्मित होने की अपेक्षा उच्च ऊर्जा वाले \(e_g\) कक्षक में जाना पसंद करेंगे।
3. यह दुर्बल क्षेत्र, उच्च चक्रण (high-spin) स्थिति है।
अतः विन्यास: \(t_{2g}^3 e_g^1\)।
4. अयुग्मित इलेक्ट्रॉनों की संख्या \(n = 4\) है। \[ \mu_s = \sqrt{4(4+2)} = \sqrt{24} \approx 4.90\,\mathrm{BM} \]

Practice Question 7 NEET Single-Correct MCQ · Synergic Bonding in Carbonyls

In which of the following homoleptic metal carbonyl complexes is the carbon-oxygen (\(\mathrm{C-O}\)) bond length expected to be the longest?

(A) \(\mathrm{[Mn(CO)_6]^+}\)
(B) \(\mathrm{[Cr(CO)_6]}\)
(C) \(\mathrm{[V(CO)_6]^-}\)
(D) \(\mathrm{[Fe(CO)_5]}\)
Correct Answer: (C) Worked Solution:

Synergic Bonding Mechanics:
1. In metal carbonyls, bonding consists of \(\sigma\)-donation from \(\mathrm{CO}\) to metal and \(\pi\)-backdonation from metal \(d\)-orbitals into the empty \(\pi^*\) (antibonding) orbitals of \(\mathrm{CO}\).
2. Populating the antibonding \(\pi^*\) orbital of \(\mathrm{CO}\) decreases the \(\mathrm{C-O}\) bond order and consequently increases the \(\mathrm{C-O}\) bond length.
3. Among isoelectronic octahedral hexacarbonyl species \(\mathrm{[Mn(CO)_6]^+}\), \(\mathrm{[Cr(CO)_6]}\), and \(\mathrm{[V(CO)_6]^-}\):
• The vanadium complex carries a net negative charge (\(-1\)), meaning its electron cloud is the least tightly held by the nucleus.
• The central vanadium has the highest electron density and undergoes the most extensive \(\mathrm{M \rightarrow C}\) \(\pi\)-backdonation.
4. Maximum \(\pi\)-backdonation into the \(\pi^*\) orbital of \(\mathrm{CO}\) causes the greatest reduction in \(\mathrm{C-O}\) bond order, resulting in the longest \(\mathrm{C-O}\) bond length.
Trend of \(\mathrm{C-O}\) bond length: \(\mathrm{[V(CO)_6]^- > [Cr(CO)_6] > [Mn(CO)_6]^+}\).

निम्नलिखित में से किस धातु कार्बोनिल संकुल में कार्बन-ऑक्सीजन (\(\mathrm{C-O}\)) आबंध की लंबाई सर्वाधिक (longest) होगी?

(A) \(\mathrm{[Mn(CO)_6]^+}\)
(B) \(\mathrm{[Cr(CO)_6]}\)
(C) \(\mathrm{[V(CO)_6]^-}\)
(D) \(\mathrm{[Fe(CO)_5]}\)
सही उत्तर: (C) विस्तृत हल:

सहक्रियात्मक बंधन एवं आबंध लंबाई:
1. धातु कार्बोनिल में धातु से \(\mathrm{CO}\) के रिक्त विपरीत-आबंधी \(\pi^*\) आण्विक कक्षक में इलेक्ट्रॉन का पश्च-दान (back-bonding) होता है।
2. \(\pi^*\) कक्षक में इलेक्ट्रॉन घनत्व बढ़ने से \(\mathrm{C-O}\) आबंध कोटि (bond order) घटती है तथा आबंध लंबाई बढ़ती है।
3. \(\mathrm{[V(CO)_6]^-}\) पर ऋण आवेश (\(-1\)) होने के कारण वेनेडियम पर इलेक्ट्रॉन घनत्व सर्वाधिक है, जिससे इसमें \(\pi\)-पश्च-दान अधिकतम होता है।
4. अतः \(\mathrm{[V(CO)_6]^-}\) में \(\mathrm{C-O}\) आबंध लंबाई सबसे अधिक (तथा आबंध सामर्थ्य सबसे कम) होगी।

(A) \(8{,}000\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}\)
(B) \(12{,}000\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}\)
(C) \(9{,}000\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}\)

चतुष्फलकीय एवं अष्टफलकीय विपाटन ऊर्जा में संबंध: \[ \Delta_\mathrm{t} = \frac{4}{9}\Delta_\mathrm{o} \] \[ \Delta_\mathrm{t} = \frac{4}{9} \times 18{,}000\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}} = 8{,}000\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}} \] अतः सही उत्तर \(8{,}000\,\mathrm{cm^{-1}}\) है।

11. Common Pitfalls & High-Yield Revision Checklist / सामान्य गलतियाँ एवं त्वरित दोहराव

English Checklist
Top 6 NEET Pitfalls to Avoid
  1. Confusing Primary and Secondary Valency: Remember that primary valency equals the oxidation state (ionisable, non-directional), while secondary valency equals the coordination number (non-ionisable, directional).
  2. Coordination Number of Polydentate Ligands: Do not just count the number of ligands! Multiply by the denticity. For \(\mathrm{[Fe(ox)_3]^{3-}}\), the coordination number is \(3 \times 2 = 6\), not 3!
  3. Assuming All 4-Coordinate Complexes are Tetrahedral: Always check the field strength of the ligand and metal oxidation state. \(\mathrm{[NiCl_4]^{2-}}\) is tetrahedral (\(sp^3\), paramagnetic), but \(\mathrm{[Ni(CN)_4]^{2-}}\) is square planar (\(dsp^2\), diamagnetic). All \(\mathrm{Pt(II)}\) and \(\mathrm{Pd(II)}\) complexes are square planar regardless of ligand.
  4. Misidentifying Optical Activity: trans-\(\mathrm{[CoCl_2(en)_2]^+}\) has a center of inversion and a plane of symmetry, making it optically inactive. Only the cis-isomer is optically active!
  5. Tetrahedral Splitting Subscripts: Never write 'g' (gerade) in tetrahedral orbital symbols (\(e\) and \(t_2\)). Tetrahedra lack a center of inversion (\(i\)).
  6. C-O Bond Length in Carbonyls: Remember that increased negative charge on the complex increases \(\mathrm{M \rightarrow C}\) backbonding, which weakens the \(\mathrm{C-O}\) bond and lengthens the \(\mathrm{C-O}\) distance.
