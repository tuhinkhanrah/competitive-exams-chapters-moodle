Learning Objectives & Syllabus Overview / अधिगम उद्देश्य एवं पाठ्यक्रम सारांश

English

Electrochemistry bridges chemical transformations and electrical energy. Spontaneous redox reactions can generate electricity (galvanic cells), while external electrical energy can drive non-spontaneous chemical changes (electrolytic cells). After completing this tutorial, you will be able to:

  • Distinguish between galvanic and electrolytic cells and interpret cell notation using standard electrode potentials.
  • Apply the Nernst equation to compute cell potential (\(E_{\text{cell}}\)), standard Gibbs energy change (\(\Delta_r G^\circ\)), and equilibrium constant (\(K_c\)).
  • Relate resistance, resistivity (\(\rho\)), conductivity (\(\kappa\)), cell constant (\(G^*\)), and molar conductivity (\(\Lambda_m\)), and explain their concentration dependence for strong and weak electrolytes.
  • Use Kohlrausch law of independent migration of ions to determine \(\Lambda_m^\circ\), degree of dissociation (\(\alpha\)), and acid dissociation constant (\(K_a\)).
  • Apply Faraday's laws of electrolysis and predict electrode products in aqueous and molten systems.
  • Summarize the chemistry of primary batteries, secondary batteries, \(\mathrm{H_2\text{–}O_2}\) fuel cells, and electrochemical corrosion.

1. Electrochemical Cells & Electrode Potentials / 1. वैद्युतरासायनिक सेल एवं इलेक्ट्रोड विभव

English

Galvanic vs. Electrolytic Operation

A galvanic (voltaic) cell converts the chemical energy of a spontaneous redox reaction (\(\Delta_r G < 0\)) into electrical work. In the classic Daniell cell, zinc is oxidised at the anode and \(\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}\) ions are reduced at the cathode:

\[\mathrm{Zn(s)} + \mathrm{Cu^{2+}(aq)} \rightarrow \mathrm{Zn^{2+}(aq)} + \mathrm{Cu(s)}, \qquad E^\circ_{\text{cell}} = 1.10\ \mathrm{V}\text{ (at }298\ \mathrm{K},\ 1\ \mathrm{M}\text{)}\]

When an opposing external potential \(E_{\text{ext}}\) is applied across the Daniell cell, three regimes arise:

  • \(E_{\text{ext}} < 1.10\ \mathrm{V}\): Acts as a galvanic cell; electrons flow from \(\mathrm{Zn}\) to \(\mathrm{Cu}\), conventional current flows from \(\mathrm{Cu}\) to \(\mathrm{Zn}\), \(\mathrm{Zn}\) dissolves, and \(\mathrm{Cu}\) deposits.
  • \(E_{\text{ext}} = 1.10\ \mathrm{V}\): No net electron flow (\(I = 0\)) and no chemical reaction occurs.
  • \(E_{\text{ext}} > 1.10\ \mathrm{V}\): Acts as an electrolytic cell; polarity of chemical roles reverses—electrons are forced from \(\mathrm{Cu}\) to \(\mathrm{Zn}\), \(\mathrm{Zn}^{2+}\) deposits at the zinc electrode (now cathode), and \(\mathrm{Cu}\) dissolves at the copper electrode (now anode).

IUPAC Cell Notation & Standard Electrode Potential

By IUPAC convention, the oxidation half-cell (anode) is written on the left and the reduction half-cell (cathode) on the right. A single vertical bar (\(|\)) represents a phase boundary, and a double vertical bar (\(\parallel\)) represents the salt bridge:

\[\mathrm{Zn(s)} \mid \mathrm{Zn^{2+}(aq)} \parallel \mathrm{Cu^{2+}(aq)} \mid \mathrm{Cu(s)}, \qquad E_{\text{cell}} = E_{\text{right}} - E_{\text{left}} = E_{\text{cathode}} - E_{\text{anode}}\]

An individual half-cell potential cannot be measured in isolation. Hence, the Standard Hydrogen Electrode (SHE), \(\mathrm{Pt(s)} \mid \mathrm{H_2(g,\ 1\ bar)} \mid \mathrm{H^+(aq,\ 1\ M)}\), is assigned \(E^\circ = 0.00\ \mathrm{V}\) at all temperatures. When all species are at unit concentration (or \(1\ \mathrm{bar}\) for gases), the measured reduction potential is the standard electrode potential (\(E^\circ\)).

गैल्वैनी बनाम वैद्युत-अपघटनी कार्यप्रणाली

गैल्वैनी (वोल्टीय) सेल एक स्वतः प्रवर्तित रेडॉक्स अभिक्रिया (\(\Delta_r G < 0\)) की रासायनिक ऊर्जा को विद्युत कार्य में परिवर्तित करता है। डेनियल सेल में ऐनोड पर जिंक का ऑक्सीकरण और कैथोड पर \(\mathrm{Cu^{2+}}\) आयनों का अपचयन होता है:

\[\mathrm{Zn(s)} + \mathrm{Cu^{2+}(aq)} \rightarrow \mathrm{Zn^{2+}(aq)} + \mathrm{Cu(s)}, \qquad E^\circ_{\text{cell}} = 1.10\ \mathrm{V}\ (298\ \mathrm{K},\ 1\ \mathrm{M}\text{ पर})\]

जब डेनियल सेल पर विपरीत दिशा में बाह्य विभव \(E_{\text{ext}}\) लगाया जाता है, तो तीन स्थितियाँ उत्पन्न होती हैं:

  • \(E_{\text{ext}} < 1.10\ \mathrm{V}\): सेल गैल्वैनी सेल की तरह कार्य करता है; इलेक्ट्रॉन \(\mathrm{Zn}\) से \(\mathrm{Cu}\) की ओर और विद्युत धारा \(\mathrm{Cu}\) से \(\mathrm{Zn}\) की ओर प्रवाहित होती है।
  • \(E_{\text{ext}} = 1.10\ \mathrm{V}\): कोई धारा (\(I = 0\)) प्रवाहित नहीं होती और रासायनिक अभिक्रिया रुक जाती है।
  • \(E_{\text{ext}} > 1.10\ \mathrm{V}\): यह वैद्युत-अपघटनी सेल के रूप में कार्य करता है; अभिक्रिया विपरीत दिशा में चलती है—इलेक्ट्रॉन \(\mathrm{Cu}\) से \(\mathrm{Zn}\) की ओर जाते हैं, जिंक इलेक्ट्रोड पर \(\mathrm{Zn}\) निक्षेपित होता है और कॉपर घुलता है।
शिक्षण आरेख 1: गैल्वैनी (डेनियल) सेल की संरचना, जिसमें ऐनोड ऑक्सीकरण, कैथोड अपचयन, लवण सेतु और इलेक्ट्रॉन प्रवाह दर्शाए गए हैं।

IUPAC सेल निरूपण एवं मानक इलेक्ट्रोड विभव

IUPAC परिपाटी के अनुसार बाईं ओर ऑक्सीकरण अर्ध-सेल (ऐनोड) तथा दाईं ओर अपचयन अर्ध-सेल (कैथोड) लिखा जाता है। एकल ऊर्ध्वाधर रेखा (\(|\)) प्रावस्था सीमा तथा द्वि-रेखा (\(\parallel\)) लवण सेतु को दर्शाती है:

\[\mathrm{Zn(s)} \mid \mathrm{Zn^{2+}(aq)} \parallel \mathrm{Cu^{2+}(aq)} \mid \mathrm{Cu(s)}, \qquad E_{\text{cell}} = E_{\text{right}} - E_{\text{left}} = E_{\text{cathode}} - E_{\text{anode}}\]

किसी एकल अर्ध-सेल का विभव सीधे नहीं मापा जा सकता। इसलिए मानक हाइड्रोजन इलेक्ट्रोड (SHE), \(\mathrm{Pt(s)} \mid \mathrm{H_2(g,\ 1\ bar)} \mid \mathrm{H^+(aq,\ 1\ M)}\), का विभव सभी तापों पर \(E^\circ = 0.00\ \mathrm{V}\) माना जाता है। उच्च \(E^\circ\) मान प्रबल ऑक्सीकारक (अपचयित होने की उच्च प्रवृत्ति) को दर्शाता है, जबकि निम्न या ऋणात्मक \(E^\circ\) मान प्रबल अपचायक (जैसे \(\mathrm{Li}\), जिसका \(E^\circ = -3.05\ \mathrm{V}\) है) को दर्शाता है।

2. Nernst Equation, Equilibrium Constant & Gibbs Energy / 2. नेर्नस्ट समीकरण, साम्य स्थिरांक एवं गिब्स ऊर्जा

English

When ionic concentrations differ from \(1\ \mathrm{M}\) (or gas pressures differ from \(1\ \mathrm{bar}\)), electrode and cell potentials vary according to the Nernst equation. For a general cell reaction \(a\mathrm{A} + b\mathrm{B} \xrightarrow{ne^-} c\mathrm{C} + d\mathrm{D}\):

\[E_{\text{cell}} = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - \frac{RT}{nF}\ln Q = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - \frac{2.303RT}{nF}\log Q, \qquad Q = \frac{[\mathrm{C}]^c[\mathrm{D}]^d}{[\mathrm{A}]^a[\mathrm{B}]^b}\]

At \(T = 298\ \mathrm{K}\), substituting \(R = 8.314\ \mathrm{J\ K^{-1}\ mol^{-1}}\) and \(F = 96487\ \mathrm{C\ mol^{-1}}\) gives \(\frac{2.303RT}{F} \approx 0.059\ \mathrm{V}\):

\[E_{\text{cell}} = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - \frac{0.059}{n}\log Q \qquad (\text{at } 298\ \mathrm{K})\]

Note that pure solids and pure liquids have unit activity (\(=1\)) and are omitted from \(Q\). For a single metal electrode \(\mathrm{M}^{n+}(\mathrm{aq}) + ne^- \rightarrow \mathrm{M(s)}\), \(E = E^\circ - \frac{0.059}{n}\log\frac{1}{[\mathrm{M}^{n+}]}\).

Thermodynamic Connections: \(\Delta_r G^\circ\) and \(K_c\)

  • Gibbs Energy: \(\Delta_r G = -nFE_{\text{cell}}\) and \(\Delta_r G^\circ = -nFE^\circ_{\text{cell}}\). A spontaneous cell reaction requires \(E_{\text{cell}} > 0\) (\(\Delta_r G < 0\)).
  • Equilibrium Constant: At equilibrium, \(E_{\text{cell}} = 0\) and \(Q = K_c\), yielding: \[E^\circ_{\text{cell}} = \frac{0.059}{n}\log K_c \quad (\text{at } 298\ \mathrm{K}) \qquad \text{and} \qquad \Delta_r G^\circ = -RT\ln K_c\]
NEET UG Crucial Concept: \(E^\circ_{\text{cell}}\) is an intensive property (independent of multiplying the reaction by a stoichiometric factor), whereas \(\Delta_r G^\circ\) is an extensive property (proportional to \(n\)). Never add or subtract \(E^\circ\) values directly when combining two half-reactions to form a third half-reaction; always convert via \(\Delta G^\circ = -nFE^\circ\)!
Original Worked Example 1 (Hypothetical Values): Consider the galvanic cell at \(298\ \mathrm{K}\): \[\mathrm{Fe(s)} \mid \mathrm{Fe^{2+}(0.01\ M)} \parallel \mathrm{Ag^+(0.10\ M)} \mid \mathrm{Ag(s)}\] Given \(E^\circ_{\mathrm{Fe^{2+}/Fe}} = -0.44\ \mathrm{V}\) and \(E^\circ_{\mathrm{Ag^+/Ag}} = +0.80\ \mathrm{V}\). Calculate \(E_{\text{cell}}\) and \(\Delta_r G^\circ\).

Solution:
1. Overall reaction: \(\mathrm{Fe(s)} + 2\mathrm{Ag^+(aq)} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fe^{2+}(aq)} + 2\mathrm{Ag(s)}\), so \(n = 2\).
2. \(E^\circ_{\text{cell}} = E^\circ_{\mathrm{Ag^+/Ag}} - E^\circ_{\mathrm{Fe^{2+}/Fe}} = 0.80 - (-0.44) = 1.24\ \mathrm{V}\).
3. \(Q = \frac{[\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}]}{[\mathrm{Ag^+}]^2} = \frac{0.01}{(0.10)^2} = \frac{0.01}{0.01} = 1\).
4. \(E_{\text{cell}} = 1.24 - \frac{0.059}{2}\log(1) = 1.24\ \mathrm{V}\).
5. \(\Delta_r G^\circ = -nFE^\circ_{\text{cell}} = -2 \times 96500 \times 1.24 = -239320\ \mathrm{J\ mol^{-1}} = -239.32\ \mathrm{kJ\ mol^{-1}}\) (using \(F \approx 96500\ \mathrm{C\ mol^{-1}}\)).

\[E_{\text{cell}} = E^\circ_{\text{cell}} - \frac{0.059}{n}\log Q, \qquad Q = \frac{[\mathrm{C}]^c[\mathrm{D}]^d}{[\mathrm{A}]^a[\mathrm{B}]^b}\]

शुद्ध ठोस एवं द्रव की सक्रियता इकाई (\(=1\)) ली जाती है।

ऊष्मागतिकी संबंध: \(\Delta_r G^\circ\) एवं \(K_c\)

  • गिब्स ऊर्जा: \(\Delta_r G = -nFE_{\text{cell}}\) तथा \(\Delta_r G^\circ = -nFE^\circ_{\text{cell}}\)। स्वतः प्रवर्तित अभिक्रिया के लिए \(E_{\text{cell}} > 0\) आवश्यक है।
  • साम्य स्थिरांक: साम्यावस्था पर \(E_{\text{cell}} = 0\) तथा \(Q = K_c\) होता है, अतः \(298\ \mathrm{K}\) पर: \[E^\circ_{\text{cell}} = \frac{0.059}{n}\log K_c \qquad \text{एवं} \qquad \Delta_r G^\circ = -RT\ln K_c\]
NEET UG महत्वपूर्ण सावधानी: \(E^\circ_{\text{cell}}\) एक गहन (intensive) गुण है, जबकि \(\Delta_r G^\circ\) एक विस्तीर्ण (extensive) गुण है। जब दो अर्ध-अभिक्रियाओं को जोड़कर तीसरी अर्ध-अभिक्रिया बनाई जाए, तो \(E^\circ\) मानों को सीधे न जोड़ें; सदैव \(\Delta G^\circ = -nFE^\circ\) का उपयोग करें।
मौलिक हल किया गया उदाहरण 1 (कल्पित मान): \(298\ \mathrm{K}\) पर सेल \(\mathrm{Fe(s)} \mid \mathrm{Fe^{2+}(0.01\ M)} \parallel \mathrm{Ag^+(0.10\ M)} \mid \mathrm{Ag(s)}\) के लिए \(E_{\text{cell}}\) और \(\Delta_r G^\circ\) ज्ञात कीजिए। दिया है: \(E^\circ_{\mathrm{Fe^{2+}/Fe}} = -0.44\ \mathrm{V}\), \(E^\circ_{\mathrm{Ag^+/Ag}} = +0.80\ \mathrm{V}\)।

हल:
1. संपूर्ण अभिक्रिया: \(\mathrm{Fe(s)} + 2\mathrm{Ag^+(aq)} \rightarrow \mathrm{Fe^{2+}(aq)} + 2\mathrm{Ag(s)}\), अतः \(n = 2\)।
2. \(E^\circ_{\text{cell}} = 0.80 - (-0.44) = 1.24\ \mathrm{V}\)।
3. \(Q = \frac{[\mathrm{Fe^{2+}}]}{[\mathrm{Ag^+}]^2} = \frac{0.01}{(0.10)^2} = 1\)।
4. \(E_{\text{cell}} = 1.24 - \frac{0.059}{2}\log(1) = 1.24\ \mathrm{V}\)।
5. \(\Delta_r G^\circ = -2 \times 96500 \times 1.24 = -239.32\ \mathrm{kJ\ mol^{-1}}\) (\(F \approx 96500\ \mathrm{C\ mol^{-1}}\) लेने पर)।

3. Conductance of Electrolytic Solutions & Kohlrausch Law / 3. वैद्युत-अपघटनी विलयनों का चालकत्व एवं कोलराउश नियम

English

Key Electrical Quantities & Units

Quantity Formula Common / SI Units
Resistance (\(R\)) & Resistivity (\(\rho\)) \(R = \rho\frac{l}{A}\) \(R\): \(\Omega\); \(\rho\): \(\Omega\ \mathrm{m}\) or \(\Omega\ \mathrm{cm}\)
Conductance (\(G\)) \(G = \frac{1}{R} = \kappa\frac{A}{l}\) \(\mathrm{S}\) (siemens) or \(\Omega^{-1}\)
Conductivity (\(\kappa\)) \(\kappa = \frac{1}{\rho} = \frac{G^*}{R}\) \(\mathrm{S\ m^{-1}}\) or \(\mathrm{S\ cm^{-1}}\) (\(1\ \mathrm{S\ cm^{-1}} = 100\ \mathrm{S\ m^{-1}}\))
Cell Constant (\(G^*\)) \(G^* = \frac{l}{A} = R\kappa\) \(\mathrm{m^{-1}}\) or \(\mathrm{cm^{-1}}\)
Molar Conductivity (\(\Lambda_m\)) \(\Lambda_m = \frac{\kappa \times 1000}{M}\) \(\mathrm{S\ cm^2\ mol^{-1}}\) (with \(\kappa\) in \(\mathrm{S\ cm^{-1}}\), \(M\) in \(\mathrm{mol\ L^{-1}}\))

Electronic vs. Ionic Conductance: Metallic (electronic) conductance is due to electron movement and decreases with rising temperature (due to increased lattice vibrations). Electrolytic (ionic) conductance is due to ion migration and increases with rising temperature (due to decreased solvent viscosity and greater dissociation/mobility). Measurement of ionic solution resistance requires an AC source in a Wheatstone bridge to prevent electrolysis and composition change.

Effect of Dilution on \(\kappa\) and \(\Lambda_m\)

  • Conductivity (\(\kappa\)) always decreases on dilution for both strong and weak electrolytes because the number of current-carrying ions per unit volume decreases.
  • Molar conductivity (\(\Lambda_m = \kappa V\)) increases on dilution because the increase in volume \(V\) containing \(1\ \mathrm{mol}\) of electrolyte more than compensates for the decrease in \(\kappa\).
  • Strong Electrolytes: \(\Lambda_m\) increases slowly and linearly with \(\sqrt{c}\) (Debye–Hückel–Onsager equation): \(\Lambda_m = \Lambda_m^\circ - A\sqrt{c}\). Extrapolation to \(\sqrt{c} = 0\) gives \(\Lambda_m^\circ\).
  • Weak Electrolytes: \(\Lambda_m\) increases steeply at low concentrations due to a sharp rise in degree of dissociation (\(\alpha\)). Thus, \(\Lambda_m^\circ\) cannot be obtained by graphical extrapolation; it is found using Kohlrausch law.

Kohlrausch Law of Independent Migration of Ions

At infinite dilution, each ion migrates independently of its co-ion. For an electrolyte yielding \(\nu_+\) cations and \(\nu_-\) anions:

\[\Lambda_m^\circ = \nu_+\lambda_+^\circ + \nu_-\lambda_-^\circ, \qquad \alpha = \frac{\Lambda_m}{\Lambda_m^\circ}, \qquad K_a = \frac{c\alpha^2}{1-\alpha} = \frac{c\Lambda_m^2}{\Lambda_m^\circ(\Lambda_m^\circ - \Lambda_m)}\]
Original Worked Example 2 (Hypothetical Values): At \(298\ \mathrm{K}\), \(\Lambda_m^\circ\) values for \(\mathrm{HCl}\), \(\mathrm{HCOONa}\), and \(\mathrm{NaCl}\) are \(426.0\), \(104.7\), and \(126.5\ \mathrm{S\ cm^2\ mol^{-1}}\) respectively. A \(0.01\ \mathrm{M}\) solution of formic acid (\(\mathrm{HCOOH}\)) has conductivity \(\kappa = 5.25 \times 10^{-4}\ \mathrm{S\ cm^{-1}}\). Find \(\Lambda_m^\circ(\mathrm{HCOOH})\), \(\alpha\), and \(K_a\).

Solution:
1. \(\Lambda_m^\circ(\mathrm{HCOOH}) = \Lambda_m^\circ(\mathrm{HCl}) + \Lambda_m^\circ(\mathrm{HCOONa}) - \Lambda_m^\circ(\mathrm{NaCl}) = 426.0 + 104.7 - 126.5 = 404.2\ \mathrm{S\ cm^2\ mol^{-1}}\).
2. \(\Lambda_m = \frac{\kappa \times 1000}{M} = \frac{5.25 \times 10^{-4} \times 1000}{0.01} = 52.5\ \mathrm{S\ cm^2\ mol^{-1}}\).
3. \(\alpha = \frac{52.5}{404.2} \approx 0.13\).
4. \(K_a = \frac{c\alpha^2}{1-\alpha} = \frac{0.01 \times (0.13)^2}{1 - 0.13} \approx \frac{1.69 \times 10^{-4}}{0.87} \approx 1.94 \times 10^{-4}\ \mathrm{mol\ L^{-1}}\).

4. Electrolysis, Faraday's Laws & Product Prediction / 4. वैद्युत-अपघटन, फैराडे के नियम एवं उत्पादों का पूर्वानुमान

English

Faraday's Laws of Electrolysis

  • First Law: Mass deposited/liberated at an electrode is proportional to charge passed (\(Q = It\)): \[w = ZIt = \frac{E}{F}Q = \frac{M}{nF}It \qquad (1\ \mathrm{F} = 96487\ \mathrm{C\ mol^{-1}} \approx 96500\ \mathrm{C\ mol^{-1}})\] where \(M\) is molar mass and \(n\) is the number of electrons per mole of species reduced/oxidised.
  • Second Law: When the same charge \(Q\) passes through different electrolytic cells connected in series, the masses liberated are proportional to their equivalent weights: \(\frac{w_1}{w_2} = \frac{E_1}{E_2}\).

Predicting Products of Electrolysis

At the cathode, the species with the higher \(E^\circ_{\text{red}}\) is reduced preferentially. At the anode, the species with the lower \(E^\circ_{\text{red}}\) (higher oxidation potential) is oxidised preferentially, subject to kinetic overpotential and electrode reactivity:

Original Worked Example 3 (Hypothetical Values): Two cells containing aqueous \(\mathrm{AgNO_3}\) and molten \(\mathrm{Al_2O_3}\) are connected in series. If \(2.16\ \mathrm{g}\) of \(\mathrm{Ag}\) (atomic mass \(108\ \mathrm{g\ mol^{-1}}\)) is deposited in the first cell, what mass of \(\mathrm{Al}\) (atomic mass \(27\ \mathrm{g\ mol^{-1}}\)) is deposited in the second cell?

Solution:
By Faraday's Second Law, moles of electrons passed are identical in both cells:
1. For \(\mathrm{Ag^+ + e^- \rightarrow Ag}\): \(n(\mathrm{e^-}) = \frac{2.16}{108} = 0.02\ \mathrm{mol}\).
2. For \(\mathrm{Al^{3+} + 3e^- \rightarrow Al}\): \(0.02\ \mathrm{mol\ e^-}\) deposits \(\frac{0.02}{3}\ \mathrm{mol\ Al}\).
3. Mass of \(\mathrm{Al} = \frac{0.02}{3} \times 27 = 0.18\ \mathrm{g}\).

English — Practice Question 5 (Numerical MCQ)

  • (B) \(156\ \mathrm{S\ cm^2\ mol^{-1}}\)
  • (C) \(195\ \mathrm{S\ cm^2\ mol^{-1}}\)
  • (D) \(260\ \mathrm{S\ cm^2\ mol^{-1}}\)
Answer: (A) \(130\ \mathrm{S\ cm^2\ mol^{-1}}\)
Solution:
1. Cell constant \(G^* = R_{\mathrm{KCl}} \times \kappa_{\mathrm{KCl}} = 120 \times 0.0065 = 0.78\ \mathrm{cm^{-1}}\).

7. Common Pitfalls, Revision Checklist & Final Recap / 7. सामान्य त्रुटियाँ, पुनरीक्षण सूची एवं अंतिम सारांश

English

Common NEET UG Pitfalls to Avoid

  • Adding \(E^\circ\) values directly for half-cells: Always use \(\Delta G^\circ = -nFE^\circ\) when combining two half-reactions into a third half-reaction.
  • Unit mismatch in \(\Lambda_m\): When \(\kappa\) is in \(\mathrm{S\ cm^{-1}}\) and \(M\) is in \(\mathrm{mol\ L^{-1}}\), multiply by \(1000\) to get \(\mathrm{S\ cm^2\ mol^{-1}}\); when \(\kappa\) is in \(\mathrm{S\ m^{-1}}\) and \(c\) is in \(\mathrm{mol\ m^{-3}}\), use \(\Lambda_m = \kappa / c\) without \(1000\).
  • Electrons in stoichiometry: Remember \(n=6\) for \(\mathrm{Cr_2O_7^{2-} \rightarrow 2Cr^{3+}}\) and \(n=5\) for \(\mathrm{MnO_4^- \rightarrow Mn^{2+}}\).
Final Recap: Galvanic cells convert spontaneous redox energy into electricity (\(E_{\text{cell}} = E_{\text{cathode}} - E_{\text{anode}}\), \(\Delta_r G^\circ = -nFE^\circ_{\text{cell}} = -RT\ln K_c\)). Dilution lowers \(\kappa\) but raises \(\Lambda_m\); Kohlrausch law unlocks \(\Lambda_m^\circ\) and \(K_a\) for weak electrolytes. Faraday's laws govern electrolysis stoichiometry, while batteries, fuel cells, and rusting are practical electrochemical cells.
हिन्दी

NEET UG में बचने योग्य सामान्य त्रुटियाँ

  • अर्ध-सेलों के \(E^\circ\) मानों को सीधे जोड़ना: तीसरी अर्ध-अभिक्रिया का \(E^\circ\) निकालने के लिए सदैव \(\Delta G^\circ = -nFE^\circ\) का उपयोग करें।
  • \(\Lambda_m\) में मात्रक त्रुटि: यदि \(\kappa\) \(\mathrm{S\ cm^{-1}}\) में और \(M\) \(\mathrm{mol\ L^{-1}}\) में हो, तो \(1000\) से गुणा करें; यदि \(\kappa\) \(\mathrm{S\ m^{-1}}\) में और \(c\) \(\mathrm{mol\ m^{-3}}\) में हो, तो सीधे \(\Lambda_m = \kappa / c\) लगाएँ।
  • इलेक्ट्रॉन संख्या (\(n\)) की भूल: \(\mathrm{Cr_2O_7^{2-} \rightarrow 2Cr^{3+}}\) में \(n=6\) तथा \(\mathrm{MnO_4^- \rightarrow Mn^{2+}}\) में \(n=5\) होता है।
अंतिम सारांश: गैल्वैनी सेल स्वतः रेडॉक्स ऊर्जा को विद्युत में बदलते हैं (\(E_{\text{cell}} = E_{\text{cathode}} - E_{\text{anode}}\), \(\Delta_r G^\circ = -nFE^\circ_{\text{cell}} = -RT\ln K_c\))। तनुता से \(\kappa\) घटता है पर \(\Lambda_m\) बढ़ता है; कोलराउश नियम दुर्बल वैद्युत-अपघट्यों का \(\Lambda_m^\circ\) व \(K_a\) देता है। फैराडे के नियम वैद्युत-अपघटन की मात्रा निर्धारित करते हैं।
