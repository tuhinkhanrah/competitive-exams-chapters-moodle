1. Learning Objectives & Chapter Roadmap / अधिगम उद्देश्य एवं अध्याय रूपरेखा

English

Transition (\(d\)-block) and inner transition (\(f\)-block) elements bridge the reactive \(s\)-block metals and the diverse \(p\)-block elements. Because their penultimate \((n-1)d\) or antepenultimate \((n-2)f\) orbitals are progressively filled, these elements display rich redox chemistry, magnetism, colour, and catalytic activity—all of which are high-yield topics in NEET UG.

  • Identify the position of \(d\)-block (Groups 3–12) and \(f\)-block (4\(f\) and 5\(f\) series) elements and distinguish true transition metals from Group 12 elements.
  • Write ground-state and ionic electronic configurations, accounting for half-filled and completely filled subshell stabilities.
  • Explain trends in melting points, enthalpies of atomisation, atomic/ionic radii (including lanthanoid contraction), and ionisation enthalpies.
  • Evaluate oxidation-state stability using standard electrode potentials (\(E^\circ\)), hydration enthalpies, and halide/oxide formation.
  • Calculate spin-only magnetic moments and predict colour, complex formation, catalytic behaviour, and interstitial/alloy properties.
  • Master the preparation, structures, pH-dependent equilibria, and redox reactions of \(\mathrm{K_2Cr_2O_7}\) and \(\mathrm{KMnO_4}\).
  • Compare lanthanoids and actinoids in configuration, contraction, oxidation states, and chemical reactivity.

2. Position & Electronic Configurations of d-Block Elements / d-ब्लॉक तत्वों की स्थिति एवं इलेक्ट्रॉनिक विन्यास

English

2.1 What Defines a Transition Metal?

The \(d\)-block spans Groups 3 to 12 across four long periods: the \(3d\) series (\(\mathrm{Sc}\) to \(\mathrm{Zn}\)), \(4d\) series (\(\mathrm{Y}\) to \(\mathrm{Cd}\)), \(5d\) series (\(\mathrm{La}\) and \(\mathrm{Hf}\) to \(\mathrm{Hg}\)), and \(6d\) series (\(\mathrm{Ac}\) and \(\mathrm{Rf}\) to \(\mathrm{Cn}\)).

By the IUPAC definition, a transition metal is an element that possesses an incompletely filled \(d\) subshell either in its neutral ground-state atom or in at least one of its common oxidation states.

  • Why \(\mathrm{Zn}, \mathrm{Cd}, \mathrm{Hg}\) (Group 12) are NOT transition metals: They have a completely filled \((n-1)d^{10}ns^2\) configuration in the neutral atom and retain the \(d^{10}\) core in their common \(+2\) oxidation state (\(\mathrm{Zn}^{2+}, \mathrm{Cd}^{2+}, \mathrm{Hg}^{2+}\)).
  • Why \(\mathrm{Cu}, \mathrm{Ag}, \mathrm{Au}\) (Group 11) ARE transition metals: Although their neutral atoms have \(d^{10}s^1\), their common ions such as \(\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}\) (\(3d^9\)), \(\mathrm{Ag}^{2+}\) (\(4d^9\)), and \(\mathrm{Au}^{3+}\) (\(5d^8\)) contain incomplete \(d\) orbitals.

2.2 Electronic Configurations & Exceptions

The general valence configuration of \(d\)-block elements is \((n-1)d^{1\text{–}10}ns^{1\text{–}2}\), with palladium (\(\mathrm{Pd}, Z=46\)) as a notable exception having \(4d^{10}5s^0\). Because the energy gap between \((n-1)d\) and \(ns\) orbitals is very small, extra stability from half-filled (\(d^5\)) or completely filled (\(d^{10}\)) subshells (driven by high exchange energy and symmetrical electron distribution) causes deviations:

  • Chromium (\(\mathrm{Cr}, Z=24\)): \([\mathrm{Ar}]\,3d^5 4s^1\) (not \(3d^4 4s^2\)).
  • Copper (\(\mathrm{Cu}, Z=29\)): \([\mathrm{Ar}]\,3d^{10} 4s^1\) (not \(3d^9 4s^2\)).
  • Important Rule for Ion Formation: When a \(d\)-block atom forms a cation, electrons are always removed from the outermost \(ns\) orbital first, and only then from \((n-1)d\). For instance, \(\mathrm{Fe}\) (\([\mathrm{Ar}]\,3d^6 4s^2\)) forms \(\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}\) (\([\mathrm{Ar}]\,3d^6\)) and \(\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}\) (\([\mathrm{Ar}]\,3d^5\)).

3. Physical Properties, Atomic Radii & Ionisation Enthalpies / भौतिक गुण, परमाण्विक त्रिज्या एवं आयनन एन्थैल्पी

English

3.1 Metallic Bonding, Melting Points & Enthalpies of Atomisation

Almost all transition metals exhibit high tensile strength, malleability, ductility, and high thermal/electrical conductivity. Because both \(ns\) and \((n-1)d\) unpaired electrons participate in interatomic metallic bonding, their melting points and enthalpies of atomisation (\(\Delta_a H^\circ\)) are very high:

  • Mid-Series Peak: Metallic bond strength increases with the number of unpaired \(d\) electrons, peaking around \(d^5\) (\(\mathrm{Cr}, \mathrm{Mo}, \mathrm{W}\)).
  • Dip at \(\mathrm{Mn}\) and \(\mathrm{Tc}\): Although \(\mathrm{Mn}\) has five unpaired \(3d\) electrons, its stable half-filled \(3d^5\) subshell holds electrons more tightly, reducing their participation in metallic bonding and causing an anomalous dip in melting point and \(\Delta_a H^\circ\).
  • Group 12 Minimum: \(\mathrm{Zn}, \mathrm{Cd}, \mathrm{Hg}\) have \(d^{10}\) configurations with no unpaired \(d\) electrons; hence they have weak metallic bonding, the lowest \(\Delta_a H^\circ\) (e.g., \(\mathrm{Zn} = 126\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)), and \(\mathrm{Hg}\) is liquid at room temperature.
  • Heavy Series Advantage: \(4d\) and \(5d\) metals have higher \(\Delta_a H^\circ\) than \(3d\) metals, leading to frequent metal–metal bonding in their compounds.

3.1 धात्विक आबंधन, गलनांक एवं कणन एन्थैल्पी

लगभग सभी संक्रमण धातुएँ उच्च तनन सामर्थ्य, आघातवर्ध्यता, तन्यता तथा उच्च ऊष्मीय एवं विद्युत चालकता दर्शाती हैं। चूँकि अंतर-परमाण्विक धात्विक आबंधन में \(ns\) के साथ \((n-1)d\) अयुग्मित इलेक्ट्रॉन भी भाग लेते हैं, इसलिए इनके गलनांक तथा कणन एन्थैल्पी (\(\Delta_a H^\circ\)) बहुत उच्च होते हैं:

  • श्रेणी के मध्य में शिखर: अयुग्मित \(d\) इलेक्ट्रॉनों की संख्या बढ़ने के साथ धात्विक बंध की प्रबलता बढ़ती है और \(d^5\) (\(\mathrm{Cr}, \mathrm{Mo}, \mathrm{W}\)) के निकट अधिकतम होती है।
  • \(\mathrm{Mn}\) और \(\mathrm{Tc}\) में गिरावट: यद्यपि \(\mathrm{Mn}\) में पाँच अयुग्मित \(3d\) इलेक्ट्रॉन होते हैं, परंतु इसका स्थायी अर्ध-पूरित \(3d^5\) उपकोश इलेक्ट्रॉनों को दृढ़ता से बाँधे रखता है, जिससे धात्विक बंध दुर्बल हो जाता है और गलनांक तथा \(\Delta_a H^\circ\) में असामान्य गिरावट आती है।
  • वर्ग 12 में न्यूनतम मान: \(\mathrm{Zn}, \mathrm{Cd}, \mathrm{Hg}\) में \(d^{10}\) विन्यास के कारण कोई अयुग्मित \(d\) इलेक्ट्रॉन नहीं होता; अतः इनका धात्विक आबंधन दुर्बल होता है, \(\Delta_a H^\circ\) न्यूनतम होती है (जैसे \(\mathrm{Zn} = 126\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)), और \(\mathrm{Hg}\) कमरे के ताप पर द्रव है।

3.2 Atomic & Ionic Radii and Lanthanoid Contraction

Across the \(3d\) series, metallic radii decrease from \(\mathrm{Sc}\) (\(164\,\mathrm{pm}\)) to \(\mathrm{Ni}\) (\(125\,\mathrm{pm}\)) due to increasing effective nuclear charge (\(Z_{\text{eff}}\)) and poor shielding by \(3d\) electrons, followed by a slight increase at \(\mathrm{Cu}\) (\(128\,\mathrm{pm}\)) and \(\mathrm{Zn}\) (\(137\,\mathrm{pm}\)) caused by inter-electronic repulsion in the filled \(3d^{10}\) shell. Density increases steadily from \(\mathrm{Sc}\) (\(3.43\,\mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}\)) to \(\mathrm{Cu}\) (\(8.9\,\mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}\)).

Down a group, radii increase from \(3d\) to \(4d\), but \(4d\) and \(5d\) elements have virtually identical radii (e.g., \(\mathrm{Zr} = 160\,\mathrm{pm}\) and \(\mathrm{Hf} = 159\,\mathrm{pm}\)). This occurs because \(14\) electrons enter the \(4f\) subshell before the \(5d\) series begins. Because \(4f\) electrons shield nuclear charge very poorly, the resulting lanthanoid contraction cancels out the expected shell-size increase, making pairs like \(\mathrm{Zr}/\mathrm{Hf}\) and \(\mathrm{Nb}/\mathrm{Ta}\) chemically very similar and difficult to separate.

3.3 Ionisation Enthalpies (\(IE\))

  • First ionisation enthalpies (\(IE_1\)) increase gradually across the \(3d\) series, much less steeply than in \(s\)- or \(p\)-block periods, because inner \(3d\) electrons partially shield the outer \(4s\) electrons.
  • \(IE_2\) Anomalies: \(\mathrm{Cr}\) and \(\mathrm{Cu}\) have unusually high \(IE_2\) because the second electron must be removed from stable \(3d^5\) (\(\mathrm{Cr}^+\)) and \(3d^{10}\) (\(\mathrm{Cu}^+\)) configurations.
  • \(IE_3\) Anomalies: \(\mathrm{Mn}\) has a very high \(IE_3\) (removing an electron from stable \(3d^5\) of \(\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}\)), whereas \(\mathrm{Fe}\) has a comparatively lower \(IE_3\) because \(\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}\) (\(3d^6\)) readily loses an electron to attain stable \(3d^5\) (\(\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}\)). High \(IE_3\) values of \(\mathrm{Ni}, \mathrm{Cu}, \mathrm{Zn}\) make oxidation states above \(+2\) rare for them.

3.2 परमाण्विक व आयनिक त्रिज्या तथा लैन्थेनॉयड संकुचन

\(3d\) श्रेणी में बाएँ से दाएँ जाने पर प्रभावी नाभिकीय आवेश (\(Z_{\text{eff}}\)) बढ़ने और \(3d\) इलेक्ट्रॉनों के दुर्बल परिरक्षण के कारण धात्विक त्रिज्या \(\mathrm{Sc}\) (\(164\,\mathrm{pm}\)) से \(\mathrm{Ni}\) (\(125\,\mathrm{pm}\)) तक घटती है। इसके बाद \(\mathrm{Cu}\) (\(128\,\mathrm{pm}\)) और \(\mathrm{Zn}\) (\(137\,\mathrm{pm}\)) में पूर्ण-पूरित \(3d^{10}\) इलेक्ट्रॉनों के मध्य प्रतिकर्षण के कारण त्रिज्या में थोड़ी वृद्धि होती है। घनत्व \(\mathrm{Sc}\) (\(3.43\,\mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}\)) से \(\mathrm{Cu}\) (\(8.9\,\mathrm{g\,cm^{-3}}\)) तक लगातार बढ़ता है।

वर्ग में नीचे जाने पर \(3d\) से \(4d\) तक त्रिज्या बढ़ती है, किंतु \(4d\) और \(5d\) तत्वों की त्रिज्याएँ लगभग समान होती हैं (जैसे \(\mathrm{Zr} = 160\,\mathrm{pm}\) तथा \(\mathrm{Hf} = 159\,\mathrm{pm}\))। ऐसा \(5d\) श्रेणी से पूर्व \(4f\) कक्षकों में \(14\) इलेक्ट्रॉनों के भरने के कारण होता है। \(4f\) इलेक्ट्रॉनों का परिरक्षण प्रभाव अत्यंत दुर्बल होने से उत्पन्न लैन्थेनॉयड संकुचन संभावित आकार-वृद्धि को संतुलित कर देता है, जिससे \(\mathrm{Zr}/\mathrm{Hf}\) और \(\mathrm{Nb}/\mathrm{Ta}\) जैसे युग्मों के गुण अत्यंत समान हो जाते हैं।

3.3 आयनन एन्थैल्पी (\(IE\))

  • \(3d\) श्रेणी में प्रथम आयनन एन्थैल्पी (\(IE_1\)) धीरे-धीरे बढ़ती है (मुख्य वर्ग तत्वों की तुलना में बहुत कम तीव्रता से), क्योंकि आंतरिक \(3d\) इलेक्ट्रॉन बाह्य \(4s\) इलेक्ट्रॉनों को आंशिक रूप से परिरक्षित करते हैं।
  • \(IE_2\) अपवाद: \(\mathrm{Cr}\) और \(\mathrm{Cu}\) की \(IE_2\) असामान्य रूप से उच्च होती है क्योंकि दूसरा इलेक्ट्रॉन क्रमशः स्थायी \(3d^5\) (\(\mathrm{Cr}^+\)) और \(3d^{10}\) (\(\mathrm{Cu}^+\)) विन्यास से निकालना पड़ता है।
  • \(IE_3\) अपवाद: \(\mathrm{Mn}\) की \(IE_3\) बहुत उच्च होती है (स्थायी \(3d^5\) \(\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}\) से इलेक्ट्रॉन निकालने के कारण), जबकि \(\mathrm{Fe}\) की \(IE_3\) अपेक्षाकृत कम होती है क्योंकि \(\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}\) (\(3d^6\)) आसानी से एक इलेक्ट्रॉन त्यागकर स्थायी \(3d^5\) (\(\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}\)) बना लेता है।

4. Oxidation States, Electrode Potentials & Stability / ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाएँ, इलेक्ट्रोड विभव एवं स्थायित्व

English

4.1 Variable Oxidation States

Because \((n-1)d\) and \(ns\) orbitals have comparable energies, transition metals show multiple oxidation states that differ by one unit (e.g., \(\mathrm{V}^{2+}, \mathrm{V}^{3+}, \mathrm{V}^{4+}, \mathrm{V}^{5+}\)), unlike \(p\)-block elements where states typically differ by two units. In the \(3d\) series, \(\mathrm{Mn}\) shows the maximum number of oxidation states (from \(+2\) to \(+7\)), while \(\mathrm{Sc}\) shows only \(+3\) and \(\mathrm{Zn}\) shows only \(+2\).

  • Group Trend Contrast with \(p\)-Block: In the \(p\)-block, lower oxidation states become more stable down a group (inert pair effect). In the \(d\)-block, higher oxidation states become more stable down a group; hence \(\mathrm{Mo(VI)}\) and \(\mathrm{W(VI)}\) are far more stable than \(\mathrm{Cr(VI)}\), making \(\mathrm{Cr_2O_7^{2-}}\) a strong oxidising agent whereas \(\mathrm{MoO_3}\) and \(\mathrm{WO_3}\) are not.
  • Zero Oxidation State: Formed with \(\pi\)-acceptor ligands such as \(\mathrm{CO}\) in \(\mathrm{Ni(CO)_4}\) and \(\mathrm{Fe(CO)_5}\).

4.2 Standard Electrode Potentials: \(E^\circ(\mathrm{M}^{2+}/\mathrm{M})\) and \(E^\circ(\mathrm{M}^{3+}/\mathrm{M}^{2+})\)

The transformation \(\mathrm{M(s)} \to \mathrm{M^{2+}(aq)}\) depends on three thermodynamic terms: enthalpy of atomisation (\(\Delta_a H^\circ\), endothermic), sum of \(IE_1 + IE_2\) (endothermic), and hydration enthalpy (\(\Delta_{\text{hyd}} H^\circ\), exothermic).

  • Why Copper has a positive \(E^\circ(\mathrm{Cu^{2+}/Cu}) = +0.34\,\mathrm{V}\): Its high \(\Delta_a H^\circ\) and very high \(IE_1 + IE_2\) are not compensated by its \(\Delta_{\text{hyd}} H^\circ\). Therefore, \(\mathrm{Cu}\) does not liberate \(\mathrm{H_2}\) from dilute acids; it reacts only with oxidising acids (\(\mathrm{HNO_3}\), hot conc. \(\mathrm{H_2SO_4}\)).
  • More negative \(E^\circ\) than expected: \(\mathrm{Mn}\) (\(-1.18\,\mathrm{V}\)) due to stable \(3d^5\) in \(\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}\); \(\mathrm{Zn}\) (\(-0.76\,\mathrm{V}\)) due to stable \(3d^{10}\) in \(\mathrm{Zn}^{2+}\); \(\mathrm{Ni}\) (\(-0.25\,\mathrm{V}\)) due to its highly negative \(\Delta_{\text{hyd}} H^\circ\).
  • \(\mathrm{Cr}^{2+}\) vs \(\mathrm{Mn}^{3+}\) (both \(d^4\)): \(\mathrm{Cr}^{2+}\) is a strong reducing agent (\(E^\circ_{\mathrm{Cr^{3+}/Cr^{2+}}} = -0.41\,\mathrm{V}\)) because it oxidises to \(\mathrm{Cr}^{3+}\) (\(d^3\), stable half-filled \(t_{2g}^3\) level in aqueous octahedral complexes). Conversely, \(\mathrm{Mn}^{3+}\) is a strong oxidising agent (\(E^\circ_{\mathrm{Mn^{3+}/Mn^{2+}}} = +1.57\,\mathrm{V}\)) because it reduces to stable half-filled \(d^5\) (\(\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}\)).
  • Disproportionation of \(\mathrm{Cu}^+(\mathrm{aq})\): \(\mathrm{Cu}^+\) is unstable in water and disproportionates: \[2\mathrm{Cu^+}(\mathrm{aq}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Cu^{2+}}(\mathrm{aq}) + \mathrm{Cu(s)}\] because the much more negative \(\Delta_{\text{hyd}} H^\circ\) of \(\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}(\mathrm{aq})\) more than compensates for the high \(IE_2\) of \(\mathrm{Cu}\).

4.3 Halides, Oxides & Stability of Highest Oxidation States

Highest oxidation states are stabilised only by small, highly electronegative atoms—fluorine (high lattice or covalent bond enthalpy, e.g., \(\mathrm{VF_5}, \mathrm{CrF_6}, \mathrm{CoF_3}\)) and oxygen (ability to form \(p\pi\text{–}d\pi\) multiple bonds). Oxygen is superior to fluorine: the highest Mn fluoride is \(\mathrm{MnF_4}\), whereas the highest Mn oxide is \(\mathrm{Mn_2O_7}\) (containing a \(\mathrm{Mn-O-Mn}\) bridge). Note that \(\mathrm{CuI_2}\) does not exist because \(\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}\) oxidises \(\mathrm{I}^-\) to \(\mathrm{I_2}\): \(2\mathrm{Cu}^{2+} + 4\mathrm{I}^- \rightarrow \mathrm{Cu_2I_2(s)} + \mathrm{I_2}\).

Oxide Acid-Base Trend: As oxidation state increases, ionic character decreases and acidic character increases—e.g., \(\mathrm{V_2O_3}\) (basic) \(\to\) \(\mathrm{V_2O_4}\) (less basic) \(\to\) \(\mathrm{V_2O_5}\) (amphoteric, mainly acidic); \(\mathrm{CrO}\) (basic) \(\to\) \(\mathrm{Cr_2O_3}\) (amphoteric) \(\to\) \(\mathrm{CrO_3}\) (acidic); \(\mathrm{Mn_2O_7}\) is a covalent green oil giving \(\mathrm{HMnO_4}\).

4.1 परिवर्ती ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाएँ

\((n-1)d\) और \(ns\) कक्षकों की ऊर्जा लगभग समान होने के कारण संक्रमण धातुएँ ऐसी अनेक ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाएँ दर्शाती हैं जिनमें एक इकाई का अंतर होता है (जैसे \(\mathrm{V}^{2+}, \mathrm{V}^{3+}, \mathrm{V}^{4+}, \mathrm{V}^{5+}\)), जबकि \(p\)-ब्लॉक में यह अंतर प्रायः दो इकाई का होता है। \(3d\) श्रेणी में \(\mathrm{Mn}\) सर्वाधिक ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाएँ (\(+2\) से \(+7\)) दर्शाता है, जबकि \(\mathrm{Sc}\) केवल \(+3\) तथा \(\mathrm{Zn}\) केवल \(+2\) दर्शाता है।

  • \(p\)-ब्लॉक से विपरीत वर्ग-प्रवृत्ति: \(p\)-ब्लॉक में वर्ग में नीचे जाने पर निम्न ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाएँ अधिक स्थायी होती हैं (अक्रिय युग्म प्रभाव), किंतु \(d\)-ब्लॉक में वर्ग में नीचे जाने पर उच्च ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाएँ अधिक स्थायी होती हैं। इसी कारण \(\mathrm{Mo(VI)}\) तथा \(\mathrm{W(VI)}\), \(\mathrm{Cr(VI)}\) की तुलना में अधिक स्थायी हैं (अतः \(\mathrm{Cr_2O_7^{2-}}\) प्रबल ऑक्सीकारक है, जबकि \(\mathrm{MoO_3}\) और \(\mathrm{WO_3}\) नहीं)।
  • शून्य ऑक्सीकरण अवस्था: \(\pi\)-ग्राही लिगैंड जैसे \(\mathrm{CO}\) के साथ \(\mathrm{Ni(CO)_4}\) और \(\mathrm{Fe(CO)_5}\) में पाई जाती है।

4.2 मानक इलेक्ट्रोड विभव: \(E^\circ(\mathrm{M}^{2+}/\mathrm{M})\) एवं \(E^\circ(\mathrm{M}^{3+}/\mathrm{M}^{2+})\)

\(\mathrm{M(s)} \to \mathrm{M^{2+}(aq)}\) रूपांतरण तीन ऊष्मागतिक कारकों पर निर्भर करता है: कणन एन्थैल्पी (\(\Delta_a H^\circ\)), \(IE_1 + IE_2\) का योग, तथा जलयोजन एन्थैल्पी (\(\Delta_{\text{hyd}} H^\circ\))।

  • कॉपर का धनात्मक \(E^\circ(\mathrm{Cu^{2+}/Cu}) = +0.34\,\mathrm{V}\) क्यों है: इसकी उच्च \(\Delta_a H^\circ\) और अत्यधिक \(IE_1 + IE_2\) की क्षतिपूर्ति इसकी \(\Delta_{\text{hyd}} H^\circ\) से नहीं हो पाती। अतः \(\mathrm{Cu}\) तनु अम्लों से \(\mathrm{H_2}\) विस्थापित नहीं करता, बल्कि केवल ऑक्सीकारक अम्लों (\(\mathrm{HNO_3}\), गर्म सांद्र \(\mathrm{H_2SO_4}\)) से अभिक्रिया करता है।
  • अपेक्षा से अधिक ऋणात्मक \(E^\circ\): \(\mathrm{Mn}\) (\(-1.18\,\mathrm{V}\)) स्थायी \(3d^5\) के कारण; \(\mathrm{Zn}\) (\(-0.76\,\mathrm{V}\)) स्थायी \(3d^{10}\) के कारण; तथा \(\mathrm{Ni}\) (\(-0.25\,\mathrm{V}\)) अत्यधिक ऋणात्मक \(\Delta_{\text{hyd}} H^\circ\) के कारण।
  • \(\mathrm{Cr}^{2+}\) बनाम \(\mathrm{Mn}^{3+}\) (दोनों \(d^4\)): \(\mathrm{Cr}^{2+}\) प्रबल अपचायक है (\(E^\circ_{\mathrm{Cr^{3+}/Cr^{2+}}} = -0.41\,\mathrm{V}\)) क्योंकि यह \(\mathrm{Cr}^{3+}\) (\(d^3\), जलीय अष्टफलकीय संकुल में स्थायी अर्ध-पूरित \(t_{2g}^3\)) में ऑक्सीकृत हो जाता है। इसके विपरीत, \(\mathrm{Mn}^{3+}\) प्रबल ऑक्सीकारक है (\(E^\circ_{\mathrm{Mn^{3+}/Mn^{2+}}} = +1.57\,\mathrm{V}\)) क्योंकि यह स्थायी \(d^5\) (\(\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}\)) में अपचयित होता है।
  • \(\mathrm{Cu}^+(\mathrm{aq})\) का असमानुपातन: जलीय विलयन में \(\mathrm{Cu}^+\) अस्थायी होकर असमानुपातन दर्शाता है: \[2\mathrm{Cu^+}(\mathrm{aq}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Cu^{2+}}(\mathrm{aq}) + \mathrm{Cu(s)}\] क्योंकि \(\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}(\mathrm{aq})\) की अत्यधिक ऋणात्मक \(\Delta_{\text{hyd}} H^\circ\), \(\mathrm{Cu}\) की उच्च \(IE_2\) की क्षतिपूर्ति से भी अधिक होती है।

4.3 हैलाइड, ऑक्साइड एवं उच्च ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाओं का स्थायित्व

उच्चतम ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाएँ केवल छोटे, उच्च विद्युतऋणात्मक तत्वों—फ्लोरीन (उच्च जालक/सहसंयोजक बंध एन्थैल्पी, जैसे \(\mathrm{VF_5}, \mathrm{CrF_6}, \mathrm{CoF_3}\)) तथा ऑक्सीजन (\(p\pi\text{–}d\pi\) बहुबंध बनाने की क्षमता)—द्वारा ही स्थायी होती हैं। ऑक्सीजन, फ्लोरीन से श्रेष्ठ है: \(\mathrm{Mn}\) का उच्चतम फ्लोराइड \(\mathrm{MnF_4}\) है, जबकि उच्चतम ऑक्साइड \(\mathrm{Mn_2O_7}\) है (जिसमें \(\mathrm{Mn-O-Mn}\) सेतु होता है)। \(\mathrm{CuI_2}\) अस्तित्व में नहीं है क्योंकि \(\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}\), \(\mathrm{I}^-\) को \(\mathrm{I_2}\) में ऑक्सीकृत कर देता है: \(2\mathrm{Cu}^{2+} + 4\mathrm{I}^- \rightarrow \mathrm{Cu_2I_2(s)} + \mathrm{I_2}\)।

ऑक्साइडों की अम्ल-क्षार प्रवृत्ति: ऑक्सीकरण अंक बढ़ने पर आयनिक लक्षण घटता है और अम्लीय प्रकृति बढ़ती है—जैसे \(\mathrm{V_2O_3}\) (क्षारीय) \(\to\) \(\mathrm{V_2O_4}\) (कम क्षारीय) \(\to\) \(\mathrm{V_2O_5}\) (उभयधर्मी/मुख्यतः अम्लीय); \(\mathrm{CrO}\) (क्षारीय) \(\to\) \(\mathrm{Cr_2O_3}\) (उभयधर्मी) \(\to\) \(\mathrm{CrO_3}\) (अम्लीय)।

5. Magnetic Behaviour, Colour, Catalysis & Materials / चुंबकीय व्यवहार, रंग, उत्प्रेरण एवं अन्य गुण

English
  • Paramagnetism & Spin-Only Magnetic Moment: In \(3d\) ions, orbital angular momentum is quenched, so the magnetic moment depends solely on the number of unpaired electrons \(n\):
    \[\mu = \sqrt{n(n+2)}\;\mathrm{BM}\]
    Diamagnetic ions have \(n = 0\) (\(d^0\) or \(d^{10}\)), e.g., \(\mathrm{Sc}^{3+}, \mathrm{Ti}^{4+}, \mathrm{Cu}^+, \mathrm{Zn}^{2+}\). Maximum spin-only moment in \(3d\) ions is \(5.92\,\mathrm{BM}\) for \(d^5\) (\(\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}, \mathrm{Fe}^{3+}\), \(n=5\)).
  • Coloured Ions: Arises from \(d\text{–}d\) electronic transitions within split \(d\) orbitals by absorbing visible light; the observed colour is complementary to the absorbed colour. Ions with \(d^0\) (\(\mathrm{Sc}^{3+}, \mathrm{Ti}^{4+}\)) or \(d^{10}\) (\(\mathrm{Cu}^+, \mathrm{Zn}^{2+}\)) are colourless. Note: \(\mathrm{MnO_4^-}\) (purple) and \(\mathrm{Cr_2O_7^{2-}}\) (orange) are intensely coloured despite \(d^0\) configuration due to ligand-to-metal charge transfer.
  • Complex Formation: Transition ions readily form complexes (e.g., \([\mathrm{Fe(CN)_6}]^{4-}, [\mathrm{Cu(NH_3)_4}]^{2+}\)) due to small ionic size, high charge density, and availability of vacant \(d\) orbitals.
  • Catalytic Activity: Due to variable oxidation states and ability to adsorb reactants on surface, lowering activation energy. Key examples: \(\mathrm{V_2O_5}\) (Contact Process for \(\mathrm{H_2SO_4}\)), finely divided \(\mathrm{Fe}\) (Haber's Process for \(\mathrm{NH_3}\)), \(\mathrm{Ni}\) (hydrogenation), \(\mathrm{TiCl_4 + Al(CH_3)_3}\) (Ziegler–Natta catalyst for polythene), \(\mathrm{PdCl_2}\) (Wacker process), and \(\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}\) catalyzing \(2\mathrm{I}^- + \mathrm{S_2O_8^{2-}} \rightarrow \mathrm{I_2} + 2\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}\).
  • Interstitial Compounds: Small atoms (\(\mathrm{H, C, N}\)) occupy interstitial voids in metal lattices (e.g., \(\mathrm{TiC}, \mathrm{Mn_4N}, \mathrm{VH_{0.56}}\)). They are non-stoichiometric, very hard, chemically inert, have higher melting points than pure metals, and retain metallic conductivity.
  • Alloys: Formed readily because transition metals have similar atomic radii (within \(\approx 15\%\)), e.g., ferrous alloys (steels with \(\mathrm{Cr, V, W, Mo, Mn}\)), brass (\(\mathrm{Cu\text{–}Zn}\)), and bronze (\(\mathrm{Cu\text{–}Sn}\)).

6.1 Potassium Dichromate (\(\mathrm{K_2Cr_2O_7}\))

Preparation from Chromite Ore (\(\mathrm{FeCr_2O_4}\)):

  1. Roasting with \(\mathrm{Na_2CO_3}\) in air: \(4\mathrm{FeCr_2O_4} + 8\mathrm{Na_2CO_3} + 7\mathrm{O_2} \rightarrow 8\mathrm{Na_2CrO_4}\text{ (yellow)} + 2\mathrm{Fe_2O_3} + 8\mathrm{CO_2}\).

  3. Metathesis with \(\mathrm{KCl}\): \(\mathrm{Na_2Cr_2O_7} + 2\mathrm{KCl} \rightarrow \mathrm{K_2Cr_2O_7} + 2\mathrm{NaCl}\) (\(\mathrm{K_2Cr_2O_7}\) is less soluble and crystallises out).

Oxidising Action (Acidic Medium, \(E^\circ = +1.33\,\mathrm{V}\)): \(\mathrm{Cr_2O_7^{2-}} + 14\mathrm{H}^+ + 6\mathrm{e}^- \rightarrow 2\mathrm{Cr}^{3+} + 7\mathrm{H_2O}\). One mole of \(\mathrm{Cr_2O_7^{2-}}\) (\(n\)-factor \(= 6\)) oxidises \(6\,\mathrm{mol}\;\mathrm{Fe}^{2+} \to \mathrm{Fe}^{3+}\), \(6\,\mathrm{mol}\;\mathrm{I}^- \to 3\,\mathrm{mol}\;\mathrm{I_2}\), \(3\,\mathrm{mol}\;\mathrm{H_2S} \to 3\,\mathrm{mol}\;\mathrm{S}\), and \(3\,\mathrm{mol}\;\mathrm{Sn}^{2+} \to \mathrm{Sn}^{4+}\).

6.2 Potassium Permanganate (\(\mathrm{KMnO_4}\))

Preparation from Pyrolusite (\(\mathrm{MnO_2}\)):

  1. Alkaline oxidative fusion: \(2\mathrm{MnO_2} + 4\mathrm{KOH} + \mathrm{O_2} \rightarrow 2\mathrm{K_2MnO_4}\text{ (dark green)} + 2\mathrm{H_2O}\).

  3. Laboratory oxidation of \(\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}\) by peroxodisulphate: \(2\mathrm{Mn}^{2+} + 5\mathrm{S_2O_8^{2-}} + 8\mathrm{H_2O} \rightarrow 2\mathrm{MnO_4^-} + 10\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}} + 16\mathrm{H}^+\).

Properties: \(\mathrm{KMnO_4}\) is isostructural with \(\mathrm{KClO_4}\). On heating at \(513\,\mathrm{K}\), it decomposes: \(2\mathrm{KMnO_4} \rightarrow \mathrm{K_2MnO_4} + \mathrm{MnO_2} + \mathrm{O_2}\). Note that green \(\mathrm{MnO_4^{2-}}\) (\(d^1\)) is paramagnetic (1 unpaired electron), whereas purple \(\mathrm{MnO_4^-}\) (\(d^0\)) is diamagnetic (with weak temperature-independent paramagnetism).

Medium-Dependent Oxidising Action:

  • Acidic medium (\(n\)-factor \(= 5\), \(E^\circ = +1.52\,\mathrm{V}\)): \(\mathrm{MnO_4^-} + 8\mathrm{H}^+ + 5\mathrm{e}^- \rightarrow \mathrm{Mn}^{2+} + 4\mathrm{H_2O}\).   • Neutral / faintly alkaline medium (\(n\)-factor \(= 3\), \(E^\circ = +1.69\,\mathrm{V}\)): \(\mathrm{MnO_4^-} + 2\mathrm{H_2O} + 3\mathrm{e}^- \rightarrow \mathrm{MnO_2} + 4\mathrm{OH}^-\). English

7.1 The Lanthanoids (\(4f\) Series: \(\mathrm{Ce, Z=58}\) to \(\mathrm{Lu, Z=71}\))

  • Electronic Configuration: General form \([\mathrm{Xe}]\,4f^{1\text{–}14}5d^{0\text{–}1}6s^2\). Only \(\mathrm{La}\) (\(5d^1 6s^2\)), \(\mathrm{Ce}\) (\(4f^1 5d^1 6s^2\)), \(\mathrm{Gd}\) (\(4f^7 5d^1 6s^2\)), and \(\mathrm{Lu}\) (\(4f^{14} 5d^1 6s^2\)) have a \(5d^1\) electron; all \(\mathrm{Ln}^{3+}\) ions have clean \(4f^n\) (\(n=0\text{–}14\)) configurations.
  • Oxidation States: \(+3\) is predominant for all. Exceptions arise from \(f^0, f^7, f^{14}\) stability: \(\mathrm{Ce}^{4+}\) (\(4f^0\), strong oxidant, \(E^\circ = +1.74\,\mathrm{V}\)) and \(\mathrm{Tb}^{4+}\) (\(4f^7\), oxidant); \(\mathrm{Eu}^{2+}\) (\(4f^7\), strong reductant) and \(\mathrm{Yb}^{2+}\) (\(4f^{14}\), reductant).
  • Lanthanoid Contraction: Steady decrease in \(\mathrm{Ln}^{3+}\) radii from \(\mathrm{La}^{3+}\) (\(106\,\mathrm{pm}\)) to \(\mathrm{Lu}^{3+}\) (\(85\,\mathrm{pm}\)) due to poor \(4f\) shielding. Consequences: (i) identical radii of \(4d/5d\) pairs (\(\mathrm{Zr/Hf}\)), (ii) difficulty in separating lanthanoids, and (iii) decreasing basicity of \(\mathrm{Ln(OH)_3}\) from \(\mathrm{La(OH)_3}\) (most basic) to \(\mathrm{Lu(OH)_3}\) (least basic).
  • Alloy: Mischmetall (\(\approx 95\%\) lanthanoid metal, \(\approx 5\%\) \(\mathrm{Fe}\), traces of \(\mathrm{S, C, Ca, Al}\)) is used in Mg-based alloys for bullets, shells, and lighter flints.

7.2 The Actinoids (\(5f\) Series: \(\mathrm{Th, Z=90}\) to \(\mathrm{Lr, Z=103}\))

  • All actinoids are radioactive. Because \(5f, 6d,\) and \(7s\) orbitals have very similar energies, early actinoids display a much wider range of oxidation states (up to \(+7\) in \(\mathrm{Np}\) and \(\mathrm{Pu}\)), though \(+3\) is common.
  • Actinoid Contraction: Greater from element to element than lanthanoid contraction because \(5f\) electrons provide even poorer shielding than \(4f\) electrons.

8. Original Worked Examples (NEET UG Level) / मौलिक हल किए गए उदाहरण (NEET UG स्तर)

Worked Example 1: Spin-Only Magnetic Moment & Colour / हल उदाहरण 1: प्रचक्रण-मात्र चुंबकीय आघूर्ण एवं रंग
English

Problem: A hypothetical coordination compound contains a trivalent metal ion \(\mathrm{M}^{3+}\) of the \(3d\) series with atomic number \(Z = 24\). Determine its \(d\)-electron count, spin-only magnetic moment, and whether its aqueous solution is coloured.

Solution:

  1. Atomic number \(Z = 24\) corresponds to Chromium (\(\mathrm{Cr}\)), whose ground-state configuration is \([\mathrm{Ar}]\,3d^5 4s^1\).
  2. Removing 3 electrons (first the \(4s^1\) electron, then two \(3d\) electrons) gives \(\mathrm{Cr}^{3+}\): \([\mathrm{Ar}]\,3d^3\).
  3. Number of unpaired electrons \(n = 3\). Therefore, \[\mu = \sqrt{3(3+2)} = \sqrt{15} \approx 3.87\;\mathrm{BM}\]
  4. Because the \(3d\) subshell is incompletely filled (\(3d^3\)), \(d\text{–}d\) transitions occur in the visible region, so its aqueous solution is coloured (violet).

Worked Example 3: Lanthanoid Configuration & Redox Behaviour / हल उदाहरण 3: लैन्थेनॉयड विन्यास एवं रेडॉक्स व्यवहार
English

Problem: Europium (\(\mathrm{Eu}, Z = 63\)) forms a stable \(+2\) ion in solid compounds, yet \(\mathrm{Eu}^{2+}(\mathrm{aq})\) acts as a strong reducing agent. Explain using its electronic configuration.

Solution: Neutral \(\mathrm{Eu}\) (\(Z = 63\)) has the configuration \([\mathrm{Xe}]\,4f^7 6s^2\). Losing the two \(6s\) electrons yields \(\mathrm{Eu}^{2+}\) with a half-filled \(4f^7\) configuration, which imparts extra stability. However, the universal stable oxidation state for lanthanoids in aqueous solution is \(+3\), so \(\mathrm{Eu}^{2+}\) readily loses an electron to become \(\mathrm{Eu}^{3+}\), making \(\mathrm{Eu}^{2+}\) a strong reducing agent.

10. Common Pitfalls, Revision Checklist & Final Recap / सामान्य त्रुटियाँ, पुनरावृत्ति सूची एवं सार

English
Common NEET UG Pitfalls to Avoid
  • Forgetting \(4s\)-first ionisation: Never remove \(3d\) electrons before \(4s\) when writing \(\mathrm{M}^{2+}\) or \(\mathrm{M}^{3+}\) configurations (e.g., \(\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}\) is \(3d^6\), not \(3d^4 4s^2\)).
  • Confusing \(\mathrm{MnO_4^{2-}}\) and \(\mathrm{MnO_4^-}\): Green manganate (\(\mathrm{MnO_4^{2-}}\)) is paramagnetic (\(d^1\)); purple permanganate (\(\mathrm{MnO_4^-}\)) is diamagnetic (\(d^0\)).
  • Ignoring medium in \(\mathrm{KMnO_4} + \mathrm{I}^-\): Acidic medium gives \(\mathrm{I_2}\) (\(n\)-factor 5 for \(\mathrm{MnO_4^-}\)); neutral/faintly alkaline medium gives \(\mathrm{IO_3^-}\) (\(n\)-factor 3 for \(\mathrm{MnO_4^-}\)).
  • Mixing up \(p\)-block vs \(d\)-block group trends: Down a \(d\)-block group, higher oxidation states become more stable (\(\mathrm{W(VI)} > \mathrm{Cr(VI)}\)).

Final Recap

Transition elements possess incomplete \(d\) subshells in the atom or common ions, giving rise to high melting points (dip at \(\mathrm{Mn}/\mathrm{Tc}\), minimum at \(\mathrm{Zn}/\mathrm{Cd}/\mathrm{Hg}\)), variable oxidation states differing by 1 unit, paramagnetism (\(\mu = \sqrt{n(n+2)}\;\mathrm{BM}\)), coloured ions, complexes, catalysts, interstitials, and alloys. \(\mathrm{K_2Cr_2O_7}\) and \(\mathrm{KMnO_4}\) are powerful tetrahedral oxidants whose reactivity depends sharply on pH. In the \(f\)-block, poor \(4f\) and \(5f\) shielding causes lanthanoid and actinoid contractions, while actinoids show wider oxidation states and radioactivity.

हिन्दी
NEET UG में बचने योग्य सामान्य त्रुटियाँ
  • \(4s\)-प्रथम आयनन नियम भूलना: \(\mathrm{M}^{2+}\) या \(\mathrm{M}^{3+}\) लिखते समय \(3d\) से पहले सदैव \(4s\) इलेक्ट्रॉन निकालें (जैसे \(\mathrm{Fe}^{2+} = 3d^6\), न कि \(3d^4 4s^2\))।
  • \(\mathrm{MnO_4^{2-}}\) और \(\mathrm{MnO_4^-}\) में भ्रम: हरा मैंगनेट (\(\mathrm{MnO_4^{2-}}\)) अनुचुंबकीय (\(d^1\)) है, जबकि बैंगनी परमैंगनेट (\(\mathrm{MnO_4^-}\)) प्रतिचुंबकीय (\(d^0\)) है।
  • \(\mathrm{KMnO_4} + \mathrm{I}^-\) में माध्यम की अनदेखी: अम्लीय माध्यम में \(\mathrm{I_2}\) (\(n\)-गुणक 5) तथा उदासीन/हल्के क्षारीय माध्यम में \(\mathrm{IO_3^-}\) (\(n\)-गुणक 3) बनता है।
  • \(p\)-ब्लॉक और \(d\)-ब्लॉक प्रवृत्तियों का मिश्रण: \(d\)-ब्लॉक में वर्ग में नीचे जाने पर उच्च ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाएँ अधिक स्थायी होती हैं (\(\mathrm{W(VI)} > \mathrm{Cr(VI)}\))।

अंतिम सार

संक्रमण तत्वों में परमाणु या सामान्य आयनों में अपूर्ण \(d\)-उपकोश होता है, जिसके कारण ये उच्च गलनांक (\(\mathrm{Mn}/\mathrm{Tc}\) में गिरावट, \(\mathrm{Zn}/\mathrm{Cd}/\mathrm{Hg}\) में न्यूनतम), 1 इकाई अंतर वाली परिवर्ती ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाएँ, अनुचुंबकत्व (\(\mu = \sqrt{n(n+2)}\;\mathrm{BM}\)), रंगीन आयन, संकुल, उत्प्रेरक, अंतराकाशी यौगिक तथा मिश्रधातु बनाते हैं। \(\mathrm{K_2Cr_2O_7}\) तथा \(\mathrm{KMnO_4}\) प्रबल चतुष्फलकीय ऑक्सीकारक हैं। \(f\)-ब्लॉक में \(4f\) व \(5f\) के दुर्बल परिरक्षण से लैन्थेनॉयड एवं ऐक्टिनॉयड संकुचन होता है, तथा ऐक्टिनॉयड अधिक ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाएँ एवं रेडियोएक्टिवता दर्शाते हैं।
