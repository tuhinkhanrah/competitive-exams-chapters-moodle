1. Early Models & Geiger-Marsden \(\alpha\)-Scattering Experiment

Thomson Model vs. Rutherford Scattering

J. J. Thomson's plum pudding model (1898) assumed positive charge was spread uniformly throughout a sphere of radius \(\sim 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{m}\) with electrons embedded like seeds in a watermelon. This model could not explain large-angle scattering or discrete atomic emission spectra.

In 1911, H. Geiger and E. Marsden (under Ernest Rutherford) directed a collimated beam of \(5.5\,\mathrm{MeV}\) \(\alpha\)-particles from a \(^{214}_{83}\mathrm{Bi}\) source onto a thin gold foil (thickness \(\approx 2.1 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}\) or \(\sim 400\) atoms). Scattered \(\alpha\)-particles produced tiny flashes of light on a rotatable zinc sulfide (\(\mathrm{ZnS}\)) screen observed through a microscope.

थॉमसन मॉडल एवं गाइगर-मार्सडेन प्रकीर्णन प्रयोग

थॉमसन का 'तरबूज मॉडल' बड़े कोणों पर प्रकीर्णन और रेखीय स्पेक्ट्रम समझाने में असमर्थ रहा। 1911 में गाइगर-मार्सडेन ने स्वर्ण पन्नी (\(2.1 \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{m}\)) पर \(5.5\,\mathrm{MeV}\) \(\alpha\)-कणों की बौछार की और घूर्णनशील \(\mathrm{ZnS}\) पर्दे द्वारा प्रकीर्णन का प्रेक्षण किया।

Apparatus Diagram: Geiger-Marsden \(\alpha\)-particle scattering apparatus showing collimator, gold foil, and rotating detector screen.

Key Observations & Rutherford Nuclear Hypothesis
  • >99.86% of \(\alpha\)-particles: Passed straight through with scattering angle \(\theta < 1^\circ\).
  • Only about 0.14%: Deflected by \(\theta > 1^\circ\).
  • Only 1 in 8,000: Deflected by \(\theta > 90^\circ\), and extremely rare particles bounced almost backwards (\(\theta \approx 180^\circ\)).

Rutherford's Deduction: The entire positive charge and >99.9% of mass is concentrated in a tiny central region called the nucleus (radius \(\sim 10^{-15}\,\mathrm{m}\) to \(10^{-14}\,\mathrm{m}\)), while electrons orbit in vast empty space (\(\sim 10^{-10}\,\mathrm{m}\)). The scattering distribution obeys:

\[ N(\theta) \propto \frac{1}{\sin^4(\theta/2)} \]
प्रेक्षण एवं नाभिकीय निष्कर्ष

\(99.86\%\) \(\alpha\)-कण सीधे निकल गए। केवल \(0.14\%\) कण \(1^\circ\) से अधिक विक्षेपित हुए तथा 8000 में से 1 कण \(90^\circ\) से अधिक कोण पर लौटा। निष्कर्ष: परमाणु का समस्त धनावेश तथा द्रव्यमान केंद्र में अतिसूक्ष्म नाभिक (\(10^{-15}\,\mathrm{m}\)) में केंद्रित है। प्रकीर्णित कणों की संख्या: \(N(\theta) \propto \frac{1}{\sin^4(\theta/2)}\)।

Scattering Plot: Number of scattered particles \(N(\theta)\) vs. scattering angle \(\theta\), verifying \(N(\theta) \propto 1/\sin^4(\theta/2)\).

2. Trajectory, Impact Parameter & Distance of Closest Approach

Impact Parameter (\(b\)) & Hyperbolic Trajectory

The impact parameter (\(b\)) is the perpendicular distance of the initial velocity vector of the \(\alpha\)-particle from the center of the target nucleus. The trajectory is a hyperbola with the nucleus at the outer focus:

\[ b = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \frac{Z e^2 \cot(\theta/2)}{K_\alpha} = \frac{Z e^2 \cot(\theta/2)}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 \left(\frac{1}{2}m v^2\right)} \]
  • For head-on collision (\(b = 0\)): \(\cot(\theta/2) = 0 \implies \theta = 180^\circ\). The \(\alpha\)-particle rebounds along its incoming path.
  • For large impact parameter (\(b \gg 0\)): \(\cot(\theta/2) \to \infty \implies \theta \approx 0^\circ\) (almost zero deflection).

Distance of Closest Approach (\(d\)): In a head-on collision (\(b=0\)), the particle comes to a momentary halt at distance \(d\). By conservation of mechanical energy (\(K_i = U_f\)):

\[ K_\alpha = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{(2e)(Ze)}{d} \implies d = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{2Ze^2}{K_\alpha} \]

For gold (\(Z = 79\)) and \(K_\alpha = 7.7\,\mathrm{MeV}\), \(d \approx 3.0 \times 10^{-14}\,\mathrm{m} = 30\,\mathrm{fm}\). This sets an upper limit on the nuclear radius: \(R_{\text{nucleus}} < d\).

संघट्ट प्राचल एवं निकटतम पहुँच की दूरी

संघट्ट प्राचल (\(b\)): नाभिक के केंद्र से \(\alpha\)-कण के प्रारंभिक वेग सदिश की लंबवत दूरी। सूत्र: \(b = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \frac{Ze^2 \cot(\theta/2)}{K_\alpha}\)। जब \(b=0\), तब \(\theta=180^\circ\)।

निकटतम पहुँच की दूरी (\(d\)): सम्मुख टक्कर में संपूर्ण गतिज ऊर्जा स्थितिज ऊर्जा में बदल जाती है: \(d = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{2Ze^2}{K_\alpha}\)। यह नाभिक के आकार की ऊपरी सीमा दर्शाती है (\(R_0 < d\))।

Trajectory Diagram: Hyperbolic paths of \(\alpha\)-particles for different impact parameters (\(b\)) and head-on distance of closest approach (\(d\)).

3. Rutherford's Planetary Model & Classical Instability Paradox

Planetary Orbital Mechanics & Energy Relations

Centripetal acceleration is supplied by electrostatic Coulomb attraction:

\[ \frac{m v^2}{r} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{Z e^2}{r^2} \implies K = \frac{1}{2}m v^2 = \frac{Z e^2}{8\pi\varepsilon_0 r} \]

Potential energy is \(U = -\frac{Z e^2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r}\). Thus, total mechanical energy is:

\[ E = K + U = -\frac{Z e^2}{8\pi\varepsilon_0 r} \quad \implies \quad E = -K = \frac{U}{2} \]

Failures of Classical Electrodynamics:

  1. Orbital Collapse (Instability): An accelerated orbiting charge radiates electromagnetic waves continuously (Maxwell's electrodynamics). Losing energy, its orbital radius must shrink, spiraling into the nucleus within \(\sim 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{s}\). Classically, stable atoms cannot exist!
  2. Continuous Spectrum Dilemma: A spiraling electron's orbital frequency increases continuously, predicting a continuous emission spectrum, directly contradicting observed sharp discrete spectral lines.
रदरफोर्ड मॉडल एवं चिरसम्मत अस्थायित्व विरोधाभास

कूलाम आकर्षण बल अभिकेंद्रीय बल देता है। ऊर्जा संबंध: \(K = \frac{Ze^2}{8\pi\varepsilon_0 r}\), \(U = -\frac{Ze^2}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r}\), तथा \(E = -K = \frac{U}{2}\)। ऋणात्मक कुल ऊर्जा इलेक्ट्रॉन के परिबद्ध होने का प्रमाण है।

चिरसम्मत विफलता: त्वरित इलेक्ट्रॉन विद्युत-चुंबकीय ऊर्जा उत्सर्जित करते हुए \(10^{-8}\,\mathrm{s}\) में सर्पिलाकार गति कर नाभिक में गिर जाना चाहिए। साथ ही यह सतत स्पेक्ट्रम की भविष्यवाणी करता है, जबकि स्पेक्ट्रम असतत (रेखीय) होता है।

Classical Collapse Diagram: Accelerating electron radiating electromagnetic waves and spiraling into the nucleus according to classical electrodynamics.

4. Bohr's Quantum Model for Hydrogen-like Atoms

Bohr's Three Postulates (1913)
  1. Stationary Orbits: Electrons revolve in certain stable, non-radiating orbits called stationary orbits.
  2. Quantization of Angular Momentum: Orbital angular momentum is quantized in units of \(h/(2\pi) = \hbar\):
    \[ L = m v_n r_n = \frac{n h}{2\pi}, \quad n = 1, 2, 3, \dots \]
  3. Frequency Condition: Radiation of frequency \(\nu\) is emitted or absorbed only during quantum transitions between stationary levels:
    \[ h\nu = E_i - E_f \implies \nu = \frac{E_i - E_f}{h} \]

Derived Formulae for Hydrogenic Species (Atomic Number \(Z\)):

  • Orbital Radius (\(r_n\)):
    \[ r_n = \frac{\varepsilon_0 n^2 h^2}{\pi m Z e^2} = \frac{n^2}{Z} a_0, \quad a_0 \approx 0.529\,\text{Å} = 0.0529\,\mathrm{nm} \]
  • Orbital Speed (\(v_n\)):
    \[ v_n = \frac{Z e^2}{2\varepsilon_0 n h} = \frac{Z}{n} v_1, \quad v_1 \approx \frac{c}{137} \approx 2.18 \times 10^6\,\mathrm{m/s} \]
  • Total Energy (\(E_n\)):
    \[ E_n = -\frac{m Z^2 e^4}{8 \varepsilon_0^2 h^2 n^2} = -13.6\,\mathrm{eV} \times \frac{Z^2}{n^2} \]
  • Time Period & Frequency: \(T_n \propto \frac{n^3}{Z^2}\) and \(f_n \propto \frac{Z^2}{n^3}\).
बोर के अभिगृहीत एवं व्युत्पत्तियाँ

अभिगृहीत: (1) स्थायी अविकिरणी कक्षाएँ; (2) कोणीय संवेग क्वाण्टीकरण \(mvr = \frac{nh}{2\pi}\); (3) आवृत्ति शर्त \(h\nu = E_i - E_f\)।

प्रमुख सूत्र: त्रिज्या \(r_n = \frac{n^2}{Z}a_0\) (\(a_0 \approx 0.529\,\text{Å}\)); चाल \(v_n = \frac{Z}{n}v_1\) (\(v_1 \approx c/137\)); कुल ऊर्जा \(E_n = -13.6\,\mathrm{eV}\frac{Z^2}{n^2}\); आवर्तकाल \(T_n \propto \frac{n^3}{Z^2}\)।

5. Energy Levels & Hydrogen Spectral Series

Rydberg Formula & Emission Series

When an electron transitions from outer orbit \(n_2\) to inner orbit \(n_1\) (\(n_2 > n_1\)), the emitted wavelength \(\lambda\) is given by:

\[ \frac{1}{\lambda} = R Z^2 \left( \frac{1}{n_1^2} - \frac{1}{n_2^2} \right), \quad R = \frac{m e^4}{8 \varepsilon_0^2 h^3 c} \approx 1.09737 \times 10^7\,\mathrm{m}^{-1} \]

Useful shortcut: \(1/R \approx 912\,\text{Å} = 91.2\,\mathrm{nm}\).

  • Longest Wavelength (\(\lambda_{\max}\)): Smallest \(\Delta E\) (\(n_2 = n_1 + 1\), first line / \(\alpha\)-line).
  • Shortest Wavelength (\(\lambda_{\min}\), Series Limit): Largest \(\Delta E\) (\(n_2 = \infty\)).
  • Number of Spectral Lines: On de-excitation from level \(n\) to ground state (\(n=1\)): \(N = \frac{n(n-1)}{2}\).

हाइड्रोजन वर्णक्रम श्रेणियाँ

रिडबर्ग सूत्र: \(\frac{1}{\lambda} = R Z^2 \left(\frac{1}{n_1^2} - \frac{1}{n_2^2}\right)\)। लाइमन पराबैंगनी में, बामर दृश्य क्षेत्र में, तथा पाश्चन, ब्रैकेट व फुंड अवरक्त क्षेत्र में होती हैं। स्तर \(n\) से मूल स्तर में संक्रमण पर कुल रेखाएँ: \(N = \frac{n(n-1)}{2}\)।

6. De Broglie's Matter Wave Explanation & Model Limitations

De Broglie Standing Wave Resonance (1923)

Louis de Broglie explained Bohr's angular momentum quantization by treating the electron as a matter wave (\(\lambda = h/p\)). For constructive standing wave interference along a circular orbit of radius \(r_n\), the circumference must fit an integral number of wavelengths:

\[ 2\pi r_n = n \lambda = n \left( \frac{h}{m v_n} \right) \implies m v_n r_n = \frac{n h}{2\pi} \]

Matter Wave Loop: Resonant circular standing electron wave with \(n=4\) wavelengths fitting along the circumference (\(2\pi r = 4\lambda\)).

Limitations of Bohr's Theory
  1. Single-Electron Only: Valid only for hydrogenic species (\(\mathrm{H}, \mathrm{He}^+, \mathrm{Li}^{2+}\)); fails for multi-electron atoms due to inter-electronic repulsion.
  2. Fine Structure: Cannot explain spectral line doublets observed under high resolution.
  3. Relative Intensities: Cannot predict transition probabilities or line brightness.
  4. Zeeman & Stark Effects: Fails to account for spectral splitting in magnetic (Zeeman) or electric (Stark) fields.
  5. Heisenberg Uncertainty: Violates \(\Delta x \Delta p \ge \hbar/2\) by assuming exact simultaneous orbital paths and momenta.
डी ब्रोग्ली व्याख्या एवं कमियाँ

डी ब्रोग्ली के अनुसार कक्षा एक संनादी अप्रगामी तरंग है: \(2\pi r_n = n\lambda = n\frac{h}{mv} \implies mvr = \frac{nh}{2\pi}\)।

सीमाएँ: केवल एकल-इलेक्ट्रॉन पर लागू; स्पेक्ट्रमी रेखाओं की सूक्ष्म संरचना, तीव्रता, जीमान प्रभाव तथा स्टार्क प्रभाव समझाने में असमर्थ; हाइजेनबर्ग अनिश्चितता सिद्धांत के प्रतिकूल।

7. High-Yield Worked Examples

Worked Example 1 • Distance of Closest Approach

Problem: An \(\alpha\)-particle of kinetic energy \(K\) approaches a gold nucleus (\(Z=79\)) head-on and reaches distance of closest approach \(d\). What is the new distance of closest approach if its kinetic energy is doubled (\(2K\))?

Solution: Since all kinetic energy converts to electrostatic potential energy at closest approach: \(K = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0}\frac{2Ze^2}{d} \implies d \propto \frac{1}{K}\). When kinetic energy is doubled (\(K' = 2K\)), the distance becomes \(d' = d/2\).

हल: सम्मुख टक्कर में \(d \propto \frac{1}{K}\)। अतः गतिज ऊर्जा दोगुनी करने पर निकटतम पहुँच दूरी आधी (\(d/2\)) हो जाती है।

Worked Example 2 • Ratio of Wavelengths

Problem: Calculate the ratio of the longest wavelength of the Balmer series to the shortest wavelength of the Lyman series in Hydrogen.

Solution:

  • Balmer \(\lambda_{\max}\) (\(n_1 = 2, n_2 = 3\)): \(\frac{1}{\lambda_{\text{B, max}}} = R\left(\frac{1}{4} - \frac{1}{9}\right) = \frac{5R}{36} \implies \lambda_{\text{B, max}} = \frac{36}{5R}\).
  • Lyman \(\lambda_{\min}\) (\(n_1 = 1, n_2 = \infty\)): \(\frac{1}{\lambda_{\text{L, min}}} = R\left(\frac{1}{1} - 0\right) = R \implies \lambda_{\text{L, min}} = \frac{1}{R}\).

Ratio: \(\frac{\lambda_{\text{B, max}}}{\lambda_{\text{L, min}}} = \frac{36/(5R)}{1/R} = \frac{36}{5} = 7.2\).

हल: बामर \(\lambda_{\max} = \frac{36}{5R}\), लाइमन \(\lambda_{\min} = \frac{1}{R}\)। अनुपात: \(\frac{36}{5} = 7.2\)।

Worked Example 3 • Frequency and Time Period

Problem: In a hydrogen atom, compare the orbital frequency in the second excited state (\(n=3\)) to that in the ground state (\(n=1\)).

Solution: Orbital frequency \(f = \frac{v}{2\pi r} \propto \frac{1/n}{n^2} = \frac{1}{n^3}\). For \(n=3\) relative to \(n=1\): \(\frac{f_3}{f_1} = \left(\frac{1}{3}\right)^3 = \frac{1}{27}\).

हल: आवृत्ति \(f \propto \frac{1}{n^3}\)। द्वितीय उत्तेजित अवस्था (\(n=3\)) के लिए अनुपात: \(\frac{f_3}{f_1} = \frac{1}{3^3} = \frac{1}{27}\)।

8. Original Practice Questions for NEET UG

Practice Question 1

An \(\alpha\)-particle of kinetic energy \(K\) is projected towards a target nucleus of atomic number \(Z\). At the distance of closest approach \(d\), the electrostatic potential energy of the system is:

(A) \(K/2\)
(B) \(K\)
(C) \(2K\)
(D) Zero

Correct Answer: (B) \(K\)

Worked Solution: At closest approach in a head-on collision, the \(\alpha\)-particle stops momentarily (\(v = 0\)). By conservation of mechanical energy, all initial kinetic energy \(K\) is converted into electrostatic potential energy (\(U = K\)).
अभ्यास प्रश्न 1

गतिज ऊर्जा \(K\) का एक \(\alpha\)-कण परमाणु क्रमांक \(Z\) के लक्ष्य नाभिक की ओर प्रक्षेपित किया जाता है। निकटतम पहुँच की दूरी \(d\) पर निकाय की स्थिरवैद्युत स्थितिज ऊर्जा होगी:

(A) \(K/2\)
(B) \(K\)
(C) \(2K\)
(D) शून्य

सही उत्तर: (B) \(K\)

विस्तृत हल: निकटतम पहुँच की दूरी पर कण क्षणिक विराम में आ जाता है। ऊर्जा संरक्षण से संपूर्ण प्रारंभिक गतिज ऊर्जा स्थितिज ऊर्जा में बदल जाती है (\(U = K\))।
Practice Question 2

If the radius of the first Bohr orbit of Hydrogen atom is \(r_1\), then the radius of the third orbit of \(\mathrm{Li}^{2+}\) ion is:

(A) \(r_1\)
(B) \(3 r_1\)
(C) \(9 r_1\)
(D) \(r_1 / 3\)

Correct Answer: (B) \(3 r_1\)

Worked Solution: Radius of \(n\)-th Bohr orbit is \(r_n = r_1 \frac{n^2}{Z}\). For \(\mathrm{Li}^{2+}\), \(Z = 3\), and for the third orbit \(n = 3\): \(r_3 = r_1 \frac{3^2}{3} = 3 r_1\).
अभ्यास प्रश्न 2

यदि हाइड्रोजन परमाणु की प्रथम बोर कक्षा की त्रिज्या \(r_1\) है, तो \(\mathrm{Li}^{2+}\) आयन की तीसरी कक्षा की त्रिज्या होगी:

(A) \(r_1\)
(B) \(3 r_1\)
(C) \(9 r_1\)
(D) \(r_1 / 3\)

सही उत्तर: (B) \(3 r_1\)

विस्तृत हल: \(r_n = r_1 \frac{n^2}{Z}\)। \(\mathrm{Li}^{2+}\) के लिए \(Z=3\) तथा \(n=3\) होने पर: \(r_3 = r_1 \frac{3^2}{3} = 3 r_1\)।
Practice Question 3

The ratio of the speed of an electron in the ground state of Hydrogen to the speed of light in vacuum (\(c\)) is approximately:

(A) \(1 / 137\)
(B) \(1 / 2\)
(C) \(1 / 100\)
(D) \(1 / 1370\)

Correct Answer: (A) \(1 / 137\)

Worked Solution: In the ground state of hydrogen, \(v_1 = \frac{e^2}{2 \varepsilon_0 h}\). The ratio \(\frac{v_1}{c} = \frac{e^2}{2 \varepsilon_0 h c} = \alpha \approx \frac{1}{137}\) (the fine-structure constant).
अभ्यास प्रश्न 3

हाइड्रोजन परमाणु की मूल अवस्था में इलेक्ट्रॉन की चाल तथा प्रकाश की चाल (\(c\)) का अनुपात लगभग होता है:

(A) \(1 / 137\)
(B) \(1 / 2\)
(C) \(1 / 100\)
(D) \(1 / 1370\)

सही उत्तर: (A) \(1 / 137\)

विस्तृत हल: मूल कक्षा में इलेक्ट्रॉन की चाल \(v_1 \approx 2.18 \times 10^6\,\mathrm{m/s}\) होती है। अतः \(\frac{v_1}{c} \approx \frac{1}{137}\) (बारीक संरचना स्थिरांक)।
Practice Question 4

An electron in a hydrogen atom de-excites from principal quantum state \(n = 5\) to the ground state. The maximum number of distinct spectral lines emitted is:

(A) 5
(B) 10
(C) 15
(D) 20

Correct Answer: (B) 10

Worked Solution: The maximum number of emission spectral lines is given by \(N = \frac{n(n - 1)}{2} = \frac{5 \times 4}{2} = 10\).

9. Common Pitfalls & NEET Revision Checklist

High-Yield Exam Traps to Avoid

  • Confusing \(n\) with Excited State: Ground state is \(n=1\). The \(k\)-th excited state is \(n = k + 1\) (e.g., first excited state is \(n=2\)).
  • Signs of Energy: Kinetic energy \(K > 0\), while potential energy \(U < 0\) and total energy \(E < 0\). Always remember: \(E = -K = U/2\).
  • Longest vs. Shortest Wavelength: Maximum wavelength \(\lambda_{\max}\) occurs at minimum energy transition (\(n_2 = n_1 + 1\)). Minimum wavelength \(\lambda_{\min}\) occurs at series limit (\(n_2 = \infty\)).
  • Impact Parameter: At \(\theta = 180^\circ\) (head-on collision), \(b = 0\). For undeflected particles (\(\theta \to 0^\circ\)), \(b \to \infty\).
  • उत्तेजित अवस्था: प्रथम उत्तेजित अवस्था का अर्थ \(n=2\) है, न कि \(n=1\)। \(k\)-वीं उत्तेजित अवस्था के लिए \(n = k + 1\)।
  • ऊर्जा चिह्न: गतिज ऊर्जा सदैव धनात्मक होती है (\(K > 0\)), स्थितिज एवं कुल ऊर्जा ऋणात्मक होती हैं: \(E = -K = U/2\)।
  • अधिकतम तरंगदैर्घ्य न्यूनतम ऊर्जा संक्रमण (\(n_2 = n_1 + 1\)) पर तथा न्यूनतम तरंगदैर्घ्य श्रेणी सीमा (\(n_2 = \infty\)) पर मिलती है।
