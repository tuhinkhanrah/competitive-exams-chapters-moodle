English

The p-block comprises Groups 13 to 18, characterised by the general valence electronic configuration \(ns^2 np^{1\text{–}6}\) (except helium, which is \(1s^2\)). Moving from Group 15 to Group 18 reveals a rich spectrum of non-metals, metalloids, and metals, governed by atomic size, ionisation enthalpy, electron gain enthalpy, electronegativity, and the absence of \(d\)-orbitals in second-period elements.

  • Explain periodic trends in atomic radii, ionisation enthalpy, electronegativity, and oxidation states across Groups 15, 16, 17, and 18.
  • Account for the anomalous behaviour of nitrogen, oxygen, and fluorine compared to heavier congeners.
  • Master the preparation, properties, and reactions of \(\mathrm{N}_2\), \(\mathrm{NH}_3\), \(\mathrm{HNO}_3\), \(\mathrm{PH}_3\), \(\mathrm{PCl}_3\), \(\mathrm{PCl}_5\), \(\mathrm{O}_2\), \(\mathrm{O}_3\), \(\mathrm{SO}_2\), \(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{SO}_4\), \(\mathrm{Cl}_2\), and \(\mathrm{HCl}\).
  • Determine basicity, reducing character, and structures of oxoacids of phosphorus, sulphur, and halogens.
  • Predict VSEPR geometries and hydrolysis products of interhalogen compounds and xenon fluorides/oxyfluorides.

Group 15 Elements: Pnictogens & Nitrogen–Phosphorus Chemistry / वर्ग 15 के तत्व: निक्टोजन एवं नाइट्रोजन–फॉस्फोरस रसायन

English

1. General Trends & Electronic Configuration

Group 15 includes nitrogen (\(\mathrm{N}\)), phosphorus (\(\mathrm{P}\)), arsenic (\(\mathrm{As}\)), antimony (\(\mathrm{Sb}\)), bismuth (\(\mathrm{Bi}\)), and synthetic radioactive moscovium (\(\mathrm{Mc}\), \(Z=115\)). Down the group, metallic character increases: \(\mathrm{N}, \mathrm{P}\) are non-metals, \(\mathrm{As}, \mathrm{Sb}\) are metalloids, and \(\mathrm{Bi}\) is a typical metal.

  • Valence Shell: \(ns^2 np^3\). Half-filled \(p\)-orbitals impart extra stability, giving Group 15 elements higher first ionisation enthalpies than corresponding Group 16 elements.
  • Atomic Radii: Increase sharply from \(\mathrm{N}\) to \(\mathrm{P}\), then modestly from \(\mathrm{As}\) to \(\mathrm{Bi}\) due to poor shielding by filled \(d\)- and \(f\)-orbitals.
  • Oxidation States: Common states are \(-3, +3, +5\). Down the group, stability of \(-3\) and \(+5\) decreases while \(+3\) increases due to the inert pair effect (reluctance of \(ns^2\) electrons to participate in bonding). \(\mathrm{BiF}_5\) is the only well-characterised \(\mathrm{Bi(V)}\) compound.
  • Covalency: Nitrogen is limited to a maximum covalency of 4 (one \(2s\) + three \(2p\) orbitals; no \(2d\) orbitals), so it cannot form \(\mathrm{NCl}_5\) or \(\mathrm{NF}_5\). Heavier elements expand their octet using vacant \(d\)-orbitals (e.g., \(\mathrm{PF}_6^-\)).

2. Anomalous Behaviour of Nitrogen & Hydride Trends

Because of its small size, high electronegativity (\(3.0\)), high ionisation enthalpy, and lack of \(d\)-orbitals, nitrogen forms strong \(p\pi\text{–}p\pi\) multiple bonds with itself (\(\mathrm{N\equiv N}\), bond enthalpy \(941.4\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)) and with \(\mathrm{C}, \mathrm{O}\). Heavier congeners form single \(\mathrm{E–E}\) bonds (\(\mathrm{P}_4\), \(\mathrm{As}\), \(\mathrm{Sb}\)). Interestingly, the \(\mathrm{N–N}\) single bond is weaker than \(\mathrm{P–P}\) due to strong interelectronic repulsion between non-bonding lone pairs at short bond distance, making catenation weaker in nitrogen.

3. Important Compounds of Nitrogen

  • Dinitrogen (\(\mathrm{N}_2\)): Prepared in the lab by heating \(\mathrm{NH}_4\mathrm{Cl(aq)} + \mathrm{NaNO}_2\mathrm{(aq)} \rightarrow \mathrm{N}_2 + 2\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O} + \mathrm{NaCl}\) or thermal decomposition of \((\mathrm{NH}_4)_2\mathrm{Cr}_2\mathrm{O}_7\). Very pure \(\mathrm{N}_2\) is obtained by heating sodium or barium azide: \(\mathrm{Ba(N_3)_2} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ba} + 3\mathrm{N}_2\).
  • Ammonia (\(\mathrm{NH}_3\)): Manufactured by Haber's process (\(\mathrm{N}_2 + 3\mathrm{H}_2 \rightleftharpoons 2\mathrm{NH}_3\), \(\Delta_f H^\circ = -46.1\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)) at \(200\ \mathrm{atm}\) (\(200 \times 10^5\ \mathrm{Pa}\)), \(\sim 700\ \mathrm{K}\), using iron oxide catalyst with \(\mathrm{K}_2\mathrm{O}\) and \(\mathrm{Al}_2\mathrm{O}_3\) promoters. Trigonal pyramidal shape; acts as a Lewis base forming complexes like \([\mathrm{Cu(NH_3)_4}]^{2+}\) (deep blue) and \([\mathrm{Ag(NH_3)_2}]^+\) (colourless).
  • Oxides of Nitrogen: \(\mathrm{N}_2\mathrm{O}\) (\(+1\), neutral), \(\mathrm{NO}\) (\(+2\), neutral), \(\mathrm{N}_2\mathrm{O}_3\) (\(+3\), acidic), \(\mathrm{NO}_2\) (\(+4\), odd-electron brown gas that dimerises to colourless \(\mathrm{N}_2\mathrm{O}_4\)), and \(\mathrm{N}_2\mathrm{O}_5\) (\(+5\), acidic; covalence of \(\mathrm{N}\) is 4).
  • Nitric Acid (\(\mathrm{HNO}_3\)): Manufactured by Ostwald's process (catalytic oxidation of \(\mathrm{NH}_3\) over \(\mathrm{Pt/Rh}\) gauze at \(500\ \mathrm{K}\), \(9\ \mathrm{bar}\)). Oxidises metals depending on concentration:
    \[3\mathrm{Cu} + 8\mathrm{HNO}_3(\text{dil.}) \rightarrow 3\mathrm{Cu(NO_3)_2} + 2\mathrm{NO} + 4\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}\] \[\mathrm{Cu} + 4\mathrm{HNO}_3(\text{conc.}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Cu(NO_3)_2} + 2\mathrm{NO}_2 + 2\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}\] \[4\mathrm{Zn} + 10\mathrm{HNO}_3(\text{dil.}) \rightarrow 4\mathrm{Zn(NO_3)_2} + \mathrm{N}_2\mathrm{O} + 5\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}\]
    \(\mathrm{Cr}\) and \(\mathrm{Al}\) become passive in conc. \(\mathrm{HNO}_3\) due to a protective oxide film. In the Brown Ring Test for \(\mathrm{NO}_3^-\), \(\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}\) reduces \(\mathrm{NO}_3^-\) to \(\mathrm{NO}\), forming the brown complex \([\mathrm{Fe(H_2O)_5(NO)}]^{2+}\).

4. Phosphorus Allotropes, Halides, and Oxoacids

  • Allotropes: White phosphorus (\(\mathrm{P}_4\)) has discrete tetrahedral molecules with \(60°\) bond angles, causing severe angular strain and high reactivity (glows in dark, catches fire in air, soluble in \(\mathrm{CS}_2\), reacts with boiling \(\mathrm{NaOH}\) to give \(\mathrm{PH}_3\) and \(\mathrm{NaH}_2\mathrm{PO}_2\)). Red phosphorus is polymeric (linked \(\mathrm{P}_4\) tetrahedra), non-poisonous, and insoluble in \(\mathrm{CS}_2\). Black phosphorus (\(\alpha\) and \(\beta\) forms) is the most thermodynamically stable.
  • Phosphorus Halides: \(\mathrm{PCl}_3\) (pyramidal, \(sp^3\)) hydrolyses to \(\mathrm{H}_3\mathrm{PO}_3 + 3\mathrm{HCl}\). \(\mathrm{PCl}_5\) (trigonal bipyramidal in gas/liquid, \(sp^3d\)) has two longer axial bonds (\(240\ \mathrm{pm}\)) and three shorter equatorial bonds (\(202\ \mathrm{pm}\)) due to greater axial–equatorial repulsion; hydrolyses stepwise via \(\mathrm{POCl}_3\) to \(\mathrm{H}_3\mathrm{PO}_4\).
  • Oxoacids of Phosphorus: Only \(\mathrm{H}\) atoms in \(\mathrm{P–OH}\) bonds are ionisable (determine basicity); \(\mathrm{P–H}\) bonds are non-ionisable and confer reducing character. \(\mathrm{H}_3\mathrm{PO}_3\) disproportionates on heating: \(4\mathrm{H}_3\mathrm{PO}_3 \rightarrow 3\mathrm{H}_3\mathrm{PO}_4 + \mathrm{PH}_3\).

1. सामान्य प्रवृत्तियाँ एवं इलेक्ट्रॉनिक विन्यास

वर्ग 15 में नाइट्रोजन (\(\mathrm{N}\)), फॉस्फोरस (\(\mathrm{P}\)), आर्सेनिक (\(\mathrm{As}\)), ऐन्टिमनी (\(\mathrm{Sb}\)), बिस्मथ (\(\mathrm{Bi}\)) तथा संश्लेषित रेडियोधर्मी मॉस्कोवियम (\(\mathrm{Mc}\), \(Z=115\)) आते हैं। वर्ग में नीचे जाने पर धात्विक गुण बढ़ता है: \(\mathrm{N}, \mathrm{P}\) अधातु हैं, \(\mathrm{As}, \mathrm{Sb}\) उपधातु हैं तथा \(\mathrm{Bi}\) एक विशिष्ट धातु है।

  • संयोजी कोश: \(ns^2 np^3\)। अर्ध-पूरित \(p\)-कक्षक अतिरिक्त स्थायित्व प्रदान करते हैं, जिससे वर्ग 15 के तत्वों की प्रथम आयनन एन्थैल्पी संगत वर्ग 16 के तत्वों से अधिक होती है।
  • परमाणु त्रिज्या: \(\mathrm{N}\) से \(\mathrm{P}\) तक तीव्र वृद्धि होती है, परंतु \(\mathrm{As}\) से \(\mathrm{Bi}\) तक पूर्ण पूरित \(d\)- और \(f\)-कक्षकों के दुर्बल परिरक्षण प्रभाव के कारण मामूली वृद्धि होती है।
  • ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाएँ: सामान्य अवस्थाएँ \(-3, +3, +5\) हैं। वर्ग में नीचे जाने पर \(-3\) तथा \(+5\) का स्थायित्व घटता है, जबकि अक्रिय युग्म प्रभाव (\(ns^2\) इलेक्ट्रॉनों की बंध बनाने में अनिच्छा) के कारण \(+3\) का स्थायित्व बढ़ता है। \(\mathrm{BiF}_5\) एकमात्र सुपरिभाषित \(\mathrm{Bi(V)}\) यौगिक है।
  • सहसंयोजकता: नाइट्रोजन की अधिकतम सहसंयोजकता 4 तक सीमित है (एक \(2s\) + तीन \(2p\) कक्षक; \(2d\) कक्षक अनुपस्थित), अतः यह \(\mathrm{NCl}_5\) या \(\mathrm{NF}_5\) नहीं बनाता। भारी तत्व रिक्त \(d\)-कक्षकों से अष्टक का प्रसार करते हैं (जैसे \(\mathrm{PF}_6^-\))।

2. नाइट्रोजन का असंगत व्यवहार एवं हाइड्राइड प्रवृत्तियाँ

छोटे आकार, उच्च विद्युतऋणात्मकता (\(3.0\)), उच्च आयनन एन्थैल्पी और \(d\)-कक्षकों की अनुपस्थिति के कारण नाइट्रोजन स्वयं के साथ (\(\mathrm{N\equiv N}\), बंध एन्थैल्पी \(941.4\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)) तथा \(\mathrm{C}, \mathrm{O}\) के साथ प्रबल \(p\pi\text{–}p\pi\) बहुबंध बनाता है। भारी तत्व एकल \(\mathrm{E–E}\) बंध बनाते हैं। छोटी बंध लंबाई के कारण अबंधी एकाकी इलेक्ट्रॉन युग्मों के बीच प्रबल प्रतिकर्षण से \(\mathrm{N–N}\) एकल बंध \(\mathrm{P–P}\) से दुर्बल होता है, जिससे नाइट्रोजन में शृंखलन प्रवृत्ति कम होती है।

3. नाइट्रोजन के प्रमुख यौगिक

  • डाइनाइट्रोजन (\(\mathrm{N}_2\)): प्रयोगशाला में \(\mathrm{NH}_4\mathrm{Cl(aq)} + \mathrm{NaNO}_2\mathrm{(aq)} \rightarrow \mathrm{N}_2 + 2\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O} + \mathrm{NaCl}\) या \((\mathrm{NH}_4)_2\mathrm{Cr}_2\mathrm{O}_7\) के ऊष्मीय अपघटन से बनाई जाती है। अति शुद्ध \(\mathrm{N}_2\) सोडियम या बेरियम ऐज़ाइड को गर्म करके प्राप्त होती है: \(\mathrm{Ba(N_3)_2} \rightarrow \mathrm{Ba} + 3\mathrm{N}_2\)।
  • अमोनिया (\(\mathrm{NH}_3\)): हाबर प्रक्रम (\(\mathrm{N}_2 + 3\mathrm{H}_2 \rightleftharpoons 2\mathrm{NH}_3\), \(\Delta_f H^\circ = -46.1\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)) द्वारा \(200\ \mathrm{atm}\), \(\sim 700\ \mathrm{K}\) पर आयरन ऑक्साइड उत्प्रेरक तथा \(\mathrm{K}_2\mathrm{O}\), \(\mathrm{Al}_2\mathrm{O}_3\) वर्धकों की उपस्थिति में निर्मित। त्रिकोणीय पिरामिडी संरचना; लुइस क्षार के रूप में \([\mathrm{Cu(NH_3)_4}]^{2+}\) (गहरा नीला) और \([\mathrm{Ag(NH_3)_2}]^+\) (रंगहीन) संकुल बनाती है।
  • नाइट्रोजन के ऑक्साइड: \(\mathrm{N}_2\mathrm{O}\) (\(+1\), उदासीन), \(\mathrm{NO}\) (\(+2\), उदासीन), \(\mathrm{N}_2\mathrm{O}_3\) (\(+3\), अम्लीय), \(\mathrm{NO}_2\) (\(+4\), विषम-इलेक्ट्रॉन भूरी गैस जो रंगहीन \(\mathrm{N}_2\mathrm{O}_4\) में द्वितय होती है), तथा \(\mathrm{N}_2\mathrm{O}_5\) (\(+5\), अम्लीय; \(\mathrm{N}\) की सहसंयोजकता 4)।
  • नाइट्रिक अम्ल (\(\mathrm{HNO}_3\)): ओस्टवाल्ड प्रक्रम (\(\mathrm{Pt/Rh}\) जाली उत्प्रेरक, \(500\ \mathrm{K}\), \(9\ \mathrm{bar}\)) द्वारा निर्मित। सांद्रता के अनुसार धातुओं का ऑक्सीकरण करता है: तनु अम्ल से \(\mathrm{Cu}\) द्वारा \(\mathrm{NO}\) तथा \(\mathrm{Zn}\) द्वारा \(\mathrm{N}_2\mathrm{O}\) बनता है, जबकि सांद्र अम्ल से दोनों \(\mathrm{NO}_2\) देते हैं। \(\mathrm{Cr}\) व \(\mathrm{Al}\) ऑक्साइड परत बनने से सांद्र \(\mathrm{HNO}_3\) में निष्क्रिय हो जाते हैं। भूरा वलय परीक्षण में \([\mathrm{Fe(H_2O)_5(NO)}]^{2+}\) संकुल बनता है।

4. फॉस्फोरस के अपररूप, हैलाइड एवं ऑक्सोअम्ल

  • अपररूप: श्वेत फॉस्फोरस (\(\mathrm{P}_4\)) में \(60°\) कोण वाले चतुष्फलकीय अणु होते हैं, जिससे कोणीय तनाव व उच्च अभिक्रियाशीलता होती है (अंधेरे में चमकता है, \(\mathrm{CS}_2\) में विलेय, उबलते \(\mathrm{NaOH}\) से \(\mathrm{PH}_3\) व \(\mathrm{NaH}_2\mathrm{PO}_2\) देता है)। लाल फॉस्फोरस बहुलकीय, विषहीन व \(\mathrm{CS}_2\) में अविलेय है। काला फॉस्फोरस (\(\alpha\) व \(\beta\)) ऊष्मागतिकीय रूप से सर्वाधिक स्थायी है।
  • फॉस्फोरस हैलाइड: \(\mathrm{PCl}_3\) (पिरामिडी, \(sp^3\)) जल-अपघटित होकर \(\mathrm{H}_3\mathrm{PO}_3 + 3\mathrm{HCl}\) देता है। \(\mathrm{PCl}_5\) (त्रिकोणीय द्वीपिरामिडी, \(sp^3d\)) में दो अक्षीय बंध (\(240\ \mathrm{pm}\)) तीन निरक्षीय बंधों (\(202\ \mathrm{pm}\)) से लंबे होते हैं।

Group 16 Elements: Chalcogens & Sulphur Chemistry / वर्ग 16 के तत्व: कैल्कोजन एवं सल्फर रसायन

English

1. Periodic Trends & Anomalous Oxygen

Group 16 comprises oxygen (\(\mathrm{O}\)), sulphur (\(\mathrm{S}\)), selenium (\(\mathrm{Se}\)), tellurium (\(\mathrm{Te}\)), polonium (\(\mathrm{Po}\)), and livermorium (\(\mathrm{Lv}\), \(Z=116\)), with valence configuration \(ns^2 np^4\).

  • Electron Gain Enthalpy (\(\Delta_{eg}H\)): Oxygen has a less negative \(\Delta_{eg}H\) (\(-141\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)) than sulphur (\(-200\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)) due to compact \(2p\) orbitals causing strong interelectronic repulsion. Order of magnitude of negative \(\Delta_{eg}H\): \(\mathrm{S} > \mathrm{Se} > \mathrm{Te} > \mathrm{Po} > \mathrm{O}\).
  • Oxidation States: Oxygen shows \(-2\) (except \(+2\) in \(\mathrm{OF}_2\) and \(+1\) in \(\mathrm{O}_2\mathrm{F}_2\)). Heavier elements exhibit \(+2, +4, +6\), with \(+4\) stability increasing down the group (inert pair effect).
  • Hydrides (\(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{E}\)): From \(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}\) to \(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{Te}\), \(\mathrm{H–E}\) bond dissociation enthalpy decreases, so acidic character increases (\(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O} < \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{S} < \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{Se} < \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{Te}\)), reducing power increases, and thermal stability decreases. \(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}\) is a liquid due to intermolecular hydrogen bonding, whereas \(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{S}\) is a gas.
  • Halides: \(\mathrm{SF}_6\) is octahedral and kinetically inert due to steric crowding. \(\mathrm{SF}_4\) (\(sp^3d\)) has a see-saw geometry.

2. Dioxygen, Simple Oxides, and Ozone

  • Dioxygen (\(\mathrm{O}_2\)): Prepared by heating \(\mathrm{KClO}_3\) with \(\mathrm{MnO}_2\) catalyst, or thermal decomposition of \(\mathrm{Ag}_2\mathrm{O}\), \(\mathrm{HgO}\), \(\mathrm{Pb}_3\mathrm{O}_4\). \(\mathrm{O}_2\) is paramagnetic due to two unpaired electrons in \(\pi^*\) molecular orbitals.
  • Simple Oxides: Classified as acidic (\(\mathrm{SO}_2, \mathrm{Cl}_2\mathrm{O}_7, \mathrm{Mn}_2\mathrm{O}_7\)), basic (\(\mathrm{Na}_2\mathrm{O}, \mathrm{CaO}\)), amphoteric (\(\mathrm{Al}_2\mathrm{O}_3\), reacts with both acids and alkalies), and neutral (\(\mathrm{CO}, \mathrm{NO}, \mathrm{N}_2\mathrm{O}\)).
  • Ozone (\(\mathrm{O}_3\)): Endothermic allotrope prepared by passing dry \(\mathrm{O}_2\) through a silent electric discharge (\(3\mathrm{O}_2 \rightarrow 2\mathrm{O}_3\), \(\Delta H^\circ = +142\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)). Angular (\(\sim 117°\)), resonance hybrid with equal \(\mathrm{O–O}\) bond lengths (\(128\ \mathrm{pm}\)). Acts as a strong oxidising agent (\(\mathrm{PbS} + 4\mathrm{O}_3 \rightarrow \mathrm{PbSO}_4 + 4\mathrm{O}_2\)) and is estimated quantitatively using buffered \(\mathrm{KI}\) (pH 9.2) and \(\mathrm{Na}_2\mathrm{S}_2\mathrm{O}_3\). Depleted by \(\mathrm{NO}\) from supersonic jets and freons.

3. Allotropes of Sulphur, \(\mathrm{SO}_2\), Oxoacids, and \(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{SO}_4\)

  • Sulphur Allotropes: Rhombic (\(\alpha\))-sulphur (stable below \(369\ \mathrm{K}\), soluble in \(\mathrm{CS}_2\)) transforms to monoclinic (\(\beta\))-sulphur above \(369\ \mathrm{K}\) (transition temperature). Both consist of puckered crown-shaped \(\mathrm{S}_8\) rings. At \(\sim 1000\ \mathrm{K}\), \(\mathrm{S}_2\) vapour is paramagnetic like \(\mathrm{O}_2\).
  • Sulphur Dioxide (\(\mathrm{SO}_2\)): Angular resonance hybrid; moist \(\mathrm{SO}_2\) acts as a reducing agent (decolourises acidified \(\mathrm{KMnO}_4\) and reduces \(\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}\) to \(\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}\)).
  • Oxoacids of Sulphur: \(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{SO}_3\) (sulphurous, S in \(+4\)), \(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{SO}_4\) (sulphuric, S in \(+6\)), \(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{S}_2\mathrm{O}_7\) (oleum / pyrosulphuric, has \(\mathrm{S–O–S}\) linkage, S in \(+6\)), and \(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{S}_2\mathrm{O}_8\) (peroxodisulphuric acid, has \(\mathrm{S–O–O–S}\) peroxide linkage, S in \(+6\)).
  • Contact Process for \(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{SO}_4\): Catalytic oxidation of \(\mathrm{SO}_2\) to \(\mathrm{SO}_3\) using \(\mathrm{V}_2\mathrm{O}_5\) at \(720\ \mathrm{K}\) and \(2\ \mathrm{bar}\), followed by absorption in conc. \(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{SO}_4\) to form oleum (\(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{S}_2\mathrm{O}_7\)) and dilution. \(\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{SO}_4\) has \(K_{a_1} \gg K_{a_2}\) (\(K_{a_2} = 1.2 \times 10^{-2}\)), acts as a strong dehydrating agent (charring sucrose), and oxidises \(\mathrm{Cu}, \mathrm{C}, \mathrm{S}\) to \(\mathrm{SO}_2\).

Group 17 & Group 18 Elements: Halogens, Interhalogens, and Noble Gases / वर्ग 17 एवं 18 के तत्व: हैलोजन, अंतरहैलोजन एवं उत्कृष्ट गैसें

English

1. Halogens (Group 17: \(\mathrm{F, Cl, Br, I, At, Ts}\))

Valence configuration \(ns^2 np^5\). Halogens have the smallest atomic radii and highest negative electron gain enthalpies in their respective periods.

  • Key Anomalies of Fluorine:
    1. Negative electron gain enthalpy order: \(\mathrm{Cl} > \mathrm{F} > \mathrm{Br} > \mathrm{I}\) (compact \(2p\) orbitals of \(\mathrm{F}\) repel incoming electrons).
    2. Bond dissociation enthalpy order: \(\mathrm{Cl}_2 > \mathrm{Br}_2 > \mathrm{F}_2 > \mathrm{I}_2\) (high lone-pair–lone-pair repulsion in small \(\mathrm{F}_2\)).
    3. Oxidising power order: \(\mathrm{F}_2 > \mathrm{Cl}_2 > \mathrm{Br}_2 > \mathrm{I}_2\). \(\mathrm{F}_2\) is the strongest oxidising agent despite lower \(\Delta_{eg}H\) because of its low \(\mathrm{F–F}\) bond dissociation enthalpy and very high hydration enthalpy of \(\mathrm{F}^-\).
    4. \(\mathrm{F}\) shows only \(-1\) oxidation state and forms only one oxoacid (\(\mathrm{HOF}\)).
  • Hydrogen Halides (\(\mathrm{HX}\)): Acidic strength: \(\mathrm{HF} < \mathrm{HCl} < \mathrm{HBr} < \mathrm{HI}\) (as \(\mathrm{H–X}\) bond dissociation enthalpy decreases). \(\mathrm{HF}\) has the highest boiling point (\(293\ \mathrm{K}\)) due to hydrogen bonding.
  • Chlorine (\(\mathrm{Cl}_2\)) & \(\mathrm{HCl}\): Prepared by \(\mathrm{MnO}_2 + 4\mathrm{HCl}\) or \(\mathrm{KMnO}_4 + \mathrm{HCl}\), and industrially by Deacon's process (\(4\mathrm{HCl} + \mathrm{O}_2 \xrightarrow{\mathrm{CuCl}_2, 723\,\mathrm{K}} 2\mathrm{Cl}_2 + 2\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}\)). With cold, dilute \(\mathrm{NaOH}\), \(\mathrm{Cl}_2\) gives \(\mathrm{NaCl} + \mathrm{NaOCl}\); with hot, conc. \(\mathrm{NaOH}\), it gives \(\mathrm{NaCl} + \mathrm{NaClO}_3\) (disproportionation). Bleaching action of \(\mathrm{Cl}_2\) is permanent and due to oxidation via nascent oxygen from \(\mathrm{HOCl}\). Aqua regia is \(3\) parts conc. \(\mathrm{HCl}\) + \(1\) part conc. \(\mathrm{HNO}_3\), dissolving \(\mathrm{Au}\) and \(\mathrm{Pt}\).
  • Oxoacids of Chlorine: \(\mathrm{HOCl}\) (\(+1\)), \(\mathrm{HOClO}\) (\(+3\)), \(\mathrm{HOClO}_2\) (\(+5\)), \(\mathrm{HOClO}_3\) (\(+7\)). Acidic strength and thermal stability increase with oxidation state of \(\mathrm{Cl}\): \(\mathrm{HClO} < \mathrm{HClO}_2 < \mathrm{HClO}_3 < \mathrm{HClO}_4\).
  • Interhalogen Compounds (\(\mathrm{XX}', \mathrm{XX}'_3, \mathrm{XX}'_5, \mathrm{XX}'_7\)): \(\mathrm{X}\) is larger and more electropositive. More reactive than halogens (except \(\mathrm{F}_2\)) because \(\mathrm{X–X}'\) bond is weaker than \(\mathrm{X–X}\). Hydrolysis always yields \(\mathrm{HX}'\) (from smaller halogen) and an oxoacid anion of \(\mathrm{X}\).

2. Noble Gases (Group 18: \(\mathrm{He, Ne, Ar, Kr, Xe, Rn, Og}\))

Closed-shell configuration \(ns^2 np^6\) (\(\mathrm{He}: 1s^2\)), very high ionisation enthalpy, and positive electron gain enthalpy make them chemically inert. Low boiling points (\(\mathrm{He}: 4.2\ \mathrm{K}\)) arise from weak London dispersion forces.

  • Neil Bartlett's Discovery (1962): Noticing that first IE of \(\mathrm{O}_2\) (\(1175\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)) \(\approx\) first IE of \(\mathrm{Xe}\) (\(1170\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)), he prepared \(\mathrm{Xe}^+[\mathrm{PtF}_6]^-\) from \(\mathrm{Xe}\) and \(\mathrm{PtF}_6\).
  • Xenon Compounds:
    \[2\mathrm{XeF}_2 + 2\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O} \rightarrow 2\mathrm{Xe} + 4\mathrm{HF} + \mathrm{O}_2 \quad (\text{Redox})\] \[6\mathrm{XeF}_4 + 12\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O} \rightarrow 4\mathrm{Xe} + 2\mathrm{XeO}_3 + 24\mathrm{HF} + 3\mathrm{O}_2 \quad (\text{Disproportionation})\] \[\mathrm{XeF}_6 + 3\mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O} \rightarrow \mathrm{XeO}_3 + 6\mathrm{HF} \quad (\text{Non-redox}); \quad \mathrm{XeF}_6 + \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O} \rightarrow \mathrm{XeOF}_4 + 2\mathrm{HF}\]
    \(\mathrm{He}\) is used as an \(\mathrm{O}_2\) diluent in deep-sea diving (very low blood solubility) and in MRI superconducting magnets; \(\mathrm{Ar}\) provides an inert atmosphere in arc welding.

1. हैलोजन (वर्ग 17: \(\mathrm{F, Cl, Br, I, At, Ts}\))

संयोजी विन्यास \(ns^2 np^5\)। हैलोजन अपने-अपने आवर्त में सबसे छोटे परमाणु आकार और सर्वाधिक ऋणात्मक इलेक्ट्रॉन लब्धि एन्थैल्पी वाले तत्व हैं।

  • फ्लोरीन के प्रमुख अपवाद:
    1. ऋणात्मक इलेक्ट्रॉन लब्धि एन्थैल्पी क्रम: \(\mathrm{Cl} > \mathrm{F} > \mathrm{Br} > \mathrm{I}\) (\(\mathrm{F}\) के छोटे \(2p\)-कक्षकों में प्रतिकर्षण के कारण)।
    2. बंध वियोजन एन्थैल्पी क्रम: \(\mathrm{Cl}_2 > \mathrm{Br}_2 > \mathrm{F}_2 > \mathrm{I}_2\) (छोटे \(\mathrm{F}_2\) अणु में एकाकी युग्म प्रतिकर्षण के कारण)।
    3. ऑक्सीकारक क्षमता क्रम: \(\mathrm{F}_2 > \mathrm{Cl}_2 > \mathrm{Br}_2 > \mathrm{I}_2\)। कम \(\Delta_{eg}H\) के बावजूद \(\mathrm{F}_2\) प्रबलतम ऑक्सीकारक है क्योंकि इसकी \(\mathrm{F–F}\) बंध वियोजन एन्थैल्पी कम तथा \(\mathrm{F}^-\) की जलयोजन एन्थैल्पी अति उच्च होती है।

Worked Example 2: Comparing Oxidising Power of Halogens Using Thermodynamic Cycle

Problem: Chlorine has a more negative electron gain enthalpy (\(-349\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)) than fluorine (\(-333\ \mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)), yet \(\mathrm{F}_2\) oxidises \(\mathrm{Cl}^-\) to \(\mathrm{Cl}_2\) in aqueous solution. The standard reduction potential \(E^\circ(\mathrm{X}_2/\mathrm{X}^-)\) depends on three enthalpy terms for \(\frac{1}{2}\mathrm{X}_2\mathrm{(g)} + e^- \rightarrow \mathrm{X}^-\mathrm{(aq)}\):

Common Pitfalls & Revision Checklist / सामान्य त्रुटियाँ एवं पुनरीक्षण सूची

English
Frequent NEET UG Traps to Avoid:
  • Confusing the basicity of \(\mathrm{H}_3\mathrm{PO}_3\) and \(\mathrm{H}_3\mathrm{PO}_2\) with the total number of \(\mathrm{H}\) atoms: \(\mathrm{H}_3\mathrm{PO}_2\) is monobasic (1 \(\mathrm{P–OH}\)) and \(\mathrm{H}_3\mathrm{PO}_3\) is dibasic (2 \(\mathrm{P–OH}\)).
  • Forgetting that \(\mathrm{Cl}_2\) has higher \(\Delta_{\text{diss}}H^\circ\) and more negative \(\Delta_{eg}H\) than \(\mathrm{F}_2\), yet \(\mathrm{F}_2\) is the stronger oxidising agent due to low \(\mathrm{F–F}\) bond energy and high hydration enthalpy of \(\mathrm{F}^-\).
  • Mixing up dilute \(\mathrm{HNO}_3\) products: \(\mathrm{Cu}\) gives \(\mathrm{NO}\), whereas \(\mathrm{Zn}\) gives \(\mathrm{N}_2\mathrm{O}\).
  • Assuming \(\mathrm{XeF}_6\) hydrolysis is redox: \(\mathrm{XeF}_6 \rightarrow \mathrm{XeO}_3\) or \(\mathrm{XeOF}_4\) is non-redox, whereas \(\mathrm{XeF}_4 + \mathrm{H}_2\mathrm{O}\) is a disproportionation reaction.
