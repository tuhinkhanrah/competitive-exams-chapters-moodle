1. Learning Objectives and Chapter Overview

English

The \(d\)- and \(f\)-block elements occupy the central and bottom sections of the modern periodic table. For NEET UG, questions frequently test electronic configuration exceptions, periodic trends in radii and enthalpy of atomisation, spin-only magnetic moments, colour of aqueous ions, electrode potentials, disproportionation, reactions of \(\mathrm{K_2Cr_2O_7}\) and \(\mathrm{KMnO_4}\), and lanthanoid/actinoid contraction.

  • Distinguish between \(d\)-block elements and true transition elements using IUPAC electronic criteria.
  • Explain anomalies in ground-state configurations, metallic radii, ionisation enthalpies, and standard electrode potentials.
  • Predict magnetic moments, colours, complexation tendencies, and catalytic or interstitial behaviour of \(3d\) metal ions.
  • Write balanced redox equations for \(\mathrm{Cr_2O_7^{2-}}\) and \(\mathrm{MnO_4^-}\) across different pH conditions.
  • Compare lanthanoids and actinoids in electronic structure, oxidation states, contraction effects, and chemical reactivity.

2. Position, IUPAC Definition, and Electronic Configurations of d-Block Elements

English

The \(d\)-block comprises Groups 3 to 12, positioned between the \(s\)-block and \(p\)-block. In these elements, electrons progressively enter the penultimate \((n-1)d\) subshell across four transition series: \(3d\) (\(\mathrm{Sc}\) to \(\mathrm{Zn}\)), \(4d\) (\(\mathrm{Y}\) to \(\mathrm{Cd}\)), \(5d\) (\(\mathrm{La}\) and \(\mathrm{Hf}\) to \(\mathrm{Hg}\)), and \(6d\) (\(\mathrm{Ac}\) and \(\mathrm{Rf}\) to \(\mathrm{Cn}\)).

IUPAC Definition of a Transition Element: An element whose atom has an incompletely filled \(d\) subshell either in its neutral ground state or in any of its common oxidation states.

Why Group 12 elements are excluded: Zinc (\(\mathrm{Zn}\)), cadmium (\(\mathrm{Cd}\)), mercury (\(\mathrm{Hg}\)), and copernicium (\(\mathrm{Cn}\)) have a completely filled \((n-1)d^{10}ns^2\) configuration in the ground state as well as in their stable \(+2\) oxidation state. Consequently, they are \(d\)-block metals but not transition metals. By contrast, coinage metals like \(\mathrm{Cu}\) (\(3d^{10}4s^1\)), \(\mathrm{Ag}\) (\(4d^{10}5s^1\)), and \(\mathrm{Au}\) (\(5d^{10}6s^1\)) are true transition metals because their common cations (\(\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}: 3d^9\), \(\mathrm{Ag}^{2+}: 4d^9\), \(\mathrm{Au}^{3+}: 5d^8\)) possess partially filled \(d\) orbitals.

General Outer Configuration: \((n-1)d^{1-10}ns^{1-2}\). Because the energy difference between \((n-1)d\) and \(ns\) orbitals is very small, half-filled (\(d^5\)) and completely filled (\(d^{10}\)) configurations gain extra stability from symmetry and exchange energy. Thus, in the \(3d\) series, chromium (\(\mathrm{Cr}, Z=24\)) is \([\mathrm{Ar}]3d^5 4s^1\) (not \(3d^4 4s^2\)) and copper (\(\mathrm{Cu}, Z=29\)) is \([\mathrm{Ar}]3d^{10} 4s^1\) (not \(3d^9 4s^2\)). Similarly, in the \(4d\) series, palladium (\(\mathrm{Pd}, Z=46\)) adopts \([\mathrm{Kr}]4d^{10} 5s^0\).

When transition metals form cations, electrons are always removed first from the outermost \(ns\) orbital and then from the \((n-1)d\) orbital.

3. Physical Properties, Atomic Radii, and Ionisation Enthalpies

English

Metallic Bonding, Melting Points, and Enthalpy of Atomisation

Transition metals display high tensile strength, ductility, thermal/electrical conductivity, and high melting points (except \(\mathrm{Zn}\), \(\mathrm{Cd}\), \(\mathrm{Hg}\), and \(\mathrm{Mn}\) which show atypical crystal packing). Both \(ns\) and unpaired \((n-1)d\) electrons participate in metallic bonding. Across any series, enthalpy of atomisation and melting point rise to a maximum near \(d^5\) (e.g., \(\mathrm{Cr}\), \(\mathrm{Mo}\), \(\mathrm{W}\)) where one unpaired electron per \(d\) orbital maximises interatomic bonding, and then decrease toward \(d^{10}\). Manganese (\(\mathrm{Mn}\)) and technetium (\(\mathrm{Tc}\)) show anomalous dips in melting point due to complex crystal structures and half-filled \(d^5\) electron localisation. Zinc has the lowest enthalpy of atomisation in the \(3d\) series (\(126\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)) because no \(3d\) electrons take part in metallic bonding. Heavier \(4d\) and \(5d\) metals have higher enthalpies of atomisation than \(3d\) metals, favouring frequent metal–metal bonding.

Atomic and Ionic Radii & Lanthanoid Contraction

Across a \(d\)-series, atomic and ionic radii initially decrease from \(\mathrm{Sc}\) to \(\mathrm{Cr}\) as nuclear charge increases, remain nearly constant in the middle (\(\mathrm{Fe}, \mathrm{Co}, \mathrm{Ni}\)) where increased nuclear attraction is balanced by \(d\)–\(d\) electron repulsion, and slightly increase at the end (\(\mathrm{Cu}, \mathrm{Zn}\)).

Down a group, radii increase from the \(3d\) to \(4d\) series, but the \(4d\) and \(5d\) series exhibit virtually identical radii (for example, \(\mathrm{Zr} = 160\,\mathrm{pm}\) and \(\mathrm{Hf} = 159\,\mathrm{pm}\)). This occurs because the \(4f\) subshell is filled prior to the \(5d\) series. Poor shielding of nuclear charge by \(4f\) electrons causes a steady contraction across the lanthanoids (lanthanoid contraction), which cancels the expected size increase from an extra shell. Consequently, \(4d\) and \(5d\) congeners have very similar physical and chemical properties and occur together in ores.

Ionisation Enthalpies

First ionisation enthalpies (\(\Delta_i H_1\)) increase gradually across the \(3d\) series, with minor irregularities because removing an electron alters the relative energies of \(3d\) and \(4s\) orbitals. Crucial NEET exam patterns include:

  • Unusually high \(\Delta_i H_2\) for \(\mathrm{Cr}\) and \(\mathrm{Cu}\): Removing the second electron disrupts stable \(3d^5\) (\(\mathrm{Cr}^+\)) and \(3d^{10}\) (\(\mathrm{Cu}^+\)) configurations.
  • Low \(\Delta_i H_2\) for \(\mathrm{Zn}\): Removing the second electron from \(\mathrm{Zn}^+\) (\(3d^{10}4s^1\)) yields the stable \(3d^{10}\) configuration.
  • \(\Delta_i H_3\) of \(\mathrm{Fe}\) is lower than \(\mathrm{Mn}\): \(\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}\) (\(3d^6\)) easily loses an electron to form stable half-filled \(\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}\) (\(3d^5\)), whereas \(\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}\) (\(3d^5\)) resists oxidation to \(3d^4\).

3. भौतिक गुण, परमाणु त्रिज्या एवं आयनन एन्थैल्पी

हिन्दी

संक्रमण धातुएँ उच्च तन्यता, आघातवर्ध्यता, ऊष्मीय/विद्युत चालकता और उच्च गलनांक प्रदर्शित करती हैं (\(\mathrm{Zn}\), \(\mathrm{Cd}\), \(\mathrm{Hg}\) और \(\mathrm{Mn}\) अपवाद हैं)। धात्विक आबंधन में \(ns\) तथा अयुग्मित \((n-1)d\) दोनों प्रकार के इलेक्ट्रॉन भाग लेते हैं। प्रत्येक श्रेणी में \(d^5\) विन्यास (\(\mathrm{Cr}\), \(\mathrm{Mo}\), \(\mathrm{W}\)) के निकट कणन एन्थैल्पी और गलनांक अधिकतम होते हैं, क्योंकि प्रत्येक \(d\) कक्षक में एक अयुग्मित इलेक्ट्रॉन प्रबल अंतरापरमाण्विक आबंधन बनाता है। जटिल क्रिस्टल संरचना के कारण \(\mathrm{Mn}\) और \(\mathrm{Tc}\) के गलनांक में असामान्य गिरावट दिखती है। \(3d\) श्रेणी में जिंक की कणन एन्थैल्पी न्यूनतम (\(126\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)) होती है क्योंकि इसमें \(3d\) इलेक्ट्रॉन आबंधन में भाग नहीं लेते।

परमाणु त्रिज्या एवं लैंथेनॉयड आकुंचन: \(d\)-श्रेणी में बाएँ से दाएँ जाने पर प्रारंभ में त्रिज्या घटती है, मध्य में (\(\mathrm{Fe}, \mathrm{Co}, \mathrm{Ni}\)) लगभग स्थिर रहती है और अंत में (\(\mathrm{Cu}, \mathrm{Zn}\)) थोड़ी बढ़ती है। वर्ग में ऊपर से नीचे \(3d\) से \(4d\) में त्रिज्या बढ़ती है, किंतु \(4d\) और \(5d\) श्रेणियों की त्रिज्याएँ लगभग समान होती हैं (जैसे \(\mathrm{Zr} = 160\,\mathrm{pm}\), \(\mathrm{Hf} = 159\,\mathrm{pm}\))। इसका कारण \(5d\) से पहले \(4f\) कक्षकों का भरना और \(4f\) इलेक्ट्रॉनों का दुर्बल परिरक्षण प्रभाव (लैंथेनॉयड आकुंचन) है।

3d, 4d और 5d श्रेणियों की धात्विक त्रिज्याओं की तुलनात्मक प्रवृत्ति

आयनन एन्थैल्पी के मुख्य बिंदु: \(\mathrm{Cr}\) और \(\mathrm{Cu}\) की द्वितीय आयनन एन्थैल्पी (\(\Delta_i H_2\)) अत्यधिक उच्च होती है क्योंकि दूसरा इलेक्ट्रॉन क्रमशः स्थायी \(3d^5\) और \(3d^{10}\) विन्यास से निकलता है। \(\mathrm{Fe}\) की तृतीय आयनन एन्थैल्पी (\(\Delta_i H_3\)), \(\mathrm{Mn}\) से कम होती है क्योंकि \(\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}\) (\(3d^6\)) आसानी से स्थायी \(\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}\) (\(3d^5\)) में परिवर्तित हो जाता है, जबकि \(\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}\) (\(3d^5\)) से इलेक्ट्रॉन निकालना कठिन है।

4. Oxidation States, Electrode Potentials, and Halide/Oxide Stability

English
  • Variable Oxidation States: Transition metals exhibit multiple oxidation states differing by unity (e.g., \(\mathrm{V}^{2+}, \mathrm{V}^{3+}, \mathrm{V}^{4+}, \mathrm{V}^{5+}\)), unlike \(p\)-block elements where states typically differ by 2 units. In the \(3d\) series, \(\mathrm{Mn}\) shows the largest range (\(+2\) to \(+7\)), whereas \(\mathrm{Sc}\) shows only \(+3\) and \(\mathrm{Zn}\) shows only \(+2\).
  • Group Trend (Opposite to \(p\)-Block): In \(d\)-block groups, higher oxidation states become more stable down the group. Thus, \(\mathrm{Mo(VI)}\) and \(\mathrm{W(VI)}\) in \(\mathrm{MoO_3}\) and \(\mathrm{WO_3}\) are far more stable (and non-oxidising) than \(\mathrm{Cr(VI)}\) in \(\mathrm{Cr_2O_7^{2-}}\), which is a strong oxidising agent in acid.
  • Zero Oxidation State: \(\pi\)-acceptor ligands such as \(\mathrm{CO}\) stabilise zero oxidation states in \(\mathrm{Ni(CO)_4}\) and \(\mathrm{Fe(CO)_5}\).
  • Standard Electrode Potentials \(E^\circ(M^{2+}/M)\): Copper is the only \(3d\) metal with a positive \(E^\circ(\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}/\mathrm{Cu}) = +0.34\,\mathrm{V}\), so it cannot liberate \(\mathrm{H_2}\) from dilute non-oxidising acids. Its high atomisation and ionisation enthalpies are not compensated by its hydration enthalpy. Values for \(\mathrm{Mn}\) (\(-1.18\,\mathrm{V}\)), \(\mathrm{Ni}\) (\(-0.25\,\mathrm{V}\)), and \(\mathrm{Zn}\) (\(-0.76\,\mathrm{V}\)) are more negative than expected due to stable \(d^5\) (\(\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}\)), high negative \(\Delta_{\mathrm{hyd}}H^\circ\) (\(\mathrm{Ni}^{2+}\)), and stable \(d^{10}\) (\(\mathrm{Zn}^{2+}\)).
  • \(E^\circ(M^{3+}/M^{2+})\) & Redox Behaviour: \(\mathrm{Cr}^{2+}\) (\(d^4\)) is a strong reducing agent (\(E^\circ = -0.41\,\mathrm{V}\)) because oxidation to \(\mathrm{Cr}^{3+}\) (\(d^3\)) yields a stable half-filled \(t_{2g}^3\) level in aqueous solution. Conversely, \(\mathrm{Mn}^{3+}\) (\(d^4\)) is a strong oxidising agent (\(E^\circ = +1.57\,\mathrm{V}\)) because reduction to \(\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}\) (\(d^5\)) yields a stable half-filled \(d^5\) subshell.
  • Disproportionation of \(\mathrm{Cu}^+\): In aqueous solution, \(\mathrm{Cu}^+\) undergoes disproportionation: \[2\mathrm{Cu}^+(\mathrm{aq}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Cu}^{2+}(\mathrm{aq}) + \mathrm{Cu}(\mathrm{s})\] because the much more negative hydration enthalpy of \(\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}(\mathrm{aq})\) more than compensates for the second ionisation enthalpy of \(\mathrm{Cu}\). Also, \(\mathrm{CuI_2}\) does not exist because \(\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}\) oxidises \(\mathrm{I}^-\) to \(\mathrm{I_2}\): \(2\mathrm{Cu}^{2+} + 4\mathrm{I}^- \rightarrow \mathrm{Cu_2I_2}(\mathrm{s}) + \mathrm{I_2}\).
  • Oxides vs Fluorides: Both oxygen and fluorine stabilise high oxidation states due to small size and high electronegativity, but oxygen is superior because it forms \(p\pi\text{–}d\pi\) multiple bonds (e.g., highest Mn fluoride is \(\mathrm{MnF_4}\), whereas highest Mn oxide is \(\mathrm{Mn_2O_7}\)). As oxidation number rises, oxide character shifts from basic (\(\mathrm{CrO}, \mathrm{V_2O_3}\)) to amphoteric (\(\mathrm{Cr_2O_3}, \mathrm{V_2O_5}\)) to acidic (\(\mathrm{CrO_3}, \mathrm{Mn_2O_7}\)).

4. ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाएँ, इलेक्ट्रोड विभव एवं हैलाइड/ऑक्साइड स्थायित्व

हिन्दी
  • परिवर्ती ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाएँ: संक्रमण धातुएँ इकाई के अंतर से विभिन्न ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाएँ दर्शाती हैं (जैसे \(\mathrm{V}^{2+}, \mathrm{V}^{3+}, \mathrm{V}^{4+}, \mathrm{V}^{5+}\))। \(3d\) श्रेणी में \(\mathrm{Mn}\) सर्वाधिक (\(+2\) से \(+7\)) अवस्थाएँ दर्शाता है, जबकि \(\mathrm{Sc}\) केवल \(+3\) तथा \(\mathrm{Zn}\) केवल \(+2\) दर्शाता है।
  • वर्ग प्रवृत्ति (\(p\)-ब्लॉक के विपरीत): \(d\)-ब्लॉक के वर्गों में नीचे जाने पर उच्च ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाएँ अधिक स्थायी होती हैं; अतः \(\mathrm{Mo(VI)}\) और \(\mathrm{W(VI)}\), \(\mathrm{Cr(VI)}\) की तुलना में अधिक स्थायी हैं।
  • शून्य ऑक्सीकरण अवस्था: \(\pi\)-ग्राही लिगैंड जैसे \(\mathrm{CO}\), \(\mathrm{Ni(CO)_4}\) तथा \(\mathrm{Fe(CO)_5}\) में शून्य ऑक्सीकरण अवस्था को स्थायी बनाते हैं।
  • मानक इलेक्ट्रोड विभव \(E^\circ(M^{2+}/M)\): कॉपर एकमात्र \(3d\) धातु है जिसका \(E^\circ(\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}/\mathrm{Cu}) = +0.34\,\mathrm{V}\) धनात्मक है, अतः यह तनु अम्लों से \(\mathrm{H_2}\) मुक्त नहीं करता। इसकी उच्च कणन एवं आयनन एन्थैल्पी की भरपाई जलयोजन एन्थैल्पी से नहीं हो पाती।
  • \(E^\circ(M^{3+}/M^{2+})\) एवं रेडॉक्स व्यवहार: दोनों \(d^4\) होने पर भी \(\mathrm{Cr}^{2+}\) प्रबल अपचायक है (क्योंकि \(\mathrm{Cr}^{3+}\) में स्थायी \(t_{2g}^3\) विन्यास बनता है), जबकि \(\mathrm{Mn}^{3+}\) प्रबल ऑक्सीकारक है (क्योंकि \(\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}\) में स्थायी \(d^5\) विन्यास बनता है)।
  • \(\mathrm{Cu}^+\) का असमानुपातन: जलीय विलयन में \(2\mathrm{Cu}^+(\mathrm{aq}) \rightarrow \mathrm{Cu}^{2+}(\mathrm{aq}) + \mathrm{Cu}(\mathrm{s})\) होता है क्योंकि \(\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}\) की अत्यधिक ऋणात्मक जलयोजन एन्थैल्पी इसकी द्वितीय आयनन एन्थैल्पी की भरपाई कर देती है। \(\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}\) आयोडाइड को \(\mathrm{I_2}\) में ऑक्सीकृत कर देता है: \(2\mathrm{Cu}^{2+} + 4\mathrm{I}^- \rightarrow \mathrm{Cu_2I_2}(\mathrm{s}) + \mathrm{I_2}\)।
  • ऑक्साइड बनाम फ्लुओराइड: ऑक्सीजन बहु-आबंध (\(p\pi\text{–}d\pi\)) बनाने की क्षमता के कारण फ्लुओरीन से भी उच्च ऑक्सीकरण अवस्था को स्थायी करती है (जैसे \(\mathrm{MnF_4}\) की तुलना में \(\mathrm{Mn_2O_7}\))। ऑक्सीकरण संख्या बढ़ने पर ऑक्साइड की प्रकृति क्षारीय (\(\mathrm{CrO}\)) से उभयधर्मी (\(\mathrm{Cr_2O_3}\)) और फिर अम्लीय (\(\mathrm{CrO_3}, \mathrm{Mn_2O_7}\)) हो जाती है।

5. Magnetism, Colour, Complexation, Catalysis, and Interstitial Compounds

English
Spin-Only Magnetic Moment: \(\mu = \sqrt{n(n+2)}\,\mathrm{BM}\)  (where \(n\) = number of unpaired electrons; \(1\,\mathrm{BM} \approx 9.27 \times 10^{-24}\,\mathrm{J\,T^{-1}}\))
  • Magnetism: Ions with \(n \ge 1\) are paramagnetic (maximum \(\mu = \sqrt{35} \approx 5.92\,\mathrm{BM}\) for \(d^5\) ions like \(\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}\) and \(\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}\)); ions with \(n = 0\) (\(d^0\) or \(d^{10}\)) are diamagnetic.
  • Colour of Aquated Ions: Visible light excites an electron between split \(d\) orbitals (\(d\text{–}d\) transition). The observed colour is complementary to the absorbed wavelength. Thus, \(d^0\) (\(\mathrm{Sc}^{3+}, \mathrm{Ti}^{4+}\)) and \(d^{10}\) (\(\mathrm{Cu}^+, \mathrm{Zn}^{2+}\)) ions are colourless, whereas \(d^1\text{–}d^9\) ions are coloured (e.g., \(\mathrm{Ti}^{3+}\) purple, \(\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}\) green, \(\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}\) yellow, \(\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}\) pink, \(\mathrm{Ni}^{2+}\) green, \(\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}\) blue).
  • Complex Formation & Catalysis: Small ionic size, high charge, and vacant \(d\) orbitals promote coordination complexes. Multiple oxidation states and surface adsorption enable catalysis—for example, \(\mathrm{V_2O_5}\) (Contact Process), finely divided \(\mathrm{Fe}\) (Haber Process), \(\mathrm{Ni}\) (hydrogenation), \(\mathrm{TiCl_4}/\mathrm{Al(CH_3)_3}\) (Ziegler–Natta catalyst), and \(\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}\) catalysing \(2\mathrm{I}^- + \mathrm{S_2O_8^{2-}} \rightarrow \mathrm{I_2} + 2\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}\).
  • Interstitial Compounds & Alloys: Small non-metal atoms (\(\mathrm{H, C, N}\)) trapped in metal lattice voids yield non-stoichiometric compounds (\(\mathrm{TiC}, \mathrm{Mn_4N}, \mathrm{VH_{0.56}}\)) that have higher melting points than pure metals, extreme hardness, metallic conductivity, and chemical inertness. Similar metallic radii (within ~15%) allow transition metals to form solid-solution alloys such as brass (\(\mathrm{Cu\text{–}Zn}\)), bronze (\(\mathrm{Cu\text{–}Sn}\)), and stainless steel.

5. चुंबकीय गुण, रंग, संकुल निर्माण, उत्प्रेरण एवं अंतराकाशी यौगिक

हिन्दी
चक्रण-मात्र चुंबकीय आघूर्ण: \(\mu = \sqrt{n(n+2)}\,\mathrm{BM}\)  (जहाँ \(n\) = अयुग्मित इलेक्ट्रॉनों की संख्या)
  • चुंबकत्व: \(n \ge 1\) वाले आयन अनुचुंबकीय होते हैं (\(\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}\) तथा \(\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}\) जैसे \(d^5\) आयनों के लिए अधिकतम \(\mu \approx 5.92\,\mathrm{BM}\)), जबकि \(d^0\) और \(d^{10}\) आयन प्रतिचुंबकीय होते हैं।
  • जलीय आयनों का रंग: दृश्य प्रकाश के अवशोषण से \(d\text{–}d\) संक्रमण होता है और पूरक रंग दिखाई देता है। अतः \(d^0\) (\(\mathrm{Sc}^{3+}, \mathrm{Ti}^{4+}\)) तथा \(d^{10}\) (\(\mathrm{Cu}^+, \mathrm{Zn}^{2+}\)) आयन रंगहीन होते हैं, जबकि \(d^1\text{–}d^9\) आयन रंगीन होते हैं (जैसे \(\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}\) नीला, \(\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}\) हरा, \(\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}\) गुलाबी)।
  • संकुल एवं उत्प्रेरण: छोटा आकार, उच्च आवेश और रिक्त \(d\) कक्षक संकुल निर्माण में सहायक हैं। परिवर्ती ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाओं के कारण ये उत्तम उत्प्रेरक हैं—जैसे \(\mathrm{V_2O_5}\) (संपर्क प्रक्रम), \(\mathrm{Fe}\) (हेबर प्रक्रम), \(\mathrm{Ni}\) (हाइड्रोजनीकरण), और \(\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}\) द्वारा \(2\mathrm{I}^- + \mathrm{S_2O_8^{2-}} \rightarrow \mathrm{I_2} + 2\mathrm{SO_4^{2-}}\) का उत्प्रेरण।
  • अंतराकाशी यौगिक एवं मिश्रातु: धातु जालक की रिक्तियों में छोटे परमाणु (\(\mathrm{H, C, N}\)) फँसने से अरससमीकरणमितीय यौगिक (\(\mathrm{TiC}, \mathrm{Mn_4N}, \mathrm{VH_{0.56}}\)) बनते हैं जो अत्यंत कठोर, उच्च गलनांक वाले, विद्युत चालक और रासायनिक रूप से अक्रिय होते हैं। लगभग समान त्रिज्या (~15% के भीतर) के कारण ये पीतल (\(\mathrm{Cu\text{–}Zn}\)), कांसा (\(\mathrm{Cu\text{–}Sn}\)) और स्टील जैसी मिश्रातु बनाते हैं।

7. Inner Transition Elements (f-Block): Lanthanoids and Actinoids

English

The \(f\)-block consists of two series placed at the bottom of the periodic table: Lanthanoids (\(4f\) series: \(\mathrm{Ce}, Z=58\) to \(\mathrm{Lu}, Z=71\)) and Actinoids (\(5f\) series: \(\mathrm{Th}, Z=90\) to \(\mathrm{Lr}, Z=103\)).

  • Lanthanoids: Common configuration is \([\mathrm{Xe}]4f^{1-14}5d^{0-1}6s^2\), and all \(\mathrm{Ln}^{3+}\) ions have \([\mathrm{Xe}]4f^n\) (\(n = 1\text{–}14\)). The predominant oxidation state is \(+3\). Exceptions occur to attain \(f^0, f^7,\) or \(f^{14}\) stability: \(\mathrm{Ce}^{4+}\) (\(4f^0\)) and \(\mathrm{Tb}^{4+}\) (\(4f^7\)) are strong oxidants, while \(\mathrm{Eu}^{2+}\) (\(4f^7\)) and \(\mathrm{Yb}^{2+}\) (\(4f^{14}\)) are strong reductants. \(\mathrm{La}^{3+}\) (\(4f^0\)) and \(\mathrm{Lu}^{3+}\) (\(4f^{14}\)) are colourless and diamagnetic; other \(\mathrm{Ln}^{3+}\) ions are coloured and paramagnetic.
  • Mischmetall: An alloy containing ~95% lanthanoid metal, ~5% iron, and traces of \(\mathrm{S, C, Ca, Al}\), used in Mg-based alloys for bullets, shells, and lighter flints.
  • Actinoids: All actinoids are radioactive. Because \(5f\), \(6d\), and \(7s\) orbitals have comparable energies, actinoids show a much wider range of oxidation states (\(+3\) to \(+7\)), especially in the first half (\(\mathrm{Th}^{+4}, \mathrm{Pa}^{+5}, \mathrm{U}^{+6}, \mathrm{Np}^{+7}\)). Actinoid contraction is steeper from element to element than lanthanoid contraction because \(5f\) electrons shield nuclear charge even more poorly than \(4f\) electrons.

7. आंतरिक संक्रमण तत्व (f-ब्लॉक): लैंथेनॉयड एवं ऐक्टिनॉयड

Worked Examples

Example 1: Calculating Spin-Only Magnetic Moment of a \(3d\) Metal Ion

English

Problem: A transition metal \(M\) has atomic number \(26\). Suppose it forms an aqueous trivalent ion \(M^{3+}(\mathrm{aq})\) and an aqueous divalent ion \(M^{2+}(\mathrm{aq})\) in two separate test tubes. Calculate the difference in their spin-only magnetic moments (in \(\mathrm{BM}\)).

Solution:

Atomic number \(Z = 26\) corresponds to iron (\(\mathrm{Fe}\)), with ground-state configuration \([\mathrm{Ar}]3d^6 4s^2\).

  • For \(\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}\): configuration is \([\mathrm{Ar}]3d^6\), which has \(n = 4\) unpaired electrons. \[\mu(\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}) = \sqrt{4(4+2)} = \sqrt{24} \approx 4.90\,\mathrm{BM}\]
  • For \(\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}\): configuration is \([\mathrm{Ar}]3d^5\), which has \(n = 5\) unpaired electrons. \[\mu(\mathrm{Fe}^{3+}) = \sqrt{5(5+2)} = \sqrt{35} \approx 5.92\,\mathrm{BM}\]

Difference: \(5.92 - 4.90 = 1.02\,\mathrm{BM}\).

Example 2: Stoichiometry of Permanganate Redox Titration in Different Media

English

Problem: Assume \(1\,\mathrm{mol}\) of \(\mathrm{KMnO_4}\) is used separately to oxidise potassium iodide (\(\mathrm{KI}\)) in (a) strongly acidic medium and (b) faintly alkaline medium. How many moles of \(\mathrm{I^-}\) are oxidised in each case, and what are the iodine-containing products?

Solution:

  • (a) Acidic medium: \(\mathrm{MnO_4^-}\) accepts \(5e^-\) to form \(\mathrm{Mn}^{2+}\), while \(2\mathrm{I^-} \rightarrow \mathrm{I_2} + 2e^-\) (1 mole of \(\mathrm{I^-}\) loses \(1e^-\)). \[2\mathrm{MnO_4^-} + 10\mathrm{I^-} + 16\mathrm{H}^+ \rightarrow 2\mathrm{Mn}^{2+} + 5\mathrm{I_2} + 8\mathrm{H_2O}\] Thus, \(1\,\mathrm{mol}\) of \(\mathrm{KMnO_4}\) oxidises \(5\,\mathrm{mol}\) of \(\mathrm{I^-}\) to \(\mathrm{I_2}\).
  • (b) Faintly alkaline medium: \(\mathrm{MnO_4^-}\) accepts \(3e^-\) to form \(\mathrm{MnO_2}\), while \(\mathrm{I^-}\) is oxidised to iodate (\(\mathrm{IO_3^-}\), oxidation state \(+5\), losing \(6e^-\) per \(\mathrm{I^-}\)). \[2\mathrm{MnO_4^-} + \mathrm{I^-} + \mathrm{H_2O} \rightarrow 2\mathrm{MnO_2} + \mathrm{IO_3^-} + 2\mathrm{OH}^-\] Thus, \(1\,\mathrm{mol}\) of \(\mathrm{KMnO_4}\) oxidises \(0.5\,\mathrm{mol}\) of \(\mathrm{I^-}\) to \(\mathrm{IO_3^-}\).

Example 3: Predicting Colour and Diamagnetism in \(d\)- and \(f\)-Block Ions

English

Problem: Identify which ion in the set \(\{\mathrm{Ti^{4+}}, \mathrm{V^{3+}}, \mathrm{Cu^+}, \mathrm{La^{3+}}\}\) is both coloured and paramagnetic in aqueous solution.

Solution:

  • \(\mathrm{Ti^{4+}}\) (\(Z=22\)): \([\mathrm{Ar}]3d^0 \Rightarrow\) no \(d\) electrons \(\Rightarrow\) colourless and diamagnetic.
  • \(\mathrm{Cu^+}\) (\(Z=29\)): \([\mathrm{Ar}]3d^{10} \Rightarrow\) completely filled \(d\) subshell \(\Rightarrow\) colourless and diamagnetic.
  • \(\mathrm{La^{3+}}\) (\(Z=57\)): \([\mathrm{Xe}]4f^0 \Rightarrow\) empty \(4f\) subshell \(\Rightarrow\) colourless and diamagnetic.
  • \(\mathrm{V^{3+}}\) (\(Z=23\)): \([\mathrm{Ar}]3d^2 \Rightarrow\) 2 unpaired electrons \(\Rightarrow\) green-coloured and paramagnetic (\(\mu = \sqrt{8} \approx 2.83\,\mathrm{BM}\)).

हिन्दी

प्रश्न: मान लीजिए \(1\,\mathrm{mol}\) \(\mathrm{KMnO_4}\) का उपयोग (a) प्रबल अम्लीय माध्यम और (b) हल्के क्षारीय माध्यम में पोटैशियम आयोडाइड (\(\mathrm{KI}\)) को ऑक्सीकृत करने के लिए किया जाता है। प्रत्येक स्थिति में कितने मोल \(\mathrm{I^-}\) ऑक्सीकृत होंगे और आयोडीन युक्त उत्पाद क्या होंगे?

हल:

Practice Question 2 (Assertion–Reason)

Assertion (A): Zirconium (\(\mathrm{Zr}\)) and hafnium (\(\mathrm{Hf}\)) have almost identical atomic radii and closely similar chemical properties.

Reason (R): The filling of \(4f\) orbitals before \(5d\) orbitals causes lanthanoid contraction due to poor shielding by \(4f\) electrons.

9. Common Pitfalls and NEET UG Revision Checklist

English
  • Pitfall 1: Removing \(3d\) electrons before \(4s\) when writing cation configurations. Fix: Always remove \(ns\) electrons first (e.g., \(\mathrm{Fe}^{2+}\) is \(3d^6\), not \(3d^4 4s^2\)).
  • Pitfall 2: Treating \(\mathrm{Cu, Ag, Au}\) as non-transition metals because of \(d^{10}s^1\) ground state. Fix: Their common ions (\(\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}, \mathrm{Ag}^{2+}, \mathrm{Au}^{3+}\)) have incomplete \(d\) subshells, so they ARE transition metals; only Group 12 (\(\mathrm{Zn, Cd, Hg}\)) are not.
  • Pitfall 3: Confusing acidic and alkaline \(\mathrm{KMnO_4}\) products with \(\mathrm{I^-}\). Fix: Acidic \(\mathrm{MnO_4^-}\) gives \(\mathrm{I_2}\) (\(n=5\)); neutral/alkaline \(\mathrm{MnO_4^-}\) gives \(\mathrm{IO_3^-}\) (\(n=3\)).
  • Pitfall 4: Assuming \(\mathrm{MnO_4^-}\) is paramagnetic because \(\mathrm{Mn}\) has unpaired electrons in lower states. Fix: In \(\mathrm{MnO_4^-}\), \(\mathrm{Mn}\) is \(+7\) (\(d^0\)), so it is diamagnetic (whereas \(\mathrm{MnO_4^{2-}}\) is \(d^1\), paramagnetic).

9. सामान्य त्रुटियाँ एवं NEET UG पुनरावृत्ति जाँच-सूची

हिन्दी
  • त्रुटि 1: धनायन विन्यास लिखते समय \(4s\) से पहले \(3d\) इलेक्ट्रॉन निकालना। सुधार: सदैव पहले \(ns\) इलेक्ट्रॉन निकालें (जैसे \(\mathrm{Fe}^{2+} = 3d^6\))।
  • त्रुटि 2: \(\mathrm{Cu, Ag, Au}\) को गैर-संक्रमण धातु मान लेना। सुधार: इनके आयनों (\(\mathrm{Cu}^{2+}, \mathrm{Ag}^{2+}, \mathrm{Au}^{3+}\)) में अपूर्ण \(d\) उपकोश होता है; केवल वर्ग 12 (\(\mathrm{Zn, Cd, Hg}\)) गैर-संक्रमण हैं।
  • त्रुटि 3: \(\mathrm{I^-}\) के साथ अम्लीय और क्षारीय \(\mathrm{KMnO_4}\) के उत्पादों में भ्रम। सुधार: अम्लीय में \(\mathrm{I_2}\) (\(n=5\)) तथा हल्के क्षारीय में \(\mathrm{IO_3^-}\) (\(n=3\)) बनता है।
  • त्रुटि 4: \(\mathrm{MnO_4^-}\) को अनुचुंबकीय मानना। सुधार: \(\mathrm{MnO_4^-}\) में \(\mathrm{Mn(+7)}\) (\(d^0\)) प्रतिचुंबकीय है, जबकि \(\mathrm{MnO_4^{2-}}\) (\(d^1\)) अनुचुंबकीय है।

10. Concise Final Recap

English

Transition elements (\(d\)-block, excluding Group 12) owe their rich chemistry—variable oxidation states, coloured ions, paramagnetism (\(\mu = \sqrt{n(n+2)}\,\mathrm{BM}\)), catalytic activity, complexation, and interstitial compounds—to partially filled \((n-1)d\) orbitals and small energy gaps between \((n-1)d\) and \(ns\) levels. Down the \(d\)-block, higher oxidation states gain stability and \(4d/5d\) radii converge due to lanthanoid contraction. In the \(f\)-block, lanthanoids predominantly exhibit \(+3\) with moderate contraction, whereas radioactive actinoids show wider oxidation states (\(+3\) to \(+7\)) and stronger contraction.

10. संक्षिप्त अंतिम सारांश

हिन्दी

संक्रमण तत्वों (वर्ग 12 को छोड़कर \(d\)-ब्लॉक) के विशिष्ट गुण—परिवर्ती ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाएँ, रंगीन आयन, अनुचुंबकत्व (\(\mu = \sqrt{n(n+2)}\,\mathrm{BM}\)), उत्प्रेरकीय सक्रियता, संकुल निर्माण और अंतराकाशी यौगिक—आंशिक रूप से भरे \((n-1)d\) कक्षकों तथा \((n-1)d\) व \(ns\) स्तरों के मध्य कम ऊर्जा अंतर के कारण होते हैं। वर्ग में नीचे उच्च ऑक्सीकरण अवस्थाओं का स्थायित्व बढ़ता है और लैंथेनॉयड आकुंचन से \(4d/5d\) त्रिज्याएँ समान हो जाती हैं। \(f\)-ब्लॉक में लैंथेनॉयड मुख्यतः \(+3\) अवस्था दर्शाते हैं, जबकि रेडियोधर्मी ऐक्टिनॉयड \(+3\) से \(+7\) तक की अवस्थाएँ तथा अधिक तीव्र आकुंचन प्रदर्शित करते हैं।
