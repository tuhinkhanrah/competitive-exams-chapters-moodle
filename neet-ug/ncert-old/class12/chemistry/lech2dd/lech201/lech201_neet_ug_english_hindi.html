English

Learning Objectives and Overview

Organohalogen compounds are formed when one or more hydrogen atoms of an aliphatic or aromatic hydrocarbon are replaced by halogen atoms (\(\mathrm{F}, \mathrm{Cl}, \mathrm{Br}, \mathrm{I}\)). In haloalkanes (alkyl halides), the halogen is bonded to an \(sp^3\)-hybridized carbon, whereas in haloarenes (aryl halides), the halogen is bonded directly to an \(sp^2\)-hybridized carbon of an aromatic ring. These compounds serve as vital industrial solvents, synthetic intermediates, and clinically important molecules (such as the antibiotic chloramphenicol, the thyroid hormone thyroxine, the antimalarial chloroquine, and the surgical anaesthetic halothane).

By mastering this tutorial, you will be able to:

  • Classify organohalides by halogen count, carbon hybridization (\(sp^3\) vs. \(sp^2\)), and carbon degree (\(1^\circ, 2^\circ, 3^\circ\)), and assign IUPAC names accurately.
  • Predict physical property trends (boiling point, melting point, density, and solubility) from molecular structure and intermolecular forces.
  • Select appropriate synthetic routes to haloalkanes and haloarenes from alcohols, hydrocarbons, amines, and other halides.
  • Distinguish \(\mathrm{S_N1}\) and \(\mathrm{S_N2}\) nucleophilic substitutions using kinetics, carbocation stability, steric hindrance, and stereochemical outcomes (inversion vs. racemisation).
  • Compare substitution and \(\beta\)-elimination pathways, apply Zaitsev's rule, and handle organometallic (Grignard) reagents safely.
  • Explain why haloarenes resist nucleophilic substitution, how nitro groups activate them, and why halogens are ortho/para-directing yet deactivating in electrophilic aromatic substitution.
  • Recall key uses, toxicity, and environmental impacts of polyhalogen compounds such as chloroform, iodoform, \(\mathrm{CCl_4}\), freons, and DDT.

Classification and IUPAC Nomenclature

Classification by Halogen Count

Organohalides are grouped as monohalo, dihalo, or polyhalo (tri-, tetra-, etc.) compounds depending on whether one, two, or more halogen atoms are present. Dihaloalkanes carrying two identical halogens are further divided into:

  • Geminal dihalides (gem-dihalides): Both halogen atoms are attached to the same carbon atom (common name: alkylidene halide; e.g., \(\mathrm{CH_3CHCl_2}\), ethylidene chloride or 1,1-dichloroethane).
  • Vicinal dihalides (vic-dihalides): Halogen atoms are attached to adjacent carbon atoms (common name: alkylene dihalide; e.g., \(\mathrm{ClCH_2CH_2Cl}\), ethylene dichloride or 1,2-dichloroethane).

Classification by Hybridization of the C–X Carbon

For monohalogen compounds, the reactivity depends strongly on the hybridization of the carbon bearing the halogen:

  • \(sp^3\;\mathrm{C-X}\) compounds:
    • Alkyl halides (\(\mathrm{R-X}\)): General formula \(\mathrm{C}_n\mathrm{H}_{2n+1}\mathrm{X}\); classified as primary (\(1^\circ\)), secondary (\(2^\circ\)), or tertiary (\(3^\circ\)) according to whether the halogen-bearing carbon is attached to 0/1, 2, or 3 other carbons.
    • Allylic halides: Halogen is bonded to an \(sp^3\) carbon adjacent to a \(\mathrm{C=C}\) double bond (e.g., \(\mathrm{CH_2=CH-CH_2Br}\), 3-bromopropene).
    • Benzylic halides: Halogen is bonded to an \(sp^3\) carbon directly attached to an aromatic ring (e.g., \(\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2Cl}\), chlorophenylmethane).
  • \(sp^2\;\mathrm{C-X}\) compounds:
    • Vinylic halides: Halogen is attached directly to an \(sp^2\) carbon of a \(\mathrm{C=C}\) double bond (e.g., \(\mathrm{CH_2=CHCl}\), chloroethene).
    • Aryl halides: Halogen is attached directly to an \(sp^2\) carbon of an aromatic ring (e.g., \(\mathrm{C_6H_5Br}\), bromobenzene).

Nomenclature Rules

In the common system, alkyl halides are named by stating the alkyl group followed by the halide (e.g., isopropyl chloride). In the IUPAC system, they are named as halosubstituted hydrocarbons using lowest-locant numbering, alphabetical prefixes, and numerical locants (1,2-, 1,3-, 1,4- instead of o-, m-, p-) for disubstituted benzenes.

हैलोजन परमाणुओं की संख्या के आधार पर वर्गीकरण

एक, दो या अधिक हैलोजन परमाणुओं की उपस्थिति के आधार पर इन्हें क्रमशः मोनोहैलो, डाइहैलो या पॉलिहैलो (ट्राइ-, टेट्रा- आदि) यौगिकों में बाँटा जाता है। समान हैलोजन युक्त डाइहैलोऐल्केन दो प्रकार के होते हैं:

  • जेमिनल डाइहैलाइड (gem-डाइहैलाइड): दोनों हैलोजन परमाणु एक ही कार्बन परमाणु से जुड़े होते हैं (सामान्य नाम: ऐल्किलिडीन हैलाइड; जैसे \(\mathrm{CH_3CHCl_2}\), एथिलिडीन क्लोराइड या 1,1-डाइक्लोरोएथेन)।
  • विसिनल डाइहैलाइड (vic-डाइहैलाइड): दोनों हैलोजन परमाणु निकटवर्ती कार्बन परमाणुओं से जुड़े होते हैं (सामान्य नाम: ऐल्किलीन डाइहैलाइड; जैसे \(\mathrm{ClCH_2CH_2Cl}\), एथिलीन डाइक्लोराइड या 1,2-डाइक्लोरोएथेन)।

  • \(sp^3\;\mathrm{C-X}\) आबंध युक्त यौगिक:
    • ऐल्किल हैलाइड (\(\mathrm{R-X}\)): सामान्य सूत्र \(\mathrm{C}_n\mathrm{H}_{2n+1}\mathrm{X}\); हैलोजन युक्त कार्बन से जुड़े अन्य कार्बनों की संख्या के आधार पर प्राथमिक (\(1^\circ\)), द्वितीयक (\(2^\circ\)) तथा तृतीयक (\(3^\circ\)) में वर्गीकृत।
    • ऐलिलिक हैलाइड: हैलोजन परमाणु \(\mathrm{C=C}\) द्वि-आबंध के निकटवर्ती \(sp^3\)-संकरित कार्बन से जुड़ा होता है (जैसे \(\mathrm{CH_2=CH-CH_2Br}\), 3-ब्रोमोप्रोपीन)।
    • बेंजिलिक हैलाइड: हैलोजन परमाणु ऐरोमैटिक वलय से सीधे जुड़े \(sp^3\)-संकरित कार्बन पर स्थित होता है (जैसे \(\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2Cl}\), क्लोरोफेनिलमेथेन)।
  • \(sp^2\;\mathrm{C-X}\) आबंध युक्त यौगिक:
    • वाइनिलिक हैलाइड: हैलोजन परमाणु \(\mathrm{C=C}\) द्वि-आबंध के \(sp^2\)-संकरित कार्बन से सीधे जुड़ा होता है (जैसे \(\mathrm{CH_2=CHCl}\), क्लोरोएथीन)।
    • ऐरिल हैलाइड: हैलोजन परमाणु ऐरोमैटिक वलय के \(sp^2\)-संकरित कार्बन से सीधे जुड़ा होता है (जैसे \(\mathrm{C_6H_5Br}\), ब्रोमोबेन्ज़ीन)।

Nature of the C–X Bond and Physical Properties

Because halogens are more electronegative than carbon, the \(\mathrm{C^{\delta+}-X^{\delta-}}\) bond is polarised. Moving down Group 17 from \(\mathrm{F}\) to \(\mathrm{I}\), atomic radius increases, so the \(\mathrm{C-X}\) bond length increases and bond dissociation enthalpy decreases.

Bond Bond Length (pm) Bond Enthalpy (\(\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)) Dipole Moment (Debye)
\(\mathrm{CH_3-F}\) 139 452 1.847
\(\mathrm{CH_3-Cl}\) 178 351 1.860
\(\mathrm{CH_3-Br}\) 193 293 1.830
\(\mathrm{CH_3-I}\) 214 234 1.636
NEET High-Yield Fact — Dipole Moment Anomaly: Even though fluorine is more electronegative than chlorine, \(\mathrm{CH_3Cl}\) (\(1.860\;\mathrm{D}\)) has a slightly higher dipole moment than \(\mathrm{CH_3F}\) (\(1.847\;\mathrm{D}\)) because the \(\mathrm{C-F}\) bond (\(139\;\mathrm{pm}\)) is much shorter than the \(\mathrm{C-Cl}\) bond (\(178\;\mathrm{pm}\)), and dipole moment \(\mu = q \times d\). Also, among polychloromethanes, dipole moment decreases as \(\mathrm{CH_2Cl_2} > \mathrm{CHCl_3} > \mathrm{CCl_4}\) (zero due to tetrahedral symmetry).
  • Boiling Points: Haloalkanes have higher boiling points than parent alkanes of comparable molecular mass due to stronger dipole–dipole and van der Waals forces.
    • For the same alkyl group: \(\mathrm{R-I} > \mathrm{R-Br} > \mathrm{R-Cl} > \mathrm{R-F}\) (larger halogen size and electron cloud increase van der Waals dispersion forces).
    • For isomeric haloalkanes: Boiling point decreases with branching because compact, spherical molecules have smaller surface area and weaker intermolecular forces (e.g., 1-bromobutane \(375\;\mathrm{K}\) > 2-bromobutane \(364\;\mathrm{K}\) > 2-bromo-2-methylpropane \(346\;\mathrm{K}\)).
  • Melting Points of Dihalobenzenes: Boiling points of o-, m-, and p-dichlorobenzene are nearly identical (\(453\;\mathrm{K}\), \(446\;\mathrm{K}\), \(448\;\mathrm{K}\)), but the para-isomer has a markedly higher melting point (\(323\;\mathrm{K}\) vs. \(256\;\mathrm{K}\) and \(249\;\mathrm{K}\)) because its high symmetry allows tighter packing in the crystal lattice.
  • Density & Solubility: Bromo, iodo, and polychloro derivatives are denser than water; density increases with carbon count, halogen count, and halogen atomic mass. Haloalkanes are only slightly soluble in water because breaking water's strong hydrogen bonds requires more energy than is released by forming weaker haloalkane–water attractions; however, they dissolve readily in organic solvents.

Synthetic Routes to Haloalkanes and Haloarenes

Preparation of Haloalkanes

  1. From Alcohols: Alcohol reactivity with halogen acids follows \(3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ\).
    • Groove's / Lucas-type method: \(\mathrm{R-OH} + \mathrm{HCl} \xrightarrow{\mathrm{ZnCl_2}} \mathrm{R-Cl} + \mathrm{H_2O}\) (catalyst \(\mathrm{ZnCl_2}\) is needed for \(1^\circ\) and \(2^\circ\) alcohols; \(3^\circ\) alcohols react at room temperature with conc. \(\mathrm{HCl}\) alone).
    • Darzen's method (best for pure \(\mathrm{R-Cl}\)): \(\mathrm{R-OH} + \mathrm{SOCl_2} \longrightarrow \mathrm{R-Cl} + \mathrm{SO_2}\uparrow + \mathrm{HCl}\uparrow\). Both byproducts are gases that escape, yielding pure alkyl chloride.
    • Using phosphorus halides: \(3\mathrm{R-OH} + \mathrm{PX_3} \longrightarrow 3\mathrm{R-X} + \mathrm{H_3PO_3}\) (\(\mathrm{X = Cl, Br}\); \(\mathrm{PBr_3}\) and \(\mathrm{PI_3}\) are generated in situ using red \(\mathrm{P}/\mathrm{X_2}\)); \(\mathrm{R-OH} + \mathrm{PCl_5} \longrightarrow \mathrm{R-Cl} + \mathrm{POCl_3} + \mathrm{HCl}\).
    • Preparing \(\mathrm{R-Br}\) and \(\mathrm{R-I}\): Constant-boiling \(\mathrm{HBr}\) (\(48\%\)) or \(\mathrm{NaBr}/\mathrm{H_2SO_4}\) gives \(\mathrm{R-Br}\). For \(\mathrm{R-I}\), alcohols are heated with \(\mathrm{NaI}\) or \(\mathrm{KI}\) in \(95\%\;\mathrm{H_3PO_4}\) (never \(\mathrm{H_2SO_4}\), because \(\mathrm{H_2SO_4}\) oxidises the \(\mathrm{HI}\) formed into \(\mathrm{I_2}\)).

    Limitation: These alcohol methods fail for aryl halides because the \(\mathrm{C-O}\) bond in phenols has partial double-bond character due to resonance and is too strong to cleave.

  2. From Hydrocarbons:
    • Free-radical halogenation of alkanes: Yields a mixture of isomeric mono- and polyhaloalkanes, difficult to separate.
    • Electrophilic addition of \(\mathrm{HX}\) to alkenes: Follows Markovnikov's rule (e.g., propene + \(\mathrm{HI}\) gives 2-iodopropane as major product). With \(\mathrm{HBr}\) in the presence of organic peroxides (Kharasch effect), anti-Markovnikov addition occurs.
    • Addition of \(\mathrm{Br_2/CCl_4}\) to alkenes: Forms colourless vic-dibromides, discharging the reddish-brown colour of bromine (diagnostic test for unsaturation).
  3. Halogen Exchange Reactions:
    • Finkelstein reaction (for alkyl iodides): \(\mathrm{R-X} + \mathrm{NaI} \xrightarrow{\text{dry acetone}} \mathrm{R-I} + \mathrm{NaX}\downarrow\) (\(\mathrm{X = Cl, Br}\)). Precipitation of \(\mathrm{NaCl}/\mathrm{NaBr}\) in dry acetone drives the equilibrium forward (Le Chatelier's principle).
    • Swarts reaction (for alkyl fluorides): Heating \(\mathrm{R-Cl}\) or \(\mathrm{R-Br}\) with metallic fluorides such as \(\mathrm{AgF}\), \(\mathrm{Hg_2F_2}\), \(\mathrm{CoF_2}\), or \(\mathrm{SbF_3}\) yields \(\mathrm{R-F}\).

Preparation of Haloarenes

  1. Electrophilic Aromatic Substitution: Arenes react with \(\mathrm{Cl_2}\) or \(\mathrm{Br_2}\) in the dark in the presence of a Lewis acid catalyst (\(\mathrm{Fe}\) or anhydrous \(\mathrm{FeCl_3}\)) to give ortho and para isomers (easily separated by their melting point difference). Iodination is reversible and requires an oxidising agent (\(\mathrm{HNO_3}\) or \(\mathrm{HIO_4}\)) to oxidise \(\mathrm{HI}\) back to \(\mathrm{I_2}\). Direct fluorination is too violent to be practical.
  2. Sandmeyer's Reaction (from Primary Aromatic Amines): Diazotisation of aniline with \(\mathrm{NaNO_2} + \mathrm{HX}\) at \(273\text{–}278\;\mathrm{K}\) forms a benzenediazonium halide, which reacts with \(\mathrm{Cu_2Cl_2}\) or \(\mathrm{Cu_2Br_2}\) to give aryl chloride or bromide plus \(\mathrm{N_2}\). For aryl iodide, simply shaking the diazonium salt with aqueous \(\mathrm{KI}\) suffices (no cuprous salt needed).

Chemical Reactions of Haloalkanes and Stereochemistry

1. Nucleophilic Substitution (\(\mathrm{S_N2}\) vs. \(\mathrm{S_N1}\))

Because the \(\mathrm{C-X}\) bond is polar, an electron-rich nucleophile (\(\mathrm{Nu}^-\)) attacks the \(\delta+\) carbon and displaces the halide leaving group (\(\mathrm{X}^-\)). For a given alkyl group, reactivity follows leaving-group ability: \(\mathrm{R-I} > \mathrm{R-Br} > \mathrm{R-Cl} \gg \mathrm{R-F}\).

Ambident Nucleophiles

Species with two nucleophilic donor atoms are called ambident nucleophiles:

  • Cyanide (\(\mathrm{C\equiv N^-}\)): With ionic \(\mathrm{KCN}\), attack occurs through carbon to form alkyl cyanides (\(\mathrm{R-CN}\)) because the \(\mathrm{C-C}\) bond is stronger than \(\mathrm{C-N}\). With covalent \(\mathrm{AgCN}\), nitrogen's lone pair attacks to give isocyanides (\(\mathrm{R-NC}\)) as the major product.
  • Nitrite (\(\mathrm{O=N-O^-}\)): Ionic \(\mathrm{KNO_2}\) links through oxygen to yield alkyl nitrites (\(\mathrm{R-O-N=O}\)), whereas covalent \(\mathrm{AgNO_2}\) links through nitrogen to yield nitroalkanes (\(\mathrm{R-NO_2}\)).

Key Stereochemical Concepts

  • Optical Activity: Compounds that rotate the plane of plane-polarised light (measured by a polarimeter) are optically active: clockwise rotation is dextrorotatory (\(+ / d\)) and anticlockwise is laevorotatory (\(- / l\)).
  • Chirality & Enantiomers: A tetrahedral carbon attached to four different groups is a stereocentre (asymmetric carbon). Molecules non-superimposable on their mirror images are chiral, and the mirror-image pair are enantiomers (identical melting/boiling points and refractive index, opposite optical rotation).
  • Racemic Mixture: An equimolar (\(50:50\)) mixture of \((+)\) and \((-)\) enantiomers, denoted \(dl\) or \((\pm)\), has zero net optical rotation.
  • Retention of Configuration: Preservation of spatial arrangement around a stereocentre when no bond to it is broken (note: the sign of optical rotation may still change between reactant and product).

2. Elimination Reactions (\(\beta\)-Elimination) and Zaitsev's Rule

Heating a haloalkane with a \(\beta\)-hydrogen in alcoholic \(\mathrm{KOH}\) eliminates \(\mathrm{HX}\) across the \(\alpha\)- and \(\beta\)-carbons to form an alkene. By Zaitsev's rule, dehydrohalogenation preferentially yields the more substituted (more stable) alkene (e.g., 2-bromopentane gives \(81\%\) pent-2-ene and \(19\%\) pent-1-ene). Bulky bases (like tert-butoxide) and higher temperatures favour elimination over substitution.

3. Reactions with Metals

  • Grignard Reagents (\(\mathrm{RMgX}\)): Formed by reacting \(\mathrm{R-X}\) with \(\mathrm{Mg}\) in dry ether. The \(\mathrm{C^{\delta-}-Mg^{\delta+}}\) bond is highly polar; \(\mathrm{RMgX}\) reacts immediately with any proton donor (\(\mathrm{H_2O}\), \(\mathrm{ROH}\), \(\mathrm{RNH_2}\)) to give hydrocarbons (\(\mathrm{RMgX} + \mathrm{H_2O} \longrightarrow \mathrm{RH} + \mathrm{Mg(OH)X}\)), so strictly anhydrous conditions are mandatory.
  • Wurtz Reaction: \(2\mathrm{R-X} + 2\mathrm{Na} \xrightarrow{\text{dry ether}} \mathrm{R-R} + 2\mathrm{NaX}\) (doubles the carbon chain for symmetrical alkanes).

उभयदंती नाभिकरागी (Ambident Nucleophiles)

ऐसे नाभिकरागी जिनमें दो भिन्न आक्रमणकारी केंद्र होते हैं, उभयदंती नाभिकरागी कहलाते हैं:

  • सायनाइड (\(\mathrm{C\equiv N^-}\)): आयनिक \(\mathrm{KCN}\) के साथ आक्रमण कार्बन के माध्यम से होकर ऐल्किल सायनाइड (\(\mathrm{R-CN}\)) बनता है क्योंकि \(\mathrm{C-C}\) आबंध \(\mathrm{C-N}\) से अधिक स्थायी है। सहसंयोजी \(\mathrm{AgCN}\) के साथ नाइट्रोजन का एकाकी युग्म आक्रमण कर मुख्यतः आइसोसायनाइड (\(\mathrm{R-NC}\)) बनाता है।
  • नाइट्राइट (\(\mathrm{O=N-O^-}\)): आयनिक \(\mathrm{KNO_2}\) ऑक्सीजन से जुड़कर ऐल्किल नाइट्राइट (\(\mathrm{R-O-N=O}\)) बनाता है, जबकि सहसंयोजी \(\mathrm{AgNO_2}\) नाइट्रोजन से जुड़कर नाइट्रोऐल्केन (\(\mathrm{R-NO_2}\)) देता है।

Reactions of Haloarenes and Polyhalogen Compounds

Why Haloarenes Resist Nucleophilic Substitution

  1. Resonance Effect: Lone pairs on the halogen delocalise into the benzene ring, giving the \(\mathrm{C-X}\) bond partial double-bond character that makes cleavage difficult.
  2. Hybridization Difference: The \(sp^2\) carbon in haloarenes (\(33.3\%\;s\)-character) is more electronegative than the \(sp^3\) carbon in haloalkanes (\(25\%\;s\)-character), holding the electron pair more tightly. Thus, \(\mathrm{C-Cl}\) bond length in chlorobenzene is \(169\;\mathrm{pm}\) compared to \(177\;\mathrm{pm}\) in chloroalkanes.
  3. Instability of Phenyl Cation: Self-ionisation would form a phenyl cation that is not resonance-stabilised, ruling out \(\mathrm{S_N1}\).
  4. Electronic Repulsion: An electron-rich nucleophile faces electrostatic repulsion from the electron-rich \(\pi\)-cloud of the arene.

Consequently, converting chlorobenzene to phenol requires drastic conditions (\(623\;\mathrm{K}\), \(300\;\mathrm{atm}\) in aq. \(\mathrm{NaOH}\)). However, electron-withdrawing \(-\mathrm{NO_2}\) groups at ortho and para positions dramatically increase reactivity (4-nitrochlorobenzene reacts at \(443\;\mathrm{K}\); 2,4-dinitrochlorobenzene at \(368\;\mathrm{K}\); 2,4,6-trinitrochlorobenzene with warm water alone) because they stabilise the negative charge of the carbanion intermediate by resonance. A meta-\(\mathrm{NO_2}\) group has no activating effect because the negative charge never lands on the meta carbon in resonance structures.

Electrophilic Aromatic Substitution in Haloarenes

Halogens show a dual electronic effect: resonance (\(+R/+M\)) increases electron density at ortho and para positions (making them o, p-directing), while the stronger inductive (\(-I\)) effect withdraws electron density from the ring (making them ring-deactivating). Thus, halogenation, nitration, sulphonation, and Friedel–Crafts reactions occur more slowly than in benzene and yield para-substituted products as major isomers.

हैलोऐरीनों की अभिक्रियाएँ तथा पॉलिहैलोजन यौगिक

हैलोऐरीन नाभिकरागी प्रतिस्थापन के प्रति कम क्रियाशील क्यों होते हैं?

  1. अनुनाद प्रभाव: हैलोजन के एकाकी इलेक्ट्रॉन युग्म बेन्ज़ीन वलय के साथ संयुग्मन में रहते हैं, जिससे \(\mathrm{C-X}\) आबंध में आंशिक द्वि-आबंध गुण आ जाता है और इसे तोड़ना कठिन होता है।
  2. संकरण में अंतर: हैलोऐरीन का \(sp^2\) कार्बन (\(33.3\%\;s\)-गुण) हैलोऐल्केन के \(sp^3\) कार्बन (\(25\%\;s\)-गुण) से अधिक विद्युतऋणात्मक होता है। अतः क्लोरोबेन्ज़ीन में \(\mathrm{C-Cl}\) आबंध लंबाई \(169\;\mathrm{pm}\) होती है, जबकि क्लोरोऐल्केन में \(177\;\mathrm{pm}\) होती है।
  3. फेनिल धनायन का अस्थायित्व: स्वतः आयनन से बना फेनिल धनायन अनुनाद द्वारा स्थायी नहीं होता, अतः \(\mathrm{S_N1}\) क्रियाविधि संभव नहीं है।
  4. इलेक्ट्रॉनिक प्रतिकर्षण: इलेक्ट्रॉन-धनी नाभिकरागी वलय के \(\pi\)-इलेक्ट्रॉन अभ्र से प्रतिकर्षित होता है।

इसी कारण क्लोरोबेन्ज़ीन से फीनॉल बनाने हेतु कठोर परिस्थितियाँ (\(623\;\mathrm{K}\), \(300\;\mathrm{atm}\) जलीय \(\mathrm{NaOH}\)) चाहिए। परंतु ऑर्थो तथा पैरा स्थितियों पर इलेक्ट्रॉन-अपनायक \(-\mathrm{NO_2}\) समूह होने पर क्रियाशीलता बहुत बढ़ जाती है (4-नाइट्रोक्लोरोबेन्ज़ीन \(443\;\mathrm{K}\) पर; 2,4-डाइनाइट्रोक्लोरोबेन्ज़ीन \(368\;\mathrm{K}\) पर; 2,4,6-ट्राइनाइट्रोक्लोरोबेन्ज़ीन केवल गुनगुने जल से अभिक्रिया करता है) क्योंकि यह कार्बऋणायन मध्यवर्ती के ऋणावेश को अनुनाद द्वारा स्थायी करता है। मेटा-\(\mathrm{NO_2}\) का कोई सक्रियण प्रभाव नहीं होता क्योंकि अनुनादी संरचनाओं में ऋणावेश कभी भी मेटा कार्बन पर नहीं आता।

Worked Examples

Example 1: Predicting \(\mathrm{S_N2}\) vs. \(\mathrm{S_N1}\) Reactivity

Problem: Arrange 1-bromohexane, 2-bromohexane, and 2-bromo-2-methylpentane in increasing order of reactivity toward (a) \(\mathrm{S_N2}\) displacement and (b) \(\mathrm{S_N1}\) solvolysis.

Solution:

  • Classify each substrate by degree of carbon bearing \(\mathrm{Br}\): 1-bromohexane is \(1^\circ\), 2-bromohexane is \(2^\circ\), and 2-bromo-2-methylpentane is \(3^\circ\).
  • (a) \(\mathrm{S_N2}\) rate decreases as steric crowding around the electrophilic carbon increases: 2-bromo-2-methylpentane (\(3^\circ\)) < 2-bromohexane (\(2^\circ\)) < 1-bromohexane (\(1^\circ\)).
  • (b) \(\mathrm{S_N1}\) rate increases with carbocation stability (\(3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ\)): 1-bromohexane (\(1^\circ\)) < 2-bromohexane (\(2^\circ\)) < 2-bromo-2-methylpentane (\(3^\circ\)).

Example 2: Applying Zaitsev's Rule in Dehydrohalogenation

Problem: Identify all alkenes formed when 2-chloro-2-methylpentane is heated with sodium ethoxide in ethanol, and determine the major product.

Solution:

  • In 2-chloro-2-methylpentane, \(\mathrm{C2}\) is the \(\alpha\)-carbon. There are two non-equivalent sets of \(\beta\)-hydrogens: at \(\mathrm{C1}\) (methyl) and at \(\mathrm{C3}\) (methylene).
  • Loss of \(\mathrm{H}\) from \(\mathrm{C1}\) gives 2-methylpent-1-ene (a disubstituted alkene).
  • Loss of \(\mathrm{H}\) from \(\mathrm{C3}\) gives 2-methylpent-2-ene (a trisubstituted alkene).
  • By Zaitsev's rule, the more substituted alkene predominates, so 2-methylpent-2-ene is the major product.

Example 3: Handling Ambident Nucleophiles and Grignard Reagents

Problem: Starting from 1-bromopropane, write the major organic products obtained upon treatment with (a) \(\mathrm{AgCN}\) in aqueous ethanol, and (b) \(\mathrm{Mg}\) in dry ether followed by \(\mathrm{D_2O}\).

Solution:

  • (a) \(\mathrm{AgCN}\) is largely covalent, so nitrogen donates its lone pair to give propyl isocyanide (\(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2-NC}\)) as the chief product.
  • (b) Reaction with \(\mathrm{Mg}\)/dry ether forms propylmagnesium bromide (\(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2MgBr}\)). Subsequent hydrolysis with heavy water (\(\mathrm{D_2O}\)) transfers \(\mathrm{D}^+\) to the carbanionic carbon, yielding 1-deuteropropane (\(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2D}\)).

Example 4: Explaining Ortho/Para Activation in Nucleophilic Aromatic Substitution

Problem: Why does 1-chloro-2,4-dinitrobenzene hydrolyse with aqueous \(\mathrm{NaOH}\) at \(368\;\mathrm{K}\), whereas 1-chloro-3,5-dinitrobenzene shows no comparable activation?

Solution:

Nucleophilic attack of \(\mathrm{OH}^-\) on the \(\mathrm{C-Cl}\) carbon generates a resonance-stabilised carbanion intermediate in which negative charge is delocalised onto the ortho and para carbons relative to chlorine. When \(-\mathrm{NO_2}\) groups sit at the ortho (C2) and para (C4) positions, they directly delocalise and stabilise this negative charge. At the meta (C3, C5) positions, the negative charge never resides on the nitro-bearing carbons, so no resonance stabilisation occurs.

  • ज़ैत्सेव नियम के अनुसार अधिक प्रतिस्थापित ऐल्कीन मुख्य उत्पाद होता है, अतः 2-मेथिलपेन्ट-2-ईन मुख्य उत्पाद है।

उदाहरण 3: उभयदंती नाभिकरागी तथा ग्रिग्नार्ड अभिकर्मक

प्रश्न: 1-ब्रोमोप्रोपेन की अभिक्रिया (a) जलीय एथेनॉल में \(\mathrm{AgCN}\) से तथा (b) शुष्क ईथर में \(\mathrm{Mg}\) और फिर \(\mathrm{D_2O}\) से कराने पर प्राप्त मुख्य कार्बनिक उत्पाद लिखिए।

हल:

  • (a) सहसंयोजी \(\mathrm{AgCN}\) में नाइट्रोजन आक्रमण करता है, जिससे मुख्य उत्पाद प्रोपिल आइसोसायनाइड (\(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2-NC}\)) बनता है।
  • (b) \(\mathrm{Mg}\)/शुष्क ईथर से प्रोपिलमैग्नीशियम ब्रोमाइड (\(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2MgBr}\)) बनता है, जो भारी जल (\(\mathrm{D_2O}\)) से अभिक्रिया कर 1-ड्यूटेरोप्रोपेन (\(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2D}\)) देता है।

उदाहरण 4: नाभिकरागी ऐरोमैटिक प्रतिस्थापन में ऑर्थो/पैरा सक्रियण

प्रश्न: 1-क्लोरो-2,4-डाइनाइट्रोबेन्ज़ीन \(368\;\mathrm{K}\) पर जलीय \(\mathrm{NaOH}\) से जल-अपघटित हो जाता है, जबकि 1-क्लोरो-3,5-डाइनाइट्रोबेन्ज़ीन में ऐसा सक्रियण क्यों नहीं दिखता?

हल:

\(\mathrm{C-Cl}\) कार्बन पर \(\mathrm{OH}^-\) के आक्रमण से बने कार्बऋणायन मध्यवर्ती में ऋणावेश क्लोरीन के सापेक्ष ऑर्थो तथा पैरा कार्बनों पर विस्थानीकृत होता है। जब \(-\mathrm{NO_2}\) समूह ऑर्थो (C2) व पैरा (C4) पर होते हैं, तो वे इस ऋणावेश को अनुनाद द्वारा स्थायी करते हैं। मेटा (C3, C5) पर होने पर ऋणावेश कभी भी नाइट्रो-युक्त कार्बन पर नहीं आता, अतः कोई सक्रियण नहीं होता।

Practice Question 2

Arrange the following isomeric chlorides in order of increasing boiling point:

I. 1-Chlorobutane    II. 2-Chloro-2-methylpropane    III. 2-Chlorobutane

  • (A) \(\text{I} < \text{III} < \text{II}\)
  • (B) \(\text{II} < \text{III} < \text{I}\)
  • (C) \(\text{III} < \text{II} < \text{I}\)
  • (D) \(\text{II} < \text{I} < \text{III}\)

Answer: (B) \(\text{II} < \text{III} < \text{I}\)

Solution: Among isomeric alkyl halides, boiling point decreases as branching increases because branching makes the molecule more compact, reducing surface area and van der Waals attractions. Thus, the most branched isomer (2-chloro-2-methylpropane, II) has the lowest boiling point, followed by 2-chlorobutane (III), and the straight-chain isomer 1-chlorobutane (I) has the highest boiling point.

Common Pitfalls and NEET Revision Checklist

Common Exam Traps to Avoid:
  • Do not use \(\mathrm{H_2SO_4}\) with \(\mathrm{KI}\) to convert alcohols to \(\mathrm{R-I}\); \(\mathrm{H_2SO_4}\) oxidises \(\mathrm{HI}\) to \(\mathrm{I_2}\). Use \(95\%\;\mathrm{H_3PO_4}\).
  • Do not confuse aqueous \(\mathrm{KOH}\) (gives alcohol via substitution) with alcoholic \(\mathrm{KOH}\) (gives alkene via \(\beta\)-elimination).
  • Do not confuse \(\mathrm{KCN}\) (gives nitrile, \(\mathrm{R-CN}\)) with \(\mathrm{AgCN}\) (gives isonitrile, \(\mathrm{R-NC}\)), or \(\mathrm{KNO_2}\) (\(\mathrm{R-ONO}\)) with \(\mathrm{AgNO_2}\) (\(\mathrm{R-NO_2}\)).
  • Remember that retention of configuration does not guarantee the same \((+)\) or \((-)\) sign of optical rotation in product vs. reactant.
  • In dipole moments, \(\mathrm{CH_3Cl} > \mathrm{CH_3F} > \mathrm{CH_3Br} > \mathrm{CH_3I}\), and chlorobenzene has a lower dipole moment than cyclohexyl chloride due to \(sp^2\) carbon electronegativity and resonance.

Quick Revision Checklist:

  • Can you distinguish allylic, benzylic, vinylic, aryl, geminal, and vicinal halides on sight?
  • Can you write the reagents for Darzen's, Finkelstein, Swarts, Sandmeyer, Wurtz, Wurtz–Fittig, and Fittig reactions?
  • Can you rank substrates for \(\mathrm{S_N1}\) vs. \(\mathrm{S_N2}\) and predict Zaitsev major alkenes?

Concise Final Recap

Haloalkanes (\(sp^3\;\mathrm{C-X}\)) are polar substrates that readily undergo \(\mathrm{S_N2}\) substitution (favoured by \(1^\circ\) halides, polar aprotic solvents, giving inversion) or \(\mathrm{S_N1}\) substitution (favoured by \(3^\circ\)/allylic/benzylic halides, polar protic solvents, giving racemisation), as well as Zaitsev \(\beta\)-elimination with alcoholic \(\mathrm{KOH}\) and Grignard formation with \(\mathrm{Mg}\)/dry ether. Haloarenes (\(sp^2\;\mathrm{C-X}\)) resist nucleophilic substitution due to resonance, shorter \(\mathrm{C-X}\) bonds, and phenyl cation instability unless activated by ortho/para \(-\mathrm{NO_2}\) groups, and undergo o, p-directed yet deactivated electrophilic aromatic substitution.

संक्षिप्त अंतिम पुनरावलोकन

हैलोऐल्केन (\(sp^3\;\mathrm{C-X}\)) ध्रुवीय क्रियाधार हैं जो सुगमता से \(\mathrm{S_N2}\) (\(1^\circ\) हैलाइड, ध्रुवीय अप्रोटिक विलायक, विन्यास प्रतिलोमन) या \(\mathrm{S_N1}\) (\(3^\circ\)/ऐलिलिक/बेंजिलिक हैलाइड, ध्रुवीय प्रोटिक विलायक, रेसिमीकरण) अभिक्रियाएँ, ऐल्कोहॉलीय \(\mathrm{KOH}\) के साथ ज़ैत्सेव \(\beta\)-विलोपन तथा शुष्क ईथर में \(\mathrm{Mg}\) के साथ ग्रिग्नार्ड अभिकर्मक देते हैं। हैलोऐरीन (\(sp^2\;\mathrm{C-X}\)) अनुनाद, छोटे \(\mathrm{C-X}\) आबंध और फेनिल धनायन के अस्थायित्व के कारण नाभिकरागी प्रतिस्थापन के प्रति निष्क्रिय होते हैं (जब तक कि o/p \(-\mathrm{NO_2}\) से सक्रिय न हों) तथा इलेक्ट्रॉनरागी प्रतिस्थापन में o, p-निर्देशक व विसक्रियक व्यवहार दर्शाते हैं।
