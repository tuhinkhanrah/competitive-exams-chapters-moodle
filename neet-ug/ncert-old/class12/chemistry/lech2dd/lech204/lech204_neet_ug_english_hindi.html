Learning Objectives and Syllabus Overview

Nitrogen-containing organic compounds play a central role in biological systems (proteins, alkaloids, neurotransmitters such as adrenaline) and industrial chemistry (polymers, pharmaceuticals, and azo dyes). This tutorial builds exam-ready mastery of aliphatic and aromatic amines alongside arenediazonium salts.

After completing this tutorial, you will be able to:

  • Explain the \(sp^3\) pyramidal geometry of nitrogen in amines and classify them as primary (\(1^\circ\)), secondary (\(2^\circ\)), or tertiary (\(3^\circ\)).
  • Assign accurate IUPAC and common names to alkylamines and arylamines.
  • Select appropriate synthetic routes—including chain-lengthening, chain-shortening, and phthalimide methods—while recognizing their substrate limitations.
  • Predict boiling point and aqueous solubility trends using intermolecular hydrogen bonding.
  • Analyze basicity in both gas and aqueous phases by balancing inductive (\(+I\)), solvation, steric, and resonance effects.
  • Distinguish \(1^\circ\), \(2^\circ\), and \(3^\circ\) amines chemically and control electrophilic substitution in aniline.
  • Design multi-step aromatic syntheses using arenediazonium salts as versatile intermediates.

Structure, Classification, and Nomenclature

Amines are organic derivatives of ammonia (\(\mathrm{NH_3}\)) formed by replacing one, two, or all three hydrogen atoms with alkyl (\(\mathrm{R}\)) or aryl (\(\mathrm{Ar}\)) groups. The nitrogen atom is trivalent and retains one unshared electron pair (lone pair). Consequently, nitrogen is \(sp^3\) hybridized with a pyramidal geometry. Because lone pair–bond pair repulsion exceeds bond pair–bond pair repulsion, the \(\mathrm{C-N-C}\) or \(\mathrm{C-N-H}\) bond angle is compressed slightly below the ideal tetrahedral value of \(109.5^\circ\) (for example, \(108^\circ\) in trimethylamine).

Classification

  • Primary (\(1^\circ\)) amine: One H of \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) is replaced (\(\mathrm{R-NH_2}\) or \(\mathrm{Ar-NH_2}\)). Note that classification depends on the number of carbon groups bonded to nitrogen, not the degree of the attached carbon; thus, \((\mathrm{CH_3})_3\mathrm{C-NH_2}\) is a \(1^\circ\) amine.
  • Secondary (\(2^\circ\)) amine: Two H atoms of \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) are replaced (\(\mathrm{R-NH-R'}\)).
  • Tertiary (\(3^\circ\)) amine: All three H atoms of \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) are replaced (\(\mathrm{R-NR'R''}\)).
  • Simple vs. Mixed: Secondary and tertiary amines are simple if all attached groups are identical, and mixed if at least two groups differ.

Nomenclature Rules

In the common system, aliphatic amines are written as one word: alkylamine (using prefixes di- or tri- for identical groups). In the IUPAC system, primary amines are named as alkanamines by replacing the terminal -e of the alkane with -amine (e.g., methanamine). When multiple \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\) groups are present, the -e is retained (e.g., ethane-1,2-diamine). For \(2^\circ\) and \(3^\circ\) amines, the locant N- indicates substituents directly bonded to nitrogen. For arylamines, \(\mathrm{C_6H_5NH_2}\) is accepted as both aniline and benzenamine.

संरचना, वर्गीकरण एवं नामपद्धति

ऐमीन अमोनिया (\(\mathrm{NH_3}\)) के कार्बनिक व्युत्पन्न हैं, जो अमोनिया के एक, दो या तीनों हाइड्रोजन परमाणुओं को ऐल्किल (\(\mathrm{R}\)) अथवा ऐरिल (\(\mathrm{Ar}\)) समूहों द्वारा प्रतिस्थापित करने पर प्राप्त होते हैं। इनमें नाइट्रोजन परमाणु त्रिसंयोजी होता है और उस पर एक अनाबंधित इलेक्ट्रॉन युगल (एकाकी युगल) उपस्थित रहता है। अतः नाइट्रोजन \(sp^3\) संकरित होता है और इसकी ज्यामिति पिरामिडीय (pyramidal) होती है। एकाकी युगल–आबंध युगल प्रतिकर्षण, आबंध युगल–आबंध युगल प्रतिकर्षण से अधिक होने के कारण \(\mathrm{C-N-C}\) अथवा \(\mathrm{C-N-H}\) आबंध कोण आदर्श चतुष्फलकीय कोण \(109.5^\circ\) से थोड़ा कम हो जाता है (उदाहरण के लिए, ट्राइमेथिलऐमीन में यह \(108^\circ\) होता है)।

ट्यूटोरियल आरेख 1: \(sp^3\)-संकरित नाइट्रोजन और \(108^\circ\) C–N–C आबंध कोण के साथ ट्राइमेथिलऐमीन की पिरामिडीय ज्यामिति।

वर्गीकरण

  • प्राथमिक (\(1^\circ\)) ऐमीन: \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) का एक H परमाणु प्रतिस्थापित होता है (\(\mathrm{R-NH_2}\) या \(\mathrm{Ar-NH_2}\))। ध्यान दें कि वर्गीकरण नाइट्रोजन से जुड़े कार्बन समूहों की संख्या पर निर्भर करता है, न कि संलग्न कार्बन की कोटि पर; अतः \((\mathrm{CH_3})_3\mathrm{C-NH_2}\) एक \(1^\circ\) ऐमीन है।
  • द्वितीयक (\(2^\circ\)) ऐमीन: \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) के दो H परमाणु प्रतिस्थापित होते हैं (\(\mathrm{R-NH-R'}\))।
  • तृतीयक (\(3^\circ\)) ऐमीन: \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) के तीनों H परमाणु प्रतिस्थापित होते हैं (\(\mathrm{R-NR'R''}\))।
  • सरल बनाम मिश्रित ऐमीन: यदि \(2^\circ\) या \(3^\circ\) ऐमीन में सभी जुड़े हुए समूह समान हों तो वे सरल कहलाते हैं, और यदि कम से कम दो समूह भिन्न हों तो मिश्रित कहलाते हैं।

Synthetic Routes to Amines

Choosing the right preparation method in NEET UG problems often hinges on whether the carbon skeleton must remain unchanged, lengthen by one carbon, or shorten by one carbon, and whether the target is aliphatic or aromatic.

1. Carbon-Skeleton Preserving Methods

  • Reduction of Nitro Compounds: Nitroarenes and nitroalkanes are reduced to \(1^\circ\) amines by catalytic hydrogenation (\(\mathrm{H_2/Pd}\), \(\mathrm{Pt}\), or \(\mathrm{Ni}\) in ethanol) or active metals in acid (\(\mathrm{Sn/HCl}\) or \(\mathrm{Fe/HCl}\)). Industrial preference: \(\mathrm{Fe}\) scrap with \(\mathrm{HCl}\) is favored because the \(\mathrm{FeCl_2}\) formed hydrolyzes to regenerate \(\mathrm{HCl}\), so only a catalytic amount of acid is needed to start the reaction.
  • Ammonolysis of Alkyl/Benzyl Halides: Heating \(\mathrm{R-X}\) with ethanolic \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) in a sealed tube at \(373\,\mathrm{K}\) causes nucleophilic substitution (\(\mathrm{RI} > \mathrm{RBr} > \mathrm{RCl}\)). Because the resulting \(1^\circ\) amine is also nucleophilic, it reacts further to yield a mixture of \(1^\circ\), \(2^\circ\), \(3^\circ\) amines and a quaternary ammonium salt (\(\mathrm{R_4N^+X^-}\)). Using a large excess of \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) makes the \(1^\circ\) amine the major product; treating the ammonium salt with \(\mathrm{NaOH}\) liberates the free amine.
  • Reduction of Amides: Treating \(\mathrm{R-CONH_2}\) with \(\mathrm{LiAlH_4}\) followed by \(\mathrm{H_2O}\) reduces the carbonyl to \(-\mathrm{CH_2}-\), giving \(\mathrm{R-CH_2-NH_2}\) with the same carbon count.
  • Gabriel Phthalimide Synthesis (Pure \(1^\circ\) Aliphatic Amines): Phthalimide reacts with ethanolic \(\mathrm{KOH}\) to form potassium phthalimide, which undergoes \(\mathrm{S_N2}\) alkylation with \(\mathrm{R-X}\). Subsequent alkaline hydrolysis (\(\mathrm{NaOH(aq)}\)) yields exclusively a \(1^\circ\) aliphatic amine free from \(2^\circ\) or \(3^\circ\) contamination. Crucial limitation: Aromatic primary amines (like aniline) cannot be prepared this way because aryl halides do not undergo nucleophilic substitution with the phthalimide anion.

2. Chain-Modulating Methods (Ascent vs. Descent)

  • Ascent of Series (Reduction of Nitriles): Converting an alkyl halide \(\mathrm{R-X}\) with \(\mathrm{NaCN/KCN}\) gives \(\mathrm{R-C\equiv N}\), which upon reduction with \(\mathrm{LiAlH_4}\), \(\mathrm{H_2/Ni}\), or \(\mathrm{Na(Hg)/C_2H_5OH}\) yields \(\mathrm{R-CH_2-NH_2}\) (one carbon more than \(\mathrm{R-X}\)).
  • Descent of Series (Hoffmann Bromamide Degradation): Treating a \(1^\circ\) amide with \(\mathrm{Br_2}\) in aqueous or ethanolic \(\mathrm{NaOH}\) (or \(\mathrm{KOH}\)) induces migration of the \(\mathrm{R}\) or \(\mathrm{Ar}\) group from carbonyl carbon to nitrogen, forming a \(1^\circ\) amine with one carbon atom fewer:
    \[\mathrm{R-CONH_2 + Br_2 + 4NaOH \longrightarrow R-NH_2 + Na_2CO_3 + 2NaBr + 2H_2O}\]

ऐमीनों के संश्लेषण की प्रमुख विधियाँ

नीट यूजी (NEET UG) के प्रश्नों में सही विरचन विधि का चयन इस बात पर निर्भर करता है कि कार्बन श्रृंखला को समान रखना है, एक कार्बन बढ़ाना है, या एक कार्बन घटाना है, तथा उत्पाद ऐलिफैटिक है या ऐरोमैटिक।

1. कार्बन-श्रृंखला संरक्षित रखने वाली विधियाँ

  • नाइट्रो यौगिकों का अपचयन: नाइट्रोऐरीन तथा नाइट्रोऐल्केन का एथेनॉल में उत्प्रेरकीय हाइड्रोजनीकरण (\(\mathrm{H_2/Pd}\), \(\mathrm{Pt}\), या \(\mathrm{Ni}\)) अथवा अम्लीय माध्यम में धातुओं (\(\mathrm{Sn/HCl}\) या \(\mathrm{Fe/HCl}\)) द्वारा \(1^\circ\) ऐमीन में अपचयन होता है। औद्योगिक वरीयता: \(\mathrm{HCl}\) के साथ \(\mathrm{Fe}\) की छीलन को प्राथमिकता दी जाती है क्योंकि बनने वाला \(\mathrm{FeCl_2}\) जल-अपघटित होकर \(\mathrm{HCl}\) को पुनः मुक्त करता है, अतः अभिक्रिया प्रारंभ करने के लिए केवल अल्प मात्रा में \(\mathrm{HCl}\) आवश्यक होता है।
  • ऐल्किल/बेन्ज़िल हैलाइडों का ऐमोनीअपघटन: बंद नलिका में \(373\,\mathrm{K}\) पर एथेनॉलिक \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) के साथ \(\mathrm{R-X}\) को गर्म करने पर नाभिकस्नेही प्रतिस्थापन होता है (\(\mathrm{RI} > \mathrm{RBr} > \mathrm{RCl}\))। प्राप्त \(1^\circ\) ऐमीन स्वयं नाभिकस्नेही होने के कारण आगे अभिक्रिया करके \(1^\circ\), \(2^\circ\), \(3^\circ\) ऐमीनों तथा चतुष्क अमोनियम लवण (\(\mathrm{R_4N^+X^-}\)) का मिश्रण बनाता है। अत्यधिक मात्रा में \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) लेने पर \(1^\circ\) ऐमीन मुख्य उत्पाद बनता है; लवण को \(\mathrm{NaOH}\) से अभिकृत करने पर मुक्त ऐमीन प्राप्त होता है।
  • ऐमाइडों का अपचयन: \(\mathrm{R-CONH_2}\) को \(\mathrm{LiAlH_4}\) तत्पश्चात \(\mathrm{H_2O}\) के साथ अभिकृत करने पर समान कार्बन संख्या वाला \(\mathrm{R-CH_2-NH_2}\) प्राप्त होता है।
  • गैब्रिएल थैलिमाइड संश्लेषण (शुद्ध \(1^\circ\) ऐलिफैटिक ऐमीन): थैलिमाइड एथेनॉलिक \(\mathrm{KOH}\) से अभिक्रिया कर पोटैशियम थैलिमाइड बनाता है, जो \(\mathrm{R-X}\) के साथ \(\mathrm{S_N2}\) अभिक्रिया करता है। क्षारीय जल-अपघटन (\(\mathrm{NaOH(aq)}\)) से \(2^\circ\) या \(3^\circ\) ऐमीन से पूर्णतः मुक्त शुद्ध \(1^\circ\) ऐलिफैटिक ऐमीन प्राप्त होता है। महत्वपूर्ण सीमा: इस विधि से ऐरोमैटिक प्राथमिक ऐमीन (जैसे ऐनिलीन) नहीं बनाए जा सकते क्योंकि ऐरिल हैलाइड थैलिमाइड ऋणायन के साथ नाभिकस्नेही प्रतिस्थापन नहीं देते।

2. कार्बन-श्रृंखला परिवर्तित करने वाली विधियाँ (आरोहण बनाम अवरोहण)

Physical Properties and Hydrogen Bonding

  • Physical State & Appearance: Lower aliphatic amines are gases with a fishy odor; \(1^\circ\) amines with three or more carbons are liquids, and higher homologues are solids. Pure arylamines are colorless, but darken on storage due to atmospheric oxidation.
  • Water Solubility: Lower aliphatic amines dissolve readily in water by forming hydrogen bonds with \(\mathrm{H_2O}\). As molar mass increases, the hydrophobic alkyl group grows larger and solubility drops sharply; higher amines and aniline are practically insoluble in water. Alcohols are more soluble and higher-boiling than amines of comparable molar mass because oxygen (electronegativity \(3.5\)) is more electronegative than nitrogen (electronegativity \(3.0\)), producing stronger \(\mathrm{O-H\cdots O}\) hydrogen bonds than \(\mathrm{N-H\cdots N}\) bonds.
  • Boiling Point Order of Isomeric Amines: Primary amines have two \(\mathrm{N-H}\) bonds for extensive intermolecular association, secondary amines have one, and tertiary amines have none. Therefore, for isomeric amines of comparable molar mass:
    \[\text{Boiling Point: } \mathrm{1^\circ\;Amine > 2^\circ\;Amine > 3^\circ\;Amine > Alkane}\] \[\text{Overall for similar molar mass: } \mathrm{Alcohol > 1^\circ\;Amine > 2^\circ\;Amine > 3^\circ\;Amine > Alkane}\]

Chemical Reactivity, Basicity, and Distinction Tests

1. Basic Strength: Gas Phase vs. Aqueous Phase

Amines act as Lewis bases via the lone pair on nitrogen. Basicity is quantified by \(K_b\) and \(\mathrm{p}K_b = -\log K_b\); a larger \(K_b\) (smaller \(\mathrm{p}K_b\)) denotes a stronger base. Amines form water-soluble salts with mineral acids, allowing separation from water-insoluble neutral organic compounds, and regenerate the free amine on treatment with \(\mathrm{NaOH}\).

  • Gas Phase: Only the electron-releasing \(+I\) effect of alkyl groups operates, stabilizing the conjugate ammonium ion. Hence, basicity increases with alkyl substitution: \(3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ > \mathrm{NH_3}\).
  • Aqueous Phase: Basic strength reflects a balance of three factors: (i) \(+I\) inductive effect (\(3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ\)), (ii) hydration/solvation of the substituted ammonium cation via H-bonding with water (\(1^\circ > 2^\circ > 3^\circ\)), and (iii) steric hindrance by bulky alkyl groups. This interplay gives two high-yield NEET UG orders:
    \[\text{Ethyl series (aq): } \mathrm{(C_2H_5)_2NH > (C_2H_5)_3N > C_2H_5NH_2 > NH_3}\] \[\text{Methyl series (aq): } \mathrm{(CH_3)_2NH > CH_3NH_2 > (CH_3)_3N > NH_3}\]
  • Arylamines vs. Ammonia: Aniline (\(\mathrm{p}K_b = 9.38\)) is much weaker than \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) (\(\mathrm{p}K_b = 4.75\)) and aliphatic amines (\(\mathrm{p}K_b \approx 3.0\text{–}4.2\)). In aniline, the nitrogen lone pair is delocalized into the benzene ring across five resonance structures, whereas the protonated anilinium ion has only two Kekulé structures. Electron-donating groups (\(-\mathrm{OCH_3}\), \(-\mathrm{CH_3}\)) increase basicity of substituted anilines, while electron-withdrawing groups (\(-\mathrm{NO_2}\), \(-\mathrm{SO_3H}\), \(-\mathrm{COOH}\), \(-\mathrm{X}\)) decrease it. Note that benzylamine (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2NH_2}\), \(\mathrm{p}K_b = 4.70\)) is far more basic than aniline because its \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\) group is separated from the ring by a \(-\mathrm{CH_2}-\) spacer.

3. Electrophilic Aromatic Substitution (EAS) of Aniline

The \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\) group is a powerful ortho/para-directing activator, causing poly-substitution and side reactions unless moderated:

  • Bromination: Aniline with \(\mathrm{Br_2/H_2O}\) at room temperature immediately precipitates white 2,4,6-tribromoaniline. To obtain 4-bromoaniline selectively, protect \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\) by acetylation (\(\mathrm{(CH_3CO)_2O}\)/pyridine → acetanilide), brominate with \(\mathrm{Br_2/CH_3COOH}\), and hydrolyze (\(\mathrm{H^+}\) or \(\mathrm{OH^-}\)). The amide lone pair delocalizes into the carbonyl oxygen, moderating ring activation.
  • Nitration: Direct nitration of aniline with \(\mathrm{HNO_3/H_2SO_4}\) at \(288\,\mathrm{K}\) yields tarry oxidation products plus a mixture of p-nitroaniline (51%), m-nitroaniline (47%), and o-nitroaniline (2%). Why so much meta product? In strongly acidic medium, aniline protonates to the meta-directing anilinium ion (\(-\mathrm{NH_3^+}\)). Protecting as acetanilide before nitration gives p-nitroaniline cleanly after hydrolysis.
  • Sulphonation: Aniline with conc. \(\mathrm{H_2SO_4}\) forms anilinium hydrogensulphate, which on heating at \(453\text{–}473\,\mathrm{K}\) gives sulphanilic acid (p-aminobenzenesulphonic acid), existing predominantly as a dipolar zwitterion.
  • Failure of Friedel–Crafts Reactions: Aniline does not undergo Friedel–Crafts alkylation or acylation because it forms a salt with the Lewis acid catalyst \(\mathrm{AlCl_3}\), placing a positive charge on nitrogen that strongly deactivates the ring.

  • गैसीय प्रावस्था: इसमें केवल ऐल्किल समूहों का इलेक्ट्रॉन-विमोची (\(+I\)) प्रभाव कार्य करता है, जो संयुग्मी अमोनियम आयन को स्थायित्व प्रदान करता है। अतः क्षारीयता क्रम: \(3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ > \mathrm{NH_3}\)।
  • जलीय प्रावस्था: यहाँ क्षारीय प्रबलता तीन कारकों के संतुलन पर निर्भर करती है: (i) \(+I\) प्रेरणिक प्रभाव (\(3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ\)), (ii) जल के साथ H-आबंधन द्वारा प्रतिस्थापित अमोनियम धनायन का जलयोजन/विलायकन (\(1^\circ > 2^\circ > 3^\circ\)), तथा (iii) बड़े ऐल्किल समूहों की त्रिविम बाधा। इनके संयुक्त प्रभाव से नीट यूजी (NEET UG) के दो अत्यंत महत्वपूर्ण क्रम प्राप्त होते हैं:
    \[\text{Ethyl series (aq): } \mathrm{(C_2H_5)_2NH > (C_2H_5)_3N > C_2H_5NH_2 > NH_3}\] \[\text{Methyl series (aq): } \mathrm{(CH_3)_2NH > CH_3NH_2 > (CH_3)_3N > NH_3}\]
  • ऐरिलऐमीन बनाम अमोनिया: ऐनिलीन (\(\mathrm{p}K_b = 9.38\)), \(\mathrm{NH_3}\) (\(\mathrm{p}K_b = 4.75\)) तथा ऐलिफैटिक ऐमीनों (\(\mathrm{p}K_b \approx 3.0\text{–}4.2\)) की तुलना में बहुत दुर्बल क्षारक है। ऐनिलीन में नाइट्रोजन का एकाकी युगल बेन्ज़ीन वलय के साथ पाँच अनुनादी संरचनाओं में विस्थानीकृत रहता है, जबकि प्रोटॉनीकृत ऐनिलीनियम आयन की केवल दो केकुले संरचनाएँ होती हैं। प्रतिस्थापित ऐनिलीन में इलेक्ट्रॉन-दाता समूह (\(-\mathrm{OCH_3}\), \(-\mathrm{CH_3}\)) क्षारीयता बढ़ाते हैं, जबकि इलेक्ट्रॉन-आकर्षी समूह (\(-\mathrm{NO_2}\), \(-\mathrm{SO_3H}\), \(-\mathrm{COOH}\), \(-\mathrm{X}\)) क्षारीयता घटाते हैं। ध्यान दें कि बेन्ज़िलऐमीन (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2NH_2}\), \(\mathrm{p}K_b = 4.70\)) ऐनिलीन से कहीं अधिक क्षारीय है क्योंकि इसमें \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\) समूह वलय से \(-\mathrm{CH_2}-\) द्वारा पृथक रहता है।

3. ऐनिलीन का इलेक्ट्रॉनस्नेही ऐरोमैटिक प्रतिस्थापन

\(-\mathrm{NH_2}\) समूह एक प्रबल ऑर्थो/पैरा-निर्देशक सक्रियकारी समूह है, जिसके कारण नियंत्रण के बिना बहु-प्रतिस्थापन तथा अवांछित उप-उत्पाद बनते हैं:

  • ब्रोमीनन: कमरे के ताप पर \(\mathrm{Br_2/H_2O}\) से ऐनिलीन तुरंत 2,4,6-ट्राइब्रोमोऐनिलीन का श्वेत अवक्षेप देता है। एकल-ब्रोमीनन (4-ब्रोमोऐनिलीन) के लिए पहले ऐनिलीन का ऐसिटिलीकरण (\(\mathrm{(CH_3CO)_2O}\)/पिरिडीन → ऐसिटैनिलाइड) करके \(\mathrm{Br_2/CH_3COOH}\) से अभिक्रिया कराते हैं और अंत में जल-अपघटन (\(\mathrm{H^+}\) या \(\mathrm{OH^-}\)) करते हैं।

Worked Examples

Example 1: Predicting Aqueous Basicity Order of Substituted Anilines and Aliphatic Amines

Problem: Arrange the following four bases in increasing order of basic strength in aqueous solution: p-nitroaniline, aniline, p-methoxyaniline, and N-methylmethanamine. (Assume standard \(298\,\mathrm{K}\) aqueous conditions.)

Solution:

  1. Aliphatic amines are significantly stronger bases than arylamines because the lone pair on nitrogen in aliphatic amines is localized and reinforced by \(+I\) inductive effects, whereas in arylamines it is delocalized into the benzene ring. Thus, N-methylmethanamine (\((\mathrm{CH_3})_2\mathrm{NH}\), a \(2^\circ\) aliphatic amine) is the strongest base here.
  2. Among the three arylamines, \(-\mathrm{OCH_3}\) at the para position exerts a strong \(+R\) (resonance electron-donating) effect that outweighs its \(-I\) effect, increasing electron density at nitrogen and stabilizing the conjugate acid. Hence, p-methoxyaniline is more basic than aniline.
  3. Conversely, \(-\mathrm{NO_2}\) at the para position is strongly electron-withdrawing (\(-R\) and \(-I\)), pulling electron density away from \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\) and making p-nitroaniline the weakest base.

Increasing Order: p-nitroaniline < aniline < p-methoxyaniline < N-methylmethanamine.

Example 2: Distinguishing Isomeric Amines Using Chemical Tests

Problem: Two isomeric liquid amines with molecular formula \(\mathrm{C_3H_9N}\)—Compound X and Compound Y—are tested separately. Compound X reacts with \(\mathrm{CHCl_3}\) and ethanolic \(\mathrm{KOH}\) to give a pungent-smelling gas, whereas Compound Y does not react with \(\mathrm{CHCl_3}/\mathrm{KOH}\) but reacts with benzenesulphonyl chloride to give a precipitate insoluble in aqueous \(\mathrm{KOH}\). Identify X and Y (assume unbranched carbon chains where possible).

Solution:

  1. A positive carbylamine test (\(\mathrm{CHCl_3 + alc.\;KOH}\)) is exclusive to primary (\(1^\circ\)) amines. Among unbranched \(\mathrm{C_3H_9N}\) isomers, Compound X is propan-1-amine (\(\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2NH_2}\)).
  2. Compound Y fails the carbylamine test (so it is not \(1^\circ\)) and reacts with Hinsberg's reagent (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5SO_2Cl}\)) to yield an alkali-insoluble sulphonamide. This behavior is characteristic of a secondary (\(2^\circ\)) amine lacking an acidic \(\mathrm{N-H}\) bond in its sulphonamide product. Therefore, Compound Y is N-methylethanamine (\(\mathrm{CH_3NHCH_2CH_3}\)).

Example 3: Multi-Step Aromatic Conversion via Diazonium Intermediate

Problem: Propose an efficient route to convert p-toluidine (4-methylaniline) into 3-bromobenzoic acid.

Solution:

  1. Protect & Direct Bromination: Directly brominating p-toluidine gives dibromination ortho to \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\). Protect \(-\mathrm{NH_2}\) with \(\mathrm{(CH_3CO)_2O}\)/pyridine to form 4-methylacetanilide, then treat with \(\mathrm{Br_2/CH_3COOH}\). Because \(-\mathrm{NHCOCH_3}\) is a stronger ortho/para director than \(-\mathrm{CH_3}\), bromine enters ortho to \(-\mathrm{NHCOCH_3}\) (which is meta to \(-\mathrm{CH_3}\)). Hydrolyze with \(\mathrm{H^+/H_2O}\) to obtain 2-bromo-4-methylaniline.
  2. Diazotisation & Reductive Deamination: Treat 2-bromo-4-methylaniline with \(\mathrm{NaNO_2 + HCl}\) at \(273\text{–}278\,\mathrm{K}\) to form the diazonium salt, then reduce with \(\mathrm{H_3PO_2/H_2O}\) (or \(\mathrm{C_2H_5OH}\)) to replace \(-\mathrm{N_2^+Cl^-}\) with \(-\mathrm{H}\), yielding 3-bromotoluene.
  3. Side-Chain Oxidation: Oxidize 3-bromotoluene with hot alkaline \(\mathrm{KMnO_4}\) followed by acidification (\(\mathrm{H_3O^+}\)) to obtain 3-bromobenzoic acid.

Common Pitfalls and NEET UG Revision Checklist

Common Exam Pitfalls to Avoid:

  • Confusing \((\mathrm{CH_3})_3\mathrm{C-NH_2}\) as a \(3^\circ\) amine—it is a \(1^\circ\) amine because nitrogen is bonded to only one carbon atom.
  • Applying the gas-phase basicity order (\(3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ\)) to aqueous solutions instead of the solvation-modified methyl (\(\mathrm{2^\circ > 1^\circ > 3^\circ}\)) and ethyl (\(\mathrm{2^\circ > 3^\circ > 1^\circ}\)) orders.
  • Attempting to make aniline via Gabriel phthalimide synthesis or attempting Friedel–Crafts reactions on aniline.
  • Forgetting that carbylamine test is positive for both aliphatic and aromatic \(1^\circ\) amines, whereas nitrous acid gives \(\mathrm{N_2}\) gas immediately at \(273\text{–}278\,\mathrm{K}\) only with aliphatic \(1^\circ\) amines (aromatic \(1^\circ\) amines form stable diazonium salts).

Concise Final Recap

Amines are \(sp^3\)-hybridized pyramidal Lewis bases whose boiling points follow \(1^\circ > 2^\circ > 3^\circ\) due to intermolecular H-bonding. Synthetic control relies on nitrile reduction (+1 C), Hoffmann bromamide degradation (-1 C), and Gabriel synthesis (pure \(1^\circ\) aliphatic amines). In water, \(2^\circ\) amines are the strongest bases among simple alkylamines, while aniline is weakly basic due to lone-pair resonance. Chemical distinction uses carbylamine (\(1^\circ\) only) and Hinsberg's reagent (\(1^\circ\) alkali-soluble, \(2^\circ\) alkali-insoluble, \(3^\circ\) unreactive). Finally, arenediazonium salts prepared at \(273\text{–}278\,\mathrm{K}\) serve as premier intermediates for introducing \(-\mathrm{Cl}\), \(-\mathrm{Br}\), \(-\mathrm{I}\), \(-\mathrm{F}\), \(-\mathrm{CN}\), \(-\mathrm{OH}\), \(-\mathrm{NO_2}\), \(-\mathrm{H}\), and azo dye linkages onto aromatic rings.

संक्षिप्त अंतिम पुनरावृत्ति

ऐमीन \(sp^3\)-संकरित पिरामिडीय लूइस क्षारक हैं, जिनका क्वथनांक अंतर-आण्विक H-आबंधन के कारण \(1^\circ > 2^\circ > 3^\circ\) क्रम का पालन करता है। संश्लेषण नियंत्रण नाइट्राइल अपचयन (+1 C), हॉफमान ब्रोमैमाइड निम्नीकरण (-1 C) और गैब्रिएल संश्लेषण (शुद्ध \(1^\circ\) ऐलिफैटिक ऐमीन) पर आधारित है। जल में सरल ऐल्किलऐमीनों में \(2^\circ\) ऐमीन सबसे प्रबल क्षारक होते हैं, जबकि एकाकी युगल के अनुनाद के कारण ऐनिलीन दुर्बल क्षारक है। रासायनिक विभेद हेतु कार्बिलऐमीन (केवल \(1^\circ\)) और हिन्सबर्ग अभिकर्मक (\(1^\circ\) क्षार-विलेय, \(2^\circ\) क्षार-अविलेय, \(3^\circ\) अक्रियाशील) का उपयोग होता है। अंततः, \(273\text{–}278\,\mathrm{K}\) पर बने ऐरीनडाइऐज़ोनियम लवण ऐरोमैटिक वलय पर \(-\mathrm{Cl}\), \(-\mathrm{Br}\), \(-\mathrm{I}\), \(-\mathrm{F}\), \(-\mathrm{CN}\), \(-\mathrm{OH}\), \(-\mathrm{NO_2}\), \(-\mathrm{H}\) तथा ऐज़ो रंजक बंध प्रविष्ट करने के सर्वोत्तम मध्यवर्ती हैं।
