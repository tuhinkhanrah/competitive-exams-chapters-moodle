Evolution: Origin of Life, Mechanisms, Population Genetics, and Human Lineage

उद्विकास (Evolution): जीवन की उत्पत्ति, क्रियाविधि, समष्टि आनुवंशिकी एवं मानव वंशावली

1. Learning Objectives and Exam Overview | 1. अधिगम उद्देश्य एवं परीक्षा अवलोकन

English

Evolutionary biology investigates how cosmic, geological, and biological transformations shaped the diversity of living organisms on Earth. For NEET UG, this topic tests conceptual clarity on chemical evolution, paleontological and anatomical evidences, mechanisms of variation and selection, Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium calculations, and the chronological sequence of plant, vertebrate, and hominid evolution.

By completing this tutorial, you will be able to:

  • Trace the timeline from the Big Bang and early Earth conditions to chemical and cellular evolution.
  • Distinguish between homologous and analogous structures, divergent and convergent evolution, and adaptive radiation.
  • Compare Lamarckian inheritance, Darwinian natural selection, and Hugo de Vries' mutation theory.
  • Apply the Hardy-Weinberg equations (\( p + q = 1 \) and \( p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1 \)) to evaluate genetic equilibrium and evolutionary forces.
  • Sequence key milestones in geological history and human evolution, including cranial capacities and cultural shifts.
हिन्दी

उद्विकासीय जीवविज्ञान (Evolutionary Biology) यह अध्ययन करता है कि ब्रह्मांडीय, भूवैज्ञानिक और जैविक परिवर्तनों ने पृथ्वी पर जीवों की विविधता को कैसे आकार दिया। NEET UG परीक्षा के लिए यह अध्याय रासायनिक उद्विकास, जीवाश्म व शारीरिक प्रमाण, विभिन्नता व प्राकृतिक वरण की क्रियाविधि, हार्डी-वाइनबर्ग साम्यावस्था की गणना तथा पादप, कशेरुकी एवं मानव उद्विकास के कालक्रम की अवधारणात्मक स्पष्टता का परीक्षण करता है।

इस ट्यूटोरियल को पूरा करने के बाद आप निम्न में सक्षम होंगे:

  • बिग बैंग और प्रारंभिक पृथ्वी की परिस्थितियों से रासायनिक एवं कोशिकीय उद्विकास के कालक्रम को समझना।
  • समजात (homologous) एवं समवृत्ति (analogous) अंगों, अपसारी व अभिसारी उद्विकास तथा अनुकूली विकिरण (adaptive radiation) में अंतर करना।
  • लैमार्कवाद, डार्विन के प्राकृतिक वरण सिद्धांत और ह्यूगो डी व्रीज़ के उत्परिवर्तन सिद्धांत की तुलना करना।
  • आनुवंशिक साम्यावस्था एवं उद्विकासीय कारकों के मूल्यांकन हेतु हार्डी-वाइनबर्ग समीकरण (\( p + q = 1 \) तथा \( p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1 \)) का उपयोग करना।

2. Cosmic Timeline, Early Earth, and Chemical Evolution | 2. ब्रह्मांडीय कालक्रम, आदिम पृथ्वी एवं रासायनिक उद्विकास

English

Understanding biological evolution begins with cosmic history. Because stellar light takes millions of years to reach Earth, observing distant stars is equivalent to looking backward in time. According to the Big Bang theory, the universe originated roughly 20 billion years ago through a singular, unimaginable expansion. As the universe expanded and cooled, hydrogen and helium formed, condensed under gravity into galaxies, and gave rise to our solar system. Earth formed approximately 4.5 billion years ago.

Primitive Earth lacked an oxidising atmosphere. Molten surface activity released water vapour, methane (\( \mathrm{CH_4} \)), carbon dioxide (\( \mathrm{CO_2} \)), and ammonia (\( \mathrm{NH_3} \)). Solar ultraviolet (UV) rays split water molecules into hydrogen and oxygen; the light \( \mathrm{H_2} \) escaped into space, while oxygen reacted with \( \mathrm{NH_3} \) and \( \mathrm{CH_4} \) to produce water, \( \mathrm{CO_2} \), and other compounds, eventually forming the ozone layer. As the planet cooled, condensed water fell as rain, filling basins to create oceans. Life appeared about 4 billion years ago (roughly 500 million years after Earth's formation).

Competing Hypotheses on the Origin of Life

Hypothesis / Theory Core Proposition Scientific Status
Special Creation All species were created in their present form; diversity never changes; Earth is ~4,000 years old. Refuted by fossil records, geological dating, and evolutionary biology.
Panspermia Units of life called spores arrived on Earth from outer space. Speculative astronomical idea; does not explain how life first arose.
Spontaneous Generation Living organisms arose suddenly from decaying matter like straw or mud. Disproved by Louis Pasteur using pre-sterilised flasks with killed yeast (life comes only from pre-existing life).
Oparin-Haldane Chemical Evolution First life arose from pre-existing non-living organic molecules (RNA, proteins) formed from inorganic precursors under reducing conditions. Supported by laboratory simulations (S.L. Miller, 1953) and meteorite analyses.

S.L. Miller's Simulation (1953) and Early Cellular Forms

To test the Oparin-Haldane hypothesis, S.L. Miller recreated primitive Earth conditions in a closed laboratory apparatus. He passed electric sparks (simulating lightning) through a gaseous mixture of \( \mathrm{CH_4} \), \( \mathrm{H_2} \), \( \mathrm{NH_3} \), and water vapour maintained at \( 800\,^\circ\mathrm{C} \). After cooling via a condenser, the trapped liquid yielded amino acids. Subsequent similar experiments by other researchers produced sugars, nitrogenous bases, pigments, and fats, and comparable organic molecules were detected in meteorites.

The first non-cellular life forms (giant macromolecular aggregates of RNA, proteins, and polysaccharides capable of self-replication) likely originated about 3 billion years ago. The first cellular life forms (single-celled, aquatic organisms) appeared around 2,000 million years ago (mya).

जैविक उद्विकास को समझने की शुरुआत ब्रह्मांड के इतिहास से होती है। तारों से आने वाले प्रकाश को पृथ्वी तक पहुँचने में लाखों वर्ष लगते हैं, इसलिए रात में दूरस्थ तारों को देखना वास्तव में अतीत में झाँकने जैसा है। बिग बैंग सिद्धांत के अनुसार, ब्रह्मांड की उत्पत्ति लगभग 20 अरब वर्ष पूर्व एक अकल्पनीय महाविस्फोट से हुई। विस्तार के साथ तापमान गिरा, हाइड्रोजन व हीलियम बने, और गुरुत्वाकर्षण से संघनित होकर आकाशगंगाएँ बनीं। पृथ्वी का निर्माण लगभग 4.5 अरब वर्ष पूर्व हुआ।

3. Evidences for Evolution | 3. उद्विकास के प्रमाण

English

Multiple independent lines of scientific inquiry confirm that life forms have changed over geological time and share common ancestry.

  • Paleontological Evidence: Fossils are preserved hard remains of ancient organisms embedded in sedimentary rock layers. Different strata contain distinct fossil assemblages, showing that life forms varied over time and many groups (such as dinosaurs) became extinct. Fossil ages are determined using radioactive dating.
  • Embryological Evidence (Haeckel vs. von Baer): Ernst Haeckel proposed that vertebrate embryos exhibit shared features absent in adults—such as vestigial gill slits behind the head—and suggested embryos repeat ancestral adult stages. However, Karl Ernst von Baer disproved Haeckel's proposal, demonstrating that embryos never pass through the adult stages of other animals.
  • Biochemical & Artificial Selection Evidence: Shared proteins and genes performing identical functions across diverse taxa indicate common ancestry. Furthermore, selective breeding by humans (creating distinct dog breeds or crop varieties within centuries) illustrates that nature could generate far greater diversity over millions of years.

Evolutionary Pattern Divergent evolution (indicates common ancestry). Convergent evolution (no common ancestry for that trait).

Plant Examples Thorn of Bougainvillea and tendril of Cucurbita (both are modified axillary buds/stems). Sweet potato (root modification) and potato (stem modification).

Natural Selection in Action & Anthropogenic Evolution

Industrial Melanism (England): Prior to industrialisation (1850s), tree trunks were covered with pale lichens (which act as indicators of clean, unpolluted air). White-winged moths camouflaged well and outnumbered dark-winged (melanised) moths. After industrialisation (1920s), soot darkened tree trunks and killed lichens; predators spotted white moths easily, reversing the proportion in favour of melanised moths. Notably, rural unpolluted areas retained low melanic counts, and neither variant was completely wiped out.

Anthropogenic Evolution: Excessive human use of herbicides, pesticides, and antibiotics selects pre-existing resistant variants in microbes and pests within months or years rather than centuries. This demonstrates that evolution is not a directed, deterministic process, but a stochastic process driven by chance events in nature and chance mutations in organisms.

हिन्दी

विभिन्न स्वतंत्र वैज्ञानिक प्रमाण यह सिद्ध करते हैं कि भूवैज्ञानिक काल में जीवों के रूप बदले हैं और वे साझा पूर्वजों से उत्पन्न हुए हैं।

  • जीवाश्मिकीय प्रमाण (Paleontological Evidence): अवसादी चट्टानों की विभिन्न परतों में दबे प्राचीन जीवों के कठोर अवशेष जीवाश्म कहलाते हैं। अलग-अलग आयु की चट्टानी परतों में भिन्न-भिन्न जीवाश्म मिलते हैं, जो दर्शाते हैं कि समय के साथ जीव रूप बदले और कई समूह (जैसे डायनासोर) विलुप्त हो गए। जीवाश्मों की आयु रेडियोधर्मी काल-निर्धारण से ज्ञात की जाती है।
  • भ्रूणीय प्रमाण (हेकेल बनाम वॉन बेयर): अर्न्स्ट हेकेल ने सभी कशेरुकी भ्रूणों में वयस्कों में अनुपस्थित साझा लक्षणों (जैसे सिर के पीछे अवशेषी क्लोम दरारें) के आधार पर प्रस्तावित किया कि भ्रूण अन्य जंतुओं की वयस्क अवस्थाओं से गुजरते हैं। परंतु कार्ल अर्न्स्ट वॉन बेयर ने सावधानीपूर्वक अध्ययन कर इस प्रस्ताव को अस्वीकार कर दिया और स्पष्ट किया कि भ्रूण कभी भी अन्य जंतुओं की वयस्क अवस्थाओं से नहीं गुजरते।
  • जैवरासायनिक व कृत्रिम वरण प्रमाण: विविध जीवों में समान कार्य करने वाले प्रोटीन और जीन की समानता साझा पूर्वजता को दर्शाती है। साथ ही, मानव द्वारा कृत्रिम प्रजनन से सैकड़ों वर्षों में कुत्तों व फसलों की नई नस्लें तैयार करना यह दर्शाता है कि प्रकृति लाखों वर्षों में विशाल विविधता उत्पन्न कर सकती है।

प्रकृति में प्राकृतिक वरण एवं मानवजनित उद्विकास

औद्योगिक कृष्णता (Industrial Melanism - इंग्लैंड): औद्योगीकरण से पूर्व (1850 के दशक में) पेड़ों के तनों पर सफेद लाइकेन उगे थे (लाइकेन प्रदूषण-रहित क्षेत्र के सूचक हैं)। इस पृष्ठभूमि में सफेद पंख वाले शलभ (moths) छलावरण के कारण अधिक संख्या में जीवित रहे। औद्योगीकरण के बाद (1920 में) धुएँ और कालिख से तने काले हो गए; परिणामस्वरूप परभक्षियों ने सफेद शलभों को आसानी से पकड़ लिया और गहरे (मेलैनीकृत) शलभों की संख्या बढ़ गई। ग्रामीण अप्रदूषित क्षेत्रों में गहरे शलभ कम ही रहे और कोई भी प्रकार पूर्णतः नष्ट नहीं हुआ।

मानवजनित उद्विकास (Anthropogenic Evolution): शाकनाशियों, कीटनाशियों और प्रतिजैविकों (antibiotics) के अत्यधिक उपयोग से कुछ ही महीनों या वर्षों में प्रतिरोधी किस्में चयनित हो जाती हैं। यह सिद्ध करता है कि उद्विकास कोई पूर्व-निर्धारित प्रक्रिया नहीं, बल्कि प्राकृतिक संयोग और आकस्मिक उत्परिवर्तनों पर आधारित एक प्रसंभाव्य प्रक्रिया (stochastic process) है।

Adaptive Radiation and Parallel Convergent Radiation

Adaptive radiation is the evolution of different species in a given geographical area starting from a single ancestral point and radiating into diverse habitats. Two classic examples are:

  • Darwin's Finches (Galapagos Islands): From an original seed-eating finch ancestor, multiple species evolved on the islands with altered beak morphologies adapted to insectivorous and vegetarian diets.
  • Australian Marsupials: Numerous distinct marsupials (e.g., kangaroo, koala, wombat, bandicoot, sugar glider, Tasmanian wolf) evolved from a common ancestral stock within Australia.

When more than one adaptive radiation occurs in an isolated geographical area representing similar habitats, it results in convergent evolution. In Australia, placental mammals radiated into forms remarkably similar to corresponding marsupials: e.g., Placental wolf & Tasmanian wolf; Mole & Marsupial mole; Anteater & Numbat; Mouse & Marsupial mouse; Lemur & Spotted cuscus; Flying squirrel & Flying phalanger; Bobcat & Tasmanian tiger cat.

5. Hardy-Weinberg Principle and Modes of Natural Selection | 5. हार्डी-वाइनबर्ग सिद्धांत एवं प्राकृतिक वरण के प्रकार

English

The Hardy-Weinberg principle states that allele frequencies in a population remain stable and constant from generation to generation—a state termed genetic equilibrium—provided no evolutionary forces act upon the gene pool.

\[ p + q = 1 \qquad \text{and} \qquad (p + q)^2 = p^2 + 2pq + q^2 = 1 \]

Here, for a diploid population with two alleles \( A \) and \( a \) at a locus: \( p \) = frequency of dominant allele \( A \), \( q \) = frequency of recessive allele \( a \), \( p^2 \) = frequency of homozygous dominant (\( AA \)) individuals, \( 2pq \) = frequency of heterozygous (\( Aa \)) individuals, and \( q^2 \) = frequency of homozygous recessive (\( aa \)) individuals. Any deviation between observed and expected frequencies indicates that evolutionary change is occurring.

Five Factors Affecting Hardy-Weinberg Equilibrium

  1. Gene Migration / Gene Flow: Movement of a section of a population into another population alters allele frequencies in both populations. Repeated migration constitutes gene flow.
  2. Genetic Drift: Random change in allele frequencies occurring purely by chance (especially pronounced in small populations). When a small drifted sample colonises a new area and establishes a distinct species, the original drifted group acts as founders—known as the Founder Effect.
  3. Mutation: Sudden heritable changes creating new alleles.
  4. Genetic Recombination: Crossing over and independent assortment during gametogenesis generating new allele combinations.
  5. Natural Selection: Differential reproductive success favouring advantageous heritable variations.

Three Modes of Natural Selection

  • Stabilising Selection: More individuals acquire the mean character value; the distribution peak becomes higher and narrower.
  • Directional Selection: More individuals acquire a value other than the mean; the peak shifts in one direction.
  • Disruptive Selection: More individuals acquire peripheral character values at both ends of the curve, forming two distinct peaks.

  2. आनुवंशिक विचलन (Genetic Drift): संयोगवश (by chance) एलील आवृत्ति में होने वाला परिवर्तन। जब विचलित समष्टि का एक छोटा समूह नए स्थान पर नई प्रजाति बनाता है, तो उसे संस्थापक और इस घटना को संस्थापक प्रभाव (Founder Effect) कहते हैं।
  3. उत्परिवर्तन (Mutation): अचानक होने वाले वंशागत परिवर्तन जो नए एलील बनाते हैं।
  4. आनुवंशिक पुनर्योजन (Genetic Recombination): युग्मकजनन के दौरान जीन विनिमय से बनने वाले नए संयोजन।
  5. प्राकृतिक वरण (Natural Selection): लाभदायक वंशागत विभिन्नताओं को अधिक प्रजनन सफलता मिलना।

  • स्थायीकरण (Stabilising Selection): अधिकतर व्यष्टि औसत लक्षण मान अर्जित करते हैं; वक्र का शिखर ऊँचा और संकरा हो जाता है।
  • दिशात्मक (Directional Selection): अधिकतर व्यष्टि औसत से भिन्न एक दिशा का मान अर्जित करते हैं; शिखर एक ओर खिसक जाता है।
  • विदारक (Disruptive Selection): अधिकतर व्यष्टि वितरण वक्र के दोनों सिरों (परिधीय मान) को अर्जित करते हैं, जिससे दो शिखर बनते हैं।

Key Geological Milestones

  • ~2,000 mya: First cellular forms appeared; some evolved light-harvesting pigments to split water and release \( \mathrm{O_2} \).
  • ~500 mya: Invertebrates formed and became active.
  • ~350 mya: Jawless fish evolved; stout-finned lobe-fins (e.g., Coelacanth, rediscovered in South Africa in 1938) could move onto land and return to water, evolving into the first amphibians (ancestors of modern frogs and salamanders).
  • ~320 mya: Seaweeds and few plants existed; plants were the first organisms to invade land. Plant lineage progressed from Chlorophyte ancestors → Tracheophyte ancestors → Rhynia-type plants → Psilophyton → ferns, conifers, cycads, ginkgos, gnetales, and angiosperms (monocots and dicots), while Zosterophyllum led to lycopods.
  • Reptilian & Mammalian Divergence: Amphibians evolved into reptiles (laying thick-shelled eggs resistant to drying). Early reptiles diverged into Sauropsids (giving rise to turtles, lizards, snakes, tuataras, crocodiles, dinosaurs, and birds) and Synapsids (pelycosaurs → therapsids → mammals).
  • ~200 mya: Some land reptiles returned to water as fish-like reptiles (e.g., Ichthyosaurus). Giant ferns (pteridophytes) fell to form coal deposits. Dinosaurs dominated land, notably Tyrannosaurus rex (~20 feet tall with dagger-like teeth).
  • ~65 mya: Dinosaurs suddenly disappeared (possibly due to climatic shifts or evolution into birds). Shrew-like viviparous mammals expanded. Due to continental drift, South American mammals were overridden by North American fauna when the continents joined, whereas Australian pouched mammals survived due to geographic isolation.

Example 1: Calculating Allele and Genotype Frequencies under Hardy-Weinberg Equilibrium

Problem (Hypothetical Assumption): In an isolated population of 500 beetles assumed to be in Hardy-Weinberg equilibrium, wing colour is governed by a single autosomal gene with complete dominance. Brown wing (\( B \)) is dominant over white wing (\( b \)). If 45 beetles in this population have white wings, calculate: (a) the frequency of the recessive allele \( b \), (b) the frequency of the dominant allele \( B \), and (c) the number of heterozygous brown-winged beetles.

Solution:

  1. White-winged beetles are homozygous recessive (\( bb \)). Their genotype frequency is: \[ q^2 = \frac{45}{500} = 0.09 \]
  2. Taking the square root gives the recessive allele frequency \( q \): \[ q = \sqrt{0.09} = 0.3 \]
  3. Using \( p + q = 1 \), the dominant allele frequency \( p \) is: \[ p = 1 - 0.3 = 0.7 \]
  4. The frequency of heterozygous individuals (\( Bb \)) is \( 2pq \): \[ 2pq = 2 \times 0.7 \times 0.3 = 0.42 \]
  5. Therefore, the number of heterozygous brown-winged beetles = \( 0.42 \times 500 = 210 \).

Example 2: Classifying Homologous vs. Analogous Structures

Problem: A biology student examines four pairs of structures: (i) thorns of Bougainvillea and tendrils of Cucurbita, (ii) flippers of a penguin and flippers of a dolphin, (iii) forelimbs of a bat and a cheetah, and (iv) tubers of potato and sweet potato. Solution:

Because both peripheral extremes (7 mm and 13 mm) are favoured while the mean phenotype (10 mm) is selected against, this represents disruptive selection. Consequently, the single-peaked normal distribution splits into a bimodal curve with two distinct peaks.

8. Common Pitfalls and NEET Revision Checklist | 8. सामान्य त्रुटियाँ एवं NEET पुनरावृत्ति सूची

English

High-Frequency Exam Traps to Avoid

  • Embryology Trap: Do not credit Ernst Haeckel's biogenetic claim as accepted fact; remember Karl Ernst von Baer disproved it.
  • Homology vs. Analogy Trap: Sweet potato (root) and potato (stem) are analogous (convergent), whereas Bougainvillea thorns and Cucurbita tendrils are homologous (divergent).
  • Dryopithecus vs. Ramapithecus: Ramapithecus was more man-like; Dryopithecus was more ape-like (both ~15 mya).
  • Hardy-Weinberg Trap: Given the count of recessive individuals, always compute \( q^2 \) first, take \( \sqrt{q^2} = q \), then find \( p = 1 - q \) before calculating \( 2pq \) or \( p^2 \).

Quick Revision Checklist

  • Big Bang: ~20 billion years ago | Earth: ~4.5 bya | First life: ~4 bya | First non-cellular life: ~3 bya | First cellular life: ~2,000 mya.
  • Plants invaded land before animals (~320 mya seaweeds/early plants; ~350 mya lobe-finned fish like Coelacanth).
  • Dinosaurs disappeared ~65 mya; Ichthyosaurus was a fish-like reptile (~200 mya).
  • Cranial capacities: H. habilis (650–800 cc) → H. erectus (~900 cc) → Neanderthal (~1,400 cc).

  • समजात बनाम समवृत्ति त्रुटि: शकरकंद (जड़) और आलू (तना) समवृत्ति (अभिसारी) हैं, जबकि बोगेनविलिया के काँटे और कुकुरबिटा के प्रतान समजात (अपसारी) हैं।

  • हार्डी-वाइनबर्ग गणना त्रुटि: अप्रभावी व्यष्टियों की संख्या से पहले \( q^2 \) निकालें, फिर \( q = \sqrt{q^2} \) और \( p = 1 - q \) ज्ञात करने के बाद ही \( 2pq \) या \( p^2 \) की गणना करें।

9. Concise Final Recap | 9. संक्षिप्त अंतिम सारांश

English

Life on Earth originated through chemical evolution in a reducing atmosphere, progressing from self-replicating macromolecular aggregates to aquatic single-celled organisms. Subsequent biodiversity arose via branching descent and natural selection acting on heritable variations, modulated by mutations, recombination, gene flow, and genetic drift. Comparative anatomy, paleontology, biochemistry, and human-driven selection confirm common ancestry, culminating in the geological rise of modern Homo sapiens equipped with advanced cranial capacity, language, and culture.
