1. Sinusoidal AC and Root Mean Square (RMS) Values / ज्यावक्रीय AC और वर्ग माध्य मूल (RMS) मान

English

A sinusoidal alternating voltage varies harmonically with time \(t\):

\[v(t) = v_m \sin(\omega t), \qquad \omega = 2\pi \nu = \frac{2\pi}{T}\]

Here, \(v_m\) is the peak voltage (amplitude), \(\omega\) is the angular frequency in \(\mathrm{rad/s}\), \(\nu\) is the linear frequency in \(\mathrm{Hz}\), and \(T\) is the time period.

Because a sine wave is symmetric about zero, the average value of alternating current or voltage over one complete cycle is zero. However, Joule heating in a resistor depends on \(i^2 R\), which is always positive. To express AC power in the same form as DC power (\(P = I^2 R\)), we define the root mean square (RMS) or effective value—the equivalent steady DC value that produces the same average heating effect in a given resistor over one full cycle.

\[I_{\mathrm{rms}} = I = \sqrt{\langle i^2 \rangle} = \frac{i_m}{\sqrt{2}} \approx 0.707\,i_m, \qquad V_{\mathrm{rms}} = V = \frac{v_m}{\sqrt{2}} \approx 0.707\,v_m\]

Standard AC ratings (such as a \(220\,\mathrm{V}\) household supply) always specify RMS values unless stated otherwise. For \(V = 220\,\mathrm{V}\), the peak voltage is \(v_m = \sqrt{2} \times 220\,\mathrm{V} \approx 311\,\mathrm{V}\).

\[v(t) = v_m \sin(\omega t), \qquad \omega = 2\pi \nu = \frac{2\pi}{T}\]

यहाँ \(v_m\) शिखर वोल्टता (आयाम), \(\omega\) कोणीय आवृत्ति (\(\mathrm{rad/s}\) में), \(\nu\) रैखिक आवृत्ति (\(\mathrm{Hz}\) में), और \(T\) आवर्तकाल है।

\[I_{\mathrm{rms}} = I = \sqrt{\langle i^2 \rangle} = \frac{i_m}{\sqrt{2}} \approx 0.707\,i_m, \qquad V_{\mathrm{rms}} = V = \frac{v_m}{\sqrt{2}} \approx 0.707\,v_m\]

घरेलू आपूर्ति (\(220\,\mathrm{V}\)) जैसे मानक AC अंकन सदैव RMS मान को दर्शाते हैं। \(V = 220\,\mathrm{V}\) के लिए, शिखर वोल्टता \(v_m = \sqrt{2} \times 220\,\mathrm{V} \approx 311\,\mathrm{V}\) होती है।

2. Phasors and Single-Element AC Circuits (R, L, and C) / फेज़र और एकल-अवयव AC परिपथ (R, L, और C)

English

Although alternating voltages and currents are scalar quantities, their amplitudes and relative phases combine mathematically like vectors. A phasor is a rotating vector of length equal to the peak amplitude (\(v_m\) or \(i_m\)) that rotates counterclockwise about the origin at angular speed \(\omega\). Its vertical projection gives the instantaneous value at time \(t\).

  • Purely Resistive Circuit (\(R\)): By Kirchhoff's loop rule, \(v_m \sin(\omega t) = iR\), so \(i = i_m \sin(\omega t)\) with \(i_m = v_m / R\). Current and voltage are in phase (\(\phi = 0\)). Average power is \(P = I^2 R = VI\).
  • Purely Inductive Circuit (\(L\)): The self-induced back emf opposes changes in current: \(v - L\,\frac{\mathrm{d}i}{\mathrm{d}t} = 0\). Integrating gives \(i = i_m \sin(\omega t - \pi/2)\), where \(i_m = v_m / X_L\) and inductive reactance is \(X_L = \omega L = 2\pi \nu L\). Thus, current lags voltage by \(\pi/2\) (\(90°\)). Average power over a full cycle is zero.
  • Purely Capacitive Circuit (\(C\)): Since \(q = C v = C v_m \sin(\omega t)\), differentiating \(i = \frac{\mathrm{d}q}{\mathrm{d}t}\) yields \(i = i_m \sin(\omega t + \pi/2)\), where \(i_m = v_m / X_C\) and capacitive reactance is \(X_C = \frac{1}{\omega C} = \frac{1}{2\pi \nu C}\). Thus, current leads voltage by \(\pi/2\) (\(90°\)). Average power over a full cycle is zero.

यद्यपि प्रत्यावर्ती वोल्टता और धारा अदिश राशियाँ हैं, लेकिन उनके आयाम और सापेक्ष कलाएँ गणितीय रूप से सदिशों की तरह जुड़ती हैं। फेज़र (Phasor) एक घूर्णी सदिश है जिसकी लंबाई शिखर आयाम (\(v_m\) या \(i_m\)) के बराबर होती है और जो मूल बिंदु के परितः वामावर्त दिशा में कोणीय वेग \(\omega\) से घूमता है। इसका ऊर्ध्वाधर प्रक्षेप समय \(t\) पर तात्क्षणिक मान देता है।

  • शुद्ध प्रतिरोधक परिपथ (\(R\)): किरखोफ के लूप नियम से \(v_m \sin(\omega t) = iR\), अतः \(i = i_m \sin(\omega t)\) जहाँ \(i_m = v_m / R\)। धारा और वोल्टता समान कला (\(\phi = 0\)) में होते हैं। औसत शक्ति \(P = I^2 R = VI\) होती है।

3. Series LCR Circuit, Impedance, and Resonance / श्रेणी LCR परिपथ, प्रतिबाधा और अनुनाद

English

When a resistor \(R\), inductor \(L\), and capacitor \(C\) are connected in series to an AC source \(v = v_m \sin(\omega t)\), the same current \(i = i_m \sin(\omega t + \phi)\) flows through all three elements. However, the voltage across \(R\) (\(V_R\)) is in phase with \(I\), the voltage across \(L\) (\(V_L\)) leads \(I\) by \(\pi/2\), and the voltage across \(C\) (\(V_C\)) lags \(I\) by \(\pi/2\).

Because \(V_L\) and \(V_C\) are \(180°\) out of phase, they directly oppose each other. Adding the phasors vectorially resolves the apparent paradox where \(V_R + V_L + V_C\) exceeds the source voltage \(V\):

\[V = \sqrt{V_R^2 + (V_C - V_L)^2}, \qquad Z = \sqrt{R^2 + (X_C - X_L)^2}, \qquad i_m = \frac{v_m}{Z}, \qquad \tan\phi = \frac{X_C - X_L}{R}\]

Here, \(Z\) is the impedance of the circuit (in \(\Omega\)). If \(X_C > X_L\), the circuit is predominantly capacitive (\(\phi > 0\), current leads voltage). If \(X_L > X_C\), it is predominantly inductive (\(\phi < 0\), current lags voltage).

Electrical Resonance and Quality Factor

Resonance occurs when the inductive and capacitive reactances exactly cancel (\(X_L = X_C\)). At this resonant angular frequency \(\omega_0\), the impedance reaches its minimum value \(Z_{\min} = R\), the phase angle \(\phi = 0\), and the current amplitude reaches its maximum \(i_{\max} = v_m / R\):

\[\omega_0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}, \qquad \nu_0 = \frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}}, \qquad Q = \frac{\omega_0 L}{R} = \frac{1}{\omega_0 C R} = \frac{1}{R}\sqrt{\frac{L}{C}}\]

The dimensionless quality factor \(Q\) measures the sharpness of the resonance peak. A smaller resistance \(R\) yields a higher \(Q\) and a sharper peak, which is essential in radio tuning circuits and airport metal detectors (where metal alters the coil's inductance, shifting the circuit away from resonance and triggering an alarm).

Key NEET Insight: Resonance requires both \(L\) and \(C\) in the circuit so their out-of-phase voltages can cancel. Neither an \(RL\) nor an \(RC\) circuit can exhibit resonance.

हिन्दी

जब एक प्रतिरोधक \(R\), प्रेरक \(L\) और संधारित्र \(C\) को श्रेणीक्रम में किसी AC स्रोत \(v = v_m \sin(\omega t)\) से जोड़ा जाता है, तो तीनों अवयवों में समान धारा \(i = i_m \sin(\omega t + \phi)\) प्रवाहित होती है। परंतु \(R\) के सिरों पर वोल्टता (\(V_R\)) धारा के साथ समान कला में होती है, \(L\) के सिरों पर वोल्टता (\(V_L\)) धारा से \(\pi/2\) अग्रगामी होती है, और \(C\) के सिरों पर वोल्टता (\(V_C\)) धारा से \(\pi/2\) पश्चगामी होती है।

चूँकि \(V_L\) और \(V_C\) परस्पर \(180°\) विपरीत कला में होते हैं, वे एक-दूसरे का विरोध करते हैं। फेज़र योग करने पर यह विरोधाभास समाप्त हो जाता है कि क्यों \(V_R + V_L + V_C\) का बीजगणितीय योग स्रोत वोल्टता \(V\) से अधिक हो सकता है:

\[V = \sqrt{V_R^2 + (V_C - V_L)^2}, \qquad Z = \sqrt{R^2 + (X_C - X_L)^2}, \qquad i_m = \frac{v_m}{Z}, \qquad \tan\phi = \frac{X_C - X_L}{R}\]

यहाँ \(Z\) परिपथ की प्रतिबाधा (\(\Omega\) में) है। यदि \(X_C > X_L\), तो परिपथ मुख्यतः धारितीय होता है (\(\phi > 0\), धारा वोल्टता से अग्रगामी)। यदि \(X_L > X_C\), तो परिपथ मुख्यतः प्रेरणिक होता है (\(\phi < 0\), धारा वोल्टता से पश्चगामी)।

विद्युत अनुनाद एवं विशेषता गुणांक (Quality Factor)

अनुनाद तब होता है जब प्रेरणिक और धारितीय प्रतिघात एक-दूसरे को निरस्त कर देते हैं (\(X_L = X_C\))। इस अनुनादी कोणीय आवृत्ति \(\omega_0\) पर, प्रतिबाधा न्यूनतम \(Z_{\min} = R\) हो जाती है, कलांतर \(\phi = 0\) होता है, और धारा आयाम अधिकतम \(i_{\max} = v_m / R\) हो जाता है:

\[\omega_0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}}, \qquad \nu_0 = \frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}}, \qquad Q = \frac{\omega_0 L}{R} = \frac{1}{\omega_0 C R} = \frac{1}{R}\sqrt{\frac{L}{C}}\]

विमाहीन विशेषता गुणांक \(Q\) अनुनाद शिखर की तीक्ष्णता को मापता है। कम प्रतिरोध \(R\) से अधिक \(Q\) और अधिक तीक्ष्ण शिखर प्राप्त होता है, जो रेडियो समस्वरण (tuning) परिपथों और हवाई अड्डों के धातु संसूचक (metal detector) में उपयोगी होता है।

NEET मुख्य बिंदु: अनुनाद के लिए परिपथ में \(L\) और \(C\) दोनों का होना आवश्यक है ताकि उनकी विपरीत कला वाली वोल्टताएँ निरस्त हो सकें। केवल \(RL\) या \(RC\) परिपथ में अनुनाद संभव नहीं है।

4. Power in AC Circuits and Transformers / AC परिपथों में शक्ति और ट्रांसफॉर्मर

English

Average Power, Power Factor, and Wattless Current

For a circuit with RMS voltage \(V\), RMS current \(I\), and phase difference \(\phi\), the average power dissipated over a complete cycle is:

\[P = V I \cos\phi = I^2 R, \qquad \cos\phi = \frac{R}{Z}\]

The quantity \(\cos\phi\) is called the power factor. Energy is dissipated only in the resistive element \(R\).

  • If \(\phi = 0\) (purely resistive or series LCR at resonance), \(\cos\phi = 1\) and power dissipation is maximum (\(P = VI\)).
  • If \(\phi = \pm\pi/2\) (purely inductive or capacitive), \(\cos\phi = 0\) and \(P = 0\); the current flowing without any power loss is called wattless current.
  • In power transmission, a low power factor requires a larger current \(I = P / (V \cos\phi)\) to deliver the same power, increasing \(I^2 R_{\mathrm{line}}\) heat losses. Connecting an appropriate capacitor in parallel neutralizes the lagging inductive wattless component, raising \(\cos\phi\) toward \(1\).

Transformers

A transformer steps alternating voltage up or down using mutual induction between two insulated coils—a primary coil (\(N_p\) turns) and a secondary coil (\(N_s\) turns)—wound on a soft-iron core. Assuming negligible winding resistance and complete flux linkage:

\[\frac{V_s}{V_p} = \frac{N_s}{N_p} = \frac{I_p}{I_s} \quad \text{(for an ideal, 100% efficient transformer)}\]
  • Step-up transformer: \(N_s > N_p \implies V_s > V_p\) and \(I_s < I_p\).
  • Step-down transformer: \(N_s < N_p \implies V_s < V_p\) and \(I_s > I_p\).

Energy Losses in Real Transformers & Remedies: (1) Flux leakage — reduced by winding primary and secondary coils one over the other; (2) Winding resistance (\(I^2R\)) — reduced using thick copper wire for high-current coils; (3) Eddy currents — reduced by using a laminated iron core; (4) Hysteresis loss — reduced by choosing a soft-iron core with a narrow hysteresis loop.

औसत शक्ति, शक्ति गुणांक और वाटहीन धारा

RMS वोल्टता \(V\), RMS धारा \(I\) और कलांतर \(\phi\) वाले परिपथ में एक पूर्ण चक्र में व्यय औसत शक्ति होती है:

\[P = V I \cos\phi = I^2 R, \qquad \cos\phi = \frac{R}{Z}\]

राशि \(\cos\phi\) को शक्ति गुणांक कहते हैं। ऊर्जा का क्षय केवल प्रतिरोधक अवयव \(R\) में ही होता है।

  • यदि \(\phi = 0\) (शुद्ध प्रतिरोधक या अनुनाद पर श्रेणी LCR), तो \(\cos\phi = 1\) और शक्ति क्षय अधिकतम (\(P = VI\)) होता है।
  • यदि \(\phi = \pm\pi/2\) (शुद्ध प्रेरक या संधारित्र), तो \(\cos\phi = 0\) और \(P = 0\); बिना किसी शक्ति हानि के प्रवाहित होने वाली धारा को वाटहीन धारा कहते हैं।
  • विद्युत संचरण में कम शक्ति गुणांक होने पर समान शक्ति देने के लिए अधिक धारा \(I = P / (V \cos\phi)\) की आवश्यकता होती है, जिससे \(I^2 R_{\mathrm{line}}\) ऊष्मा हानि बढ़ जाती है। समांतर क्रम में उपयुक्त संधारित्र जोड़ने से पश्चगामी प्रेरणिक घटक निरस्त होता है और \(\cos\phi\) का मान \(1\) के निकट आ जाता है।

ट्रांसफॉर्मर (Transformer)

ट्रांसफॉर्मर दो विद्युतरोधी कुंडलियों—प्राथमिक कुंडली (\(N_p\) फेरे) और द्वितीयक कुंडली (\(N_s\) फेरे)—के बीच अन्योन्य प्रेरण के सिद्धांत पर प्रत्यावर्ती वोल्टता को बढ़ाता या घटाता है। नगण्य प्रतिरोध और पूर्ण फ्लक्स बंधन मानने पर:

\[\frac{V_s}{V_p} = \frac{N_s}{N_p} = \frac{I_p}{I_s} \quad \text{(100% दक्ष आदर्श ट्रांसफॉर्मर के लिए)}\]
  • उच्चायी (Step-up) ट्रांसफॉर्मर: \(N_s > N_p \implies V_s > V_p\) और \(I_s < I_p\)।
  • अपचायी (Step-down) ट्रांसफॉर्मर: \(N_s < N_p \implies V_s < V_p\) और \(I_s > I_p\)।

वास्तविक ट्रांसफॉर्मर में ऊर्जा हानियाँ एवं निवारण: (1) फ्लक्स क्षरण — प्राथमिक व द्वितीयक कुंडलियों को एक-दूसरे के ऊपर लपेटकर कम किया जाता है; (2) कुंडली का प्रतिरोध (\(I^2R\)) — उच्च धारा वाली कुंडलियों में मोटे ताँबे के तार से कम किया जाता है; (3) भँवर धाराएँ — पटलित (laminated) नर्म लोहे के क्रोड से कम की जाती हैं; (4) शैथिल्य हानि — संकीर्ण शैथिल्य पाश वाले नर्म लोहे के क्रोड का उपयोग करके कम की जाती है।

ट्यूटोरियल आरेख 4: पटलित नर्म लोहे के क्रोड से जुड़ी प्राथमिक (Nₚ) और द्वितीयक (Nₛ) कुंडलियों वाले ट्रांसफॉर्मर का आरेख।

Example 1: Determining Peak and RMS Quantities in a Resistive Load

Problem (Hypothetical Assumption): An electric iron rated at \(1200\,\mathrm{W}\) is operated on an assumed \(240\,\mathrm{V}\), \(50\,\mathrm{Hz}\) AC mains supply. Find (a) the resistance of its heating element, (b) the RMS current, and (c) the peak voltage and peak current.

Solution:

(a) Using \(P = V^2 / R\) with RMS voltage \(V = 240\,\mathrm{V}\):

\[R = \frac{V^2}{P} = \frac{240^2}{1200} = \frac{57600}{1200} = 48\,\mathrm{Ω}\]

(b) The RMS current is:

\[I = \frac{P}{V} = \frac{1200}{240} = 5.0\,\mathrm{A}\]

(c) The peak voltage and peak current are:

\[v_m = \sqrt{2}\,V \approx 1.414 \times 240 \approx 339.4\,\mathrm{V}, \qquad i_m = \sqrt{2}\,I \approx 1.414 \times 5.0 \approx 7.07\,\mathrm{A}\]

Example 2: Solving a Series LCR Circuit and Finding Resonance

Problem (Hypothetical Assumption): A series LCR circuit with \(R = 12\,\mathrm{Ω}\), \(L = \frac{100}{\pi}\,\mathrm{mH} \approx 31.83\,\mathrm{mH}\), and \(C = \frac{2000}{\pi}\,\mathrm{µF} \approx 636.6\,\mathrm{µF}\) is connected to an assumed \(260\,\mathrm{V}\) (RMS), \(50\,\mathrm{Hz}\) AC source. Calculate (a) the impedance \(Z\), (b) the RMS current \(I\) and power factor \(\cos\phi\), (c) the average power dissipated, and (d) the resonant frequency \(\nu_0\).

Solution:

(a) First, find the angular frequency \(\omega = 2\pi\nu = 2\pi(50) = 100\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}\). Then:

\[X_L = \omega L = (100\pi)\left(\frac{0.1}{\pi}\right) = 10\,\mathrm{Ω}, \qquad X_C = \frac{1}{\omega C} = \frac{1}{(100\pi)\left(\frac{2\times 10^{-3}}{\pi}\right)} = 5\,\mathrm{Ω}\] \[Z = \sqrt{R^2 + (X_C - X_L)^2} = \sqrt{12^2 + (5 - 10)^2} = \sqrt{144 + 25} = 13\,\mathrm{Ω}\]

(b) The RMS current and power factor are:

\[I = \frac{V}{Z} = \frac{260}{13} = 20\,\mathrm{A}, \qquad \cos\phi = \frac{R}{Z} = \frac{12}{13} \approx 0.923\]

(c) Average power dissipated:

\[P = I^2 R = (20)^2 \times 12 = 4800\,\mathrm{W} = 4.8\,\mathrm{kW}\]

(d) Resonant frequency:

\[\nu_0 = \frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{LC}} = \frac{1}{2\pi\sqrt{\left(\frac{0.1}{\pi}\right)\left(\frac{2\times 10^{-3}}{\pi}\right)}} = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{2 \times 10^{-4}}} = \frac{100}{2\sqrt{2}} = 25\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{Hz} \approx 35.36\,\mathrm{Hz}\]

Example 3: Applying Ideal Transformer Relations

Problem (Hypothetical Assumption): An ideal step-down transformer has \(4000\) turns on its primary coil and \(400\) turns on its secondary coil. Its primary is connected to an assumed \(2200\,\mathrm{V}\) AC line, and the secondary supplies a pure resistive load of \(44\,\mathrm{Ω}\). Find the secondary voltage, secondary current, and primary current.

Solution:

From the transformer turns ratio:

\[V_s = \left(\frac{N_s}{N_p}\right) V_p = \left(\frac{400}{4000}\right) \times 2200 = 220\,\mathrm{V}\]

The secondary current through the \(44\,\mathrm{Ω}\) load is:

\[I_s = \frac{V_s}{R_{\mathrm{load}}} = \frac{220}{44} = 5.0\,\mathrm{A}\]

For an ideal transformer, \(I_p V_p = I_s V_s\):

\[I_p = \left(\frac{N_s}{N_p}\right) I_s = \left(\frac{400}{4000}\right) \times 5.0 = 0.50\,\mathrm{A}\]

Practice Question 1

An alternating current in a circuit is described by \(i = 10\sqrt{2}\sin(100\pi t)\,\mathrm{A}\), where \(t\) is in seconds. What are the linear frequency and the RMS value of this current?

  • (A) \(50\,\mathrm{Hz},\; 10\,\mathrm{A}\)
  • (B) \(100\,\mathrm{Hz},\; 10\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{A}\)
  • (C) \(50\,\mathrm{Hz},\; 10\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{A}\)
  • (D) \(100\,\mathrm{Hz},\; 10\,\mathrm{A}\)

Answer: (A) \(50\,\mathrm{Hz},\; 10\,\mathrm{A}\)

Worked Solution: Comparing \(i = 10\sqrt{2}\sin(100\pi t)\) with \(i = i_m \sin(\omega t)\), we have peak current \(i_m = 10\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{A}\) and angular frequency \(\omega = 100\pi\,\mathrm{rad/s}\). Thus, \(\nu = \frac{\omega}{2\pi} = \frac{100\pi}{2\pi} = 50\,\mathrm{Hz}\), and \(I_{\mathrm{rms}} = \frac{i_m}{\sqrt{2}} = \frac{10\sqrt{2}}{\sqrt{2}} = 10\,\mathrm{A}\).

Practice Question 2

A pure inductor \(L\) and a pure capacitor \(C\) have equal reactances at an angular frequency \(\omega\). If the angular frequency of the AC source is doubled to \(2\omega\), what is the ratio of the new inductive reactance to the new capacitive reactance \(\left(X_L' / X_C'\right)\)?

  • (A) \(1\)
  • (B) \(2\)
  • (C) \(4\)
  • (D) \(1/4\)

Answer: (C) \(4\)

Worked Solution: Initially, \(X_L = \omega L\) and \(X_C = \frac{1}{\omega C}\) are equal, so \(X_L = X_C\). When \(\omega\) is doubled to \(2\omega\), \(X_L' = 2\omega L = 2X_L\) and \(X_C' = \frac{1}{2\omega C} = \frac{X_C}{2} = \frac{X_L}{2}\). Therefore, \(\frac{X_L'}{X_C'} = \frac{2X_L}{X_L/2} = 4\).

Practice Question 3

In a series \(LCR\) circuit driven by an AC source, the RMS voltages measured across the resistor, inductor, and capacitor are \(V_R = 40\,\mathrm{V}\), \(V_L = 90\,\mathrm{V}\), and \(V_C = 60\,\mathrm{V}\), respectively. What are the RMS voltage of the source and the power factor of the circuit?

  • (A) \(190\,\mathrm{V},\; 0.5\)
  • (B) \(50\,\mathrm{V},\; 0.8\)
  • (C) \(50\,\mathrm{V},\; 0.6\)
  • (D) \(110\,\mathrm{V},\; 0.8\)

Answer: (B) \(50\,\mathrm{V},\; 0.8\)

Worked Solution: Because \(V_L\) and \(V_C\) are \(180°\) out of phase and both are \(90°\) out of phase with \(V_R\), the source RMS voltage is \(V = \sqrt{V_R^2 + (V_L - V_C)^2} = \sqrt{40^2 + (90 - 60)^2} = \sqrt{1600 + 900} = 50\,\mathrm{V}\). The power factor is \(\cos\phi = \frac{V_R}{V} = \frac{40}{50} = 0.8\).

Worked Solution: The resonant angular frequency is \(\omega_0 = \frac{1}{\sqrt{LC}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{0.4 \times 10 \times 10^{-6}}} = \frac{1}{\sqrt{4 \times 10^{-6}}} = 500\,\mathrm{rad/s}\). The quality factor is \(Q = \frac{\omega_0 L}{R} = \frac{500 \times 0.4}{10} = 20\).

Practice Question 5 (Assertion–Reason)

Assertion (A): In a purely capacitive AC circuit, the average power dissipated over one complete cycle is zero even though a non-zero RMS current flows through the capacitor.

Reason (R): The current in a purely capacitive circuit leads the applied alternating voltage by a phase angle of \(\pi/2\), making the power factor \(\cos\phi = 0\).

  • (A) Both (A) and (R) are true, and (R) is the correct explanation of (A).
  • (B) Both (A) and (R) are true, but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A).
  • (C) (A) is true, but (R) is false.
  • (D) Both (A) and (R) are false.

Answer: (A) Both (A) and (R) are true, and (R) is the correct explanation of (A).

Worked Solution: Average power in an AC circuit is \(P = VI\cos\phi\). In a pure capacitor, current leads voltage by \(\phi = \pi/2\), so \(\cos(\pi/2) = 0\) and \(P = 0\), even when \(I \neq 0\) (wattless current).

Practice Question 6

An ideal transformer has \(500\) turns in its primary coil and \(2500\) turns in its secondary coil. If the primary is connected to a \(220\,\mathrm{V}\) AC mains and the secondary delivers a current of \(2\,\mathrm{A}\) to a load, what are the secondary voltage and the primary current?

  • (A) \(44\,\mathrm{V},\; 0.4\,\mathrm{A}\)
  • (B) \(1100\,\mathrm{V},\; 0.4\,\mathrm{A}\)
  • (C) \(1100\,\mathrm{V},\; 10\,\mathrm{A}\)
  • (D) \(44\,\mathrm{V},\; 10\,\mathrm{A}\)

Answer: (C) \(1100\,\mathrm{V},\; 10\,\mathrm{A}\)

Worked Solution: For an ideal transformer, \(\frac{V_s}{V_p} = \frac{N_s}{N_p} = \frac{2500}{500} = 5\), so \(V_s = 5 \times 220 = 1100\,\mathrm{V}\). Since \(\frac{I_p}{I_s} = \frac{N_s}{N_p} = 5\), we get \(I_p = 5 \times 2 = 10\,\mathrm{A}\).

Common Pitfalls & Revision Checklist / सामान्य त्रुटियाँ और पुनरावृत्ति जाँच-सूची

English
  • RMS vs. Peak Confusion: Always check whether a problem gives \(V_{\mathrm{rms}}\) or \(v_m\); divide peak values by \(\sqrt{2}\) before computing power \(P = V I \cos\phi\).
  • Algebraic Addition of Voltages: Never add \(V_R\), \(V_L\), and \(V_C\) arithmetically. Always use \(V = \sqrt{V_R^2 + (V_L - V_C)^2}\).
  • Frequency Units: Remember \(\omega = 2\pi\nu\). Do not forget the factor of \(2\pi\) when converting between angular frequency (\(\mathrm{rad/s}\)) and linear frequency (\(\mathrm{Hz}\)).
  • Transformer Core & DC: A transformer works only with AC (changing flux); it cannot step up or step down a steady DC voltage.
हिन्दी
  • RMS बनाम शिखर मान की भ्रांति: सदैव जाँचें कि प्रश्न में \(V_{\mathrm{rms}}\) दिया है या \(v_m\); शक्ति \(P = V I \cos\phi\) की गणना से पहले शिखर मान को \(\sqrt{2}\) से विभाजित करें।
  • वोल्टताओं का बीजगणितीय योग: \(V_R\), \(V_L\) और \(V_C\) को कभी सीधे न जोड़ें। सदैव \(V = \sqrt{V_R^2 + (V_L - V_C)^2}\) का उपयोग करें।
  • आवृत्ति की इकाइयाँ: याद रखें \(\omega = 2\pi\nu\)। कोणीय आवृत्ति (\(\mathrm{rad/s}\)) और रैखिक आवृत्ति (\(\mathrm{Hz}\)) के बीच रूपांतरण में \(2\pi\) का गुणक न भूलें।
  • ट्रांसफॉर्मर और DC: ट्रांसफॉर्मर केवल AC (परिवर्ती फ्लक्स) पर कार्य करता है; यह स्थिर DC वोल्टता को परिवर्तित नहीं कर सकता।

Concise Final Recap / संक्षिप्त अंतिम सारांश

English

Sinusoidal AC is characterized by RMS values \(I = i_m/\sqrt{2}\) and \(V = v_m/\sqrt{2}\). In pure elements, current is in phase with voltage in \(R\), lags by \(\pi/2\) in \(L\) (\(X_L = \omega L\)), and leads by \(\pi/2\) in \(C\) (\(X_C = 1/\omega C\)). In a series LCR circuit, \(Z = \sqrt{R^2 + (X_C - X_L)^2}\), resonance occurs at \(\omega_0 = 1/\sqrt{LC}\) where \(Z = R\) and \(\cos\phi = 1\), average power is \(P = VI\cos\phi = I^2R\), and ideal transformers satisfy \(V_s/V_p = N_s/N_p = I_p/I_s\).

हिन्दी

ज्यावक्रीय AC को RMS मानों \(I = i_m/\sqrt{2}\) और \(V = v_m/\sqrt{2}\) से व्यक्त किया जाता है। शुद्ध अवयवों में धारा \(R\) में वोल्टता के समान कला में होती है, \(L\) में \(\pi/2\) पश्चगामी (\(X_L = \omega L\)) और \(C\) में \(\pi/2\) अग्रगामी (\(X_C = 1/\omega C\)) होती है। श्रेणी LCR परिपथ में \(Z = \sqrt{R^2 + (X_C - X_L)^2}\), अनुनाद \(\omega_0 = 1/\sqrt{LC}\) पर होता है जहाँ \(Z = R\) व \(\cos\phi = 1\), औसत शक्ति \(P = VI\cos\phi = I^2R\) होती है, तथा आदर्श ट्रांसफॉर्मर \(V_s/V_p = N_s/N_p = I_p/I_s\) का पालन करते हैं।
