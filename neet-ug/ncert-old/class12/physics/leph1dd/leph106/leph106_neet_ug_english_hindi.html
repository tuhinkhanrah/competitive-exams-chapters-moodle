Electromagnetic Induction

विद्युत चुंबकीय प्रेरण (Electromagnetic Induction)

Learning Objectives and Overview | अधिगम उद्देश्य एवं सारांश

English

Earlier studies of electromagnetism showed that electric currents generate magnetic fields. The converse question—whether changing magnetic fields can generate electric currents—was answered affirmatively by the experiments of Michael Faraday and Joseph Henry. This phenomenon, known as electromagnetic induction, forms the working principle behind electric generators, transformers, and induction devices.

By completing this NEET UG tutorial, you will be able to:

  • Compute magnetic flux through planar and curved surfaces in uniform and non-uniform magnetic fields.
  • Apply Faraday's laws of induction to determine instantaneous and average induced electromotive force (emf), current, and total induced charge.
  • Predict the direction of induced current using Lenz's law and connect it to the conservation of energy.
  • Calculate motional emf in translating conductors and rotational emf in spinning rods or spoked wheels.
  • Determine self-inductance, mutual inductance, and magnetic energy density in coils and solenoids.
  • Analyze the working principle and sinusoidal emf output of an alternating current (AC) generator.

1. Magnetic Flux and Faraday's Laws of Induction | चुंबकीय फ्लक्स एवं फैराडे के प्रेरण नियम

English

Experimental Foundation

Faraday and Henry demonstrated three key ways to induce an electric current in a closed conducting coil:

  • Moving a bar magnet toward or away from a stationary coil (or moving the coil relative to the magnet). Faster relative motion produces a larger deflection in a connected galvanometer.
  • Moving a current-carrying primary coil relative to a secondary coil.
  • Changing the current in a stationary primary coil (for example, by closing or opening a switch), which induces a momentary current in a nearby stationary secondary coil. Inserting a soft iron core inside the coils markedly increases the induced effect.

The unifying feature of all three observations is a time-varying magnetic flux linked with the circuit.

Magnetic Flux

Magnetic flux \(\Phi_B\) measures the total magnetic field passing through a given surface. For a flat surface of area vector \(\mathbf{A}\) (directed normal to the surface) placed in a uniform magnetic field \(\mathbf{B}\):

\[ \Phi_B = \mathbf{B} \cdot \mathbf{A} = B A \cos\theta \]

Here, \(\theta\) is the angle between \(\mathbf{B}\) and the area vector \(\mathbf{A}\). If the surface is curved or the magnetic field varies across it, the surface is divided into small area elements \(\mathrm{d}\mathbf{A}_i\), and the total flux is summed:

\[ \Phi_B = \sum_{\text{all}} \mathbf{B}_i \cdot \mathrm{d}\mathbf{A}_i = \int \mathbf{B} \cdot \mathrm{d}\mathbf{A} \]

Magnetic flux is a scalar quantity. Its SI unit is the weber (Wb), which is equivalent to \(\mathrm{T \cdot m^2}\). Its dimensional formula is \([\mathrm{M\,L^2\,T^{-2}\,A^{-1}}]\).

Faraday's Laws of Electromagnetic Induction

First Law: Whenever the magnetic flux linked with a circuit changes, an electromotive force (emf) is induced in the circuit, lasting only as long as the flux continues to change.

Second Law: The magnitude of the induced emf is equal to the time rate of change of magnetic flux through the circuit. For a closely wound coil of \(N\) turns:

\[ \varepsilon = -N \frac{\mathrm{d}\Phi_B}{\mathrm{d}t} \]

For a finite change in flux \(\Delta\Phi_B\) over a time interval \(\Delta t\), the average induced emf is \(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{avg}} = -N \frac{\Delta\Phi_B}{\Delta t}\). If the circuit is closed with total resistance \(R\), the induced current and total charge flowing in time \(\Delta t\) are:

\[ I = \frac{|\varepsilon|}{R} = \frac{N}{R}\left|\frac{\mathrm{d}\Phi_B}{\mathrm{d}t}\right|, \qquad q = \int I\,\mathrm{d}t = \frac{N |\Delta\Phi_B|}{R} \]
High-Yield NEET Insight: Time-Independence of Induced Charge

Notice that \(q = \frac{N|\Delta\Phi_B|}{R}\) depends only on the net change in magnetic flux and circuit resistance, not on how fast or slow the change occurs. Increasing the speed of a magnet increases the induced emf and current, but the total charge circulated for the same flux change remains identical.

चुंबकीय फ्लक्स

चुंबकीय फ्लक्स \(\Phi_B\) किसी दिए गए पृष्ठ से लंबवत गुजरने वाले कुल चुंबकीय क्षेत्र की माप है। यदि एकसमान चुंबकीय क्षेत्र \(\mathbf{B}\) में क्षेत्रफल सदिश \(\mathbf{A}\) (पृष्ठ के लंबवत) वाला एक समतल पृष्ठ रखा हो, तो:

\[ \Phi_B = \mathbf{B} \cdot \mathbf{A} = B A \cos\theta \]

यहाँ \(\theta\), चुंबकीय क्षेत्र \(\mathbf{B}\) तथा क्षेत्रफल सदिश \(\mathbf{A}\) के बीच का कोण है। यदि पृष्ठ वक्र हो या चुंबकीय क्षेत्र असमान हो, तो पृष्ठ को सूक्ष्म क्षेत्रफल अल्पांशों \(\mathrm{d}\mathbf{A}_i\) में विभाजित करके कुल फ्लक्स का योग किया जाता है:

\[ \Phi_B = \sum_{\text{all}} \mathbf{B}_i \cdot \mathrm{d}\mathbf{A}_i = \int \mathbf{B} \cdot \mathrm{d}\mathbf{A} \]

चुंबकीय फ्लक्स एक अदिश राशि है। इसका SI मात्रक वेबर (Wb) है, जो \(\mathrm{T \cdot m^2}\) के तुल्य है। इसका विमीय सूत्र \([\mathrm{M\,L^2\,T^{-2}\,A^{-1}}]\) है।

ट्यूटोरियल आरेख 1: एक समतल पृष्ठ से गुजरता एकसमान चुंबकीय क्षेत्र B, जहाँ क्षेत्रफल सदिश A क्षेत्र B के साथ θ कोण बनाता है।

फैराडे के विद्युत चुंबकीय प्रेरण के नियम

प्रथम नियम: जब भी किसी परिपथ से संबद्ध चुंबकीय फ्लक्स में परिवर्तन होता है, तो परिपथ में एक विद्युत वाहक बल (emf) प्रेरित होता है, जो केवल तब तक बना रहता है जब तक फ्लक्स में परिवर्तन होता रहता है।

द्वितीय नियम: प्रेरित विद्युत वाहक बल का परिमाण परिपथ से गुजरने वाले चुंबकीय फ्लक्स के परिवर्तन की समय दर के बराबर होता है। \(N\) फेरों वाली सघन रूप से लिपटी कुंडली के लिए:

\[ \varepsilon = -N \frac{\mathrm{d}\Phi_B}{\mathrm{d}t} \]

यदि समयांतराल \(\Delta t\) में फ्लक्स में परिमित परिवर्तन \(\Delta\Phi_B\) होता है, तो औसत प्रेरित emf \(\varepsilon_{\mathrm{avg}} = -N \frac{\Delta\Phi_B}{\Delta t}\) होता है। यदि परिपथ बंद है और उसका कुल प्रतिरोध \(R\) है, तो समयांतराल \(\Delta t\) में प्रेरित धारा तथा प्रवाहित कुल आवेश निम्न होंगे:

\[ I = \frac{|\varepsilon|}{R} = \frac{N}{R}\left|\frac{\mathrm{d}\Phi_B}{\mathrm{d}t}\right|, \qquad q = \int I\,\mathrm{d}t = \frac{N |\Delta\Phi_B|}{R} \]
महत्वपूर्ण NEET बिंदु: प्रेरित आवेश की समय से स्वतंत्रता

ध्यान दें कि \(q = \frac{N|\Delta\Phi_B|}{R}\) केवल चुंबकीय फ्लक्स में कुल परिवर्तन और परिपथ के प्रतिरोध पर निर्भर करता है, इस बात पर नहीं कि परिवर्तन कितनी तेजी या धीमी गति से हुआ है। चुंबक की चाल बढ़ाने पर प्रेरित emf और धारा बढ़ती है, किंतु समान फ्लक्स परिवर्तन के लिए प्रवाहित कुल आवेश अपरिवर्तित रहता है।

2. Lenz's Law and Conservation of Energy | लेंज का नियम एवं ऊर्जा संरक्षण

English

The negative sign in Faraday's equation \(\varepsilon = -N\frac{\mathrm{d}\Phi_B}{\mathrm{d}t}\) is explained by Lenz's law:

The polarity of induced emf is always such that it tends to produce a current that opposes the change in magnetic flux that produced it.

Connection to Conservation of Energy: When the North pole of a bar magnet is pushed toward a closed conducting loop, the induced current flows counter-clockwise (as viewed from the magnet side), creating a North magnetic pole facing the approaching magnet. This generates a repulsive force. External mechanical work must be performed to push the magnet against this repulsion, and that mechanical work is converted into electrical energy and dissipated as Joule heat (\(I^2 R\)) in the loop. If the induced current instead attracted the magnet, the magnet would accelerate indefinitely without external work, violating energy conservation.

3. Motional and Rotational Electromotive Force | गतिक एवं घूर्णी विद्युत वाहक बल

English

Translating Conductor in a Uniform Magnetic Field

Consider a straight conductor PQ of length \(l\) sliding at constant velocity \(v\) along frictionless U-shaped conducting rails in a uniform, time-independent magnetic field \(\mathbf{B}\) directed perpendicular to the plane of the rails. If the distance from the fixed end RS to the rod PQ is \(x\), the enclosed flux is \(\Phi_B = B l x\). As the rod moves inward (\(\mathrm{d}x/\mathrm{d}t = -v\)), the induced emf is:

\[ \varepsilon = -\frac{\mathrm{d}\Phi_B}{\mathrm{d}t} = -B l \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} = B l v \]

In general vector form, when velocity \(\mathbf{v}\), magnetic field \(\mathbf{B}\), and length vector \(\mathbf{l}\) are oriented arbitrarily:

\[ \varepsilon = (\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B}) \cdot \mathbf{l} \]

This same result follows from the magnetic Lorentz force \(\mathbf{F} = q(\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B})\) acting on free charge carriers inside the moving rod. Notice that if the rod is stationary and \(\mathbf{B}\) changes with time, \(\mathbf{v} = 0\), so the force on charges arises from a non-conservative induced electric field \(\mathbf{E}\) generated by the time-varying magnetic field.

Rotating Conductor in a Uniform Magnetic Field

When a metallic rod of length \(R\) rotates with constant angular speed \(\omega\) (frequency \(\nu = \omega / 2\pi\)) about one hinged end in a plane perpendicular to a uniform magnetic field \(B\), an element \(\mathrm{d}r\) at distance \(r\) has speed \(v = \omega r\). Integrating \(\mathrm{d}\varepsilon = B(\omega r)\mathrm{d}r\) from \(0\) to \(R\) gives:

\[ \varepsilon = \frac{1}{2} B \omega R^2 = \pi B \nu R^2 \]

Spoked Wheel Rule: If a wheel has \(k\) metallic spokes connecting the axle to a conducting rim, all spokes are connected in parallel between the axle and the rim. Therefore, the net emf between the axle and the rim remains \(\frac{1}{2}B\omega R^2\), completely independent of the number of spokes.

एकसमान चुंबकीय क्षेत्र में स्थानांतरीय गति करता चालक

मान लीजिए \(l\) लंबाई की एक सीधी चालक छड़ PQ, कागज़ के तल के लंबवत एकसमान एवं समय-स्वतंत्र चुंबकीय क्षेत्र \(\mathbf{B}\) में घर्षणरहित U-आकार की चालक पटरियों पर नियत वेग \(v\) से फिसलती है। यदि स्थिर सिरे RS से छड़ PQ की दूरी \(x\) है, तो परिबद्ध फ्लक्स \(\Phi_B = B l x\) होगा। छड़ के अंदर की ओर चलने पर (\(\mathrm{d}x/\mathrm{d}t = -v\)), प्रेरित विद्युत वाहक बल:

\[ \varepsilon = -\frac{\mathrm{d}\Phi_B}{\mathrm{d}t} = -B l \frac{\mathrm{d}x}{\mathrm{d}t} = B l v \]

व्यापक सदिश रूप में, जब वेग \(\mathbf{v}\), चुंबकीय क्षेत्र \(\mathbf{B}\), और लंबाई सदिश \(\mathbf{l}\) किसी भी अभिविन्यास में हों:

\[ \varepsilon = (\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B}) \cdot \mathbf{l} \]

यही परिणाम गतिमान छड़ के भीतर मुक्त आवेश वाहकों पर लगने वाले चुंबकीय लॉरेंत्ज़ बल \(\mathbf{F} = q(\mathbf{v} \times \mathbf{B})\) से भी प्राप्त होता है। ध्यान दें कि यदि छड़ स्थिर रहे और \(\mathbf{B}\) समय के साथ बदले, तो \(\mathbf{v} = 0\) होने के कारण आवेशों पर बल परिवर्ती चुंबकीय क्षेत्र द्वारा उत्पन्न एक असंरक्षी प्रेरित विद्युत क्षेत्र \(\mathbf{E}\) के कारण लगता है।

4. Self-Inductance, Mutual Inductance, and Magnetic Energy | स्व-प्रेरकत्व, अन्योन्य प्रेरकत्व एवं चुंबकीय ऊर्जा

English

Inductance is a geometric and material property of a coil, analogous to electrical capacitance, with SI unit henry (H) and dimensional formula \([\mathrm{M\,L^2\,T^{-2}\,A^{-2}}]\).

Self-Inductance (\(L\))

When the current \(I\) in an isolated coil of \(N\) turns changes, the changing flux linkage \(N\Phi_B = L I\) induces a back emf in the same coil that opposes the change in current:

\[ \varepsilon = -L \frac{\mathrm{d}I}{\mathrm{d}t} \]

Self-inductance \(L\) acts as electrical inertia (analogous to mass \(m\) in mechanics). For a long solenoid of length \(l\), cross-sectional area \(A\), \(n\) turns per unit length, and core relative permeability \(\mu_r\):

\[ L = \mu_r \mu_0 n^2 A l = \frac{\mu_r \mu_0 N^2 A}{l} \]

Work done against the back emf to establish a steady current \(I\) is stored as magnetic potential energy \(U_B\). Using \(B = \mu_0 n I\) for an air-core solenoid, the magnetic energy density \(u_B\) (energy per unit volume) is:

\[ U_B = \frac{1}{2} L I^2, \qquad u_B = \frac{B^2}{2\mu_0} \]

Mutual Inductance (\(M\))

When a time-varying current \(I_2\) in coil 2 changes the flux linked with a nearby coil 1 (\(N_1\Phi_1 = M_{12} I_2\)), an emf is induced in coil 1:

\[ \varepsilon_1 = -M_{12} \frac{\mathrm{d}I_2}{\mathrm{d}t}, \qquad M_{12} = M_{21} = M \]

Standard Geometries for Mutual Inductance:

  • Two long co-axial solenoids of equal length \(l\), turn densities \(n_1, n_2\), inner radius \(r_1\), and outer radius \(r_2\) (with medium \(\mu_r\)): \[ M = \mu_r \mu_0 n_1 n_2 \pi r_1^2 l \]
  • Two concentric coplanar circular coils with \(N_1, N_2\) turns and radii \(r_1 \ll r_2\): \[ M = \frac{\mu_0 N_1 N_2 \pi r_1^2}{2 r_2} \]

स्व-प्रेरकत्व (\(L\))

जब \(N\) फेरों वाली एकल कुंडली में धारा \(I\) परिवर्तित होती है, तो बदलता हुआ फ्लक्स बंधता \(N\Phi_B = L I\) उसी कुंडली में एक विरोधी विद्युत वाहक बल (back emf) प्रेरित करता है जो धारा परिवर्तन का विरोध करता है:

\[ \varepsilon = -L \frac{\mathrm{d}I}{\mathrm{d}t} \]

स्व-प्रेरकत्व \(L\) वैद्युत जड़त्व की भूमिका निभाता है (यांत्रिकी में द्रव्यमान \(m\) के समरूप)। \(l\) लंबाई, \(A\) अनुप्रस्थ काट क्षेत्रफल, प्रति इकाई लंबाई \(n\) फेरों तथा कोर की सापेक्ष चुंबकशीलता \(\mu_r\) वाली एक लंबी परिनालिका के लिए:

\[ L = \mu_r \mu_0 n^2 A l = \frac{\mu_r \mu_0 N^2 A}{l} \]

विरोधी emf के विरुद्ध स्थायी धारा \(I\) स्थापित करने में किया गया कार्य चुंबकीय स्थितिज ऊर्जा \(U_B\) के रूप में संचित होता है। वायु-कोर परिनालिका के लिए \(B = \mu_0 n I\) का उपयोग करने पर चुंबकीय ऊर्जा घनत्व \(u_B\) (प्रति एकांक आयतन ऊर्जा) प्राप्त होता है:

\[ U_B = \frac{1}{2} L I^2, \qquad u_B = \frac{B^2}{2\mu_0} \]

5. Alternating Current (AC) Generator | प्रत्यावर्ती धारा (AC) जनित्र

English

An AC generator converts mechanical energy into electrical energy by rotating an armature coil of \(N\) turns and area \(A\) with constant angular speed \(\omega = 2\pi\nu\) in a uniform magnetic field \(B\), about an axis perpendicular to \(\mathbf{B}\). Slip rings and carbon brushes transfer the induced alternating emf to an external circuit.

If \(\theta = \omega t\) is the angle between \(\mathbf{B}\) and the area vector \(\mathbf{A}\) at time \(t\), the magnetic flux through each turn is \(\Phi_B = B A \cos(\omega t)\). Applying Faraday's law yields:

\[ \varepsilon = -N \frac{\mathrm{d}}{\mathrm{d}t}(B A \cos\omega t) = N B A \omega \sin(\omega t) = \varepsilon_0 \sin(2\pi\nu t) \]

Here, \(\varepsilon_0 = N B A \omega = 2\pi \nu N B A\) is the peak (maximum) emf. Note the phase relationship: when the coil plane is perpendicular to \(\mathbf{B}\) (\(\theta = 0^\circ, 180^\circ\)), flux is maximum (\(\pm BA\)) but induced emf is zero; when the coil plane is parallel to \(\mathbf{B}\) (\(\theta = 90^\circ, 270^\circ\)), flux is zero but the rate of change of flux—and hence induced emf—is maximum (\(\pm\varepsilon_0\)).

Example 1: Calculating Instantaneous EMF and Induced Charge from Time-Varying Flux

(Assuming a hypothetical coil with \(N = 20\) turns and resistance \(R = 8\,\Omega\).)

The magnetic flux per turn linked with a \(20\)-turn closed coil of resistance \(8\,\Omega\) varies with time \(t\) (in seconds) as \(\Phi_B(t) = (3t^2 - 4t + 5)\,\mathrm{mWb}\). Calculate: (a) the magnitude of instantaneous induced emf at \(t = 2\,\mathrm{s}\), and (b) the total charge flowing through the coil between \(t = 0\,\mathrm{s}\) and \(t = 2\,\mathrm{s}\).

Solution:

(a) Differentiating \(\Phi_B(t)\) with respect to \(t\): \[ \frac{\mathrm{d}\Phi_B}{\mathrm{d}t} = (6t - 4)\,\mathrm{mWb/s} \] At \(t = 2\,\mathrm{s}\), \(\frac{\mathrm{d}\Phi_B}{\mathrm{d}t} = 6(2) - 4 = 8\,\mathrm{mWb/s} = 8 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb/s}\). Therefore, the magnitude of induced emf is: \[ |\varepsilon| = N \left|\frac{\mathrm{d}\Phi_B}{\mathrm{d}t}\right| = 20 \times 8 \times 10^{-3} = 0.16\,\mathrm{V} \]

(b) Flux per turn at \(t = 0\,\mathrm{s}\) is \(\Phi_B(0) = 5\,\mathrm{mWb}\). At \(t = 2\,\mathrm{s}\), \(\Phi_B(2) = 3(2)^2 - 4(2) + 5 = 9\,\mathrm{mWb}\). The net change in flux per turn is \(|\Delta\Phi_B| = 9 - 5 = 4\,\mathrm{mWb} = 4 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{Wb}\). Total induced charge: \[ q = \frac{N |\Delta\Phi_B|}{R} = \frac{20 \times 4 \times 10^{-3}}{8} = 1.0 \times 10^{-2}\,\mathrm{C} = 10\,\mathrm{mC} \]

Example 2: Applying Motional EMF and Magnetic Braking Force on Sliding Rails

(Assuming hypothetical rail spacing \(l = 0.50\,\mathrm{m}\), speed \(v = 4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}\), resistance \(R = 2.0\,\Omega\), and field \(B = 0.80\,\mathrm{T}\).)

A conducting rod of length \(0.50\,\mathrm{m}\) slides without friction at a constant speed of \(4.0\,\mathrm{m/s}\) along two parallel rails connected by a \(2.0\,\Omega\) resistor. A uniform magnetic field of \(0.80\,\mathrm{T}\) acts perpendicular to the plane of the rails. Find: (a) the induced emf, (b) the induced current, and (c) the external force needed to keep the rod moving at constant speed.

Solution:

(a) Motional emf across the rod: \[ \varepsilon = B l v = 0.80 \times 0.50 \times 4.0 = 1.6\,\mathrm{V} \] (b) Induced current through the resistor: \[ I = \frac{\varepsilon}{R} = \frac{1.6}{2.0} = 0.80\,\mathrm{A} \] (c) By Lenz's law, the magnetic force \(F_B = B I l\) opposes the motion of the rod. To maintain constant velocity, the external force must balance \(F_B\): \[ F_{\mathrm{ext}} = B I l = 0.80 \times 0.80 \times 0.50 = 0.32\,\mathrm{N} \]

Example 4: Finding Mutual Inductance and Induced EMF in Concentric Coils

(Assuming hypothetical coil radii \(r_1 = 2.0\,\mathrm{cm}\), \(r_2 = 20\,\mathrm{cm}\), turns \(N_1 = 100\), \(N_2 = 50\), and \(\mu_0 = 4\pi \times 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\cdot m/A}\).)

Solution:

  • (A) A और R दोनों सत्य हैं, तथा R, A की सही व्याख्या है।
  • (B) A और R दोनों सत्य हैं, परंतु R, A की सही व्याख्या नहीं है।

Common Pitfalls and Revision Checklist | सामान्य त्रुटियाँ एवं पुनरावलोकन सूची

English

Common Pitfalls to Avoid in NEET UG

  • Angle \(\theta\) Confusion: In \(\Phi_B = BA\cos\theta\), \(\theta\) is the angle between \(\mathbf{B}\) and the normal (area vector \(\mathbf{A}\)), not the angle with the plane of the coil. If a problem gives angle \(\alpha\) with the plane, use \(\theta = 90^\circ - \alpha\).
  • Open vs. Closed Circuit: An emf is induced across an open loop whenever flux changes, but an induced current requires a closed conducting path.
  • Number of Spokes: Adding more metallic spokes to a rotating wheel does not multiply the emf between the axle and rim; spokes are in parallel.
  • Electric Flux Does Not Induce Current: Moving a loop in a uniform electric field between capacitor plates does not induce a magnetic-induction current.

Quick Revision Checklist

  • Verify units: \(1\,\mathrm{G} = 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{T}\), \(1\,\mathrm{Wb} = 1\,\mathrm{T\cdot m^2}\).
  • Remember \(q = \frac{N|\Delta\Phi_B|}{R}\) is independent of time \(\Delta t\).
  • For a rotating rod/disc: \(\varepsilon = \frac{1}{2}B\omega R^2 = \pi B \nu R^2\).
  • Energy densities: \(u_B = \frac{B^2}{2\mu_0}\) and \(u_E = \frac{1}{2}\varepsilon_0 E^2\).
हिन्दी

NEET UG में बचने योग्य सामान्य त्रुटियाँ

  • कोण \(\theta\) का भ्रम: \(\Phi_B = BA\cos\theta\) में \(\theta\), चुंबकीय क्षेत्र \(\mathbf{B}\) और अभिलंब (क्षेत्रफल सदिश \(\mathbf{A}\)) के बीच का कोण है, न कि कुंडली के तल के साथ कोण। यदि तल के साथ कोण \(\alpha\) दिया हो, तो \(\theta = 90^\circ - \alpha\) लें।
  • खुला बनाम बंद परिपथ: फ्लक्स बदलने पर खुले लूप के सिरों पर भी emf प्रेरित होता है, परंतु प्रेरित धारा के लिए बंद चालक पथ आवश्यक है।
  • तीलियों की संख्या: घूमते पहिये में अधिक धात्विक तीलियाँ जोड़ने से धुरी और रिम के बीच का emf नहीं बढ़ता; तीलियाँ समांतर क्रम में होती हैं।
  • विद्युत फ्लक्स से धारा प्रेरित नहीं होती: संधारित्र की प्लेटों के बीच एकसमान विद्युत क्षेत्र में लूप की गति से चुंबकीय-प्रेरण धारा उत्पन्न नहीं होती।

त्वरित पुनरावलोकन सूची

  • मात्रक जांचें: \(1\,\mathrm{G} = 10^{-4}\,\mathrm{T}\), \(1\,\mathrm{Wb} = 1\,\mathrm{T\cdot m^2}\)।
  • याद रखें \(q = \frac{N|\Delta\Phi_B|}{R}\) समय \(\Delta t\) से स्वतंत्र होता है।
  • घूर्णन करती छड़/चकती के लिए: \(\varepsilon = \frac{1}{2}B\omega R^2 = \pi B \nu R^2\)।
  • ऊर्जा घनत्व: \(u_B = \frac{B^2}{2\mu_0}\) तथा \(u_E = \frac{1}{2}\varepsilon_0 E^2\)।

Concise Final Recap | संक्षिप्त अंतिम सार

English

Electromagnetic induction links changing magnetic flux (\(\Phi_B = \mathbf{B}\cdot\mathbf{A}\)) to induced emf via Faraday's law (\(\varepsilon = -N\frac{\mathrm{d}\Phi_B}{\mathrm{d}t}\)), while Lenz's law ensures energy conservation by opposing the flux change. Motional emf (\(Blv\)) and rotational emf (\(\frac{1}{2}B\omega R^2\)) govern moving conductors; self-inductance (\(L\)) and mutual inductance (\(M\)) quantify flux linkage per unit current and magnetic energy storage (\(\frac{1}{2}LI^2\)); and rotating coils in uniform magnetic fields generate sinusoidal alternating emf (\(\varepsilon = NBA\omega\sin\omega t\)).

हिन्दी

विद्युत चुंबकीय प्रेरण फैराडे के नियम (\(\varepsilon = -N\frac{\mathrm{d}\Phi_B}{\mathrm{d}t}\)) के माध्यम से परिवर्ती चुंबकीय फ्लक्स (\(\Phi_B = \mathbf{B}\cdot\mathbf{A}\)) को प्रेरित emf से जोड़ता है, जबकि लेंज का नियम फ्लक्स परिवर्तन का विरोध करके ऊर्जा संरक्षण सुनिश्चित करता है। गतिक emf (\(Blv\)) तथा घूर्णी emf (\(\frac{1}{2}B\omega R^2\)) गतिमान चालकों को नियंत्रित करते हैं; स्व-प्रेरकत्व (\(L\)) और अन्योन्य प्रेरकत्व (\(M\)) प्रति एकांक धारा फ्लक्स बंधता तथा चुंबकीय ऊर्जा संचय (\(\frac{1}{2}LI^2\)) को मापते हैं; और एकसमान चुंबकीय क्षेत्र में घूमती कुंडलियाँ ज्यावक्रीय प्रत्यावर्ती emf (\(\varepsilon = NBA\omega\sin\omega t\)) उत्पन्न करती हैं।
