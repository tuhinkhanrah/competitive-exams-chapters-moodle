1. The Bar Magnet and Magnetic Field Lines / छड़ चुम्बक और चुम्बकीय क्षेत्र रेखाएँ

English

A freely suspended bar magnet aligns approximately along the geographic north–south direction. The end pointing toward geographic north is the north pole (N), and the end pointing toward geographic south is the south pole (S). Like poles repel each other, while unlike poles attract.

Inseparability of Poles: Magnetic monopoles do not exist. Slicing a bar magnet either transversely or longitudinally always produces two smaller magnets, each possessing both a north and a south pole.

Properties of Magnetic Field Lines:

  • Continuous closed loops: Outside the magnet, lines run from N to S; inside the magnet, they continue from S to N. They never start or terminate at a point.
  • Direction: The tangent at any point on a field line gives the direction of the net magnetic field \(\mathbf{B}\) at that point.
  • Field strength: Higher density of field lines per unit area indicates a stronger magnetic field magnitude \(B\).
  • Non-intersection: Two magnetic field lines never intersect, because intersection would imply two different directions of \(\mathbf{B}\) at a single point.

Solenoid Equivalence: A finite current-carrying solenoid produces the same far-field magnetic pattern as a bar magnet of identical magnetic dipole moment \(\mathbf{m}\). Cutting a bar magnet is analogous to cutting a solenoid into two shorter solenoids.

2. Magnetic Dipole in a Uniform Field & Electrostatic Analogy / एकसमान क्षेत्र में चुम्बकीय द्विध्रुव एवं स्थिरवैद्युत सादृश्य

English

When a small magnetic dipole (compass needle or short bar magnet) of magnetic moment \(\mathbf{m}\) (directed from S to N) is placed in a uniform magnetic field \(\mathbf{B}\) at an angle \(\theta\) with \(\mathbf{B}\), the net translational force is zero, but it experiences a restoring torque:

\[ \boldsymbol{\tau} = \mathbf{m} \times \mathbf{B}, \qquad \tau = mB \sin\theta \]

Fixing the zero of magnetic potential energy \(U_m\) at \(\theta = 90°\) (when \(\mathbf{m} \perp \mathbf{B}\)), the potential energy at angle \(\theta\) is:

\[ U_m = -\mathbf{m} \cdot \mathbf{B} = -mB \cos\theta \]
  • Most Stable Equilibrium (\(\theta = 0°\)): \(\tau = 0\), \(U_m = -mB\) (minimum).
  • Most Unstable Equilibrium (\(\theta = 180°\)): \(\tau = 0\), \(U_m = +mB\) (maximum).
  • Work done by external agent in slowly rotating the dipole from \(\theta_1\) to \(\theta_2\): \(W_{\text{ext}} = U_m(\theta_2) - U_m(\theta_1) = mB(\cos\theta_1 - \cos\theta_2)\).

3. Gauss's Law for Magnetism / चुम्बकत्व के लिए गाउस का नियम

English

Because magnetic field lines always form closed loops, the number of magnetic field lines entering any closed Gaussian surface \(S\) equals the number of lines leaving it. Mathematically, the net magnetic flux \(\phi_B\) through any closed surface is always zero:

\[ \phi_B = \sum_{\text{all}} \mathbf{B} \cdot \Delta\mathbf{S} = 0 \]

Physical Meaning: Unlike electrostatics (where \(\sum \mathbf{E}\cdot\Delta\mathbf{S} = q/\varepsilon_0\)), there are no isolated magnetic charges (monopoles) acting as sources or sinks of \(\mathbf{B}\). The simplest magnetic entity is a dipole or a current loop.

हिन्दी

चूँकि चुम्बकीय क्षेत्र रेखाएँ सदैव बंद लूप बनाती हैं, इसलिए किसी भी बंद गाउसीय पृष्ठ \(S\) में प्रवेश करने वाली क्षेत्र रेखाओं की संख्या बाहर निकलने वाली क्षेत्र रेखाओं की संख्या के बराबर होती है। गणितीय रूप से, किसी भी बंद पृष्ठ से गुजरने वाला नेट चुम्बकीय फ्लक्स \(\phi_B\) सदैव शून्य होता है:

\[ \phi_B = \sum_{\text{सभी}} \mathbf{B} \cdot \Delta\mathbf{S} = 0 \]

भौतिक अर्थ: स्थिरवैद्युतिकी (\(\sum \mathbf{E}\cdot\Delta\mathbf{S} = q/\varepsilon_0\)) के विपरीत, \(\mathbf{B}\) के स्रोत या सिंक के रूप में कोई विलगित चुम्बकीय आवेश (एकध्रुव) नहीं होता। सबसे सरल चुम्बकीय इकाई एक द्विध्रुव या धारा लूप है।

4. Magnetisation, Magnetic Intensity, and Susceptibility / चुम्बकन, चुम्बकीय तीव्रता एवं चुम्बकीय प्रवृत्ति

English

Magnetisation (\(\mathbf{M}\)): Defined as the net magnetic dipole moment per unit volume of a material:

\[ \mathbf{M} = \frac{\mathbf{m}_{\text{net}}}{V} \qquad (\text{Unit: }\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}},\;\text{Dimensions: }[\mathrm{L^{-1}\,A}]) \]

When a material core is placed inside a long solenoid of \(n\) turns per unit length carrying current \(I\), the total magnetic field \(\mathbf{B}\) inside is the sum of the external field \(\mathbf{B}_0 = \mu_0 n I\) and the material contribution \(\mathbf{B}_m = \mu_0 \mathbf{M}\):

\[ \mathbf{B} = \mathbf{B}_0 + \mathbf{B}_m = \mu_0(\mathbf{H} + \mathbf{M}) \]

Here, \(\mathbf{H}\) is the magnetic intensity (unit \(\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}\)), representing the external magnetising influence (\(\mathbf{H} = \mathbf{B}/\mu_0 - \mathbf{M}\); for a solenoid, \(H = nI\)).

For linear isotropic materials, \(\mathbf{M} = \chi \mathbf{H}\), where \(\chi\) is the dimensionless magnetic susceptibility. Substituting this yields:

\[ \mathbf{B} = \mu_0(1 + \chi)\mathbf{H} = \mu_0 \mu_r \mathbf{H} = \mu \mathbf{H} \]

where \(\mu_r = 1 + \chi\) is the dimensionless relative magnetic permeability, and \(\mu = \mu_0 \mu_r\) is the magnetic permeability of the substance.

हिन्दी

चुम्बकन (\(\mathbf{M}\)): किसी पदार्थ के प्रति एकांक आयतन में नेट चुम्बकीय द्विध्रुव आघूर्ण को चुम्बकन कहते हैं:

\[ \mathbf{M} = \frac{\mathbf{m}_{\text{net}}}{V} \qquad (\text{मात्रक: }\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}},\;\text{विमा: }[\mathrm{L^{-1}\,A}]) \]

जब प्रति एकांक लंबाई \(n\) फेरों और \(I\) धारा वाली लंबी परिनालिका के भीतर कोई क्रोड रखा जाता है, तो कुल चुम्बकीय क्षेत्र \(\mathbf{B}\) बाह्य क्षेत्र \(\mathbf{B}_0 = \mu_0 n I\) तथा पदार्थ के योगदान \(\mathbf{B}_m = \mu_0 \mathbf{M}\) का योग होता है:

\[ \mathbf{B} = \mathbf{B}_0 + \mathbf{B}_m = \mu_0(\mathbf{H} + \mathbf{M}) \]

यहाँ \(\mathbf{H}\) चुम्बकीय तीव्रता (मात्रक \(\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}\)) है, जो बाह्य चुम्बकन कारक को दर्शाती है (\(\mathbf{H} = \mathbf{B}/\mu_0 - \mathbf{M}\); परिनालिका के लिए \(H = nI\))।

रैखिक पदार्थों के लिए \(\mathbf{M} = \chi \mathbf{H}\), जहाँ \(\chi\) विमाहीन चुम्बकीय प्रवृत्ति है। इससे प्राप्त होता है:

\[ \mathbf{B} = \mu_0(1 + \chi)\mathbf{H} = \mu_0 \mu_r \mathbf{H} = \mu \mathbf{H} \]

जहाँ \(\mu_r = 1 + \chi\) विमाहीन आपेक्षिक चुम्बकीय पारगम्यता तथा \(\mu = \mu_0 \mu_r\) पदार्थ की चुम्बकीय पारगम्यता है।

5. Classification of Magnetic Materials / चुम्बकीय पदार्थों का वर्गीकरण

English

Based on their magnetic susceptibility \(\chi\) and response to an external magnetic field, materials are classified into three main categories:

  • Diamagnetic Materials: Atoms have zero permanent dipole moment. An external field induces a tiny opposing dipole moment (Lenz's law at atomic scale), so \(\mathbf{M}\) is opposite to \(\mathbf{H}\). Field lines are slightly expelled, and specimens move from stronger to weaker magnetic fields. Diamagnetism is universal and temperature-independent. Examples: bismuth, copper, lead, silicon, water, NaCl, nitrogen (at STP).
  • Superconductors (Perfect Diamagnets): Metals cooled below a critical temperature exhibit zero electrical resistance and complete expulsion of magnetic field lines—the Meissner effect, for which \(\chi = -1\), \(\mu_r = 0\), and \(\mu = 0\).
  • Paramagnetic Materials: Individual atoms possess permanent dipole moments, randomly oriented due to thermal agitation. An external field weakly aligns them along \(\mathbf{H}\), slightly concentrating field lines inside. Specimens move from weaker to stronger fields. Susceptibility \(\chi\) decreases with rising temperature. Examples: aluminium, sodium, calcium, oxygen (at STP), copper chloride.
  • Ferromagnetic Materials: Atomic dipoles interact strongly (quantum exchange interaction) to align spontaneously in macroscopic regions called domains (~1 mm, containing ~\(10^{11}\) atoms). In an external field, favourably oriented domains grow and align, strongly concentrating field lines. Above a high temperature, domain alignment breaks down and the material becomes paramagnetic.
    • Soft ferromagnets (e.g., soft iron): lose magnetisation when the external field is removed; ideal for electromagnet cores.
    • Hard ferromagnets (e.g., alnico, lodestone): retain magnetisation after field removal; used for permanent magnets.

चुम्बकीय प्रवृत्ति \(\chi\) तथा बाह्य चुम्बकीय क्षेत्र के प्रति व्यवहार के आधार पर पदार्थों को तीन मुख्य वर्गों में बाँटा जाता है:

  • प्रतिचुम्बकीय (Diamagnetic) पदार्थ: परमाणुओं का स्थायी द्विध्रुव आघूर्ण शून्य होता है। बाह्य क्षेत्र में विपरीत दिशा में एक क्षीण आघूर्ण प्रेरित होता है (\(\mathbf{M}\), \(\mathbf{H}\) के विपरीत)। क्षेत्र रेखाएँ बाहर की ओर प्रतिकर्षित होती हैं और पदार्थ प्रबल से दुर्बल क्षेत्र की ओर गति करता है। यह गुण सार्वत्रिक और ताप से स्वतंत्र होता है। उदाहरण: बिस्मथ, ताँबा, सीसा, सिलिकॉन, जल, NaCl, नाइट्रोजन (STP पर)।
  • अतिचालक (पूर्ण प्रतिचुम्बक): अत्यंत निम्न ताप पर ठंडी की गई धातुएँ शून्य प्रतिरोध तथा चुम्बकीय क्षेत्र रेखाओं का पूर्ण निष्कासन दर्शाती हैं—जिसे माइस्नर प्रभाव (Meissner effect) कहते हैं। इसके लिए \(\chi = -1\), \(\mu_r = 0\), तथा \(\mu = 0\) होता है।
  • अनुचुम्बकीय (Paramagnetic) पदार्थ: परमाणुओं में स्थायी द्विध्रुव आघूर्ण होता है, जो ऊष्मीय विक्षोभ के कारण यादृच्छिक रूप से अभिविन्यासित रहता है। बाह्य क्षेत्र में ये दुर्बल रूप से \(\mathbf{H}\) के अनुदिश संरेखित होते हैं और क्षेत्र रेखाएँ भीतर सघन हो जाती हैं। पदार्थ दुर्बल से प्रबल क्षेत्र की ओर गति करता है। ताप बढ़ने पर \(\chi\) घटती है। उदाहरण: ऐलुमिनियम, सोडियम, कैल्शियम, ऑक्सीजन (STP पर), कॉपर क्लोराइड।
  • लौहचुम्बकीय (Ferromagnetic) पदार्थ: परमाण्विक द्विध्रुव परस्पर प्रबल अन्योन्यक्रिया द्वारा स्थूल आयतनों में स्वतः संरेखित हो जाते हैं, जिन्हें डोमेन (domains) (~1 mm, ~\(10^{11}\) परमाणु) कहते हैं। बाह्य क्षेत्र में अनुकूल डोमेन बढ़ते हैं और संरेखित होते हैं। उच्च ताप पर डोमेन संरचना नष्ट हो जाती है और पदार्थ अनुचुम्बकीय बन जाता है।
    • नर्म लौहचुम्बकीय (जैसे नर्म लोहा): बाह्य क्षेत्र हटाने पर चुम्बकन समाप्त हो जाता है; विद्युत-चुम्बक क्रोड के लिए उपयुक्त।
    • कठोर लौहचुम्बकीय (जैसे ऐल्निको, लोडस्टोन): क्षेत्र हटाने पर भी चुम्बकन बना रहता है; स्थायी चुम्बक बनाने में प्रयुक्त।

ट्यूटोरियल आरेख 3: प्रतिचुम्बकीय एवं अनुचुम्बकीय छड़ों में चुम्बकीय क्षेत्र रेखाओं का व्यवहार।

Example 1: Calculating Torque and Work Done in Rotating a Magnetic Dipole

Assume a short bar magnet has a magnetic dipole moment \(m = 0.80\,\mathrm{J\,T^{-1}}\) and is placed in a uniform magnetic field \(B = 0.40\,\mathrm{T}\). Calculate: (a) the magnitude of the torque when its axis makes an angle of \(30°\) with \(\mathbf{B}\), and (b) the external work required to rotate the magnet slowly from its stable equilibrium position (\(\theta_1 = 0°\)) to \(\theta_2 = 120°\).

Solution:

(a) Torque magnitude: \[ \tau = mB\sin\theta = (0.80\,\mathrm{J\,T^{-1}})(0.40\,\mathrm{T})\sin 30° = 0.32 \times 0.5 = 0.16\,\mathrm{N\,m}. \]

(b) External work done equals the change in magnetic potential energy: \[ W_{\text{ext}} = U_m(120°) - U_m(0°) = mB(\cos 0° - \cos 120°) \] Since \(\cos 0° = 1\) and \(\cos 120° = -0.5\): \[ W_{\text{ext}} = 0.80 \times 0.40 \times [1 - (-0.5)] = 0.32 \times 1.5 = 0.48\,\mathrm{J}. \]

Example 2: Determining Axial and Equatorial Magnetic Fields of a Short Bar Magnet

Assume a short bar magnet has a magnetic moment \(m = 0.60\,\mathrm{J\,T^{-1}}\). Find the magnitude and direction of the magnetic field at a distance \(r = 20\,\mathrm{cm}\) from its centre on (a) its axis, and (b) its equatorial line (normal bisector). Take \(\mu_0 / 4\pi = 10^{-7}\,\mathrm{T\,m\,A^{-1}}\).

Solution:

Convert distance to SI units: \(r = 20\,\mathrm{cm} = 0.20\,\mathrm{m}\), so \(r^3 = (0.20)^3 = 8 \times 10^{-3}\,\mathrm{m^3}\).

(a) On the axis: \[ B_A = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{2m}{r^3} = 10^{-7} \times \frac{2 \times 0.60}{8 \times 10^{-3}} = 1.5 \times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T} \] Direction: along the magnetic moment \(\mathbf{m}\) (from S to N).

(b) On the equatorial line: \[ B_E = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{m}{r^3} = \frac{B_A}{2} = 7.5 \times 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{T} \] Direction: opposite to the magnetic moment \(\mathbf{m}\) (from N to S).

Example 3: Finding Magnetic Intensity, Magnetisation, and Flux Density in a Solenoid Core

Assume a long solenoid has \(n = 1500\,\mathrm{turns\,m^{-1}}\) carrying a current \(I = 1.5\,\mathrm{A}\). Its core is filled with a ferromagnetic material of relative permeability \(\mu_r = 250\). Calculate (a) magnetic intensity \(H\), (b) susceptibility \(\chi\), (c) magnetisation \(M\), and (d) total magnetic field \(B\) inside the core.

Solution:

(a) Magnetic intensity depends only on free current: \[ H = nI = 1500 \times 1.5 = 2.25 \times 10^3\,\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}. \]

(b) Susceptibility: \[ \chi = \mu_r - 1 = 250 - 1 = 249. \]

(c) Magnetisation: \[ M = \chi H = 249 \times 2250 = 5.6025 \times 10^5\,\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}} \approx 5.60 \times 10^5\,\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}. \]

(d) Total magnetic field inside the core: \[ B = \mu_0 \mu_r H = (4\pi \times 10^{-7}) \times 250 \times 2250 = 0.225\pi\,\mathrm{T} \approx 0.707\,\mathrm{T}. \]

Practice Question 1 (Single-Correct MCQ)

Which of the following statements accurately describes magnetic field lines and Gauss's law for magnetism?

  • (A) Magnetic field lines originate on the north pole and terminate on the south pole without continuing inside the magnet.
  • (B) The net magnetic flux through any closed surface is zero because isolated magnetic monopoles do not exist.
  • (C) Two magnetic field lines can intersect in a region where the magnetic field is non-uniform.
  • (D) Magnetic field lines represent the exact path of a moving positively charged particle in a magnetic field.

Answer: (B)

Worked Solution: Magnetic field lines form continuous closed loops (continuing from S to N inside a magnet), so the number of lines entering any closed surface equals the number leaving it, giving \(\sum \mathbf{B}\cdot\Delta\mathbf{S} = 0\). Option (C) is false because field lines never intersect, and (D) is false because magnetic force \(q\mathbf{v}\times\mathbf{B}\) is perpendicular to \(\mathbf{B}\).

A magnetic needle of magnetic moment \(m = 0.50\,\mathrm{J\,T^{-1}}\) is placed in a uniform magnetic field \(B = 0.20\,\mathrm{T}\) in its most stable orientation. What is the external work required to rotate it slowly to its most unstable equilibrium orientation?

  • (A) \(0.05\,\mathrm{J}\)
  • (B) \(0.10\,\mathrm{J}\)
  • (C) \(0.20\,\mathrm{J}\)
  • (D) \(0.40\,\mathrm{J}\)

Answer: (C)

Worked Solution: Most stable equilibrium is at \(\theta_1 = 0°\) (\(U_1 = -mB\)), and most unstable equilibrium is at \(\theta_2 = 180°\) (\(U_2 = +mB\)). Thus: \[ W_{\text{ext}} = U_2 - U_1 = mB - (-mB) = 2mB = 2 \times 0.50 \times 0.20 = 0.20\,\mathrm{J}. \]

अभ्यास प्रश्न 3 (संख्यात्मक बहुविकल्पीय प्रश्न)

एक छोटे छड़ चुम्बक के केंद्र से \(r\) दूरी पर (जहाँ \(r\) चुम्बक की लंबाई से बहुत अधिक है), अक्षीय चुम्बकीय क्षेत्र का परिमाण \(6.4 \times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}\) है। उसी चुम्बक के केंद्र से \(2r\) दूरी पर निरक्षीय रेखा पर चुम्बकीय क्षेत्र का परिमाण क्या होगा?

  • (A) \(4.0 \times 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{T}\)
  • (B) \(8.0 \times 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{T}\)
  • (C) \(1.6 \times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}\)
  • (D) \(3.2 \times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}\)

उत्तर: (A)

हल: दिया है \(B_A(r) = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{2m}{r^3} = 6.4 \times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}\), अतः \(\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{m}{r^3} = 3.2 \times 10^{-5}\,\mathrm{T}\)। निरक्षीय रेखा पर \(2r\) दूरी पर: \[ B_E(2r) = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{m}{(2r)^3} = \frac{1}{8}\left(\frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{m}{r^3}\right) = \frac{3.2 \times 10^{-5}}{8} = 4.0 \times 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{T} \]

Practice Question 4 (Numerical MCQ)

A paramagnetic sample placed in a magnetising field of intensity \(H = 4.0 \times 10^3\,\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}\) develops a magnetisation \(M = 0.16\,\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}\). What is the relative magnetic permeability \(\mu_r\) of the sample?

  • (A) \(0.99996\)
  • (B) \(1.00004\)
  • (C) \(1.00040\)
  • (D) \(1.00400\)

Answer: (B)

Worked Solution: Magnetic susceptibility is \(\chi = \frac{M}{H} = \frac{0.16}{4.0 \times 10^3} = 4.0 \times 10^{-5}\). Therefore: \[ \mu_r = 1 + \chi = 1 + 0.00004 = 1.00004. \]

Practice Question 5 (Statement-Based MCQ)

Read the following two statements carefully:

Statement I: When placed in a non-uniform magnetic field, a diamagnetic rod tends to move from the stronger part of the field to the weaker part.

Statement II: For a superconductor exhibiting the Meissner effect, the magnetic susceptibility is \(\chi = -1\) and the relative permeability is \(\mu_r = 0\).

  • (A) Both Statement I and Statement II are correct.
  • (B) Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect.
  • (C) Statement I is correct but Statement II is incorrect.
  • (D) Statement I is incorrect but Statement II is correct.

Answer: (A)

Worked Solution: Diamagnetic materials are weakly repelled by a magnetic field and therefore move from stronger to weaker regions. Superconductors are perfect diamagnets that expel all magnetic field lines (\(\mathbf{B} = 0\) inside), which requires \(\chi = -1\) and \(\mu_r = 1 + \chi = 0\). Both statements are correct.

Practice Question 6 (Assertion–Reason MCQ)

Assertion (A): Soft iron is preferred over alnico for making the core of an electromagnet.

Reason (R): Soft iron is a soft ferromagnetic material whose magnetisation disappears readily when the external magnetising field is removed, whereas alnico retains its magnetisation.

  • (A) Both (A) and (R) are true and (R) is the correct explanation of (A).
  • (B) Both (A) and (R) are true but (R) is not the correct explanation of (A).
  • (C) (A) is true but (R) is false.
  • (D) (A) is false but (R) is true.

Answer: (A)

Worked Solution: An electromagnet must become strongly magnetised when current flows and demagnetised as soon as current is switched off. Soft iron provides high permeability and loses magnetisation readily when the external field is removed, whereas alnico is a hard ferromagnet suited for permanent magnets. Thus (R) is true and correctly explains (A).

Common Pitfalls & Revision Checklist / सामान्य त्रुटियाँ एवं पुनरावृत्ति जाँच-सूची

English
Common NEET UG Pitfalls to Avoid:
  • Zero of Potential Energy: \(U_m = -mB\cos\theta\) assumes \(U_m = 0\) at \(\theta = 90°\). Never use \(U_m = 0\) at \(\theta = 0°\) unless explicitly stated; always compute work done as \(W_{\text{ext}} = U_2 - U_1 = mB(\cos\theta_1 - \cos\theta_2)\).
  • \(\mathbf{B}\) vs. \(\mathbf{H}\): Magnetic intensity \(H = nI\) inside a long solenoid depends only on free current and turns per unit length, not on the core material, whereas \(B = \mu_0\mu_r H\) depends strongly on the core.
  • Sign of \(\chi\): Confusing diamagnetic (\(\chi < 0\), \(\mu_r < 1\)) with paramagnetic (\(\chi > 0\), \(\mu_r > 1\)). Remember: diamagnetism is present in all materials, even when masked by stronger para- or ferromagnetism.
  • Check units: \(B\) in \(\mathrm{T}\) (\(1\,\mathrm{T} = 10^4\,\mathrm{G}\)), \(m\) in \(\mathrm{J\,T^{-1}}\) or \(\mathrm{A\,m^2}\), \(M\) and \(H\) in \(\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}\), \(\chi\) and \(\mu_r\) are dimensionless.
  • Verify the \(2:1\) ratio between axial and equatorial fields at the same far distance \(r\).
हिन्दी
NEET UG में बचने योग्य सामान्य त्रुटियाँ:
  • स्थितिज ऊर्जा का शून्य: \(U_m = -mB\cos\theta\) में \(\theta = 90°\) पर \(U_m = 0\) माना गया है। कार्य सदैव \(W_{\text{ext}} = U_2 - U_1 = mB(\cos\theta_1 - \cos\theta_2)\) से निकालें।
  • \(\mathbf{B}\) बनाम \(\mathbf{H}\): लंबी परिनालिका में चुम्बकीय तीव्रता \(H = nI\) केवल मुक्त धारा और प्रति मीटर फेरों पर निर्भर करती है, क्रोड के पदार्थ पर नहीं, जबकि \(B = \mu_0\mu_r H\) क्रोड पर निर्भर करता है।
  • \(\chi\) का चिह्न: प्रतिचुम्बकीय (\(\chi < 0\), \(\mu_r < 1\)) और अनुचुम्बकीय (\(\chi > 0\), \(\mu_r > 1\)) में भ्रम न रखें।
  • मात्रकों की जाँच करें: \(B\) का मात्रक \(\mathrm{T}\) (\(1\,\mathrm{T} = 10^4\,\mathrm{G}\)), \(m\) का \(\mathrm{J\,T^{-1}}\) या \(\mathrm{A\,m^2}\), \(M\) और \(H\) का \(\mathrm{A\,m^{-1}}\), तथा \(\chi\) और \(\mu_r\) विमाहीन हैं।
  • समान दूरस्थ दूरी \(r\) पर अक्षीय और निरक्षीय क्षेत्र का अनुपात \(2:1\) याद रखें।

Concise Final Recap / संक्षिप्त अंतिम पुनरावलोकन

English

Magnetic monopoles do not exist, so magnetic field lines form closed loops and Gauss's law gives \(\sum \mathbf{B}\cdot\Delta\mathbf{S} = 0\). A short bar magnet acts like an equivalent solenoid with axial field \(B_A = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{2m}{r^3}\) and equatorial field \(B_E = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{m}{r^3}\). Inside matter, \(\mathbf{B} = \mu_0(\mathbf{H} + \mathbf{M}) = \mu_0(1 + \chi)\mathbf{H}\), classifying materials into diamagnetic (\(-1 \le \chi < 0\)), paramagnetic (\(0 < \chi < \varepsilon\)), and ferromagnetic (\(\chi \gg 1\)) substances.

हिन्दी

चुम्बकीय एकध्रुवों का अस्तित्व नहीं होता, इसलिए चुम्बकीय क्षेत्र रेखाएँ बंद लूप बनाती हैं और गाउस का नियम \(\sum \mathbf{B}\cdot\Delta\mathbf{S} = 0\) देता है। छोटा छड़ चुम्बक एक तुल्य परिनालिका की भाँति कार्य करता है जिसका अक्षीय क्षेत्र \(B_A = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{2m}{r^3}\) और निरक्षीय क्षेत्र \(B_E = \frac{\mu_0}{4\pi}\frac{m}{r^3}\) होता है। द्रव्य के भीतर \(\mathbf{B} = \mu_0(\mathbf{H} + \mathbf{M}) = \mu_0(1 + \chi)\mathbf{H}\) से पदार्थों को प्रतिचुम्बकीय (\(-1 \le \chi < 0\)), अनुचुम्बकीय (\(0 < \chi < \varepsilon\)), और लौहचुम्बकीय (\(\chi \gg 1\)) में वर्गीकृत किया जाता है।
