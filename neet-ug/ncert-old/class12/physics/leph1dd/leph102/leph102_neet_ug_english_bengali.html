1. Conservative Force, Potential Energy, and Electrostatic Potential

English

Electrostatic force between stationary charges obeys an inverse-square law and is therefore a conservative force. When an external agent moves a test charge \(q\) infinitesimally slowly (with zero acceleration, so \(\mathbf{F}_{\text{ext}} = -\mathbf{F}_{E}\)) from an initial point \(R\) to a final point \(P\), the work done by the external force is independent of the path chosen and depends only on the endpoints. This work is stored as the change in electrostatic potential energy \(\Delta U\):

\[ \Delta U = U_{P} - U_{R} = W_{RP} = \int_{R}^{P} \mathbf{F}_{\text{ext}} \cdot d\mathbf{r} = -\int_{R}^{P} \mathbf{F}_{E} \cdot d\mathbf{r} \]

Only differences in potential energy have physical meaning; we can add any arbitrary constant to \(U\) without changing physical results. By convention, potential energy is taken as zero when charges are infinitely far apart (\(U_{\infty} = 0\)).

Dividing the potential energy change by the test charge \(q\) defines the electrostatic potential difference between \(P\) and \(R\):

\[ V_{P} - V_{R} = \frac{U_{P} - U_{R}}{q} = \frac{W_{RP}}{q} \]

Taking \(V_{\infty} = 0\), the electrostatic potential \(V\) at any point is the work done by an external agent in bringing a unit positive test charge (without acceleration) from infinity to that point. Its SI unit is the volt (\(\mathrm{V}\)), where \(1\,\mathrm{V} = 1\,\mathrm{J\,C^{-1}}\).

স্থির আধানের মধ্যে ক্রিয়াশীল তড়িৎ বল বিপরীত-বর্গীয় সূত্র মেনে চলে এবং এটি একটি সংরক্ষী বল (conservative force)। কোনো বাহ্যিক সংস্থা যদি একটি পরীক্ষামূলক আধান \(q\)-কে ত্বরণহীনভাবে অসীম ধীর গতিতে (অর্থাৎ \(\mathbf{F}_{\text{ext}} = -\mathbf{F}_{E}\)) প্রারম্ভিক বিন্দু \(R\) থেকে অন্তিম বিন্দু \(P\)-তে নিয়ে যায়, তবে বাহ্যিক বল দ্বারা কৃতকার্য পথের ওপর নির্ভর করে না, কেবল প্রারম্ভিক ও অন্তিম অবস্থানের ওপর নির্ভর করে। এই কৃতকার্যই স্থিতিশক্তির পরিবর্তন \(\Delta U\) হিসেবে সঞ্চিত হয়:

\[ \Delta U = U_{P} - U_{R} = W_{RP} = \int_{R}^{P} \mathbf{F}_{\text{ext}} \cdot d\mathbf{r} = -\int_{R}^{P} \mathbf{F}_{E} \cdot d\mathbf{r} \]

স্থিতিশক্তির পরম মান নয়, কেবল তার পার্থক্যই ভৌতভাবে তাৎপর্যপূর্ণ। প্রচলিত প্রথা অনুযায়ী অসীম দূরত্বে স্থিতিশক্তি শূন্য ধরা হয় (\(U_{\infty} = 0\))।

স্থিতিশক্তির পরিবর্তনকে পরীক্ষামূলক আধান \(q\) দিয়ে ভাগ করলে \(P\) ও \(R\) বিন্দুর মধ্যে স্থিরতড়িৎ বিভব পার্থক্য পাওয়া যায়:

\[ V_{P} - V_{R} = \frac{U_{P} - U_{R}}{q} = \frac{W_{RP}}{q} \]

অসীমে বিভব শূন্য ধরলে (\(V_{\infty} = 0\)), কোনো বিন্দুতে স্থিরতড়িৎ বিভব \(V\) হলো একক ধনাত্মক আধানকে ত্বরণহীনভাবে অসীম থেকে ওই বিন্দুতে আনতে বাহ্যিক বল দ্বারা কৃতকার্য। এর SI একক ভোল্ট (\(\mathrm{V}\)), যেখানে \(1\,\mathrm{V} = 1\,\mathrm{J\,C^{-1}}\)।

2. Potential Due to a Point Charge, System of Charges, and Electric Dipole

English

Point Charge: Integrating the radial Coulomb force on a unit positive charge from \(\infty\) to distance \(r\) gives the potential due to a point charge \(Q\):

\[ V(r) = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}}\frac{Q}{r} \]

Here \(V > 0\) for \(Q > 0\) and \(V < 0\) for \(Q < 0\). Notice that \(V \propto 1/r\), whereas electric field magnitude \(E \propto 1/r^{2}\).

Superposition Principle: For discrete charges \(q_{1}, q_{2}, \ldots, q_{n}\) at distances \(r_{1P}, r_{2P}, \ldots, r_{nP}\) from point \(P\), the net potential is the scalar algebraic sum:

\[ V_{P} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}}\sum_{i=1}^{n}\frac{q_{i}}{r_{iP}} \]

For a uniformly charged spherical shell of radius \(R\) and total charge \(Q\), the potential is \(V = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}}\frac{Q}{r}\) outside (\(r \ge R\)), and remains constant at \(V = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}}\frac{Q}{R}\) everywhere inside (\(r < R\)) because the internal electric field is zero.

Electric Dipole: For a dipole of moment \(\mathbf{p}\) (magnitude \(p = 2qa\), directed from \(-q\) to \(+q\)), at a far point \((r \gg a)\) making angle \(\theta\) with \(\mathbf{p}\), the potential is:

\[ V(r, \theta) = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}}\frac{p\cos\theta}{r^{2}} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}}\frac{\mathbf{p}\cdot\hat{\mathbf{r}}}{r^{2}} \]
  • Axial points (\(\theta = 0\) or \(\pi\)): \(V = \pm \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}}\frac{p}{r^{2}}\).
  • Equatorial plane (\(\theta = \pi/2\)): \(V = 0\) everywhere (though \(\mathbf{E} \neq 0\)).
  • Unlike a point charge (\(V \propto 1/r\)), dipole potential falls off faster as \(1/r^{2}\) and depends on \(\theta\).

3. Equipotential Surfaces and Relation Between Field and Potential

English

An equipotential surface is a locus of points all having the exact same electrostatic potential. Moving a test charge anywhere on an equipotential surface requires zero work (\(W = q\Delta V = 0\)).

  • Orthogonality: The electric field \(\mathbf{E}\) is always perpendicular to the equipotential surface at every point; otherwise a tangential component of \(\mathbf{E}\) would do non-zero work along the surface.
  • Common Shapes: Concentric spheres around an isolated point charge; parallel planes perpendicular to a uniform electric field. Two equipotential surfaces can never intersect, as that would imply two different potentials at the same point.
  • Field-Potential Relation: Electric field points in the direction of steepest decrease of potential, with magnitude equal to the rate of change of potential per unit normal displacement:
\[ E = -\frac{dV}{dr} \quad \text{(in general, } E_{x} = -\frac{\partial V}{\partial x},\; E_{y} = -\frac{\partial V}{\partial y},\; E_{z} = -\frac{\partial V}{\partial z} \text{)} \]

4. Potential Energy of Charge Systems and Dipoles in External Fields

English

System of Charges (No External Field): The electrostatic potential energy \(U\) of a system is the total work required to assemble the charges from infinity. For two charges \(q_{1}, q_{2}\) separated by \(r_{12}\):

\[ U = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}}\frac{q_{1}q_{2}}{r_{12}} \]

For \(n\) charges, sum over all unique pairs (\(i < j\)). For three charges, \(U = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}}\left(\frac{q_{1}q_{2}}{r_{12}} + \frac{q_{1}q_{3}}{r_{13}} + \frac{q_{2}q_{3}}{r_{23}}\right)\). Like charges have \(U > 0\) (repulsive), whereas unlike charges have \(U < 0\) (attractive).

In an External Field: A single charge \(q\) at position \(\mathbf{r}\) in external potential \(V(\mathbf{r})\) has energy \(U = qV(\mathbf{r})\). For two charges \(q_{1}, q_{2}\) in an external field, the total potential energy includes both external interactions and mutual interaction:

\[ U = q_{1}V(\mathbf{r}_{1}) + q_{2}V(\mathbf{r}_{2}) + \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}}\frac{q_{1}q_{2}}{r_{12}} \]

Unit of Energy (Electron Volt): \(1\,\mathrm{eV} = 1.6 \times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{J}\) is the energy gained by an electron accelerated through a potential difference of \(1\,\mathrm{V}\).

Dipole in a Uniform External Field \(\mathbf{E}\): Taking zero potential energy at \(\theta = \pi/2\), the potential energy of a dipole \(\mathbf{p}\) inclined at angle \(\theta\) to \(\mathbf{E}\) is:

\[ U(\theta) = -\mathbf{p}\cdot\mathbf{E} = -pE\cos\theta \quad \text{and} \quad W_{\theta_{1}\to\theta_{2}} = pE(\cos\theta_{1} - \cos\theta_{2}) \]

Stable equilibrium occurs at \(\theta = 0°\) (\(U_{\min} = -pE\)), and unstable equilibrium occurs at \(\theta = 180°\) (\(U_{\max} = +pE\)).

\[ U = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}}\frac{q_{1}q_{2}}{r_{12}} \]

তিনটি আধানের জন্য সকল অনন্য জোড়ার যোগফল নেওয়া হয়: \(U = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}}\left(\frac{q_{1}q_{2}}{r_{12}} + \frac{q_{1}q_{3}}{r_{13}} + \frac{q_{2}q_{3}}{r_{23}}\right)\)। সমধর্মী আধানের ক্ষেত্রে \(U > 0\) এবং বিপরীতধর্মী আধানের ক্ষেত্রে \(U < 0\) হয়।

বাহ্যিক তড়িৎ ক্ষেত্রে স্থিতিশক্তি: বাহ্যিক বিভব \(V(\mathbf{r})\)-এ \(q\) আধানের স্থিতিশক্তি \(qV(\mathbf{r})\)। দুটি আধানের ক্ষেত্রে বাহ্যিক ক্ষেত্রের সাথে ক্রিয়া এবং পারস্পরিক ক্রিয়া উভয়ই যুক্ত হয়:

\[ U = q_{1}V(\mathbf{r}_{1}) + q_{2}V(\mathbf{r}_{2}) + \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}}\frac{q_{1}q_{2}}{r_{12}} \]

শক্তির একক (ইলেকট্রন ভোল্ট): \(1\,\mathrm{eV} = 1.6 \times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{J}\) হলো \(1\,\mathrm{V}\) বিভব পার্থক্যের মধ্য দিয়ে ত্বরান্বিত একটি ইলেকট্রনের অর্জিত শক্তি।

সুষম বাহ্যিক ক্ষেত্র \(\mathbf{E}\)-তে দ্বিমেরু: \(\theta = \pi/2\) অবস্থানে স্থিতিশক্তি শূন্য ধরলে, \(\theta\) কোণে আনত দ্বিমেরুর স্থিতিশক্তি ও ঘূর্ণনে কৃতকার্য:

\[ U(\theta) = -\mathbf{p}\cdot\mathbf{E} = -pE\cos\theta \quad \text{এবং} \quad W_{\theta_{1}\to\theta_{2}} = pE(\cos\theta_{1} - \cos\theta_{2}) \]

\(\theta = 0°\)-তে সুস্থিত সাম্যাবস্থা (\(U_{\min} = -pE\)) এবং \(\theta = 180°\)-তে অস্থিত সাম্যাবস্থা (\(U_{\max} = +pE\)) থাকে।

5. Electrostatics of Conductors, Shielding, and Dielectrics

English

Conductors in Electrostatic Equilibrium

  • Zero Internal Field & Excess Charge: Free electrons redistribute until \(\mathbf{E} = 0\) everywhere inside the conductor material. By Gauss's law, no excess charge can reside in the interior; all excess charge stays on the outer surface.
  • Equipotential Volume: Since \(\mathbf{E} = 0\) inside and tangential field is zero on the surface, the electrostatic potential \(V\) is constant throughout the volume and on the surface.
  • Surface Field: Just outside a charged conductor with surface charge density \(\sigma\), the field is strictly normal to the surface: \(\mathbf{E} = \frac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_{0}}\hat{\mathbf{n}}\).
  • Electrostatic Shielding: Inside any charge-free cavity of a conductor, \(\mathbf{E} = 0\) regardless of external fields or charges on the outer surface. Sensitive electronics are shielded using hollow metallic enclosures.

Dielectrics and Polarisation

Dielectrics are insulators without free charge carriers. In an external field \(\mathbf{E}_{0}\), non-polar molecules (e.g., \(\mathrm{H}_{2}, \mathrm{O}_{2}, \mathrm{CO}_{2}\)) develop induced dipole moments, while polar molecules (e.g., \(\mathrm{HCl}, \mathrm{H}_{2}\mathrm{O}\)) align their permanent dipoles with the field. The dipole moment per unit volume is the polarisation \(\mathbf{P} = \varepsilon_{0}\chi_{e}\mathbf{E}\). Uniform polarisation produces bound surface charge densities \(\pm\sigma_{p}\) that create an opposing internal field, reducing (but not cancelling) the net field inside the dielectric to \(E = E_{0}/K\), where \(K > 1\) is the dielectric constant.

6. Capacitance, Parallel-Plate Capacitors, and Dielectric Slabs

English

A capacitor consists of two conductors carrying charges \(+Q\) and \(-Q\) separated by an insulator, with potential difference \(V\). Its capacitance \(C = Q/V\) depends purely on geometry and the intervening medium, not on \(Q\) or \(V\). The SI unit is the farad (\(1\,\mathrm{F} = 1\,\mathrm{C\,V^{-1}}\)); practical values are in \(\mathrm{µF}\), \(\mathrm{nF}\), or \(\mathrm{pF}\). The maximum field an insulator withstands without breakdown is its dielectric strength (about \(3 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{V\,m^{-1}}\) for air).

For a parallel-plate capacitor of plate area \(A\) and separation \(d\) (\(d^{2} \ll A\)), the field between plates is uniform, \(E = \sigma/\varepsilon_{0} = Q/(\varepsilon_{0}A)\), and zero outside. Hence:

\[ C_{0} = \frac{\varepsilon_{0}A}{d} \quad \text{(vacuum/air)} \qquad \text{and} \qquad C = \frac{K\varepsilon_{0}A}{d} = KC_{0} \quad \text{(fully filled with dielectric } K\text{)} \]

Partially Filled Parallel-Plate Capacitor: If a dielectric slab of thickness \(t < d\) and dielectric constant \(K\) is inserted parallel to the plates, the potential difference is \(V = E_{0}(d - t) + \frac{E_{0}}{K}t\), yielding:

\[ C = \frac{\varepsilon_{0}A}{d - t + \frac{t}{K}} \]
NEET UG Key Distinction — Battery Connected vs. Disconnected (on inserting dielectric \(K\)):
  • Battery remains connected: \(V\) stays constant; \(C \to KC_{0}\), \(Q \to KQ_{0}\), \(E\) stays constant, and stored energy increases (\(U \to KU_{0}\)).
  • Battery is disconnected first: \(Q\) stays constant; \(C \to KC_{0}\), \(V \to V_{0}/K\), \(E \to E_{0}/K\), and stored energy decreases (\(U \to U_{0}/K\)).

কোনো অন্তরক দ্বারা পৃথকীকৃত \(+Q\) ও \(-Q\) আধানযুক্ত দুটি পরিবাহীর সংস্থাকে ধারক (capacitor) বলে। এর ধারকত্ব \(C = Q/V\), যা কেবল পরিবাহীর জ্যামিতিক আকার ও মধ্যবর্তী মাধ্যমের ওপর নির্ভর করে, \(Q\) বা \(V\)-এর ওপর নয়। এর SI একক ফ্যারাড (\(1\,\mathrm{F} = 1\,\mathrm{C\,V^{-1}}\))। অন্তরক মাধ্যম ভেঙে না গিয়ে যে সর্বোচ্চ তড়িৎ ক্ষেত্র সহ্য করতে পারে, তাকে পরাবৈদ্যুতিক শক্তি (dielectric strength) বলে (বায়ুর জন্য প্রায় \(3 \times 10^{6}\,\mathrm{V\,m^{-1}}\))।

আংশিক পরাবৈদ্যুতিক ফলক: \(t < d\) বেধের \(K\) পরাবৈদ্যুতিক ধ্রুবকবিশিষ্ট একটি ফলক পাতদুটির মাঝে রাখলে বিভব পার্থক্য হয় \(V = E_{0}(d - t) + \frac{E_{0}}{K}t\), ফলে ধারকত্ব:

\[ C = \frac{\varepsilon_{0}A}{d - t + \frac{t}{K}} \]

  • ব্যাটারি যুক্ত থাকলে: \(V\) ধ্রুবক থাকে; \(C \to KC_{0}\), \(Q \to KQ_{0}\), \(E\) অপরিবর্তিত থাকে এবং সঞ্চিত শক্তি বাড়ে (\(U \to KU_{0}\))।

7. Combinations of Capacitors and Electrostatic Energy

English

Series Combination: All capacitors carry the same charge \(Q\), while total potential \(V = V_{1} + V_{2} + \dots + V_{n}\):

\[ \frac{1}{C_{\text{eq}}} = \frac{1}{C_{1}} + \frac{1}{C_{2}} + \dots + \frac{1}{C_{n}} \]

Parallel Combination: All capacitors share the same potential difference \(V\), while total charge \(Q = Q_{1} + Q_{2} + \dots + Q_{n}\):

\[ C_{\text{eq}} = C_{1} + C_{2} + \dots + C_{n} \]

Energy Stored and Energy Density: Work done in charging a capacitor is stored in the electric field between its plates:

\[ U = \frac{Q^{2}}{2C} = \frac{1}{2}CV^{2} = \frac{1}{2}QV \qquad \text{and} \qquad u = \frac{1}{2}\varepsilon_{0}E^{2} \;\text{ (energy per unit volume in vacuum)} \]

Charge Sharing & Energy Loss: When two capacitors \((C_{1}, V_{1})\) and \((C_{2}, V_{2})\) are connected in parallel (positive to positive), they reach a common potential \(V_{c} = \frac{C_{1}V_{1} + C_{2}V_{2}}{C_{1} + C_{2}}\), and the electrostatic energy lost as heat/radiation is:

\[ \Delta U_{\text{loss}} = \frac{1}{2}\frac{C_{1}C_{2}}{C_{1} + C_{2}}(V_{1} - V_{2})^{2} \]

Worked Examples

Zero-Potential Points on the Line Joining Two Charges

English

Problem: Assume two point charges \(q_{1} = +6 \times 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{C}\) and \(q_{2} = -4 \times 10^{-8}\,\mathrm{C}\) are held \(20\,\mathrm{cm}\) apart in vacuum. Find the distances from \(q_{1}\) along the line joining them where the net electrostatic potential is zero (taking \(V = 0\) at infinity).

Solution: Let \(q_{1}\) be at \(x = 0\) and \(q_{2}\) at \(x = 20\,\mathrm{cm}\). Since \(|q_{1}| > |q_{2}|\), any zero-potential point must lie closer to \(q_{2}\), either between the charges (\(0 < x < 20\,\mathrm{cm}\)) or beyond \(q_{2}\) (\(x > 20\,\mathrm{cm}\)).

Case 1 (Between the charges):

\[ \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_{0}}\left(\frac{6 \times 10^{-8}}{x} - \frac{4 \times 10^{-8}}{20 - x}\right) = 0 \implies \frac{6}{x} = \frac{4}{20 - x} \implies 120 - 6x = 4x \implies x = 12\,\mathrm{cm} \]

Case 2 (Beyond \(q_{2}\)):

\[ \frac{6}{x} - \frac{4}{x - 20} = 0 \implies 6x - 120 = 4x \implies x = 60\,\mathrm{cm} \]

Result: The potential is zero at \(12\,\mathrm{cm}\) and \(60\,\mathrm{cm}\) from \(q_{1}\) toward the side of \(q_{2}\).

English

Three capacitors of capacitances \(6\,\mathrm{µF}\), \(6\,\mathrm{µF}\), and \(3\,\mathrm{µF}\) are connected such that the two \(6\,\mathrm{µF}\) capacitors are in series, and this series branch is in parallel with the \(3\,\mathrm{µF}\) capacitor. If a \(40\,\mathrm{V}\) supply is applied across the combination, what is the total charge drawn from the supply?

  • (A) \(120\,\mathrm{µC}\)
  • (B) \(240\,\mathrm{µC}\)
  • (C) \(480\,\mathrm{µC}\)
  • (D) \(600\,\mathrm{µC}\)

Answer: (B)

Solution: Series combination of two \(6\,\mathrm{µF}\) capacitors gives \(C_{s} = \frac{6 \times 6}{6 + 6} = 3\,\mathrm{µF}\). In parallel with the \(3\,\mathrm{µF}\) capacitor, \(C_{\text{eq}} = 3 + 3 = 6\,\mathrm{µF}\). Total charge \(Q = C_{\text{eq}}V = 6\,\mathrm{µF} \times 40\,\mathrm{V} = 240\,\mathrm{µC}\).

  • (A) \(120\,\mathrm{µC}\)
  • (B) \(240\,\mathrm{µC}\)
  • (C) \(480\,\mathrm{µC}\)
  • (D) \(600\,\mathrm{µC}\)

উত্তর: (B)

সমাধান: দুটি \(6\,\mathrm{µF}\) ধারকের শ্রেণি সমবায় \(C_{s} = \frac{6 \times 6}{6 + 6} = 3\,\mathrm{µF}\)। এটি \(3\,\mathrm{µF}\)-এর সাথে সমান্তরালে থাকায় তুল্য ধারকত্ব \(C_{\text{eq}} = 3 + 3 = 6\,\mathrm{µF}\)। মোট আধান \(Q = C_{\text{eq}}V = 6\,\mathrm{µF} \times 40\,\mathrm{V} = 240\,\mathrm{µC}\)।

Practice Question 7 (Statement-Based MCQ)

English

Consider the following two statements regarding a parallel-plate capacitor and conductors:

Statement I: Electrostatic shielding protects a charge-free cavity inside a conductor from external electric fields, and also shields the exterior from any charge placed inside the cavity.

Statement II: The electric energy density in a region with electric field \(E\) in vacuum is \(\frac{1}{2}\varepsilon_{0}E^{2}\).

  • (A) Both Statement I and Statement II are correct.
  • (B) Both Statement I and Statement II are incorrect.
  • (C) Statement I is correct, but Statement II is incorrect.
  • (D) Statement I is incorrect, but Statement II is correct.

Answer: (D)

Solution: Electrostatic shielding is one-way: a charge-free cavity is shielded from external fields, but placing a charge inside the cavity induces charges on the inner and outer surfaces, producing a field outside. Thus Statement I is incorrect, while Statement II is correct.

Practice Question 8 (Single-Correct MCQ)

English

An electric dipole of moment \(p\) is placed in a uniform electric field \(E\). Which pair of angles \(\theta\) between \(\mathbf{p}\) and \(\mathbf{E}\) corresponds to stable equilibrium and maximum potential energy, respectively?

  • (A) \(0°, \; 90°\)
  • (B) \(0°, \; 180°\)
  • (C) \(180°, \; 0°\)
  • (D) \(90°, \; 180°\)

Answer: (B)

Solution: Potential energy is \(U(\theta) = -pE\cos\theta\). At \(\theta = 0°\), \(U = -pE\) (minimum, hence stable equilibrium). At \(\theta = 180°\), \(U = +pE\) (maximum, hence unstable equilibrium).

  • (B) \(0°, \; 180°\)
  • (C) \(180°, \; 0°\)
  • (D) \(90°, \; 180°\)

উত্তর: (B)

সমাধান: স্থিতিশক্তি \(U(\theta) = -pE\cos\theta\)। \(\theta = 0°\)-তে \(U = -pE\) (সর্বনিম্ন, অর্থাৎ সুস্থিত সাম্যাবস্থা) এবং \(\theta = 180°\)-তে \(U = +pE\) (সর্বোচ্চ, অর্থাৎ অস্থিত সাম্যাবস্থা)।

Common Pitfalls and Revision Checklist

English
  • Sign Errors in Scalar Potential: Always substitute the algebraic sign (\(+\) or \(-\)) of charges when computing potential \(V\) and potential energy \(U\).
  • Zero Potential vs. Zero Field: On the equatorial plane of a dipole, \(V = 0\) everywhere, but \(\mathbf{E} \neq 0\). Inside a charged spherical shell, \(\mathbf{E} = 0\), but \(V \neq 0\) (it equals the surface potential).
  • Battery Condition Trap: Before applying \(U = \frac{1}{2}CV^{2}\) or \(U = \frac{Q^{2}}{2C}\) to dielectric or plate-separation changes, always check whether \(V\) is fixed (battery connected) or \(Q\) is fixed (battery disconnected).
  • Series vs. Parallel Memory Check: Capacitor series/parallel formulas are the inverse of resistor formulas (\(C_{\text{parallel}} = C_{1} + C_{2}\)).
বাংলা (Bengali)
  • স্কেলার বিভবে চিহ্নের ভুল: বিভব \(V\) এবং স্থিতিশক্তি \(U\) নির্ণয়ের সময় সর্বদা আধানের চিহ্ন (\(+\) বা \(-\)) বসাতে হবে।
  • শূন্য বিভব বনাম শূন্য ক্ষেত্র: দ্বিমেরুর নিরক্ষীয় তলে সর্বত্র \(V = 0\) হলেও \(\mathbf{E} \neq 0\)। আবার আহিত গোলকীয় খোলকের ভেতরে \(\mathbf{E} = 0\) হলেও \(V \neq 0\) (পৃষ্ঠের বিভবের সমান)।
  • ব্যাটারির শর্ত: পরাবৈদ্যুতিক প্রবেশ বা পাতের ব্যবধান পরিবর্তনের অঙ্কে \(V\) ধ্রুবক (ব্যাটারি যুক্ত) নাকি \(Q\) ধ্রুবক (ব্যাটারি বিচ্ছিন্ন), তা প্রথমেই যাচাই করে নেবে।
  • শ্রেণি ও সমান্তরাল সমবায়ের সতর্কতা: ধারকের তুল্য ধারকত্বের সূত্র রোধের সূত্রের বিপরীত (\(C_{\text{parallel}} = C_{1} + C_{2}\))।

Concise Final Recap

English

Electrostatic potential \(V\) and potential energy \(U\) transform vector force problems into scalar algebra because the Coulomb force is conservative. Electric field always points normal to equipotential surfaces in the direction of decreasing potential (\(E = -dV/dr\)). Conductors maintain constant potential throughout their volume and shield charge-free cavities from external fields. Capacitors store energy in the electric field (\(u = \frac{1}{2}\varepsilon_{0}E^{2}\)), and inserting a dielectric of constant \(K\) increases capacitance by a factor \(K\) while reducing internal field via bound surface polarisation charges.

বাংলা (Bengali)

কুলম্ব বল সংরক্ষী হওয়ায় স্থিরতড়িৎ বিভব \(V\) এবং স্থিতিশক্তি \(U\) ভেক্টর সমস্যাকে সহজ স্কেলার বীজগণিতে রূপান্তরিত করে। তড়িৎ ক্ষেত্র সর্বদা সমবিভব তলের সাথে লম্বভাবে বিভব হ্রাসের দিকে ক্রিয়া করে (\(E = -dV/dr\))। পরিবাহীর সমগ্র আয়তনে বিভব ধ্রুবক থাকে এবং আধানমুক্ত গহ্বর বাইরের ক্ষেত্র থেকে সুরক্ষিত থাকে। ধারক তার মধ্যবর্তী তড়িৎ ক্ষেত্রে শক্তি সঞ্চয় করে (\(u = \frac{1}{2}\varepsilon_{0}E^{2}\)), এবং \(K\) পরাবৈদ্যুতিক মাধ্যম প্রবেশ করালে মেরুকরণের ফলে ভেতরের ক্ষেত্র কমে যায় ও ধারকত্ব \(K\) গুণ বৃদ্ধি পায়।
