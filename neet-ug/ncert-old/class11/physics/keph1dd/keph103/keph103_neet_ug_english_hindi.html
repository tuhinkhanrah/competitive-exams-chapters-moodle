1. Learning Objectives & Chapter Overview | अधिगम उद्देश्य एवं अध्याय सारांश

English

Motion along a straight line requires only positive and negative signs to indicate direction. However, real-world motion—such as a ball curving through the air, a boat crossing a flowing river, or a satellite orbiting Earth—occurs in two or three dimensions. To analyze motion in a plane, we first master vectors and then apply them to kinematics, projectiles, and circular paths.

By the end of this tutorial, you will be able to:

  • Distinguish between scalar and vector quantities and perform graphical and analytical vector operations.
  • Resolve any two-dimensional or three-dimensional vector into mutually perpendicular components using unit vectors \(\hat{i}, \hat{j}, \hat{k}\).
  • Analyze motion in a plane with constant acceleration by treating \(x\)- and \(y\)-motions independently.
  • Derive and apply equations for projectile trajectory, time of flight, maximum height, and horizontal range.
  • Calculate angular speed, linear speed, and centripetal acceleration in uniform circular motion.

2. Scalars, Vectors & Vector Algebra | अदिश, सदिश एवं सदिश बीजगणित

English

Scalars vs. Vectors

  • Scalar Quantity: Has only magnitude (a numerical value with a proper unit) and no direction. Examples: mass, distance, speed, time, temperature, density, work, and electric current. Scalars combine via ordinary algebra (addition/subtraction requires identical units).
  • Vector Quantity: Has both magnitude and direction, and must obey the triangle law or parallelogram law of vector addition. Examples: displacement, velocity, acceleration, force, and linear momentum. A vector \(\mathbf{A}\) has magnitude \(|\mathbf{A}| = A\).

Equality & Free Vectors: Two vectors \(\mathbf{A}\) and \(\mathbf{B}\) are equal (\(\mathbf{A} = \mathbf{B}\)) if and only if they have the same magnitude and the same direction. Displacing a free vector parallel to itself leaves it unchanged. A null (zero) vector \(\mathbf{0}\) has zero magnitude and an undefined direction (for example, the displacement of an object returning to its starting point).

Multiplication by a Real Number: Multiplying vector \(\mathbf{A}\) by a positive scalar \(\lambda > 0\) scales its magnitude to \(\lambda A\) in the same direction. Multiplying by a negative scalar \(-\lambda\) (\(\lambda > 0\)) scales its magnitude to \(\lambda A\) and reverses its direction by \(180^\circ\).

Graphical Addition & Subtraction

  • Triangle (Head-to-Tail) Law: Place the tail of \(\mathbf{B}\) at the head of \(\mathbf{A}\). The resultant \(\mathbf{R} = \mathbf{A} + \mathbf{B}\) runs from the tail of \(\mathbf{A}\) to the head of \(\mathbf{B}\).
  • Parallelogram Law: Place the tails of \(\mathbf{A}\) and \(\mathbf{B}\) at a common origin \(O\). Complete the parallelogram; the diagonal starting from \(O\) represents \(\mathbf{R} = \mathbf{A} + \mathbf{B}\).
  • Properties: Vector addition is commutative (\(\mathbf{A} + \mathbf{B} = \mathbf{B} + \mathbf{A}\)) and associative (\((\mathbf{A} + \mathbf{B}) + \mathbf{C} = \mathbf{A} + (\mathbf{B} + \mathbf{C})\)).
  • Subtraction: Subtracting \(\mathbf{B}\) from \(\mathbf{A}\) means adding \(-\mathbf{B}\) to \(\mathbf{A}\): \(\mathbf{A} - \mathbf{B} = \mathbf{A} + (-\mathbf{B})\).

  • गुणधर्म: सदिश योग क्रमविनिमेय (\(\mathbf{A} + \mathbf{B} = \mathbf{B} + \mathbf{A}\)) और साहचर्य (\((\mathbf{A} + \mathbf{B}) + \mathbf{C} = \mathbf{A} + (\mathbf{B} + \mathbf{C})\)) होता है।

Resolution of Vectors & Analytical Addition | सदिशों का वियोजन एवं विश्लेषणात्मक योग

English

A unit vector has magnitude \(1\), is dimensionless, and specifies direction only. Along the rectangular \(x\)-, \(y\)-, and \(z\)-axes, unit vectors are denoted by \(\hat{i}\), \(\hat{j}\), and \(\hat{k}\) respectively (\(|\hat{i}| = |\hat{j}| = |\hat{k}| = 1\)). Any vector \(\mathbf{A}\) in the \(x\)-\(y\) plane making an angle \(\theta\) with the \(+x\)-axis can be resolved into orthogonal components:

\[\mathbf{A} = A_x\hat{i} + A_y\hat{j}, \quad A_x = A\cos\theta, \quad A_y = A\sin\theta\] \[A = \sqrt{A_x^2 + A_y^2}, \quad \tan\theta = \frac{A_y}{A_x}\]

In three dimensions, \(\mathbf{A} = A_x\hat{i} + A_y\hat{j} + A_z\hat{k}\) with \(A = \sqrt{A_x^2 + A_y^2 + A_z^2}\).

Analytical Addition: To add \(\mathbf{A} = A_x\hat{i} + A_y\hat{j}\) and \(\mathbf{B} = B_x\hat{i} + B_y\hat{j}\), add corresponding components: \(R_x = A_x + B_x\) and \(R_y = A_y + B_y\). When two vectors of magnitudes \(A\) and \(B\) have an angle \(\theta\) between them, the resultant \(\mathbf{R} = \mathbf{A} + \mathbf{B}\) has magnitude (Law of Cosines) and direction \(\alpha\) with \(\mathbf{A}\) given by:

\[R = \sqrt{A^2 + B^2 + 2AB\cos\theta}, \qquad \tan\alpha = \frac{B\sin\theta}{A + B\cos\theta}\] \[\text{Law of Sines: } \frac{R}{\sin\theta} = \frac{A}{\sin\beta} = \frac{B}{\sin\alpha}\]

Here \(\beta\) is the angle between \(\mathbf{R}\) and \(\mathbf{B}\). Notice that \(|A - B| \le |\mathbf{A} + \mathbf{B}| \le A + B\).

हिन्दी

एकांक सदिश (Unit Vector) का परिमाण \(1\) होता है, यह विमाहीन तथा मात्रकहीन होता है और केवल दिशा बताता है। आयताकार \(x\)-, \(y\)- और \(z\)-अक्षों के अनुदिश एकांक सदिश क्रमशः \(\hat{i}\), \(\hat{j}\) और \(\hat{k}\) हैं (\(|\hat{i}| = |\hat{j}| = |\hat{k}| = 1\))। \(x\)-\(y\) समतल में \(+x\)-अक्ष से \(\theta\) कोण बनाने वाले किसी सदिश \(\mathbf{A}\) को दो लंबवत घटकों में वियोजित किया जा सकता है:

\[\mathbf{A} = A_x\hat{i} + A_y\hat{j}, \quad A_x = A\cos\theta, \quad A_y = A\sin\theta\] \[A = \sqrt{A_x^2 + A_y^2}, \quad \tan\theta = \frac{A_y}{A_x}\]

त्रिविमीय आकाश में, \(\mathbf{A} = A_x\hat{i} + A_y\hat{j} + A_z\hat{k}\) तथा \(A = \sqrt{A_x^2 + A_y^2 + A_z^2}\) होता है।

विश्लेषणात्मक योग: \(\mathbf{A} = A_x\hat{i} + A_y\hat{j}\) और \(\mathbf{B} = B_x\hat{i} + B_y\hat{j}\) को जोड़ने के लिए संगत घटकों को जोड़ते हैं: \(R_x = A_x + B_x\) तथा \(R_y = A_y + B_y\)। यदि \(A\) और \(B\) परिमाण वाले दो सदिशों के बीच कोण \(\theta\) हो, तो परिणामी \(\mathbf{R} = \mathbf{A} + \mathbf{B}\) का परिमाण (कोज्या नियम) और \(\mathbf{A}\) के साथ बना कोण \(\alpha\) निम्न होते हैं:

\[R = \sqrt{A^2 + B^2 + 2AB\cos\theta}, \qquad \tan\alpha = \frac{B\sin\theta}{A + B\cos\theta}\] \[\frac{R}{\sin\theta} = \frac{A}{\sin\beta} = \frac{B}{\sin\alpha}\]

यहाँ \(\beta\), सदिश \(\mathbf{R}\) और \(\mathbf{B}\) के बीच का कोण है। ध्यान दें कि \(|A - B| \le |\mathbf{A} + \mathbf{B}| \le A + B\) सदैव सत्य होता है।

3. Kinematics in Two Dimensions | द्विविमीय शुद्धगतिकी

English

In the \(x\)-\(y\) plane, a particle's position vector is \(\mathbf{r} = x\hat{i} + y\hat{j}\). If it moves from \(\mathbf{r}\) at time \(t\) to \(\mathbf{r}'\) at time \(t'\), its displacement is \(\Delta\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{r}' - \mathbf{r} = \Delta x\hat{i} + \Delta y\hat{j}\), which depends only on the initial and final positions, not on the path taken. Thus, \(|\Delta\mathbf{r}| \le \text{path length}\).

  • Average Velocity: \(\overline{\mathbf{v}} = \frac{\Delta\mathbf{r}}{\Delta t}\), directed along \(\Delta\mathbf{r}\). Note that \(\text{average speed} \ge |\overline{\mathbf{v}}|\).
  • Instantaneous Velocity: \(\mathbf{v} = \lim_{\Delta t \to 0} \frac{\Delta\mathbf{r}}{\Delta t} = \frac{d\mathbf{r}}{dt} = v_x\hat{i} + v_y\hat{j}\), where \(v_x = \frac{dx}{dt}\) and \(v_y = \frac{dy}{dt}\). The instantaneous velocity vector is always tangent to the path at that point.
  • Average & Instantaneous Acceleration: \(\overline{\mathbf{a}} = \frac{\Delta\mathbf{v}}{\Delta t}\) and \(\mathbf{a} = \frac{d\mathbf{v}}{dt} = a_x\hat{i} + a_y\hat{j}\), where \(a_x = \frac{dv_x}{dt}\) and \(a_y = \frac{dv_y}{dt}\). Unlike 1D motion, in 2D motion \(\mathbf{v}\) and \(\mathbf{a}\) can have any angle between \(0^\circ\) and \(180^\circ\).

Motion in a Plane with Constant Acceleration

When acceleration \(\mathbf{a}\) is constant in both magnitude and direction, two-dimensional motion splits into two independent simultaneous one-dimensional motions along the \(x\)- and \(y\)-axes:

\[\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{v}_0 + \mathbf{a}t \implies \begin{cases} v_x = v_{0x} + a_x t \\ v_y = v_{0y} + a_y t \end{cases}\] \[\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{r}_0 + \mathbf{v}_0 t + \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{a}t^2 \implies \begin{cases} x = x_0 + v_{0x}t + \frac{1}{2}a_x t^2 \\ y = y_0 + v_{0y}t + \frac{1}{2}a_y t^2 \end{cases}\]
हिन्दी

\(x\)-\(y\) समतल में किसी कण का स्थिति सदिश \(\mathbf{r} = x\hat{i} + y\hat{j}\) होता है। यदि कण समय \(t\) पर \(\mathbf{r}\) से समय \(t'\) पर \(\mathbf{r}'\) तक जाता है, तो विस्थापन \(\Delta\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{r}' - \mathbf{r} = \Delta x\hat{i} + \Delta y\hat{j}\) केवल प्रारंभिक और अंतिम स्थितियों पर निर्भर करता है, तय किए गए वास्तविक पथ पर नहीं। अतः \(|\Delta\mathbf{r}| \le \text{पथ की लंबाई}\)।

  • औसत वेग: \(\overline{\mathbf{v}} = \frac{\Delta\mathbf{r}}{\Delta t}\), जिसकी दिशा \(\Delta\mathbf{r}\) के अनुदिश होती है। ध्यान रखें कि \(\text{औसत चाल} \ge |\overline{\mathbf{v}}|\)।
  • तात्क्षणिक वेग: \(\mathbf{v} = \lim_{\Delta t \to 0} \frac{\Delta\mathbf{r}}{\Delta t} = \frac{d\mathbf{r}}{dt} = v_x\hat{i} + v_y\hat{j}\), जहाँ \(v_x = \frac{dx}{dt}\) और \(v_y = \frac{dy}{dt}\)। किसी बिंदु पर तात्क्षणिक वेग सदिश सदैव उस बिंदु पर पथ की स्पर्श रेखा के अनुदिश होता है।
  • औसत एवं तात्क्षणिक त्वरण: \(\overline{\mathbf{a}} = \frac{\Delta\mathbf{v}}{\Delta t}\) और \(\mathbf{a} = \frac{d\mathbf{v}}{dt} = a_x\hat{i} + a_y\hat{j}\), जहाँ \(a_x = \frac{dv_x}{dt}\) और \(a_y = \frac{dv_y}{dt}\)। एकविमीय गति के विपरीत, द्विविमीय गति में \(\mathbf{v}\) और \(\mathbf{a}\) के बीच का कोण \(0^\circ\) से \(180^\circ\) के बीच कुछ भी हो सकता है।

एकसमान त्वरण से समतल में गति

जब त्वरण \(\mathbf{a}\) परिमाण और दिशा दोनों में नियत रहता है, तो समतल में गति को \(x\)- और \(y\)-अक्षों के अनुदिश दो स्वतंत्र एवं साथ-साथ होने वाली एकविमीय गतियों के रूप में हल किया जा सकता है:

\[\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{v}_0 + \mathbf{a}t \implies \begin{cases} v_x = v_{0x} + a_x t \\ v_y = v_{0y} + a_y t \end{cases}\] \[\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{r}_0 + \mathbf{v}_0 t + \frac{1}{2}\mathbf{a}t^2 \implies \begin{cases} x = x_0 + v_{0x}t + \frac{1}{2}a_x t^2 \\ y = y_0 + v_{0y}t + \frac{1}{2}a_y t^2 \end{cases}\]

4. Projectile Motion | प्रक्षेप्य गति

English

A projectile is an object launched into the air that moves solely under the influence of gravity (assuming negligible air resistance). If launched from the origin \((0,0)\) with initial speed \(v_0\) at angle \(\theta_0\) above the horizontal:

  • Horizontal component: \(a_x = 0\), so \(v_x = v_0\cos\theta_0\) remains constant throughout the flight, and \(x = (v_0\cos\theta_0)t\).
  • Vertical component: \(a_y = -g\), so \(v_y = v_0\sin\theta_0 - gt\) and \(y = (v_0\sin\theta_0)t - \frac{1}{2}gt^2\).
Tutorial Diagram 2: Parabolic path of an oblique projectile. At the highest point, vertical velocity is zero and speed is minimum.
\[\text{Equation of Trajectory: } y = (\tan\theta_0)x - \frac{g}{2(v_0\cos\theta_0)^2}x^2 \quad \text{(Parabola)}\] \[\text{Time of Ascent } (t_m) = \frac{v_0\sin\theta_0}{g}, \qquad \text{Time of Flight } (T_f) = \frac{2v_0\sin\theta_0}{g}\] \[\text{Maximum Height } (h_m) = \frac{(v_0\sin\theta_0)^2}{2g}, \qquad \text{Horizontal Range } (R) = \frac{v_0^2\sin 2\theta_0}{g}\]
  • Maximum Range: For a fixed \(v_0\), \(R\) is maximum when \(\sin 2\theta_0 = 1\), i.e., \(\theta_0 = 45^\circ\), giving \(R_{\max} = \frac{v_0^2}{g}\).
  • Equal Ranges: Complementary angles \(\theta_0\) and \(90^\circ - \theta_0\) (or \(45^\circ + \alpha\) and \(45^\circ - \alpha\)) yield the same horizontal range for the same launch speed \(v_0\).
  • Horizontal Launch from Height \(H\): With \(v_{0x} = u\) and \(v_{0y} = 0\), time to reach ground is \(t = \sqrt{\frac{2H}{g}}\), horizontal distance is \(x = u\sqrt{\frac{2H}{g}}\), and impact speed is \(v = \sqrt{u^2 + 2gH}\).

5. Uniform Circular Motion | एकसमान वृत्तीय गति

English

When an object travels along a circle of radius \(R\) at a constant speed \(v\), its motion is called uniform circular motion (UCM). Even though speed is constant, velocity \(\mathbf{v}\) is always tangent to the circle and changes direction continuously. Therefore, the object experiences an acceleration directed toward the center of the circle, called centripetal acceleration (\(\mathbf{a}_c\)).

Tutorial Diagram 3: In UCM, velocity \(\mathbf{v}\) is tangential and centripetal acceleration \(\mathbf{a}_c\) points radially inward.
\[\omega = \frac{\Delta\theta}{\Delta t} = \frac{2\pi}{T} = 2\pi\nu, \qquad v = R\omega = \frac{2\pi R}{T} = 2\pi R\nu\] \[a_c = \frac{v^2}{R} = \omega^2 R = 4\pi^2\nu^2 R\]

Key Insight: In UCM, the magnitude of \(\mathbf{a}_c\) is constant, but its direction changes continuously. Hence \(\mathbf{a}_c\) is not a constant vector, and its average over one complete revolution is \(\mathbf{0}\). Constant-acceleration kinematic equations (\(\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{v}_0 + \mathbf{a}t\)) do not apply to UCM.

हिन्दी

जब कोई वस्तु \(R\) त्रिज्या के वृत्ताकार पथ पर नियत चाल \(v\) से चलती है, तो उसकी गति को एकसमान वृत्तीय गति (UCM) कहते हैं। यद्यपि चाल नियत रहती है, परंतु वेग \(\mathbf{v}\) की दिशा (जो सदैव स्पर्शरेखीय होती है) लगातार बदलती रहती है। इस कारण वस्तु पर वृत्त के केंद्र की ओर एक त्वरण कार्य करता है, जिसे अभिकेंद्र त्वरण (\(\mathbf{a}_c\)) कहते हैं।

ट्यूटोरियल आरेख 3: एकसमान वृत्तीय गति में वेग \(\mathbf{v}\) स्पर्शरेखीय तथा अभिकेंद्र त्वरण \(\mathbf{a}_c\) केंद्र की ओर होता है।
\[\omega = \frac{\Delta\theta}{\Delta t} = \frac{2\pi}{T} = 2\pi\nu, \qquad v = R\omega = \frac{2\pi R}{T} = 2\pi R\nu\] \[a_c = \frac{v^2}{R} = \omega^2 R = 4\pi^2\nu^2 R\]

महत्वपूर्ण तथ्य: एकसमान वृत्तीय गति में \(\mathbf{a}_c\) का परिमाण नियत रहता है, परंतु इसकी दिशा निरंतर बदलती है। अतः \(\mathbf{a}_c\) एक नियत सदिश नहीं है, और एक पूर्ण चक्र में इसका औसत मान \(\mathbf{0}\) होता है। नियत त्वरण के शुद्धगतिकी समीकरण (\(\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{v}_0 + \mathbf{a}t\)) यहाँ लागू नहीं होते।

Solution:

Using the Law of Cosines with \(A = 10\,\mathrm{N}\), \(B = 6\,\mathrm{N}\), and \(\theta = 60^\circ\):

\[R = \sqrt{A^2 + B^2 + 2AB\cos 60^\circ} = \sqrt{10^2 + 6^2 + 2(10)(6)(0.5)} = \sqrt{100 + 36 + 60} = \sqrt{196} = 14\,\mathrm{N}\]

Initial velocity components: \(v_{0x} = 20\cos 30^\circ = 10\sqrt{3}\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) and \(v_{0y} = 20\sin 30^\circ = 10\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\).

(c) Horizontal range: \(R = v_{0x} T_f = 10\sqrt{3} \times 2.0 = 20\sqrt{3} \approx 34.6\,\mathrm{m}\).

8. Common Pitfalls & Revision Checklist | सामान्य त्रुटियाँ एवं पुनरावृत्ति सूची

English

Avoid These Common NEET Mistakes:

  • Zero velocity at projectile peak? False! Only the vertical component \(v_y = 0\) at the highest point; the horizontal component \(v_x = v_0\cos\theta_0\) is still present, and acceleration remains \(g\) downward.
  • Adding scalar to vector: You can never add a scalar to a vector, nor add a component \(A_x\) (a scalar) directly to \(\mathbf{A}\) (a vector).
  • Applying \(\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{v}_0 + \mathbf{a}t\) to UCM: Invalid, because \(\mathbf{a}_c\) changes direction continuously.
  • Non-coplanar vectors summing to zero: Three vectors that do not lie in the same plane can never add up to give a null vector.

Quick Revision Checklist:

  • Can you write \(\mathbf{A} = A_x\hat{i} + A_y\hat{j}\) and find \(|\mathbf{A}|\) and \(\theta\)?
  • Do you remember \(R = 4h_m\cot\theta_0\) and \(R_{\max} = \frac{v_0^2}{g}\) at \(\theta_0 = 45^\circ\)?
  • Can you convert between \(\nu\) (rev/s), \(T\), \(\omega = 2\pi\nu\), \(v = R\omega\), and \(a_c = \omega^2 R\)?
हिन्दी

NEET में इन सामान्य गलतियों से बचें:

  • प्रक्षेप्य के उच्चतम बिंदु पर वेग शून्य? गलत! उच्चतम बिंदु पर केवल ऊर्ध्वाधर घटक \(v_y = 0\) होता है; क्षैतिज घटक \(v_x = v_0\cos\theta_0\) बना रहता है और त्वरण \(g\) नीचे की ओर कार्य करता रहता है।
  • अदिश और सदिश का योग: किसी अदिश को सदिश में नहीं जोड़ा जा सकता, और न ही किसी सदिश के घटक \(A_x\) (अदिश) को सीधे सदिश \(\mathbf{A}\) में जोड़ा जा सकता है।
  • UCM में \(\mathbf{v} = \mathbf{v}_0 + \mathbf{a}t\) का प्रयोग: यह अमान्य है क्योंकि अभिकेंद्र त्वरण \(\mathbf{a}_c\) की दिशा लगातार बदलती रहती है।
  • असमतलीय सदिशों का योग: तीन सदिश जो एक ही समतल में स्थित नहीं हैं, कभी भी जुड़कर शून्य सदिश नहीं बना सकते।

  • क्या आप \(\nu\) (चक्कर/सेकंड), \(T\), \(\omega = 2\pi\nu\), \(v = R\omega\) और \(a_c = \omega^2 R\) के बीच रूपांतरण कर सकते हैं?
