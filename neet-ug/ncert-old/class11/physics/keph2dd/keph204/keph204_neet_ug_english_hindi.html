English

Learning Objectives and Overview

Thermodynamics is the macroscopic study of heat, temperature, work, and energy transformations in bulk systems. Unlike mechanics—which tracks the motion of a body as a whole—thermodynamics focuses on the internal state of a system specified by measurable macroscopic variables such as pressure (\(P\)), volume (\(V\)), and temperature (\(T\)).

  • Distinguish between thermal equilibrium, temperature, heat, work, and internal energy.
  • Apply the Zeroth, First, and Second Laws of Thermodynamics with consistent sign conventions.
  • Analyze isothermal, adiabatic, isobaric, isochoric, and cyclic processes on \(P\)-\(V\) diagrams.
  • Relate specific heat capacities (\(C_p\), \(C_v\), and \(\gamma\)) and evaluate Carnot engine efficiency.

English

Thermal Equilibrium and the Zeroth Law

A thermodynamic system is in thermodynamic equilibrium when its macroscopic state variables (\(P\), \(V\), \(T\), mass, and composition) do not change with time. Whether two systems exchange thermal energy depends on the boundary separating them:

  • Adiabatic wall: An insulating boundary that prevents any heat transfer between systems.
  • Diathermic wall: A conducting boundary that allows heat to flow until both systems reach the same temperature (thermal equilibrium).
Zeroth Law of Thermodynamics: If system \(A\) and system \(B\) are separately in thermal equilibrium with a third system \(C\), then \(A\) and \(B\) are in thermal equilibrium with each other: \[ T_A = T_C \quad \text{and} \quad T_B = T_C \implies T_A = T_B \]

This law establishes temperature (\(T\)) as the fundamental property that determines the direction of heat flow: heat flows spontaneously from higher temperature to lower temperature until temperatures equalize.

Heat, Internal Energy, Work, and the First Law

Internal Energy (\(U\)): The sum of the disordered kinetic energies (translational, rotational, vibrational) and intermolecular potential energies of all molecules in the frame where the system's center of mass is at rest. Bulk kinetic energy of a moving container is not part of \(U\).

Heat (\(Q\)) and Work (\(W\)): Both are modes of energy transfer (energy in transit), not properties stored inside a state. Heat is energy transferred due to a temperature difference, whereas work is energy transferred by mechanical means (such as moving a piston). Thus, \(U\) is a state variable (path-independent), while \(Q\) and \(W\) are path functions.

First Law of Thermodynamics (Conservation of Energy): \[ \Delta Q = \Delta U + \Delta W \] For a gas expanding against an external pressure \(P\): \[ \Delta W = P\,\Delta V \implies \Delta Q = \Delta U + P\,\Delta V \] Notice that \(\Delta U = \Delta Q - \Delta W\) is independent of the path taken between two equilibrium states.
Quantity Positive Sign (\(+\)) Negative Sign (\(-\))
Heat (\(\Delta Q\)) Heat supplied to the system Heat rejected by the system
Work (\(\Delta W\)) Work done by the system (expansion) Work done on the system (compression)
Internal Energy (\(\Delta U\)) Internal energy increases Internal energy decreases

ऊष्मा (\(Q\)) और कार्य (\(W\)): ये दोनों ऊर्जा संचरण की विधियाँ (संक्रमित होती हुई ऊर्जा) हैं, न कि किसी अवस्था में संचित गुण। ऊष्मा तापांतर के कारण प्रवाहित ऊर्जा है, जबकि कार्य यांत्रिक साधनों (जैसे पिस्टन को खिसकाना) द्वारा स्थानांतरित ऊर्जा है। अतः \(U\) एक अवस्था चर (पथ से स्वतंत्र) है, जबकि \(Q\) और \(W\) पथ फलन हैं।

Specific Heat Capacity and State Variables

Specific Heat Capacity (\(s\)) is the heat required per unit mass to raise the temperature by \(1\,\mathrm{K}\): \(s = \frac{1}{m}\frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta T}\) (unit: \(\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}\)).

Molar Specific Heat Capacity (\(C\)) is the heat required per mole (\(\mu\)) for a \(1\,\mathrm{K}\) rise: \(C = \frac{1}{\mu}\frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta T}\) (unit: \(\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}\)).

  • Solids (Equipartition estimate): Each vibrating atom has \(3\) vibrational degrees of freedom, giving average energy \(3 k_B T\) per atom and \(U = 3RT\) per mole. Since \(\Delta V \approx 0\) for solids, \(C \approx 3R\) at room temperature (breaks down at low temperatures).
  • Water & Calorie: \(1\,\mathrm{cal}\) is the heat needed to raise \(1\,\mathrm{g}\) of water from \(14.5\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\) to \(15.5\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\); \(1\,\mathrm{cal} = 4.186\,\mathrm{J}\). Specific heat of water is \(4186\,\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}\).
  • Ideal Gases (Mayer's Relation): Because \(U\) of an ideal gas depends only on \(T\), \(C_v = \left(\frac{\Delta U}{\Delta T}\right)_v\) and \(C_p = \left(\frac{\Delta U}{\Delta T}\right)_p + P\left(\frac{\Delta V}{\Delta T}\right)_p = C_v + R\), yielding: \[ C_p - C_v = R \quad \text{and} \quad \gamma = \frac{C_p}{C_v} \]

Equation of State and Variable Classification: Equilibrium states obey an equation of state, such as \(PV = \mu RT\) for an ideal gas. If a system is mentally divided into two equal halves: extensive variables (\(U, V, M, \mu\)) halve, whereas intensive variables (\(P, T, \rho\)) remain unchanged. Non-equilibrium processes (like free expansion into vacuum or explosive combustion) cannot be assigned single uniform \(P\) or \(T\) values during transition.

विशिष्ट ऊष्मा धारिता और अवस्था चर

विशिष्ट ऊष्मा धारिता (\(s\)): एकांक द्रव्यमान का ताप \(1\,\mathrm{K}\) बढ़ाने के लिए आवश्यक ऊष्मा: \(s = \frac{1}{m}\frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta T}\) (मात्रक: \(\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}\))।

मोलर विशिष्ट ऊष्मा धारिता (\(C\)): एकांक मोल (\(\mu\)) का ताप \(1\,\mathrm{K}\) बढ़ाने के लिए आवश्यक ऊष्मा: \(C = \frac{1}{\mu}\frac{\Delta Q}{\Delta T}\) (मात्रक: \(\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}\))।

  • ठोसों के लिए: प्रत्येक कंपन करने वाले परमाणु की औसत ऊर्जा \(3 k_B T\) होती है, जिससे प्रति मोल \(U = 3RT\) प्राप्त होता है। चूँकि ठोसों में \(\Delta V \approx 0\), अतः कमरे के ताप पर \(C \approx 3R\) होता है (निम्न ताप पर यह नियम विफल हो जाता है)।
  • जल और कैलोरी: \(1\,\mathrm{g}\) जल का ताप \(14.5\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\) से \(15.5\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\) तक बढ़ाने के लिए आवश्यक ऊष्मा \(1\,\mathrm{cal}\) कहलाती है; \(1\,\mathrm{cal} = 4.186\,\mathrm{J}\)। जल की विशिष्ट ऊष्मा \(4186\,\mathrm{J\,kg^{-1}\,K^{-1}}\) होती है।
  • आदर्श गैस (मेयर संबंध): आदर्श गैस की आंतरिक ऊर्जा \(U\) केवल \(T\) पर निर्भर करती है, अतः \(C_v = \left(\frac{\Delta U}{\Delta T}\right)_v\) तथा \(C_p = C_v + R\), जिससे प्राप्त होता है: \[ C_p - C_v = R \quad \text{तथा} \quad \gamma = \frac{C_p}{C_v} \]

अवस्था समीकरण और चरों का वर्गीकरण: साम्य अवस्थाएँ अवस्था समीकरण (जैसे आदर्श गैस के लिए \(PV = \mu RT\)) का पालन करती हैं। यदि किसी निकाय को दो बराबर भागों में बाँटा जाए, तो मात्रात्मक चर (Extensive) (\(U, V, M, \mu\)) आधे हो जाते हैं, जबकि मात्रा-स्वतंत्र चर (Intensive) (\(P, T, \rho\)) अपरिवर्तित रहते हैं। असाम्य प्रक्रमों (जैसे निर्वात में मुक्त प्रसार या विस्फोटक दहन) में संक्रमण के दौरान एकसमान \(P\) या \(T\) परिभाषित नहीं होते।

Thermodynamic Processes

A quasi-static process is an idealized, infinitely slow process in which pressure and temperature differences between the system and surroundings are infinitesimal, keeping the system in equilibrium at every step. Below are the key processes for \(\mu\) moles of an ideal gas:

Process Condition & Relation Work Done (\(W\)) First Law Outcome
Isothermal \(T = \text{const}\), \(PV = \text{const}\) \(\mu RT \ln\left(\frac{V_2}{V_1}\right)\) \(\Delta U = 0 \implies Q = W\)
Adiabatic \(\Delta Q = 0\), \(PV^\gamma = \text{const}\) \(\frac{P_1 V_1 - P_2 V_2}{\gamma - 1} = \frac{\mu R(T_1 - T_2)}{\gamma - 1}\) \(\Delta U = -W\)
Isobaric \(P = \text{const}\), \(\frac{V}{T} = \text{const}\) \(P(V_2 - V_1) = \mu R(T_2 - T_1)\) \(\Delta Q = \mu C_p \Delta T\)
Isochoric \(V = \text{const}\), \(\frac{P}{T} = \text{const}\) \(W = 0\) \(\Delta Q = \Delta U = \mu C_v \Delta T\)
Cyclic Initial state = Final state Area enclosed by \(P\)-\(V\) loop \(\Delta U = 0 \implies Q_{\text{net}} = W_{\text{net}}\)

Slope Comparison on a \(P\)-\(V\) Diagram: Differentiating \(PV = \text{const}\) gives \(\left(\frac{dP}{dV}\right)_{\text{iso}} = -\frac{P}{V}\), whereas differentiating \(PV^\gamma = \text{const}\) gives \(\left(\frac{dP}{dV}\right)_{\text{adia}} = -\gamma\frac{P}{V}\). Since \(\gamma > 1\), an adiabatic curve is always steeper than an isothermal curve at any intersection point.

ऊष्मागतिक प्रक्रम

अर्ध-स्थैतिक प्रक्रम (Quasi-static process) एक आदर्श, अत्यंत धीमा प्रक्रम है जिसमें निकाय और परिवेश के बीच दाब तथा ताप का अंतर अतिसूक्ष्म होता है, जिससे निकाय प्रत्येक चरण पर साम्य में बना रहता है। \(\mu\) मोल आदर्श गैस के प्रमुख प्रक्रम नीचे दिए गए हैं:

प्रक्रम शर्त और संबंध किया गया कार्य (\(W\)) प्रथम नियम का परिणाम
समतापी \(T = \text{नियत}\), \(PV = \text{नियत}\) \(\mu RT \ln\left(\frac{V_2}{V_1}\right)\) \(\Delta U = 0 \implies Q = W\)
रुद्धोष्म \(\Delta Q = 0\), \(PV^\gamma = \text{नियत}\) \(\frac{P_1 V_1 - P_2 V_2}{\gamma - 1} = \frac{\mu R(T_1 - T_2)}{\gamma - 1}\) \(\Delta U = -W\)
समदाबी \(P = \text{नियत}\), \(\frac{V}{T} = \text{नियत}\) \(P(V_2 - V_1) = \mu R(T_2 - T_1)\) \(\Delta Q = \mu C_p \Delta T\)
समआयतनिक \(V = \text{नियत}\), \(\frac{P}{T} = \text{नियत}\) \(W = 0\) \(\Delta Q = \Delta U = \mu C_v \Delta T\)
चक्रीय प्रारंभिक अवस्था = अंतिम अवस्था \(P\)-\(V\) लूप द्वारा घिरा क्षेत्रफल \(\Delta U = 0 \implies Q_{\text{कुल}} = W_{\text{कुल}}\)

\(P\)-\(V\) आरेख पर ढाल की तुलना: \(PV = \text{नियत}\) का अवकलन करने पर \(\left(\frac{dP}{dV}\right)_{\text{समतापी}} = -\frac{P}{V}\), जबकि \(PV^\gamma = \text{नियत}\) से \(\left(\frac{dP}{dV}\right)_{\text{रुद्धोष्म}} = -\gamma\frac{P}{V}\) प्राप्त होता है। चूँकि \(\gamma > 1\), किसी भी प्रतिच्छेदन बिंदु पर रुद्धोष्म वक्र का ढाल समतापी वक्र से सदैव अधिक तीव्र होता है।

Second Law, Reversibility, and the Carnot Engine

While the First Law tracks energy conservation, the Second Law of Thermodynamics restricts the direction of natural processes and limits heat-to-work conversion:

  • Kelvin-Planck Statement: No process is possible whose sole result is the absorption of heat from a reservoir and its complete conversion into work (efficiency \(\eta\) can never be \(100\%\)).
  • Clausius Statement: No process is possible whose sole result is the transfer of heat from a colder object to a hotter object (refrigerator COP can never be infinite).

Reversible vs. Irreversible Processes: A process is reversible only if it is quasi-static and free of dissipative effects (friction, viscosity, inelasticity). Spontaneous natural processes (free expansion, heat conduction across a finite temperature difference, gas diffusion, combustion) are irreversible.

Carnot Engine (Ideal Reversible Cycle): Consists of two isothermal steps (at source \(T_1\) and sink \(T_2\), in kelvin) connected by two adiabatic steps: \[ \eta = \frac{W}{Q_1} = 1 - \frac{Q_2}{Q_1} = 1 - \frac{T_2}{T_1} \qquad \text{and} \qquad \frac{Q_1}{Q_2} = \frac{T_1}{T_2} \] Carnot's Theorem: Between two given temperatures \(T_1\) and \(T_2\), no engine can have an efficiency greater than a Carnot engine, and its efficiency is independent of the working substance.

द्वितीय नियम, उत्क्रमणीयता और कार्नो इंजन

प्रथम नियम ऊर्जा संरक्षण बताता है, जबकि ऊष्मागतिकी का द्वितीय नियम प्राकृतिक प्रक्रमों की दिशा और ऊष्मा से कार्य में रूपांतरण की सीमा तय करता है:

  • केल्विन-प्लांक कथन: ऐसा कोई प्रक्रम संभव नहीं है जिसका एकमात्र परिणाम किसी ऊष्मा भंडार से ऊष्मा अवशोषित कर उसे पूर्णतः कार्य में बदलना हो (दक्षता \(\eta\) कभी \(100\%\) नहीं हो सकती)।
  • क्लॉसियस कथन: ऐसा कोई प्रक्रम संभव नहीं है जिसका एकमात्र परिणाम ठंडे पिंड से गर्म पिंड की ओर ऊष्मा का स्थानांतरण हो (प्रशीतक का निष्पादन गुणांक कभी अनंत नहीं हो सकता)।

उत्क्रमणीय एवं अनुत्क्रमणीय प्रक्रम: कोई प्रक्रम तभी उत्क्रमणीय होता है जब वह अर्ध-स्थैतिक हो और घर्षण, श्यानता जैसे क्षयकारी प्रभावों से मुक्त हो। प्रकृति के सभी स्वतः प्रक्रम (मुक्त प्रसार, सीमित तापांतर पर ऊष्मा चालन, गैस विसरण, दहन) अनुत्क्रमणीय होते हैं।

कार्नो इंजन (आदर्श उत्क्रमणीय चक्र): इसमें दो समतापी चरण (स्रोत \(T_1\) तथा सिंक \(T_2\) केल्विन में) दो रुद्धोष्म चरणों से जुड़े होते हैं: \[ \eta = \frac{W}{Q_1} = 1 - \frac{Q_2}{Q_1} = 1 - \frac{T_2}{T_1} \qquad \text{तथा} \qquad \frac{Q_1}{Q_2} = \frac{T_1}{T_2} \] कार्नो प्रमेय: दो दिए गए तापों \(T_1\) और \(T_2\) के बीच कार्य करने वाले किसी भी इंजन की दक्षता कार्नो इंजन से अधिक नहीं हो सकती, और इसकी दक्षता कार्यकारी पदार्थ की प्रकृति से स्वतंत्र होती है।

Worked Examples

Example 1: Applying the First Law to Isobaric Heating

Problem (Assumed values): Suppose \(2.0\,\mathrm{mol}\) of an ideal monatomic gas (\(C_v = \frac{3}{2}R\)) is heated at constant pressure from \(300\,\mathrm{K}\) to \(400\,\mathrm{K}\). Take \(R = 8.31\,\mathrm{J\,mol^{-1}\,K^{-1}}\). Calculate the work done by the gas, the change in internal energy, and the heat supplied.

Solution:

  • Temperature change: \(\Delta T = 400 - 300 = 100\,\mathrm{K}\).
  • Work done at constant pressure: \[ W = \mu R \Delta T = 2.0 \times 8.31 \times 100 = 1662\,\mathrm{J} \]
  • Change in internal energy: \[ \Delta U = \mu C_v \Delta T = 2.0 \times \frac{3}{2} \times 8.31 \times 100 = 2493\,\mathrm{J} \]
  • By the First Law of Thermodynamics, heat supplied: \[ \Delta Q = \Delta U + W = 2493 + 1662 = 4155\,\mathrm{J} \]

Example 2: Adiabatic Compression of an Ideal Gas

Problem (Assumed values): An ideal monatomic gas (\(\gamma = \frac{5}{3}\)) initially at pressure \(P_1 = 1.0 \times 10^5\,\mathrm{Pa}\) and temperature \(T_1 = 300\,\mathrm{K}\) is compressed adiabatically to \(\frac{1}{8}\) of its initial volume. Find its final pressure and final temperature.

Solution:

  • Using \(P_1 V_1^\gamma = P_2 V_2^\gamma\) with \(\frac{V_1}{V_2} = 8\): \[ P_2 = P_1 \left(\frac{V_1}{V_2}\right)^{5/3} = (1.0 \times 10^5) \times 8^{5/3} = (1.0 \times 10^5) \times 32 = 3.2 \times 10^6\,\mathrm{Pa} \]
  • Using \(T_1 V_1^{\gamma - 1} = T_2 V_2^{\gamma - 1}\): \[ T_2 = T_1 \left(\frac{V_1}{V_2}\right)^{2/3} = 300 \times 8^{2/3} = 300 \times 4 = 1200\,\mathrm{K} \]

Example 3: Evaluating a Carnot Engine

Problem (Assumed values): A Carnot engine operates between a hot reservoir at \(327\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\) and a cold reservoir at \(27\,^\circ\mathrm{C}\). If it absorbs \(1200\,\mathrm{J}\) of heat per cycle from the source, find its efficiency, work output per cycle, and heat rejected to the sink.

Solution:

  • Convert temperatures to kelvin: \(T_1 = 327 + 273 = 600\,\mathrm{K}\), \(T_2 = 27 + 273 = 300\,\mathrm{K}\).
  • Efficiency: \[ \eta = 1 - \frac{T_2}{T_1} = 1 - \frac{300}{600} = 0.50 \text{ (or } 50\%) \]
  • Work done per cycle: \(W = \eta Q_1 = 0.50 \times 1200 = 600\,\mathrm{J}\).
  • Heat rejected to the sink: \(Q_2 = Q_1 - W = 1200 - 600 = 600\,\mathrm{J}\).

An ideal gas undergoes a cyclic process \(A \to B \to C \to A\) as shown in the \(P\)-\(V\) diagram below. What is the net heat absorbed by the gas in one complete cycle?

  • (A) \(+450\,\mathrm{J}\)
  • (B) \(-450\,\mathrm{J}\)
  • (C) \(+900\,\mathrm{J}\)
  • (D) \(0\,\mathrm{J}\)

Answer: (A) \(+450\,\mathrm{J}\)

Solution: For a cyclic process, \(\Delta U = 0\), so \(Q_{\text{net}} = W_{\text{net}}\). Since the cycle \(A \to B \to C \to A\) proceeds clockwise on the \(P\)-\(V\) diagram, the net work done by the gas is positive and equals the area of the triangle: \[ W_{\text{net}} = \frac{1}{2} \times (4.0 - 1.0)\,\mathrm{m^3} \times (500 - 200)\,\mathrm{Pa} = \frac{1}{2} \times 3.0 \times 300 = +450\,\mathrm{J} \] Hence, \(Q_{\text{net}} = +450\,\mathrm{J}\).

हल: नियत आयतन पर संपूर्ण ऊष्मा आंतरिक ऊर्जा बढ़ाने में जाती है (\(\Delta Q = \Delta U\))। नियत दाब पर समान \(\Delta T\) के लिए आंतरिक ऊर्जा में समान वृद्धि के साथ-साथ गैस प्रसारित होकर \(P\Delta V = \mu R \Delta T\) कार्य भी करती है, इसलिए \(C_p = C_v + R > C_v\)।

English

Common Pitfalls and Revision Checklist

  • Kelvin vs. Celsius Trap: Always convert \(T_1\) and \(T_2\) to kelvin in \(PV = \mu RT\), adiabatic relations, and Carnot efficiency \(\eta = 1 - \frac{T_2}{T_1}\). Only temperature differences (\(\Delta T\)) have the same numerical value in \(^\circ\mathrm{C}\) and \(\mathrm{K}\).
  • Sign Convention Check: In \(\Delta Q = \Delta U + \Delta W\), remember that expansion means \(\Delta W > 0\) and compression means \(\Delta W < 0\).
  • Ideal Gas Internal Energy: For an ideal gas, \(U\) depends only on \(T\), regardless of whether the process is isobaric, isochoric, or isothermal.
  • Cyclic Loop Direction: Clockwise loop on a \(P\)-\(V\) graph gives \(W > 0\); counterclockwise loop gives \(W < 0\).

Concise Final Recap

Thermodynamics connects heat, work, and internal energy through the Zeroth Law (defines temperature), the First Law (\(\Delta Q = \Delta U + \Delta W\), energy conservation), and the Second Law (limits efficiency to \(\eta_{\max} = 1 - \frac{T_2}{T_1}\) for a reversible Carnot engine).

हिन्दी

सामान्य त्रुटियाँ एवं पुनरावृत्ति सूची

  • केल्विन बनाम सेल्सियस की भूल: \(PV = \mu RT\), रुद्धोष्म संबंधों और कार्नो दक्षता \(\eta = 1 - \frac{T_2}{T_1}\) में \(T_1\) व \(T_2\) को सदैव केल्विन में रखें। केवल तापांतर (\(\Delta T\)) का मान \(^\circ\mathrm{C}\) और \(\mathrm{K}\) में समान होता है।
  • चिह्न परिपाटी की जाँच: \(\Delta Q = \Delta U + \Delta W\) में प्रसार के लिए \(\Delta W > 0\) तथा संपीडन के लिए \(\Delta W < 0\) लें।
  • आदर्श गैस की आंतरिक ऊर्जा: आदर्श गैस के लिए \(U\) केवल \(T\) पर निर्भर करती है, चाहे प्रक्रम समदाबी हो, समआयतनिक हो या समतापी।
  • चक्रीय लूप की दिशा: \(P\)-\(V\) ग्राफ पर दक्षिणावर्त लूप से \(W > 0\) तथा वामावर्त लूप से \(W < 0\) प्राप्त होता है।
