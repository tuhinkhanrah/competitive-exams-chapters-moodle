1. Electric Charge, Materials, and Fundamental Properties

English

Electric charge is an intrinsic scalar property of matter responsible for electrostatic attraction and repulsion. Experiments show exactly two polarities—conventionally termed positive and negative. Like charges repel each other, while unlike charges attract. A neutral object contains equal amounts of positive and negative charge; charging a solid typically involves transferring loosely bound electrons from one body to another. Losing electrons yields a net positive charge, whereas gaining electrons yields a net negative charge.

Materials differ in how freely charges move within them:

  • Conductors: Substances (such as metals, the human body, and Earth) possessing mobile charge carriers that distribute excess charge rapidly over their outer surface.
  • Insulators: Substances (such as glass, dry wood, nylon, and plastics) with tightly bound electrons, where transferred charge stays localised at the rubbed region.
  • Semiconductors: Materials whose resistance to charge movement lies between conductors and insulators.

Every isolated system obeys three universal charge laws:

  1. Additivity: Total charge of a system is the algebraic sum of all individual charges, accounting for their signs: \(Q_{\text{total}} = q_1 + q_2 + \dots + q_n\).
  2. Conservation: The net charge of an isolated system remains constant over time. Rubbing two bodies merely redistributes electrons; even when particles are created (e.g., a neutron transforming into a proton and an electron), total charge before and after remains unchanged.
  3. Quantisation: All observable free charges are integer multiples of the elementary charge \(e = 1.602 \times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}\):
\[ q = n e, \quad n \in \{0, \pm 1, \pm 2, \pm 3, \dots\} \]

At macroscopic scales (charges of microcoulombs, \(1\,\mathrm{µC} = 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{C}\)), \(n\) is around \(10^{13}\), so discrete steps of \(e\) are imperceptible and charge behaves as a continuous distribution. At atomic scales, quantisation cannot be ignored.

Worked Example 1 (English)

Problem: Suppose a neutral plastic rod acquires a negative charge of \(-4.8\,\mathrm{µC}\) when rubbed with a woollen cloth (assumed isolated system). Calculate the number of electrons transferred and the resulting change in mass of the rod. (Use \(e = 1.6 \times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}\), \(m_e = 9.1 \times 10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}\)).

Solution:

Because the rod becomes negatively charged, electrons move from the woollen cloth onto the rod. Using \(|q| = n e\):

\[ n = \frac{4.8 \times 10^{-6}\,\mathrm{C}}{1.6 \times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}} = 3.0 \times 10^{13} \text{ electrons} \]

Each electron carries mass \(m_e\), so the mass of the rod increases by:

\[ \Delta m = n m_e = (3.0 \times 10^{13})(9.1 \times 10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}) = 2.73 \times 10^{-17}\,\mathrm{kg} \]

2. Coulomb's Law and the Superposition Principle

English

When charged bodies have dimensions negligible compared to their separation, they are modelled as point charges. Coulomb's law states that the electrostatic force between two stationary point charges \(q_1\) and \(q_2\) separated by distance \(r\) in vacuum acts along the line joining them, is directly proportional to \(|q_1 q_2|\), and varies inversely as \(r^2\):

\[ F = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \frac{|q_1 q_2|}{r^2} \]

Here \(\varepsilon_0 = 8.854 \times 10^{-12}\,\mathrm{C^2\,N^{-1}\,m^{-2}}\) is the permittivity of free space, and \(k = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \approx 9 \times 10^9\,\mathrm{N\,m^2\,C^{-2}}\). In vector form, if \(\mathbf{r}_1\) and \(\mathbf{r}_2\) are the position vectors of \(q_1\) and \(q_2\), let \(\mathbf{r}_{21} = \mathbf{r}_2 - \mathbf{r}_1\) and \(\hat{\mathbf{r}}_{21} = \mathbf{r}_{21}/r_{21}\). The force exerted on \(q_2\) by \(q_1\) is:

\[ \mathbf{F}_{21} = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \frac{q_1 q_2}{r_{21}^2} \hat{\mathbf{r}}_{21} = -\mathbf{F}_{12} \]

This single vector equation handles both attraction (\(q_1 q_2 < 0\)) and repulsion (\(q_1 q_2 > 0\)) and satisfies Newton's third law. Compared with gravity, electrostatic forces are enormously stronger: for an electron and a proton, \(F_e / F_G \approx 2.4 \times 10^{39}\), and for two protons, \(F_e / F_G \approx 1.3 \times 10^{36}\).

Principle of Superposition: The force between any pair of charges is unaffected by the presence of other charges. For \(n\) charges, the net force on \(q_1\) is the vector sum of individual pairwise Coulomb forces:

\[ \mathbf{F}_1 = \sum_{i=2}^{n} \mathbf{F}_{1i} = \frac{q_1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \sum_{i=2}^{n} \frac{q_i}{r_{1i}^2} \hat{\mathbf{r}}_{1i} \]

\[ \mathbf{F}_1 = \sum_{i=2}^{n} \mathbf{F}_{1i} = \frac{q_1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \sum_{i=2}^{n} \frac{q_i}{r_{1i}^2} \hat{\mathbf{r}}_{1i} \]

Worked Example 2 (English)

Problem: Two point charges \(q_1 = +4\,\mathrm{µC}\) and \(q_2 = +16\,\mathrm{µC}\) are fixed \(0.30\,\mathrm{m}\) apart in vacuum. Determine the position on the line joining them where a third charge \(q_3\) experiences zero net electrostatic force.

Solution:

Since \(q_1\) and \(q_2\) have the same sign, their forces on \(q_3\) oppose each other only at a point between them. Let this null point be at distance \(x\) from \(q_1\) (and \(0.30 - x\) from \(q_2\)). Setting \(|\mathbf{F}_{31}| = |\mathbf{F}_{32}|\):

\[ \frac{k (4\,\mathrm{µC}) |q_3|}{x^2} = \frac{k (16\,\mathrm{µC}) |q_3|}{(0.30 - x)^2} \]

Taking the positive square root of both sides:

\[ \frac{2}{x} = \frac{4}{0.30 - x} \implies 4x = 0.60 - 2x \implies 6x = 0.60 \implies x = 0.10\,\mathrm{m} \]

Thus, \(q_3\) must be placed \(0.10\,\mathrm{m}\) (\(10\,\mathrm{cm}\)) from \(q_1\) between the two charges.

3. Electric Field Intensity and Electric Field Lines

English

A source charge \(Q\) modifies the space around it by establishing an electric field \(\mathbf{E}(\mathbf{r})\). Operationally, it is the electrostatic force experienced per unit positive test charge \(q_0\) in the limit \(q_0 \to 0\) (so the test charge does not disturb the source configuration):

\[ \mathbf{E}(\mathbf{r}) = \lim_{q_0 \to 0} \frac{\mathbf{F}}{q_0} \quad \text{and} \quad \mathbf{F} = q\mathbf{E} \]

For a point charge \(Q\) at the origin, the electric field at position \(\mathbf{r}\) is:

\[ \mathbf{E}(\mathbf{r}) = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \frac{Q}{r^2} \hat{\mathbf{r}} \]

The SI unit of electric field is \(\mathrm{N\,C^{-1}}\) (or \(\mathrm{V\,m^{-1}}\)). A positive charge experiences force along \(\mathbf{E}\), whereas a negative charge experiences force opposite to \(\mathbf{E}\). By superposition, the net field due to multiple charges is \(\mathbf{E} = \sum \mathbf{E}_i\).

Properties of Electric Field Lines:

  • The tangent at any point on a field line gives the direction of the net electric field \(\mathbf{E}\) at that point.
  • Relative closeness (density) of lines per unit perpendicular area indicates field magnitude (\(E \propto 1/r^2\) for a point charge matches solid angle spreading).
  • Field lines originate on positive charges and terminate on negative charges (or extend to/from infinity for isolated charges).
  • In a charge-free region, field lines are continuous curves without sudden breaks.
  • Two field lines can never intersect; intersection would imply two distinct directions of \(\mathbf{E}\) at a single point.
  • Electrostatic field lines never form closed loops because the electrostatic field is conservative.

कोई स्रोत आवेश \(Q\) अपने चारों ओर के आकाश में एक विद्युत क्षेत्र \(\mathbf{E}(\mathbf{r})\) उत्पन्न करता है। किसी बिंदु पर विद्युत क्षेत्र की तीव्रता, उस बिंदु पर रखे गए अति सूक्ष्म धनात्मक परीक्षण आवेश \(q_0\) (\(q_0 \to 0\)) पर लगने वाले बल और \(q_0\) के अनुपात के बराबर होती है:

\[ \mathbf{E}(\mathbf{r}) = \lim_{q_0 \to 0} \frac{\mathbf{F}}{q_0} \quad \text{तथा} \quad \mathbf{F} = q\mathbf{E} \]

मूल बिंदु पर स्थित बिंदु आवेश \(Q\) के कारण स्थिति सदिश \(\mathbf{r}\) पर विद्युत क्षेत्र:

\[ \mathbf{E}(\mathbf{r}) = \frac{1}{4\pi\varepsilon_0} \frac{Q}{r^2} \hat{\mathbf{r}} \]

विद्युत क्षेत्र का SI मात्रक \(\mathrm{N\,C^{-1}}\) (या \(\mathrm{V\,m^{-1}}\)) है। धनावेश पर बल \(\mathbf{E}\) की दिशा में तथा ऋणावेश पर बल \(\mathbf{E}\) के विपरीत दिशा में लगता है।

विद्युत क्षेत्र रेखाओं के गुण:

  • क्षेत्र रेखा के किसी बिंदु पर खींची गई स्पर्श रेखा उस बिंदु पर नेट विद्युत क्षेत्र की दिशा बताती है।
  • लंबवत एकांक क्षेत्रफल से गुजरने वाली रेखाओं का घनत्व विद्युत क्षेत्र की प्रबलता को दर्शाता है।
  • ये रेखाएँ धनावेश से प्रारंभ होकर ऋणावेश पर समाप्त होती हैं।
  • आवेश-मुक्त क्षेत्र में ये सतत वक्र होती हैं और बीच में कहीं टूटती नहीं हैं।
  • दो क्षेत्र रेखाएँ कभी एक-दूसरे को प्रतिच्छेद नहीं करतीं, अन्यथा कटान बिंदु पर क्षेत्र की दो दिशाएँ होंगी जो असंभव है।
  • स्थिरवैद्युत क्षेत्र रेखाएँ कभी बंद लूप नहीं बनातीं।

4. Electric Dipole and Behaviour in External Fields

English

An electric dipole consists of two equal and opposite point charges \(+q\) and \(-q\) separated by a small distance \(2a\). Its electric dipole moment \(\mathbf{p}\) has magnitude \(p = 2qa\) (unit: \(\mathrm{C\,m}\)) and points by convention from \(-q\) to \(+q\).

Although the net charge of a dipole is zero, its electric field is non-zero because the charges are spatially separated. At distance \(r\) from the dipole centre:

Axial point: \(\quad \mathbf{E}_{\text{axial}} = \frac{2\mathbf{p}r}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 (r^2 - a^2)^2} \xrightarrow{r \gg a} \frac{2\mathbf{p}}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r^3}\) (along \(\mathbf{p}\))

Equatorial point: \(\quad \mathbf{E}_{\text{eq}} = \frac{-\mathbf{p}}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 (r^2 + a^2)^{3/2}} \xrightarrow{r \gg a} \frac{-\mathbf{p}}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r^3}\) (opposite to \(\mathbf{p}\))

Notice two crucial NEET facts: (i) far-field dipole intensity decays as \(1/r^3\) (faster than \(1/r^2\) for a point charge), and (ii) for the same large distance \(r\), \(E_{\text{axial}} = 2 E_{\text{eq}}\).

Dipole in a Uniform Electric Field: The forces \(+q\mathbf{E}\) and \(-q\mathbf{E}\) cancel, so the net force is zero, but they form a couple producing a torque:

\[ \boldsymbol{\tau} = \mathbf{p} \times \mathbf{E}, \quad \tau = pE\sin\theta \]

Here \(\theta\) is the angle between \(\mathbf{p}\) and \(\mathbf{E}\). Equilibrium occurs at \(\theta = 0^\circ\) (stable) and \(\theta = 180^\circ\) (unstable). In a non-uniform field, the net force is generally non-zero: if \(\mathbf{p}\) is parallel to \(\mathbf{E}\), the dipole is pulled toward the stronger field; if antiparallel, toward the weaker field.

हिन्दी (Hindi)

दो बराबर एवं विपरीत बिंदु आवेशों (\(+q\) तथा \(-q\)) का एक युग्म, जो परस्पर अल्प दूरी \(2a\) पर स्थित हों, विद्युत द्विध्रुव कहलाता है। इसका विद्युत द्विध्रुव आघूर्ण \(\mathbf{p}\) एक सदिश राशि है जिसका परिमाण \(p = 2qa\) (मात्रक: \(\mathrm{C\,m}\)) तथा दिशा \(-q\) से \(+q\) की ओर होती है।

जहाँ \(\theta\), \(\mathbf{p}\) और \(\mathbf{E}\) के बीच का कोण है। \(\theta = 0^\circ\) पर स्थायी संतुलन तथा \(\theta = 180^\circ\) पर अस्थायी संतुलन होता है। असमान विद्युत क्षेत्र में द्विध्रुव पर नेट बल अशून्य होता है।

5. Electric Flux, Continuous Charge Distributions, and Gauss's Law

English

Electric Flux: A small planar area element is represented as a vector \(\Delta\mathbf{S} = \Delta S\,\hat{\mathbf{n}}\), where \(\hat{\mathbf{n}}\) is the unit normal (taken as the outward normal for any closed surface). The electric flux \(\Delta\phi\) through \(\Delta\mathbf{S}\) is:

\[ \Delta\phi = \mathbf{E} \cdot \Delta\mathbf{S} = E\,\Delta S\cos\theta \]

Its SI unit is \(\mathrm{N\,m^2\,C^{-1}}\) (or \(\mathrm{V\,m}\)). When \(\theta = 90^\circ\), flux is zero.

Continuous Charge Densities: Macroscopic charge distributions are described by linear charge density \(\lambda = \Delta Q/\Delta l\) (\(\mathrm{C\,m^{-1}}\)), surface charge density \(\sigma = \Delta Q/\Delta S\) (\(\mathrm{C\,m^{-2}}\)), and volume charge density \(\rho = \Delta Q/\Delta V\) (\(\mathrm{C\,m^{-3}}\)).

Gauss's Law: The total electric flux through any closed Gaussian surface \(S\) equals \(1/\varepsilon_0\) times the net charge \(q_{\text{enc}}\) enclosed inside \(S\):

\[ \phi_E = \oint_S \mathbf{E} \cdot d\mathbf{S} = \frac{q_{\text{enc}}}{\varepsilon_0} \]

Remember: \(\mathbf{E}\) on the left side is the total field due to all charges (both inside and outside \(S\)), whereas \(q_{\text{enc}}\) on the right includes only charges inside \(S\). A Gaussian surface must never pass through a discrete point charge.

Three Standard Symmetric Applications:

  1. Infinitely long straight wire (uniform \(\lambda\), cylindrical Gaussian surface of radius \(r\)): \(\mathbf{E} = \frac{\lambda}{2\pi\varepsilon_0 r}\hat{\mathbf{n}}\) (radial, \(E \propto 1/r\)).
  2. Uniformly charged infinite plane sheet (uniform \(\sigma\), pillbox Gaussian surface): \(\mathbf{E} = \frac{\sigma}{2\varepsilon_0}\hat{\mathbf{n}}\) (independent of distance from the sheet).
  3. Thin uniformly charged spherical shell (radius \(R\), total charge \(q = 4\pi R^2\sigma\)):
    • Outside (\(r \ge R\)): \(\mathbf{E} = \frac{q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r^2}\hat{\mathbf{r}}\) (behaves as if all charge is at the centre).
    • Inside (\(r < R\)): \(\mathbf{E} = 0\) (enclosed charge is zero).

गॉस का नियम (Gauss's Law): किसी भी बंद गॉसीय पृष्ठ \(S\) से गुजरने वाला कुल विद्युत फ्लक्स, उस पृष्ठ के भीतर परिबद्ध कुल आवेश \(q_{\text{enc}}\) का \(1/\varepsilon_0\) गुना होता है:

\[ \phi_E = \oint_S \mathbf{E} \cdot d\mathbf{S} = \frac{q_{\text{enc}}}{\varepsilon_0} \]

ध्यान दें: बाएँ पक्ष में \(\mathbf{E}\) अंदर व बाहर स्थित सभी आवेशों का परिणामी क्षेत्र है, जबकि दाएँ पक्ष में \(q_{\text{enc}}\) केवल पृष्ठ के भीतर स्थित आवेश है।

तीन प्रमुख सममित अनुप्रयोग:

  1. अनंत लंबाई का एकसमान आवेशित सीधा तार (रैखिक घनत्व \(\lambda\)): \(\mathbf{E} = \frac{\lambda}{2\pi\varepsilon_0 r}\hat{\mathbf{n}}\) (त्रिज्यीय, \(E \propto 1/r\))।
  2. एकसमान आवेशित अनंत समतल चादर (पृष्ठीय घनत्व \(\sigma\)): \(\mathbf{E} = \frac{\sigma}{2\varepsilon_0}\hat{\mathbf{n}}\) (दूरी से स्वतंत्र)।
  3. पतला एकसमान आवेशित गोलीय खोल (त्रिज्या \(R\), कुल आवेश \(q = 4\pi R^2\sigma\)):
    • बाहर (\(r \ge R\)): \(\mathbf{E} = \frac{q}{4\pi\varepsilon_0 r^2}\hat{\mathbf{r}}\) (संपूर्ण आवेश केंद्र पर केंद्रित प्रतीत होता है)।
    • भीतर (\(r < R\)): \(\mathbf{E} = 0\) (परिबद्ध आवेश शून्य)।

Practice Question 1 (English — Single-Correct MCQ)

An initially neutral isolated metal sphere is given a positive charge of \(+3.2\,\mathrm{µC}\). How many electrons were removed from the sphere, and what happens to its mass? (\(e = 1.6 \times 10^{-19}\,\mathrm{C}\), \(m_e = 9.1 \times 10^{-31}\,\mathrm{kg}\))

  • (A) \(2.0 \times 10^{13}\) electrons removed; mass increases by \(1.82 \times 10^{-17}\,\mathrm{kg}\)
  • (B) \(2.0 \times 10^{13}\) electrons removed; mass decreases by \(1.82 \times 10^{-17}\,\mathrm{kg}\)
  • (C) \(5.0 \times 10^{12}\) electrons removed; mass decreases by \(4.55 \times 10^{-18}\,\mathrm{kg}\)
  • (D) \(2.0 \times 10^{19}\) electrons removed; mass decreases by \(1.82 \times 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{kg}\)
Correct Answer: (B)

Worked Solution: A positive charge means electrons are removed. Number of electrons \(n = q/e = (3.2 \times 10^{-6})/(1.6 \times 10^{-19}) = 2.0 \times 10^{13}\). Removing electrons reduces mass by \(\Delta m = n m_e = 2.0 \times 10^{13} \times 9.1 \times 10^{-31} = 1.82 \times 10^{-17}\,\mathrm{kg}\).

Practice Question 2 (English — Single-Correct MCQ)

Two identical small conducting spheres \(A\) and \(B\) carry charges \(+6\,\mathrm{µC}\) and \(-2\,\mathrm{µC}\), respectively, and experience an attractive force of magnitude \(F\) when separated by a distance \(d\). They are touched together and then placed at a separation \(d/2\). The new electrostatic force between them has magnitude and nature:

  • (A) \(\frac{4}{3}F\), repulsive
  • (B) \(\frac{4}{3}F\), attractive
  • (C) \(\frac{3}{4}F\), repulsive
  • (D) \(\frac{1}{3}F\), repulsive
Correct Answer: (A)

Worked Solution: Initially, \(F = \frac{k(6)(2)}{d^2} = \frac{12k}{d^2}\) (in \(\mathrm{µC}^2\) units). Upon touching, total charge \(+6 - 2 = +4\,\mathrm{µC}\) divides equally, giving \(+2\,\mathrm{µC}\) on each sphere. At separation \(d/2\), the new force is \(F' = \frac{k(2)(2)}{(d/2)^2} = \frac{16k}{d^2} = \frac{4}{3}F\), which is repulsive since both spheres now carry positive charge.

अभ्यास प्रश्न 2 (हिन्दी — एकल-सही बहुविकल्पीय)

दो सर्वसम छोटे चालक गोलों \(A\) तथा \(B\) पर क्रमशः \(+6\,\mathrm{µC}\) और \(-2\,\mathrm{µC}\) आवेश हैं। जब वे \(d\) दूरी पर होते हैं, तो उनके बीच \(F\) परिमाण का आकर्षण बल लगता है। उन्हें परस्पर स्पर्श कराकर पुनः \(d/2\) दूरी पर रख दिया जाता है। अब उनके बीच लगने वाले बल का परिमाण और प्रकृति होगी:

  • (A) \(\frac{4}{3}F\), प्रतिकर्षी
  • (B) \(\frac{4}{3}F\), आकर्षी
  • (C) \(\frac{3}{4}F\), प्रतिकर्षी
  • (D) \(\frac{1}{3}F\), प्रतिकर्षी
सही उत्तर: (A)

व्याख्या सहित हल: प्रारंभ में \(F = \frac{k(6)(2)}{d^2} = \frac{12k}{d^2}\)। स्पर्श कराने पर कुल आवेश \(+4\,\mathrm{µC}\) बराबर बँटकर प्रत्येक गोले पर \(+2\,\mathrm{µC}\) हो जाता है। \(d/2\) दूरी पर नया बल \(F' = \frac{k(2)(2)}{(d/2)^2} = \frac{16k}{d^2} = \frac{4}{3}F\) होगा तथा समान आवेश होने से यह प्रतिकर्षी होगा।

Practice Question 3 (English — Single-Correct MCQ)

Two point charges \(+9q\) and \(-q\) are fixed on the \(x\)-axis at \(x = 0\) and \(x = d\), respectively. At what coordinate on the \(x\)-axis is the net electric field zero?

  • (A) \(x = 0.75d\)
  • (B) \(x = 1.5d\)
  • (C) \(x = -0.5d\)
  • (D) \(x = 3.0d\)
Correct Answer: (B)

Worked Solution: Between \(0\) and \(d\), both fields point toward \(+x\), so they cannot cancel. For \(x < 0\), \(+9q\) is both larger and closer, so it dominates. For \(x > d\), the fields oppose; setting magnitudes equal: \(\frac{9q}{x^2} = \frac{q}{(x-d)^2} \implies \frac{3}{x} = \frac{1}{x-d} \implies 3x - 3d = x \implies x = 1.5d\).

  • (A) \(x = 0.75d\)
  • (B) \(x = 1.5d\)
  • (C) \(x = -0.5d\)
  • (D) \(x = 3.0d\)
सही उत्तर: (B)

व्याख्या सहित हल: \(0\) से \(d\) के बीच दोनों क्षेत्र एक ही दिशा में हैं। \(x < 0\) पर \(+9q\) निकट व बड़ा होने के कारण प्रबल रहेगा। अतः शून्य बिंदु \(x > d\) पर होगा। परिमाण बराबर करने पर: \(\frac{9q}{x^2} = \frac{q}{(x-d)^2} \implies \frac{3}{x} = \frac{1}{x-d} \implies x = 1.5d\)।

Practice Question 4 (English — Conceptual MCQ)

Three particles enter a uniform downward electric field (along \(-y\)) with identical horizontal velocities along \(+x\). Particle 1 curves upward, Particle 2 moves straight without deflection, and Particle 3 curves downward with a larger vertical displacement than Particle 1 over the same horizontal span. Which conclusion is correct?

  • (A) Particle 1 is positive, Particle 2 is neutral, and Particle 3 has the smallest \(|q|/m\) ratio.
  • (B) Particle 1 is negative, Particle 2 is neutral, and Particle 3 is positive with a larger \(|q|/m\) ratio than Particle 1.
  • (C) Particle 1 is negative, Particle 2 is positive, and Particle 3 is neutral.
  • (D) Both Particles 1 and 3 are positive, with Particle 1 having a larger \(|q|/m\) ratio.
Correct Answer: (B)

Worked Solution: A downward field pushes positive charges downward (\(-y\)) and negative charges upward (\(+y\)). Thus, Particle 1 is negative, Particle 2 is uncharged (neutral), and Particle 3 is positive. Vertical deflection is \(y = \frac{1}{2}\left(\frac{|q|E}{m}\right)t^2\), so greater deflection for the same transit time \(t\) implies a higher charge-to-mass ratio \(|q|/m\).

  • (A) कण 1 धनात्मक है, कण 2 उदासीन है, तथा कण 3 का \(|q|/m\) अनुपात न्यूनतम है।
  • (B) कण 1 ऋणात्मक है, कण 2 उदासीन है, तथा कण 3 धनात्मक है जिसका \(|q|/m\) अनुपात कण 1 से अधिक है।
  • (C) कण 1 ऋणात्मक है, कण 2 धनात्मक है, तथा कण 3 उदासीन है।
  • (D) कण 1 और 3 दोनों धनात्मक हैं, तथा कण 1 का \(|q|/m\) अनुपात अधिक है।

Practice Question 7 (English — Assertion–Reason MCQ)

Reason (R): Any concentric spherical Gaussian surface drawn inside the shell encloses zero net charge, and by spherical symmetry the electric field has uniform radial magnitude over that surface.

  • (A) Both A and R are true, and R is the correct explanation of A.
  • (B) Both A and R are true, but R is NOT the correct explanation of A.

दो बड़ी, पतली समांतर कुचालक चादरों पर एकसमान पृष्ठीय आवेश घनत्व क्रमशः \(+3\sigma\) तथा \(-\sigma\) (\(\sigma > 0\)) हैं। दोनों चादरों के बीच के क्षेत्र में विद्युत क्षेत्र का परिमाण कितना होगा?

  • (A) \(\frac{\sigma}{\varepsilon_0}\)
  • (B) \(\frac{2\sigma}{\varepsilon_0}\)
  • (C) \(\frac{\sigma}{2\varepsilon_0}\)
  • (D) \(\frac{4\sigma}{\varepsilon_0}\)
सही उत्तर: (B)

व्याख्या सहित हल: दोनों चादरों के बीच \(+3\sigma\) चादर का क्षेत्र (\(\frac{3\sigma}{2\varepsilon_0}\)) इससे दूर तथा \(-\sigma\) चादर का क्षेत्र (\(\frac{\sigma}{2\varepsilon_0}\)) इसकी ओर अर्थात् एक ही दिशा में होते हैं। अतः परिणामी क्षेत्र \(E = \frac{3\sigma}{2\varepsilon_0} + \frac{\sigma}{2\varepsilon_0} = \frac{2\sigma}{\varepsilon_0}\)।

8. Common Pitfalls, Revision Checklist & Final Recap

English

Common Pitfalls to Avoid in NEET UG:

  • Zero net flux \(\neq\) zero enclosed charge distribution: \(\phi_E = 0\) means \(q_{\text{enc}} = 0\) (net charge is zero), not necessarily that there are no charges inside (e.g., a dipole inside a closed surface gives \(\phi_E = 0\)).
  • Dipole vs. Point Charge Decay: Point charge field falls off as \(1/r^2\), whereas far-field dipole intensity falls off as \(1/r^3\) and infinite line charge falls off as \(1/r\).
  • Sheet vs. Conducting Plate: A single thin non-conducting sheet has \(E = \sigma/(2\varepsilon_0)\), whereas just outside a charged conductor surface the field is \(\sigma/\varepsilon_0\) (only one side or surface-charge contribution).

Final Recap & Revision Checklist:

  • Charge is scalar, additive, conserved, and quantised (\(q = ne\)).
  • Coulomb's law + superposition gives forces and fields for any discrete system.
  • Dipole moment \(\mathbf{p}\) points from \(-q\) to \(+q\); torque \(\boldsymbol{\tau} = \mathbf{p} \times \mathbf{E}\).
  • Gauss's law \(\oint \mathbf{E}\cdot d\mathbf{S} = q_{\text{enc}}/\varepsilon_0\) simplifies field calculations whenever spherical, cylindrical, or planar symmetry exists.
हिन्दी (Hindi)

NEET UG में बचने योग्य सामान्य त्रुटियाँ:

  • शून्य फ्लक्स का अर्थ आवेशों की अनुपस्थिति नहीं है: \(\phi_E = 0\) का अर्थ केवल यह है कि परिबद्ध नेट आवेश शून्य है (जैसे बंद पृष्ठ के भीतर एक विद्युत द्विध्रुव)।
  • दूरी पर निर्भरता का अंतर: बिंदु आवेश के लिए \(E \propto 1/r^2\), लघु द्विध्रुव के लिए \(E \propto 1/r^3\), अनंत तार के लिए \(E \propto 1/r\), तथा अनंत समतल चादर के लिए \(E\) दूरी से स्वतंत्र होता है।
  • क्षेत्र रेखाओं की दिशा: द्विध्रुव के अक्षीय बिंदु पर क्षेत्र \(\mathbf{p}\) की दिशा में, जबकि विषुवतीय बिंदु पर \(\mathbf{p}\) के विपरीत दिशा में होता है।

त्वरित पुनरावृत्ति सारांश (Final Recap):

  • आवेश अदिश, योज्य, संरक्षित तथा क्वांटमीकृत (\(q = ne\)) होता है।
  • कूलॉम का नियम और अध्यारोपण सिद्धांत सभी स्थिरवैद्युत बलों व क्षेत्रों का आधार हैं।
  • द्विध्रुव आघूर्ण \(\mathbf{p}\) की दिशा \(-q\) से \(+q\) होती है; एकसमान क्षेत्र में बल-आघूर्ण \(\boldsymbol{\tau} = \mathbf{p} \times \mathbf{E}\)।
  • गॉस का नियम \(\oint \mathbf{E}\cdot d\mathbf{S} = q_{\text{enc}}/\varepsilon_0\) सममित आवेश वितरणों के लिए सबसे प्रभावी उपकरण है।
