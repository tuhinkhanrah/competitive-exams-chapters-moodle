1. Learning Objectives & Chapter Overview | अधिगम उद्देश्य एवं अध्याय अवलोकन

English

Scope for NEET UG: Organohalogen compounds are formed when one or more hydrogen atoms in an aliphatic or aromatic hydrocarbon are replaced by halogen atoms (\(\mathrm{F}, \mathrm{Cl}, \mathrm{Br}, \mathrm{I}\)). They serve as vital synthetic intermediates, industrial solvents, and biologically active agents—such as the thyroid hormone thyroxine (iodine-containing), the antibiotic chloramphenicol, the antimalarial drug chloroquine, and the surgical anaesthetic halothane.

After completing this tutorial, you will be able to:

  • Classify organohalides by halogen count and carbon hybridisation (\(sp^3\) vs. \(sp^2\)), and assign accurate IUPAC names.
  • Explain periodic trends in \(\mathrm{C-X}\) bond length, bond enthalpy, dipole moment, boiling point, melting point, and density.
  • Select optimal synthetic routes for haloalkanes and haloarenes, including halogen-exchange and diazonium reactions.
  • Distinguish \(\mathrm{S_N1}\), \(\mathrm{S_N2}\), and \(\beta\)-elimination mechanisms using kinetics, carbocation stability, steric hindrance, and stereochemistry.
  • Rationalise the low nucleophilic reactivity of haloarenes and their ortho/para-directing yet deactivating behaviour in electrophilic substitution.
  • Evaluate environmental and physiological hazards of polyhalogenated compounds.

  • \(\mathrm{C-X}\) आबंध लंबाई, आबंध एन्थैल्पी, द्विध्रुव आघूर्ण, क्वथनांक, गलनांक और घनत्व की आवर्ती प्रवृत्तियों को समझाने में।
  • हैलोजन विनिमय और डाइऐजोनियम अभिक्रियाओं सहित हैलोऐल्केन तथा हैलोऐरीन के निर्माण की श्रेष्ठ विधियाँ चुनने में।

  • हैलोऐरीन की नाभिकरागी प्रतिस्थापन के प्रति कम क्रियाशीलता तथा इलेक्ट्रॉनरागी प्रतिस्थापन में उनके ऑर्थो/पैरा-निर्देशक परंतु विसक्रियणकारी व्यवहार को स्पष्ट करने में।
  • पॉलिहैलोजन यौगिकों के पर्यावरणीय एवं शारीरिक दुष्प्रभावों का मूल्यांकन करने में।

2. Classification & IUPAC Nomenclature | वर्गीकरण एवं IUPAC नामकरण

English

A. Classification by Halogen Count and Carbon Hybridisation

  • By Number of Halogens: Monohalo (1 halogen), dihalo (2 halogens), and polyhalo (3 or more halogens) alkanes or arenes.
  • Compounds with \(sp^3\;\mathrm{C-X}\) Bond:
    • Alkyl halides (Haloalkanes, \(\mathrm{C_nH_{2n+1}X}\)): Classified as primary (\(1^\circ\)), secondary (\(2^\circ\)), or tertiary (\(3^\circ\)) depending on whether the halogen-bearing carbon is attached to one, two, or three other carbons.
    • Allylic halides: Halogen is bonded to an \(sp^3\) carbon adjacent to a \(\mathrm{C=C}\) double bond (e.g., \(\mathrm{CH_2=CH-CH_2Br}\)).
    • Benzylic halides: Halogen is bonded to an \(sp^3\) carbon directly attached to an aromatic ring (e.g., \(\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2Cl}\)).
  • Compounds with \(sp^2\;\mathrm{C-X}\) Bond:
    • Vinylic halides: Halogen is directly bonded to an \(sp^2\) carbon of an alkene (\(\mathrm{CH_2=CH-Cl}\)).
    • Aryl halides (Haloarenes): Halogen is directly bonded to an \(sp^2\) carbon of an aromatic ring (\(\mathrm{C_6H_5-Br}\)).

B. Geminal vs. Vicinal Dihalides & Nomenclature

Dihalides with identical halogens on the same carbon are geminal (gem)-dihalides (common name: alkylidene halides, e.g., \(\mathrm{CH_3CHCl_2}\), ethylidene chloride / 1,1-dichloroethane). When halogens are on adjacent carbons, they are vicinal (vic)-dihalides (common name: alkylene dihalides, e.g., \(\mathrm{ClCH_2CH_2Cl}\), ethylene dichloride / 1,2-dichloroethane).

In IUPAC nomenclature, halo-substituents are treated as prefixes with equal priority to alkyl groups; numbering gives the lowest locant set, and substituents are cited alphabetically. For disubstituted benzenes, common names use o-, m-, p-, whereas IUPAC uses 1,2-, 1,3-, and 1,4-.

3. Nature of the C–X Bond & Physical Properties | C–X आबंध की प्रकृति एवं भौतिक गुणधर्म

English

Because halogens are more electronegative than carbon, the \(\mathrm{C^{\delta+}-X^{\delta-}}\) bond is polar. Moving down Group 17 from fluorine to iodine, atomic size increases sharply:

  • Bond Length: Increases from \(\mathrm{C-F}\) (\(139\,\mathrm{pm}\)) < \(\mathrm{C-Cl}\) (\(178\,\mathrm{pm}\)) < \(\mathrm{C-Br}\) (\(193\,\mathrm{pm}\)) < \(\mathrm{C-I}\) (\(214\,\mathrm{pm}\)).
  • Bond Dissociation Enthalpy: Decreases down the group: \(\mathrm{C-F}\) (\(452\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)) > \(\mathrm{C-Cl}\) (\(351\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)) > \(\mathrm{C-Br}\) (\(293\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)) > \(\mathrm{C-I}\) (\(234\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)).
  • Dipole Moment (\(\mu\)) Anomaly: Although \(\mathrm{F}\) is more electronegative than \(\mathrm{Cl}\), the \(\mathrm{C-F}\) bond is much shorter than \(\mathrm{C-Cl}\). Since dipole moment \(\mu = q \times d\), the larger bond distance in \(\mathrm{CH_3Cl}\) outweighs the slightly smaller charge, giving the NEET-favourite order: \[\mathrm{CH_3Cl\,(1.860\,D) > CH_3F\,(1.847\,D) > CH_3Br\,(1.830\,D) > CH_3I\,(1.636\,D)}\]

Boiling & Melting Points: Haloalkanes have higher boiling points than parent alkanes of comparable molar mass due to stronger dipole–dipole and van der Waals forces. For a fixed alkyl group: \(\mathrm{RI > RBr > RCl > RF}\). Among isomers, branching lowers boiling point because spherical molecules have smaller surface area and weaker van der Waals attractions. For isomeric dihalobenzenes, boiling points are nearly identical, but the para-isomer has a significantly higher melting point than ortho and meta isomers because its symmetry allows tighter packing in the crystal lattice.

Density & Solubility: Bromo, iodo, and polychloro derivatives are denser than water (\(>1\,\mathrm{g\,mL^{-1}}\)); density rises with carbon count, halogen count, and halogen atomic mass. Haloalkanes are only sparingly soluble in water because the energy released by new dipole–water interactions cannot compensate for breaking strong hydrogen bonds between water molecules.

हैलोजन की विद्युतऋणात्मकता कार्बन से अधिक होने के कारण \(\mathrm{C^{\delta+}-X^{\delta-}}\) आबंध ध्रुवीय होता है। वर्ग 17 में फ्लुओरीन से आयोडीन की ओर नीचे जाने पर परमाणु आकार तेजी से बढ़ता है:

  • आबंध लंबाई: \(\mathrm{C-F}\) (\(139\,\mathrm{pm}\)) < \(\mathrm{C-Cl}\) (\(178\,\mathrm{pm}\)) < \(\mathrm{C-Br}\) (\(193\,\mathrm{pm}\)) < \(\mathrm{C-I}\) (\(214\,\mathrm{pm}\)) के क्रम में बढ़ती है।
  • आबंध वियोजन एन्थैल्पी: नीचे जाने पर घटती है: \(\mathrm{C-F}\) (\(452\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)) > \(\mathrm{C-Cl}\) (\(351\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)) > \(\mathrm{C-Br}\) (\(293\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\)) > \(\mathrm{C-I}\) (\(234\,\mathrm{kJ\,mol^{-1}}\))।
  • द्विध्रुव आघूर्ण (\(\mu\)) अपवाद: यद्यपि \(\mathrm{F}\) की विद्युतऋणात्मकता \(\mathrm{Cl}\) से अधिक है, परंतु \(\mathrm{C-F}\) आबंध लंबाई \(\mathrm{C-Cl}\) से बहुत कम होती है। चूँकि \(\mu = q \times d\), अतः \(\mathrm{CH_3Cl}\) में बड़ी आबंध दूरी प्रभावी हो जाती है। सही क्रम (NEET के लिए अत्यंत महत्वपूर्ण): \[\mathrm{CH_3Cl\,(1.860\,D) > CH_3F\,(1.847\,D) > CH_3Br\,(1.830\,D) > CH_3I\,(1.636\,D)}\]

क्वथनांक एवं गलनांक: प्रबल द्विध्रुव–द्विध्रुव तथा वान डर वाल्स बलों के कारण हैलोऐल्केन के क्वथनांक समान अणुभार वाले हाइड्रोकार्बन से अधिक होते हैं। समान ऐल्किल समूह के लिए: \(\mathrm{RI > RBr > RCl > RF}\)। समावयवियों में शाखन (branching) बढ़ने पर क्वथनांक घटता है क्योंकि गोलाकार आकृति के कारण पृष्ठीय क्षेत्रफल कम हो जाता है। समावयवी डाइहैलोबेंजीन के क्वथनांक लगभग समान होते हैं, परंतु पैरा-समावयवी का गलनांक ऑर्थो व मेटा से काफी अधिक होता है क्योंकि इसकी सममिति क्रिस्टल जालक में सघन संकुलन (tight packing) प्रदान करती है।

घनत्व एवं विलेयता: ब्रोमो, आयोडो तथा पॉलिक्लोरो व्युत्पन्न जल से भारी होते हैं (\(>1\,\mathrm{g\,mL^{-1}}\))। हैलोऐल्केन जल में अल्प विलेय होते हैं क्योंकि जल के प्रबल हाइड्रोजन आबंधों को तोड़ने के लिए आवश्यक ऊर्जा, नए हैलोऐल्केन–जल आकर्षणों से मुक्त ऊर्जा से अधिक होती है।

4. Synthetic Methods: Preparation of Haloalkanes & Haloarenes | विरचन की विधियाँ

English

A. Preparation of Haloalkanes

  1. From Alcohols (\(\mathrm{R-OH}\)):
    • Thionyl Chloride (\(\mathrm{SOCl_2}\)): \(\mathrm{R-OH + SOCl_2 \rightarrow R-Cl + SO_2\uparrow + HCl\uparrow}\). Preferred method because both by-products are gases that escape, leaving pure alkyl chloride.
    • Hydrogen Halides (\(\mathrm{HX}\)): \(1^\circ\) and \(2^\circ\) alcohols require anhydrous \(\mathrm{ZnCl_2}\) catalyst with conc. \(\mathrm{HCl}\), while \(3^\circ\) alcohols react rapidly at room temperature. Reactivity order of alcohols: \(3^\circ > 2^\circ > 1^\circ\).
    • Phosphorus Halides: \(\mathrm{PX_3}\) (\(\mathrm{PBr_3, PI_3}\) generated in situ from red \(\mathrm{P + X_2}\)) and \(\mathrm{PCl_5}\) convert alcohols to \(\mathrm{R-X}\).
    • Why \(\mathrm{H_2SO_4}\) is avoided with \(\mathrm{KI}\): \(\mathrm{H_2SO_4}\) oxidises the \(\mathrm{HI}\) formed into \(\mathrm{I_2}\); hence \(95\%\;\mathrm{H_3PO_4}\) is used instead.
    • Limitation: Phenols do not form aryl halides by these routes because the \(\mathrm{C-O}\) bond in phenol has partial double-bond character.
  2. From Hydrocarbons: Free-radical halogenation of alkanes (\(\mathrm{Cl_2}/h\nu\)) yields hard-to-separate mixtures. Addition of \(\mathrm{HX}\) to unsymmetrical alkenes follows Markovnikov's rule (major product via more stable carbocation). Addition of \(\mathrm{Br_2/CCl_4}\) to alkenes discharges reddish-brown colour, forming colourless vic-dibromides (unsaturation test).
  3. Halogen Exchange:
    • Finkelstein Reaction (for \(\mathrm{R-I}\)): \(\mathrm{R-Cl/Br + NaI \xrightarrow{\text{dry acetone}} R-I + NaCl/NaBr\downarrow}\). Precipitated \(\mathrm{NaCl/NaBr}\) drives the equilibrium forward (Le Chatelier's principle).
    • Swarts Reaction (for \(\mathrm{R-F}\)): Heating \(\mathrm{R-Cl/Br}\) with metallic fluorides such as \(\mathrm{AgF, Hg_2F_2, CoF_2,}\) or \(\mathrm{SbF_3}\).

B. Preparation of Haloarenes

  • Electrophilic Aromatic Substitution: Arenes react with \(\mathrm{Cl_2}\) or \(\mathrm{Br_2}\) in the dark using a Lewis acid catalyst (\(\mathrm{Fe}\) or \(\mathrm{FeCl_3}\)) to give o- and p-haloarenes (easily separated by melting point difference). Iodination is reversible and requires an oxidising agent (\(\mathrm{HNO_3}\) or \(\mathrm{HIO_4}\)) to consume \(\mathrm{HI}\). Direct fluorination is too violent.
  • Sandmeyer's Reaction (from Primary Aromatic Amines): Aniline with \(\mathrm{NaNO_2 + HX}\) at \(273\text{–}278\,\mathrm{K}\) forms benzene diazonium halide, which reacts with \(\mathrm{Cu_2Cl_2}\) or \(\mathrm{Cu_2Br_2}\) to yield aryl chloride/bromide + \(\mathrm{N_2}\). For aryl iodide, simply warm/shake the diazonium salt with aqueous \(\mathrm{KI}\) (no \(\mathrm{Cu_2X_2}\) needed).

5. Haloalkane Reactions: SN1, SN2, Stereochemistry, Elimination & Metals | हैलोऐल्केन की अभिक्रियाएँ

English

A. Nucleophilic Substitution (\(\mathrm{S_N2}\) vs. \(\mathrm{S_N1}\))

In nucleophilic substitution, an electron-rich nucleophile (\(\mathrm{Nu^-}\)) replaces the leaving halide ion (\(\mathrm{X^-}\)). Leaving group ability follows \(\mathrm{R-I > R-Br > R-Cl \gg R-F}\).

Ambident Nucleophiles: Species with two nucleophilic centres, such as \(\mathrm{CN^-}\) and \(\mathrm{NO_2^-}\). Ionic \(\mathrm{KCN}\) attacks through carbon to give alkyl cyanides (nitriles, \(\mathrm{R-CN}\)) because \(\mathrm{C-C}\) is stronger than \(\mathrm{C-N}\); covalent \(\mathrm{AgCN}\) has carbon bonded to \(\mathrm{Ag}\), so nitrogen lone pair attacks to form isocyanides (\(\mathrm{R-NC}\)). Similarly, \(\mathrm{KNO_2}\) yields alkyl nitrites (\(\mathrm{R-O-N=O}\)), whereas \(\mathrm{AgNO_2}\) yields nitroalkanes (\(\mathrm{R-NO_2}\)).

English

B. Key Stereochemical Terms

  • Chirality & Enantiomers: A carbon bonded to four different groups is an asymmetric carbon (stereocentre). Molecules non-superimposable on their mirror images are chiral and optically active; non-superimposable mirror-image isomers are enantiomers (equal and opposite optical rotation, identical b.p./m.p.).
  • Racemic Mixture: An equimolar (\(50:50\)) mixture of \((+)\) and \((-)\) enantiomers has zero net optical rotation (\((\pm)\) or \(dl\)).
  • Retention of Configuration: When no bond to the stereocentre is broken during reaction, spatial configuration is preserved (note: the sign of optical rotation may still change!).

C. \(\beta\)-Elimination (Dehydrohalogenation) & Organometallics

  • Zaitsev (Saytzeff) Rule: Heating a haloalkane having a \(\beta\)-hydrogen with alcoholic \(\mathrm{KOH}\) eliminates \(\mathrm{HX}\) to form an alkene. The more substituted alkene (more alkyl groups on \(\mathrm{C=C}\)) is the major product (e.g., 2-bromobutane gives but-2-ene as major product).
  • Substitution vs. Elimination: Aqueous \(\mathrm{KOH}\) favours substitution (alcohol), while alcoholic \(\mathrm{KOH}\) or a bulky base (like tert-butoxide) favours elimination (alkene).
  • Grignard Reagents (\(\mathrm{RMgX}\)): Prepared by \(\mathrm{R-X + Mg \xrightarrow{\text{dry ether}} R^{\delta-}-Mg^{\delta+}X}\). Because the \(\mathrm{C-Mg}\) bond is strongly polar, \(\mathrm{RMgX}\) acts as a powerful base/nucleophile and reacts instantly with any proton donor (\(\mathrm{H_2O}\), alcohols, amines) to yield hydrocarbons (\(\mathrm{RH}\)). Strictly anhydrous conditions are essential.
  • Wurtz Reaction: \(2\mathrm{R-X + 2Na \xrightarrow{\text{dry ether}} R-R + 2NaX}\) (doubles the carbon chain; best for symmetrical alkanes).

6. Reactivity of Haloarenes | हैलोऐरीन की अभिक्रियाशीलता

English

A. Why Haloarenes Resist Nucleophilic Substitution

  1. Resonance Effect: Lone pairs on halogen delocalise into the benzene ring, imparting partial double-bond character to \(\mathrm{C-X}\), making cleavage difficult.
  2. Carbon Hybridisation: The \(sp^2\) ring carbon has higher \(s\)-character and electronegativity than an \(sp^3\) alkyl carbon, holding electrons tightly. Thus, \(\mathrm{C-Cl}\) in chlorobenzene (\(169\,\mathrm{pm}\)) is shorter and stronger than in chloroalkane (\(177\,\mathrm{pm}\)).
  3. Instability of Phenyl Cation: Self-ionisation would form a phenyl cation that is not resonance-stabilised, ruling out \(\mathrm{S_N1}\).
  4. Electronic Repulsion: Electron-rich nucleophiles experience repulsion from the electron-rich \(\pi\)-cloud of the arene.

Activation by \(-\mathrm{NO_2}\): Chlorobenzene requires drastic conditions (\(\mathrm{NaOH}\), \(623\,\mathrm{K}\), \(300\,\mathrm{atm}\)) to form phenol. Introducing \(-\mathrm{NO_2}\) at ortho and/or para positions dramatically lowers the required temperature (4-nitrochlorobenzene: \(443\,\mathrm{K}\); 2,4-dinitrochlorobenzene: \(368\,\mathrm{K}\); 2,4,6-trinitrochlorobenzene: warm \(\mathrm{H_2O}\)) because \(-\mathrm{NO_2}\) resonance-stabilises the negative charge of the intermediate carbanion at o/p carbons. A meta-\(\mathrm{NO_2}\) group shows no activation because the negative charge never lands on the meta carbon in resonance structures.

B. Electrophilic Substitution & Metal Coupling

  • Orientation vs. Reactivity: Halogens are o,p-directing (resonance increases electron density at o/p and stabilises the arenium ion) yet ring-deactivating (stronger \(-I\) inductive withdrawal lowers overall ring reactivity compared to benzene). Thus, halogenation, nitration, sulphonation, and Friedel-Crafts reactions yield para (major) and ortho (minor) products.
  • Wurtz-Fittig & Fittig Reactions: \(\mathrm{Ar-X + R-X + 2Na \xrightarrow{\text{dry ether}} Ar-R + 2NaX}\) (Wurtz-Fittig); \(2\mathrm{Ar-X + 2Na \xrightarrow{\text{dry ether}} Ar-Ar\,(\text{biphenyl}) + 2NaX}\) (Fittig).

  • वुर्ट्ज़-फिटिग एवं फिटिग अभिक्रियाएँ: \(\mathrm{Ar-X + R-X + 2Na \xrightarrow{\text{शुष्क ईथर}} Ar-R + 2NaX}\) (वुर्ट्ज़-फिटिग); \(2\mathrm{Ar-X + 2Na \xrightarrow{\text{शुष्क ईथर}} Ar-Ar\,(\text{बाइफेनिल}) + 2NaX}\) (फिटिग)।

English

Many polyhalogenated compounds resist microbial degradation and pose serious health or environmental hazards:

  • Dichloromethane (\(\mathrm{CH_2Cl_2}\)): Paint remover and aerosol propellant; depresses the central nervous system (CNS), impairs hearing/vision, and causes skin/corneal burns.

Worked Example 1 (SN1 vs. SN2 Reactivity): Arrange the following bromides in increasing order of \(\mathrm{S_N1}\) reactivity and decreasing order of \(\mathrm{S_N2}\) reactivity: (I) 1-bromopropane, (II) 2-bromopropane, (III) 2-bromo-2-methylpropane, (IV) benzyl bromide.

Solution:

  • \(\mathrm{S_N1}\) rate depends on carbocation stability: \(1^\circ\;\mathrm{CH_3CH_2CH_2^+} < 2^\circ\;\mathrm{(CH_3)_2CH^+} < \text{benzylic}\;\mathrm{C_6H_5CH_2^+} \approx 3^\circ\;\mathrm{(CH_3)_3C^+}\) (note: \(3^\circ\) alkyl carbocation with 9 \(\alpha\)-H hyperconjugation is comparable to or slightly more stable than \(1^\circ\) benzylic in solvolysis, and both are far faster than simple \(2^\circ\) and \(1^\circ\) alkyl halides). Thus, increasing \(\mathrm{S_N1}\) order: (I) < (II) < (IV) < (III).
  • \(\mathrm{S_N2}\) rate depends on steric hindrance at the \(\alpha\)-carbon and transition-state stabilisation: unhindered \(1^\circ\) substrates react fastest, whereas \(3^\circ\) substrates are virtually unreactive. Benzyl bromide (\(1^\circ\), resonance-stabilised TS) reacts faster than simple \(1^\circ\) alkyl halides. Thus, decreasing \(\mathrm{S_N2}\) order: (IV) > (I) > (II) > (III).

Worked Example 2 (Elimination & Synthetic Sequence): An optically active secondary alkyl bromide \(\mathrm{C_5H_{11}Br}\) (assumed isomer: 2-bromopentane) is heated with alcoholic \(\mathrm{KOH}\) to give major alkene X. Solution:

10. Common Pitfalls & Revision Checklist | सामान्य त्रुटियाँ एवं पुनरावृत्ति जाँच-सूची

English
High-Frequency NEET Traps to Avoid:
  • Assuming \(\mathrm{CH_3F}\) has a higher dipole moment than \(\mathrm{CH_3Cl}\) — False! \(\mathrm{CH_3Cl > CH_3F}\) due to larger \(\mathrm{C-Cl}\) bond length.
  • Thinking \((+)\) configuration always stays \((+)\) on retention — False! Retention preserves 3D spatial arrangement, not necessarily the optical rotation sign.
  • Using \(\mathrm{H_2SO_4}\) with \(\mathrm{KI}\) to make \(\mathrm{R-I}\) from alcohols — Wrong! \(\mathrm{H_2SO_4}\) oxidises \(\mathrm{HI}\) to \(\mathrm{I_2}\); use \(95\%\;\mathrm{H_3PO_4}\).
  • Expecting meta-\(\mathrm{NO_2}\) to activate nucleophilic substitution in chlorobenzene — Wrong! Only ortho and para \(-\mathrm{NO_2}\) stabilise the carbanion by resonance.
  • Checklist: Can I distinguish \(sp^3\) (alkyl, allylic, benzylic) vs. \(sp^2\) (vinylic, aryl) halides?
  • Checklist: Do I know the exact reagent outcomes for \(\mathrm{KCN}\) vs. \(\mathrm{AgCN}\) and \(\mathrm{KNO_2}\) vs. \(\mathrm{AgNO_2}\)?
  • Checklist: Can I predict \(\mathrm{S_N1}\) vs. \(\mathrm{S_N2}\) vs. Zaitsev \(\beta\)-elimination products?

11. Concise Final Recap | संक्षिप्त अंतिम पुनरावृत्ति

English

Haloalkanes (\(sp^3\;\mathrm{C-X}\)) undergo facile nucleophilic substitution (\(\mathrm{S_N2}\): \(1^\circ\) fastest, 100% inversion; \(\mathrm{S_N1}\): \(3^\circ\)/allylic/benzylic fastest, racemisation), Zaitsev \(\beta\)-elimination with alcoholic \(\mathrm{KOH}\), and form moisture-sensitive Grignard reagents (\(\mathrm{RMgX}\)) in dry ether. Haloarenes (\(sp^2\;\mathrm{C-X}\)) resist nucleophilic substitution due to resonance, shorter \(\mathrm{C-X}\) bonds, and unstable phenyl cations unless activated by o/p-\(\mathrm{NO_2}\) groups, while halogens act as o,p-directing yet deactivating substituents in electrophilic aromatic substitution.

हैलोऐल्केन (\(sp^3\;\mathrm{C-X}\)) सुगमता से नाभिकरागी प्रतिस्थापन (\(\mathrm{S_N2}\): \(1^\circ\) तीव्रतम, 100% प्रतिलोमन; \(\mathrm{S_N1}\): \(3^\circ\)/ऐलिलिक/बेंजिलिक तीव्रतम, रेसिमीकरण), ऐल्कोहॉलीय \(\mathrm{KOH}\) के साथ जैत्सेफ \(\beta\)-विलोपन तथा शुष्क ईथर में नमी-संवेदनशील ग्रिन्यार अभिकर्मक (\(\mathrm{RMgX}\)) देते हैं। हैलोऐरीन (\(sp^2\;\mathrm{C-X}\)) अनुनाद, छोटे \(\mathrm{C-X}\) आबंध तथा अस्थायी फेनिल धनायन के कारण नाभिकरागी प्रतिस्थापन का विरोध करते हैं (जब तक o/p-\(\mathrm{NO_2}\) द्वारा सक्रिय न हों), जबकि इलेक्ट्रॉनरागी ऐरोमैटिक प्रतिस्थापन में हैलोजन ऑर्थो, पैरा-निर्देशक परंतु विसक्रियणकारी होते हैं।
