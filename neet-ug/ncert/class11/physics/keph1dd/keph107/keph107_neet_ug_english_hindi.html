1. Learning Objectives & Chapter Overview / शिक्षण उद्देश्य एवं अध्याय अवलोकन

English

Gravitation governs both everyday terrestrial phenomena—such as objects falling toward the Earth—and large-scale celestial dynamics, including planetary orbits and artificial satellites. For NEET UG, this chapter is a high-scoring unit because most questions test direct formula relationships, proportional reasoning, and conservation laws.

By the end of this tutorial, you will be able to:

  • Apply Newton's Universal Law of Gravitation in scalar and vector forms, along with the principle of superposition.
  • Use spherical shell theorems to evaluate gravitational force inside and outside uniform spherical mass distributions.
  • Calculate how the acceleration due to gravity \(g\) varies with altitude \(h\) above the surface and depth \(d\) below the surface.
  • Compute gravitational field intensity, gravitational potential, and gravitational potential energy for point masses and discrete systems.
  • Relate Kepler's three laws of planetary motion to angular momentum conservation and inverse-square central forces.
  • Determine escape speed, orbital speed, time period, and mechanical energy components of orbiting satellites.

2. Universal Law of Gravitation & Spherical Shells / सार्वत्रिक गुरुत्वाकर्षण नियम एवं गोलीय कोश

English

Every particle in the universe attracts every other particle with a force that acts along the line joining them. This interaction is always attractive, forms an action–reaction pair, and is independent of the intervening medium.

Scalar Magnitude:

\[ F = \frac{G m_1 m_2}{r^2} \]

Vector Form (force on mass \(m_2\) due to \(m_1\)):

\[ \mathbf{F}_{21} = -\frac{G m_1 m_2}{r^2}\,\hat{\mathbf{r}}_{21} = -\frac{G m_1 m_2}{|\mathbf{r}|^3}\,\mathbf{r} \]

where \(\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{r}_2 - \mathbf{r}_1\) points from \(m_1\) to \(m_2\), and \(G = 6.67 \times 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2\cdot kg^{-2}}\) is the Universal Gravitational Constant (dimensions: \([\mathrm{M^{-1}L^3T^{-2}}]\)).

Principle of Superposition

When multiple point masses \(m_1, m_2, \dots, m_n\) exert gravitational forces on a test mass \(m_0\), the net force is the vector sum of the individual forces:

\[ \mathbf{F}_{\text{net}} = \mathbf{F}_{01} + \mathbf{F}_{02} + \dots + \mathbf{F}_{0n} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \mathbf{F}_{0i} \]

Gravitational Force Due to a Uniform Spherical Shell

  • Point outside the shell (\(r > R\)): The entire mass \(M\) of the shell behaves as if it is concentrated at its geometric centre: \(F = \frac{GMm}{r^2}\).
  • Point inside the shell (\(r < R\)): Gravitational forces from all surface elements cancel out vectorially at every interior point, so \(F_{\text{inside}} = 0\).
Important Fact (No Gravitational Shielding): Although a hollow spherical shell exerts zero net force on a particle inside it, it does not block or shield the particle from the gravitational pull of external bodies outside the shell.
हिन्दी

ब्रह्मांड में प्रत्येक कण दूसरे कण को उनके केंद्रों को मिलाने वाली रेखा के अनुदिश आकर्षित करता है। यह अन्योन्यक्रिया सदैव आकर्षक (attractive) होती है, क्रिया–प्रतिक्रिया युग्म बनाती है, और दोनों कणों के बीच स्थित माध्यम पर निर्भर नहीं करती।

अदिश परिमाण:

\[ F = \frac{G m_1 m_2}{r^2} \]

सदिश रूप (द्रव्यमान \(m_1\) के कारण \(m_2\) पर बल):

\[ \mathbf{F}_{21} = -\frac{G m_1 m_2}{r^2}\,\hat{\mathbf{r}}_{21} = -\frac{G m_1 m_2}{|\mathbf{r}|^3}\,\mathbf{r} \]

जहाँ \(\mathbf{r} = \mathbf{r}_2 - \mathbf{r}_1\), \(m_1\) से \(m_2\) की ओर दिष्ट है, तथा \(G = 6.67 \times 10^{-11}\,\mathrm{N\cdot m^2\cdot kg^{-2}}\) सार्वत्रिक गुरुत्वाकर्षण नियतांक है (विमा: \([\mathrm{M^{-1}L^3T^{-2}}]\))।

अध्यारोपण का सिद्धांत (Principle of Superposition)

जब अनेक बिंदु द्रव्यमान \(m_1, m_2, \dots, m_n\) किसी परीक्षण द्रव्यमान \(m_0\) पर बल लगाते हैं, तो परिणामी बल सभी व्यक्तिगत बलों का सदिश योग होता है:

\[ \mathbf{F}_{\text{net}} = \mathbf{F}_{01} + \mathbf{F}_{02} + \dots + \mathbf{F}_{0n} = \sum_{i=1}^{n} \mathbf{F}_{0i} \]

एकसमान गोलीय कोश के कारण गुरुत्वाकर्षण बल

  • कोश के बाहर स्थित बिंदु (\(r > R\)): कोश का संपूर्ण द्रव्यमान \(M\) उसके केंद्र पर केंद्रित माना जा सकता है: \(F = \frac{GMm}{r^2}\)।
  • कोश के भीतर स्थित बिंदु (\(r < R\)): कोश के विभिन्न भागों द्वारा लगाए गए बल आपस में निरस्त हो जाते हैं, अतः भीतर किसी भी बिंदु पर \(F_{\text{inside}} = 0\) होता है।
महत्वपूर्ण तथ्य (गुरुत्वीय परिरक्षण असंभव): यद्यपि एक खोखला गोलीय कोश अपने भीतर रखे कण पर शून्य नेट बल लगाता है, फिर भी यह बाहर स्थित अन्य पिंडों के गुरुत्वाकर्षण प्रभाव को रोक नहीं सकता।

3. Acceleration Due to Gravity & Its Variations / गुरुत्वीय त्वरण एवं इसमें परिवर्तन

English

Modeling Earth as a uniform sphere of mass \(M_E\), radius \(R_E\), and mean density \(\rho\), the gravitational force on a mass \(m\) at the surface is \(mg = \frac{GM_E m}{R_E^2}\). Thus, surface gravity is:

\[ g = \frac{G M_E}{R_E^2} = \frac{4}{3}\pi G R_E \rho \approx 9.8\,\mathrm{m\cdot s^{-2}} \]

1. Variation with Altitude (Height \(h\) above the surface)

At height \(h\), the distance from Earth's centre is \(r = R_E + h\). Treating Earth's mass as concentrated at its centre:

\[ g(h) = \frac{G M_E}{(R_E + h)^2} = \frac{g}{\left(1 + \frac{h}{R_E}\right)^2} \]

For small altitudes (\(h \ll R_E\)), binomial expansion gives:

\[ g(h) \approx g\left(1 - \frac{2h}{R_E}\right) \]

2. Variation with Depth (Depth \(d\) below the surface)

At depth \(d\), the point mass is at distance \(r = R_E - d\) from the centre. The outer spherical shell of thickness \(d\) exerts zero force; only the inner solid sphere of radius \((R_E - d)\) contributes:

\[ g(d) = \frac{G M_E}{R_E^3}(R_E - d) = g\left(1 - \frac{d}{R_E}\right) \]

At the centre of the Earth (\(d = R_E\)), \(g = 0\).

हिन्दी

पृथ्वी को द्रव्यमान \(M_E\), त्रिज्या \(R_E\) और औसत घनत्व \(\rho\) वाला एकसमान ठोस गोला मानने पर, सतह पर स्थित द्रव्यमान \(m\) पर बल \(mg = \frac{GM_E m}{R_E^2}\) होता है। अतः सतह पर गुरुत्वीय त्वरण:

\[ g = \frac{G M_E}{R_E^2} = \frac{4}{3}\pi G R_E \rho \approx 9.8\,\mathrm{m\cdot s^{-2}} \]

1. ऊँचाई के साथ परिवर्तन (सतह से \(h\) ऊँचाई पर)

ऊँचाई \(h\) पर पृथ्वी के केंद्र से दूरी \(r = R_E + h\) होती है:

\[ g(h) = \frac{G M_E}{(R_E + h)^2} = \frac{g}{\left(1 + \frac{h}{R_E}\right)^2} \]

कम ऊँचाई (\(h \ll R_E\)) के लिए द्विपद प्रसार से:

\[ g(h) \approx g\left(1 - \frac{2h}{R_E}\right) \]

2. गहराई के साथ परिवर्तन (सतह से \(d\) गहराई पर)

गहराई \(d\) पर केंद्र से दूरी \(r = R_E - d\) होती है। \(d\) मोटाई का बाहरी गोलीय कोश कोई बल नहीं लगाता; केवल \((R_E - d)\) त्रिज्या का आंतरिक गोला ही बल लगाता है:

\[ g(d) = \frac{G M_E}{R_E^3}(R_E - d) = g\left(1 - \frac{d}{R_E}\right) \]

पृथ्वी के केंद्र (\(d = R_E\)) पर \(g = 0\) होता है।

ट्यूटोरियल आरेख 1: पृथ्वी के केंद्र से दूरी \(r\) के साथ गुरुत्वीय त्वरण \(g(r)\) का परिवर्तन।

4. Gravitational Field, Potential & Potential Energy / गुरुत्वीय क्षेत्र, विभव एवं स्थितिज ऊर्जा

English

Because the gravitational force is conservative, the work done in moving a mass between two points is path-independent and can be expressed as a change in potential energy. Taking zero potential energy at infinity (\(r \to \infty\)):

Physical Quantity Formula SI Unit Dimensions & Type
Gravitational Field Intensity (\(E\) or \(g\)) \(\mathbf{E} = -\frac{GM}{r^2}\hat{\mathbf{r}}\) \(\mathrm{m\cdot s^{-2}}\) or \(\mathrm{N\cdot kg^{-1}}\) \([\mathrm{LT^{-2}}]\), Vector
Gravitational Potential (\(V\)) \(V(r) = -\frac{GM}{r}\) \(\mathrm{J\cdot kg^{-1}}\) \([\mathrm{L^2T^{-2}}]\), Scalar
Gravitational Potential Energy (\(U\)) \(U(r) = -\frac{Gm_1 m_2}{r}\) \(\mathrm{J}\) \([\mathrm{ML^2T^{-2}}]\), Scalar
  • System of Particles: Total gravitational potential energy of a multi-particle system is the scalar sum over all unique pairs: \(U_{\text{total}} = \sum_{i < j} \left(-\frac{Gm_i m_j}{r_{ij}}\right)\). For \(N\) particles, there are \(\frac{N(N-1)}{2}\) pairs.
  • Change in Potential Energy near Earth's Surface: Raising a mass \(m\) from Earth's surface (\(r_1 = R_E\)) to height \(h\) (\(r_2 = R_E + h\)) gives the exact change: \[ \Delta U = U_2 - U_1 = GM_E m\left(\frac{1}{R_E} - \frac{1}{R_E + h}\right) = \frac{mgh}{1 + \frac{h}{R_E}} \] Only when \(h \ll R_E\) does this reduce to the familiar approximation \(\Delta U \approx mgh\).
हिन्दी

भौतिक राशि सूत्र SI मात्रक विमा एवं प्रकार
गुरुत्वीय क्षेत्र की तीव्रता (\(E\) या \(g\)) \(\mathbf{E} = -\frac{GM}{r^2}\hat{\mathbf{r}}\) \(\mathrm{m\cdot s^{-2}}\) या \(\mathrm{N\cdot kg^{-1}}\) \([\mathrm{LT^{-2}}]\), सदिश

5. Kepler's Laws of Planetary Motion / केपलर के ग्रहीय गति के नियम

English
  1. Law of Orbits: Every planet moves in an elliptical orbit with the Sun located at one of the two foci. The closest point to the Sun is perihelion (\(r_P\)) and the farthest point is aphelion (\(r_A\)). The semi-major axis is \(a = \frac{r_P + r_A}{2}\).
  2. Law of Areas: The line joining the Sun and a planet sweeps out equal areas in equal intervals of time: \[ \frac{dA}{dt} = \frac{L}{2m} = \text{constant} \] This is a direct consequence of the conservation of angular momentum (\(L = mvr\sin\theta = \text{constant}\)), which holds for any central force. Consequently, at perihelion and aphelion where \(\mathbf{v} \perp \mathbf{r}\): \(v_P r_P = v_A r_A\), so the planet moves fastest at perihelion and slowest at aphelion.
  3. Law of Periods: The square of the orbital time period \(T\) is proportional to the cube of the semi-major axis \(a\): \[ T^2 = \left(\frac{4\pi^2}{GM_S}\right) a^3 \implies T^2 \propto a^3 \]

हिन्दी
  1. कक्षाओं का नियम: प्रत्येक ग्रह सूर्य के चारों ओर एक दीर्घवृत्ताकार कक्षा में परिक्रमा करता है, और सूर्य इस दीर्घवृत्त की दो नाभियों (foci) में से एक पर स्थित होता है। सूर्य से निकटतम बिंदु को उपसौर (perihelion, \(r_P\)) तथा दूरस्थ बिंदु को अपसौर (aphelion, \(r_A\)) कहते हैं। अर्ध-दीर्घ अक्ष \(a = \frac{r_P + r_A}{2}\) होता है।
  2. क्षेत्रफल का नियम: सूर्य से ग्रह को मिलाने वाली रेखा समान समय अंतरालों में समान क्षेत्रफल पार करती है: \[ \frac{dA}{dt} = \frac{L}{2m} = \text{नियतांक} \] यह नियम कोणीय संवेग संरक्षण (\(L = mvr\sin\theta = \text{नियतांक}\)) का सीधा परिणाम है, जो किसी भी केंद्रीय बल के लिए मान्य है। उपसौर और अपसौर पर \(v_P r_P = v_A r_A\) होता है; अतः ग्रह की चाल उपसौर पर अधिकतम और अपसौर पर न्यूनतम होती है।
  3. आवर्तकाल का नियम: किसी ग्रह के परिक्रमण काल \(T\) का वर्ग उसकी कक्षा के अर्ध-दीर्घ अक्ष \(a\) के घन के अनुक्रमानुपाती होता है: \[ T^2 = \left(\frac{4\pi^2}{GM_S}\right) a^3 \implies T^2 \propto a^3 \]
ट्यूटोरियल आरेख 2: दीर्घवृत्ताकार ग्रहीय कक्षा, नाभि पर सूर्य, उपसौर, अपसौर तथा पार किया गया क्षेत्रफल।

6. Escape Speed & Orbiting Satellites / पलायन वेग एवं परिक्रमा करते उपग्रह

English

Escape Speed (\(v_e\))

Escape speed is the minimum initial speed needed for a projectile launched from a planet's surface to reach infinity with zero residual speed (total mechanical energy \(E = 0\)):

\[ \frac{1}{2}m v_e^2 - \frac{GM_E m}{R_E} = 0 \implies v_e = \sqrt{\frac{2GM_E}{R_E}} = \sqrt{2g R_E} \approx 11.2\,\mathrm{km\cdot s^{-1}} \text{ (for Earth)} \]

Notice that \(v_e\) is independent of the mass of the projected body and the angle/direction of projection (neglecting air resistance). On the Moon, \(v_e \approx 2.3\,\mathrm{km\cdot s^{-1}}\), which is too low to retain atmospheric gases.

Circular Orbit of a Satellite (Radius \(r = R_E + h\))

Gravitational attraction provides the required centripetal force: \(\frac{mv_o^2}{r} = \frac{GM_E m}{r^2}\).

  • Orbital Speed: \(v_o = \sqrt{\frac{GM_E}{r}} = \sqrt{\frac{g R_E^2}{R_E + h}}\). Close to Earth's surface (\(h \ll R_E\)), \(v_o \approx \sqrt{g R_E} \approx 7.92\,\mathrm{km\cdot s^{-1}}\), so \(v_e = \sqrt{2}\,v_o\).
  • Time Period: \(T = \frac{2\pi r}{v_o} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{r^3}{GM_E}}\). Close to Earth's surface, \(T_0 = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{R_E}{g}} \approx 85\,\mathrm{minutes}\).
  • Energy of an Orbiting Satellite: \[ K = +\frac{GM_E m}{2r}, \qquad U = -\frac{GM_E m}{r}, \qquad E = K + U = -\frac{GM_E m}{2r} \] Thus, \(E = -K = \frac{U}{2}\). The negative total energy signifies a bound system.

Weightlessness in Orbit: An astronaut inside an orbiting satellite experiences apparent weightlessness not because gravity is zero, but because both the astronaut and the spacecraft are in continuous free fall toward Earth with the exact same centripetal acceleration \(g(h)\).

हिन्दी

पलायन वेग (\(v_e\))

पलायन वेग वह न्यूनतम प्रारंभिक चाल है जिससे किसी पिंड को ग्रह की सतह से प्रक्षेपित करने पर वह गुरुत्वीय क्षेत्र से बाहर (अनंत पर शून्य वेग के साथ, कुल यांत्रिक ऊर्जा \(E = 0\)) पहुँच जाए:

\[ \frac{1}{2}m v_e^2 - \frac{GM_E m}{R_E} = 0 \implies v_e = \sqrt{\frac{2GM_E}{R_E}} = \sqrt{2g R_E} \approx 11.2\,\mathrm{km\cdot s^{-1}} \text{ (पृथ्वी के लिए)} \]

ध्यान दें कि \(v_e\) प्रक्षेपित वस्तु के द्रव्यमान तथा प्रक्षेपण कोण/दिशा से स्वतंत्र होता है (वायु प्रतिरोध नगण्य मानने पर)। चंद्रमा पर \(v_e \approx 2.3\,\mathrm{km\cdot s^{-1}}\) बहुत कम होने के कारण वहाँ वायुमंडल नहीं टिक पाता।

वृत्ताकार कक्षा में उपग्रह की गति (त्रिज्या \(r = R_E + h\))

गुरुत्वाकर्षण बल आवश्यक अभिकेंद्रीय बल प्रदान करता है: \(\frac{mv_o^2}{r} = \frac{GM_E m}{r^2}\)।

  • कक्षीय चाल: \(v_o = \sqrt{\frac{GM_E}{r}} = \sqrt{\frac{g R_E^2}{R_E + h}}\)। पृथ्वी की सतह के अत्यंत निकट (\(h \ll R_E\)), \(v_o \approx \sqrt{g R_E} \approx 7.92\,\mathrm{km\cdot s^{-1}}\), अतः \(v_e = \sqrt{2}\,v_o\)।
  • परिक्रमण काल: \(T = \frac{2\pi r}{v_o} = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{r^3}{GM_E}}\)। पृथ्वी की सतह के निकट, \(T_0 = 2\pi\sqrt{\frac{R_E}{g}} \approx 85\,\mathrm{मिनट}\)।
  • परिक्रमा करते उपग्रह की ऊर्जा: \[ K = +\frac{GM_E m}{2r}, \qquad U = -\frac{GM_E m}{r}, \qquad E = K + U = -\frac{GM_E m}{2r} \] अर्थात् \(E = -K = \frac{U}{2}\)। ऋणात्मक कुल ऊर्जा एक बद्ध निकाय (bound system) को दर्शाती है।

भारहीनता: कक्षीय उपग्रह में अंतरिक्ष यात्री को भारहीनता इसलिए महसूस होती है क्योंकि यान और यात्री दोनों एकसमान अभिकेंद्रीय त्वरण \(g(h)\) से पृथ्वी की ओर मुक्त पतन (free fall) की अवस्था में होते हैं।

Worked Example 1: Altitude Dependence of Gravity

Assumed Scenario: A hypothetical planet has radius \(R_p = 4000\,\mathrm{km}\) and surface acceleration due to gravity \(g_0 = 10\,\mathrm{m\cdot s^{-2}}\). Calculate the altitude \(h\) above its surface at which the acceleration due to gravity drops to \(2.5\,\mathrm{m\cdot s^{-2}}\).

Solution: Using the exact altitude formula:

\[ g(h) = \frac{g_0}{\left(1 + \frac{h}{R_p}\right)^2} \]

Substituting \(g(h) = 2.5\,\mathrm{m\cdot s^{-2}}\) and \(g_0 = 10\,\mathrm{m\cdot s^{-2}}\):

\[ 2.5 = \frac{10}{\left(1 + \frac{h}{R_p}\right)^2} \implies \left(1 + \frac{h}{R_p}\right)^2 = 4 \implies 1 + \frac{h}{R_p} = 2 \] \[ h = R_p = 4000\,\mathrm{km} \]

Worked Example 2: Total Energy & Binding Energy of a Satellite

Assumed Scenario: A hypothetical satellite of mass \(m = 300\,\mathrm{kg}\) revolves around Earth in a circular orbit at an altitude \(h = 2R_E\). Taking \(R_E = 6.4 \times 10^6\,\mathrm{m}\) and \(g = 9.8\,\mathrm{m\cdot s^{-2}}\), find (a) its total mechanical energy and (b) the minimum extra energy needed to free it from Earth's gravity.

Solution: The orbital radius is \(r = R_E + h = R_E + 2R_E = 3R_E\).

(a) Using \(GM_E = gR_E^2\):

\[ E = -\frac{GM_E m}{2r} = -\frac{g R_E^2 m}{2(3R_E)} = -\frac{mg R_E}{6} \] \[ E = -\frac{300 \times 9.8 \times 6.4 \times 10^6}{6} = -3.136 \times 10^9\,\mathrm{J} \]

(b) To escape to infinity (\(E_\infty = 0\)), the minimum extra energy required is:

\[ \Delta E = 0 - E = +3.136 \times 10^9\,\mathrm{J} \]

Worked Example 3: Kepler's Third Law for an Elliptical Orbit

Assumed Scenario: A hypothetical asteroid orbits the Sun in an elliptical path with perihelion distance \(r_P = 0.5\,\mathrm{AU}\) and aphelion distance \(r_A = 3.5\,\mathrm{AU}\) (where \(1\,\mathrm{AU}\) is Earth's mean orbital radius, with period \(T_E = 1\,\mathrm{year}\)). Find the orbital period of the asteroid.

Solution: The semi-major axis of the asteroid's orbit is:

\[ a = \frac{r_P + r_A}{2} = \frac{0.5 + 3.5}{2} = 2.0\,\mathrm{AU} \]

By Kepler's Law of Periods (\(T^2 \propto a^3\)):

\[ \left(\frac{T}{T_E}\right)^2 = \left(\frac{a}{a_E}\right)^3 \implies T^2 = (1\,\mathrm{year})^2 \times \left(\frac{2.0}{1.0}\right)^3 = 8\,\mathrm{year}^2 \] \[ T = 2\sqrt{2}\,\mathrm{years} \approx 2.83\,\mathrm{years} \]

Practice Question 1 (Single-Correct MCQ)

Two point masses \(m\) and \(4m\) are fixed at a separation \(d\). At what distance from the mass \(m\) on the line joining them should a third point mass \(m_0\) be placed so that it experiences zero net gravitational force?

  • (A) \(\frac{d}{2}\)
  • (B) \(\frac{d}{3}\)
  • (C) \(\frac{d}{4}\)
  • (D) \(\frac{2d}{3}\)
Correct Answer: (B)

Worked Solution: Let \(m_0\) be placed at distance \(x\) from \(m\), so its distance from \(4m\) is \((d - x)\). For zero net force, the magnitudes of the two opposing gravitational attractions must be equal:

\[ \frac{G m m_0}{x^2} = \frac{G (4m) m_0}{(d - x)^2} \implies \frac{1}{x} = \frac{2}{d - x} \implies d - x = 2x \implies x = \frac{d}{3} \]

Three identical particles, each of mass \(m\), are placed at the vertices of an equilateral triangle of side \(L\). Taking gravitational potential energy at infinity to be zero, what is the total gravitational potential energy of the system?

  • (A) \(-\frac{Gm^2}{L}\)
  • (B) \(-\frac{2Gm^2}{L}\)
  • (C) \(-\frac{3Gm^2}{L}\)
  • (D) \(-\frac{6Gm^2}{L}\)
Correct Answer: (C)

Worked Solution: For 3 particles, there are \(\frac{3 \times 2}{2} = 3\) distinct pairs, each separated by distance \(L\). Summing the pair potential energies:

\[ U_{\text{total}} = 3 \times \left(-\frac{Gm^2}{L}\right) = -\frac{3Gm^2}{L} \]

Practice Question 7 (Single-Correct MCQ)

A satellite orbits the Earth in a circular orbit with total mechanical energy \(-E_0\) (where \(E_0 > 0\)). What are its kinetic energy \(K\) and gravitational potential energy \(U\), respectively?

  • (A) \(K = E_0,\; U = -2E_0\)
  • (B) \(K = 2E_0,\; U = -3E_0\)
  • (C) \(K = \frac{E_0}{2},\; U = -\frac{3E_0}{2}\)
  • (D) \(K = E_0,\; U = -E_0\)
Correct Answer: (A)

Worked Solution: For a circularly orbiting satellite, \(K = \frac{GM_E m}{2r}\), \(U = -\frac{GM_E m}{r}\), and \(E = -\frac{GM_E m}{2r}\). Thus \(K = -E = E_0\) and \(U = 2E = -2E_0\).

Practice Question 8 (Single-Correct MCQ)

A point mass \(m\) is placed inside a uniform hollow spherical shell of mass \(M\) and radius \(R\) at a distance \(\frac{R}{2}\) from its centre. What is the magnitude of the gravitational force exerted by the shell on \(m\)?

  • (A) \(\frac{4GMm}{R^2}\)
  • (B) \(\frac{GMm}{2R^2}\)
  • (C) \(\frac{GMm}{R^2}\)
  • (D) Zero
Correct Answer: (D)

Worked Solution: By the spherical shell theorem, the gravitational field and force inside a uniform hollow spherical shell are identically zero at every interior point, regardless of its distance from the centre.

9. Common Pitfalls, Revision Checklist & Final Recap / सामान्य त्रुटियाँ, पुनरावृत्ति सूची एवं सारांश

English

Common NEET UG Pitfalls to Avoid

  • Using \(g\left(1 - \frac{2h}{R_E}\right)\) for large heights: This approximation is valid only when \(h \ll R_E\). If \(h\) is comparable to \(R_E\) (e.g., \(h = R_E/2\) or \(h = R_E\)), always use the exact formula \(g(h) = \frac{g}{\left(1 + h/R_E\right)^2}\).
  • Forgetting the negative sign in \(U\) and \(E\): Taking \(U(\infty) = 0\), both \(U\) and \(E\) for bound systems are negative. Moving to a higher orbit increases \(E\) (makes it less negative) and increases \(U\), while decreasing \(K\).
  • Confusing \(mgh\) with exact \(\Delta U\): Use \(\Delta U = \frac{mgh}{1 + h/R_E}\) whenever \(h\) is not negligible compared to \(R_E\).

High-Yield Revision Checklist & Recap

  • \(F = \frac{Gm_1 m_2}{r^2}\); \(g = \frac{GM_E}{R_E^2}\) is maximum at Earth's surface and zero at the centre.
  • Inside a uniform hollow shell: \(E = 0\), \(V = -\frac{GM}{R}\) (constant, non-zero).
  • Kepler's Laws: Elliptical orbit; \(dA/dt = L/(2m) = \text{const}\); \(T^2 \propto a^3\).
  • Escape speed \(v_e = \sqrt{2gR_E} = \sqrt{2}\,v_o\); Satellite energies: \(E = -K = U/2\).
हिन्दी

NEET UG में बचने योग्य सामान्य त्रुटियाँ

  • अधिक ऊँचाई के लिए \(g\left(1 - \frac{2h}{R_E}\right)\) का उपयोग करना: यह सन्निकटन केवल तभी मान्य है जब \(h \ll R_E\) हो। यदि \(h\), \(R_E\) के तुलनीय हो (जैसे \(h = R_E/2\) या \(R_E\)), तो सदैव सटीक सूत्र \(g(h) = \frac{g}{\left(1 + h/R_E\right)^2}\) का प्रयोग करें।
  • \(U\) और \(E\) में ऋणात्मक चिह्न भूलना: \(U(\infty) = 0\) मानने पर बद्ध निकायों के लिए \(U\) और \(E\) दोनों ऋणात्मक होते हैं। ऊँची कक्षा में जाने पर \(E\) और \(U\) बढ़ते हैं (कम ऋणात्मक होते हैं), जबकि गतिज ऊर्जा \(K\) घटती है।
  • \(mgh\) और सटीक \(\Delta U\) में भ्रम: जब \(h\) की तुलना \(R_E\) से नगण्य न हो, तब सदैव \(\Delta U = \frac{mgh}{1 + h/R_E}\) का उपयोग करें।

त्वरित पुनरावृत्ति सूची एवं अंतिम सारांश

  • \(F = \frac{Gm_1 m_2}{r^2}\); \(g = \frac{GM_E}{R_E^2}\) पृथ्वी की सतह पर अधिकतम और केंद्र पर शून्य होता है।
  • एकसमान खोखले कोश के भीतर: तीव्रता \(E = 0\), परंतु विभव \(V = -\frac{GM}{R}\) (नियत, अशून्य)।
  • केपलर के नियम: दीर्घवृत्ताकार कक्षा; \(dA/dt = L/(2m) = \text{नियत}\); \(T^2 \propto a^3\)।
  • पलायन वेग \(v_e = \sqrt{2gR_E} = \sqrt{2}\,v_o\); उपग्रह की ऊर्जा: \(E = -K = U/2\)।
